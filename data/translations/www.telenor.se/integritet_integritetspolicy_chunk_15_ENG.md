##

  * Read more about treatment outside of Sweden

Depending on the service you have, your data may be transferred to
countries outside of Sweden or the EU/EEA (so-called "third countries"). If your data
is transferred to any third country, we will take appropriate protective measures,
such as:

    * Concluding [**EU Commission's model clauses**](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/SV/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32021D0914&from=FI) with the recipient
    * Ensuring that **Binding Corporate Rules** are in place (binding internal corporate rules approved by the relevant authority).

You should also be aware that we may transfer data to other
countries, for example if you choose to use our services to contact
someone in or from another such country.

If you want to know more, you can [contact
us](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt1).
