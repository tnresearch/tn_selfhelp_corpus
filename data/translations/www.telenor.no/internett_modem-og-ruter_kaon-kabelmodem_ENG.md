# Help with KAON Modem (VM3000G)

## KAON Modem (VM3000G)

This modem handles the highest speeds we can offer and is optimal for
achieving the best result when streaming 4K Ultra HD video and VR gaming.

### KAON Connection

Here you will find help and guidance for connecting the modem.

[Connect modem ➜](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/koble-kabelmodem/)

  * #### Lamp Status

.

 **POWER:**

    * No light - Not connected to power

    * Solid blue light - Connected to power

 **DS (Downstream)**

    * No light - No signal connection

    * Blinking green - Searching for frequency

    * Solid green - Signal connection OK

 **US (Upstream)**

    * No light - No signal connection

    * Blinking green - Searching for frequency

    * Solid green - Signal connection OK

 **ONLINE:**

    * No light - Not connected to the internet

    * Blinking green - Connecting to the internet

    * Solid green - Connected to the internet

 **ETHERNET:**

    * No light - No connection to other equipment

    * Solid green - Connection ok  
  
---  
  
  * #### Reset modem

    1. Modem must be connected to power

    2. Find something thin such as a pen/paperclip

    3. Press the button in the hole marked RESET

    4. Hold the button down for 5 seconds

    5. The modem will restart automatically.

 _Note: The lights will blink for a while before stabilizing._
