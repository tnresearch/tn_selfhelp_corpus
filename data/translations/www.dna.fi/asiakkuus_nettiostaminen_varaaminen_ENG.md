# Click & Collect

You can reserve some products online or choose a store as the pick-up location for your order. Read the instructions below!

## Order and pay online, pick up from the store

#### Here's how:

#### **1. Place your order**

You can order a product online and pick it up from any DNA Store. Delivery to the store is possible for some products: you will see "Pick up from DNA Store" as a delivery option in the online store when the product in question can be ordered online and picked up from the store.

#### 2. Wait for a message

You will receive a message when the product is ready for pick-up, within 1-3 business days. Your order will be kept at the store for 7 days. Bring identification!

#### 3. Pick up the product from DNA Store

You can find the nearest DNA Store and its opening hours here:

[Locations and opening hours](https://www.dna.fi/dnakaupat)

## Reserve online, pick up and pay in store

#### Here's how:

#### **1. Reserve a product online**

You can reserve a product online and pick it up from the store immediately or at the latest the next business day. Reserving a product is possible when the product is immediately in stock at the desired store. You know that a product is reservable if you see a "Pick up from store" button under the shopping cart in the online store.

Please check the opening hours of the store you have selected before making the reservation; if the DNA Store you have selected is closed at the time of pick-up, the reservation cannot be transferred to another store.

#### 2. Pick up the product at the latest the next business day

You will receive a text message when the reservation is made. The product is available for pick-up after this confirmation. The reservation is valid until the next day and will automatically expire if you do not pick up the product during the reservation period.

#### 3. Pay for the product at DNA Store

The reservation is not binding – you can make the final purchase decision only in the store. You can find the nearest DNA Store and its opening hours here:

[Locations and opening hours](https://www.dna.fi/dnakaupat)

## Reservation Terms

Please note that a reservation is not an online purchase and does not constitute a binding order. A reservation is also not yet a binding purchase order or agreement to buy or sell the product. The purchase made in the store after the reservation is not subject to the distance selling provisions of the Consumer Protection Act or its right of withdrawal.

The same terms apply to the purchase as when buying products from the store without a reservation. The actual agreement for the sale of the product is made in the store. DNA has the right not to enter into an agreement with a person under the age of 18.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reserve all products in the store?

Currently, only a portion of DNA Online Store products are covered by Click & Collect. There are two options:

_Reserving a product in the store:_

You can reserve some products to your chosen store. This is possible when the product is in stock at the store at the time of reservation. If there is a "Pick up from store" button under the product description page, the product is generally reservable.

_Delivery of an ordered product to the store:_

Delivery to the store is possible for some products: you will see "Pick up from store" as a delivery option during the ordering process in the online store when the product in question can be ordered online and picked up from the store.

Which stores are involved in Click & Collect?

All DNA Store stores are involved. The delivery policy varies depending on whether you are reserving a product from the store or ordering a product from the online store for pick-up at the store:

_Reserving a product in the store:_

You can reserve a product in DNA Stores where it is immediately available at the time of reservation. You can see the stores on the product description page when you click the button under the shopping cart: "Pick up from store".

_Delivery of an ordered product to the store:_

When you order and pay for a product immediately online, you can choose any DNA Store [store](https://www.dna.fi/dnakaupat) as its delivery location. You will receive a message when the product is ready for pick-up.

How quickly can I pick up the product?

A reserved product is available for pick-up on the same day. The reservation is valid until the next business day. If you have already ordered the product and selected the store as the delivery location, the delivery time is 1-3 business days. A product ordered to the store must be picked up within a week of the order.

In both cases, you will receive a message when the product is ready for pick-up.

Can anyone pick up the product?

Yes, with a power of attorney.
