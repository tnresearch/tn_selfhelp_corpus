## Frequently Asked Questions about Prepaid Cards

  * Why do I have to register my prepaid card?

The Swedish Parliament has decided to introduce a new law requiring all
prepaid cards, regardless of operator, to be registered to a user. From August
1, 2022, all new prepaid cards must be registered to be used. In February
2023, all existing prepaid cards must also be registered to their user.

  * Why can't I top up my prepaid card?

Since February 1, 2023, all prepaid cards must be **registered** in order to
be topped up. This is a consequence of the law change that came into force on
August 1, 2022.

[Register your prepaid card](/link/9d9b1dd3829a415580e98ad6af78f872.aspx)  

Problems topping up the card can also be due to

    * the card is no longer **valid**. It needs to be topped up at least once a year, otherwise it will be terminated. In the app [Telenor Top-up](/link/8ad148929ae541328599af3f428082a6.aspx) you can see if your prepaid card is still active.
    * your credit card is not **open for online purchases**. Change the setting with the card issuer, or try another way to pay, such as Swish.

  * How do I buy a Hello prepaid card?

You can do this on the page [Order
start package](/link/ae66340a13b54008a7c18b198ea6ad32.aspx). Hello is one of our
fixed-price top-ups.

  * I already have a prepaid card with you but want Hello. How do I do that?

Great that you want Hello! It is one of our fixed-price top-ups. You can choose
between 1 GB and 10 GB. To get Hello, do the following:

    1. Go to [telenor.se/ladda ](/link/8ad148929ae541328599af3f428082a6.aspx)
    2. Choose one of the Hello top-ups

  * How much does it cost to use the prepaid card abroad?

If you travel within the EU/EEA, it costs the same as at home. If you travel
outside the EU/EEA [you can see here](/link/a728a2eee9b24f87867517e059303cae.aspx)
what it costs. If you travel outside the EU/EEA, you must top up with
Extra Balance. In order for your prepaid card to work abroad, you must first
have made calls with it in Sweden.
