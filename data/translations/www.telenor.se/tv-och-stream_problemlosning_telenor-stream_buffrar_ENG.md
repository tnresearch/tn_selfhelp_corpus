# It just keeps buffering – what should I do?

Are you experiencing picture disturbances while watching Telenor Stream? Start by
checking if you have the latest version of the app downloaded. You can do this
by going to Google Play or the App Store and looking under Updates.

The problems may also be due to a poor connection or high load. The surroundings
can affect your data network, for example, thick concrete walls, radio waves
or many people streaming at the same time.

### Here's what to do:

If you are watching on your computer

  * Restart the streaming service.
  * Check that you have a download speed of at least 6 Mbit/s. You can easily do this at [Bredbandskollen.se](http://www.Bredbandskollen.se "Bredbandskollen").
  * Do you have the latest version of the web browser? If not, you need to update.
  * Check if there are any [service disruptions](/link/7efe29cb89ca42a083e61f0ffa35e282.aspx) right now.
  * Check if the service works better on another device, such as your mobile phone.
  * Reduce the distance between the wireless router and the computer. Or disconnect the router completely and connect a network cable directly between the wall socket and the computer.
  * If it works when you connect directly to the wall, you should factory reset your router: Take a paperclip and press it into the hole labeled Reset on the back of the router. Press and hold the button until the router restarts and wait about five minutes. 

If you are watching on your mobile phone or tablet

  * Try restarting the streaming service.
  * Check if there are any current [service disruptions](/link/c27c15a8727d44248258b57663b3b491.aspx).
  * If you have access to wifi – try connecting to it instead.
  * Have you tested if you have a fast enough internet connection? To stream, you need to have at least 6 Mbit/s download speed. Go to [Bredbandskollen.se](http://www.Bredbandskollen.se "Bredbandskollen") and check.
  * You can also try streaming via another device, such as your computer. This will give you a better idea of where the problem lies.

If you are using Apple TV

  * Check if there are any [service disruptions](/link/7efe29cb89ca42a083e61f0ffa35e282.aspx).
  * When using Apple TV, you need a download speed of at least 10 Mbit/s. Go to [Bredbandskollen.se](http://www.Bredbandskollen.se "Bredbandskollen") and test.
  * Have you updated your software recently? If not, go in and do so.

If you are using Chromecast

  * Check if you have the latest software.
  * Restart by unplugging and plugging it back in.

[More about Chromecast from Google](https://support.google.com/chromecast/?hl=sv
"google.com/chromecast")
