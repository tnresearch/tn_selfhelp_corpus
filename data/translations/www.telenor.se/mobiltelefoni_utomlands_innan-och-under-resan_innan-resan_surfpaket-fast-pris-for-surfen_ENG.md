# Surf Packages – fixed price for surfing

You’ve landed. Your vacation has just begun. You want to check the map, emails, send a picture in the group chat. But most of all. You want to avoid worrying about the costs.

For you, we recommend Telenor Surf Packages*. Telenor Surf Packages cost 399 Swedish krona and include 1, 2 or 3 GB of data, depending on the country. 1 GB is enough for approximately 80 minutes of music and 40 minutes of social media per day.

You can use the Surf Package for 14 days, from the moment you start surfing in the country where the Surf Package is valid. If you are going to be away for more than 14 days, you need to top up with another Surf Package.

Surf Packages do not work with Telenor prepaid cards.

## Is the country you are travelling to not on the list?

Then we do not offer Telenor Surf Packages in that country. Please note that in the EU and EEA it actually costs [nothing extra to surf](/roaming/). This applies regardless of whether you have a prepaid mobile card or a subscription with us. Our subscriptions also include data in Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar (Burma), USA, Turkey, Switzerland, United Kingdom (including Gibraltar), Andorra, Serbia and Montenegro.

## How to order a Telenor Surf Package

You can order in advance if you wish (up to 30 days before your departure), or by following the instructions in the welcome SMS you receive when you arrive at your destination. The Surf Package is activated only when you start surfing in the country it is valid for.

If you want to order in advance, text [the correct code] to 222. You can find the codes under the heading "In the following countries we offer Telenor Surf Packages" above.

## If you want more data

When the data included in the Surf Package (1 or 2 GB) starts to run out, you will receive an SMS asking if you want to buy a new Surf Package. If you text [the correct code] to 222, your data pot will be refilled. You can find the codes under the heading "In the following countries we offer Telenor Surf Packages" above.

If you choose not to buy a new Surf Package, but continue to surf abroad when your pot is empty, it costs as much as [standard price](/roaming/).

*Please note that Telenor Surf Packages only apply to data. You call, text and send MMS to [standard price](/roaming/) in the country.

** The Surf Package applies to Guernsey, Jersey and Isle of Man. Therefore, it is the same code, 2GUE, to order the Surf Package.
