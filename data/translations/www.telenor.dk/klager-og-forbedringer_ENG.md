## Tell us about your experience

We are sorry that we did not meet your expectations. Tell us about your
experience - we would very much like to help. At Telenor, we take responsibility, so we are ready to listen to you. We can often resolve your inquiry immediately.  

You can call us directly at 72 100 100 or write to us at
[kundeservice@telenor.dk](mailto:kundeservice@telenor.dk)

**Complaint about repair**  
If you wish to complain to Telenor about a repair or a repair process,
you should write to [info@care1.dk](mailto:info@care1.dk). Write
repair complaint in the subject line.

## If we cannot agree on a solution

**Billing Complaints Board**  
Should you unexpectedly not have your complaint resolved, you can write to
Telenor's Billing Complaints Board at
[regningsklagenaevn@telenor.dk](mailto:regningsklagenaevn@telenor.dk).

Telenor's Billing Complaints Board was established on February 1, 2020 and is an
internal investigation unit consisting of a lawyer and a special team of
customer service employees. The Billing Complaints Board investigates and processes your
case. You can expect to receive a decision in your case within 6 weeks. If you
disagree with Telenor's decision, you can contact the boards below.
