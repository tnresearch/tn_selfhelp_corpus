# When will I receive my delivery?

Delivery times vary depending on the product.

### Mobile phone, mobile broadband and home phone

If the products you ordered are in stock, the delivery time is 1-3 business days. If
they are not in stock, the products are usually delivered within 1-3 weeks.
The delivery time for home phone via broadband is normally 3-5 weeks.

### Broadband via fiber

In most cases, you can activate your broadband service via fiber directly
with your social security number or with the information we send to you via SMS.
However, please note that you forfeit your 14-day right of withdrawal if you choose to
activate the service before the cancellation period has expired. In some cases where Telenor
does not have exclusive rights to broadband, it may take up to 10 business days before you can
start using the service.

### TV

The delivery time from order is 5-10 days.

Here's how it works:  
In connection with the delivery, your broadband connection will be adjusted to
support TV. An HD box, remote control, HDMI cable, network cable and in some
cases a new DSL modem will be sent to you. A program card will also be sent
separately.

For those of you who have broadband via the telephone jack (DSL), one of the modem's ports will be
reconfigured (this is indicated either with a color marking directly on
the modem's port or is noted in the modem's accompanying manual). It will then only work for the HD box, not for a regular computer. If you have a computer connected there today, move it to another port, for example 3X or 4X
(it may take up to 30 minutes before the connection starts working after you
change port).
