# Connect a different router

If you want to use a router other than the one you borrow from Telenor, connect
it by following the manufacturer's instructions.  

 **Tip!** Most routers have a port labeled WAN or
Internet. Connect your network cable between this port and the fiber outlet. A
simple thing to do!

### Do you want to use wifi?

Then choose your wireless network in your mobile or computer settings. If
you are using a router from Telenor, you will find the network name and password on
the underside of the device. This is usually also the case for routers from other
providers.

More help to get started with your wifi:

[Wifi settings for Windows](/support/fast-broadband/wifi/get-started/connect-
to-wifi-via-windows-10/)  
[Wifi settings for macOS](/support/fast-broadband/wifi/get-started/connect-
to-wifi-via-macos/)  
[Wifi settings for Android](/support/fast-broadband/wifi/get-started/connect-
to-wifi-via-android/)  
[Wifi settings for iOS (iPhone and iPad)](/support/fast-
broadband/wifi/get-started/connect-to-wifi-via-iphone-and-ipad/)

If you do not want wifi, connect your computer to the router via a
network cable.
