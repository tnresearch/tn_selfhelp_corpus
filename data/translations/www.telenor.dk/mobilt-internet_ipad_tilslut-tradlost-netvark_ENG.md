# Connect Your iPad to a Wireless Network (Wi-Fi)

You can connect all iPads to a wireless network. Your iPad remembers all networks it has previously connected to and will automatically go online via the networks it recognizes.

  * ## Tap "Settings"

  * ## Tap "Wi-Fi" and select the network you want to log in to
