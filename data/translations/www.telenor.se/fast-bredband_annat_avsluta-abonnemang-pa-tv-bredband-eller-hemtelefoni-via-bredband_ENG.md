# Terminate or Transfer Broadband or TV

Do you need to terminate your broadband or TV subscription, perhaps because you are moving? Then you have a few different options.

If you want to terminate a mobile subscription, you can find information [here](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/hantera-abonnemang/avsluta-abonnemang/).

Easiest!

Take with you

To check if you can take your subscription with you, search for your new address in our address search. If we offer the same services as you have now, you can move your subscriptions with you. All you need to do is call us at [020-222 222](tel:020222222) and tell us that you are moving.  
  
[Broadband Address Search](/handla/bredband/)  
  
[TV Address Search](/handla/tv/)

Win-win!

Transfer

If you are moving and have a contract period on your subscription, the best thing to do is to transfer the subscription – preferably to the person who is moving in after you. You avoid a high final invoice and hassle with returning equipment. And the person taking over avoids setup fees and can start using the service on the day they move in.

When a person has passed away, the subscription is automatically terminated. If you would rather have the subscription transferred, you need to notify us.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Terminating Subscriptions

  * What is the notice period for my subscription?

### Mobile Telephony

The notice period for mobile subscriptions is one month.

You can find your agreement in My Telenor - either [here on the web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) or in the [app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

### Broadband and TV

Different subscriptions have different notice periods. What applies to your subscription can be found in the campaign terms and conditions you received when you ordered the subscription. From May 1, 2014, the notice period for all newly signed and upgraded subscriptions is 1 month. If you signed your subscription before May 1, 2014, the notice period is normally 3 calendar months.

  * How do I find out my contract period?

You can see any contract period in My Telenor [here on the web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx). If you have a mobile subscription, you can also see it in our [app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

  
[Information about **notice period** can be found here](/link/8ea002ab5007459a87a3d2f6bf776146.aspx)

  * Can I pause my subscription?

It is not possible to pause mobile subscriptions or broadband subscriptions. But if you have mobile broadband, you can change the data volume to 1GB whenever you want, for example when you leave the summer cottage for the winter. You change the data volume in [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).
