# Problems with the Telenor Stream app for smart TV

If you have downloaded the app but are having trouble getting it to work, you can try this:

  * **Check that your account is active and that you are logged in**  
**To use the Telenor Stream app, you must be logged in to your Telenor
Stream account. If you are already logged in and are experiencing problems, try logging out
and logging back in again.**

  * **Reinstall the app**  
**Sometimes it can help to uninstall the app and then reinstall it.**

  * **Restart the TV**
  * **Check that the TV is connected to the internet**
  * **Update the TV's software**  
**Updates can be found in different places depending on the manufacturer of your
TV.  
**Samsung:** Settings > Support > Software Update > Update Now  
**LG:** Settings > All Settings > Support > Software Update >
Search for Updates

  * **Reset**  
**Still not working? Then you can try resetting the TV. But keep in
mind that you will then be logged out of all apps on the TV.**  
**Samsung** : Reset Smart Hub  
 **LG:** Settings > All Settings > General > System > Reset
to original settings

**  
Still not working?** [Contact us](/support/old-kontakta-oss/) and we
will help you.
