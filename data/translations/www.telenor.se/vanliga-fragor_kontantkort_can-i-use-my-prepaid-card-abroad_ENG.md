# Can I use my prepaid card abroad?

Yes, you can use your prepaid card within EU/EEA (except Réunion and St
Martin).

[To see roaming prices for the rest of the world, click here](/roaming/).

Prices when travelling within EU/EEA: Same as in Sweden.  
Prices when travelling outside EU/EEA: [Click here and choose your
destination](/roaming/).

If you have Telenor Fastpris (including Hello), you must first top up your
card with Flexible/Balance.
