# Recommendations in T-We

Wondering how our recommendations work? Here you will find information about
how we tailor the content shown in T-We.

Home: Under this menu you will find recommendations.

## Recommendations and Personalized Content

You will receive suggestions for programs and movies in several places in T-We, but they will always
be available from the Home menu. You access this by pressing the T-We
button on the remote control.

The recommendations may be editorially driven, but as we learn more about
what you watch and are interested in, they will become more personal and tailored
to you. It is important to note that the suggestions you receive are based on what you watch
from a given profile, so if everyone in the family uses the same profile, this will
affect the result.

### Thumbs Up or Down?

You can influence your recommendations by rating the content you have watched. If
you have watched a movie or series that you like, you will find a thumb icon on
the information page for this.

Press the thumb icon once to give a thumbs up, and once again to
switch to thumbs down. This way, you tell us what you want in terms of
recommended content.

  *  **Thumbs Up** - I like the content and want more recommendations with similar content

  *  **Thumbs Down** - I do not like the content and want fewer recommendations with similar content.

## Questions and Answers

  * #### How is data collected for recommendations?

Information about what you watch and when is stored across TV channels,
archives and movie rentals.

Based on this, we use an algorithm that analyzes the data and
compares your preferences with others. If other TV viewers have the same type
of preferences as you, you may be recommended a movie or series
that they liked, but that you may not have seen yet.

In addition, the algorithm uses information about genre, actors, etc. to
find content similar to what you have already watched.

For Telenor's processing of personal data, [see
the privacy policy.](https://www.telenor.no/om/personvern/)

  * #### Are the recommendations based on everything I watch?

Almost everything. If you browse through channels and watch a few seconds of each program, that will not
affect your profile. Erotic rental movies are also excluded from
the recommendation engine.

  * #### Can I remove information about what I have watched?

Yes. In the [profile menu](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/profiler/) you can
reset each individual profile. This means that the profile starts from scratch
with regard to recommendations.

You can also [delete your search history](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-
we/sok/) for your profile, but this only affects searches and not the
recommendations you receive.

  * #### Can I turn off personalized recommendations?

Yes, you can. Here's how:

    1. Turn on the TV and decoder

    2. Press **Menu**

    3. Under **My T-We** press **Reservation**

    4. Press **Send SMS**

    5. Enter the code received via SMS

If you opt out of personalized recommendations, you will still receive recommendations
from our editorial staff. You can change your mind at any time and turn the personalized
recommendations back on.
