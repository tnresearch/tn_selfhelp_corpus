# Balance and Usage

Wondering how much you've used since your last invoice or charge? You get
a full overview in the [My Telenor](/privat/mobil/app/mitt-telenor/) app and on
My Pages.

### Check balance or usage on My Pages or in the My Telenor app

[

My Pages

Click here to see usage and balance

](/mitt-telenor/mobil/)

If you are not logged in, you must do so first. Then select the number you want
to see usage or balance for. **Note:** The usage for **Telenor Prepaid** is not
available.

[

My Telenor

Click here to open the app

](https://tlnr.no/mt)

Once you're in the app, scroll left at the top to select the number
you want to view.

## Usage back in time?

Do you want to see what the usage was last autumn, or just last month?

  1. Go to My Pages and retrieve previous invoices via the link below

Select the desired year and month

  2. Click on the invoice number

  3. You can now choose to view the invoice in a browser or as a PDF

[Retrieve invoice on My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/faktura/)
