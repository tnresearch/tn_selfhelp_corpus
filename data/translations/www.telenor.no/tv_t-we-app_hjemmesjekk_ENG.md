## Are you getting a message in the T-We App saying you are not at home, even though you are?

For some TV channels and streaming services, the rights are such that content
can only be viewed on mobile devices and Apple TV when you are at home. In practice, this means that your mobile phone, tablet or Apple TV must be **on the same
local network as your T-We box.**

For mobile phones and tablets, the first check should be to ensure that you are
actually connected to your own WiFi network and not the mobile network.

## Is your T-We box connected to the home network and connected to power?

For the mobile phone, tablet or Apple TV to find out if you are
at home, it needs to be able to connect to your T-We box. In other words, your
T-We box must be connected to power and connected to the local network. The T-We box must
also not be turned off with the power switch on the back, but can be turned off
with the remote control ("standby"). Check that there is a light in the display or on the on/off
button on the front of the T-We box.

If you have changed the power saving settings on your T-We box, and you have
chosen maximum power saving, this setting must be set back to normal
power saving in the settings menu on the T-We box.

## Have you given the app access?

To ensure that your mobile phone or tablet can search for and find your T-We
box, you must give the T-We app permission to connect to your local
network or location.

It asks for this the first time you install the app, but if you did not give
permission then, or have made changes afterwards - this must be turned
on again for the app to work.

 **Here's how:**

 **iPhone:**

  1. Open Settings

  2. Scroll down to Telenor T-We

  3. Check that access to Local Network is turned on

 **Android:**

  1. Open settings on your mobile

  2. Scroll down to Apps

  3. Scroll to find T-We App

  4. Tap on Permissions and check that Location is allowed

## Are you using a Mesh solution or multiple routers that effectively give you
separate networks?

Certain types of network setups can prevent the T-We app from connecting
with the T-We box. This is especially true if you have set up multiple routers in
your home network.

A type of network equipment that often can interfere is Google WiFi and some other
WiFi Mesh products. If you have set up your network with multiple routers,
or combined e.g. the router from Telenor with a Google WiFi device, this can
create problems. For devices that are connected, it will then appear as two
different wireless networks, and they will not be able to connect with each other.
Some WiFi repeaters can also block the app and T-We box from seeing
each other.

Some mesh solutions can be configured in so-called "bridge mode" – check
the documentation for your solution.
