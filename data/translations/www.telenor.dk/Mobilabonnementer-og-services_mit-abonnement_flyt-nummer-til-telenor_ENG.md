# How do I transfer my current mobile number to Telenor?

We are happy that you want to transfer your number to Telenor. We can transfer
your Danish mobile number to Telenor, regardless of which telecom company you are transferring from.

There are three important things you need to be aware of when you want to switch
to Telenor, but keep your current phone number:

  * **Do not cancel your phone number**  
It is important that you **DO NOT** cancel your phone number with your current
company. If you cancel your mobile number yourself, you lose the right to the number,
and therefore we cannot transfer it to us. Therefore, it is important that you
choose **Transfer Number** **(and not cancel it)** when you purchase a new
subscription with us – then we will take care of all the practicalities in connection with
the number transfer.

  * **Fill out the power of attorney**  
When you have ordered number transfer, you will receive an email with a power of attorney, which
it is important that you fill out. There you must include your
SIM card number. See [here](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/opsatning/find-simkort-nummer/), where you can find it. If you do not
fill out the power of attorney within the period described in the email,
you will automatically be assigned a random mobile number. All this information is also
in the email you receive.

**Check activation date**  
You will receive an email from us with the activation date, so you know when your
Telenor subscription is active.

### When you have ordered a number transfer, you will receive:

  * An email with a power of attorney for the number transfer, which it is **important that you fill out**
  * An email with the date when your number has been transferred and becomes active with Telenor
  * A new SIM card by mail or by email, if you have ordered eSIM

If you have ordered a physical SIM card, you must put the SIM card
in your mobile on the activation date. The SIM card fits all mobile phones. If you have ordered eSIM,
you can read how to activate it [here](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-
og-services/opsatning/esim/).

If you are in doubt about anything regarding number transfer, please feel free to
contact customer service on 72 100 100.

We look forward to welcoming you to Telenor.
