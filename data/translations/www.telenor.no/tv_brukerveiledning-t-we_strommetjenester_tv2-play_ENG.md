# Help with TV 2 Play Basis

Here you will find guides to get started with TV 2 Play and help if you get stuck.

## TV 2 Play Basis is included in T-We!

[TV 2 Play Basis](/tv/t-we/tv2-play/) is available on your **TV box, in the T-We app, in the TV 2 Play app and on tv2play.no.**  
**To access our streaming services, the TV box must be connected to the internet.** [Here's how to do it.](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/koble-til/)

## Log in to TV 2 Play

Is this the first time you're going to use TV 2 Play? The video below shows how to activate your account and log in to your T-We box:

[I have a TV 2 Play account already](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/tv2-play/#konto)[I want to log in to the T-We App](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/tv2-play/#app)

## Here's how to do it

  1. Find the program you want to watch on your T-We Box and press **Play in TV 2 Play**

  2. Press **Confirm** to proceed

  3. Scan the QR code with your mobile phone, or go directly to the link provided on your PC or mobile phone

  4. Select **Continue with Telenor T-We**

  5. Log in with your **Telenor ID**

  6. Fill out the registration form - email address, mobile number, name and date of birth and press **Save information**

  7. Enter the one-time code that appears on your TV screen and proceed

  8. On the TV screen, select the desired profile to complete the login. Now you are ready to stream!

## T-We App

## I have a TV 2 Play account already

If you already have an account for TV 2 Play, you can link it to your T-We subscription. This way you get the basic package at no extra cost, and you get a discount if you want to upgrade to packages with more content.

  * #### Link existing TV 2 Play account to T-We

T-We App

    1. In the T-We app, select Open in TV 2 Play on the content you want to watch

    2. Press the Log in button

    3. Select Continue with Telenor T-We

    4. Log in with [Telenor ID](/telenor-id/)

    5. Press Run on

    6. Fill in mobile number and other information and select Save the information

    7. Enter your password for your existing TV 2 Play account

    8. Select Run on to link the accounts

    9. Select Log in to log in to the account

    10. Select Play... to watch the desired content

  * #### Link existing TV 2 Play account to T-We

On TV box

    1. On your TV box, select Open in TV 2 Play on the content you want to watch

    2. Press Confirm to proceed

    3. Scan QR code with your mobile phone, or go to the link that appears on the screen

    4. Select Continue with Telenor T-We

    5. Log in with [Telenor ID](/telenor-id/)

    6. Press Run on

    7. Fill in mobile number and other information and select Save the information

    8. Enter your password for your existing TV 2 Play account

    9. Select Run on to link the accounts

    10. Log in to the TV 2 website again

    11. Enter the one-time code that appears on your TV screen, or confirm that the code is the same (if you have used the QR code) and select Go further

    12. Select desired profile

    13. Select Play... to start streaming!

If you are logged in to TV 2 Play with another account before, you must log out and back in after you have linked the account. You log out by selecting "Settings" at the bottom of the menu, and then "Log out".

  * #### I have upgraded to a larger package from TV 2 Play before

If you have upgraded to a package larger than Basic, this must be terminated before you can merge the accounts. The upgrade is often prepaid, but you retain access until the expiration date. Once this date has passed, you can link your existing account with the T-We account.

 **Here's how to do it:**

    1. Open [TV 2 Play in your browser](https://play.tv2.no/)

    2. Press Log in, enter your email address and press Go further

    3. Enter your existing password

    4. Press your profile in the upper right corner

    5. Select Account

    6. Select My purchases

    7. Press Stop on the package you subscribe to

    8. Log out

Once the package you have paid for has expired, you can merge your account with the T-We account.

 **Link existing TV 2 Play account with T-We account:**

    1. Press Log in again, and select Continue with Telenor T-We

    2. Log in with [Telenor ID](/telenor-id/)

    3. Press Run on

    4. Fill in mobile number and other information and select Save the information

    5. Enter your password for your existing TV 2 Play account

    6. Select Run on to link the accounts

    7. Select Log in to log in to the account

    8. Select Play... to watch the desired content  

If you want to upgrade the package again, you can do so after the accounts have been merged. You do this by logging in to [TV 2 Play in your browser](https://play.tv2.no/).

  * #### I have had TV 2 Play from another provider

To link your TV 2 accounts, you must first terminate the subscription you have with your current provider.

Once that is done, you can follow the guide at the top here, "Link existing TV 2 Play account to T-We".
