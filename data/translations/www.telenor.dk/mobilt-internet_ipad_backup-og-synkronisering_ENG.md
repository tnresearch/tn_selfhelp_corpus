# Back Up and Sync Your iPad

Backups and automatic synchronizations are your lifeline if something goes wrong, and you lose your iPad or important data and images.

## iCloud or iTunes?

There is no difference in the security and service offered by iCloud and iTunes. The difference lies solely in whether you prefer manual or automatic backup and synchronization.

**Benefits of iCloud**  
iCloud automatically synchronizes all your iOS devices and backs up when you are on a wireless network and the mobile is connected to a power outlet. You choose which content to automatically synchronize. Apple gives you 5 GB of storage for free and you have the option to purchase extra space if you need it.

See the guide to backing up with iCloud further down this page.

**Benefits of iTunes**  
Use iTunes if you often connect your iPad to your PC/Mac or do not have an iCloud account.

  * ## Press "Settings"

  * ## Select "iCloud" and log in with your Apple ID

  * ## Choose what you want to back up and press "Backup"

Before pressing "**Backup**", you must choose which items from your iPad you want to back up.

  * ## Press "Back Up Now"

Your iPad will now be backed up.
