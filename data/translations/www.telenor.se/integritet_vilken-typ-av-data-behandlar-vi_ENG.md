# What type of data do we process?

Customer Data

Customer data is information that you provide related to the service(s) you use with Telenor. This data can therefore be linked to you as an individual.

Examples of customer data:

  * Name
  * Personal identity number
  * Login details
  * Contact information such as address, phone number and email address
  * Your agreements and services with us and how you use them
  * Information about delivery and service of hardware
  * Information about your financial status through credit checks.

Traffic Data

Traffic data is related to what happens when you use any of our communication services, i.e. when you make calls, send SMS or browse the internet. This type of data is generated automatically.

Examples of traffic data:

  * When you call, email or visit a website
  * Information about who has communicated with whom
  * Time of communication
  * How long a communication lasted
  * Which network was used for such communication
  * Information about where you are when you call or are connected (for example, which cell tower is used).

Cookies

Cookies are a text file that is stored in your device or disappears when you leave the page. Our website uses different types of cookies. Some are absolutely necessary for the website to function and cannot be turned off, while others you can choose whether to accept.

Cookies are primarily used to improve the customer experience, analyze how visitors navigate the website, or for marketing. Our website contains third-party cookies from other actors that Telenor collaborates with. Information from these is collected and shared with Telenor, but can also be used for other purposes.

[Read more about Cookies >](/support/integritet/information-om-cookies/)
