# Connect to wifi via Windows 10

  * ### Click the arrow in the bottom right corner of your desktop

Then select the wifi symbol to see all networks in your vicinity.

  * ### Select your network

You can find the network name, SSID, on the underside of your router. If you are using
our network, it will start with TN. Choose whether you want to use 5 GHz or 2.4 GHz.

Tip: 5 GHz = better speed and 2.4 GHz = better range.

  * ### Click connect

Enter the password, Wireless Key, which you can find on the back of the router. Click
Next.

  * ### Done!

After a few seconds, you will be connected to your wifi network and
can start surfing.
