## Frequently asked questions and answers

  * How to turn on surfing during your trip

To be able to surf during your trip, you must activate data roaming.

[Instructions on how to do it in your
mobile phone](/link/288523ae6c5a4fe083145390f68c3609.aspx)  
  

**Do you have a mobile broadband?**

    1. Make sure your subscription is open for roaming. If you have a modem: see the menu in the client. If you have a wifi puck, see the menu in the router.
    2. Make sure the router/modem/tablet is open for roaming.
    3. In some clients/routers, there may be a need to manually connect when roaming and confirm that you accept it.

  * How much of my surfing allowance can I use abroad?

You can use your entire surfing allowance within the EU/EEA as well as in Thailand, Malaysia,
Myanmar (Burma), USA, Switzerland, United Kingdom (incl. Gibraltar), Turkey,
Andorra, Serbia and Montenegro. If you have unlimited surfing, you can use 100
GB.

  * Can I surf as much as I want abroad if I have a subscription with unlimited surfing?

Almost!

With our subscriptions, you have a total of 100 GB/month to surf in the EU/EEA,
Switzerland, United Kingdom (incl. Gibraltar), Turkey, Serbia, Montenegro,
Andorra, Thailand, USA, Malaysia and Myanmar (Burma).

If you have Telenor Family benefits, you share your 100 GB among family members in
[My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * Does my prepaid card work outside of Sweden?

Our prepaid cards work in most countries. If you travel within the EU/EEA, your
prepaid card works just like at home. For your prepaid card to work abroad, you
must first have called with it in Sweden.

[Read more about what applies in the country you are visiting
here](/link/a728a2eee9b24f87867517e059303cae.aspx).

  * Why should I pay for calls made in an EU/EEA country?

In some countries within the EU/EEA, for example in Spain, they charge for calls
to, among other things, authorities and car rental companies (premium calls). So if
you have been in a country where you should be able to call without extra cost but
still received an invoice for calls made abroad, that may be the reason.
