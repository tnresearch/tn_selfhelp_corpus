## Features

#### Dimensions and Weight

  * Height 181 mm
  * Width 96.6 mm (bottom), 90mm (top)
  * Depth 96.6 mm (bottom), 90mm (top)
  * Weight 510 g

#### Information

Manufacturer: Huawei  
Model: H155-382  
SIM card: Nano-SIM  
Manufacturer's warranty: 24 months

####  
Connections and Connectivity

  * Interface type: WLAN & Ethernet
  * Ethernet (LAN) connections: 1 pc GE
  * Network technology: 5G, 4G
  * Supported 4G/5G frequencies: 700/800/900/1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz
  * DL 4G/5G 4x4 MIMO: 1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz, 2x2 MIMO: 700/800/900 MHz
  * UL 5G 2x2 MIMO (5G SA): 1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz
  * 5CC DL carrier aggregation support in 4G network
  * 2CC DL CA in 5G network (NSA/SA)
  * 2CC UL CA support (1CC 5G + 1CC 4G NSA, 2CC 5G SA) 
  * 256 QAM modulation support
  * WLAN: 802.11 b/g/n/a/ac/ax
  * WLAN frequency bands: 2.4 GHz (2x2 MIMO) and 5 GHz (2x2 MIMO)
  * 5 GHz Wi-Fi Preferred support
  * WLAN theoretical maximum speed: 2974 Mbit/s

#### Network Features

  
**(Figures are theoretical maximum speeds, actual speeds depend on
the strength of the received signal, the capabilities of the mobile
network, and the type of subscription used)**

• 5G maximum speed from the network 3.6 Gbit/s (NSA/SA 5G)  
• 5G maximum speed towards the network 125 Mbit/s (NSA 5G), 500 Mbit/s (SA 5G)  
• 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 2.0 Gbit/s  
• 4G LTE maximum speed towards the network 150 Mbit/s
