# Call Forwarding

When your mobile is turned off, out of coverage, etc., calls are
automatically forwarded to your Voicemail. But you also have the option to forward these
calls to an alternative number.

This forwarding (conditional forwarding) costs the standard call charges for
your subscription - and you can activate and deactivate a forwarding yourself.
