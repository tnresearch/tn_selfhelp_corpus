# Equipment Return

If you terminate your broadband or TV subscription, you need to send back
borrowed equipment such as a router and TV box. If the equipment is not
returned to us, we will charge you a fee. Here we explain how
you should send back the equipment, and why.

  1. The first thing that happens after you have cancelled your subscription is that you will receive a **termination letter** from us. The letter states what applies to the return of your equipment. If you have cancelled both broadband and TV, you can send all the equipment in the same package.  
  

  2. We recommend that you check if you can find the equipment you have at home in the **list of equipment to be returned** below. It may be that you do not need to send it back, even if it says so in the letter.  
  

  3. Then you send the equipment to us. See the **checklist** below!   
  

  4. We will take care of the equipment when it arrives at us. We will send an SMS to you confirming that we have received it. 

Checklist for your package

  * Pack the equipment properly so that it is not damaged. If it works well, it should be useful to another of our customers later. Do not forget to include associated cables, power adapters, remote controls and any TV cards.   
  

  * If you use the original packaging to return the equipment, make sure that the old address label is completely covered.  
  

  * To create a return label and send back your equipment free of charge, [click here.](https://myreturns.postnord.com/telenor-fixed?lang=sv)  
  

  * If you do not want to create a return label via the link above, you should send your package to **Telenor Sverige AB, c/o PostNord TPL AB, 341 06 Ljungby**
  
**

  * The equipment must be sent back as a business package. Your [Postnord agent](https://www.postnord.se/vara-verktyg/hitta-servicestalle) will help you with that.  
  

  * Request a consignment ID so that you can track the package if necessary.

### Is your equipment not on the list?

If your router or TV box is not on the list above **and** the
termination letter states that we will charge 0 SEK if you do not
return it, then you do not need to do so.  
  
Leave the equipment at a recycling center instead, so that it – or
at least parts of it – can be reused. Do you not have a recycling center nearby? Or are you the least unsure about whether you should return it? Then send it to us, and we will ensure that it is either (depending on its condition) sent to another customer or recycled.  
  
**Tip!** The equipment should be recycled as electronic waste. Check with your municipality where
you can hand it in. If you live in an apartment, your landlord or
housing association may have containers for recycling electronic waste.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * How do I know if you have received my return?

When we have received the return, we **will send an SMS with a confirmation**
to you. Have you not received an SMS? Call us and provide the tracking number and we will help you.  
  
Good to know: if you have sent in several returns, you will receive several
SMS messages.

  * I have several TV boxes/routers at home, which one should I send back?

If you have several TV boxes belonging to the subscription, all should be sent back.
[Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) if you are unsure about
other equipment.

  * Why does it say different things in the termination letter and here about sending back equipment?

Both what is stated in the letter and here is correct. You are welcome to send back even
the equipment that we have not listed here, but you do not need to. We are working
to make this clearer in the letters.

  * I have received a reminder despite having sent it in, why?

We have probably had time to send out the reminder before we received the package from
you. If you requested a consignment ID when you sent in the package, you can go in and
search on [PostNord's website](https://www.postnord.se/online-
verktyg/verktyg/sok/spara-brev-paket-och-pall). You can then see if the package has arrived or not. If it has arrived, you can ignore the reminder.

  * I have received a reminder despite you saying that I did not need to return it.

If the termination letter states that we will charge 0 SEK if
you do not return the equipment, you do not need to do so.

  * What happens if I do not send in the equipment?

First you will receive a reminder letter. If you, despite the reminder, do not return the TV
box/router so that we have it within two months after the subscription
has ended, we will send an invoice for the fee stated in the termination letter.

  * I have purchased a recording box, do I have to return it?

You must send back the TV box itself, but you take out and keep the hard drive (that is, the part that
records and that you have purchased). The hard drive cannot be used in other TV boxes and it is not possible to play anything afterwards without the TV box. The information you can retrieve from the hard drive is a
list of recordings.
