# Install wireless TV if you have Arris 2853 with two WiFi extenders

For wireless TV to work for you, you need to use two WiFi extenders:
one that transmits the TV signals, and one that receives them.  

**Before you start:** begin by finding out which port on your
service distributor you should use for TV. [Find the right
port](/support/portar/)  

Then follow the instructions below.

  * ### Connect the first WiFi extender to the TV port in the service distributor

Use a standard network cable.

  * ### Place the second WiFi extender where you have your TV box

Then connect the second WiFi extender to the TV box. Use a
network cable.

  * ### Start both WiFi extenders 

Wait until the WiFi extender's light shines with a white glow.

  * ### Press the synchronization buttons on the back of both WiFi extenders 

The button is labeled **WPS**.  

When the devices synchronize, the WiFi extenders' lights will blink with a blue
glow. When the lights turn to a solid green glow, everything is
ready.
