# Speed Issues

Don't you think your browsing is fast enough? There are a few things you can do.

  * ### Test your speed

You can start by testing your browsing speed on, for example,
[bredbandskollen.se](http://www.bredbandskollen.se). Make sure the network
cable is connected directly to the computer first if there is an outlet. This
will give you the fairest result.  
  
 **Tip!** Before testing the speed: turn off all activities that require
a lot of bandwidth, such as TV streaming and file downloads.

  * ### Move the router and wifi extender

Place your router on a table or shelf instead of in a hidden location.
This will improve the reception. Also, make sure you are not more than
about 20 meters away from the router. The closer you sit, the better the
reception.  
  
If you are using a wifi extender: make sure it is placed halfway to where
you have poor coverage.

  * ### Watch out for concrete walls and kitchen equipment

Do not place the router near concrete walls or large metal objects, as they
can degrade the signals. Also, do not place your router near a microwave,
refrigerator and freezer, aquarium, TV, cordless phones or devices that use
Bluetooth.

  * ### Replace old equipment

Really good wifi requires both the router, mobile, tablet and computer
to support the latest standard. It has been available on most mobiles,
tablets and computers since 2013. If you have a computer that is older
than that, it may limit your browsing speed.

  * ### Restart the router in the evening

There may be a lot of wifi traffic in the air during the evening and on
weekends. This means that you are indirectly competing with your neighbors'
wifi. When you restart your router, it performs an automatic channel search
and will choose the channel that is least congested at the moment. This
will hopefully improve your speed.
