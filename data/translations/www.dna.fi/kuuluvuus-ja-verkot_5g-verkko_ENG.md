# 5G – Even Faster and More Responsive Network

A lot is being said about 5G, but what does it really mean? There are many
things coming that will improve internet usage and make everyday life easier;
we don't even know how to imagine everything yet. The biggest changes will
only be visible in a few years, as 5G technology is constantly developing
and also improving 4G network speeds.

DNA is building 5G services for both faster and smoother
mobile data usage and as a fast network connection for homes and offices.
Ultimately, 5G is not just about the internet, but about what kind of
future it enables.

## When will 5G arrive?

5G is already here in part, but it won't happen all at once.
4G was launched as early as 2011, and it continues to get faster all the
time as more capacity is put into use on the network. 5G will not replace
4G, but will complement it gradually.

## What does 5G enable?

With 5G, you can watch high-quality content from streaming services like
Netflix and Areena without buffering, online gaming is lag-free, and remote
work runs smoothly. In the future, network slicing will allow 5G network
services to be customized to the needs of both homes and businesses.
With network slicing, we enable the user to have their own interference-
and congestion-free band – whether it's a home internet connection or a
company's IoT application.

## What happens to 4G?

4G is not going anywhere, as 5G technology is being built in the first
phase on top of existing 4G as a so-called capacity layer. DNA will also
continue to build new 4G base stations and develop 4G functionalities,
which will further improve the usability of 4G services.
