## About WiFi Router

The WiFi Router is a wireless router with award-winning design. It works just as well
with all our internet accesses, whether you have broadband over fiber, cable
or Wireless Broadband.

With standard encryption WPA2-PSK and Smart WiFi, the router ensures that you get a
stable and secure internet connection. Smart WiFi is a function that
automatically selects the best network for you, whether it is on 2.4GHz or
5GHz.

If you have a large home or difficult coverage conditions, you can expand with one or more [WiFi Extenders](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-
forsterker/) to a mesh network, which provides good coverage throughout the home.

  * #### What do the lights on the WiFi Router mean?

 **Bottom Light:**

    * Solid white color = Connected to power

    * Blinking white = Starting up

    * Blinking yellow = Software update in progress

    * Blinking blue = WPS in progress

    * Solid green = Connected, but not updated with the latest software

* * *

 **Top Light:**

    * Solid green = Connected

    * Blinking green = Connecting

    * Blinking yellow = Connected, but missing IP address

    * Solid yellow = Bridge/Bro mode

    * Blinking red = Connected, but missing contact with Telenor

    * Dark = Not connected

* * *

  * #### Forgotten WiFi password?

Send SMS with keyword **WIFI XXXXXXXX to 2000** to receive
network name and password for the router.

The 8 digits are the last 8 digits of the serial number (S/N). You will find
the serial number and instructions on how to send SMS on the sticker on the
underside of the router.

  * #### Router details

 **Dimensions:**  
Height: 175 mm. Width: 207 mm. Depth: 77 mm.

 **WiFi standard:**  
802.11b/g/n on 2.4Ghz and 802.11a/n/ac on 5Ghz  
Band steering: the device that is connected will automatically switch between 2.4 and 5GHz
based on signal strength  
No WiFi 6 support (802.11ax **)**

 **Standard encryption:  
** WPA2 PSK

## Change settings on your router?

Do you want to change the settings on your router, such as changing the network name
or password? You can do this by logging in to My WiFi, at
<https://wifi.telenor.no/>

To log in, you must be connected to your home network, and you log in
with the admin password that you find on the sticker under your WiFi Router.

Do you need more help or guidance to change settings on your router?
We have created a separate page with more info, you can find it by clicking the button
below.

[Go to My WiFi](/kundeservice/internett/wifi/administrere-ruter/)
