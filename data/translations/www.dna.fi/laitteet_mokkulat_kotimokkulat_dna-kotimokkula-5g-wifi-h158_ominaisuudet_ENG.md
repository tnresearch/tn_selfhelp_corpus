## Features

#### Dimensions and Weight

  * Height 225.5 mm
  * Width 103 mm
  * Depth 103 mm
  * Weight 850 g

#### Information

**Manufacturer:** Huawei

**Model:** H158-381

**SIM card:** Nano-SIM

**Manufacturer's warranty** : 24 months

#### Connections and Connectivity

**Interface type:** WLAN & Ethernet

**Ethernet (LAN) connections** : 2 pcs (1 pc 2.5GE, 1 pc GE)

**Network technology:** 5G, 4G

**Supported 4G/5G frequencies:** 700/800/900/1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz

**DL 4G/5G 4x4 MIMO:** 1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz, 2x2 MIMO: 700/800/900 MHz

**UL 5G 2x2 MIMO (5G SA):** 1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz

**WLAN:** 802.11 b/g/n/a/ac/ax

**WLAN frequency bands:** 2.4 GHz (4x4 MIMO) and 5 GHz (4x4 MIMO)

**WLAN theoretical maximum speed:** 7140 Mbit/s

  * 5CC DL carrier aggregation support in 4G network
  * 2CC/3CC DL CA in 5G network (2CC 5G NSA, 3CC 5G SA)
  * 2CC UL CA support
  * 256 QAM modulation support
  * 5 GHz Wi-Fi Preferred support

#### Network Features

(Figures are theoretical maximum speeds, actual speeds depend on
the strength of the received signal, the capabilities of the mobile network,
and the type of subscription used)

  * 5G maximum speed from the network 3.6 Gbit/s (NSA 5G), 5.4 Gbit/s (SA 5G),
  * 5G maximum speed to the network 125 Mbit/s (NSA 5G), 500 Mbit/s (SA 5G)
  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 2.0 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed to the network 150 Mbit/s

#### Sales package contents

  * DNA Home Modem 5G WiFi H158
  * Power adapter and power cord
  * Ethernet cable
  * Quick guide and user manual
  * Safety instructions
  * Warranty card
