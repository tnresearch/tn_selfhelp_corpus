## Features

Product name: Inteno EG300AC for fiber connections

  * Wireless connection: WiFi 802.11ac/n/g 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz (3x3)
  * 4 x 1 GB LAN ports (e.g., for a set-top box or computer)
  * 1 x 1 GB WAN port allows use in Ethernet locations as well
  * 1 x Tel (RJ11) port
  * IPv4 and IPv6 compatible
  * 2 x USB ports, WPS, Parental Control, Firewall and NAT
  * DNA's customized settings

Sales package contents: Inteno EG300AC modem, power adapter, Ethernet
(RJ45) cable,  
SFP module, 3 x fiber optic cable, DNA quick guide.
