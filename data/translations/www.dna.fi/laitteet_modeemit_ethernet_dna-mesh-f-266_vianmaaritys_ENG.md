## Troubleshooting

No lights are on on the modem (power cord is connected)

**Possible cause:**

The modem is not receiving power. The transformer or modem may be faulty.

**Proposed solution:**

Connect the power cord as instructed in the manual and check that the outlet
has power. Bring the transformer and modem to a DNA Store for replacement.

All lights indicating a network connection are on, but you cannot connect to the internet

**Possible cause:**

The modem may be frozen.

**Proposed solution:**

Disconnect the modem's power cord for about a minute and wait for the network
connection status lights to come back on. Restart your computer. Also try
with an Ethernet cable. You can also reset the device.

All lights indicating a network connection are on (Computer reports "limited
connection")

**Possible cause:**

The modem is not connecting to the internet.

**Proposed solution:**

Check the connection and connections. Also check the settings of your
computer/terminal (e.g., internet settings and network card). If necessary,
reset the modem, disconnect the modem's power cord for about a minute and
wait for the lights to come back on.

All lights indicating a network connection are on (settings checked)

**Possible cause:**

The firewall may be blocking traffic.

**Proposed solution:**

Check your computer's firewall settings.

All lights indicating a network connection are on (settings checked, using
your own connection sharing device, e.g., router)

**Possible cause:**

Your connection sharing device is frozen or faulty.

**Proposed solution:**

Connect the modem to your computer directly with an Ethernet cable or use
the modem's wireless connection. You can also try setting the modem to
bridge mode. Contact the seller of routers or other additional devices.

Wireless network does not work

**Possible cause:**

If the connection works with an Ethernet cable, the problem is with the WiFi
network.

**Proposed solution:**

Restart the modem, check the settings and try the WiFi tips at: [dna.fi/tuki-wifi](http://dna.fi/tuki-wifi). If these do not
work, contact customer service.

I cannot log in to the modem at 192.168.101.1 or dna.wifi/

**Possible cause:**

The F-266 modem is in Standalone mode, meaning the F-266 is connected to an
existing modem or dongle that is not of the Sagemcom brand.
Alternatively, the F-266 is connected to a Sagemcom F-3890, F-3896, or
F-5670 modem, in which case the WiFi network of the main modem acting as the
modem is in use.

**Solution proposal:**

Log in to the first modem or dongle in use and find out what IP address the
F-266 modem has been assigned. The address will start with 192.168.
Then log in to the identified IP address.

Older devices do not connect via WiFi

  * Log in to the device's settings page and press the gear icon on the right
    side of the WiFi menu.
  * Change the Security drop-down menu from WPA2/WPA3 Personal to **WPA2/WPA Personal**.
  * Save the change by selecting **Apply** at the bottom of the page.

A whining sound comes from the modem

**Possible cause:**

A faulty power supply of the modem causes a whining sound in the device.
Despite the sound, there is no danger or problem with the functionality of
the device.

**Proposed solution:**

Contact DNA's consumer customer service and report the problem, so that DNA
will deliver a new F-266 modem power supply free of charge.
Alternatively, you can pick up a new power supply from a DNA Store after
reporting the problem there.
