# Renew Subscription

Here you will find information on how to continue with your subscription
with Telenor.

You do not need to do anything to renew your subscription. If you want a
subscription with more or less data than before, you should [change
data amount](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/surf/byta-surfmangd/) instead.

You can see which subscription you have by logging in to My Telenor – either
here [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in [our app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-
old/).

## Frequently Asked Questions about Renewing a Subscription

  * How do I change my mobile subscription?

[app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx). You can of course also
[contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx).  
  
[More about changing subscription](/link/ed95470d8b3e4432805c88d0864acb7d.aspx)
