# Your Final Bills

When you cancel your subscription, it takes 30 days for the subscription to expire. It
is important that you continue to pay your bills – this avoids
interest and fees. If you have credit on your final bill, the amount will be
automatically paid to your NemKonto (Danish bank account) 6 days after your final bill is
calculated. And if your final bill is less than 50 DKK, we will not charge it.  
  

**How We Calculate Your Final Bill**

On your final bill, you only pay for any extra usage before your
cancellation date. If you have overpaid for your subscription, it will be credited on
your final bill. And if you have credit, you can also see it here – the amount
will be automatically paid to your NemKonto 6 days after your final
bill is calculated.

  
Once your subscription has expired, your final bill will be calculated on the 15th of
the month following – and your bill will be available on My Telenor around the 20th. It can
e.g. be that:

  * If your subscription expires on May 5th – your final bill will be calculated on May 15th
  * If your subscription expires on May 17th – your final bill will be calculated on June 15th

**Good to Know About Your Bill**  

  * The payment period is from the 16th to the 15th of the month
  * You pay in advance for your subscription and selected services – i.e. before you have used it
  * You pay in arrears for any extra usage – i.e. after you have used it
  * Smart Agreement: The first installment typically falls in the month after the agreement is entered into, and therefore the last installment also falls in the month after the Smart Agreement stops.

**Your Previous Bills – After Cancelling Your Subscription  
** After your cancellation, you still have access to the My Telenor app and [My
Telenor](/mit-telenor/), where you can view your previous bills.
