# Reset hardware

If you choose to reset the hardware, your personal settings on the
phone will be deleted. If you want to keep the settings, we recommend that you
[back up your iPhone
](~/link/2c82cdbc031c4318ab8c0a8362cb0c10.aspx)before resetting it.

This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest operating system (iOS)** from
Apple. So feel free to start by making sure it is installed on your phone –
not least so that it works as well and securely as possible.  
  
[Here's how to update
the operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-
mobil/iphone/uppdatera-operativsystemet/)  
  
Now you have the latest iOS installed, right? Then let's go!

  * ### Go to the home screen

  * ### Select "Settings"

  * ### Scroll down, select "General"

  * ### Scroll down, select "Transfer or Reset iPhone"

  * ### Select "Erase All Content and Settings" 

  * ### Select "Continue" and then "Erase All Content and Settings"

  * ### Data and settings are being deleted

All data and settings are now being deleted, and your iPhone will restart
automatically.
