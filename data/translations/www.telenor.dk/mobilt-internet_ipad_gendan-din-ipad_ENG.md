# Restore Your iPad

You may need to restore your iPad if it is locked and you have forgotten
the passcode – or if, for example, you have purchased a new one and want to
set it up exactly like your old one.

If your iPad suddenly starts behaving strangely, restoring it can also help.

## Before You Restore Your iPad

When you restore your iPad, be aware that it will delete all your contacts
and all your content. Therefore, take a backup before you start restoring
your iPad.

[See our guide to synchronization and backup](/kundeservice/mobilt-
internet/ipad/backup-og-synkronisering/ "Backup and synchronize your iPad")

Once you have taken a backup, follow the guide here step by step.

  * ## Tap "Settings"

  * ## Tap "General" and select "Reset"

  * ## Select "Reset All Settings"

This will reset all your settings.
