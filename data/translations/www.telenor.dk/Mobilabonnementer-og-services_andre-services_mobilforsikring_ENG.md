#  Mobile Insurance

## Mobile Insurance covers all small and large accidents – and you receive
a replacement phone already the day after.

To give you the best possible help, we have entered into a partnership with
SquareTrade, who is one of the world's leading companies in
Mobile Insurance. You can get Mobile Insurance for all smartphones you buy on
telenor.dk or in your Local Telenor store.

If you didn't add Mobile Insurance to the cart when you bought a new smartphone on
telenor.dk, you can buy Mobile Insurance in your local Telenor store or in
[My Telenor](/mit-telenor/ "My Telenor"). Only phones purchased with
a subscription after 27.04.2020 can purchase Mobile Insurance via My Telenor.

## How Mobile Insurance Covers

In addition to everyday accidents, Mobile Insurance also covers mechanical and electronic
failures that occur during normal use, once the warranty period has expired.
Intentional damage, loss and theft are not covered. The insurance covers 2
damages within a period of 12 months.

  * Broken screen
  * Liquid damage
  * Speaker and audio failure
  * Charging failure
  * Touchscreen failure
  * WiFi failure

## Mobile Insurance covers your smartphone worldwide

We deliver already the day after within Europe, so you get your smartphone
directly to the sunbed in Italy or your hotel room in London - unless you
are on a non-bridge island or in a lonely alpine cabin, as deliveries typically
take one extra day. If your phone breaks down elsewhere in the world, it is
of course still covered, but delivery is limited to Europe.

## Report your damage easily and quickly to SquareTrade. Fill out the form
[here](https://www.squaretrade.dk/da/anmeld-en-skade)

When you take out Mobile Insurance with Telenor, it is SquareTrade who handles
your damage claim from start to finish. Therefore, you must contact SquareTrade,
when you have damage to your smartphone and want to report it.

  * If you report the damage before 7:00 PM Danish time, you will receive a replacement phone the next day. If you report the damage on Friday after 7:00 PM or on the weekend, the replacement phone will be delivered the following Tuesday.
  * Once the damage is approved and you have paid your deductible to SquareTrade, they will continuously send you emails with practical details and updates on the further process and delivery of the replacement phone.
  * Your damage claim will go faster when you have a purchase receipt, serial or IMEI number ready when you call. 
  * You will receive a replacement phone and send the damaged smartphone back. 

When the driver comes and delivers your new mobile or tablet, you just hand over the broken one to him. He will then take care of the rest.

## Opening Hours at SquareTrade

Report damage online, press [here](https://www.squaretrade.dk/da/anmeld-en-skade)  
SquareTrade customer service, press [here](https://www.squaretrade.dk/da/kontakt-os)

## Remember to return the damaged smartphone

No later than 7 days after you receive your replacement phone, you must return the
damaged smartphone. If you do not return the smartphone, you will be charged up to DKK 3,000 on the card you used to pay
the deductible. The price may vary from smartphone to smartphone.

## A replacement phone is factory refurbished and as new

A replacement phone is a smartphone identical to the one that has been damaged. It
appears completely new, but will typically contain recycled components. Should it happen that we do not have your model in stock, you will be offered another model with comparable features. All replacement phones
are of course delivered with a 24-month warranty

## Prices for Mobile Insurance and deductible

Mobile insurance costs from 10/4/2024 from DKK 49/month depending on the price of the mobile.
Your deductible depends on the cash price of your smartphone without any
discounts.
