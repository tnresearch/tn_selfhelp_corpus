# Restart your router

If the light continues to shine or blink after restarting, it is due to a
service disruption.

In [My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/) you can see if your subscription is affected by a
service disruption.  
  
You can also find information on our [page for
service disruptions.](/support/driftinformation/driftstorningar-pa-tv--och-
bredbandsnatet/)
