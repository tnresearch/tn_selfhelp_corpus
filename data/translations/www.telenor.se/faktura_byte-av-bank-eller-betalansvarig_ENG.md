# Change of bank or payer

Have you changed banks and want to continue with direct debit or e-invoice? Or do
you want to change who is responsible for payment on a mobile subscription?

## If you have changed banks

  1. Log in to your old bank and cancel your authorization.
  2. Then log in to your new bank and register [direct debit](/support/faktura/autogiro/) or [e-invoice](/support/faktura/e-faktura/).

During the transition period, you may receive a paper invoice before your
application for direct debit or e-invoice has been approved.

## Change payer

## Common questions about changing bank or payer

  * How do I see if my application for direct debit/e-invoice has been approved?

In the My Telenor app, you can see how you pay your upcoming invoices. Click
on invoices, then on unpaid invoices, and at the bottom you will see your payment method.  
  
Please note that if you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV from us, you have
two customer numbers and will receive two invoices. This means you must submit an
application for each customer number.

### Direct Debit

When it comes to seeing if a direct debit application has been approved, it varies
from bank to bank. Most banks send a confirmation by mail when your direct debit
application has been approved. Contact your bank to find out their procedures and how long it takes.

### E‑invoice

For e‑invoice, you can see directly in your online banking that everything is okay. Please note
that you cannot see the actual e‑invoice until we have sent it to your bank.
We do this once a month.

### If you still receive a paper invoice...

...it doesn't necessarily mean something has gone wrong. Depending on when you
set up direct debit or e‑invoice, a paper invoice may be sent before your
authorization for direct debit or e‑invoice is processed by the bank.
