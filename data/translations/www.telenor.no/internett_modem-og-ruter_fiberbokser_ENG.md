# Fiber Boxes

Here you will find information about the different fiber boxes we deliver.

## What is a fiber box?

The fiber box is an important part of ensuring a stable and fast internet connection in
your home. Fiber boxes are often also called ONT (Optical Network Terminal).

Its function is to connect the fiber line to your WiFi equipment, so that
you can connect to the internet. When you have fiber from Telenor, this will be connected
to your home via a fiber cable. This is first connected to an outdoor
fiber box, and then a new indoor fiber cable is run to the fiber box
inside the house.

The fiber box is the property of Telenor. Fiber boxes should always be left alone and not
touched, other than to restart, unless you have been otherwise instructed by us in
connection with troubleshooting.
