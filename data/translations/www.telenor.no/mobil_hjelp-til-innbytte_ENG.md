# Mobile Trade-in

Here you will find help and guidance when you want to trade in your old mobile phone.

## Give your old mobile a new life!

Do you have a mobile phone you no longer use, or do you want a newer version?
Now you can with [trade-in from Telenor](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/) get money for
your old mobile - and with that make your next mobile purchase a little cheaper.

## How do I trade in my mobile phone?

You can trade in your mobile phone both via telenor.no and in the Telenor store.

If you want to trade in immediately, you can visit a Telenor store and get
a valuation and trade-in there and then. [Find your nearest
Telenor store](https://www.telenor.no/telenorbutikken/)

If you want to send it in by post, click the button below.

Then you fill in basic information such as IMEI and the condition of the mobile
phone and get an estimated value. Once you have completed the ordering process, and
clicked on "Order trade-in", we will send you a return envelope that you use
to send the mobile phone to us.

[See estimated value and order trade-in](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/bestill/)

## Checklist before submission:

  1. Turn off tracking features such as Find my iPhone, Samsung recovery and Find my device

  2. Remove SIM card and any memory card

  3. Reset the mobile phone - Read more about how to do it here: [How to reset your mobile phone](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/mobil/din-mobil/nullstille-mobilen/)

## Packing and submission

  1. Once you have gone through the checklist, place the mobile phone in the return envelope. Charger and other accessories should not be sent in.

  2. Sign the attached contract and place it in the box

  3. Attach the return label and deliver the package to the nearest post office/store. Postage is already paid.

## Price offer and payout

Once we have received the submitted mobile phone, an examination of the mobile phone will be carried out after a quarantine period of 14
days and you will receive a price offer. If you
approve this, the amount will be paid to the account number you have provided.

If it turns out that there are any challenges with the condition of the mobile phone
or the final price offer does not match the estimate you originally received,
we will contact you with more information.

## Frequently asked questions about trade-in

  * #### The equipment I want to use for trade-in is in poor condition - Can I still trade it in with it?

Even if the mobile phone is in poor condition or has a cracked screen, it may have a value.
Your mobile phone can get a new life! We then ensure that it is reset and
prepared for a new owner, completely safely.

It is easy and non-binding to [check the value of the old
mobile phone](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/bestill/). You can either get help in
the Telenor store, or check yourself online.

  * #### Does trade-in also apply to iPads and tablets?

Yes, it does!

  * #### Why is there a 14-day quarantine period?

A 14-day quarantine period is required by law, in accordance with
Brukthandelforskriften §11.

  * #### I bought a mobile phone with Splitt, can I trade it in?

Yes, you can! With trade-in, the monthly price of your new mobile phone will be lower.

It is easy and non-binding to [check the value of the old
mobile phone](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/bestill/). You can either get help in
the Telenor store, or check yourself online.

  * #### I bought a mobile phone with SWAP, can I trade it in?

We are discontinuing SWAP in connection with the launch of Splitt. [You can read more
about what this means for you with an active SWAP
agreement](https://staging.telenor.no/swap/swap-avvikles/)

  * #### What happens if I do not accept the price proposal for Trade-in?

If the amount we can offer is not the same as the value you estimated when you
filled out the form, we will contact you and give you information about this. You
can then choose to say no to the offer, and we will return the mobile phone to you without
cost.

  * #### What happens to the mobile phones after submission?

When we receive your mobile phone, it is thoroughly checked and all content is safely
deleted. If it is in good condition, it can then be prepared and sold on to a new
owner. If it is in poor condition, parts that still work will be reused and
remaining parts will be recycled.
