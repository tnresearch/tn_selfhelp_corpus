# Change Mobile Subscription

Here you will find information on how to make changes to your subscription.

## Subscription Change

You must be the owner of the number to change or switch subscriptions.

Click on the link below to log in to My Pages. Select the number you want to change the subscription for and click **Switch Subscription**.

If you want to change to a prepaid plan, you must contact us at 915 09000.

[Switch Subscription on My Pages](/mitt-telenor/mobil/)

## Other Questions

  * #### How do I change the address on my mobile number?

Contact us at customer service, and we will help you.

  * #### What happens to Data Rollover if I switch subscriptions or ownership?

    * When changing subscriptions: The data amount you have saved up will carry over, but at the end of the month, it is the data amount included in the subscription you have switched to that will roll over.

    * When transferring ownership: Data Rollover will be reset immediately when the transfer of ownership is completed.

  * #### What happens to My Cloud if I cancel or change my mobile subscription?

All our mobile subscriptions include unlimited storage, so you can freely change between them without losing storage space.

 **Upon cancellation** of the subscription, you will lose the unlimited storage amount you have.

You will still have 2 GB of free storage in My Cloud, but if you exceed this, you will receive a notification in the app that the storage space is used up. You will also receive an email from Telenor Digital containing more information on how to purchase more storage space.

**Note** that files are stored in the cloud for 30 days before being deleted, but files stored locally on your devices will remain with you.

  * #### How do I switch from another operator to Telenor?

It is easy to move a mobile subscription from another mobile operator to Telenor.

[Order a new mobile subscription](/mobilabonnement/) or call customer service at number 915 09000. You will of course keep your mobile number if you wish.

If you order a new number, it may take up to 3 days before you can use it. If you transfer your number to Telenor, it may take up to 8 business days before it is transferred.

 **Note**, if you are going to transfer your number to Telenor, you must not cancel the number with your current operator. This will happen automatically in the transfer process.

It is also important that the subscription is ordered in the same name with Telenor as it is registered with your current operator.

  * #### What happens to my invoice when I change subscriptions?

If you change from one subscription to another, you will find that the invoice will be slightly different than usual.

Since you have paid the monthly fee for the subscription in advance, you will receive a refund of the monthly fee for your old subscription from the date the subscription was changed. This will be shown with a minus sign in front of it on the invoice. From the same date, the monthly fee for your new subscription will be added. It therefore does not matter when in the month you switch subscriptions.
