# What is Trygg 48?

Have you seen "Trygg 48" on your invoice? Trygg 48 is our insurance
that guarantees you a replacement mobile or tablet within 48 hours
(weekdays) from when your damage report is registered and complete. It
shouldn't take longer than that to get your mobile life back up and running.
If your mobile is stolen during your vacation within the EU/EEA, we will
send you a new replacement unit as soon as your damage report is registered
and complete.  
  
Trygg 48 is free for the first two months, then it costs SEK 149/month for
mobiles and SEK 59/month for tablets. During the trial period, you will
receive an SMS where you can deactivate Trygg 48. If you are satisfied, do
nothing. The non-binding insurance continues and the cost is added to your
invoice.  
  
[Here you will find our insurance terms and conditions.](/support/priser-villkor-och-
blanketter/mobiltelefoni-och-mobilt-bredband/villkor-och-blanketter/)

You can read more about pre-purchase information for Trygg 48 below:

[Pre-purchase information Trygg 48
(pdf)](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/Pdf/TA01182.pdf
"pre-purchase information Trygg 48 (pdf)")

Do you want to cancel Trygg 48? [Here's how.](/support/vanliga-fragor/skada-och-
forlust/hur-sager-jag-upp-forsakringen-trygg-48/)
