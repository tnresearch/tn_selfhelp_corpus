# Connect to WiFi

Here we help you with simple step-by-step instructions to connect your computer or mobile phone to your wireless home network.

## I don't know my password

If you have [WiFi Router II](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter-2/) or [WiFi Router](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter/), do the following:

Send SMS with the keyword **WIFI XXXXXXXX to 2000** to receive the network name and password for the router. The 8 digits are the last 8 digits of the serial number (S/N) located on the underside.

If you have any of **our older routers**, the password is written on the router after the text admin: (consisting of letters and numbers) or after the text WPA2 (consisting of 13 letters)

## Connect mobile or PC

  * #### iOS

Mobile

If you can't press Connect, you have entered the wrong password.

    1. Open **Settings**

    2. Select **Wi-Fi**

    3. Tap the switch to turn on Wi-Fi

    4. Tap the name of the network you want to connect to

    5. If the network is secured with a password: Enter the password and tap **Connect**

  * #### Android

Mobile

    1. Open **Settings**

    2. Select **Connections**

    3. Turn on **Wi-Fi**

    4. Select the network you want to connect to from the list of available networks

    5. Enter the WIFI password if any and tap **Connect**

  * #### Windows

PC

    1. Click on the icon for wireless network at the bottom next to the clock and date.

    2. Click on your network, select **Connect**

    3. Enter your network password and press **Connect**

  * #### Mac

PC

    1. Click on Wi-Fi, the icon for wireless network

    2. Tap your network

    3. Enter the network password and click **Connect**

## Connect smart devices

We are constantly getting more smart devices in our homes - TVs, refrigerators, vacuum cleaners, clocks, locks and other gadgets.

Setting up these devices usually happens via a separate app on mobile or PC. The mobile or PC must then be connected to the same network as the smart device to be connected to.  
If you experience problems connecting the device via these apps, you must contact the provider of the service or device.

Some smart devices **require the network they use to be locked to 2.4 Ghz** - we have created a guide for just this.

You can find it here: [Extra network and guest network](https://telenor.no/ekstra-nettverk/)
