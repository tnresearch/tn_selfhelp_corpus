# Antenna NR7101 - Wireless Broadband

Here you will find help and guides for setting up Wireless Broadband with a
fixed outdoor antenna - Zyxel NR7101

## About Wireless Broadband

Wireless Broadband is a secure and future-proof broadband for your home
over a world-class mobile network. Wireless Broadband is available in two versions - a
new version with an outdoor antenna, receiver or antenna adapter inside and a standard
wireless router, and an older version with its own type of router. If you have the oldest
version, you can read more about it here - [Huawei
B818](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/huawei-b818/)

The external antenna is installed by a technician and already has a SIM card
installed, so all you need to do to get online is connect with your wireless router. Below you will find a guide on how to
do this.

## Simple Steps to Get Online

The procedure below is shown with [our WiFi router](/kundeservice/internett/modem-
og-ruter/wifi-ruter/), but you can follow the same steps with other routers.

  * #### Connect to antenna adapter (PoE)

Connect a network cable between the LAN port in the PoE/antenna adapter and the WAN port in the
WiFi Router.

The antenna adapter looks like a standard black power adapter, with two outputs
labeled LAN and POE. The cable in the PoE output goes to the antenna itself,
while LAN is the one you should use for further cabling inside.

  * #### Connect network cable to router

If you want to connect a computer, T-We box or other devices with
cable, connect a network cable between the equipment and the LAN ports on the router.

  * #### Connect power supply and turn on router

Connect the power supply to the router and turn it on.

Wireless Broadband requires power via a standard power grid at the address (230V) for
connection of antenna/router. Any errors or defects in the service as a
result of you choosing to connect the antenna to a low-voltage system with
a 230V converter are your responsibility. If you still, and at your own risk, choose to
use a low-voltage system, it must be suitable for fine electronics.

  * #### Wait for the top light to turn green

This may take some time. Do not turn off the WiFi Router while this is in progress

  * #### Connect to WiFi

Follow the instructions under WiFi Router to receive the network name and password for
your wireless network.
