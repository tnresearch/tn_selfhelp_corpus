# DNA's 3G Network Has Been Shut Down

#### DNA gradually shut down its entire 3G network during 2023 and January 2024. Areas of the joint network in Eastern and Northern Finland followed in 2024. The frequencies used in the 3G network were transferred to the 4G network, which has improved the quality of the 4G service in the areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What was the shutdown of the 3G network about?

DNA completely shut down its 3G network between October 2023 and January 2024, and the 3G network of the Finnish Joint Network was shut down during 2024. Closing the network released radio frequencies for use by newer networks (4G, 5G). The decision to shut down was made and announced as early as 2021.

Why was the 3G network shut down?

The 3G network had served telecommunications for about 20 years. The decision was based on providing a better customer experience with technical renewal. 4G and 5G networks offer more capacity, reliability and speed to meet the constantly growing needs of mobile networks. DNA's 4G network coverage is already over 99% of Finns.

The network structure also simplified with the removal of one technology layer and resources were released for the development of more efficient technologies. The shutdown of the 3G network also released frequencies for use by other networks.

At the end of 2022, only less than 1% of DNA customers' mobile data was transferred over the 3G network.

In Eastern and Northern Finland, the mobile networks used by DNA are planned, implemented and maintained by the Finnish Joint Network, a joint network company founded by DNA and Telia in 2014. The shutdown of the Joint Network's 3G network in Eastern and Northern Finland took place during 2024.

I was still using the 3G network. What does this change mean for me and where can I get replacement products?

The shutdown affected all services based solely on the 3G network. The services were replaced primarily with various 4G and 5G solutions. Older devices that do not support 4G or 5G networks generally switched to using the 2G network, even if the subscription type changed to a 4G or 5G subscription.

Consumer customers can obtain replacement products from [DNA customer service](/yhteystiedot) or, for example, from the nearest [DNA Store](/dnakaupat). Business customers can also obtain replacement products from [DNA business customer service](/yrityksille/yhteystiedot) or, for example, from the nearest [DNA Store](/dnakaupat).

DNA contacted business and corporate customers using 3G in advance to find replacement solutions. Work began as early as 2021. The long transition period was intended to ensure that even remaining 3G customers had enough time to upgrade their devices and services to modern standards.

The shutdown of the 3G network was normal network development work and with the help of replacement solutions our customers got even better services. DNA does not compensate for costs arising from the change.

In which network will calls be routed after the 3G network is shut down?

If the terminal has VoLTE, i.e. Voice over LTE feature which the subscription also supports, calls will be routed over the 4G network. In this case, the phone remains on the 4G network during the call and the call quality is also better.

If the phone and subscription do not support the VoLTE service, calls will be routed over the 2G network. In this case, the phone will drop to the 2G network during calls.

Has the 3G network also been shut down elsewhere in the world?

Yes, and this is good to keep in mind when traveling. In some countries, the shutdown of the 2G network has also been decided. For example, in the United States, many operators only have 4G and 5G networks in use. So, if you travel to the United States, your subscription must support 4G calls (VoLTE) and your phone must support VoLTE roaming.
