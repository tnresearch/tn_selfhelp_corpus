# Connect to T-We Box

Here you will find guides to help you connect the T-We Box to your TV.
Find out how to correctly place the cables, install the T-We menu and
connect to the Internet.

# First Time Connecting to TV

By following this guide, you will get help placing the cables correctly and
completing the installation on the TV screen. Installation of T-We is also the same
if you have reset the T-We box.

# Do You Have TV Over Fiber?

To give you the right help with the connection, we first need to know what type of
Internet you have with your TV subscription. **Note!** It is important to use
the cables that come with the T-We box to ensure optimal quality of TV signal,
sound and picture.
