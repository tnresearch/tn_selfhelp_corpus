# Deactivate Find My iPhone

When you need to hand in your iPhone for repair or trade-in, you need to
deactivate the 'Find My iPhone' function. Here you will find guidance on
how to do this.

  1. ### Step 1

Log in to <https://www.icloud.com/find/>. Under All Devices, select the iPhone
to be deactivated

  2. ### Step 2

Click on "Erase iPhone" and confirm the deletion

  3. ### Step 3

Enter your password to confirm

  4. ### Step 4

Enter a contact number where you can be reached

  5. ### Step 5

If you wish, you can write a message that will appear on the phone after it is
deleted. Press "Done" to start the deletion

  6. ### Step 6

Deleting the phone starts

  7. ### Step 7

Select settings from the main menu and select the iPhone to be deleted. Press the
blue icon (x) to remove this from your account

  8. ### Step 8

Confirm that you want to remove the device from your account
