# How to Log In to My Telenor

My Telenor is Telenor's self-service solution where you can check your usage
and bills, purchase extra data, see great offers and much more.
You can use My Telenor both as an app on your mobile and through Telenor.dk from
your computer. You log in the same way, regardless of what you choose. On this page
we primarily show how it looks on a mobile screen, because it is easier
for you to read.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need to use MitID to log in?

MitID is a secure solution that is used in many different places on
the internet that require high security.
For you, this means that your login is secure and relatively easy to use. Many
of the other solutions that provide a high level of security require you to
use a complicated code that you must not use to log in to other
sites. In this way, it either becomes cumbersome because you have to remember many
long codes, or insecure because you reuse the same code in multiple places.
We avoid this with MitID.

Why is such a high level of security required?

A high level of security is needed because My Telenor is linked
together with many important personal information about you, e.g. your full
name, your address, date of birth, telephone number, email address and your
payment information.
Telenor must, both according to the law and because we care about your online security,
protect that information really well, so unauthorized parties cannot easily
log in to your user and use your information to commit identity theft.

I can't figure out MitID. What do I do?

[MitID has created a page where you can find many answers and
information.](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/mitid-support/?language=da-dk) If
it still bothers you, contact the Citizen Service in your municipality.

What do you need my CPR number for?

The first time you log in, you must enter your CPR number. This is so that
Telenor can find the information we have about you in our system (e.g. your name and
address, your bills, as well as which subscriptions and services you have) to you, so you can also see them in My Telenor. And there is also another important
reason: So we are sure that it is you who gets to see your information - even
if there are several people with the same name as you.

How do I sign up for My Telenor?

If it's the first time you log in to My Telenor, you don't need to worry about creating a user. If you have used MitID to confirm your identity
when you signed up as a customer, you are already registered in Telenor's
system, and then we can transfer your information to My Telenor.

Why should I use My Telenor?

There is no requirement that you use My Telenor, but it is a really
good idea. On My Telenor you can always get a quick overview of your
bills, subscriptions, products and service messages. You can also easily find
your PUK code if you need it.
It is also through My Telenor that you use ID Guard if you have NetSecurity Plus.

What do I do if I have a Telenor account through my work?

If you are a business customer of Telenor and manage your employees'
subscriptions, you do not need to use My Telenor - instead you can use
[OneScreen Business](/erhverv/selvbetjening/).

If you are an employee and have a company phone, you can log in to My Telenor
with SMS login. You will find the button for SMS login at the bottom of the login page for
My Telenor.

What do I do if I want to use My Telenor but don't have MitID?

If you want to use My Telenor but do not have MitID, you can use our SMS-
login. However, you should be aware that SMS login only gives you limited
access to My Telenor.

What will happen to my old Telenor ID?

Telenor ID is the old login solution for My Telenor, which we have now replaced
with MitID. If you want to do something with your old Telenor ID account (e.g. delete
or edit information), you can do so by logging in with your
Telenor ID login via [this page](https://manage.telenorid.com/overview).

What do I do if I can't remember my password for MitID?

If you can't remember your password or username, you should contact MitID.
[MitID](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/mitid-support/?language=da-dk) can
be contacted here.
