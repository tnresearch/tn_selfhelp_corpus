## Features

**Product Name: Huawei B2338-168**

The DNA Outdoor Modem 4G WLAN is a modem designed for outdoor installation. It consists of an indoor unit that creates Wi-Fi
and an outdoor directional 4G antenna. Data is transferred losslessly between these two units.  
You get a reliable 4G connection, even if coverage is poor, and you can find an even better
signal in areas with already good  
coverage.

  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 150 Mbit/s
  * 3G DC maximum speed 42 Mbit/s
  * HSPA+ maximum speed 21.1 Mbit/s
  * WLAN b/g/n/ac 2x2
  * 2 RJ-45 Ethernet ports, max. speed 1000 Mbit/s.
  * USB
  * 32 WLAN devices
  * Standard SIM card

### Package contents:

DNA Outdoor Modem 4G WLAN B2338-168, indoor and outdoor unit, grounding cable,
power supply, 10 meter Cat 5e POE Ethernet cable, 1.5 meter Cat 5e Ethernet-
cable, user manual

### Hardware requirements / Supported operating systems:

  * RJ45-Ethernet connection
  * WLAN: 802.11b/g/n/ac
