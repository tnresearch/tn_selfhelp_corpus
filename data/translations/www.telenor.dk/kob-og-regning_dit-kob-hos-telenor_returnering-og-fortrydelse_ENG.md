# Your Right of Return and Cancellation with Telenor

If you regret a purchase from Telenor, you have a 14-day right of cancellation. If the 14th day falls on a weekend or public holiday, your cancellation period will expire on the following weekday.

Please note that when returning an iPad/iPhone, you must disable the “Find my iPhone” function (Settings/Cloud/Find My iPhone) before returning it. If you do not do so, the product will be worthless to Telenor, and you will therefore have to pay the full price of the product.

## How to Return an Item

There are two different ways to return your item, depending on where you bought it. If you purchased the item in a Telenor store, you can return it to [one of our many stores](/find-butik/).

If you purchased the item on telenor.dk or via our customer service, you must [create a return ](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/opret-retur/) and drop off the package at a PostNord parcel shop.

## How to do it:

Regret purchasing a subscription?

Do you want to change your order? Then call us on 72 100 100

* You have a 14-day right of cancellation from the day you purchased your subscription
* Have you used your subscription within the 14 days? Then you must pay for the usage
* You do not need to return any SIM card
* Routers must be returned - see how below

Regret purchasing a physical item? (e.g. mobile, accessories or router)

If you purchased the item in a Telenor store, you can return it to [one of our many stores](/find-butik/).

If you purchased the item on telenor.dk or via our customer service, you must [create a return ](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/opret-retur/) and drop off the package at a PostNord parcel shop. You do this by following these steps:

**1. Create a return on Telenor.dk**
Fill out our return form with the reference number that is on your packing slip.

**2. You will receive a QR code**
You will receive an email or SMS with a QR code that you must show at a PostNord parcel shop.

**3. Drop off the package at PostNord**
PostNord will scan your QR code and print a return label for you. You will also receive a receipt for delivery.

**4. We receive your package**
You can follow the entire package's journey and see when it arrives at our warehouse.

Please note that you have 14 days to create your return on telenor.dk, from when you received your item. After that, you have another 14 days to deliver the return to a PostNord parcel shop.

**When is my return complete?**
Your return will be registered when we receive the item at our warehouse. You will then receive a confirmation when your return has been processed.

**Did you purchase a subscription with the item?**
If you also want to cancel the subscription, you must contact our customer service on 72 100 100, who are ready to help you.

**Did you purchase the item at Elgiganten?**
If you purchased your item at Elgiganten, you must contact them to cancel your purchase.
