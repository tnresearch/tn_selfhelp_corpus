## Before a warranty claim, please do the following

  * Clean and wipe your device with a dry cloth.
  * Remove any extra accessories from your device, such as a SIM card, memory card, or protective case.
  * Back up important data and files.
  * Disable FMI (Find my iPhone/iPad) or Samsung Knox.
  * Put your device into service mode, if possible. 
    * Most devices have the option to activate service mode, which prevents access to personal files such as photos or contacts.
