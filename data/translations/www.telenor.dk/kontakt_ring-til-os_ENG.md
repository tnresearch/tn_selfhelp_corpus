## Questions and answers about customer service

What can customer service help you with?

If you need help with your subscription, questions about your bill, or
problems with your mobile phone, our customer service can help you on weekdays from
8 AM to 8 PM.

What can support help you with?

If you need technical help with your internet connection, our support
can help you Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 8 PM.

Saturday and Sunday (including public holidays) from 9 AM to 5 PM.
