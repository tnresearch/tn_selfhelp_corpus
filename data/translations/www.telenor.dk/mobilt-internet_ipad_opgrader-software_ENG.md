# Update Your iPad

Apple software updates include improvements, bug fixes, and new
features. Therefore, to ensure your iPad functions optimally, it's a good
idea to keep it updated.

You need to be connected to a wireless network to update your operating system.

  * ## Press "Settings"

  * ## Press "General" and select "Software Update".

  * ## Press "Install Now"

  * ## Press "Agree"

  * ## Press "Agree" again

Once you have accepted the terms, your software update will begin to
download and install the latest version of iOS.

## If you are not on WiFi, follow the guide below.

**Check that iTunes is updated**  
When you need to update your iPad, it is done using iTunes. Always
make sure you have the latest version of iTunes before starting
the update:

    1. Open iTunes 
    2. Select the " **Help** " menu 
    3. Click on " **Check for Updates** " 
    4. If your version of iTunes is not the latest, you will now be notified that a newer version is available. 
    5. Then click " **Get iTunes** " and follow the on-screen instructions. 
    6. Once iTunes is updated, you can update your phone.

**How to update your iPad**

    1. Open iTunes on your computer and connect your iPad 
    2. Select your iPad when it appears under " **Devices** " in iTunes' left menu 
    3. Select the " **Summary** " tab 
    4. Click the " **Check for Update** " button 
    5. If new updates are available, click " **Update** " and follow the on-screen instructions. 
    6. In some cases, it can take a long time to update your iPad (from 5 minutes to 1 hour), depending on your internet connection. Make sure your computer doesn't run out of power. 
    7. You will see a small synchronization icon (two arrows) in the upper right corner of your iPad. As long as the icon is spinning, your iPad should be connected to the computer. When the icon stops, your iPad is updated with the latest software.
