## Features

  * EuroDocsis compatible (3.0, 2.0, 1.1 and 1.0)
  * Wireless network: 802.11b/g/n 2.4GHz & 5GHz
  * IPv6 support
  * 4 x 1 Gbps LAN ports (e.g., for a set-top box or computer)
  * WPS feature for easy connection of wireless devices
  * Supports DLNA 1.5
  * DNA's customized settings
