# Paying for Purchases Made in the App Store and iTunes Store
with a Phone Bill

## Here's how:

  1. Open "Settings" on your iPhone and click on your Apple ID.
  2. Select "Payment & Shipping" and log in with your Apple ID.
  3. Select "Add Payment Method" and "Mobile Phone."

Done! You will receive a confirmation of your order by email. The confirmation
will be sent to the email address associated with your Apple ID account.

For more detailed instructions on changing, adding, or removing payment methods,
please visit [Apple's support pages](https://support.apple.com/fi-fi/HT201266).

### A world of entertainment. Pay easily.

You can use your DNA subscription to pay for purchases from the App Store, Apple Music, iTunes, and iBooks.
It's a safe and easy way to pay for your favorite entertainment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What can you do with the service?

You can pay for purchases made in the App Store, iTunes, iBooks, and Apple Music with your DNA phone bill or the balance of your DNA prepaid subscription.
Please note that the service does not work with mobile broadband subscriptions, including
data prepaid subscriptions.

How can you enable the payment method?

You can add the payment method in the payment settings of the services, e.g., in the App Store.
More detailed instructions can be found above on the page.

On which devices does the service work?

The payment method works on iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch devices with iOS 7.0 or
a newer operating system, as well as Mac and Windows versions of iTunes. After adding the payment method, you can also use it on Apple TV and Apple Watch devices.

Is there a usage limit for the service?

The default usage limit for value-added service purchases is €150/month for phone subscriptions,
with a maximum single purchase of €50. You will receive a confirmation of all purchases
by email to the email address associated with your Apple account.

How can I prevent purchases on my subscription?

You can prevent operator billing purchases in app stores with a P3 or stricter
message restriction (entertainment and adult content restriction services). Read more
about restrictions and blocks [here](https://www.dna.fi/rajoitukset-ja-estot).

I have been billed incorrectly. Who can I contact?

Possible complaints about purchases can be made through the page
[https://getsupport.apple.com](https://getsupport.apple.com/).
