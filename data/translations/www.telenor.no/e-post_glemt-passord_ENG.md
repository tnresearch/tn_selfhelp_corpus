# Forgot Your Online E-mail Password?

Log in with your Telenor ID and follow the steps to get a new password. If you have multiple e-mail accounts, you can choose which one you want to change the password for.

### Get a New Password for Your Online E-mail

You must be logged in with your Telenor ID to get a new password. If you do not have a Telenor ID, you will be given the opportunity to create one. Click the button to proceed.
Once you are in My Pages, click **Change Password** on the e-mail account you want to change.

[Log in with Telenor ID](/mitt-telenor/e-post/)

Having trouble logging in with your Telenor ID? [Get help with login here.](/telenor-id/hjelp-med-innlogging/)

### Password Selection Rules:

  * Choose a password without the letters æ, ø, å and consisting of at least 12 characters.

  * The password must contain at least one number, one special character (@$!%*?&), one uppercase letter, and one lowercase letter.

  * #### Have you changed your password without doing it yourself?

If we suspect that your password has been compromised, the existing password will be deactivated. You must then change to a new password for the e-mail account.

If this happens often, you should consider updating the security on your PC or activating antivirus software to minimize the risk.

### Password via SMS or Mail?

Now that Online E-mail has been moved to a new e-mail solution, it is no longer possible to send SMS "Password" to 1980 to get a new password.
We also cannot send a new e-mail password by mail, among other things for privacy reasons.

If you are unable to change your password on My Pages, [contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/), and we will help you!
