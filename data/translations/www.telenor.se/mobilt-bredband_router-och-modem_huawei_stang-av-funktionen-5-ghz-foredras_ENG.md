# Turn off the "5 GHz Preferred" function

Changing frequency can make your mobile broadband faster, as different
frequency bands have different properties.  
  

All modern routers have two frequency bands – 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz.

\- 2.4 GHz has **longer range** but **lower speed**

\- 5 GHz has **shorter range** and **higher speed**  
  
If you want to test changing frequency to see if the connection improves, do
the following:

  * ### Log in to the router's interface

You do this by entering the address 192.168.8.1 in a web browser and then
filling in your Wi-Fi password.

  * ### Select Wi-Fi settings

  * ### Change the "5 GHz Preferred" function
