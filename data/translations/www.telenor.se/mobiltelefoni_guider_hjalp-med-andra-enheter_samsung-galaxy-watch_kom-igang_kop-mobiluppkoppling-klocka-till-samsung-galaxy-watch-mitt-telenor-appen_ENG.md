# Purchase Mobile Connection for watch (My Telenor app)

Follow the steps below to purchase the Mobile Connection for watch service for your Samsung
Galaxy Watch. Once completed, you can leave your mobile phone at home next time you go
out.  

Keep this in mind first:

  * You must be a private customer
  * You must have verified yourself as the owner of the subscription and have BankID
  * The watch must support e-sim and be connected to the internet
  * The person who will use the service must have another device ready to scan a QR code with

  * ### Log in to the My Telenor app 

If you haven't created a My Telenor account, you must do so first.  

[Go to the My Telenor app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/)

  * ### Select Settings and then the mobile number you want to purchase the service for

  * ### Under the heading Services and settings, scroll down to the heading Payment services

  * ### Select Add Mobile Connection for watch

  * ### Then you will receive instructions to help you purchase and activate the service. Follow them!
