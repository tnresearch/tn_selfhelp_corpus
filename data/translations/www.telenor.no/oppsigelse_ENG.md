# Cancellation

Do you want to cancel your subscription? Here you will find answers to questions about
cancellation and information on how to cancel your subscription with Telenor.

## Cancellation Period in Telenor

**Mobile and Mobile Broadband:** You must pay the monthly fee for the month you
cancel.

**TV, Broadband and Landline:** The cancellation period is one month, and
the cancellation takes effect from the first day of the following month. That is, if you cancel during September, the subscription will expire in October.

[See the terms of your subscription](/privat/vilkar/) for more information.

## Invoice upon Cancellation

When you cancel, fees that have been invoiced beyond the cancellation period
will be refunded.

You can read more about invoices upon cancellation and changes by clicking on the link
below.

[Invoice upon Change or Cancellation](/kundeservice/faktura/forklaring/)

## I want to cancel an additional service

Most additional services can be cancelled on My Pages.

To terminate additional services, click on the link below.

  1. Click on the subscription in question, and click on **See all** next to the heading **Services**

  2. Then click on the service in question, and select Cancel this service

  3. You will receive a confirmation by email

If you want to cancel a service that you cannot cancel yourself on My Pages, please
contact customer service.

[Go to My Pages](/mitt-telenor/)

## Questions and Answers

  * #### Can I cancel my mobile subscription, but keep the number?

If you no longer want a subscription with an invoice, you can change the subscription
to a prepaid card. Then you keep the number without receiving any invoice.

Call customer service at [915 09000](tel:+4791509000) to change to a prepaid card.

  * #### How do I cancel a subscription due to death?

To cancel a subscription in connection with a death, we recommend that you
call us.

  * #### Can I cancel a lease agreement for a WiFi Router or T-We Box II?

You can cancel the lease agreement for Wifi routers and T-We box II that you rent from us.
We will then send out a return label that you use to return the equipment. If
the equipment is not returned within a reasonable time, you will be invoiced for the equipment.

[Cancellation of the lease agreement can be done on My
Pages.](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/)

Older equipment for which there is no lease agreement does not need to be returned.

  * #### How do I terminate my Split agreement?

You can terminate the Split agreement at any time by buying out your mobile phone.
This is done by contacting customer service at [915 09000](tel:+4791509000)
or going to [one of our
Telenor stores](https://www.telenor.no/telenorbutikken/).

You will then receive a final invoice, where you pay for the months remaining on
the agreement. Once it is paid, the Split agreement is over – and you own the mobile phone yourself. You do not pay anything more if you buy it out during the period, the total price for the phone is the same regardless.

  * #### How do I terminate my SWAP agreement?

With the launch of [Split](/splitt/), we are also phasing out SWAP. [Here you can read
more about what this means for you with an active SWAP agreement.](/swap/swap-
avvikles/)  

* * *

  
For help and guidance, you can visit [one of our
Telenor stores](https://www.telenor.no/telenorbutikken/) or a
Telenor dealer such as Power or Elkjøp.

You can also call us at [915 09000](tel:+4791509000) if you want to
terminate your SWAP agreement.

  * #### How do I switch from one mobile operator to another?

If you want to keep the same mobile number, it is important that you do not cancel
your subscription before you switch. If you switch from one operator to another, the
new operator will cancel the subscription for you.

If you cancel the phone number yourself, you will lose the number and will get a new
mobile number.

If you want to switch to another subscription in Telenor, [see overview of our
mobile subscriptions.](/mobilabonnement/)

  * #### What happens to My Cloud if I cancel my subscription?

When cancelling a subscription in Telenor, or if you switch providers,
you will lose the extra storage space you have.

You still have 2 GB of free storage in My Cloud, but if you exceed this, you will
receive a notification in the app that the storage space is used up. In addition, you will receive an
email from Telenor Digital containing more information on how to purchase more storage space. Content that exceeds 2 GB will be deleted, so you should download them to your PC or mobile to secure the content.  

Note that the files are stored in the cloud for 30 days before being deleted, but the files
stored locally on your devices you retain.
