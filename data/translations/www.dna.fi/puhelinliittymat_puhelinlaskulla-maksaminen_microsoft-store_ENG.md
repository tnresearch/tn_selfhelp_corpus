# Paying for Purchases Made from the Microsoft Store with a Phone Bill

You can purchase from the Microsoft Store and pay for your purchases with your DNA phone bill or prepaid subscription balance. Add the payment method in the Microsoft Store's payment settings, after which you can purchase from the Microsoft Store, for example, on a Windows 10 computer or Xbox One. Please note that mobile broadband subscriptions are not covered by the service.

## Instructions for Using the DNA Phone Bill in the Microsoft Store App Store

  * Add "mobile phone" as the payment method in the Microsoft Store.
  * If a credit card has previously been added to the Microsoft Store, you can change the payment method to a phone bill in the app store's payment settings.
  * The default usage limit is €150/month and a single purchase limit of €50/purchase.
  * Microsoft will send a receipt for each purchase by email to the email address specified in your Microsoft account.
  * You can still use a credit card to pay for your purchases, but in the future you can switch to using the DNA phone bill as the payment method if you wish.

## Using the Payment Method on an Xbox One Console

  * The service is not available on original Xbox or Xbox 360 consoles.
  * The payment method is added in the same way as in the Microsoft Store on a computer.
  * Xbox Live Gold and Xbox Game Pass memberships can also be paid for with Mobile Payment.

## Blocking Mobile Payment

**If you wish, you can block Mobile Payment as follows:**

  * The Microsoft Store belongs to service group 3: Entertainment Services in terms of blocking category. You can block all purchases with P3 or a stricter message blocking category.
  * You can optionally set up a service block for your subscription, which will prevent purchases from the Microsoft Store. This is recommended, especially for children's subscriptions. You can set up a service block through [DNA Customer Service](https://www.dna.fi/asiakaspalvelu).

#### Microsoft Store Customer Service

Microsoft's customer support for the Microsoft Store is provided by Microsoft (tel. 09 8171 0400 and <https://support.microsoft.com/contactus>). Microsoft also handles any refund requests and customer complaints.

**Note.** In order to pay for your purchases with a phone bill or your subscription balance, the country settings of your Microsoft account and Windows 10 device must be Finland.

## Instructions for Paying with DNA Mobile Payment

Setting up DNA Mobile Payment

#### 1. Choose the program you want from the app store and proceed to pay by pressing the price of the app.

#### 2. Log in to your Microsoft account. You will be prompted for a password before each purchase. You can optionally turn off the prompt.

#### 3. After logging in, you can select the payment method. If you have not added a payment method to your account, you can do so now.

#### 4. Select the payment method you want to link to your account from the list.

#### 5. Select "Mobile Phone" and enter your phone number in basic format without the country code.

#### 6. A verification code will be sent to your phone via text message. The code is used to verify your identity. Enter the code you received in the field provided.

#### 7. Now you can purchase apps from the Windows Store with your phone bill or prepaid subscription balance. DNA will not send you a receipt for your purchases, but Microsoft will send a receipt for each purchase to the email address in your Microsoft account.

#### 8. After the purchase, the app download will start automatically.

Changing the Payment Method to DNA Mobile Payment

#### 1. Choose the program you want from the app store and proceed to pay by pressing the price of the app.

#### 2. The app store will go to the confirmation view. Select the desired payment method. Add a new payment method by selecting "Change".

#### 3. Add a new payment method to the Windows Store.

#### 4. Select the new payment method, i.e. "Mobile Phone".

#### 5. After selecting, enter your phone number in basic format without the country code.

#### 6. A verification code will be sent to your phone via text message. The code is used to verify your identity. Enter the code you received in the field provided.

#### 7. Now you can purchase apps from the Windows Store with your phone bill or prepaid subscription balance. DNA will not send you a receipt for your purchases, but Microsoft will send a receipt for each purchase to the email address in your Microsoft account.

#### 8. After the purchase, the app download will start automatically.
