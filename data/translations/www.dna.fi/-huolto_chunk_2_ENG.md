# Device Service Partners and Warranty

Having concerns or needing service? On this page you will find the warranty
and service partners for devices sold by DNA.

It is recommended to contact the recommended partners directly to ensure the fastest service.
You can also visit a DNA Store and ask for instructions
for easy service if you prefer!

Whenever possible, rely on the manufacturer's support for troubleshooting, as
problems can often be resolved easily without a repair.

The contact information and services of the companies listed on this page are the responsibility of those
service providers.  
If the manufacturer's warranty has expired, your device may still be covered by a
**DNA extended 3-year warranty**. Check the warranty validity on your purchase receipt.  
You can always follow the instructions on this website, even with an extended warranty.

#### Service Benefits and Offers from DNA Stores

If you wish, you can handle mobile device repairs at DNA Stores, where you can
take advantage of DNA's service customer benefits.

  * Mobile Outfitters Original screen protector -50% after service.
  * DNA Digiturva trial period at a special price. For new subscriptions only.

Always remember to back up your device's files before sending it
for service. Files may be lost from the device's memory during service.
