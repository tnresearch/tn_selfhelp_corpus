##

  * Read more about background checks

When Telenor recruits new employees or consultants, it is important for us
to verify that the individual has a suitable background for working with
us. Therefore, we, or the recruitment firms and consulting companies we
hire, regularly order so-called background checks on current candidates.

The company performing the background check is responsible for how it
is carried out and how personal data is handled in connection with it. Telenor receives
information about the result of the background check through various channels, often
verbally or via a secure portal where the result is available for a maximum of
96 hours. Results that are not privacy-sensitive may be sent via email. We
do not find out any details, only whether the person has passed the check
without remark or not. We do not store any results of background checks with
us.

In connection with the background check being carried out, a central function on
Telenor's HR department may receive hypothetical questions about what can be considered an acceptable background for working with us, but the questions are anonymous and are very
difficult for us to attribute to a single individual (which is not the intention either). If you have questions about the background check, you should first contact
the company that performed the check. Before it begins, you will receive information about
which company will perform it.

If you have questions about Telenor's handling of personal data in connection with
background checks, you can find our contact details
[here](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt2).
