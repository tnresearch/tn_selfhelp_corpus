# Online Fraud Insurance

[DNA Digital Security App Settings](dna.fi)

Even when we are prepared and act reasonably, anything can happen to people of all ages online. We don't want to leave you alone if something happens. DNA Digital Security's Online Fraud Insurance:

  * Offers legal advice and assistance when you are affected by online harassment, identity theft, payment instrument fraud, or online shopping scams.
  * Provides support to address cyberbullying.

## If you suspect damage and need advice

If you suspect you have been a victim of online fraud and want advice, contact the insurance provider HELPin customer service directly:

  * Mon–Fri 8:00 AM –6:00 PM
  * In Finnish 0290020990
  * In Swedish 0290020991

## What to do if you suspect you have been scammed

### 1. Stop

Haste always plays into the hands of scammers. If you are being pressured on the phone to make quick decisions, end the call. If you are told that something is about to happen to your money, stop and think about the situation.

Or if you have already clicked on a suspicious link or given your information, stop. We can help.

### 2. Call

HELP's experts are available for you on the phone. Call HELP as soon as possible about a possible scam:

  * In Finnish 0290020990
  * In Swedish 0290020991
  * Customer service is open Mon–Fri 8:00 AM –6:00 PM

Even if you just suspect you may have been scammed, contact us and we can talk about it.

If you only suspect an attempt to scam, you can report it, for example, to the Competition and Consumer Authority.

### 3. Follow the process

You will receive more detailed instructions from a lawyer on the phone. Otherwise, you can follow the processing of your case in the [HELP portal](https://omatsivut.help.fi/).

### 4. Get help until the matter is resolved

HELP Insurance's lawyer will contact you throughout the processing of the case, including via the electronic portal.

I have 1–2 license sizes in use, how do I get insurance for the whole family?

Larger license sizes of DNA Digital Security include insurance covering the household. If you have a DNA Digital Security subscription with 10 or 25 licenses, the insurance includes spouses and children under 20 living in the same household.

You can upgrade your DNA Digital Security to a larger license size by contacting our customer service.

I have switched to a larger license size, when does the insurance come into effect for the whole family?

The insurance comes into effect on the same day you switch the license size. If you are unsure when you made the changes, you can ask our [customer service]( /web/yksityisille/tuki/yhteystiedot/asiakaspalvelu).

How can I get insurance for my adult child?

The insurance covers the subscriber. An adult child living on their own must order DNA Digital Security themselves.

Is the insurance also for entrepreneurs?

The insurance is only for private customers. If you have taken out insurance with a business ID, but want to change it to yourself as a private customer, you need to order DNA Digital Security as an individual and terminate your current subscription.

Please note that if you terminate the DNA Digital Security subscription with a business ID and want to keep your password bank information, you must save the password vault credentials before the subscription ends. See detailed instructions on saving passwords from the [instructions for changing devices](/web/yksityisille/tuki/tietoturva/digiturva/henkilot-ja-laitteet).

Can I contact you if I don't know if something has happened?

Yes, you can. Let's find out together if there is reason to suspect, for example, identity theft or payment instrument fraud.

A family member has been or suspects they have been a victim of online fraud, what should I do?

The subscription owner should primarily contact HELP, even if the suspected fraud has happened to another family member.

DNA Digital Security's 10 and 25 license packages include insurance for the household. In this case, the insurance includes your spouse and children under 20 living in the same household.

I would like to order the product, but the online store seems suspicious. Can I ask for advice before ordering from the online store?

You can ask if the online store seems trustworthy or if it is likely to be a scam site.

Does the insurance cover products purchased from tori.fi or other similar platforms?

The insurance does not cover transactions between private individuals, even if they are made through a professional sales platform.

My information has been leaked by a third party. Can I ask for help from the insurance?

If you suspect you have been a victim of identity theft or payment instrument fraud, contact HELP for more detailed advice.

If you suspect that the leaked information is being used for financial gain and you have been a victim of identity theft, for example, contact HELP for more detailed advice.

I noticed the fraud now, but it happened before I took out DNA Digital Security. Can I ask for help?

The insurance does not cover damages that occurred before you took out the Digital Security service.

Online Fraud Insurance was linked as part of DNA Digital Security on April 1, 2025, and does not cover damages that occurred before this date.
