# Factory Reset Your Router

If you've reached this step, you've really done a lot to find the problem with your broadband. Good job, and it's a shame it's still not working as it should!

  
As a last step, you can factory reset your router. It may have become stuck, and a reset can help. But keep in mind that **all your settings will be lost** and revert to factory defaults, for example, changes to the name and password of your wireless network.

### How to Factory Reset the Router

Take a paperclip and insert it into the hole labeled Reset on the back of the router. Press and hold the button until the router restarts and wait about five minutes.
