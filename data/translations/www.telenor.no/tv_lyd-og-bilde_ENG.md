# Quality of Picture and Sound

Here you will find information about how we distribute audio and video, as well as information about what can affect your user experience on TV, mobile or tablet.

Telenor delivers TV, radio and streaming services over many accesses with high-quality equipment to ensure the best possible customer experience.

The points below describe how we distribute audio and video and information about what can affect your user experience on the TV screen via T-We Box, Apple TV, in a browser, on a tablet or mobile.

## 1. Distribution of Live TV

Telenor delivers live TV on two different technologies depending on which access form your equipment is connected to.

**Cable and IPTV:** TV signals are delivered as broadcasting where each TV channel has a dedicated bandwidth to the end customer. Each TV channel has a fixed bitrate and resolution. An HD channel is typically delivered at approximately 8 Mb/s, while a 4K/UHD channel is delivered at approximately 20 Mb/s.

**Streaming over the internet and distribution over the mobile network:** TV images are delivered with a technology called Adaptive Bit Rate (ABR) where the bitrate and resolution are adapted to both screen size and capacity on each individual customer's home network and internet access. See point 3 below for more information about ABR.

## 2. Distribution of Start Over, Week Archive, TV Archive and Film Rental

These services are always delivered using Adaptive Bit Rate (ABR) technology regardless of which access form the customer equipment is connected to. You must therefore have your decoder connected to the internet to be able to use these services.

## 3. Adaptive BitRate (ABR)

ABR is a technology where TV images are delivered with a bitrate and resolution adapted to the capacity of each individual customer's home network and internet access.

This means that your decoder or client automatically selects the best quality level of picture and sound that is possible depending on the available capacity on your internet access and home network. This prevents the picture playback from stopping while the decoder or client downloads data.

This means that the quality of the picture playback can vary and often start with slightly lower picture quality in the first few seconds before achieving the best bitrate. On live TV, ABR streaming will be slightly delayed compared to cable TV or IPTV as the live broadcast must be re-encoded in the different bitrates, and there will also be some delay in distribution over the internet.

## 4. Picture Quality of Video

Telenor delivers TV and video in both SD, HD and UHD/4K over many bitrates from 500 kb/s up to 16 Mb/s. Both the H.264 coding technology and the modern H.265 are used here to deliver the best possible picture quality.

Live TV content played from Telenor's TV platform is typically delivered with HD resolution of 1280x720 pixels and with 25 or 50 FPS (Frames Per Second / images per second). 50 FPS is used on all channels with sports and the largest national channels to ensure good quality for sports and other broadcasts with a lot of movement. 4K channels are encoded with 3840 x 2160 pixels and 50 FPS. SD resolution (Standard Definition) is between 480x270 and 1024x576 pixels.

T-We Box II (ADB 3770) supports UHD/4K/HDR, i.e. 3840x2160 pixels with 50 FPS and High Dynamic Range (High dynamic resolution). Remember that your TV or screen must also support 4K and HDR to be able to display this picture quality.

## 5. Audio

Telenor delivers different audio formats to both TV and radio broadcasts. The most common is that TV and radio channels are delivered in the AAC-LC stereo audio format with bitrates up to 160 kbps. AAC stands for Advanced Audio Coding (AAC).

In addition, a number of channels and services are delivered with AC3 5.1 audio. AC3 stands for Audio Coding 3 (Dolby digital), and describes audio files with surround sound. The AC3 format contains up to 6 separate audio channels (Front right, left and center, back right and left as well as 1 channel for subwoofer)

This is standard surround sound used in home theaters.

NRK, TV 3 Viasat 4, TV2 Sport Premium, SVT and Swedish TV 4, as well as the film channels of CMORE and Viasat are delivered with AC3 (Dolby Digital). So are Netflix, Viaplay, HBO Max and several other streaming services. Note that not all content/programs within these services are delivered to us with surround sound.
**Telenor delivers the audio further in the same quality as we receive it from the content provider.**

Audio for video from Paramount+ and TV channels is encoded in 2.0 stereo, 196 kbps.

**T-We App**

* Surround sound on movies, series and streaming services that deliver with AC3
* No surround on live TV, Start Over and Week Archive yet

## 6. Problems with Audio and Video

Problems with stuttering, sound not being in sync and reloading can be due to low capacity on your internet line, wireless network or mobile network. For the picture playback to work properly, you should have at least 5 Mbit/s available. In addition, other things such as multiple TV or video streams on the same internet line or outdated browser versions can affect stuttering/freezing.

You can try restarting the playback to see if the problems disappear. If they do not, you can troubleshoot here: [Solve problems with TV.](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/)
