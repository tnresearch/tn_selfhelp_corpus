## Termination of Internet, TV or Home Phone

The notice period is the current month plus one month. This is the period for
which you will be billed for the subscription and is calculated from the day you
submit the termination.

If you want to cancel a channel package or remove content from T-We? Go to [Choose
channels and content in T-We](/kundeservice/tv/velge-kanaler/) to see how
this is done.

## Important things to consider before terminating TV or broadband

  * The notice period applies from the day you submit the form

  * If the termination is due to moving, have you checked what is offered at the new address?

  * Do you have a subscription with a commitment that may incur a termination fee? You can [check this on My Pages](/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/)

  * Do you have TV or broadband equipment that needs to be returned?

  * Do you have an email address with Telenor that you want to keep?

 **We remind you of all the benefits you have with Telenor:**

  * [Always WiFi](/privat/internett/alltid-wifi/)

  * [Always On Net Guarantee](/privat/internett/bredband/alltid-pa-nett-garanti/)

  * [Net Protection+](/nettvern-pluss/)

 **If you are unsure, please feel free to call customer service on 915 09 000, and we will help you.**
