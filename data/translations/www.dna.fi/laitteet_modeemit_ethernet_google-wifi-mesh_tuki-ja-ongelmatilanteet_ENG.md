## Support and Troubleshooting

Google WiFi has a 24-month warranty from the date of purchase.

DNA customer service can help you with installation, feature descriptions,
and troubleshooting related to device setup or error situations if needed.

DNA customer service can also guide and forward questions to Google support if necessary.  
  
You can find direct contact information for Google support
[here](https://support.google.com/wifi/answer/7348866?hl=en)
(English).

## General Troubleshooting Guide

  1. Restart Google WiFi or briefly disconnect the Ethernet cable.
  2. Force close the app on your phone.
  3. Restart Google WiFi.
  4. If the above methods do not solve the problem, finally perform a factory reset on the Google WiFi device and possibly the modem.

## Factory Reset

  1. Press and hold the Reset button for 10 seconds and release it. Note! If you hold the button down for more than 15 seconds, the device will start showing an orange or red LED light and you will need to start the factory reset again.
  2. Wait about 5 minutes until the device starts flashing a slow blue pulse light.
  3. Redo the setup.

## Google WiFi device does not start

  1. Restore factory settings.
  2. The LED lights can be turned off with the application, so check that you have not turned the lights off. Restoring factory settings will of course also restore the lights to function.
  3. Try a different power outlet.
  4. Check in the mobile application that the device is not offline.
  5. If the problem is not resolved, contact DNA customer service.

## SSID is not visible and/or the LED light is red

  1. Restore factory settings.
  2. Make sure the network cable is connected correctly.
  3. If the problem is not resolved, contact DNA customer service.
