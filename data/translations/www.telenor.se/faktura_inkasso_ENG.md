# Collection

If you have received a demand letter from PayEx, it means we have not received any
payment from you, despite reminders.

Receiving a demand letter from PayEx is due to one of the following:

  * You paid your invoice late
  * You paid to the wrong OCR number
  * You have not paid the invoice at all

### What happens if your invoice goes to collection

  1. First, you will receive a **demand letter from PayEx**. On the back of the demand letter you will find a copy of the invoice that has been sent to collection. The demand letter contains all information about when and where payment should be made.
  2. If PayEx does not receive your payment, the **subscription will be suspended**. If the invoice relates to your mobile subscription, this means you will not be able to make or receive calls.
  3. From the time the payment is registered in [My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/), it may take additional time before the subscription is reopened. If it has not reopened after 24 hours from the time the payment was registered, contact customer service.  

Can you not use your subscription even though the invoice is shown as paid? Restart
the device you have used the subscription on. That is, the mobile if it is
your mobile subscription that has been suspended, or the router if it concerns your
broadband subscription.

  
Do you have questions about a collection claim?  
No problem. Below you will find what to do:

  * **Collection from PayEx** , [contact us](/support/old-kontakta-oss/)
  * **Collection from Intrum** , [log in to Intrum](https://www.intrum.se/kundservice/mina-sidor/)
