# Unlock your operator-locked mobile or tablet

Did you purchase a mobile, router or modem from Telenor before May 1, 2014? Then
it may be locked to us.

Operator unlocking is free of charge. [Contact us](/support/kontakta-oss/) so
we can help you with the unlocking. You do not need to be a Telenor customer today
to unlock your product.

## The following mobiles are not operator-locked to Telenor Sweden

**All mobiles purchased after May 1, 2014**, for example the following iPhones:

  * iPhone 6, 6 Plus, 6s, 6s Plus
  * iPhone 7, 7 Plus
  * iPhone 8, 8 Plus
  * iPhone X

Do you have an iPhone SE, iPhone 4s (8 GB) iPhone 5s or iPhone 5c? Then it is
operator-locked if you purchased it before May 1, 2014, but not if you purchased it
later.

## Frequently asked questions and answers about operator unlocking

  * What is an IMEI number?

The IMEI number is a 15-digit code that is unique to your mobile. It can be
said to be the mobile's personal number.

  * Where can I find the IMEI number?

If you have the mobile nearby, the easiest way is to **enter *#06#** and it will
appear on the display.

You can also find the IMEI number in My Telenor – either here [on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) or in [our
app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx). And on the box and on the
receipt. 15 digits. No more or less.

  
Do you have a modem? Look near the barcode, it is usually stated there.
