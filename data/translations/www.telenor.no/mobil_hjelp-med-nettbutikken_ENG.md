# Help with the Telenor Online Store

In the online store, you can safely and easily order the items you want. On this
page, you will find answers to the most common questions after making a purchase in our online store.

[Buy a phone in the Online Store](/mobiltelefoner/)

## When will my order arrive?

As soon as the item is shipped from the warehouse, you will receive an email with tracking information. Using
the tracking information, you can find out where your package is.

If you have not received an email with tracking information, it is because your package has not
been shipped yet.

## Change your mind about your purchase?

### It's okay to change your mind!

If you don't want the mobile phone you ordered after all, you can send it back to us
within 60 days. The open purchase policy only applies to _purchases of mobile phones_ that
were purchased in Telenor's online store. [Read more about the 60-day open
purchase policy](/privat/nettbutikk/aapent-kjop/).

For other purchases, the general rule of the right of withdrawal applies, which allows
you to cancel within 14 days after you have picked up the package at the post office.

If you do not pick up the package at the post office, it will be returned, and the purchase will be
cancelled.

[Download the cancellation form
(PDF)](https://www.telenor.no/binaries/privat/vilkar/Angreskjema%20og%20skjema%20for%20opplysninger%20om%20angrerett_fa866940-56e7-4180-b570-4399b72758f3.pdf)

  * #### How do I return items?

 **Free Returns  
** If you want to return the item, you naturally have the opportunity to return it to
us and get your money back.

 **  
How free returns work  
** You return the item quickly and easily by using the prepaid return label
that was included in the package.

received it.

Feel free to send it back in the same cardboard box it came in, so you know that the package
will arrive safely.

## Defective or Damaged Equipment

If the equipment you purchased from us is not working properly, it must be sent
to us. How this is done depends on whether the defect is covered by
the warranty terms or not.

Defects covered by the warranty terms are weaknesses in the equipment, and not defects
caused by wear and tear, misuse, or damage incurred after the purchase,
for example, physical damage, water damage, or user error.

If you have insurance with us, physical damage can be covered by an insurance claim. Click on
the link below to read more about how to register an insurance claim.

[Read more about insurance](/privat/mobil/mobiltjenester/mobilforsikring/)

### Make a Complaint about an Item

If you purchased the mobile phone from Telenor, you must first create a service order via
the link below; you _cannot_ go to a Telenor store. After you have registered the service, you will receive a username and password so you can [check the status of your
service](http://net2.emrox.net/stable/net2/customer_access).

The package with the equipment must then be delivered to a post office.  

[Create
service order](https://service.conmodo.com/neworder?key=vaS8Uhw3JBk65PRxSLL5LMI2UPemcGRa)

### Important!

 **Before you deliver your phone for service** , it is important that you turn off
passcode locks and tracking features such as [Find my iPhone](/kundeservice/mobil/din-
mobil/deaktiver-find-my-iphone/), Samsung recovery and Find my device.

If the repair is **not covered by the warranty terms** , you will be informed of how much the repair will cost you. You choose whether or not you want the item to be repaired.

If you do not want the repair, but want the item back, you will still be charged for the troubleshooting that has been done.
