# Choosing Channels and Content in T-We

Your T-We subscription includes 120 points that you can freely use to choose the channels and content you want. Read more [about how the content selector works.](/privat/tv/poengsystem/)

## How to Choose Channels and Content on TV Box II

You can change channels via a separate menu on T-We Box II (Does not apply to premium content)

  1. Press the T-We button on the remote control

  2. Go to the hamburger menu at the top left

  3. Select "Content Selector" (icon of a house)

  4. Find the desired content and press "Add Content"

  5. Go to the shopping cart and Confirm (or Confirm purchase of X number of packages)

  6. The customer (owner) will then receive an SMS with a 6-digit code that must be entered

Problems with the content selector?

If you do not have a correct mobile number registered with us, you will not be able to make the change via the menu on the TV. Your contact number can be changed on My Pages, and the update can take up to 24 hours - you will then be able to complete the desired change.

## How to Choose Channels and Content on My Pages

  1. Log in to [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/)  
(Note! Only the owner of the TV subscription can choose channels and content)

  2. Select **Internet and TV**

  3. Select **Manage** under the TV subscription in question

  4. Select **Change Content**

  5. Add content by pressing the plus icon in the bottom right corner

  6. If you want to remove a package or channel you have already chosen, you can remove it by pressing the green checkmark and then pressing **Remove**

  7. Select **Save** to confirm

[Go directly to "Change Content"](/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-
velger/)

 **Problems logging in to My Pages?**  
Here you will find [help with Telenor ID](/telenor-id/hjelp-med-innlogging/), the solution for logging in to My Pages.

## When Will the Content Be Available?

All channels you choose to add will be available shortly after ordering. Streaming services such as HBO and archives, however, will not be available until up to 2 hours after ordering.

If you want faster access to streaming services and archives, you can restart the T-We box by unplugging and plugging in the power cord or using the on/off switch.

## Initial Setup

We have pre-selected channels and content worth 120 points for you. We call this an initial setup, and you can return to this at any time.

If you are unsure which channels to choose, or have lost track of which content you have exchanged? By resetting to the initial setup, you always have the opportunity to start over with a complete point package. [See which channels are included in the initial setup.](/privat/tv/poengsystem/)

To reset:

  1. Log in to [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/tilbakestill-til-startoppsett/)  
(Note! Only the owner of the TV subscription can choose channels and content)

  2. Select the **TV subscription** in question

  3. Press the **Reset** button to reset the TV setup

## Content Locked for 30 Days?

Some types of content mean that the choice is locked for 30 days. You cannot remove locked content or release points used on this content before 30 days have passed.  
  
You will be notified of this before you save your selection. You can still add or remove other content that is not locked.

## Avoid Double Payment

Some channels are available both via points and as part of a premium package. Some streaming services can also be ordered both with points and directly from the provider.

To avoid paying double for the content, you should be especially aware of the services below.

  * #### TV 2 Sport 1

TV 2 Sport 1 is part of the initial setup, but is also part of the [TV 2 Sport Premium](/privat/tv/ekstra-kanalpakker/tv2-sport-premium/) package. If you want access to the other channels in the package, you must still have the TV 2 Sport Premium package, but you can remove TV 2 Sport 1 from the initial setup and thus release some points.

  * #### Max

Max (formerly called HBO Max/Nordic) can be selected as part of your points. Max then comes as an integrated part of your TV product and is also available in the [T-We App](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-app/).

If you choose Max in your subscription, you will also get access to the Max app. [Read more about how to create a user for the Max app...](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/hbo-max/)

If you have ordered Max directly from the provider, remember to cancel this streaming service before choosing it in the points system, so you don't have to pay double.

  * #### discovery+

discovery+ Entertainment is available as part of the optional content in T-We. If you already have discovery+, you can use the same login on T-We or create a separate account. Remember to cancel the account you no longer use with Discovery.

[Read more about how to activate discovery+ Entertainment.](/kundeservice/tv/velge-kanaler/aktiver-discoveryplus/)

  * #### V Film and Viaplay

V Film/Viaplay is now available as optional content that you can choose into your TV package with points.

 **Note** that Viaplay is not available as an app on your T-We box, but can be downloaded on mobile and tablet. After you have chosen V Film in your TV package on My Pages, you can [register for Viaplay](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/viaplay/) and download the app.

You do not need to re-register if you switch from the Viasat premium package to the points model.

  * #### V Premium

With V Premium you get included the same content that is found in V Sport and V Film & Series.

So if you have chosen V Sport and/or V Film & Series into the points model previously and you now have ordered V Premium, you can now choose to remove V Sport and/or V Film & Series and thus release points for use on other channels or channel packages.
