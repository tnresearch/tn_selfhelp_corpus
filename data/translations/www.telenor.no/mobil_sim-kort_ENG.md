# Help with SIM Cards and eSIM

Here you will find everything you need to know about SIM cards, whether you have questions about
extra SIM cards, activation, ordering, or blocking.

## What is a SIM Card?

A SIM card is a physical card that you place in the device where it will be used.
Today's SIM cards are Multi-SIMs where you can choose between 3 sizes,
depending on the device.

eSIM is an electronic SIM card that can be programmed and activated digitally.
With eSIM, you can connect selected phones, iPads, and smartwatches to
the mobile network or WiFI.

### SIM Card Size

Physical SIM cards can be punched out to all sizes.

**Unsure which size to punch out?** See the specifications for
your mobile phone in the Online Store.

[Go to the Online Store](https://nettbutikk.telenor.no/)

## Order or Activate a SIM Card

Prerequisites for ordering a SIM

You can order a new SIM card on My Pages, in My Telenor, from the chatbot
Telmi, by calling customer service, or by visiting a dealer.

* Only the owner and user of the subscription can order a new SIM card

* When ordering a physical SIM card, the delivery time is normally 6 business days

* A new physical SIM card costs NOK 100 (The price is NOK 199 when purchased in a Telenor store)

* Activating an eSIM requires verification with BankID

* #### Order SIM on My Pages?

    1. [Log in to My Pages](/mitt-telenor/mobil/)

    2. Select the subscription you want to order a SIM card for

    3. Select the device in question under **Devices and SIM**

    4. Press **Order New SIM**

    5. Choose the type of SIM card you want to order - Regular SIM card or eSIM

    6. Press **Go Further**

    7. Check the box to confirm that you accept the terms and conditions and press **Order**

* #### Order SIM in My Telenor App?

    1. Scroll down to **Devices**

    2. Select the desired device

    3. At the bottom, press **Change SIM Card**

    4. Choose **Physical SIM Card or eSIM**

    5. Read the message about BankID on your mobile, and press "I Understand"

    6. Check that the delivery address is correct (when ordering a physical SIM card), and **check the box** to confirm that you accept the terms of purchase

    7. Press **Confirm Purchase**

A physical SIM card will be sent to you by post and normally arrives after 6
business days.

You will find the QR code you need to activate the eSIM under "SIM Card" on
the subscription after a few minutes.
The QR code must be used within 14 days.

* #### Activate New SIM?

You can activate a SIM card on My Pages, [in My
Telenor](https://www.telenor.no/privat/mobil/app/mitt-telenor/) or by
calling customer service

    * The SIM card to be activated cannot be activated on another number

    * The SIM card must be new or activated within the last 6 months.

    * The SIM card will be active within 4 hours, usually within 5-10 minutes. Your old SIM card will be automatically deactivated

**Physical SIM**

    1. [Log in to My Pages](/mitt-telenor/mobil/)

    4. Press **Activate SIM**

## Problems with SIM Activation?

* #### Where do I find the SIM number?

The SIM number is found on the back of the SIM card and on the letter you received with
the SIM card when ordering.

The SIM number starts with 8947 and contains a total of 20 digits.

* #### I get an error message on My Pages or My Telenor

The error message you receive when trying to activate will indicate what is
failing:

    * **Invalid SIM number** - If the SIM number has too few characters or is in the wrong format. Check that you have entered the correct SIM number.

    * **SIM number is already being activated** - The activation is in progress, but not updated. Wait up to 15 minutes and try again.

    * **SIM card is in use on another number** - The SIM card is activated, but not to the number you are trying. It may be that you have activated it to an extra SIM such as Twin or Data SIM.

    * **SIM card has been inactive for too long** - If the SIM card has been inactive for more than 6 months, it cannot be reactivated. Order a new SIM card to solve the problem.

    * **SIM card is already activated** - The SIM card is activated on the number. If you still do not have coverage, you can try turning the mobile device off and on.

If you get other error messages that are not well described, you can [contact
customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) for further help.

The chatbot Telmi can also recognize problems if you try [activation
via chat](javascript:toggleChat\(\)).

* #### I get an error message on my mobile

There may be several reasons why you get an error message about the SIM card on your mobile.
Common error messages are _SIM card missing_, _Invalid SIM_, _Inactive SIM_
and _SIM blocked._

If you experience any of these, we recommend testing the following:

    * Check that the SIM card is placed correctly in the mobile

    * Try to activate the SIM card again - if you suspect that it has not been activated

    * Check if the SIM card works in another phone. (This can help identify whether the problem lies with the phone or the SIM card)

If the problem only applies to one mobile device, the problem may be due to a fault on the mobile
or that [the mobile is operator locked.](/kundeservice/mobil/din-
mobil/operatorlas/)

If the mobile is operator locked, you must contact the supplier you bought the mobile
from to have it opened for other SIM cards.
