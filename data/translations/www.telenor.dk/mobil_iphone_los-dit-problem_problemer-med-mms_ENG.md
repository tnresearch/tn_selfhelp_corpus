# Problems Receiving or Sending MMS

Perhaps you have changed your MMS settings? Follow the instructions below to ensure you have the correct setup.

**Enable MMS**
Select "Settings" > "Messages". Here you can turn "MMS message" on or off.

**Correct Your MMS Settings**
Select "Settings" > "General" > "Mobile" > "Mobile Data Network". In the "MMS" section, the following should be stated:

  * In the APN field, enter telenor (lowercase)
  * The Username field should be empty
  * The Password field should be empty
  * In the MMSC field, enter http://mms.telenor.dk
  * In the MMS proxy field, enter 212.088.064.008:8080
  * Turn your mobile phone off and on

## If You Cannot Send MMS

  * Restart your mobile phone. When you restart your mobile phone, it will automatically correct any errors and search for new software updates. If a software update is available, we recommend that you download it.
  * Are you within data coverage? An internet connection is required to send and receive MMS.
[Check coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/ "Check coverage map")

  * Can you send an MMS to yourself? If you receive your own MMS, the problem likely lies in the recipient's mobile setup.
  * If your mobile phone has just been reactivated after being blocked, it may take some time before you can send/receive MMS as normal.

## If You Are Not Receiving MMS on Your Mobile

  * Check that your mobile phone is set up to be able to receive MMS
  * Your mobile phone was outside of data coverage when the MMS was sent to you
  * Your mobile phone does not have enough space to receive an MMS. Delete any images, apps or other content that is filling up your mobile phone
  * You were abroad and data roaming was turned off on your mobile phone when the MMS was sent
