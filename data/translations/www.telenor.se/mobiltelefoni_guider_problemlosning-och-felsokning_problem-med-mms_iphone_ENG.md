# Problems with MMS

If you can't send or receive MMS with your iPhone, you can often solve the problem yourself. Isn't that nice?

Here we list the most common solutions to problems with sending MMS with iPhone.

  * Have you just gotten a mobile subscription with us? Then you must first send an MMS in order to be able to receive MMS in the future.
  * Check that MMS messages are enabled under _Settings_ > _Messages_.
  * Check that you are connected to the internet via a mobile data network or wifi.
  * Restart your mobile and check if there are any updates to download.
  * Check that your mobile's memory is not full.
  * Check that the APN settings are correct. You do this under _Settings_ > _Mobile Network_. Under the heading Telenor you will find _Mobile data network_. Click there and check the settings under the **heading MMS**.  

The settings should be:  
 **APN:** mms  
 **Username:** Leave blank  
 **Password:** Leave blank  
 **MMSC:**<http://mms>  
 **MMS proxy:** 172.30.253.241:8799  
 **MMS max size:** 2097152  
 **MMS UA Prof URL:** Leave blank
