# Receiving DAB Radio

Here you will find information about the digital radio broadcasting network, and what to do if there are errors with the radio signal.

## What is DAB?

DAB is an abbreviation for Digital Audio Broadcasting, meaning digital radio.

Telenor is responsible for the expansion and operation of the digital radio broadcasting network ([DAB](https://radio.no/om-oss/digitalradio-norge/)) which you can listen to via a radio, whether you are on the road or at home.

Are you experiencing errors with the radio signal?

Are you unable to receive channels from NRK, P4 and Bauer Media on your radio?

Contact us on telephone number **124** to report the error.
