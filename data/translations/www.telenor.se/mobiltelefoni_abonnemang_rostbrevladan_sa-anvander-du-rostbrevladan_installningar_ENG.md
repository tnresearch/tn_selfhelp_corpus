#  Voicemail Settings

Read more below on how to get started with your voicemail
and how to make different settings.

## How to...

Get started with voicemail

To get started with voicemail, dial 888 and follow
the instructions.

Record a personal greeting message

You can change or record a personal greeting message. If you do not
record your own message, a pre-recorded message will be used. To
record a personal message, do the following:  
  
• Dial 888.  
• Select button option 3 to go to Settings  
• Select button option 1 to go to the Change greeting message menu  
• Select button option 1 to record and activate Personal greeting phrase  
• Select button option 4 to choose and activate Telenor's Standard greeting phrase  
• Select 8 to have the greeting phrase you have active read aloud.  
• Press * to exit the menu.

Listen to messages

To listen to messages, dial 888 and press "1" in the main menu.

Change language

To change the language, do the following:

• Call your voicemail on 888.  
• Press 3 to go to the settings menu.  
• Press 5 to go to the menu for choosing a language.  
• Press 1 for Swedish.  
• Press 2 for English.  
• Press * to exit the language selection menu.

## Applies only to you with a subscription:

Adjust the time before voicemail answers

Go to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).
Select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and call forwarding".

Choose the number of rings before the call is forwarded to voicemail.

    * 2 rings corresponds to 5 seconds
    * 3 rings corresponds to 10 seconds
    * 4 rings corresponds to 15 seconds
    * 5 rings corresponds to 20 seconds 
    * 6 rings corresponds to 25 seconds 

Turn off/deactivate

Go to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).
Select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and call forwarding". Slide
the slider to the right so that "Never" is selected.

Reactivate

Go to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).
Select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and call forwarding". Choose
after how many rings you want calls to be forwarded to
voicemail.  
  

Forward to another number

Go to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).
Select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and call forwarding". Check
the box "Forward to another phone number" and fill in the number to
which the calls should be forwarded.

Remove forwarding

If you have a prepaid card, you cannot remove the forwarding.

Go to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).
Select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and call forwarding". Check
the box "To your voicemail".
