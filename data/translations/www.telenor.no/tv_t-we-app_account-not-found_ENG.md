# T-We app/Apple TV - Account not found

Here you can find help if you receive the error message "Account not found" when trying to
log in/use the T-We app on mobile or Apple TV.

With Telenor ID, you can log in with both the phone numbers and email addresses linked to your account. When using WiFi and logging in with
your phone number, we will send you an SMS with a PIN code that you must enter.

When using mobile data, we will automatically find the phone number you are using and
attempt to log you in with it.

If you have a phone number from Telenor, you usually do not have to enter a PIN code,
but if you have a phone number from another provider, we will send an SMS with a
PIN code.

**Account not found** occurs because our systems cannot see that
the mobile number you are trying to log in with is linked to a T-We subscription.

Here are several things you can try to fix the problem:

## 1.

The **OWNER of the T-We subscription** must log in to the T-We app or
[www.twe.no](http://www.twe.no) first and then grant access to all other
mobile numbers in the household that they want to have access. [Here's how to add
access](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-app/bruk/).

If you do not know who owns the T-We subscription, it is the person in your household who has access to manage the TV subscription on
[My Pages](/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/).

The access is displayed with an image like this on My Pages

## 2.

The problem may be that the device you are logging in with is logged in to another
Telenor app (e.g. My Cloud/My Telenor) with **another person's** Telenor
ID. The phone number we then receive is not the one associated with your TV
subscription.

**Here's how you can solve this issue:**

  1. Open a browser window on a PC or Mac, and clear cookies and cache

  2. Log in to [www.twe.no](http://www.twe.no) with the number belonging to the subscription owner

  3. When the owner is logged in to www.t-we.no, they can also see which other mobile numbers have access. Click on “Settings” -> “Users and Profiles”

  4. Check if the mobile number of the device logging in has been granted access. If not, the owner can add the mobile number that should have access

  5. Log out of other apps that use Telenor ID – e.g. My Telenor or My Cloud

  6. Close the T-We app completely

  7. Turn on WiFi on the device and enter one of the numbers the owner has granted access to when you restart the T-We app, and follow the instructions

## 3.

Logging in on Apple TV is done by scanning the QR code displayed on the Apple
TV or by entering the web address provided next to it.

**If you are experiencing problems logging in to Apple TV, you can try the
following:**

  1. Use a device that is already logged in to the T-We App to log in to Apple TV

  2. Use a PC/Mac to log in to Apple TV by entering the web address displayed next to the QR code, and enter the phone number of the person who owns the TV subscription when logging in

  3. If you only have access to one device, and this device cannot log in to Apple TV, you can try connecting this device to WiFi, logging out of other services that use Telenor ID on the device that cannot log in – e.g. My Telenor or My Cloud, and restarting the login on Apple TV.

  4. When prompted to enter a phone number, you must enter the number of the person who owns the TV subscription or log in with the email address and password of that person by clicking “use email” to the right of the login field.
