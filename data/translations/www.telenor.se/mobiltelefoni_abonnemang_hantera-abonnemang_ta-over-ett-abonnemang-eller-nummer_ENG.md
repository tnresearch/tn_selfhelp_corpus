# Taking over a mobile subscription or a mobile number

Do you want to take over a subscription or number belonging to someone else? Then you
have come to the right place!

Start by clicking on what you want to do and follow the instructions.

### I want to:

Take over a private subscription

Do you want to take over the payment responsibility for a subscription and mobile number that is currently owned
and **used** **by another person**?

**Keep in mind that**

  * Both the person taking over and the person transferring must have **BankID**.
  * The process must be completed within **24 hours** after it has been initiated.
  * If it concerns a **family subscription**, the entire subscription is transferred.
  * You who take over the subscription assume the terms and payment responsibility. Therefore, we do a **credit check** on you.

[Take over a subscription – start
here](/link/117dacb18f8344758fac31ace8cf33b0.aspx)

Move a number out of a family subscription

[Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) if you want to break
out a number from a family subscription.

Take over a number belonging to a corporate subscription

Information on how to [transfer numbers between companies and individuals can be found
here.](/link/bd7abcd5685b4aed84be711185b49589.aspx)

Transfer of subscription from an estate

If you want to transfer a subscription that currently belongs to an estate, fill in
the form [Transfer of subscription for
estate](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/GetPdf.aspx?ArticleNumber=TG31188).

**Tip!** Download the form and save it on your computer or mobile before you
fill it in or print it.

You also need to attach a death certificate, which you order via the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket).
Ensure that you as the new owner are included on the death certificate.

Email the certificate and completed form to us via our
[email form,](/link/315e5eb9c6e647d7bab050c041bfc2fe.aspx) or by post
to Telenor, FE 100, 106 56 Stockholm. Mark the envelope with "transfer".

When the transfer is completed, you will receive confirmation via SMS.

Please note that any installments on hardware such as mobile phones, tablets,
Apple TV, and similar, will be finally invoiced upon transfer.

Good to know about transfer

  * Transferring mobile subscriptions between private individuals is free of charge
  * A subscription can be transferred no earlier than 30 days after it was taken out
  * The person taking over the subscription also takes over its terms and payment responsibility
  * It is not possible to transfer prepaid cards
