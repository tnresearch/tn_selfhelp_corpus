# Help with login

Having trouble logging in with Telenor ID? Here you'll find help with the most
common problems.

We have updated Telenor ID and made the solution even more secure. The update
means that you continue to log in with a password and a one-time code, but
instead of using your phone number, you now use your email address. We also
use two-factor authentication, which means you will receive a one-time code
sent to your email address that you should use when you log in.

If you are experiencing problems logging in with Telenor ID, we have compiled
the most common questions and answers below.

## Common questions about login

  * Why can't I log in with my phone number?

You can no longer log in with Telenor ID using your phone number. You can only
log in using your email address. If you had a Telenor ID previously, your
email address should already be registered.

  * I get information that my phone number is already in use when I have logged in

The reason for this message is probably that your phone number is linked to an
email address other than the one you are trying to use when you log in.

If the problem persists, please [contact
customer service](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) for help.

  * I have forgotten my password

You create a new password by clicking on "Log in with Telenor ID" and then on
"Change password". If you are the owner of the subscription (payer), you can
always log in with BankID.

  * The emails you send don't arrive, why?

    * Start by checking the spam folder. If the email is not there, you can try
      sending the code again.
    * If you are a business customer who has registered an email address from
      your company, check with your IT department that **noreply@signin.telenor.se**
      is whitelisted.
    * Do you have multiple email addresses? If so, check if the email has
      arrived at any of them.
    * Still not working? [Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx)
      and we will help you.

  * What do I do if I have forgotten which email address is linked to my Telenor ID?

If you no longer have access to your email address, please [contact
customer service](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx).
Remember - if you are the owner of a subscription (payer), you can always log
in via BankID.

  * What do I do when I can't see my details/missing information when I log in via Telenor ID?

    * If you are the owner (payer) of the subscription: make sure you have
      verified yourself as the owner of the account via BankID.
    * You probably have multiple email addresses linked to your Telenor ID.
      [Contact customer service](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx)
      for help.
