# Antenna NR7302 - Wireless Broadband

Here you will find help and guides for setting up Wireless Broadband with a
fixed outdoor antenna - Zyxel NR7302

## About Wireless Broadband

Wireless Broadband is a secure and future-proof broadband for your home
over a world-class mobile network. Wireless Broadband is available in two versions - a
new version with an outdoor antenna, receiver or antenna adapter inside and a standard
wireless router, and an older version with its own type of router.  
If you have our previous version of the outdoor antenna, you can read more about it here -
[Zyxel NR7101](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/tbb-antenne-nr7101/).
If you have the oldest version, you can read more about it here - [Huawei
B818](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/huawei-b818/).

## Simple Steps to Get Online

The procedure below is shown with our [WiFi Router
II](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter-2/), but you can follow
the same steps with other routers.

  * #### Connect to antenna adapter (PoE)

Connect network cable between LAN port in PoE/antenna adapter and blue WAN
port in WiFi Router II.

The antenna adapter looks like a standard black power adapter, with two outputs
labeled LAN and POE. The cable in the PoE output goes out to the antenna itself,
while LAN is the one that should be used for further cabling inside.

  * #### Connect network cable to router

If you want to connect a computer, T-We box or other devices with
cable, connect network cable between the equipment and the yellow LAN ports on
the router.

  * #### Connect power supply and turn on router

Connect power supply to the router and turn it on.

Wireless Broadband requires power via a standard power grid at the address (230V) for
connecting the antenna/router. Any errors or defects in the service as a
result of you choosing to connect the antenna to a low-voltage system with
a 230V converter are your responsibility. If you still, and at your own risk, choose to
use a low-voltage system, it must be suitable for fine electronics.

  * #### Wait for the top light to turn green

This may take some time. Do not turn off the WiFi Router while this is in progress.  
When the top light is green, the router is connected to the internet.

Is the top light not green? Check that all cables are connected correctly

If you do not find any errors, call us at 915 09 000.

  * #### Connect to WiFi

Follow the instructions under WiFi Router to receive network name and password for
your wireless network.

    *  **Send an SMS with the keyword “wifi” to 2000 with the eight-digit code that is on the label.  
** Turn the router upside down and look at the label that is stuck on the bottom. Remember
space between “wifi” and the code.

      * Have you not received a response to SMS after a couple of minutes? Check that all cables are connected correctly, and that the router has power (bottom light should be steadily white). If you do not find any errors, call us at 915 09 000.

    *  **You will receive a new SMS with network name and password.  
** Go to settings on your mobile phone to connect to the
wireless network you received via SMS.

**Tip:** For you to easily get all your devices on the new wireless
network, you should change the network name and password to what you had before.
This is easily done by going to My WiFi:
[wifi.telenor.no](http://wifi.telenor.no/) when you are on your home network.
