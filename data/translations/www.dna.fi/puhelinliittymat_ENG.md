## Lost PUK Code?

You can find your PUK code in your subscription details in the My DNA app, by logging in to the dna.fi website, or by logging in to the chatbot.

[ Log In ](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen) [ Read More
](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/puhelinliittymat/puk-koodi)

## Using your Subscription Abroad

When you are going on a trip, take into account the restrictions, usage limits, and additional charges related to using subscriptions abroad.

[ Read More ](/tuki/puhelinliittymat/kaytto-ulkomailla)

## Transferring your Subscription to Another Person

Transferring a subscription agreement to another person is possible through number surrender. You can surrender the number by visiting a DNA Store and through the My DNA app, but receiving the number is only possible at DNA Stores.

[ Read Instructions ](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/puhelinliittymat/numeronsiirto)
