# Webshop Fraud

Webshop fraud, or online shop fraud, occurs when you are scammed when buying something from an online store. Scammers may have created a fake webshop and either sell you counterfeit goods or outright steal your money by selling you something you never receive.

## How can I recognize a fake webshop:

  * Look for the e-mark, which is a Danish certification given to secure webshops.
  * Check the webshop's reviews on google.dk, trustpilot.dk, etc., where you can read how other customers have experienced shopping there.
  * Does the webshop look reasonably professional? Are there, for example, many spelling errors or texts that appear to be machine-translated? This is often a sign of fraud.
  * Use your common sense. If you find a webshop selling goods for far less than they cost elsewhere, it may be a sign that it's too good to be true.

## What do I do if I have been a victim of webshop fraud?

If you have a mobile subscription with Telenor and have been scammed by a webshop, you are automatically covered by [Telenor Tryghedspakke (NetSikker)](/shop/telenor-tryghedspakke/). This means that Telenor's skilled partners at HELP Forsikring are ready to help you. You can get help, for example, if you do not receive the item you purchased, receive a wrong or defective item, or if the item is delayed.

Call 39 90 09 00, or send an email to kontakt@helpforsikring.dk, and tell them what the case is about and what you need help with. Read more about Telenor Tryghedspakke [here](/shop/telenor-tryghedspakke/).

If you do not have a mobile subscription with Telenor, you can report it to the police and possibly contact your bank to block your card. In some cases, the bank may help you get your money back.

You may know Telenor Tryghedspakke as NetSikker, but because it is much more than security on the internet, we have changed the name. Telenor Tryghedspakke provides the same good coverage as NetSikker – and more.
