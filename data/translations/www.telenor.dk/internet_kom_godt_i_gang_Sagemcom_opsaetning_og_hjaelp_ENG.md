## Written guide

**How to connect your router**

  1. Connect the COAX cable to the router by screwing it on gently
  2. Connect the other end of the cable to the cable TV socket by screwing it on gently. Note that you may need to use the included adapter
  3. Plug the router into the power outlet - this is done as follows: turn off the power outlet, mount the cable in the router, mount it in the power outlet and finally turn on the power outlet

**How to connect your devices to the internet wirelessly**

  1. Find your network name (SSID 2.4G/SSID 5G) and password (WPA passphrase) by looking at the back of your router  
Search for wireless networks on your device and select your network name (SSID
2.4G/SSID 5G)

  2. Enter the password (WPA passphrase)

**How to connect to the internet with a cable**

  1. Plug a network cable into one of the router's yellow ports
  2. Plug the other end of the network cable into your device  
  
 **Activate your internet at MitWifi.dk**

Before you are fully ready to get online, you need to activate your internet. Read here how to do it:

  1. Start by connecting the router via WiFi or cable when the router needs to be activated
  2. Go to the activation page by entering **MitWifi.dk** in your browser, e.g. Google Chrome or Safari
  3. Enter the unique account number and activation code. You can find both of these in the communication you have received from us
  4. Your router will now be activated, and you are ready to go online

Enjoy your new internet!

## What do the diodes (lights) on the router mean

WPS

Blinks blue when WPS is activated. When this light blinks, it is possible to connect to WiFi via WPS.

WiFi

Shows whether the 2.4 and 5 Ghz signal is on. The light is constantly on when the signal is on and blinks with traffic on the wireless network.  
Note: If bandsteering is active, only one WiFi name will be visible when searching for a signal.

Eth (network icon with the small squares)

Indicates whether there is connected active equipment in one of the four Ethernet ports on the router. The light blinks with traffic.

Internet (globe)

Indicates whether an internet connection has been established.  
 **Light:  
** **Solid green:** when an IP connection has been established  
 **Blinking:** when there is traffic on the internet  
 **Solid red:** if there is an error on the connection  
 **Off:** if there is no IP connection

Upstream (arrow up)

Shows whether the line has established an upstream connection. Note that there must be both upstream and downstream connection before the internet connection works.  
The light will blink while connecting and will light up solid green when the connection is established. Note: the light blinks synchronously with Downstream during update.

Downstream (arrow down)

Shows whether the line has established a downstream connection. Note that there must be both upstream and downstream connection before the internet connection works.  
The light will blink while connecting and will light up solid green when the connection is established. Note: the light blinks synchronously with Downstream during update.

Power

Shows whether the router is on.

## More settings

How to reset your router

  1. Gently push the end of a paperclip (or similar) into the small hole next to Reset - hold the button down for about 10 seconds, and release the button again
  2. When the button is released, all lights will go out, and the status will start blinking shortly after
  3. The router may take several minutes to start up and will blink in status while doing so **Note** that the startup may in some cases take up to 45 minutes, as all of the router's frequencies are scanned again
  4. When the router is set up, you can log in with the router's original network name and password and reinstall your personal settings

How to change the router's name and password

You can change the name and password of your wireless network by going to **mitwifi.dk**. Here you must use the username and activation code that appears in the order confirmation you have received from us.

When you are logged in to **mitwifi.dk**, there will be a section on the front page called **Wireless network**. Here you click on **Edit**, and then enter the desired name and password.

How to set up Port Forwarding

  1. Open your browser and type: **http://192.168.0.1/**  
 **Username:** admin  
 **Password:** Enter the password for your router (the code is on the side of the router after "Admin Password")  
If you have created a personal code, use that instead. Then click on **Log in**

  2. Click on **Advanced** in the menu on the left. Click on **Forwarding** and then on **Add**.
  3. In the **Custom Service** field, write the name of your Port forward
  4. In the **Server Type** field, select **IPv4**, and in **Server IP-Address** write the IP address of the device you want to Port Forward to
  5. In the **External Port Start** column, enter the ip-start port number on the WAN side
  6. In the **External Port End** column, enter the ip-end port number on the WAN side
  7. In the **Protocol** column, select **TCP** or **UDP**. If in doubt, choose **TCP/UDP**
  8. In the **Internal Port Start** column, select **Start-port** on the local network
  9. In the **Internal Port End** column, select **End-port** on the local network - finally click on **Apply/Save**
