# What happens to subscriptions upon death

Here we tell you what you need to know about how subscriptions are terminated or transferred
in the event of death.

## How it works:

  * **When we are informed that a customer has passed away, we send an information letter to the address of the estate.** This applies to mobile, TV, and broadband subscriptions alike.
  * **All of the customer's subscriptions will be terminated approximately 45 days after we are informed of their death.** The time may vary slightly as the information is retrieved once a week.
  * **If you, as a surviving relative, want the subscription to be terminated**, you do not need to do anything. **If you want the subscription to be transferred** to someone else, follow the instructions below.

###

Transfer of subscription from estate

If you want to transfer a subscription that currently belongs to an estate, fill in
the form [Transfer of subscription from
estate.](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/Pdf/TG31188.pdf)

You also need to attach a death certificate, which you order via the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket).
Ensure that you, as the transferee, are included on the death certificate.

Email the certificate and completed form to us via our
[email form,](/link/ba47672336314095bd2c64e83ad431b5.aspx) or by post
to Telenor, FE 110, 106 56 Stockholm. Mark the envelope with "transfer".

Once the transfer is complete, you will receive confirmation via SMS.

Please note that any installments on hardware such as mobile phones, tablets,
Apple TV, and similar, will be finally invoiced upon transfer.

**If you have any questions, please feel free to [contact us here.](/support/old-
kontakta-oss/)**
