# Choose the right top-up

Prepaid cards can be used for many things. In mobile phones, for mobile broadband
or to connect your smart devices such as a trail camera or
the alarm in the summer cottage.

Read more about our different top-up options below and choose the one that suits
you best.

[Top up your prepaid card >](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-
kontantkort/#/products)

Good to know:

If you have previously made a top-up with a fixed price and international minutes at the same
time, you received a validity period for that top-up. If at the next
time you have international minutes remaining and only make a top-up for a fixed price
the validity period of your international minutes will not be extended.
