# Low Speed on Mobile Broadband

Mobile Broadband runs over the mobile data network. This means that the speed
can fluctuate depending on where you are, for example if you have updated your
computer / tablet or if you have multiple online programs open at the same time.

Your speed will automatically decrease if you have used your data allowance for this
month – just as you may experience with your mobile phone if you are at the end of the month and have
used your data package.

Wait about 30 seconds and go online again

  3. Restart your tablet / computer and go online again

## What can block your signal?

**Hills and trees:** If you are in a hollow, or surrounded by hills or
trees, the network signal may have difficulty reaching your mobile.

**Weather and wind:** The mobile signal is affected by the season. The best
mobile signal day is actually a frosty winter day. While wet leaves, rain and snow
will dampen the signal.

**Buildings:** If there is a large concrete building between you and the nearest transmitter,
the walls will lower the signal strength. And if you are inside a building with thick walls, you can
also experience a poorer signal.
