# Consumption Max on Content Services

We have set a consumption max on your subscription so you can more easily keep track of your consumption of content services such as games, parking, bus tickets and donations. The service is activated automatically the first time you purchase a content service in a billing period. You can cancel the service at [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/abonnement/detaljer/mobil/).

You will find the total amount under "Content Services" on your mobile bill.

**Your consumption max is set to:**

  * 750 DKK per day
  * 1,500 DKK per week
  * 2,220 DKK per billing period

When you have purchased content services for 250 DKK, you will receive an SMS. If you reach your maximum, your mobile will automatically be blocked from purchasing more content services. Your consumption max is reset when your new billing period starts.

Please note that it may take 24 hours from when you donate money to a collection, for example, until we have registered the amount.

Do you want to see how much you have spent in this period? Check [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/abonnement/forbrug/).

If you want to set a specific limit for all consumption in addition to the subscription, you must use [usage control](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/opsatning/forbrugskontrol/).
