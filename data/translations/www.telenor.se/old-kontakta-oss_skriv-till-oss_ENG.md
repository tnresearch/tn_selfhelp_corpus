# Write to us

You'll always get the fastest response by calling us at **[020-222
222.](tel:020222222)** Prefer to write? Here's how:

  
Use the messaging function in **[My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/)**. We can
answer all types of questions there.

The messaging function in My Telenor is great when you have specific questions about your
subscriptions, but are unable to call. All you need to do is
log in and we'll help you there.

[Log in and use the messaging function](/mitt-telenor/)

  
**Tip!** In [My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/) (web and app) you can also
find answers to many questions yourself and manage your subscriptions. You can among
other things:

  * See your invoice, both upcoming and previous.
  * On your invoice you can also [find your customer number](/support/vanliga-fragor/abonnemang/var-hittar-jag-mitt-kundnummer/).
  * Buy more data, keep track of data usage in the family and share data between different users.
  * Make changes to your subscription, add services, see your agreements and much more.
