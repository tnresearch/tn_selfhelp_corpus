# Connecting WiFi Router II

If you have our first version of the WiFi Router, [you can find step-by-step
installation instructions here](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-
ruter/koble-til/)

## Video Guide

Below you can watch a YouTube video that shows and explains how to connect
your WiFi Router II. If you want to see a step-by-step guide with text and
images, just scroll past the video.

Before you begin, the fiber box, cable modem, or antenna adapter must be connected
to the internet. What kind of equipment you have depends on which technology you have
coming into your house.

### 1. Connect the network cable

The cable should be connected between the blue input labeled WAN on the router, and
the fiber box, modem, or antenna adapter.

### 2. Connect any other network cables you may have to the router (LAN)

### 3. Connect the new power supply and turn on the router

### 6. Follow the instructions from the received SMS and press the WPS button on the router

### 7. You will receive a new SMS with the network name and password

Go to the settings on your mobile phone to connect to the wireless
network you received via SMS.

**Tip:** For the easiest way to get all your devices on the new wireless
network, you should change the network name and password to what you had before.
This is easily done by going to [My WiFi](https://wifi.telenor.no) when
you are on your home network.
