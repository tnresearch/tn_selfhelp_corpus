# 7 tips: How to protect the content of your mobile

Your mobile phone contains photos, stored passwords, card details, email accounts
and other information that is not only invaluable to you – but also to a
potential thief. But how good are you at protecting it? Here we tell you what
you should do as a preventative measure.

## 1. Always use a screen lock

The most important thing to protect the content of your phone – make sure that
a **code, fingerprint or facial recognition** is required to use it. Otherwise, the thief has free rein. You choose the screen lock type in your mobile's
**Settings**.

##

## 2. Activate "Find My iPhone" or "Find My Device"

If your mobile is stolen, or you lose it, you can **find your mobile**
using the **”Find My iPhone”** or **”Find My Device”** function. You
can see your mobile on a map, lock it remotely or make it ring at
maximum volume for five minutes – even if it is set to silent mode.

**Android:**

  1. Go to **Settings** on your mobile.
  2. Tap on **Security** (or similar) > **Find My Device** > **Activate**.

**iPhone:**

  1. Go to **Settings** on your mobile.
  2. Click on **your name** at the top.
  3. Go to **Find My** > **iPhone** > **Activate**.

##

## 3. Change the PIN code for the SIM card

The PIN code is the code that **protects the SIM card itself** from being used by other people to use your subscription. If the SIM card is moved to another phone,
the same PIN code applies.

When you get your new SIM card, the code is probably set to **0000**. You should
of course change this.

**Android:**

  1. Go to **Settings** on your mobile.
  2. Select **Lock screen** > **Screen lock type** and then follow the instructions.

**iPhone:**

  1. Go to **Settings** on your mobile.
  2. Select **Cellular** and then **SIM PIN**.
  3. Select the slider to the right of SIM PIN and then follow the instructions.

##

## 4. Automatic backup of your photos

Do you also store thousands of pictures on your mobile? Irreplaceable memories of the children,
vacations and important holidays. With **storage services** such as Google Photos,
iCloud, OneDrive and Dropbox, your **photos and videos** are automatically
copied to a so-called cloud storage. This means that you rent storage space on
the provider's servers. Then your pictures don't have to take up space on your mobile, and
you still have them even if your phone disappears.

**Google Photos (Android)** and **iCloud (iPhone)** are ready to be activated in
your mobile. Other storage services – with different functions and at different
price points – can be easily downloaded as an app on your mobile. Often you are offered
free storage up to a certain limit, then you have to buy more space.

**Google Photos:**

  1. Open the **Google Photos app** on your Android phone.
  2. Log in to your Google account.
  3. Tap on your **profile picture** or **initial** in the upper right corner.
  4. Tap on **Photo settings** > **Backup**.
  5. **Activate**.

**iCloud:**

  1. Tap on **Settings** > **[your name]** > **iCloud** > **Photos**.
  2. Turn on **iCloud Photos**.

##

## 5. Activate backup

In addition to photos and videos, you should also back up other important data
from your mobile – for example, settings, contacts, SMS, app data and documents.
Then you can easily transfer all content to a new mobile.

**Android:**

  1. Go to **Settings** > **Google** > **Backup**.
  2. Activate **Backup in Google One/Drive**. Below you can see which data will be backed up.

**iPhone:**

  1. Go to **Settings** > **[your name]** > **iCloud**.
  2. Select iCloud **backup** > **Activate**.

##

## 6. Write down your IMEI number

All mobiles have a unique 15-digit so-called **IMEI number**, which is used
to identify mobiles in mobile networks. The IMEI number also works as a theft protection, so that stolen phones cannot be used. You provide the number at
**police report** and when you want to **block your subscription.**

You can find your IMEI number most easily by entering ***#06#** on your mobile and
it will be displayed on the screen. You can also find the IMEI number in **My Telenor** –
either on [the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in our app. And on the box the mobile came in.

##

## 7. Write contact information on the lock screen

You can add a message or **phone number to the lock screen** so that
someone who finds the phone can contact you, or someone you know, even though
the keypad lock is activated.

Another tip is to download the **ICE app – In Case of Emergency**
(Android). It creates a message on the lock screen that contains necessary
(but not sensitive) information about you and your contacts in an
**emergency**. Then rescue personnel can quickly reach the right relative.
The corresponding app for iPhone is called **Health** and is already installed on your
mobile.

**Android:**

  1. Go to **Settings** > **Display**.
  2. Tap on **Lock screen** > **Add text to lock screen** or **Contact information**.
  3. Write your message and save.

**iPhone:**

With iPhone, you can only add a message to the lock screen _after_ you
have lost your mobile. Then do this:

  1. Log in (with your Apple ID) to **Find Devices** by going to[ icloud.com/find.](https://icloud.com/find)
  2. Click on **All Devices** > **iPhone** > **Lost Mode**. This requires that you have activated the **Find My iPhone** function _before_ it disappears.
  3. Enter a **message** or **contact number** that you want to be displayed on the lock screen.
