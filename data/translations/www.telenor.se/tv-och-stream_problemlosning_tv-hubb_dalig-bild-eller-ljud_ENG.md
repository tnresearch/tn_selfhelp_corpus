# Poor picture or sound

Are you experiencing a pixelated picture or crackling sound? Follow these
steps to resolve the issue.

  * ### 

Restart the TV hub, TV, and router by unplugging the power cable.

  * ### 

Check the distance between the router and the TV hub.  
Try moving them closer together.

  * ### 

Perform a speed test.

Ideally, download a speed test app on the TV hub from the Google Play
Store.  
Otherwise, you can use another device (phone, tablet, or computer connected
to the same Wi-Fi) as close to the TV hub as possible.

If you get a download speed of less than 50 Mbit/s, contact your
broadband provider.

If it still doesn't work, [contact us](/support/kontakta-oss/) and we will
help you.
