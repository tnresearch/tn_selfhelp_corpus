## Setup for Online e-mail for e-mail programs on mobile and PC/Mac

See step-by-step guides for e-mail setup, or changing
settings for the program you want to use.

Account settings for Online e-mail

The settings are the same regardless of which e-mail program you use. Appearance
may vary from program to program.

Choose IMAP setup, and fill in incoming and outgoing server with associated
ports.

 **Account Name** : Optional

 **Username** : Your e-mail address (what comes before "@online.no", max 8
characters).

 **Password** : Your password

 **Incoming server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 993 w/SSL

 **Outgoing server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 465 w/SSL (You can also use 587 w/SSL or TLS)

## Manage e-mail account

On [Telenor My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mine-sider/) you can change
password, add, remove or change your e-mail account.

  * #### How to log in to My Pages

    1. Open <https://www.telenor.no/mine-sider/> on a computer (not mobile)

    2. Log in or create a TelenorID (You do not need to be a Telenor customer) 

    3. Select **Go to e-mail** under **Services**

  * #### Forgot or change password

    1. Open [Telenor My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mine-sider/)

    2. Log in or create a TelenorID (You do not need to be a Telenor customer) 

    3. Select **Go to e-mail** under **Services**

    4. Select **Change password** on the desired account and follow the instructions 

  * #### Add or change mobile number

    1. Open [Telenor My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mine-sider/)

    2. Log in or create a TelenorID (You do not need to be a Telenor customer) 

    3. Select **Go to e-mail** under **Services**

    4. Select **Change mobile number** on the desired account 

    5. Enter the mobile number and **Save** **mobile number**

  * #### Create a new Online e-mail account

    1. Open [Telenor My Pages ](https://www.telenor.no/mine-sider/)

    2. Log in or create a TelenorID (You do not need to be a Telenor customer) 

    3. Select **Go to e-mail** under services 

    4. Press **Create e-mail account**

    5. Select who you want to create the e-mail account for 

    6. Choose a desired e-mail address 

    7. Enter the mobile number to be linked to the e-mail 

    8. Choose the desired storage amount 

    9. The e-mail is created and you will receive an SMS when it is ready 

    10. Go to or Reload My Pages 

    11. Select **Change password** and enter the desired password 

    12. Press **Log in to e-mail account**

  * #### Cancel Online e-mail account

    1. Open [Telenor My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mine-sider/)

    2. Log in or create a TelenorID (You do not need to be a Telenor customer) 

    3. Select **Go to e-mail** under **Services**

    4. Select **Settings** on the e-mail account you want to delete 

    5. Select **Delete account** to confirm 

    6. Press **Yes, I am sure** to cancel   
  
Note: _When you delete your account, all your content will also be deleted_
