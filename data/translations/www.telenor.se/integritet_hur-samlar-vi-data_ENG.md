# How we collect and use your data

Data you provide

Without you necessarily realizing it, data is automatically created when you become a customer and then use our services.  Simply put, you leave digital traces that we have access to.

Examples of when you provide data:

  * When you become a customer, create accounts for services, My Telenor or our apps.  
  

  * When you contact customer service, a store, or our Facebook page.  
  

  * When you use our services and make calls, send SMS, browse the internet, stream TV, or use additional services such as email or SAFE. This can also be location-specific data created when you move with connected devices (based on your IP address).   
  

  * When you visit telenor.se and we receive data via cookies.

Data we receive or purchase

Data that we receive or purchase from external partners is called third-party data. They obtain their data from public registers or from user information from cookies or pixels.

Examples of data from external parties:

  * If you see a Telenor ad, we can find out many details. Which ad you saw and where, whether you saw it on mobile or computer, if you've seen it before, if you clicked, and various IDs for you (Cookie ID, Mobile Advertising ID, and Partner ID).  
  

  * Information from public registers such as the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket). This can include housing type and housing ID, marital status, family type, income information, credit information, purchasing power, life stage, urbanization (e.g., large city, town, etc.) and education level.  
  

  * We collaborate with advertising networks. From them, we can receive both data from their cookies and pixels as well as detailed information when you have seen one of our ads. This can include the time you visited a website, network and geolocation data based on your IP address, demographic information about your household, the device you used (model, type, alias and known aliases for the device) and supplier-specific IDs on your device. But also for categorization of a visited website within the advertising network where they have placed their cookies and to map patterns and calculate which more devices can be linked to your household.

How your data is used in our communication

We want to be relevant. Not disturb you unnecessarily. That's why we appreciate the opportunity to customize our communication with you.

When communication is customized based on our data, it is called profiling. This means, simply put, that customers are sorted into different groups based on anonymized data governed by various factors such as purchase history, age, or gender.

#### Necessary cookies

Always active

Needed for the website to function securely and correctly. Therefore, they cannot be turned off. Necessary cookies enable basic functions, so that you can, for example, navigate between pages or access secure areas of the website.

#### Functional cookies

Functional cookies

These cookies allow the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They can be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of these services may not work as intended.

Information about cookies‎

#### Statistical and Analytical

Statistical and Analytical

These cookies give us information about how you use our site and allow us to improve the user experience and improve our website. They can be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, we may have limited insight into how our website is used and therefore have difficulty improving your experience.

Information about cookies‎

#### Marketing

Marketing

These cookies are set on our website by our advertising partners. They can be used by these companies to create a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other websites. They do not directly store personal information, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less targeted ads.
