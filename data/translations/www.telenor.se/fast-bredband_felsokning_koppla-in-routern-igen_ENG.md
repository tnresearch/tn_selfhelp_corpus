# Reconnect the router

Reconnect the router to the broadband outlet and then perform a
factory reset.

### How to perform a factory reset

Take a paperclip and insert it into the hole labeled Reset on the back of the router. Press
the button until the router restarts and wait approximately five minutes.

**Note:** All your settings will be lost and revert to
factory defaults, such as changes to the name and password of your
wireless network.
