# Help with Zyxel NR5101

Here you will find help and guides for your 5G router for Mobile Broadband.

## Simple steps to get online

  * #### Insert SIM card

If you have a large-sized SIM card, you need to break out a Micro-SIM.
The power adapter must not be connected when you insert the SIM card.

You can find the SIM card opening on the underside of the router.

  * #### Connect to power

Connect the included power adapter to the back of the router and to a power source.
Then press the ON button.

  * #### Connect to WiFi

 **Windows**

    1. Left-click on the wireless network icon at the bottom next to the clock and date

    2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

    3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

 **Mac**

    1. Press the Airport icon for wireless network

    2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

    3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

  * #### Complete the wizard

    1. Make sure you are connected to the router's wireless network (step 3)

    2. Open the browser

    3. Enter 192.168.1.1 in the address field of the browser and follow the wizard

 **PIN code**

In the wizard, you will be asked to enter a PIN code. This can be found in
the letter you received with the SIM card for the router.

If you cannot find the PIN code, you can [retrieve the PUK code on My
Pages](https://www.telenor.no/privat/minesider/web/mobil/). Once you have entered
the PUK code, you will be asked to create a new PIN code.

**How to retrieve the PUK code**

    1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/privat/minesider/web/mobil/)

    2. Select the subscription in question

    3. Under **Devices and SIM**, select the SIM card in question

    4. Press **Show PUK code**.

  * #### How do I reset the router?

If you are experiencing problems connecting to the internet, it can often help to
reset the router. This resets the router to the settings it had when you received it.

    1. Find something thin, such as a paperclip or pin

    2. Insert the object into the reset hole on the underside of the router, and hold it in for 20 seconds

    3. The router will turn off and on automatically, and you can connect to the network again. 

When the router is reset, the network name and password will be what is on
the sticker on the back of the router. Follow the wizard (step 3) to set up
the router again.

  * #### Can I connect an external antenna to the router?

The router has 2 SMA antenna connectors that support external antennas, but only for
5G signals. The router's external antenna connectors do not support 4G signals.

Remember to slide the switch from INT to EXT on the back of the router if you connect
an external 5G antenna.

  * #### Where can I find the latest software?

When new software is available, the router is automatically upgraded to ensure security and stability requirements. Device and SIM card information is
required to perform the software update.

The upgrade happens at night, so it is important not to turn off the router.

 **How to see which software your router has:**

    1. Open a new browser, such as Google Chrome, on a computer that is connected to the router. Type 192.168.1.1 in the address field, press Enter

    2. Type admin in the username field. The password is the same as your original wireless password. You can find it on the sticker on the back of the router after WIFI. **Note:** If you have changed the password to the menu yourself, enter what you have chosen.

    3. On the home page under **System Info** you will see the installed version of the software in the **Firmware Version** field 

### **  
How to upgrade the software yourself:**

    1. Open a new browser, such as Google Chrome, on a computer that is connected to the router. Type **192.168.1.1** in the address field, press Enter

    2. Type admin in the username field. The password is the same as your original wireless password. You can find it on the sticker on the underside of the router after WIFI.  
 **Note:** If you have changed the password to the menu yourself, enter what you
have chosen.

    3. Press the Menu icon at the top right

    4. Go to Maintenance -> Firmware Upgrade

    5. Press Check for Latest Firmware Now

    6. Confirm that you want to be upgraded
