# My WiFi - Managing Your Router

Here you will find information on how to log in to your router to
change settings on your wireless network.

[Go to wifi.telenor.no](https://wifi.telenor.no)

Log in to My WiFi with Telenor ID!

You can log in to the router's web interface via wifi.telenor.no, and
manage your router from anywhere - From the office, the cabin, or in the
back of a taxi!  
As long as your router is online, you can connect to your home network.

Logging in to your router: This is what the login screen looks like.

## How to log in with Telenor ID

This can only be performed by the owner of the internet subscription.

  1. Open a browser and go to the address wifi.telenor.no

  2. Press "Log in with Telenor ID" and follow the steps.

If you have an internet subscription at multiple addresses, you will need to select which
address it applies to after you have logged in.

[Help with logging in with Telenor ID](/telenor-id/hjelp-med-innlogging/)

## How to log in with your admin password

As long as you are connected to your home network and have access to the
administrator password, you can complete this process.

  1. Open a browser and go to the address wifi.telenor.no

  2. Press "Log in with your administrator password"

  3. Enter your password

  4. If you have not changed to a self-selected password, enter the code that is under the router after the text admin: (consists of letters and numbers) and log in. If you have a Zyxel P8702N, the code is after the text WPA2 (consists of 13 letters).

  * #### Is this your first time logging in?

The first time you log in, you must check the box to confirm that you accept the terms of use and
privacy policy. When you log in with the default password, you will be taken to
your account page. Here you can change the language, administrator password, or go further
to the homepage.

  * #### Add phone number and email address for recovery

We need your mobile number or email address so that you can
recover the administrator password - if you forget what you changed it
to.

You can add this under "My Account". You can find My Account in the upper right corner of
the image when you are on the homepage.

Forgot admin password?

If you have changed your admin password, this will still apply after a
reset of the router or if you get a new router. If you have forgotten what it is,
it is still possible to change it.

  1. Open a browser and go to the address wifi.telenor.no

  2. Select Forgot password

  3. Enter mobile number or email address

  4. Select Send reset link

  5. You will now receive an SMS or email with further information

If you no longer have the email address or mobile number that was
registered during creation, you must [contact Customer
Service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/).
