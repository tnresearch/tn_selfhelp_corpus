## Change Email Settings

If you are experiencing problems sending or receiving email on iPhone or
iPad, you should check if you need to change any settings. Here we show you
how to change the outgoing email settings, but you can also follow
the guide to check other settings in the email program.

 **Note!** The guide may differ from your phone if you have a different version
of the operating system installed.

Account settings for Online Email

 **Account name** : Optional

 **Username** : Your email address (what is before "@online.no", max 8 characters)

 **Password** : Your password. [Have you forgotten
your password](/kundeservice/e-post/glemt-passord/index.page)?

 **Incoming server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 993 with SSL

 **Outgoing server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 465 with SSL (You can also use 587 with SSL or TLS)

Old setup with incoming server "imap.online.no" and outgoing server
"smtp.online.no" will also work.
