## Enabling mobile connectivity on a Huawei Watch smartwatch

Start by opening the details of your phone subscription in the My DNA app (under the Services menu) and navigate to the "SIM card and PUK codes" view.

Make sure the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service is visible under "Parallel card".
Select "Activate eSIM".

If you haven't yet subscribed to the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service, subscribe to it in the My DNA
app, the DNA Store or DNA's customer service: 0800 550 044 (Mon-Fri
9–20, Sat 10–16). The number is free of charge.

Copy the eSIM activation code (use the copy button) to paste it
next into your phone's Huawei Health app.

If you don't already have the app, download it from the App Gallery app store.

Open the Huawei Health app on your phone, select your device and go to eSIM management.

Select and activate the eSIM and paste the eSIM activation code

**Done!** Now you can make and receive calls on your smartwatch and
use the internet.
