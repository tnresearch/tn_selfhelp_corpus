# Prices for calling, texting and sending MMS from Sweden to abroad with
prepaid card

When you are in Sweden and call a foreign subscriber, you are making an
international call.

In the price lists below, you will see what it costs to call and send SMS and
MMS to the different zones*. Don't miss that our fixed data packages of 40 GB,
100 GB or 150 GB include international minutes to Europe and North America. If
you often call the Middle East, Europe or North America, you will get the best
price if you choose the fixed data package
[Hello](/handla/kontantkort/hello-kontantkort/).

Please note that when you call friends who are abroad with their Swedish
mobile phones, you pay for calls within Sweden. However, the person you are
calling will need to pay to receive the call. Information about [how much it
costs can be found here](/roaming/).

## Frequently asked questions about prices to abroad

  * Why can't I call Syria anymore?

Due to the fact that the EU has imposed sanctions on SyriaTel, it is no longer
possible to call, text or send MMS to SyriaTel customers. SyriaTel customers
cannot either call, text or send MMS to Telenor customers. We understand that
this may create problems for you as a customer and apologize for it, but at the
same time it is important for Telenor to comply with laws and regulations. The
measure does not affect traffic to and from Syria's other operator, MTN.
