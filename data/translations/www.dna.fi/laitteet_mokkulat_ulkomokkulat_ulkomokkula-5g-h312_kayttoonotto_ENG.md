## Getting Started

## Installing the Outdoor Modem

The Outdoor Modem 5G H312 is compatible with a Nano-SIM card. Start the installation by inserting the card into the card slot at the bottom of the outdoor modem. Make sure you insert the card in the position shown in the example picture below.

Attach the Ethernet flat cable to the network connection next to the SIM card slot. You do not need to tighten the cable shield to the Outdoor Modem at this stage.

Attach the free end of the Ethernet flat cable to the "WAN & POWER" connection of the power supply. Also, attach the power cord to the power supply and connect it to the power outlet.

Turn on the power supply using the button next to the power cord. The Outdoor Modem will automatically start up and search for available 5G and 4G mobile networks.  
  
As a sign of a successful connection, the 5G/4G signal indicator on the side of the Outdoor Modem should remain steadily lit. The other indicator next to it indicates the signal strength: green (strong), yellow (moderate), or red (weak).

## Determining the Location of the Outdoor Modem

It is recommended to choose the location of the DNA Outdoor Modem 5G H312 based on the signal strength. You can easily determine the strength using a phone or computer.

## Testing the Signal Strength

On an Android phone

1. Scan the QR code on the Outdoor Modem's nameplate with your phone and install the Huawei AI Life app.

2. Open the app, scan the QR code on the nameplate again, and connect to the Outdoor Modem's WiFi.

3. Measure the signal strength according to the instructions in the app and find the optimal location.

The Outdoor Modem can be attached to either a wall or a pole so that it is located under the protection angle (45 degrees) of the lightning rod.

On an iPhone or computer

1. Find the Outdoor Modem's WiFi and connect. You can find the password on the Outdoor Modem's nameplate.

If you are using a computer, you can alternatively connect it to the Outdoor Modem using a separate indoor router. Connect your computer to the router's LAN port with an Ethernet cable, and the Outdoor Modem's power supply LAN port to the router's WAN port with another Ethernet cable.

2. Open a web browser and log in to the Outdoor Modem's management page. You can find the IP address to enter in the browser's address bar, as well as the required password, on the Outdoor Modem's nameplate.

3. Change the WiFi name, password, and login password according to the recommendations. After making the changes, log in to the Outdoor Modem's WiFi again.

4. Log in to the Outdoor Modem's management page in the web browser with your new password. On the home page, select **Check location signal**.

5. Measure the signal strength according to the instructions in the app and find the optimal location.

The Outdoor Modem can be attached to either a wall or a pole so that it is located under the protection angle (45 degrees) of the lightning rod.

## Attaching the Outdoor Modem

The DNA Outdoor Modem 5G H312 can be attached to either a wall or a pole.

The Outdoor Modem must be in a vertical position to protect it from water damage.

The Ethernet flat cable must be attached to the Outdoor Modem so that its lowest point hangs slightly lower than the passage through which the cable runs indoors. This prevents water from flowing along the cable into the Outdoor Modem or into the home.

## Ethernet Flat Cable Feed-Through

The Ethernet flat cable can be fed through from outside to inside either through the window frame or by drilling a hole in the wall. If you run the cable through the window, make sure the window frame is protected by a soft rubber strip. Close the window carefully so that the cable is not damaged by excessive pressure.

## Connecting the Outdoor Modem to a WiFi Router

Complete the setup by connecting the Outdoor Modem to your indoor WiFi router. First, attach the Ethernet flat cable you have fed through to the WAN & POWER connection of the power supply. Then, connect a separate Ethernet flat cable from the LAN connection of the power supply to the WAN connection of your router.

Once the Outdoor Modem is connected to the internet, tighten the cable cover to the bottom of the modem.

Now you can connect devices to the internet through the router either wirelessly via WiFi or using a network cable.
