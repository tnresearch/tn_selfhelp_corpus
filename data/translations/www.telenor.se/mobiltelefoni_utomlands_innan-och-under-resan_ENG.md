## Frequently asked questions about what applies abroad

  * What applies when I travel within the EU/EEA?

When you travel in [countries included in
EU/EEA](/link/47f42c4a02df4e3c8ea6d8a800c339a5.aspx) you can surf, call and
mms at the same cost as at home. This applies to our subscriptions as well as
to our prepaid cards.

If you have a prepaid card, you can use all the surfing you have loaded on.

With our subscriptions, you can use all your surfing on your trip within the
EU/EEA. If you have unlimited surfing, you can use a maximum of 100 GB/month.

Are you going to be away for a long time and know that you will use your
subscription more abroad than in Sweden during this period? Then you should
read more in our [special terms and conditions.](/link/750c93522f4e4c5cb65e1efb9d7c9518.aspx)

  * How much of my surfing allowance can I use abroad?

You can use your entire surfing allowance within the EU/EEA as well as in
Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar (Burma), USA, Switzerland, United Kingdom
(including Gibraltar), Turkey, Andorra, Serbia and Montenegro. If you have
unlimited surfing, you can use 100 GB.

  * Does my prepaid card work outside of Sweden?

Our prepaid cards work in most countries. If you travel within the EU/EEA, your
prepaid card works just like at home. In order for your prepaid card to work
abroad, you must first have made a call with it in Sweden.

[Read more about what applies in the country you are visiting
here](/link/a728a2eee9b24f87867517e059303cae.aspx).

  * What does it cost to use a prepaid card abroad?

If you travel within the EU/EEA, it costs the same as at home. If you travel
outside the EU/EEA [you can see here](/link/a728a2eee9b24f87867517e059303cae.aspx)
what it costs. If you travel outside the EU/EEA, you must load up with
Extrasaldo. In order for your prepaid card to work abroad, you must first have
made a call with it in Sweden.

  * In which countries are surfing, calls and sms/mms included?

Calls, sms/mms and all or parts of your surfing are included in [the countries
within the EU/EEA](/link/47f42c4a02df4e3c8ea6d8a800c339a5.aspx). Prices for [calls
from Sweden to other countries can be found
here](/link/6149e35098254a5e945467a22d4f5872.aspx).

In our subscriptions, surfing is also included in Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar
(Burma), USA, Turkey, Switzerland, United Kingdom (including Gibraltar),
Andorra, Serbia and Montenegro.

**Tip!** Log in to the app [My
Telenor](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx) and you will find
everything about your subscription.
