# Activating Internet via Cable TV Outlet (COAX)

Once you have mounted your router, you are ready to activate your new
internet connection. Follow these steps:

  1. Find the activation code in your order confirmation
  2. Connect to your new router - either via WiFi or cable
  3. Go to [www.mitwifi.dk](https://mitwifi.dk/#/login), and fill in the fields to activate your internet (you will find the information in the email you received from us with the confirmed online date)

Congratulations on your connection.
