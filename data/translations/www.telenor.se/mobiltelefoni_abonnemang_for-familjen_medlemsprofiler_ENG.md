# What you can do with Telenor Family in My Telenor

In a family, everyone has their own role, and the same applies to your Telenor Family.

You can all log in to My Telenor (either [here on the web](/mitt-telenor/)
or [in the app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/)), but you will be able to
see and do different things.

You, who subscribed and therefore are responsible for payment for the
subscription, are the **owner** and can make more changes to the subscription
than the other users, whom we call **members**.

Below you will see which functions you as the owner or member can use in Telenor
Family.

###

Owner

As the owner of the main subscription, you can administer the various members. This
means that you can:

  * add and remove services and settings for yourself and the rest of the family through cost control, for example **block calls and SMS to abroad** or **block purchases in apps, games and SMS services**
  * view invoices
  * distribute data
  * purchase extra data
  * see all data usage, both the total, each individual member's and your own

In order for you as the owner to access all your services, you need to
verify your ownership of the subscription. [You can read more about how it works
here.](/link/0044777ae98a4461b3616bdf3cb5f4c5.aspx#legitimera)

Member

As a member, you can, among other things, see your own data usage and the family's total
usage.

You can also **view settings** for your subscription, for example block
calls and SMS when you are abroad, but only the owner can **change
the settings**.
