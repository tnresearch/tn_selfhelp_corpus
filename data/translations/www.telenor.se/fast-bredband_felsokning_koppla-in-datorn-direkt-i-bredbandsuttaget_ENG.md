# Connect your computer directly to the broadband outlet

To rule out that the problem lies with your computer, connect it directly
to the broadband outlet with a network cable. Or to the service distributor if you
have one.

  
After that, you can test your surfing speed at
[bredbandskollen.se](http://www.bredbandskollen.se).

Keep in mind that there are other reasons why your computer might be slow, for
example:

  * It is old
  * Drivers, operating system or antivirus program are not updated
  * It has too little free memory

[Read more about what can make your computer slow](/support/fast-broadband/via-
fiber/troubleshooting/problems-with-speed/)
