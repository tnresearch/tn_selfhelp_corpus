# Several ways to top up your prepaid card

Is your balance low again? Don't worry, there are many ways to top up
your prepaid card.

## [In the Telenor Ladda app](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/)

Top up quickly and easily directly from your mobile. In the app you can also start
[subscribing to top-ups](/support/mobiltelefoni/kontantkort/ladda-och-
saldo/prenumerera-pa-laddning/), so your balance is automatically topped up every
month. The app is available for iOS on the App Store, and for Android on Google
Play.

## At [ladda.telenor.se](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-kontantkort/)

Enter your number and choose the top-up you want.

## With a voucher

You can top up your prepaid card using a voucher in **four different
ways**.

  * [Top up here on the **web**](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-kontantkort/#voucher)
  * **Call** [220](tel:220) from the mobile phone the prepaid card is in and follow the instructions
  * **Enter** *120*vouchercode# in your mobile phone. End with the call button
  * **Text message** LADDA followed by the voucher code (12 digits) to number 220

Vouchers can be found in most grocery stores, gas stations and of course in
our own stores.

  
[Here you will find information about all the different top-ups](/handla/kontantkort/)

## Top-ups for calls abroad

###

EU/EEA countries – same as at home

If you have chosen a Fixed Price top-up (e.g. 10 GB or Hello), you call,
text and mms at the same cost as at home when you travel within the EU/EEA. Turn
on data roaming on your phone, then just continue using your mobile
as usual.

Countries outside the EU/EEA – top up with Variable/Balance

If you travel outside the EU/EEA, you need to [top up with
Variable/Balance](/link/5775b1740ff94087bf80394b7a19e43e.aspx) to be able to
use your prepaid card. You top up your prepaid card with Variable/Balance, then that's the amount you call and surf with on your trip. Variable/Balance is separate
from what you call with on a daily basis (Fixed Price). When you get home, you call and
surf with your usual plan again.

Note: Telenor prepaid cards do not work in all countries, for example the
French territories of Réunion and Saint Martin. [Here you can double-check if
your prepaid card works in the country you are going to travel
to](/link/a728a2eee9b24f87867517e059303cae.aspx).  
  
[Prices abroad](/link/e21a5c6d3f464947abcb39ef5446fd32.aspx)

## Frequently asked questions about topping up

  * Why can't I top up my prepaid card?

Since February 1, 2023, all prepaid cards must be **registered** in order to
be topped up. This is a consequence of the law change that came into force on
August 1, 2022.

[Register your prepaid card](/link/9d9b1dd3829a415580e98ad6af78f872.aspx)  

Problems topping up the card can also be due to

    * the card is no longer **valid**. It needs to be topped up at least once a year, otherwise it will be terminated. In the [Telenor Ladda](/link/8ad148929ae541328599af3f428082a6.aspx) app you can see if your prepaid card is still active.
    * your credit card is not **open for online purchases**. Change the setting with the card issuer, or try another way to pay, such as Swish.

  * What prepaid card top-ups are available?

You can choose between Telenor Fixed Price (e.g. the Hello top-up), Telenor
Variable and the add-on Telenor Surf for extra data.  
  
[More about our
prepaid card top-ups](/link/b1b895cef44f46aeb5407c054ab92f40.aspx)

  * Which top-ups can I subscribe to?

You can subscribe to all top-ups that are valid for 31 days. Not to
half-year or annual top-ups.
