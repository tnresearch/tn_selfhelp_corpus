## The invoice looks different than usual

In some cases, your invoice may look different than you are used to.
This can happen in connection with a change, order or termination and
has consequences for the period you are invoiced for. Below you will find an
explanation of what the various changes mean for your invoice. Tip from
us: Feel free to have the invoice in front of you while you check out the suggestions below.

### First invoice

As a new customer, the first invoice may be higher than expected. This is because
monthly price is invoiced in advance. Depending on the date the subscription was
created, your first invoice will therefore apply **from the day you became a customer and
some time into the future.** Usually this is one or two months ahead.

The next invoice you receive will only apply to one month or a
billing period.

### Invoice after changes

When your subscription is changed, the next invoice will include a settlement
that addresses the change. Since you pay fixed prices in advance, you will get
a negative amount for what you have paid forward in time, and a positive amount for
the change in the same period.

The difference is what you pay or get back. **NB!** In some cases
the changes may appear on different invoices.

### Invoice after termination

When you terminate, fees that are invoiced beyond the notice period
will be refunded.

If you have multiple subscriptions with us, and terminate one of them, you will get
money back on the next invoice. If you have multiple subscriptions and terminate all,
a final invoice will be sent for the account.

The final invoice will be sent electronically if you have [eInvoice on
the account](/kundeservice/faktura/avtalegiro-og-efaktura/).

## Common questions and answers about the invoice

  * #### Are you a new customer?

As a new customer, the first invoice may be higher than expected. This is due to
usually the monthly price is invoiced in advance, and any one-time costs.

    * Depending on the date the subscription was created, your first invoice will therefore apply **from the day you became a customer and some time into the future.** Usually this is one or two months ahead. 

    * There may also be setup fees and shipping for any equipment if this has been ordered

The next invoice you receive will only apply to one month or a
billing period, unless changes are made, where fixed prices are invoiced
in advance and usage is billed in arrears.

  * #### I haven't changed my subscription, but it looks like it on the invoice

 **What could have happened:**  
Sometimes Telenor changes your subscription to be able to offer you the best
subscription with the best benefits. Sometimes we clean up expired
subscriptions or similar, but still try to ensure that you get a good
subscription with the right services.

This is always notified at least one month before the change occurs. The notification usually comes via e-mail, but it can also come via SMS.

 **If you have not received a notification** :

    * Check your inbox, and possibly the spam folder, to see if the notification is there

    * Check if the contact information is correct with us - You can find and possibly update it on [My Pages](/mitt-telenor/innstillinger/)

 **Invoice explanation:**  
When changing a subscription, the difference from when the subscription was changed
and until the end of the billing period will be shown on the next invoice. This is in addition to
normal monthly price. Therefore, the invoice may be higher than you are used to.

  * #### What are 'Fixed prices'?

Fixed prices are a combined item on your invoice that shows what you pay in
total price for your subscription(s), as well as any services such as
extra SIM cards or SAFE.

  * #### What are 'Security services'?

We offer several security services to make your everyday life safer and
easier. Some of these are included in your subscription, such as Nettslett which is included in all private mobile subscriptions, or Nettsvern
which is included in both private broadband and mobile subscriptions. Others are paid
a fixed monthly fee for, such as SAFE.

Here you can read more about [our Security Services.](/sikkerhet/alle-produkter/)

  * #### Have you used your mobile to pay for services/content services?

 **What are content services?**  
If you have paid for parking, bought a bus ticket or newspaper subscription, or
donated to the TV campaign with your mobile phone, there is a good chance you have used
Strex. The purchases you make are charged to your mobile bill.  

 **What is Strex?**  
Strex is owned by the Norwegian mobile operators and is a super easy solution for
fast and secure payment via mobile bill. You only need your mobile phone -
no bank or card, just your mobile, to pay.

If you prefer that purchases are charged to your bank or credit card,
you can change your payment method by logging in to
[strex.no/minside](https://strex.no/min-side/). There you also get a full overview
of your purchases, regardless of whether you choose to charge the mobile bill or
bank/credit card.

 **Purchase in app?**  
7004 is purchases in the Apple App Store, and 7001 is purchases from Google Play.

 **Disagree with the invoiced amount?  
** Complaints and general inquiries about a content service should preferably be directed
directly to the provider of the content service. You can find contact information on
[strex.no/minside](https://strex.no/min-side/).

You can also complain to Strex (the mobile operators' payment service) by using
the contact form on <https://strex.no/klageskjema>. If you need help or
guidance from us, you can [contact customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-
oss/).

  * #### Have you been invoiced for use abroad, on a plane, ship or offshore?

Be aware that if you are in the border area between one or more countries you may experience being connected to the network in the neighboring country. The first time
you are connected to a network in a new country, you will receive a welcome message with
price information via SMS. Read the message you receive and familiarize yourself with
the content.

To prevent unwanted roaming in another network, it is recommended that you turn off
"data roaming" on your device. By "roaming" we mean that your device
is connected to another provider's network than Telenor's network.

For a complete overview, you can check [Prices abroad](/privat/mobil/utlandet/)

    * For plane, offshore and ship with coverage from Telenor Maritime/Tampnet, see at the bottom of the country overview list (in Abroad)

    * Separate prices apply for [special numbers](/kundeservice/mobil/spesialnummer/) and use outside the EU/EEA (search for country in the list)

  * #### Have you bought a new mobile with a Split agreement?

    * When purchasing the phone, you will receive a receipt for the purchase

    * Receipt for purchases made on telenor.no appears as an invoice to kr 0,- in your invoice overview on My Pages

    * The first installment of the repayment will be on the next invoice from Telenor

    1.  **Purchase of goods in the online store**

      * Here you will find the device and the amount for the phone itself. In this example it is: "Apple iPhone 14 128GB, starlight - IMEI 3500xxxxxxxxxxx" - 9.999,00kr

      * Trade-in bonus from Apple: This is a deduction Apple offers everyone who delivers their old iPhone when purchasing a new phone

    2.  **Advance payment**

      * CARD *****: Here is the amount the customer has paid for the phone at the time of purchase

    3.  **Trade-in**

      * Trade-in of equipment: In addition to Apple's deduction, the customer receives a deduction from Telenor with trade-in of the phone. The amount will vary depending on the condition of the phone delivered

    4.  **Repayment**

      * Transferred to repayment: Here is the remaining amount that is distributed over either 24 or 36 months. repayment.

  * #### Are you wondering how to see the repayment installments on the Split agreement?

    * To see which installment you are paying, look at the text "Installment X of 24"

    * This example is the first invoice after the purchase is completed
