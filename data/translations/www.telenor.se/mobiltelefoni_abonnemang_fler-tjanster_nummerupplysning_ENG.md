# Telenor's directory assistance

Best florist, cheapest car repair shop, or most mischievous babysitter? Our
directory assistance is open 24/7.

Telenor's directory assistance helps you find the names, phone numbers, postal,
email and web addresses you are looking for. You choose whether you want the
information sent to you via SMS or email. Of course, we can also
connect your call.

## Ask directory assistance

Call or send an SMS to 118 228.

If you are abroad, call +46708118228 instead. We are open 24/7.

The call costs 47.50 SEK + your usual call rate.  
The SMS costs 23.75 SEK + your usual call rate.

## Numbers outside of Sweden

Do you need to get a phone number for someone who lives or operates their
business abroad? We are happy to help! Call or send an SMS to 118 229.

If you are abroad, call +46708118229. We are open 24/7.

The call costs 62.50 SEK + your usual call rate.  
If you are abroad, your usual call rate will be the same as [the one you have in
that country](/roaming/). It is not possible to SMS directory assistance when you are
abroad.
