# Contract period and notice period

Here you can find out what notice period and any contract period applies to your mobile subscription.

## Contract period

As a Telenor customer, you can choose to tie your subscription and thus get a discount or something else nice, such as double data.

### Unsure if your subscription is tied or not?

Log in to My Telenor and check! Either here [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in [our app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/). Keep in mind that different users in the subscription may have different contract periods.

### If you want to change something during the contract period

If your mobile subscription is tied, you can **upgrade to more, but not less, data**. Even if you change the amount of data, you retain the same contract period, it is not extended if you get more data.

## 
Notice period

The notice period for mobile subscriptions is one month.

You can find your agreement in My Telenor. Log in either here [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in [the app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/).
