# Check your data roaming settings

To be able to surf abroad, you need to activate data roaming. It's a breeze! Check below:

[How to activate roaming on an
iPhone](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/andra-installningar/valj-
mobil/iphone/aktivera-inaktivera-roaming/)  
  
[How to activate roaming on a mobile from Samsung, Sony or
Xiaomi](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/andra-installningar/valj-
mobil/android/aktivera-inaktivera-roaming/)

### Do you have a mobile broadband connection?

  1. Make sure your subscription is open for roaming. If you have a modem: see the menu in the client. If you have a wifi dongle, see the menu in the router.
  2. Make sure the router/modem/tablet is open for roaming.
  3. In some clients/routers, it may be necessary to manually connect when roaming and confirm that you accept it.
