## Getting Started

## Installing the Modem

The modem uses a standard Mini-SIM card, which is the largest card size. The SIM card comes off without much force when you press it from the back of the SIM card. If you remove a SIM card of the wrong size, visit a DNA Store to exchange it for a new SIM card.

The SIM card is installed under the cover on the front of the modem. Push the cover away from the USB port. Install the SIM card into the modem with the metal part facing down and finally close the cover.

Registering to the DNA network takes a few minutes. If the connection fails to open, call DNA's [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot#asiakaspalvelu).

The internet connection is available when the power light and the network status indicator light (in a color other than red) are lit on the modem.

## Enabling Wireless Network (Wi-Fi)

**1. Connect the modem to your computer.**  
Connect the modem to a USB port on your computer.

**2. Install the modem.**  
The modem installation starts automatically. If this does not happen, click the installation icon in the My Computer window or on the Desktop. When the installation is complete, your web browser will automatically open the modem's management page. If your SIM card has an activated PIN code, enter it.

You can return to the management page by typing http://192.168.0.1 into the browser's address field. On the management page, you can change or disable your PIN code and change other modem settings.

**3. Start using the internet.**  
The web browser is open and you can start using the internet immediately.

**4. Disconnecting the internet connection.**  
When you want to go offline, disconnect the modem from your computer. The internet connection will remain as long as the modem is connected to the computer. When you want to connect to the internet again, simply connect the modem to your computer.
