# View and pay bills

In My Telenor, you can view and pay all your bills from Telenor. Under
”Bills” you will find an overview of all your bills, and you can also
keep track of any usage beyond your subscription.

On these pages, you can change the payment method for each individual account. This is
useful if, for example, you have a new payment card.

If you have purchased your phone with a smart plan, this will also be shown here.
You will find both an overview of how many months are left on the
given agreement, and how much is to be paid off.

If you have questions about your bills, you can find answers to the most
frequently asked questions [here](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/).
