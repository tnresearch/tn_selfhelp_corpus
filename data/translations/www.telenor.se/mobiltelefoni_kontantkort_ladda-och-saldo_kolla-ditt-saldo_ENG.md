# Check your balance

Here's how to check the balance and data allowance for your prepaid card:

  * In the [Telenor Ladda app](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/)
  * Log in to your Telenor Ladda account at [ladda.telenor.se](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-kontantkort/)
  * You can also dial *121# and press the call button on your mobile.

## Low balance?

[Read more about how to top up your prepaid card.](/support/mobiltelefoni/kontantkort/ladda-och-saldo/ladda-kontantkort/)

## Frequently asked questions about balance

  * The bank charged me for a top-up, but my prepaid card wasn't credited

Most likely, a timeout occurred during the purchase. First, check your balance to make sure the prepaid card hasn't been credited. You can find the balance in the [Telenor Ladda app](/link/8ad148929ae541328599af3f428082a6.aspx).

If the card hasn't been credited but money has been charged, it's a reservation from the bank. The money will be credited back to your account. Depending on your bank, it may take different amounts of time. The bank usually cannot release the reservation.
