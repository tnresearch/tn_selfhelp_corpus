## Cancellation of Wireless Broadband with or without TV

The notice period is until the end of the current month.

Wireless Broadband is a separate **broadband subscription with signals over the
mobile network - with an outdoor antenna**. If it concerns broadband on a fixed network, via
fiber or cable, [use this form.](/kundeservice/oppsigelse/tv-og-
bredband/)

## Important things to consider before cancelling TV or broadband

  * The notice period applies from the day you submit the form

  * If the cancellation is due to moving, have you checked what is offered at the new address?

  * Do you have a subscription with a commitment that may incur a cancellation fee?

  * Do you have TV or broadband equipment that needs to be returned?

  * Do you have an email address with Telenor that you want to keep?

 **We remind you of all the benefits you have with Telenor:**

  * [Always WiFi](/privat/internett/alltid-wifi/)

  * [Always On Net Guarantee](/privat/internett/bredband/alltid-pa-nett-garanti/)

  * [Net Protection+](/nettvern-pluss/)

 **If you are unsure, please feel free to call customer service on 915 09 000, and we will help you.**
