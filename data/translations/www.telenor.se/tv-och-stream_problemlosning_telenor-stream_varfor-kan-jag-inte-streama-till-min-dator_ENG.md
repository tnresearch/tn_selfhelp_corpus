# Why can't I stream to my computer?

  * ### Service Disruption

Is the streaming service affected by a service disruption? [See current service disruptions
here.](/support/driftinformation/driftstorningar-pa-tv--och-bredbandsnatet/
"Service Disruption")

  * ### Distance

Try reducing the distance between the router and the computer. Or connect with a cable.

  * ### Update

Do you have the latest version of your web browser, such as Google Chrome or
Safari? Update it if not. [Read more about our system requirements here.](/support/tv-och-
stream/kom-igang/dator/ "Settings")

  * ### Surf Speed

Check if you have a download speed of at least 6 Mbit/s. You can do this on
[Bredbandskollen.se](http://www.Bredbandskollen.se "Bredbandskollen").

  * ### Other Devices

Try streaming on another device, such as your mobile phone. This will tell you
if the problem is with your computer or your connection.
