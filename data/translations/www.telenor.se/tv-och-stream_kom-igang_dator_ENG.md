# On Your Computer

Want to watch an episode of your favorite series during your lunch break? No problem if you have Telenor Stream! Here's how.

Getting Started

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (link opens in a new window).
  2. Go to [stream.telenor.se](https://stream.telenor.se/#/home)
  3. Start watching!

Good to know

These browsers work best for Telenor Stream

  * Google Chrome
  * Microsoft Edge
  * Firefox
  * Apple Safari

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * There are missing channels when I log in to Telenor Stream – why?

If you can see some channels on your TV but not in Telenor Stream, it's because we haven't been granted permission to distribute them there.

  * Why are entire seasons and episodes of some programs missing?

In Telenor Stream, you'll find programs from both play services (such as Viaplay Film & Series) and from channels (such as SVT). Programs from the channels can be watched when they are broadcast, just like on "regular TV," and in many cases up to seven days afterwards. Then they disappear. But the programs from the play services can be watched at any time, and that's why you can see all episodes and all seasons of a series from Viaplay, but only the latest episode of På spåret (a Swedish TV show).

  * I'm waiting for my TV box, can I use Telenor Stream in the meantime?

It depends. If the start date for your TV service has passed, you can activate Telenor Stream even if you haven't received the TV box. But if the start date is further in the future, you must wait until it has passed.

  * How do I change my login details for Telenor Stream?

You can change both your username and password for Telenor Stream in [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * Can I stream to multiple devices simultaneously?

Yes, you can stream on up to two devices simultaneously on your subscription.
