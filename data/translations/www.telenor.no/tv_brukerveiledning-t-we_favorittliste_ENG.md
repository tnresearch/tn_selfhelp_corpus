# T-We Favorites List

With favorites lists on T-We, you can easily switch between all channels and the ones you watch most. Here's how.

[Switch Between Favorites Lists](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/favorittliste/#bytt)[Remove or Change Favorites Lists](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/favorittliste/#endre)[Favorite Channels in the T-We App](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/favorittliste/#favorittkanal)[Videos About Favorites Lists](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/favorittliste/#video)

## Switch Between Favorites Lists and All Channels

You can switch between your channel lists while watching TV.

  1. Press ☰ or the OPTIONS button on the remote control to bring up the options window.

  2. Press the right arrow key to select Lists.

  3. Select the channel list you want by pressing the up or down arrow keys.

  4. Press OK to confirm.

### How many channel lists can I have?

You can have one favorites list per [T-We profile](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/profiler/).

Switch between channel lists: Use the arrow keys to switch between all channels and TV favorites.

## Remove or Change Favorites Lists in T-We

It is not possible to completely remove favorites lists on T-We, but you can remove the channels you have added to the favorites list so that it no longer contains anything.

If you want to make changes, you can both remove, add, and change the order of the channels you have in your T-We favorites list.

 **Here's how:**

  1. Select ☰ at the top left in the menu.

  2. Select Favorites Lists.

  3. Press OK on the channel you want to do something with under TV Favorites.

    * Use the right/left arrow keys to move the channel.

    * Use the "down arrow" on the remote control to remove the channel from the favorites list.

If you want to add a channel again, press OK on the channel under TV Channels. This will add it to the favorites list.

## Select Favorite Channels in the T-We App

  1. Go to **Home**.

  2. Select **TV** at the top of the page.

  3. Scroll down to **All Channels**.

  4. Select **See More**.

  5. Press the heart next to the desired channel.

You can find your favorite channels under **My Content** in the app.
