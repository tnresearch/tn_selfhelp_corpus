## T-We App says I'm not at home

Are you getting a message in the T-We App that you are not at home, even though you are?

If you receive this error message, you can go through the following checklist:

  1. Are you actually connected to the network (and not the guest network)?

  2. Is the T-We box connected and turned on?

  3. Do you have a mesh network or multiple routers?

[More help with home check](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-app/hjemmesjekk/)

## Problems with Max playback in the app

If you are experiencing problems with Max playback in the T-We app, you can try
the following:

  1. Go to the menu (hamburger menu)

  2. Select "SETTINGS"

  3. Select "VIDEO QUALITY"

  4. Turn on "Reduce quality/data usage when connected..." (both options)

Playback should work normally if you reduce the video quality slightly.

## Content on TV box, web and app is different

Most of the content you have selected is available in the app or on
t-we.no. You can watch most channels and archives both at home and while traveling.

Some content may be unavailable if we lack the rights to show
it. You will be notified when you try to play the content.

If you see a **Forbidden icon** in the app, it may be due to the following:

  * You have not selected the content in your TV package.

  * The content is currently only available on your T-We Box and not available on mobile devices or web TV.

We are continuously working to make more content available, even
outside the home.

 **Note** that streaming services such as Netflix and Viaplay are separate apps and not a
part of the T-We app.
