# International Prices Abroad

At Telenor, we cover 99% of Danes' travel destinations with our two attractive
roaming packages, Roam Away and Roam Away+, so you can confidently use your
mobile when traveling around the world. On this page you will find information
about international prices abroad with and without Roam Away and Roam Away+.

## Check if you have Roam Away or Roam Away+

Before you travel abroad, you can check on [My Telenor](/mit-
telenor/abonnement/) if you have Roam Away or Roam Away+ in your
mobile subscription.

With Roam Away and Roam Away+ you can use your mobile as at home in a wide
range of countries. We will send you an SMS when you have used 80% and 100% of
the included data. If you want to use more data than you have in your
subscription, you can purchase extra data packages. Otherwise, the standard
international rates apply – but with automatic Usage Control.
