# Bills

In the My Telenor app, you can view and pay all your bills from Telenor. Under
the "Bills" tab at the bottom of the app, you will find an overview of both paid
bills and bills ready for payment. If you have questions about
your bills, you can [find answers to the most frequently asked questions
here](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/).  
  

The app is structured around five main menus, which you can get help with
here:

  * [My Telenor](/kundeservice/mit-telenor/mit-telenor-i-appen/forside/)
  * [Bills](/kundeservice/mit-telenor/mit-telenor-i-appen/regninger/)
  * [Subscriptions](/kundeservice/mit-telenor/mit-telenor-i-appen/abonnementer/)
