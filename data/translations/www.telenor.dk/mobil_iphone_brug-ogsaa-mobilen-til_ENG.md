# Use Your Mobile as a Hotspot

Follow the step-by-step guide and get online via your mobile in a moment.

When you go online via your mobile, you use data from the amount included
in your mobile subscription. If you often use your mobile as a hotspot, you may
need a subscription with more data?

[See our subscriptions](/shop/abonnementer/abonnementer-til-dig/)  
[Upgrade your subscription on My Telenor](/mit-telenor/abonnement/)

  * ## Tap "Settings"

  * ## Select "Internet Sharing"

  * ## Tap "Internet Sharing" so the button turns green

Follow the instructions, depending on whether you want to connect your device via WiFi,
Bluetooth or USB.
