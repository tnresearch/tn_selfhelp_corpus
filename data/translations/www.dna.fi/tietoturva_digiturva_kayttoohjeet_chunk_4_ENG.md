## VPN - Privacy

Setting up VPN privacy for the first time on a phone or tablet

When you use a VPN connection for the first time, you need to give it the necessary permissions.

1\. Open the DNA Digiturva app and select **VPN Privacy**.

2\. Select **Start** to continue the setup.

3\. Press the **toggle button** to the right of the location.

4\. The app will ask you to accept configurations, these need to be accepted for the VPN to work. Press **Allow**.

5\. Your phone may separately confirm that you grant permission to establish a VPN connection. **Also accept this request.** Example: confirmation on a Samsung phone to create a VPN connection.

6\. When the VPN is enabled, the toggle button on the right is active and below the location it reads "connected".

Setting up VPN privacy for the first time on a computer

When you use a VPN connection for the first time, you need to give it the necessary permissions.

1\. Open the DNA Digiturva app and press **Start** in the VPN Privacy section.

Setting up VPN privacy on a phone or tablet

1\. **Open** the DNA Digiturva app.

2\. Press the **toggle button** next to VPN Privacy.

3\. When the **toggle button is active**, the VPN is on.

4\. You can turn off VPN privacy by pressing the toggle button again. The toggle button will turn gray and instead of the location, the app will tell you it is off.

Setting up VPN privacy on a computer

1\. **Open** the DNA Digiturva app on your computer.

2\. Press **Enable** in the VPN Privacy section.

3\. The VPN is on when you see a green icon next to the VPN Privacy title and the location replaces the "Enable" text.

4\. To turn off the VPN, open the VPN Privacy section and click the **toggle button** so that it becomes inactive.

5\. **Inactive toggle button**, the VPN is off.

Choosing a virtual location on a phone or tablet

1\. **Open** the VPN Privacy section by pressing the section.

2\. Select **All locations**.

3\. **Press** on the location you want to enable.

4\. The **virtual location** you set will now appear under VPN Privacy.

5\. The **selected location** will also be visible on the app's home page.

Choosing a virtual location on a computer

1\. Open DNA Digiturva and select the **VPN Privacy section**.

2\. Select the **location** you want from the Locations list and click on it.

3\. The location will change to your selected **virtual location**.

4\. The location is also visible in the DNA Digiturva **home view**.
