#### Connect a computer to the wireless network

Windows:

  1. Left-click on the wireless network icon at the bottom next to the clock and date
  2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the lid and press 'connect'
  3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the lid, press 'connect'

Mac:  

  1. Press on Airport, the wireless network icon  

  2. Press on the network that matches the network name on the underside of the lid.  

  3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the lid, press 'connect'

#### How do I access the router's menu?

The router has its own menu, where you can, among other things, change the wireless network name
and network key.

 **Here's how to access the router's menu:**

  * Open a new browser, such as Internet Explorer, on a computer that is connected to the router. Type **192.168.1.1** in the address field, press **Enter**.
  * Enter  
 **Password** : admin

Note, if you have changed the password yourself, enter that one.

#### How do I restore factory settings?

If you are experiencing problems connecting to the Internet, it may help to reset
the router.

The network name ( **SSID** ) and network key ( **PWD/WIFI KEY** )
can be found on the underside of the router. You can also find the sticker on the inside
of the cover.

 **Here's how to reset the router manually** :

\- Open the cover on the router.

\- Press a paperclip/needle into the reset hole, and hold for 10 seconds.

\- The router turns off and on, and you can connect to the wireless network on
new.

 **Note** , after you have reset the router, the network name and
network password will be reset.

 **Here's how to restore factory settings via the menu** :

\- Open a new browser, such as Internet Explorer, on a computer
connected to the router. Type **192.168.1.1** in the address field, press
**Enter**.

\- Enter

 **Password** : admin  

\- Click **Settings**

\- Select **System Security** then **Factory Settings**.

\- Press **Restore**

\- Press **Restore** again to confirm.

\- The router turns on automatically, and you can connect again. Note,
after you have reset the router, the network name and network password will be
reset. If you have changed the login password for the router, this will also be
reset.

?

#### How do I change the wireless channel?

If you have a weak signal strength or problems finding the wireless network,
it may help to change the wireless channel.

To change the wireless channel, you must access the router's menu. The computer must
be wirelessly connected to the router to access the menu.

\- Open a new browser, such as Internet Explorer, on a computer
connected to the router. Type **192.168.1.1** in the address field, press
**Enter**.

\- Enter  
 **Password** : admin  

\- Select **Settings**

\- Click **Wi-Fi** , then **Advanced**

\- Select a channel from the drop-down menu under **Frequency (Channel)**.

\- It is recommended to choose **1** , **6** or **11**. Channel
11 may work better than channel 1 at your home, so try it out. Avoid channels
12 or 13 as some network cards cannot connect to these.

\- Press **Apply**. The settings are now saved.

\- If you still experience the same problem, repeat from step 4 and try a
different channel that may work better on your computer.

\- If you cannot find your wireless network, you should restart your PC and
router.

?

#### How do I lock the network to 4G, 3G or 2G?

If the network is unstable, it may help to lock the network to either 4G, 3G or 2G.

 **Here's how to do it:**

  * Open a new browser, such as Internet Explorer, on a computer connected to the router. Type **192.168.1.1** in the address field, press **Enter**.
  * Enter  
 **Password:** admin  

  * Select **Settings** , then **Network**
  * Select **Only 3G** or **Only 2G** from the drop-down menu under **Select** **Network**.
  * Press **Apply**.
