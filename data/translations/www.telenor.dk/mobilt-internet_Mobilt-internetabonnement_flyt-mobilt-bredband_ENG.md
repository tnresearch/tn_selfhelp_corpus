# Mobile Internet Relocation

When you register your change of address with the Civil Registration Office, we are automatically notified. Therefore, you don't need to do anything else when you move.

The Civil Registration Office recommends that you notify them no later than five days after your moving date. And in most municipalities, you can register your move at the earliest four weeks in advance.
