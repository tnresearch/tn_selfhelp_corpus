# How do I find my PUK code?

If you have entered your PIN code incorrectly three times in a row, your mobile
will be automatically locked. To unlock your mobile again, you need the PUK code.

**If you have lost your PUK code, you can find it here:**

  * Easily and quickly find the PUK code on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/) or in the app under [Subscriptions](/mit-telenor/abonnement/) – then click on the phone number for which you need the PUK code
  * [Contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/) and have it sent to you by email
  * Visit one of our [stores](/find-butik/) – remember to bring valid photo identification such as a Danish passport or driver's license

Once you have obtained your PUK code and entered it, your PIN code will also be
reset. You will then be prompted to enter a new 4-digit PIN code – this
is the code you use when, for example, restarting your mobile.
