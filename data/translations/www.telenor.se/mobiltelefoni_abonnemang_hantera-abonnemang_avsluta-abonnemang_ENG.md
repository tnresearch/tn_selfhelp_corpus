# Cancel Mobile Subscription

If you want to change or cancel any of your subscriptions, you need to talk to
us. Contact us and we will help you cancel or find a solution that
suits you!

Cancel subscriptions for **TV**, **broadband**, or **home phone via
broadband**? [Here's how](/support/fast-bredband/annat/avsluta-abonnemang-
pa-tv-bredband-eller-hemtelefoni-via-bredband/)

Here's how to contact us to cancel your mobile services

**Quick and secure!** Call us at [020-222 222](tel:020222222)  
 **Secure!** Write to us via [My Messages in My Telenor](/mitt-
telenor/)  
 **Don't have a Bank-ID?** Use our [contact form](/support/old-
kontakta-oss/skriv-till-oss/)

**Phone hours:**  
Weekdays 8 AM - 6 PM  
  

If you contact us via My Messages in My Telenor or via the contact form, your cancellation date will be the same date we receive your message.

Change or modify in My Telenor!

Is there something in your subscription that you are not happy with? Did you know that you can switch to a mobile subscription with more or less data? If you have a remaining contract period, you can only switch to more data. You can change it yourself in [My Telenor](/mitt-
telenor/). With us, you can also [upgrade your
phone](/handla/tjanster/mobil/change/) after 12 months at no extra cost.

Manage subscriptions in case of death

When a person has passed away, the subscription is automatically cancelled. If you want the subscription to be transferred, you need to notify us.

[Here you can read about how everything works](/support/det-har-hander-med-abonnemang-
vid-dodsfall/)

## Frequently Asked Questions about Cancelling a Subscription

  * What is the notice period for my subscription?

### Mobile Telephony

The notice period for mobile subscriptions is one month.

You can find your agreement in My Telenor - either [here on
the web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) or in the
[app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

### Broadband and TV

Different subscriptions have different notice periods. What applies to your subscription can be found in the campaign terms and conditions that you received when you ordered the subscription. From May 1, 2014, the notice period for all newly signed and upgraded subscriptions is 1 month. If you signed your subscription before May 1, 2014, the notice period is normally 3 calendar months.

  * How do I move my mobile number from Telenor to another operator?

Do you want to transfer your number from us to another operator? To avoid unexpected costs, we always recommend that you contact us before transferring your number.

**Here's how it works:**

    1. [Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) and we will check for any remaining contract period and ensure you are aware of any potential costs, such as if you have remaining installments on your phone. Here you will also find out if a final invoice will be sent to you and what amount it will be.
    2. If everything is in order, you can then contact the new operator and ask them to carry out the number transfer.
    3. Once the number transfer is complete, we will cancel your subscription with us.

  * How do I find out my contract period?

You can see any contract period in My Telenor [here on
the web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx). If you have a mobile subscription, you can also see it in our [app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

  
[Information about **notice period** can be found
here](/link/8ea002ab5007459a87a3d2f6bf776146.aspx)

  * Can I pause my subscription?

It is not possible to pause mobile subscriptions or broadband subscriptions. But if you have mobile broadband, you can switch the data amount to 1GB whenever you want, for example when you leave the summer cottage for the winter. You change the data amount in [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * How do I cancel the Trygg 48 insurance?

You can cancel the Trygg 48 service in the My Telenor app and My Telenor on the web.

[My Telenor app:](https://mitt.telenor.se/home) Click on settings, select the person who has Trygg 48 linked to their mobile phone, then you can cancel Trygg 48 under "Paid Services".

[My Telenor web:](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) Start by selecting the mobile subscription. Then select the person who has the mobile phone with Trygg 48. Select Settings - paid services and then Paid Services. Under Paid Services, you can then cancel Trygg48.

  * How do I cancel my prepaid card?

What a shame that you want to cancel your prepaid card. Your prepaid card will be automatically cancelled when you haven't topped it up for 12 months. [You can read more here](/link/36a8783d7c5e4a7aade2a58ef1f8c5c6.aspx). Welcome back, when you want to!

For those of you who have telematics, your prepaid card will be automatically cancelled when you haven't topped it up for 24 months.
