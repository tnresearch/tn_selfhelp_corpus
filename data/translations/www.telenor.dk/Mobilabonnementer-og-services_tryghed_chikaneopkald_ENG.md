# Harassing Calls

If you are experiencing harassing calls, you can choose to change your number and make it unlisted. Many new smartphones also allow you to block specific numbers directly in the phone's settings.

## Get a New Number

Unfortunately, we cannot block calls from specific phone numbers, but you can
[contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/) and get help changing your number.

## Get an Unlisted Number

On [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/) you can easily and quickly switch to an unlisted, hidden, or withheld number yourself. You can see how to do it
[here](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/opsatning/hvordan-far-jeg-
hemmeligt-nummer/).

### Report Harassing Calls to the Police

You can report harassing calls to your local police by calling 114. What the police do next depends on the specific case. On anklagemyndigheden.dk you can read more about how to deal with the situation if you are being stalked or harassed.

### Still Have Questions?
