# Help with Netflix

Here you will find guides on how to order, activate, or get help with Netflix
via T-We.

[Forgot Password](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/netflix/#passord)[Questions and
Answers](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/netflix/#sos)

## How to Access Netflix:

If you don't have a Netflix account already, here's how to create one with
T-We. If you already have a Netflix account, you'll find guides for that further
down the page.

  1. Go to either [Netflix Standard](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0285) or [Netflix Premium](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0286) in the content selector on My Pages (the links will take you directly to the packages)

  2. Order the desired package

  3. Once the package is activated, press **Activate Netflix Account**

  4. Email address is auto-filled, but you can change it to another address if you wish

  5. Choose a password and complete the setup

  6. Start watching your favorite movies and series!

 **Need help adding or removing content on My Pages?** Here we
show you how to [choose channels and
streaming services](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/tv/velge-kanaler/) into
the content selector for your T-We.

## I Already Have a Netflix Account

If you already have an account and/or subscription with Netflix today, you can
link it to your T-We subscription. This way, you can have everything in one place!

  * #### I Pay Directly to Netflix Today

When you switch to using points, billing from Netflix will be frozen. It is
important that you therefore use the same account for logging in with us, so that you do not
get two Netflix subscriptions.

 **How to Switch to Using Points:**

    1. Go to either [Netflix Standard](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0285) or [Netflix Premium](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0286) in the content selector on My Pages (the links will take you directly to the packages)

      * Log in with Telenor ID if you are not already logged in on My Pages

    2. Order the desired package

    3. Press Activate account here

    4. Log in with your existing Netflix account

  
You will retain your existing Netflix profile, as long as you use the same
email address for logging in. Netflix will freeze billing of you, and it
will remain frozen until you eventually choose to remove Netflix from the content selector.
Netflix will then restart billing of you again.

 _If you want to see the details of your Netflix customer relationship, you can go to
Account pages on_[ _netflix.com_](https://www.netflix.com/no-en/login)

  * #### I Have Netflix via Telenor Invoice Today

If you pay for Netflix via Telenor invoice today, but want to switch to
points, it's very easy to fix!

You just need to choose one of the two packages we offer in the content selector, and
we'll take care of the rest and switch over for you.

    1. Go to either [Netflix Standard](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0285) or [Netflix Premium](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0286) in the content selector on My Pages (the links will take you directly to the packages)

      * Log in with Telenor ID if you are not already logged in on My Pages

    2. Order the desired package

    3. Press Activate account here

    4. Log in with your existing Netflix account

Your account and profile will be transferred in full as long as you log in with the same
account you use from before.

## Forgot Password or Username for Netflix?

If you have forgotten the password or email address you used to log in
to Netflix, you can retrieve it by **clicking the button below**.

Log in with Telenor ID if you are not already logged in. You will then be taken
to Netflix, where you can see which email address should be used for
logging in.  
If you need the password, just press " _Need help?_ " under
the login field.
