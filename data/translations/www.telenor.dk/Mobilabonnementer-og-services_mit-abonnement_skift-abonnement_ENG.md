# How do I switch to a new mobile subscription?

A good mobile subscription is one that suits you and your needs. It's easy
to switch to a new subscription, but the method depends a little on whether you are
in a contract or not:

You are free from your contract period

If you are out of your contract period, you can easily switch to a new
mobile subscription. You can do this at [My Telenor](/login/) by visiting
a [Telenor store](/find-butik/) or by contacting our
[customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt/). See all mobile subscriptions
[here](/shop/abonnementer/abonnementer-til-dig/).

I am still in a contract period

You can always upgrade to a subscription with a higher monthly price if you
e.g. need more data. See all our
[subscriptions](/shop/abonnementer/abonnementer-til-dig/).

I'm unsure if I'm in a contract period

Check if you are in a contract by going to My Telenor and going to
[Subscriptions](/mit-telenor/abonnement/). This is also where you can change your
subscription.
