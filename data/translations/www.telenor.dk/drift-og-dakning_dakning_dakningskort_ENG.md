# Coverage Map

At Telenor, we deliver [world-class network](/shop/landingpage/netvark-i-
verdensklasse/). On the coverage map below, you can see how well our network
covers, and how well it handles tasks such as streaming and downloading - right
where you need it. The calculations are theoretical and may vary in
reality. Remember that you can change to "indoor" when checking coverage
- so you can also see how the coverage is in your home.

  

The type of mobile phone, router or tablet can affect how good coverage you
experience – for example, the quality of the mobile phone's antenna helps to
determine how good coverage you have. We have now rolled out a strong 5G network.
You can read more about it [here](/shop/landingpage/5g/).

  

You can see the different coverage types by clicking on the icon in the top
right of the network map.

## Questions and answers about mobile coverage

What is the difference between 4G and 5G?

4G stands for the fourth generation of the mobile network, while 5G stands for
the fifth generation. This means that 5G is the latest network technology that
opens up completely new possibilities with lightning-fast speeds. You can read
more about 5G [here](/shop/landingpage/5g/).

Is there a difference in coverage of voice and data?

Data coverage and voice coverage are not always of the same quality, as they are
supported by different technologies in the network. Voice takes place primarily
on 4G and in some cases 2G, while 5G currently only supports data.

Why is there a difference between indoor and outdoor coverage?

Building materials in walls and doors and windows can help block a good signal
indoors, which means that you can sometimes experience better coverage outdoors.
