# Change Wireless Channel

Here we show you how to change the channel on your wireless network on the Huawei
B818.

## Log in to the Router

  1. Check that you are connected to your home network.

  2. Open a browser and enter http://192.168.1.1 in the address bar, and press enter.

  3. Enter your password.

  4. The password is the same as your original wireless password. You can find it on the sticker on the back of the router after **WIFI**.   
**Note:** If you have changed the menu password yourself, enter the one you
have chosen.

## Change Channel

  1. Select **Advanced**

  2. Select **WiFi**

  3. Select **Advanced WiFi Settings**

  4. Select the desired channel for 2.4 GHz and/or 5 GHz from the dropdown menu for **Channel**

  5. Press **Save**
