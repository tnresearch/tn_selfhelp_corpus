# How your first invoice works

For those of you with TV, fixed broadband or home phone via broadband

Your first invoice from us is always higher than the invoices you will receive
afterwards. This is because you always pay for your subscription(s) in advance.  
  

## Example of a first invoice:

  1. On **March 16th** you **purchase a new broadband subscription**.
  2. On **April 7th** you receive your first invoice from us. It includes costs from half of March, all of April and all of May that you pay in advance. It is due at the end of April.

## Then you pay for one month at a time

After the first invoice, you only pay for one month at a time. Then
you continue to pay the same amount each month unless you change something in
your subscription or purchase or use services that cost money. This could
include phone calls or renting movies in the Movie Store.

Change payment method

If you want a payment method without an invoice fee, you need to switch to
autogiro or e-invoice.

[How to switch to autogiro.](/support/faktura/autogiro/)
