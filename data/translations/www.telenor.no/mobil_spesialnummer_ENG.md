# Prices for Special Numbers

Here you will find call prices and SMS prices for special numbers from your
mobile subscription.

All prices are valid from and including February 1, 2022.

## SMS to 3-, 4-, and 5-digit numbers

Number| Price  
---|---  
110, 112 and 113| 0.00  
1999, 05000, 09000| 0.00  
Other numbers| 1.00  
  
SMS to content providers (other numbers) may incur additional charges,
[read more about this on
Content Services.](/kundeservice/faktura/innholdstjenester/)

## 3-digit numbers

Number| Price  
---|---  
Emergency numbers, 110, 112 and 113| 0.00 / 0.00  
Telenor Fault Reporting 145| Same price as calling regular Norwegian phone numbers  
Nationwide weather report 175| Same price as calling regular Norwegian
phone numbers  
Route information 177| Same price as calling regular Norwegian phone numbers  
Other 3-digit numbers| Same price as calling regular Norwegian phone numbers  
  
## 4-digit numbers

Number| Prices  
---|---  
Directory Assistance 1881 and 1882| www.om.1881.no/vilkar/priser  
  
Calls from mobile phones to directory assistance are billed on the
mobile bill via Strex. Price information is provided by each
directory assistance provider.

## 5-digit numbers

Number| Price  
---|---  
5-digit numbers (02000-09999)| Same price as calling regular Norwegian
phone numbers  
  
*If you call the customer service numbers 915 09000/915 05000, the call price is the same as to a regular mobile number. If you have included call time in your mobile subscription, calls to customer service are also included.*

## 82XX Teletorg numbers

Calls to Teletorg numbers are billed via Strex in the same way as
content services.

You will be read the price when you call the individual Teletorg numbers, and
you will be able to see the charge on your mobile invoice. If you would rather
pay for these services with your credit card, you can arrange this by
registering at <https://minside.strex.no/>

## Calling special numbers from abroad

If you call Norwegian special numbers, separate prices apply. You can only call
special numbers in the 800-, 813- and 815 xx xxx series. If you call from EU/EEA
Norwegian prices apply for calling special numbers in Norway. If you call from a country
outside the EU/EEA, the price is the same as calling home to Norway from the country
you are in. [See call prices in
abroad](https://www.telenor.no/privat/mobil/utlandet/)

## Calling special numbers abroad

Calls from a mobile phone in Norway to an ordinary special number in another
country have the same prices as for other calls to the country. The only exception
is 'international green numbers' starting with 00800. These are priced with a
start price of NOK 0.99 and then NOK 0.99 per minute.
