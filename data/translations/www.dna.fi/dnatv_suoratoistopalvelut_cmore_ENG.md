# Support for the MTV Katsomo streaming service

The MTV Katsomo streaming service is included in all MTV Katsomo+ and channel package products ordered from DNA. You can watch the programs included in your subscription on MTV channels or through the streaming service.

## Getting Started

MTV Katsomo must be activated the first time you use it. Activation is only done once, and after that, you log in to the application with the username and password you created during activation.

Activation is easiest to do on a computer browser. After your subscription, you have received an email with the subject "MTV Katsomo app activation and usage instructions". Click the link in the message, it will take you directly to the MTV Katsomo activation, or follow these instructions.

Log in to the MTV Katsomo application at [www.mtv.fi/aktivoi-operaattori](http://www.mtv.fi/aktivoi-operaattori)

  1. Select the operator DNA
  2. Enter Activation Code 1 in the field.
  3. Enter Activation Code 2 in the field.
  4. Continue and follow the instructions given by the application. Note:

  * If you already have an MTV Katsomo username, you can log in with the same email address and password, and they will be linked to your DNA MTV Katsomo+ subscription.
  * If you have not previously had a username for MTV Katsomo, enter an email address that will function as your username for the service in the future and create a password.

## Using MTV Katsomo with a set-top box

  1. Open the set-top box
  2. Open My Apps from the menu
  3. Download MTV Katsomo from the Google Play Store if you haven't already downloaded it.
  4. Open the MTV Katsomo application and proceed according to the following instructions.
  5. Select "Log in"
  6. Select "Continue with activation code"
  7. Open a browser, e.g. on your phone or computer, and go to aktivoi.mtv.fi
  8. Enter the activation code displayed on your TV in the field in the browser on your phone or computer.
  9. The view in the browser will open for login. Enter the email address and password you created when activating MTV Katsomo.

If you have not yet activated MTV Katsomo with the activation codes received from DNA, do so now by selecting "Activate account through operator" from the view and see the instructions above for activating the service.

  10. Your TV is now activated.
  11. MTV Katsomo will now open on your TV screen and you can watch all the programs included in your subscription.
  12. You can close the browser on your phone or computer.

## Features and Usage

The email address and password you provided during activation will function as your credentials for the service in the future, and you can log in with them to the MTV Katsomo application on all devices where the MTV Katsomo application is available. You can also use MTV Katsomo, for example, through the DNA TV application on your computer, phone, tablet and DNA set-top box. Watch programs when you want, as much as you want.

The content available in the streaming service is determined by the content of the channel package; MTV Katsomo+ and Entertainment channel package subscribers have access to the entertainment content of the streaming service, and MTV Katsomo+ and Entertainment and Sports channel package subscribers have access to both entertainment content and all sports. In addition, the programs of MTV Katsomo's basic channels are available to all subscribers. You can set favorites on your own list or suggest program tips to your friends by sharing a link to the program page on MTV Katsomo.

### Using it abroad

You can use the MTV Katsomo application when traveling within the EU area.

### Mirroring content via Chromecast or Apple Play

Mirroring is supported.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Which channel packages include the MTV Katsomo streaming service?

The MTV Katsomo streaming service is included in DNA's MTV Katsomo+ channel package subscriptions. In addition to the channel package subscription, you must have a valid DNA TV subscription. The content available in the streaming service is determined by the content of the channel package; MTV Katsomo+ Entertainment channel package customers have access to the service's entertainment content, and MTV Katsomo+ Entertainment and Sports channel package customers can watch all the content of the service.

What kind of programs does the MTV Katsomo streaming service include?

The MTV Katsomo streaming service offers a wide range of movies and series from Hollywood, the Nordic countries and Finland - not to mention live sports and TV's favorite programs. There is also a wide selection of children's favorite programs and classics for children.

The content available in the streaming service is determined by the content of the channel package. With the MTV Katsomo+ Entertainment and Sports subscription, you have access to all the content of the entire MTV Katsomo streaming service.
