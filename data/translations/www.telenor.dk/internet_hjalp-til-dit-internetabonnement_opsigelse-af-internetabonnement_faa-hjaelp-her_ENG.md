# I want to cancel my internet

We are sorry to hear that you are considering cancelling your internet subscription with us.

Have you remembered everything that makes your Telenor subscription so special?
Your subscription includes extra security in the form of
[SikkerSurf](/shop/landingpage/blokerer-skadelige-hjemmesider/), which protects
you from harmful content, viruses, phishing, fraud and malware. This way
you can feel more secure when you are online on Telenor's network.

If you still wish to cancel your internet subscription, please contact
[customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt/).

## **When can I cancel my internet subscription?**

You can cancel your internet subscription as soon as you are no longer bound by a contract.
The contract period and other subscription terms depend on when you
purchased your Internet. You can find all information about your
internet subscription on My Telenor.

Log in to [My Telenor](/login/).
