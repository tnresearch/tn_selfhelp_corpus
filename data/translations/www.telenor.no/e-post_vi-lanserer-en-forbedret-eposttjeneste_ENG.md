# Online Email - Changes in Spring 2023

Here you can read more about what has changed and what it means for you as a customer
on email.

## We have upgraded your email service

In Spring 2023, you received an upgrade to your Online Email which gives you a secure
and user-friendly solution for the future. Everyone with an @online email address is
now migrated to the new solution.

## Price Changes for Online Email

If you had not previously paid a monthly fee for the email account, you were
moved to **Email 5 GB** which costs 39 Norwegian kroner per month. This expense
started running from June 1, 2023.

If you were already paying 29 Norwegian kroner per month for email filtering and storage, you were
moved to **Email 15 GB** which costs 49 Norwegian kroner per month. This expense
started running from July 1, 2023.

## New Benefits

Online Email is being prepared for the future with an improved spam filter, increased
storage space and a new webmail with both upgraded and completely new
features. You can find the webmail by logging in to
[Online.no](https://www.online.no).

If you want to read more about how to use the new spam filter, you can find tips
[in this article](https://www.online.no/sikkerhet/slik-endrer-du-spam-
innstillinger-online-epost).

Telenor systematically works to ensure good privacy, and as a user of
Online Email, your personal data will be safeguarded. This includes, among other
things, that we do not share information with third parties for commercial
purposes.
