## How We Use Your Personal Data

What types of personal data we use and where we collect it from

We collect and use two types of data about you: **customer data** and
**traffic data**. Some of this data we must process, for example
because the law requires it, or because it is necessary for us to be able to
enter into or fulfill our agreement with you. If you do not share this data with
us, we will not be able to enter into an agreement with you or may be prevented from
delivering the service in accordance with the agreement. For each type of data, we indicate whether you must provide the data to us or whether it is voluntary to do so.

**  
Customer data** is data that is related to you as an individual, which is
linked to the services and agreements you have with us or created when you
interact with us.
