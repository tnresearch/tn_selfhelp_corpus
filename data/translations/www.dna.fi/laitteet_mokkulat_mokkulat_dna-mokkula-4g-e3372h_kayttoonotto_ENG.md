## Getting Started

### Installing the Modem

The modem uses a standard Mini-SIM card, which is the largest card size. The SIM card comes off without much force when you press it out from the back of the SIM card. If you remove a SIM card of the wrong size, visit a DNA Store to exchange it for a new SIM card.

The SIM card is installed under the cover on the front of the modem. Push the cover away from the USB port. Insert the SIM card into the modem with the metal part facing down, and finally close the cover.

Registering to the DNA network takes a few minutes. If the connection fails to open, call DNA's [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot#asiakaspalvelu). The internet connection is available when the power light and network status indicator light are on in the modem.
