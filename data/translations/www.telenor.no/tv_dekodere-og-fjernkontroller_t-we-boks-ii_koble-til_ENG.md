# First Time Connecting to TV

By following this guide, you will get help placing the cables correctly and
completing the installation on the TV screen. Installing T-We is also the same
if you have reset the T-We box.

# What Type of Internet Do You Have?

To give you the right help with the connection, we first need to know what type
of internet you have with your TV subscription. If you are unsure what type
you have, you can look at **what equipment** you have connected.

 **NB!** It is important to use the cables that come with the T-We box to ensure
optimal quality of TV signal, sound and picture.

# How to Connect T-We Box II to the Internet

Have you already installed T-We, but haven't connected to the Internet? Here we show
you how to connect with WiFi, WPS or Ethernet cable.

 **Our recommended solution is with an Ethernet cable,** but WiFi can be a good
alternative where cabling is difficult.  
  
 **Choose whether you connect with an Ethernet cable or WiFi before starting
the guide by pressing "Next"**

# Insert the Ethernet Cable

  * The Ethernet cable should be connected between the T-We box and your router.
  * The Ethernet cable should be securely seated at both ends.
