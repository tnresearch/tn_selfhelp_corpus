# Telenor wifi-router

Here's how to connect your wifi-router from Telenor.

  * ### Connect the network cable

Plug the network cable with the red connectors into the red port on the router.

Plug the other end of the network cable into the broadband outlet on the wall.  
  
If you don't have an outlet in the wall, but a [service splitter](/support/vanliga-
fragor/port/vad-ar-en-tjanstefordelare/) instead? In that case, use the
port that is normally furthest to the left on the service splitter.

  * ### Connect power

Plug the power adapter into the power outlet and into the power input on the router.

  * ### Turn on the router

The button is on the back of the router.

  * ### Connect to wifi

On the underside of the router you will find the network name (Wi-Fi Name) and wifi password
(Wi-Fi Password) on the underside of the router.  
  
Do you want to connect automatically? Scan the QR code on the underside of the router
with your mobile camera.  

  * ### Surf directly or activate

Open a web browser and try to surf. If it works, everything is ready!  
  
If it doesn't work, you need to activate your broadband. Open a web browser and
enter the address **aktivering.telenor.se**  
  
Keep in mind that activation takes about 15 minutes after you have filled in
the information, and that you need to restart your router afterwards.

  * ### Download the Wifikontroll app

Now our best tip is to download our free app
[Wifikontroll](/handla/tjanster/appar/wifikontroll/)! With it, you can control
your wireless network.

\- Change the name and password of the network  
\- Create a guest network for friends and acquaintances  
\- Turn parental control on and off

[](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/wifikontroll/id1490238568?l=sv)[](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fon.wifikontroll.telenor)
