## Repair or Replacement Under Warranty

  * During the warranty period, repairing warranty defects is free of charge, whether you send your device by Post or through a DNA Store to a service center.

    * Home repair may be offered for some devices, you can inquire about this possibility with the service center or the manufacturer.
    * For devices and defects outside the warranty, you may be charged an inspection fee or shipping and repair costs.
  * Most devices are repaired, in which case the device must be sent to an authorized service center, which will inspect and, if necessary, replace the defective part so that the device works normally again.
    * Repair takes an average of about 7 business days, normally the service time varies between 3 and 20 days, depending on the availability of spare parts and where the device is serviced.
  * Some devices are not repaired at all, and these are always replaced with a new one during the warranty period. The exchange may be carried out by DNA or the device must be returned to the importer, service center or manufacturer, from where a new device will be delivered in its place.
  * If you send your device for repair or replacement, remember to pack the device carefully in its original packaging.

    * Follow the instructions received from the service center or DNA so that you are not charged shipping costs.
