# How We Work with Customer Contact

As the world becomes increasingly digitalized, so do the ways to be scammed. Here
we tell you how it works when you are in contact with us, so that you can
know whether it is really us who is on the phone or at the door.

## How We Know It's You

Identification with BankID

When you contact us for support issues, **you need to identify yourself with
BankID**. This is necessary because we need to be sure that it is really
the person using and paying for the subscription that we are in contact with.  
  
**When you call us**  
You need to identify yourself with BankID even before you reach one of our
employees. You will receive instructions in the call so you know when it is
time.

**When we call you**  
If you have contacted us and chosen to be called back, you will be asked to
identify yourself with BankID during the call.  
  
 **If you do not have BankID**  
If you do not have BankID, you can identify yourself using a personal code.
We will send the personal code by mail to your registered address.

Correct Contact Information

It is important to us to have your contact information. So that we can send
everything from agreements to confirmations to you.  
  
**When you become a customer**  
When you become a customer with us, you will receive an SMS from us one day
after your purchase. There we ask you to confirm your contact number and your
email address.  
  
 **When you create an account in My Telenor**  
When you create an account in My Telenor, we will therefore ask you to
confirm that your contact information is correct. We do this via email and SMS.  
  
All you need to do is follow the simple instructions we send with it. Either
confirm that the information is correct or take the opportunity to update it.  
  
  

## How We Work with Sales

Telephone Sales

Here's how you know who is calling is contracted by us.

  * If you search for the number, it will say either Loxytell or Cell ip. Keep in mind that there are more people than us at Telenor who use Loxytell or Cell ip, so it is not a guarantee that the call is coming from us. We also call from our own number, which is [020-222 222](tel:020222222).
  * Calls usually take place on weekdays between 10 am and 7 pm, but sometimes also on weekends.
  * If you are already a customer with us, we often send you an SMS, email or letter informing you that we will be in contact. However, this does not apply if you are a customer through your property owner.
  * If you ask, the salesperson will tell you that they are calling on behalf of Telenor. If you want to be absolutely sure, ask the salesperson which company they work for, and then call us at [020-222 222](tel:020222222) and ask if it is a company we work with.
  * Sometimes you will be informed that the calls may be recorded.
  * Do you not want to be contacted by us? Then you can block your number for telephone sales via NIX.

**If you choose to sign an agreement with us by phone, it works like this:**

  * The salesperson goes through what you have agreed on with the help of a recording. The recording is not a binding document but only to ensure that we are talking to the right person.
  * Then you will receive an SMS with a link to the agreement. The agreement contains our logo, a description of the agreement, links to the general terms and conditions and a number to customer service.

Door-to-Door Sales

We hire salespeople from external companies for the sale of broadband and TV via
"door-knocking".

Here's how you know it's a salesperson hired by us who visits you.

  * Salespeople always have ID cards. It should say "Telenor", the salesperson's name and an ID number. There should also be a picture of the salesperson.
  * Salespeople always wear Telenor clothing (sweater and/or jacket).
  * We always contact the housing association or property owner before visiting multi-family buildings.
  * We often send a postcard in advance stating that we will be coming.
  * We visit on weekdays and sometimes Sundays, from lunchtime to no later than 9 pm.

## More About Security

Has someone tried to scam you?

Then you can [report it to
Säkerhetskollen](https://sakerhetskollen.se/lamna-tips). It is a
gathering place for online security, and by reporting tips there you help
combat digital crime.  
  
At Säkerhetskollen you can also find [warnings about digital crimes that are currently taking place](https://sakerhetskollen.se/aktuella-brott). You can even
subscribe to their mailing list and receive the latest warnings directly in your
email. Great, we think!

Help us combat spam

Part of Telenor's work against fraud is to report scam SMS so that they do
not reach our subscribers. If you see an SMS that looks suspicious, you can
**forward the message to 7726**.

In this way, we get information about the content of the SMS and how many are
affected. We can also prevent it from being sent to more people. The number
works with all Swedish operators.
