## Enabling mobile connectivity on a Samsung Galaxy Watch smartwatch

Did you receive a QR code from DNA Kauppa for activation? Read it in the Galaxy Wearable
app, you do not need a separate activation code. eSIM is then ready for use. You can go directly to step 4 in the instructions.

Start by opening the details of your phone subscription in the My DNA app (under the Services menu) and go to the "SIM card and PUK codes" view.

Make sure that the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service is visible under "Parallel card".
Select "Activate eSIM".

If you have not yet subscribed to the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service, subscribe to it in the My DNA
app, DNA Kauppa or DNA's customer service: 0800 550 044 (Mon-Fri 9-20, Sat 10-16). The number is free of charge.

Copy the eSIM activation code (use the copy button) to paste it
next into your phone's Galaxy Wearable app.

If you don't already have the app, download it from the Google Play app store.

Open the Galaxy Wearable app on your phone and paste the activation code
into the app.

Select your watch from the device list and follow the instructions in the app.

**Done!** Now you can make and receive calls on your smartwatch and
use the internet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I want calls and internet on my Samsung Galaxy Watch. How do I proceed?

Follow the instructions on this page; the Galaxy Watch's own app will help
with activation.

How fast is the internet on the Galaxy Watch?

When your phone subscription has the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service, you surf the internet on your smartwatch
at a speed of 10 Mbit/s; with that you can easily download music and maps for your run –
the limit is not reached, as the internet on the parallel card is also unlimited.

The speed of the internet on your main subscription, i.e. your primary phone, is determined by the subscription type
accordingly, but there is no data cap for the internet on either device.

Please note that DNA Multi-SIM does not work abroad.

What does it cost to use the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service?

The DNA Multi-SIM add-on service is charged according to the current price list
connection and monthly fee. Payments are charged to the main subscription invoice. Usage-based charges
for parallel cards linked to the service are billed on the main subscription invoice and billing principles. For example,
if calls on the main subscription are billed on a per-minute basis at €0.07 from the start of the minute,
calls made on the parallel card are billed on the same
billing principle. If the main subscription
monthly price includes a fixed amount of call minutes,
calls made using the device using the parallel card consume the same
package.

I switched operators. How do I connect the Galaxy Watch to my phone?

First, remove the previous eSIM from your device. Then order the DNA Multi-SIM
add-on service from DNA, either in the My DNA app, DNA Kauppa or
customer service. Then follow the instructions on this page.

If I want to remove the Galaxy Watch subscription, how do I proceed?

After removing the eSIM from the device, remember to also cancel the DNA Multi-SIM
add-on service by contacting [DNA's
customer service.](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot#asiakaspalvelu)

Where can I find more information about Galaxy Watch smartwatches?

See the features of the watches sold by DNA
[here](https://kauppa.dna.fi/laitteet/alykellot-ja-hyvinvointi/alykellot-ja-
rannekkeet).

Is the Galaxy Watch compatible with the iPhone?

Yes, with the latest phone models. You can see an up-to-date model list
[on Samsung's website](https://www.samsung.com/fi/wearables/samsung-device-
compatibility/).

What do I do if my phone or smartwatch is lost?

If you have a location service or a "Find my device" app, use it and
temporarily close your subscription for security reasons. The additional services of the subscription (e.g. DNA Multi-SIM add-on service) will close temporarily at the same time. Do this:

**Lost phone**

The most important thing when a phone is lost is to lock the device remotely and close
the subscription of the lost phone
[in self-service](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#) or
[through customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).

If necessary, you should also restore the phone to factory settings, i.e. reset the device's data. Resetting the phone sounds final, but if
the data has been saved to the cloud, it is safe and easily recoverable to your phone.

If you are not sure if the phone has been used, remember to also change
the passwords of your most important services.

If you have the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service on your subscription, it will close
temporarily when the subscription is closed. Please note that this will also cut off the mobile connection
from the parallel device – another phone or smartwatch.

**Lost Samsung Galaxy Watch**

If you lose a Samsung Galaxy Watch watch or another Samsung device, you can
search for it, lock it and clear its data. If you have added a Google account to the device, "Find my phone" is automatically on. See instructions
[on Samsung's support pages](https://www.samsung.com/fi/support/mobile-
devices/miten-loydat-samsung-galaxy-laitteesi/).

If your smartwatch has had a mobile connection, remember to temporarily close the DNA
Multi-SIM add-on service as well. We will do it for you, contact DNA's
[customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).
