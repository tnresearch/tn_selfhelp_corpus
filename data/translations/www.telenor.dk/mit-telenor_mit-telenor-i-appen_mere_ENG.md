# More

In the My Telenor app, you will find important information under the "More" button.

_Orders_

Here you have the opportunity to view your current orders.

_Family group_

Read more about our new Bundle Discount [here](/shop/landingpage/samlerabat/).

_Coverage_

You can see what affects your coverage and learn more about what
the surroundings mean for your coverage. You can also report a coverage issue and
tip us off about any errors on your network. If, on the other hand, you experience more permanent
problems, you can use our coverage map to investigate the coverage in your
area.

_Help_

Here you will find 'frequently asked questions'.

_History_

Your history gives you an overview of your subscriptions within the interval you
want to be informed about.  
  

The app is built around five main menus, which you can get help with
here:
