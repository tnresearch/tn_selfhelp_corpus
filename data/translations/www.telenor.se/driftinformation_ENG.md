##

  * I'm having trouble calling 112. What should I do?

**Start by restarting your mobile.** If that doesn't work, remove the SIM card
from your phone and call 112 without a SIM card installed.

**Do you have an e-SIM? Temporarily turn it off.**

To turn off e-SIM on iPhone, go to: Settings > Cellular  
To turn off e-SIM on Android, go to: Settings > Network & internet

Don't forget to turn your e-SIM back on when you're finished with your 112
call.  

 **Call via broadband with Wi-Fi calling.**  

You can make Wi-Fi calls if you are connected via a broadband connection. This
is a feature that you can always have enabled. [More information is
available here.](/link/bc2556b175044d2a980f1376de2c6a66.aspx)

**Call via the 112 app.**  

You need a mobile network or Wi-Fi to be able to do so. If the mobile network
doesn't work, you can call via the 112 app if you are connected via a
broadband connection. [You can download the 112 app here.](https://www.sosalarm.se/112-och-andra-
viktiga-nummer/112-appen/)

**Do you have home phone via broadband** and there is a service disruption on
your broadband. Call via your mobile phone instead.
