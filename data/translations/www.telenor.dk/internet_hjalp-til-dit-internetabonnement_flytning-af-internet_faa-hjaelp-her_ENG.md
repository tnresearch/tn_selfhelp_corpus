# Moving Your Internet

It's easy to move your internet to a new address. You just need to be
aware that it can take up to six weeks to complete the move, so
if you want to be sure you're online on the day you move into your
new home, you should notify us of your move well in advance.

## How far in advance should I report the move?

If you want to be sure you're online on the day you move into your new
home, it's a good idea to report the move no later than six weeks in advance. However,
you can easily report a move far into the future, so it might be a good idea to
report the move when you know the move-in date for your new home.

## What does it cost to move internet?

As a general rule, it's free to move your internet. In rare cases, there may be a cost if a technician visit is necessary. The price of a
technician visit depends on the provider, but you will always be informed of
the price in advance.

## How do I report my internet move?

1\. Log in to [My Telenor](/mit-
telenor/abonnement/detaljer/bredbaand/relocation-page/)  
2\. Press **Subscriptions** in the menu  
3\. Select your subscription  
4\. Press **Change address** under your subscription details
