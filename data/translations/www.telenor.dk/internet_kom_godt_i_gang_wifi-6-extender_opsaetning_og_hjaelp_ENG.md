## Written Guide

**Setting up your new WiFi Extender**

Even though your WiFi Extender is brand new, it needs to be updated to function optimally. Before you begin, make sure your WiFi router is turned on, and have the network cable ready that came with your WiFi Extender.

  1. Connect the network cable to the input labeled "LAN1/WAN port" on your WiFi Extender and connect to a LAN input on your router (use any input on the router that says "LAN")
  2. The WiFi Extender will now be automatically updated by Telenor's systems and must NOT be turned off during the process. It takes up to 15 minutes. You will experience different lights on the WiFi Extender blinking, and it will also restart itself during the process. When the lights are steadily green again, you can proceed to the actual connection.

**Connection**

Once you have updated the WiFi Extender, there are two ways to connect it to your router – via cable and wirelessly.

**Connection via cable:**

  * Connect a network cable (RJ45) from your WiFi router to the WAN port (Port 1) in the WiFi Extender
  * Plug the WiFi Extender into the power outlet - this is done as follows: turn off the power outlet, mount the cable in the WiFi Extender, mount it in the power outlet and finally turn on the power outlet. Wait for the green light in the power lamp to light up steadily
  * Find the name of your WiFi (SSID) and your WiFi code, which are located on the underside of the WiFi Extender
  * If you experience problems when following the above steps, you can always reset the WiFi Extender by holding down the Reset button for 10 seconds and starting over

**Wireless Pairing:**

  * Place the WiFi Extender in immediate proximity to your WiFi router
  * Plug the WiFi Extender into a power outlet. Wait for the green light in the power lamp to light up steadily
  * Press the small WPS button on the back of the WiFi Extender and wait for the WPS light to blink blue.
  * Then press the WPS button on the WiFi router
  * The two WiFi devices will now connect to each other. The WiFi Extender will blink in the WPS light during the process. This can take up to two minutes
  * When the connection between the WiFi router and the WiFi Extender is complete, the lights will be green: 2, 4G and 5G
  * Place the WiFi Extender in a place where there is still good coverage. Note! The WiFi Extender may only be used indoors
  * When you have used the wireless method to connect the WiFi router and the WiFi Extender, the WiFi name (SSID) and WiFi code are the same as on the WiFi router
  * If you experience problems when following the above steps, you can always reset the WiFi Extender by holding down the Reset button for 10 seconds and starting over
