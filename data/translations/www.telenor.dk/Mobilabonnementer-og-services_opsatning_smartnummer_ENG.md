# Smart Number

With Telenor, you can get the most out of your smartwatch with Smart Number completely
free for 3 months. After that, it costs 40 DKK per month.

Smart Number can connect your smartwatch to your phone, so you can receive calls
and messages without having your phone with you. All it requires is a
smartwatch that supports eSIM.

Your smartwatch and your mobile share the amount of talk time and data you have in your
existing mobile subscription. This way, you can always keep track of how much data you
have used and have remaining.

How do I get Smart Number?

Order Smart Number on [My Telenor](/login/) under the subscription to which
the Smart Number should be linked.

How do I connect my smartwatch to my Telenor subscription?

To connect your smartwatch to your Telenor subscription, you need to have Smart Number linked to your subscription. It is Smart Number that connects your
mobile subscription to your smartwatch. Smart Number is added on [My
Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/) under **View and edit services**.

How do I activate my Smart Number on my smartwatch?

It's easy to connect your smartwatch with your Smart Number. Scan the QR code with
the app that belongs to your smartwatch – and you're ready to go.

What do I do if my Smart Number doesn't work?

Try the following before contacting customer service:

\- Check if Bluetooth is disabled on your smartwatch.  
\- Open the app that belongs to your smartwatch and check if the watch is paired with
your phone.  
\- Check if both smartwatch and mobile are updated with the latest system update.

If it still doesn't work, we are ready to help you in customer service 72
100 100.

Does Smart Number work abroad?

It depends on which watch you have:

  * With watchOS 9.1 and a newer version, roaming abroad is possible on Cellular models of Apple Watch Series 5 and newer, Apple Watch SE and newer, and Apple Watch Ultra. 
  * Samsung Watch can be used abroad.
