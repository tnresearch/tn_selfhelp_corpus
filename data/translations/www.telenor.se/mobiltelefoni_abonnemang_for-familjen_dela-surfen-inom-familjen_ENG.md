# Share surfing within the family

Do some family members surf more than others? Then you can distribute the surfing by setting
surfing limits, so that everyone gets as much as they need.

You who have the [member profile Owner](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/for-
familjen/medlemsprofiler/) in Telenor Family (that is, you who are listed as
the payment responsible for the subscription) can distribute the surfing between the other
members by **setting surfing limits –** even if you share unlimited
surfing.

You set surfing limits in My Telenor – either here [on the web](/mitt-
telenor/) or in [our app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/).
