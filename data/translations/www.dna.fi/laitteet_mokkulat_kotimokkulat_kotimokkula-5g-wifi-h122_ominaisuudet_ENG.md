## Features

  
**Product Name: Huawei 5G CPE Pro 2 (H122-373)**  
  
The DNA Home Modem 5G WiFi H122 puts bits into overdrive with blazing-fast
5G speeds. Enjoy home internet and wireless connectivity without lag and
reliably.

  * 5G maximum speed from the network 1.8 Gbit/s (NSA 5G), 3.6 Gbit/s (SA 5G) 
  * 5G maximum speed towards the network 125 Mbit/s (NSA 5G), 250 Mbit/s (SA 5G)
  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 1.6 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed towards the network 75 Mbit/s
  * WLAN 802.11b/g/n/a/ac/ax, 2x2 MIMO
  * WLAN theoretical maximum speed: 2700 Mbit/s
  * Dual-band WiFi antenna with six signal amplifiers
  * Gigabit Ethernet (LAN) connections: 2 pcs (one with WAN)
  * 5G and 4G LTE network technology
  * 5 GHz Wi-Fi preferred support
  * Nano-SIM card
