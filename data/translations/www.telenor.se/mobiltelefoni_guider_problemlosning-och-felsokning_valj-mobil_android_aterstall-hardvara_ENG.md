# Reset hardware

## Samsung, Sony and Xiaomi

  
If you choose to reset the hardware, your personal settings on the
phone will be deleted. If you want to keep the settings, we recommend that you [make a
backup](~/link/42ae33055bf24067be3b77b910fb183b.aspx) before resetting your phone.

This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest version of the Android operating system.** So feel free to start by making sure it is installed on your phone
– not least so that it works as well and securely as possible.  
  
[How to update the
operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-
mobil/android/uppdatera-operativsystem/)  
  
Now you have the latest version installed, right? Then let's go!

  * ### Go to the home screen and swipe up to open the menu

  * ### Select "Settings​"

  * ### Search for, and select, "Factory data reset"

  * ### Select "Reset to factory data"

  * ### Select "Reset"

  * ### Select "Delete all"
