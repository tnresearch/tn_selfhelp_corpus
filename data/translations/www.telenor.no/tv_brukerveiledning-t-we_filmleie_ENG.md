# T-We Movie Rental

Here you will find information about movie rental, how to rent a movie, and how to
find the different rental movies in T-We.

Movie Rental: When you have chosen the movie you want to watch, you can press "Rent" to
gain access for 48 hours. You can also watch the trailer, rate the movie, or add it
to My List. It will then appear both under "Home" and "My Content".

## How to Rent a Movie

  1. Select **Rental Movie** from the menu at the top

  2. Select **Rent** on the movie you want to watch

  3. Confirm the rental by entering your [PIN code](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/pin-kode/)

You have access to watch the movie immediately, but you can also find it again
under My Content.

The movie is available for 48 hours.

## Movie Rental in the T-We App

It is possible to watch rented movies in the T-We app, but the movie must first be rented on the T-We
Box or [twe.no](https://twe.no/).

Movies you have rented on the T-We box can be searched for in the T-We app, and they will be
available under “ **My Content** ” in the [T-We app](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-
app/), as long as the rental period lasts.

Spending Limit for Movie Rental

To avoid overspending, we have introduced a spending limit of NOK 3000 per month.
This corresponds to approximately 60 movies per month. It is not possible to remove
the limit, but you can order new movies at the turn of the month.

 **If you reach the limit, you will see this message:**

 _You have exceeded the maximum monthly spending limit for movie rental (NOK 3000). This
limit will be reset again next month._

## Movies and Movie Rental in T-We

Select Rental Movie in the menu: Here you will find all movies that are available for rent.
