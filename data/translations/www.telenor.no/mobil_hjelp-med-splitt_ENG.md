## What is Splitt and where can I buy Splitt?

Splitt is a smarter way to buy a mobile. With Splitt, you can both **split
up** the payment over 24 or 36 months, and **split up** what you pay
for the mobile now: Pay 0,- today, or a voluntary amount for a lower
monthly price.

There are no interest, fees or commitment, and you can trade in your old
mobile for _even_ lower monthly payments.

You can buy a new mobile phone with Splitt both online at
[telenor.no](/mobiltelefoner/), in [Telenor Store](/telenorbutikken/) and at
Elkjøp and Power. You can also call us on [915 09000](tel:+4791509000)

## What happens when I want to trade in my mobile?

You can **trade in your old mobile** , and **pay a lower
monthly price** for the new one. Trade-in with Splitt is smart and easy – whether it
happens in store or online.

New mobile phone with Splitt both online at telenor.no, in Telenor Store and at
Elkjøp and Power. You do not need to hand in charger and other accessories.

  * If you buy a new mobile in store, you hand in the old one at the same time. If you shop in Telenor Store, you can also get help transferring information from the old to the new mobile.

  * If you buy a new mobile online, you do not need to send the old one until you have received the new one. You will receive a pre-franked package to send it in.

## Why trade in your mobile with Splitt?

With trade-in, the monthly price of your new mobile will be lower. Even if the mobile is in
poor condition or has a cracked screen, it may have a value. Your mobile can get
new life! We then ensure that it is reset and prepared for a new owner,
completely safely.

It is easy and non-binding to check the value of the old mobile. You can
either get help in Telenor Store, or check yourself online.

[Read more about trade-in](/splitt/innbytte-med-splitt/)

[Check the value of your old mobile](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/bestill/)

I want to make a trade-in, but the mobile is damaged

 **What do I do?**

Mobiles you no longer need can still have a value – both for you and for
others. Don't let your old mobile phone lie and gather dust in a drawer, even if
the mobile is in poor condition or has a cracked screen, it may have a value. We accept
both broken and functioning phones.

NB! There may be a minimum value requirement for your old phone to receive a
trade-in bonus, usually from 100-400 NOK.

Pssst!

If you have Plus Insurance, you are guaranteed to get the highest possible
valuation when trading in. When you buy a new mobile with Splitt, you also get
the opportunity to test our best insurance – Plus Insurance – for two months
completely free of charge.

After these two months, you can of course extend the insurance if you wish. It gives you security all over the world and several benefits, such as a guaranteed replacement mobile within one working day and maximum value when trading in.

## Frequently asked questions about Splitt

  * #### How do I buy a mobile with Splitt online?

    * Find [the mobile you want in the online store](/mobiltelefoner/), and choose to buy it with a Splitt agreement over 24 or 36 months.

    * Decide whether you want to pay 0,- for the mobile today, or whether you want to pay a voluntary amount for a lower monthly price.

    * If you want to [trade in your old mobile](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/), you will get a quick and non-binding valuation of it. If you accept, the amount will be deducted from the monthly price of the new mobile.

    * Decide whether you want two months [Plus Insurance](/forsikring/plussforsikring/) free of charge (remember that the insurance cannot be purchased separately after the Splitt purchase is completed).

    * Then choose [a mobile subscription](/mobilabonnement/) that suits you and your use – or continue with the one you have.

    * The new mobile can be delivered in several ways – and within 3 days you will have it in your hands. If you have chosen Trade-in, you can also transfer everything to the new mobile before you have to send in the old one (at no extra cost).

  * #### How do I buy a mobile with Splitt in store?

    * Find [the mobile you want at Telenor Store](/telenorbutikken/), Power or Elkjøp – and choose to buy it with a Splitt agreement over 24 or 36 months.

    * Decide whether you want to pay 0,- for the mobile today, or whether you want to pay a voluntary amount for a lower monthly price.

    * If you want to [trade in your old mobile](/mobiltelefoner/innbytte/), you will get help with a valuation in Telenor Store – which can also help prepare the new mobile and send in the old one for you. The trade-in amount will be deducted from the monthly price of the new mobile.

    * Decide whether you want two months [Plus Insurance](/forsikring/plussforsikring/) free of charge (remember that the insurance cannot be purchased separately after the Splitt purchase is completed).

    * Then choose [a mobile subscription](/mobilabonnement/) that suits you and your use – or continue with the one you have.

    * Enjoy your new mobile!

  * #### Is a mobile subscription included in Splitt? 

No, Splitt is just a new and smarter way to buy the mobile itself, and you must
therefore have a mobile subscription in addition.

  * #### Do I have to have a Telenor subscription with Splitt?

Yes, when you buy a mobile with Splitt, you must have a Telenor subscription at
the date of purchase.

You can, however, change your subscription at any time, or switch to another
provider if you wish after the Splitt agreement has started.

  * #### Can I pay extra during the agreement?

Yep, you can! [Contact
customer service](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/), and we will
help you.

The monthly price will then be lower and updated automatically on the next invoice.

  * #### Is there a commitment with Splitt?

No, you can at any time terminate the Splitt agreement by buying out
your mobile phone. You do this easily by contacting customer service or visiting Telenor Store.

You will then receive a final invoice, where you pay for the months you have left of
the agreement. When it is paid, the Splitt agreement is over – and your mobile. You pay
nothing more if you buy yourself out during the period, the total price for the phone will
anyway be the same.

  * #### Do I own the mobile if I buy it with Splitt?

Both yes and no. As long as you pay down on the Splitt agreement (over 24 or 36
months) it is not legally yours, but you of course have full right of use. As soon as
the agreement is over – or if you buy out the mobile phone during the period – the mobile
is in your possession.

  * #### What do I do if I have an active SWAP agreement?

With the launch of Splitt, we are also discontinuing SWAP. But even though we have stopped selling SWAP, you who have an active SWAP agreement will be able to continue your
agreement period for a total of two years. Screen insurance and any Plus Insurance you have also apply as before, and you do not need to do anything.

If you want to trade in or terminate your current SWAP agreement [you have
several options that you can read more about here](/swap/swap-avvikles/).
