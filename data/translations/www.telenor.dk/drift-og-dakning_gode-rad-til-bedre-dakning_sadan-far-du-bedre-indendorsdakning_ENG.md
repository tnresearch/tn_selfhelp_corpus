## Questions and answers about WiFi calling

Which phones can be used for WiFi calling?

The newest mobile phones often have WiFi calling. You can read whether your mobile phone has WiFi calling under its specifications.

How much does it cost to use WiFi calling?

It costs the same as you normally pay. If you have unlimited talk time, there are therefore no costs associated with calling over the wireless network. If you normally pay a per-minute rate, you will do the same with WiFi calling.

Can WiFi calling be used abroad or on a plane?

No, unfortunately not.

Are there special requirements for the WiFi connection?

No, your phone just needs to be connected to the WiFi network. The phone will then automatically choose this network if it is stronger than the mobile network.

I experience poor coverage when talking via wireless network?

The quality of your call via WiFi cannot be better than the WiFi itself. This means that if you normally have problems with your wireless connection in a particular room, you will also experience it when using the connection for WiFi calling.

The same applies if many people are on the network at the same time; this will also negatively affect your call.

Can WiFi calling be used for emergency calls?

Yes, the selected settings will also apply during emergency calls to ensure that the mobile phone chooses the strongest signal. But be aware that the call may be disconnected if you move away from WiFi coverage during the call.

Does the recipient's mobile phone also need to have WiFi calling enabled?

No, it is not necessary to use the same call type.

What happens if I walk out the door while on the phone?

If there is 4G coverage outside the building, a handover will be performed, which means that the call will continue without interruption. If there is no coverage, or only 2G or 3G coverage, your call will be disconnected when you move out of range of the WiFi network, and you will have to call back to continue the conversation.
