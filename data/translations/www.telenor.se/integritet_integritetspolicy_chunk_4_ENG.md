**Type of data**

**Example**

**Source**

**Do you have to provide the data?**  
  
---|---|---|---  
  
Contact and identification data

|

Name, address, apartment number, email address, information about protected identity,
phone number, date of birth, personal identity number and customer number.

|

The data is mainly collected from you. Some data, such as customer number,
is created by us. Some data is also collected from other sources (e.g.
public registers and providers of business and market information).

|

The data is necessary for us to enter into and fulfill agreements with
you.  
  
Contract and service data

|

Data about your contracts and services, such as type of subscription and service,
contract period, notice period, information about unlisted number, MSISDN, IMSI,
login details, account settings and purchased/rented equipment.

|

The data is mainly collected from you. Some data, such as contract period,
is created by us.

|

The data is necessary for us to enter into and fulfill agreements with
you.  
  
Behavioral data

|

Data about how you use our services, for example consumption (such as
data usage), which program you have watched, which movie you have rented, if you have
paused watching and how you interact with us via My Telenor, Telenor's
apps, website and advertisements (includes data that may be collected
via cookies).

|

The data is mainly collected from you, e.g. when you use our services
or visit our website, but can also be collected from other sources (e.g.
other operators or providers such as Bredbandskollen and
marketing partners such as Bisnode).

|

Some of the data is necessary for us to fulfill agreements with
you. Some of the data is voluntary to provide (e.g. you can choose not to
accept certain cookies).  
  
Case data

|

Data that you provide when you interact with us via customer service (e.g.
in connection with questions and complaints), chat, social media or
customer survey, including any audio recordings.

|

The data is mainly collected from you. Some data, such as
case number, is created by us.

|

Some of the data is necessary for us to fulfill agreements with
you. Some of the data is voluntary to provide (e.g. data provided
when you participate in a customer survey).  
  
Payment and billing data

|

Invoice address, invoice items, payment terms, current claim or debt,
accounting information (e.g. receipts for purchases or returns) and data about
your payment behavior with us.

|

The data is mainly collected from you. Some data, such as
invoice number, is created by us.

|

The data is necessary for us to enter into and fulfill agreements with
you.  
  
Credit data

|

Data from credit reference agencies about creditworthiness and data about
your payment behavior with us.

|

The data is mainly obtained from sources other than you, e.g.
credit reference agencies and ourselves. In some cases, we may collect data
from you.

|

The data is necessary for us to enter into agreements with you.  
  
Device data

|

MAC address, IP address, device type, device model, information about delivery and
service of hardware, screen resolution, language settings, web settings,
platform, operating system, device ID and cookie ID.

|

The data is mainly collected from you, but can also be collected from other
sources (e.g. other operators or providers and marketing partners).

|

Some of the data is necessary for us to fulfill agreements with
you. Some of the data is voluntary to provide (e.g. you can choose not to
accept certain cookies).  
  
Data about personal circumstances

|

Data about, for example, age, marital status, family type, life stage, type of housing,
which devices are connected to your household, education level, preferences
and financial status, such as income data and purchasing power.

|

The data is mainly collected from sources other than you (e.g. providers
of business and market information). Some data, such as your preferences,
is created by us.

|

The data is mainly collected from sources other than you. If this type of
data were to be collected from you, it is voluntary to provide.
