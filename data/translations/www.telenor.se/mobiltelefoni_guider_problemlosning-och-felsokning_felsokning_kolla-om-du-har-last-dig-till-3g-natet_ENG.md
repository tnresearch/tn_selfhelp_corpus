# Check if you are locked to the 3G network

By switching to 4G, you can connect to the internet faster than with 3G on your
mobile phone. Here you can see how to switch to the 4G network on your device.

###

How to switch to 4G on your iPhone

  1. Go to the home screen.
  2. Select **Settings**.
  3. Select **Cellular**.
  4. Select **Cellular Data Options**.
  5. Select **Voice & Data**.
  6. Select **4G**.

Your iPhone is now configured for the 4G network.

How to switch to 4G on your Android phone

  1. Go to the home screen.
  2. To open the menu, swipe up on the screen.
  3. Select **Settings**.
  4. Scroll down.
  5. Select **Connections**.
  6. Select **Mobile networks**.
  7. Select an option, in this case **Network mode SIM 1**.
  8. Select **4G/3G/2G (auto connect)**.

Your Android is now configured for the 4G network.
