# Subscribe to top-up

If you have an account, you can subscribe to a top-up. The benefit of subscribing is that you don't have to remember to top up your card – it happens automatically every month. Plus, you get a free top-up every sixth time.

You can subscribe to all top-ups except annual top-ups. The subscription is easy to cancel in the [Telenor Ladda app](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/).

## How to subscribe to top-up

  1. Create an account in the [Telenor Ladda app](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/).
  2. Enter credit card details.
  3. Choose any top-up to subscribe to.
  4. Choose the date you want the card to be automatically topped up. Every sixth top-up is free!

Note: Every sixth top-up is only free if you top up with the same top-up each time.

[ Our top-ups ](/support/mobiltelefoni/kontantkort/ladda-och-saldo/ladda-kontantkort/)

## Cancel a subscription

You cancel a subscription by logging in to your top-up account in the [Telenor Ladda app](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * I already have a prepaid card with you but want Hello. How do I do that?

Great that you want Hello! It is one of our fixed-price top-ups. You can choose between 1 GB and 10 GB. To get Hello, do the following:

    1. Go to [telenor.se/ladda ](/link/8ad148929ae541328599af3f428082a6.aspx)
    2. Choose one of the Hello top-ups
