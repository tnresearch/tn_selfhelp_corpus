# Share internet with another device

Does your friend run out of data? Or do you want to use your computer in a place where you don't have access to wifi? Sharing your mobile data is the solution!

This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest operating system (iOS)** from Apple. So, please start by making sure it is installed on your mobile – not least to ensure it works as well and securely as possible.

[Here's how to update the operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-mobil/iphone/uppdatera-operativsystemet/)

Now you have the latest iOS installed, right? Then let's go!

  * ### Go to the home screen

  * ### Select "Settings"

  * ### Select "Personal Hotspot"

  * ### Set a password

The first time you activate the function, you must enter a password.

Click on "Wi-Fi Password" > Enter the password > Select "Done". The password is then used on the device you want to share the internet with.

Do you already have a password? Go directly to the next step.

  * ### Select "Allow Others to Join" and follow the instructions on the screen

If the device you want to connect cannot find your mobile, activate the "Maximize Compatibility" option so they can find each other.
