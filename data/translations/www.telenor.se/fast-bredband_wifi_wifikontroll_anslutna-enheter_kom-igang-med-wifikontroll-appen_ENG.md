# Get started with Wifi Control

  * ### Download

You can find the app in the [AppStore](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/my-telenor-
sweden/id1154435018) if you have an iPhone and in [Google
Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.telenor.mytelenor&hl=sv&referrer=utm_source%3Dtelenor_se%26utm_medium%3Dlandingpage%26utm_content%3Dbottom_cta)
if you have an Android phone. Search for Telenor Wifi Control.  
  
Tip! The app doesn't take up much space, but make sure you have enough free storage
space on your phone.

  * ### Log in to the app

You do this with the same details you use to log in to My Telenor here on the web
or by following the instructions in the app. If you don't have a My Telenor account,
you need to create one first.

  * ### Click on get started

  * ### Select the router you want to configure

  * ### Connect to power source

  * ### Connect to your wifi hotspot

  * ### The device connects

Wait while your device connects to your account. If the connection fails, you will
be prompted to enter the router's MAC address manually, or to scan the QR code on
the underside of your router.

  * ### You are connected

Tip! Change the name and password to what you had for your previous network so you
don't have to enter the new network details for all your devices in your home.
