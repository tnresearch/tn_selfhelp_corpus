# Unstable Internet Connection

An unstable internet connection that drops out is one of the most frustrating things. Everyone who has ever experienced the internet cutting out mid-important football match or exciting movie knows this.

Here we will guide you to troubleshoot problems with an unstable internet connection. This guide is for you if your internet has sporadic or regular outages where the internet simply doesn't work.

Start by finding out how the lights on your router should be lit. You can do this by clicking [here](/kundeservice/internet/kom_godt_i_gang/) and reading how the lights should be lit - on most routers, there should be a green or white light in the bottom diode on the router. We only provide support for routers purchased from Telenor.

**Note**: If the fault only affects some of the connected devices, it is a sign that there is a problem with the device and not the router/internet connection. Try restarting the device to see if this resolves the problem.

## How are the lights lit when the fault occurs?

Lights are not lit correctly

Try the following before contacting customer service:

  1. Router failures are often due to a cable losing contact with the power outlet or power disturbances. Therefore, start by making sure that all cables are firmly plugged in and that the cable has no visible damage.
  2. If your router is connected to a power strip/socket, the problem may be that it is not getting enough power. Try connecting it directly to a wall outlet.
  3. Try restarting your router by turning it off and on again. After turning off the router, wait a few seconds before turning it on again.
  4. Check our [service information](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/?gad_source=1&gclid=CjwKCAiArLyuBhA7EiwA-qo80NO_kQykVzxgbZc_av63vMzwXTCU9hUdNdblHmxtQ4H6MZDDvNRMixoC1BwQAvD_BwE) to see if the problem is due to disturbances or outages in your area.

Did you not solve your problem? Then contact Telenor Support on 72 100 100. Before contacting us, please note the following:

  * Date and time when you experienced unstable internet
  * Devices on which you experienced unstable internet

If you experienced an unstable connection on a computer, please note which network card you have and make sure the network card driver is up to date.

Lights are lit correctly

Try the following before contacting customer service:

  1. If you are trying to go online via WiFi, check that your device is connected to the correct network. If you have not changed the WiFi name, you can see the name of your connection under the bottom of the router. If your device is not connected to the network, simply reconnect.
  2. If your devices are connected with a LAN cable, try pressing all the plugs firmly in and check that there is no visible damage to the cable.
  3. Mobile/wireless devices can switch between WiFi and mobile data themselves. Disable mobile data on your device before testing, so you know if the problem is due to the WiFi connection or the connection on the mobile network.
  4. To achieve the best possible connection between your router and the connected devices, you can advantageously place your router and your devices according to the following guidelines:

  * High
  * Free-standing
  * Centrally in the room where you need coverage
  * Away from other electronic devices, cables, etc. Read the full guide with good advice for better WiFi coverage [here](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/).

If you have internet via the 5G network, you can try placing the router in a central location in the home where you know there is good mobile coverage.

Did that not solve your problem? Then contact customer service on 72 100 100.
