# How do I cancel Trygg 48 insurance?

You can cancel the Trygg 48 service in the My Telenor app and My Telenor on
the web.

[My Telenor app:](https://mitt.telenor.se/home) Click on settings,
select the person who has Trygg 48 linked to their mobile phone, then you can cancel
Trygg 48 under "Paid Services".

[My Telenor web:](/mitt-telenor/) Start by selecting the mobile subscription.
Then select the person who has the mobile phone with Trygg 48. Select Settings -
paid services and then Paid Services. Under Paid Services you can then
cancel Trygg48.
