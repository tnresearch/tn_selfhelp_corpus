## Features

### DNA Travel Modem 4G WLAN E5577

Product name: Huawei E5577  

A high-quality travel modem that creates a wireless internet connection for multiple users and devices.  
Connect up to 10 WLAN devices to the modem simultaneously. The modem has a powerful battery, allowing you to surf for  
about 10 hours on a single charge.

  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 150 Mbit/s
  * 4G DC maximum speed 42 Mbit/s 
  * HSPA+ maximum speed 21.1 Mbit/s
  * WLAN b/g/n, WLAN 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
  * USB
  * 14 WLAN devices 
  * Connection for external antenna, connection type TS9
  * Standard SIM card

### Sales package contents:

DNA Modem 4G WLAN E5577 with DNA settings, AC power adapter, charging cable
(Micro-USB), Finnish-language quick start guide.

### Hardware requirements / Supported operating systems:

  * Supported operating systems via USB: Windows® 10 (Q12017), Windows® 8 / 8.1, Windows® 7, Vista, XP SP2/SP3, Mac OS X® 10.7-10.11
  * WLAN: 802.11b/g/n
  * USB slot 1/2/3
  * Internet browser via USB connection or WLAN connection: Microsoft Internet Explorer 6.0 or later, Firefox 2.0 or later, Safari 4.0 or later, Opera 10.0 or later, Chrome 5.0 or later
