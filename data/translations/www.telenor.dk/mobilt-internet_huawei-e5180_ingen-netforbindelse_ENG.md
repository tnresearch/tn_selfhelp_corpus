# No Internet Connection on Mobile Broadband

If you cannot get online with your mobile broadband, it may be because there
is poor or no coverage in the area.

[Check our coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/
"Check our coverage map")

If there is coverage in the area, try to:

  * Take the SIM card out and gently clean it with a clean, dry cloth
  * Update your computer/tablet to the latest software  
  

## Still can't get online?

**1. Uninstall and reinstall your modem**

  * Reinstall the software for your mobile broadband
  * Open a browser and you should be online again

If you have a **Huawai modem**, the software is called "Mobile Partner"  
If you have an **Option modem**, the software is called "Globetrotter Connect"  
If you have a **Sony Ericsson modem**, the software is called "Wireless Manager"  
If you are unsure how to uninstall software, check the manual for your
computer.

**2. Turn off your firewall**

  * Turn off your firewall
  * Go online

How you turn off your firewall depends on your computer and model. Your
manual can guide you if you are unsure how to safely turn your
firewall off and on.

Remember to turn the firewall back on immediately and change the settings of
the firewall so that you can use your mobile broadband without problems in the future.

If the above has not helped, you are always welcome to [contact
us](/kundeservice/kontakt/). And if you have your equipment within reach, we can
help you faster.
