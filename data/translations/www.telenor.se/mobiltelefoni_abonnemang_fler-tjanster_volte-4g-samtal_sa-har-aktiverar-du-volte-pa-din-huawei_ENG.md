# How to activate VoLTE on your Huawei

Once you have activated VoLTE, you can make calls and talk over the 4G network. Keep in mind that
your mobile must always be updated and have the latest software. Read more
about what [VoLTE means here](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/fler-
tjanster/volte-4g-samtal/).

  * ### Open "Settings"

  * ### Scroll down and click on "More"

  * ### Click on "Mobile network"

  * ### Turn on VoLTE calls by sliding the toggle to the right

Now you can make calls and talk over the 4G network!
