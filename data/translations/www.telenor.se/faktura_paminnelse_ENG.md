#  Late Payment and Reminder

If the invoice is not paid on time, you will receive a reminder from us.

## Reminder Fee

When an invoice is paid late, a reminder fee will be added to your next invoice. You can find the amount of the reminder fee in the [price list](/support/priser-villkor-och-blanketter/) for your subscription.

## Default Interest

For each day the invoice remains unpaid, we charge interest. It is calculated as follows: original amount x 24% / 365 x number of late days.

**Example:** The invoice amount is SEK 100 and the invoice is paid 10 days late. Then the default interest will be SEK 0.66 (100 x 0.24 / 365 x 10 = 0.66 SEK).

## Pay Later

If you are unable to pay the invoice on time, please contact us as soon as possible.

## Unsure if you have received an incorrect invoice?

Have you received two invoices during the same month? This is probably because you have both a mobile subscription and a TV or broadband subscription with us. In that case, you will receive two invoices (with different OCR numbers) each month.

If you have received an invoice from us after you have cancelled your subscription and are wondering why, you can read more about [final billing here](/support/vanliga-fragor/faktura/varfor-far-jag-en-faktura-fast-jag-sagt-upp-mitt-abonnemang/).

## See Payment Status

  
In [My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/) you can quickly see if your invoice has been paid or not. _ **  
**_

  * If it says that **the invoice is paid**, you can disregard the reminder.
  * If it says that **the invoice is unpaid**, you need to pay it manually to us. You need to do this even if you normally have direct debit. The next payment will be made via direct debit as usual. 

## Does the invoice show as unpaid even though you know you have paid?

Then you have probably entered incorrect payment details. You can find the correct bank account number and OCR number on the invoice. If you have paid to the wrong OCR number, please [contact us](/support/old-kontakta-oss/). It is good if you have the payment reference number close at hand.
