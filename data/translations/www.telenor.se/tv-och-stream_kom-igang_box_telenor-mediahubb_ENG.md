# Install Telenor Mediahubb

Things to consider before you start connecting **  
**

  * **Use the new cables that come with it.** Older cables may have a different current strength than your new Mediahubb needs, which may cause the service to not work.
  * Make sure **your broadband is running** before starting the Mediahubb and that you are connected to the internet. If you have just purchased broadband from Telenor, this must be installed first. [Get help with that here](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/)
  * Your Mediahubb does not need to be visible, but keep in mind that it **must not be placed on top of other electronic equipment or in a closed cabinet**. Leave at least 20 cm of free space above it to ensure proper ventilation.

OK? Let's go!

  * ### Connect the HDMI cable from the Mediahubb to your TV

  * ### Connect the Mediahubb to your router

  * ### Connect the power cable between the Mediahubb and a power outlet

**Never** use any other power cable than the one you received with the  
Mediahubb. Doing so may cause the Mediahubb to stop working or, in the
worst case, cause a fire.

  * ### Start the Mediahubb with the button on the top

Make sure your TV is on. If you do not get the start screen from the Mediahubb's
installation flow, make sure the correct source is selected on your TV using
your TV's remote control.

  * ### Insert the batteries into the remote control

The bag the remote control is in can be composted, isn't that great?

  * ### Follow the Mediahubb installation flow on your TV

When the Mediahubb is downloading new software, the remote control will not work, this
may take a while. Wait until the download is complete before continuing with
the installation flow.

Done!  
  
Now you can start downloading apps and watching TV!

Once the Mediahubb is connected, you have the option to log in with a Google account
so you can download more apps to the Mediahubb and search for
content using voice. You can skip this during the initial
installation and do it later. You can easily create a Google account by
[googling](http://www.google.se) for "create a Google account" and then
following the instructions.
