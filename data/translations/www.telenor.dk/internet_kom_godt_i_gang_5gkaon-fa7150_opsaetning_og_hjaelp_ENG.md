# Setting up the Kaon FA7150 5G

## Watch this short video guide and get started

## Written guide

**How to set up your router**

  1. Open the small SIM card door and insert your Telenor SIM card.
 **Note**: Do not use sharp objects for this, as it may damage the SIM card.

  2. Connect the power supply to the 5G router and the wall outlet.
 **Note**: Only use the supplied power supply for the router.

**Connection**

  1. Turn on the router by pressing the round button on the back. The router will now light up. Wait for the router to connect to the network. This may take a few minutes, and you can see if the router is online by either the 5G light or the 4G light being solid.
  2. To connect to the router, search for wireless networks. You can find the network name and password under the router or on the included code card.

Enjoy your new internet!

What the LEDs (lights) on the router mean

**LEDs on the router - explanation:**

There are 6 lights: Power, System, Internet, WiFi, 4G and 5G.

  * During startup, you may experience several of the LEDs flashing or turning off. This is perfectly normal and is simply part of the startup process.
  * The Power light and System light should be green. If they are not lit, the router is turned off or is not receiving power. Check that the router is connected to a powered outlet with the supplied power supply, and that the router is turned on.
  * The WiFi light will illuminate green as soon as WiFi is enabled on the router. If it is not lit, WiFi is turned off.
  * The 4G light will illuminate green if the router is on the 4G network.
  * The 5G light will illuminate green if the router is on the 5G network and the signal is good. If it lights up orange, the signal is weak and you should move the router to a location in the house with better coverage. This could be, for example, a windowsill.
  * If you need to reset the router, you can do so by inserting a pin or similar object into the reset hole at the bottom of the router. This must be done while the router is turned on. Hold the reset button for 10 seconds. If the reset is performed correctly, most of the LEDs will turn off and immediately begin to light up/flash.

## More settings

How to log in to the router

The web interface allows you to configure the router via a web browser.

  1. Connect your computer or mobile to the Telenor 5G router with WiFi or LAN cable.
  2. Open a web browser on your computer or mobile, and type **192.168.1.1** in the browser and press **Enter** to open the login page.
  3. Use the username (WebUI Username) and password (WebUI Code) that you find under the bottom of the router.

How to change WiFi name/password

  * Log in to your router by following the guide 'How to log in to the router'. Once logged in, select the menu item **Quick Setup**, then select **WiFi Configuration**.
  * In the **SSID** field, enter your new WiFi name.
  * In the password field, enter your new WiFi password.
  * Once you have entered the new information, select **Apply**.
  * **Note:** If you have been connected via WiFi, your device will be logged off the network and you will need to log on with the new information.

Connection via WPS

If you have a device (printer, smart device, WiFi extender, etc.) that needs to be connected via WPS, it is done as follows:

  * Place the device you want to WPS-pair in immediate proximity to the powered-on router.
  * On the front of the router is a button labeled WPS. Press it once and briefly. You now have 2 minutes to complete the following steps:

  1. Press the WPS button on your own device and wait up to 2 minutes.
  2. WPS pairing is now complete.

How to reset your router

  1. Gently insert the end of a paperclip (or similar) into the small hole at Reset. Hold the button down for about 10 seconds and release the button.
  2. When the button is released, all lights will begin to flash shortly after.
  3. The router may take several minutes to start up, and the lights will flash during this time.
  4. Once the router is set up, you can log in with the router's original network name and password and reinstall your personal settings.

Router specifications

**Router Specs:**

  * 2x TS9 antenna ports
  * 1x 2.5Gbit LAN/WAN port
  * 1x 1Gbit LAN ports
  * 1x USB 3.0 port
