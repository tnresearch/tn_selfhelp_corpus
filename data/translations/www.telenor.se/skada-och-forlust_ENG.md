## Frequently Asked Questions

  * Does my home insurance apply if the fault is not covered by the warranty?

It depends entirely on what your home insurance covers. Start by contacting your
insurance company before sending in your mobile/tablet for repair.

  * What warranty does my product have?

Choose the brand below to read about how the warranty works:

[iPhone](https://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/products/sweden-universal-

  * What does Trygg 48 cover?

Trygg 48 covers, for example, moisture, drop and glass damage, theft and loss.
In the event of approved theft/loss, the insurance also covers up to SEK 10,000 in
traffic costs if someone uses your stolen or lost mobile to make calls, send
SMS and surf.  
  
Read more about Trygg 48 [here](/link/27b3a4e6adb24318a94bbb4c31f3db5a.aspx).

  * What should I do if something happens to my mobile abroad?

It depends on what has happened.  
  
[If the mobile is missing (stolen or
lost)](/link/e4d7733254ae49a6856866239a9b17a1.aspx)  
  
[If the mobile is damaged](/link/28f1c15951954aa7b0e9f54d300950e0.aspx)

**  
If you have Trygg 48**  
If you have the Trygg 48 insurance, you can have a new mobile sent to you even
abroad. If you are within the EU, the only additional cost is the shipping fee of SEK 249. If you are outside the EU, any customs duties may also apply. Remember to provide the correct address, as we cannot be responsible for you receiving your mobile if you do not.

**If you have screen insurance**  
Screen insurance applies regardless of where in the world you are. However, you
are responsible for the return costs yourself. Be sure to provide the correct
return address.

  * What happens if the damage is not covered by insurance?

If you have sustained damage to your mobile or tablet that is not covered by
Trygg 48 or Screen Insurance, you can [start a
service request.](https://service.conmodo.com/neworder?key=7oyoU9RO9W712cZmx6JGDkEFkRhACjT2)
