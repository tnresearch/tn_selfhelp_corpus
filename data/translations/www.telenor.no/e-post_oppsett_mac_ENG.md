# Online Email for Mac Mail

Here we show you how to set up email for the first time or how to
change settings in Mac Mail.

## Change settings or set up email for the first time?

If you are experiencing problems sending or receiving email, you should check
the settings in the email program you are using. Here we show you how to
change the outgoing email settings, but you can also follow the guide
to check other settings in the email program.

This guide also shows you how to set up an email account in
Mac Mail for the first time.

# Step 1

### Select Mail from the menu at the top and then Add Account.

Next step

Account settings for Online Email

 **Account Name** : Optional

 **Username** : Your email address (what is before "@online.no", max 8 characters)

 **Password** : Your password. [Have you forgotten
your password](/kundeservice/e-post/glemt-passord/index.page)?

 **Incoming server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 993 with SSL

 **Outgoing server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 465 with SSL (You can also use 587 with SSL or TLS)

Old setup with incoming server "imap.online.no" and outgoing server
"smtp.online.no" will also work.
