# Have you lost your iPad?

If you lose your iPad and your mobile broadband SIM card, contact us
immediately. We can help with the police report and block the SIM card so no one
can misuse your subscription.

**Block your SIM card:**

  * [Contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/) or visit your [local Telenor store](/find-butik/) and get a new SIM card right away.
  * Has your iPad been stolen?

**Report the theft to the police**  
**[Click here](https://www.politi.dk/da/borgerservice/anmeldelser/tyveri/ "Click
here") to report the theft directly to the police.

**Report the theft to your insurance company**  
When you report the theft to your insurance company, they will usually ask for:

  * Your policy number
  * Receipt for the police report
  * Mobile receipt
  * Description of make and model

If you purchased your iPad from Telenor, Customer Service can help you obtain a
copy of your purchase receipt.

## Do you need to reactivate your SIM card?

If you find your iPad again, you can have your SIM card reactivated either in one of our
stores or by [contacting us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).
