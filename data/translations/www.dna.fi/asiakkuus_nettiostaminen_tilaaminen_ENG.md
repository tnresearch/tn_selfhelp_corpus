# Ordering

Do business when it suits you: online, at a DNA Store, by phone, or
in chat. We are happy to help!

## Order securely online

When ordering in the DNA Online Store, your identity is verified depending on the payment method
either by online banking credentials or with a Mobile Certificate. When paying
by credit card, the payment is always made via a secure SSL connection.

## Cancelling Your Order

Right of Withdrawal

You have the right* to cancel an order in distance selling by notifying DNA within 14
days. Distance selling refers to an order made either online or by telephone. The 14-day cancellation period begins after receiving the order confirmation. For goods, the cancellation period begins, however, upon receipt of the goods, if the product is received later than the order confirmation.

Returning the product is free of charge. Read more on the Returns and Warranty page.

*According to Section 15 of Chapter 6 of the Consumer Protection Act.

## Frequently Asked Questions

The product is not available. How can I find out the product availability
in a store?

  
You can see the availability situation in the DNA Online Store when ordering; if the product is not
available at that moment, we will inform you. Depending on the device, it is worth trying e.g. another color or returning later as the availability situation varies constantly.

Why do I need bank credentials when ordering?

By identifying yourself with bank credentials or a Mobile Certificate, you confirm
your identity for the purpose of concluding the agreement and ensure secure
transactions. You will not be charged for authentication.

If you have chosen online banking payment as your payment method, the charge will occur only
at the time of payment or later with the invoice - depending on your order.

Do the products I order have a warranty?

The warranty period for devices is always manufacturer-specific, but for phones,
tablets and SIM-card enabled smartwatches you get a longer 3-year DNA Warranty for the device you purchase. DNA's warranty extension
extends the manufacturer's warranty to 36 months when the manufacturer's warranty is shorter than this.

The warranty period is always stated separately in the product information.

How can I make changes to my existing services?

With the Oma DNA app, chatbot and by logging in to DNA.fi, you can manage
the most important things related to your account, whenever it suits you best: pay bills, get to know your services and modify your orders as well
take advantage of current customer benefits. More information on the [Service Channels
page](https://www.dna.fi/asiointikanavat).

Can I pay for the product online and pick it up from a store?

Yes, this is possible with selected products. You will see "Pick-up from DNA Store" as a delivery option in the online store when the product in question is
available for free online ordering and pick-up from the store. You will receive
a text message when the product is ready for pick-up.

Currently, Click & Collect is only possible when you pay for the product
at once, in one installment. For some products, a reservation in the store is also possible - in this case, you pay for the order only in the store. Read more detailed instructions
on the phone presentation page!
