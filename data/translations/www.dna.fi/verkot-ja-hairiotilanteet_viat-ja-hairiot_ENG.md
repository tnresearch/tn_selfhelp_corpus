## Reporting a Disruption

You can report a fault or disruption by calling customer service or
through the chatbot. We are available 24/7 for fault and disruption situations.

If it concerns, for example, a modem or some other device you purchased from DNA, please
have the device at hand when you call.

[Call 044 144 044](tel:%20044144044)

Calling and waiting in the queue for customer service costs the same as a normal domestic call.
When calling from abroad, the call will be charged according to the international rate list.
Calling from a DNA landline subscription is free.
