# Problems Sending and Receiving MMS

## If You Can't Send MMS

  * Restart your mobile phone. Restarting your phone will automatically fix any errors and search for new software updates. If a software update is available, we recommend that you download it.
  * Are you within data coverage? An internet connection is required to send and receive MMS.
[Check coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/ "Check
coverage map")

  * Can you send an MMS to yourself? If you receive your own MMS, the problem likely lies in the recipient's mobile setup.
  * If your phone has just been reactivated after being blocked, it may take some time before you can send/receive MMS as normal.

**Correct Your MMS Settings**
** In the "MMS" section, the following should be stated:**
**

  * In the APN field, enter telenor (lowercase)
  * The Username field should be empty
  * The Password field should be empty
  * In the MMSC field, enter http://mms.telenor.dk
  * In the MMS port field, enter 8080
  * Turn your phone off and on

## If You Don't Receive MMS on Your Phone

  * Check that your phone is set up to be able to receive MMS
  * Your phone was out of data coverage when the MMS was sent to you
  * Your phone doesn't have enough space to receive an MMS. Delete any images, apps, or other content that is filling up your phone
  * You were abroad, and data roaming was turned off on your phone when the MMS was sent
