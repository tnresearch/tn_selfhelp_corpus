# Speed Test

On this page, you can test your speed. The maximum speed of your subscription depends on your connection. The following can influence the result of the test:

  * Programs on your computer that use the internet connection
  * If you have multiple devices on the same connection - computers, mobiles, tablets, game consoles, etc.
  * The packaging of the data that is downloaded/sent on your internet connection

To get an accurate result, you should therefore close all programs and make sure that there are no other devices on the network. It is an advantage if your computer is directly connected to Telenor's equipment using the cable we sent you.

[Click here if you cannot see the speed test](https://telenor.speedtestcustom.com/).

## Why does the test show a lower speed than the one included in my subscription?

There may be several factors that play a role in the speed when you do a speed test on your connection.

First and foremost, it may be that you are taking the speed test somewhere in your home where there is a weak WiFi connection. If this is the case, the result of the speed test may show a slower speed than the one included in your subscription. Fortunately, there are several things you can do to optimize your wireless network at home. Take a look at our [guide to better WiFi](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/) and get the most out of your internet connection.

Also remember that open programs on your computer while you are taking the speed test can help to affect the speed. Therefore, it is a very good idea to close your open programs on your computer so that you get the most accurate result.

You will also often measure a lower speed if streaming services are running while you are taking the speed test. So make sure to close Netflix, HBO and Viaplay etc., so you get a more accurate picture of what your speed actually is.

## What can you use the speed test for?

With our speed test, you can get an insight into how fast your internet speed is at your home. The speed test measures the speed of your internet precisely at the time you take the test. The test helps to give you an overview of what you can use your internet connection for - does the speed meet your needs? Or do you maybe need an even faster connection? Your internet connection determines, for example, how many people can use the connection simultaneously, how many devices can be connected at the same time, and how quickly you can download and upload content.

## Speed Test: Questions and Answers

Good advice for the speed test

**Ongoing downloads**

Make sure you are not downloading while completing the speed test, as this will degrade your download speed and therefore not give an accurate picture.

**Streaming music or video**
Make sure you are not streaming music or video while completing the speed test. Streaming audio or video requires downloading relatively large amounts of data, and this will therefore degrade your download speed when you take the test.

**Multiple users simultaneously**
Make sure there are no other devices on the network while you complete the test. Check smartphones, tablets and computers and log them off the network.

How accurate is the speed test?

There are a number of factors that can affect the result. Therefore, the theoretically highest possible speed you can achieve on your connection is tested.

If you experience black holes in your internet connection, your speed suddenly drops or your actual speed is lower than your subscription, you can get tips to fix [slow speed on your internet](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/).

Does the speed test work for both wireless internet and regular broadband?

Yes - the test measures the speed of both wireless connections and regular broadband connections.

It's nice to have wireless internet at home, but unfortunately it also costs a little on the speed. Fortunately, you can easily [optimize your wireless network](/link/3a69a96fe05a48fba95e257eb8cc8a41.aspx).
