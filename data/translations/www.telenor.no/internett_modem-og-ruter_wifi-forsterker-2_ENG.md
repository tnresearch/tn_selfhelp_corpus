# Help with WiFi Extender II

Here you will find information about your WiFi Extender, as well as guides and help
for connection.

## About WiFi Extender II

Our new extender, WiFi Extender II, functions as a link between your
[WiFi Router II](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter-2/) and
the area where you are struggling with poor coverage. It does not need to be connected with
a cable - it communicates wirelessly with the WiFi router. The extender also has two
ethernet ports, so you can connect equipment that requires a cable.

An extender is therefore a quick and easy solution to provide better coverage in
places where the existing wireless network does not reach.

WiFi Extender II is only compatible with [WiFi Router
II](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter-2/), and with one or
more extenders connected to the WiFi router, you get a MESH network at home.

## Placement of the extender

To achieve the best possible coverage in the house, it is important to place the extender
in the right place. If you place the extender too close to the WiFi router, it
will not have any effect. You should therefore place the extender on the edge of
the coverage area of your wireless router.

But be careful not to place the extender too far from the WiFi router – you should
place it somewhere where you still have a good speed on the wireless network.

We recommend that you try placing the extender in several places and see where you
get the best WiFi coverage. You check this by looking at the light on the extender:

 **The extender lights up green if it has good signals. A yellow light means the
signal is okay. A red light means you should move it closer to the WiFi router.**
