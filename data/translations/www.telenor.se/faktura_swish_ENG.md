# Pay your invoice with Swish

If you wish, you can pay your invoices with Swish in My Telenor.

The payment is smooth and you avoid filling in OCR numbers, amount, etc. My
Telenor is available both here [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) and as an [
app](/handla/tjanster/appar/mitt-telenor/).

## 
Good to know

  * You don't need to worry about accidentally paying an invoice with Swish multiple times. It simply isn't possible.
  * You cannot top up prepaid cards or buy extra data with Swish.
  * It is not possible to schedule or time payments.
  * Do you have direct debit? Then your invoice is paid automatically every month, and you neither can nor need to pay it with Swish.
  * You cannot receive refunds from us via Swish.
