# Help with ZTE MU5120

Here you will find help and guides for your 5G router for Mobile Broadband.

## Simple steps to get online

  * #### Insert SIM card

If you have a large-sized SIM card, you need to break out a Nano-SIM.
The power adapter must not be connected when you insert the SIM card.  

You can find the SIM card opening on the side of the router.  

  * #### Connect to power

Press and hold the power button until the screen lights up. Then the
device automatically connects to the Internet.  
  

You can also connect to the computer via a USB port, and then access the
Internet.  

  * #### Connect to WiFi

 **Windows**

    1. Left-click on the wireless network icon at the bottom next to the clock and date

    2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press Connect

    3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router or the self-selected password and press Connect  

 **Mac**

    1. Press the Airport icon for wireless network

    2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press Connect

    3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router or the self-selected password and press Connect  

 **Samsung/Android:**

    1. Go to settings on your mobile phone

    2. Select Connections

    3. Select Wi-Fi

    4. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press on it

    5. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router or the self-selected password and press Connect

    6. Alternatively, in the display on the router, press the WiFi symbol, press Main SSID or Guest SSID, open QR code reader and select connect  

 **iPhone/iPad:**

    1. Go to settings

    2. Select Wi-Fi

    3. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press on it

    4. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router or the self-selected password and press Connect

    5. Alternatively, in the display on the router, press the WiFi symbol, press Main SSID or Guest SSID, open QR code reader and select connect

  * #### How do I reset the router?

If you are experiencing problems connecting to the internet, it can often help to
reset the router. This resets the router to the settings it had when you received it.

 **Resetting is done by:**

    1. Press and hold the on/off button for about 3 seconds to restore the device to factory settings

  * #### Where can I find the latest software?

Here's how to find the latest update on the router:

    1. Press "Settings" in the display on the router

    2. Press "Update"

    3. Press "Check for update"
