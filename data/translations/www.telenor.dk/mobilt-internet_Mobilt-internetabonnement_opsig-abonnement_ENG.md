# Cancel Your Mobile Internet Subscription

After your 6-month commitment period, you can always cancel your mobile internet subscription.

If you want to cancel the subscription, please [contact
us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).

[Test your speed](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/hastighed/hastighedstest/
"Hastighedstest")  
  

## Your Final Bill

When you cancel your subscription, we will adjust your final bill to cover the days up to the date of your cancellation.

If you have used data exceeding your subscription during your cancellation period, you will receive a final bill from us approximately 10 days after your cancellation period ends. For example, you may have used data abroad or purchased additional data during your cancellation period.

Your cancellation period is 30 days.

## Cancel Direct Debit

If you have signed up for Direct Debit for your subscription, we recommend that you cancel the agreement only after you have paid your final bill. If you cancel the agreement beforehand, you will receive your bill by mail and be charged a billing fee of 49 DKK.
