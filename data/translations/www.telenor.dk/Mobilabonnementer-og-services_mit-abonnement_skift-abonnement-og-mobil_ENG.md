# Can I change both subscription and mobile?

If your mobile subscription is free of the commitment period, and you want to change
your mobile subscription, you also have the opportunity to buy a new mobile. This
also means that you start a new commitment period. You can see all our
mobiles [here](/shop/mobiler/).

You can also change, even if you are in a commitment, but then you will have to pay off
the old one, while paying for the new one.

## Save money on your new mobile with MobileTrade

If you want to replace your mobile, you can save money with
[MobileTrade](/shop/landingpage/mobilbyt/), just remember that the commitment period
remains the same, regardless of whether you choose to keep the phone or sell it
with MobileTrade.
