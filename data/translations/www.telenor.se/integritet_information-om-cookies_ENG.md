# Information about cookies

Sometimes they are called cookies, sometimes biscuits. But it's exactly the same thing.
On Telenor's website, cookies are used for various purposes, and as a visitor you have the right to know this according to the law on electronic communication. You also have the right to choose which cookies you accept, and we never store any information in your browser or on your device without your consent.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are saved in your browser or on your device when you visit a website. They can be used for various reasons. Some cookies are necessary and activate basic functions on the website and therefore cannot be turned off. Cookies are also used for analysis and statistics or for personalized marketing. Information from a cookie can also be used to track a visitor's browsing on other websites that use the same type of cookie.

## What type of cookies are used?

On telenor.se, two different types of cookies are used, persistent cookies and session cookies. A distinction is also made between first-party cookies and third-party cookies.

    * Persistent cookies save a file on your device for a longer period of time with an expiration date. When the expiration date has passed, the cookie is deleted from your device. If you revisit the website after the expiration date has passed, a cookie will be stored again.

    * Session cookies are temporarily stored on your device when you are on the website. As soon as you close your browser, this cookie is deleted. The purpose of these cookies is, for example, to keep track of the language you have chosen.

    * First-party cookies mean that the cookie has the same domain name sender as the website, in our case it is us at Telenor who is the sender.

    * Third-party cookies come from a different domain name sender than the one you are visiting. A cookie on our website therefore does not have Telenor.se as the sender. The same third-party cookie can be used by several different websites that collaborate to understand how visitors browse between websites. Information from these cookies can be shared with Telenor but also used for other purposes. This is controlled by the respective actor who is responsible for and delivers it.

## Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies because they are necessary for the website, for analysis and statistics, or for personalized marketing.

    * Necessary cookies. Some cookies are needed for the website to function. They can, for example, affect navigation, the shopping cart, or access to logged-in mode. You cannot therefore choose to turn off these cookies.  
  

    * Analysis and statistics. These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website. With anonymized data, we can see, for example, which parts our visitors use the most and for how long.  
  

    * Personalized marketing. There are specific cookies to map how a visitor has moved between different websites. The purpose of these is to be able to show relevant advertising to you as a customer.

## Which cookies do we use?

The number of cookies used on our website is many. By clicking on "Cookie Settings" at the bottom of the page, you will find information about what type of cookie we use and the purpose of the cookie.

## Manage cookie settings

You can always change your cookie settings. You do this by clicking on the text "Cookie Settings" at the bottom of the website.

The only cookies that are not selectable are those we call necessary cookies. They are needed for the website to function. You can choose whether you want to accept cookies for analysis and statistics as well as marketing.

## More information

Read more about cookies on the Swedish Post and Telecom Authority's website [www.pts.se.](http://www.pts.se)

Telenor Sverige AB is the data controller for the processing of your personal data.
