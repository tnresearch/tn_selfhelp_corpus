# Problems connecting to wifi (Technicolor 799)

This information applies to you if you

  * are having trouble connecting to wifi and
  * have the Technicolor 799 router and
  * have performed a factory reset* of the router

_* A factory reset is sometimes done to solve hardware problems. Then
all settings are deleted.  
_  
Here you will find help to connect to your wifi again.

## Here's how:

  * ### 

Search for available wifi networks on your device.  
  

**If you have a computer with Windows or macOS:**

Click on the arrow or wifi symbol. You will find it at the top or bottom
right of the desktop.

**If you have an iPhone, iPad or Android mobile:**

Tap Settings and then select Wifi.

  * ### 

Previously, you could access two wifi networks via your router. Now you have one.
Select the network that starts with **TN_wifi**. See the image above for example.  
  
  

  * ### 

Log in with the password you normally use.  
  
Done! Now you can continue surfing.
