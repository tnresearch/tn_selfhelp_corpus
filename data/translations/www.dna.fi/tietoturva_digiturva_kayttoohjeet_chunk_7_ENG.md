## Personal Data Protection

Setting up Personal Data Monitoring

#### Setting up on Phone or Tablet

1. Open the DNA Digiturva app.

2. Select **Personal Data Protection**.

3. Press **Continue** at the end of the summary.

4. First, add an email address. Notifications will be sent to this email if data is detected in data breaches. Press **\+ Add item** and enter the desired email address.

5. You must verify the email address you provided. Do so by opening the verification email and pressing **Verify Email Address**. The message will come from <no-reply@f-secure.com>

6. After this, you can add new items to be monitored.

#### Setting up on Computer

1. Open DNA Digiturva.

2. Click **Start** in the Personal Data Monitoring section.

3. Press **Continue** at the end of the summary.

4. First, add an email address. Notifications will be sent to this email if data is detected in data breaches. **\+ Add item** and enter the desired email address.

5. You must verify the email address you provided. Do so by opening the verification email and pressing **Verify Email Address**. The message will come from <no-reply@f-secure.com>

6. After this, you can add new items to be monitored.

Adding Items

1. Open the **Personal Data Protection** section in the DNA Digiturva app.

2. To set up a new item to be monitored, press **Add item**.

3. Select the type of the new item.

4. Enter the information and press **Save**.

5. The app will search for the item you added in data breaches and show you the search results.

Removing Items

1. Open the **Personal Data Monitoring/Protection** section in the DNA Digiturva app.

2. Press **Your Information**.

3. Press the three dots next to the item to be deleted and press **Remove entry**.

4. The app will check if you are sure you want to delete the monitoring item, press **Delete**.

Data Breach Archiving

1. Open the **Personal Data Protection** section in the DNA Digiturva app.

2. Open the **Data Breaches** section by pressing it.

3. Open the desired data breach by selecting it.

4. After the data breach information, you can choose to archive the data breach by pressing **Archive this data breach**.

5. The app will confirm that you have taken the necessary steps to minimize risks and that you are sure you want to archive the data breach. Press **Archive**.

6. You can find archived data breaches by selecting the data breaches section and pressing **Archived Data Breaches**. Select the desired breach from the list. This way you can still review the breach information.
