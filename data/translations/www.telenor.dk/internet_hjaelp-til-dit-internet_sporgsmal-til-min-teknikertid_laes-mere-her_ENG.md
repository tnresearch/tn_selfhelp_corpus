# Questions for my technician visit

The technician will arrive on the agreed date, which you can find in the email
confirmation you received from us when you ordered your new internet. You can
also see there whether it's an installation consultant or a regular technician
who is coming.

It is important to be home at the agreed time – or to have a person present
who is 18 years or older. If the technician is not let in, the visit will be
considered a wasted trip, and you will be charged for a wasted visit.

The area where the technician needs to work must be cleared so that furniture
or similar does not obstruct the technician.

Do I need to be home when the technician arrives?

It is important that an adult is present at the address when the technician
arrives. That is, a person who is 18 years or older.

If the technician is not let in, the visit will be considered a wasted trip,
and you will be charged for a wasted visit.

Can I change my technician visit time?

If you need to change your technician visit time, you can do so at [My
Telenor.](/login/ "My Telenor")

How do I prepare for the technician's visit?

It is important that you or another adult are present at the address to open
the door for the technician.

The area where the technician needs to work must be cleared so that furniture
or similar does not obstruct the technician.

What does a technician visit cost?

Technician visits in connection with the purchase of internet from Telenor are
generally free. In rare cases, there may be a cost if a technician visit is
necessary. The price of a technician visit depends on the provider, but you
will always be informed of the price in advance.

NOTE! If the technician is not let in on the agreed date, the visit will be
considered a wasted trip, and you will be charged for a wasted visit.
