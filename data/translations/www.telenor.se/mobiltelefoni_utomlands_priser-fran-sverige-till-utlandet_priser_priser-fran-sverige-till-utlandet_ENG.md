## Good to know before you call

  * All prices include VAT.
  * Prices apply to subscriptions. [Prices for prepaid cards](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/priser-fran-sverige-till-utlandet/priser/priser-fran-sverige-till-utlandet-for-kontantkort/)
  * Calls to Pay-, Premium- and Satellite numbers may have different prices. [Satellite prices](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/satellitpriser/)
  * If you call someone who is abroad with their Swedish mobile, you pay for calls within Sweden. But the person you are calling must pay to receive the call. [Choose country here](/roaming/) to see the cost.

## Frequently asked questions

  * Why can't I call Syria anymore?

Due to the fact that the EU has imposed sanctions on SyriaTel, it is no longer possible to call, text or send MMS to SyriaTel customers. SyriaTel customers also cannot call, text or send MMS to Telenor customers. We understand that this may create problems for you as a customer and apologize for it, but at the same time it is important for Telenor to comply with laws and regulations. The measure does not affect traffic to and from Syria's other operator, MTN.
