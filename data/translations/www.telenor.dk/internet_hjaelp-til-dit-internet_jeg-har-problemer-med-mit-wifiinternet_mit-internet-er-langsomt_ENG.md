# **My internet is slow**

Is it taking an unusually long time to download files, send and receive emails,
or open websites? Then you've come to the right place. In this guide
we will help you find any errors in your setup so you can get
flying on the net again.

If the error only affects some of the connected devices, it is a sign that there
are problems with the device and not the router/internet connection. Try to
restart the device to see if this resolves the problem.

This guide is for you who are experiencing internet that is slower than the
speed you pay for. Start by taking a speed test by downloading
Ookla's speedtest app, [Speedtest by Ookla](https://www.speedtest.net/apps).
If you take the test on your WiFi network, it is important that you physically be
right next to your router when you take the test.

Your actual speed depends on many things, but as a good
rule of thumb, you should be able to get your full speed if you do a
speed test via a speed test app, on a newer device, via LAN cable. Speeds
measured via WiFi will often be lower than the subscribed one, as even the newest WiFi-
technologies cannot support the highest speeds we offer.

## Is the challenge sporadic or constant?

Sporadic

If you are online via WiFi, the location of your devices in relation to
the router has a great influence on the experienced speed. To achieve the best
possible connection between your router and the connected devices, you can
advantageously place your router and your devices according to the following guidelines:

  * High
  * Free-standing
  * Centrally in the room where you need coverage
  * Away from other electronic devices, cables, etc.

If this did not solve your problem, please contact Telenor Support. Before you call, please
feel free to note the following:

  * Date and time when you experienced slow speed
  * Devices on which you experienced slow internet

If you experienced a slow connection on a computer, please note which
network card you have and make sure that the driver for the network card is updated.

Constant

Try the following before contacting customer service:

  1. If you are trying to go online via WiFi, check that your device is connected to the correct network. If you haven't changed the WiFi name, you can see the name of your connection under the bottom of the router. If your device is not connected to the network, simply reconnect
  2. If your devices are connected with a LAN cable, try pressing all the plugs in firmly and check that there are no visible damage to the cable. Also check if the cable is of type Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6e or newer. You can read on the cable what type it is. If the cable is of type Cat5, it is outdated
  3. Mobile/wireless devices can switch between WiFi and mobile data themselves. Therefore, check if your device is on the mobile network when you experience the error
  4. To achieve the best possible connection between your router and the connected devices, you can advantageously place your router and your devices according to the following guidelines:

  * High
  * Free-standing
  * Centrally in the room where you need coverage
  * Away from other electronic devices, cables, etc. Read the full guide with good advice for better WiFi coverage [here](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/).

**Slow connection on work computer**

If you experience a slow connection on a work computer, it may be caused
by a VPN connection or locally installed security software. Contact
your employer's IT department to see if they can help you.

Did you not solve your problem? Then contact Telenor Support on 72 100 100. Before you
contact us, please feel free to note the following:

  * Date and time when you experienced slow speed
  * Devices on which you experienced slow internet

If you experienced a slow connection on a computer, please note which
network card you have and make sure the driver for the network card is updated.
