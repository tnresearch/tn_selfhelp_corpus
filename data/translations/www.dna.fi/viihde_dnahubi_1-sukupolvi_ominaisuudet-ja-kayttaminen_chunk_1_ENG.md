### Hassle-free Upgrade to a New One

When you have a valid DNA TV subscription, switching from one device to a new one is
easy, we do almost everything for you. All you need to do is order a new DNA
Entertainment Box to your home and then contact us. You can also conveniently
purchase a new device and transfer the service at a DNA Store.

You will receive instructions for transferring the service in your email when you place your order.
