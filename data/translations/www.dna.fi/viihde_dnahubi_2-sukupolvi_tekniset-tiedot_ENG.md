# Technical Specifications

The DNA Entertainment Box works in all homes, as it is Antenna Ready HD- and Cable Ready HD-compatible.

The entertainment box's channel offering can be viewed in IPTV quality over the internet throughout Finland. Nelonen, Jim, Liv and Hero channels are visible over the internet if the entertainment box is used with a DNA broadband connection.

The package includes the entertainment box, power cord, antenna cable, Bluetooth remote control, USB-C, HDMI and Ethernet cables, and a quick guide.

## Basic Features:

4K/HD antenna and cable tuner (2 pcs)

7-day program guide with search functions

Google Play app store

Parental lock

4K support

## Image:

4K/UHD, HDR10

4K video resolution 3840 x 2160p @24/30/50/60 Hz​

HD video resolution 1920 x 1080p @24/30/50/60 Hz​

HDMI 2.1​

## Audio:

Possibility to connect a separate audio system (Mini-S/PDIF optical).

## Memory:

RAM memory: 3 GB

Internal memory: 16 GB

## Operating System:

Android TV 12

## Dimensions:

Height: 24 mm​

Width: 138 mm​

Depth: 137 mm

## Connections and Connectivity:

Connectors:

  * 1 Gb Ethernet RJ45 port (for internet connection, entertainment services and updates)
  * HDMI 2.1
  * USB-A 2.0 (for example, for an external hard drive, mouse or keyboard)

Bluetooth: 5.0 BLE support

Wi-Fi: 1 X Wi-Fi 6 , 2 x 2 dual band (2.4 & 5 GHz)

## Other Features:

Note. Devices sold after April 29, 2024 no longer support hard drive recording.

Channel recording to an external hard drive is not possible after the Android TV 12 operating system update (11/2024).
