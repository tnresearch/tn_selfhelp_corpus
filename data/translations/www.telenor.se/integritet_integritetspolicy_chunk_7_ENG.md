##

  * Read more about legitimate interest

We have the right to use your data if it is necessary for purposes relating to our or a third party's legitimate interest (for example, our interest in sending direct marketing to you). We may only use your data on this basis if we assess that our interest in using your data outweighs your right not to be subject to the processing. This is called a balancing of interests. Our interest in using your data must be well-founded and justified. Often, you may also have an interest in us using your data, for example, when you want to chat with us on the web.

[Contact our
Data Protection Officer](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt2) if
you want to know more about how we have made the balancing of interests for a specific purpose.
Below you will find more information about when we have assessed that we have a legitimate
interest.

  * Read more about consent

Sometimes we use your data only if you have approved it, that is, if you have given consent. Most often you give your consent while logged in to My Telenor or within the scope of the service you purchase from us. You always have the right to withdraw a given consent. You can find more information on how to
[withdraw consent](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#sam123) here.
