# T-We App - Apple TV

Here you will find help with logging in and using the T-We app with Apple TV

## Apple TV

If you have **Apple TV HD (4th generation) or Apple TV 4K (all
generations)**, download the T-We App from the App Store, and use the Apple TV
remote to navigate.

If you have an **older Apple TV (3rd generation and earlier)**, you can use
the T-We App on your iPhone or iPad to navigate/find content, and play content
on Apple TV using Airplay. This only works when the Apple TV and
iPhone/iPad are on the same network.

You will find the Airplay icon when you have started playback in the T-We App. When you
click on it, you will be able to choose which Apple TV to play on
(if you have multiple). You can also use this method with newer Apple TV.

If you have set restrictions on using AirPlay to your Apple TV, you must
approve the device you are trying to use first.

### Logging in on Apple TV

 **Open the T-We app on AppleTV - the rest of the login happens on your mobile.  
**

  1. Open the camera on your mobile

  2. Scan the QR code that appears on the TV screen

  3. Log in via your mobile. The mobile number of the person logging in must be registered in our systems

  4. Follow the steps on your mobile

  5. When you see the connection image, continue on AppleTV (see the image)

## Favorites List

With T-We, you can set up a list of [your
favorite channels](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/favorittliste/).

If you want to only browse your favorites list when watching TV, you can do
the following:

  1. Go to live TV

  2. Scroll down on the Apple TV remote

  3. Go to Favorite Channels

  4. Select the desired channel

When you select a channel from the favorites list, you can only browse between these. To
return to the overview of all channels, you can repeat the instructions
above.
