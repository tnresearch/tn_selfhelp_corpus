## Features

  
**Product Name: Huawei 4G Router 3 Prime B818-263**  
  
The DNA Home Modem 4G WiFi B818 brings fast 4G internet to your home effortlessly.
With a wireless network, you can share the connection with all your devices. CAT19-level
features enable a theoretical maximum download speed of up to 1.6
Gbit/s.  

  * A 4G home modem utilizing the latest mobile network technology
  * Fast internet and wireless network for all home devices
  * Effortless installation and use
  * Customized for DNA's internet
  * Compatibility with Micro-SIM card size
  * WiFi sharing for up to 64 different devices
  * Theoretical maximum download speed of up to 1.6 Gbit/s (Actual speed depends on the strength of the received signal, the capabilities of the mobile network, and the type of subscription used.)

### Package Contents

  * DNA Home Modem 4G WiFi B818
  * Power adapter
  * Ethernet cable
  * Quick guide
  * Warranty card
