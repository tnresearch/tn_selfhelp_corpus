# How do we use your data in our communication?

When we talk to you, we think it's important that we talk about things that
are important to both of us. As we often say, relationships should be earned.
Therefore, we at Telenor believe it is meaningful that we can tailor our
communication with you.

****

  * ### How does Telenor create relevant and personalized offers?

To get an idea of which products and services appeal to different
customer groups and customer types, we explore how our customers behave. It is
then essential for us that this is done on anonymized data, which means that
a unique user cannot be identified. Once we have built a profile, we can
create personal and relevant offers and match them.

This is called profiling, and it uses customer data, information from
public registers and other advertising networks. We do all of this on the legal
basis called legitimate interest.

To get even more relevant information, we can use traffic data, but we only
do so if you have given your consent.

  * ### Why do you see a specific ad?

Using the profiles, we create groups that we believe might be interested in a
specific offer or other marketing. Sometimes we can also get help from an
advertising network to do the profiling on the data they have. Other times, we
create such a group ourselves, on both customers and non-customers, and send
it to an advertising network for a specific campaign. When you then go to a
website where we show our ads, we can in this way ensure that you see an ad
with content that is relevant to you.

We can also send out campaigns based on profiling to you via email,
SMS or letter.

  * ### How do we create more relevant offers for you as a customer?

With the help of various partners, we can follow how a user's journey looks,
which ads seem to be more interesting and whether any of these lead to a
purchase. In this way, we can strengthen our profiles and thus learn which
ads are relevant to you as a user, and which are not. We receive this
information directly from the pages where our ads are displayed and from
users' visits to Telenor.se.

#### Necessary cookies

Always active

Needed for the website to function securely and correctly. Therefore, they
cannot be turned off. Necessary cookies enable basic functions, so that you
can, for example, navigate between pages or access secure areas of the
website.

Information about cookies‎

#### Functional cookies

Functional cookies

These cookies allow the website to provide enhanced functionality and
personalization. They can be set by us or by third-party providers whose
services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, some
or all of these services may not work as intended.

Information about cookies‎

#### Statistical and Analytical

Statistical and Analytical

These cookies give us information about how you use our site and allow us to
improve the user experience and improve our website. They can be set by us or
by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do
not allow these cookies, we may have limited insight into how our website is
used and therefore have a harder time improving your experience.

Information about cookies‎

#### Marketing

Marketing

These cookies are set on our website by our advertising partners. They can
be used by these companies to create a profile of your interests and show
you relevant ads on other websites. They do not directly store personal
information, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and
internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less
targeted ads.
