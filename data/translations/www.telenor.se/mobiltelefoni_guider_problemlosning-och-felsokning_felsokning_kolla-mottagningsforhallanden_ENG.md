# Check if the reception can be better

There may be things around you that weaken the signals from the nearest
transmitter. Check through the items below!

### Buildings

If there is a large concrete building between you and the nearest transmitter,
the signal will be weakened. And if you are in a building with many walls,
the coverage may be perceived as poor. Try standing by a window and see if
the coverage is better there.

### Weather and wind

The mobile signal is affected by the different seasons. The best coverage day
is actually a frosty winter morning. While wind, rain and snow can dampen
the signal. Wait for a change in the weather to see what happens with the
coverage.

### Mountains and trees

If you are standing on a slope, or if you are surrounded by mountains or
trees, the network signal may have difficulty reaching your mobile. Go up to
a height or stand on an open area to see if the coverage is better there.

### Many people in one place

If you are standing in the middle of a crowd, many mobiles are fighting to
capture the signal at the same time. To be able to maintain a stable signal
requires a lot from your mobile antenna. Try again when there are not so
many people and mobiles nearby.

### Mobile case

Some mobile cases can significantly dampen the radio signals to your mobile,
which in turn affects the connection. Try removing the mobile case to see if
the coverage improves.
