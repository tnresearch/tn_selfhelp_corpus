# Automatic data limit for surfing

Unexpectedly high bills when you return from your trip abroad are a thing of the past.
There is an automatic data limit on your subscription. The service is
also free.

This means that if you surf abroad for SEK 625 during the same month, we will block
you from surfing further. You will also not be able to send or receive
mms.

## How it works

When you have surfed for SEK 500 (or 80% of your data limit), we will send a
first SMS. The next SMS will come when you have surfed up the entire amount. If you want
to continue surfing, just increase the limit to the next amount. Everything is stated in the SMS we
send.  
  
Multiple countries, same limit  
You retain the same limit even if you visit multiple countries during your
trip abroad. Example: you use up the amount in Mexico and travel on to
Guatemala. Then the limit remains. If you want to continue surfing – increase the limit.  
[Here you will find information about roaming in different countries.](/roaming/)

## If you want to increase your limit

The levels you can choose from are:

  * SEK 625/month incl. VAT (your automatic level from the start)
  * SEK 2,500/month incl. VAT
  * SEK 10,000/month incl. VAT

You choose by sending MAX followed by the limit to 222. For example "MAX
2500" It may take up to one hour from when you increase the limit until
the service is active again. Therefore, we recommend that you ensure you choose the right level before your trip. Please note that the data limit is reset when
the billing period expires. You can see your billing period by logging in
to My Telenor – either [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in [our
app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/). It costs nothing to use
the data limit.

## Common questions about surfing abroad

  * How much does it cost to call/SMS/surf abroad?

[Here you will find information about our
foreign prices](/link/a728a2eee9b24f87867517e059303cae.aspx)

  * Do I need to turn off surfing abroad?

It depends on where you are going, and on which subscription you have. But within
EU/EEA you surf at the same price as you do at home, and therefore do not need
to turn off surfing.

Are you going to a country in Europe that is not part of the EU/EEA? Then you can find out
what applies to you and your subscription in the country you are visiting by
sending [the name of the country you are going to visit to 10615.
](/link/eb119f9f060843c0b746b0831425f9b6.aspx)You will then receive the welcome SMS with
your prices right now.

  
If you are going to a country where you do not have free surfing and want to avoid
costs completely, we recommend that you [turn off data-
roaming](/link/bc7779ed9f1648b88dbd9442d51a3bc5.aspx) before you board
the plane in Sweden.

  * How do I turn on surfing during the trip?

To be able to surf during the trip, you must activate data roaming.

[Instructions on how to do this in your
mobile phone](/link/288523ae6c5a4fe083145390f68c3609.aspx)  
  

**Do you have a mobile broadband?**

    1. Make sure your subscription is open for roaming. If you have a modem: see the menu in the client. If you have a wifi puck, see the menu in the router.
    2. Make sure the router/modem/tablet is open for roaming.
    3. In some clients/routers, there may be a need to manually connect when roaming and confirm that you accept it.

  * What do I do if the surfing runs out when I am abroad?

**When you are in a country where you _can_ use the subscription/prepaid card
data pot without extra cost (e.g. an EU/EEA country)**

When the surfing is finished, you will receive an SMS with the question of whether you want to buy more surfing.
You order any desired amount of surfing by replying to the SMS. You can also
wait until new surfing arrives. You can find the date under Surf in the app [My
Telenor](https://uu429.app.goo.gl/htwC).  
  

**When you are in a country where you _cannot_ use the subscription/prepaid card
data pot without extra cost (e.g. Mexico)**

When the daily data volume (i.e. the Surf Pass volume) is finished, you will receive an SMS
with the question of whether you want to buy more surfing (a Surf Pass). You order by
replying to the SMS. If you do not buy more surfing, data will be blocked.
The block is released again when a new day starts (Swedish time zone), whereupon you
will be charged for a Surf Pass (a fixed price per day for a fixed
surf volume per day), as long as roaming data is [activated
](/link/dc6ac35e5cf8424fad51b9f66abadf5f.aspx)on your smartphone provided that
your [data limit for surfing](/link/db8a67240a624315add9b688c4511c3a.aspx)has not
been exceeded. If you do not want more roaming data costs after the Surf Package's
volume has been used up, [turn off roaming data](/link/bc7779ed9f1648b88dbd9442d51a3bc5.aspx)when you receive an SMS saying that the Surf Package's volume is starting to run out (you will receive such an SMS when 80% of the Surf Package's volume has been consumed).
