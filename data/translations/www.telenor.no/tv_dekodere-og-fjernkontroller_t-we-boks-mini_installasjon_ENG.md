# Installing T-We Box Mini

## Guide

Once the T-We Box Mini is connected, you need to perform a first-time installation. Follow
the guide below to complete it. If you haven't connected your T-We box
yet, you can [follow our guide for
connection.](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-
mini/koble-til/)

## 1. Turn on box and TV

Turn on the power switch on the back of the box and turn on the TV.  
The box will now go through a startup procedure and soon the welcome image
will appear.

## 2. Welcome Image

If you cannot see the welcome image, you need to check that you have selected the correct
signal input on the TV.

**Source selection on the remote control** may vary from TV provider to TV
provider. The button most often looks like one of these.
