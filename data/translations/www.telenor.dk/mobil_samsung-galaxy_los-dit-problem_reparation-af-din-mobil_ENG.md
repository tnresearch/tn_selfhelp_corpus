# Repairing Your Mobile

Before submitting your mobile for repair, we recommend that you go through the list of solutions below. You may be able to solve the problem yourself quickly. Please note that faults or damage related to the use of non-original spare parts are not covered by warranty. If a repair is not carried out correctly, and in an antistatic environment, it may result in consequential damage or malfunction.

## Submit Your Mobile for Repair

If you cannot solve the problem yourself, simply drop off your mobile at a Telenor store. The staff at the store will ensure it is sent securely and quickly for repair. We will contact you as soon as your mobile is back from repair and ready for collection. You can easily borrow a phone while yours is being repaired if you drop it off at a store. [Find your nearest store here.](/find-butik/ "Find store")

You can also send your mobile by post. To do this, [create a case with our partner Care1](http://repair.care1.dk/telenor). Here you can also print a free shipping label for the shipment.

## What Happens Next?

Once the order is created, you will receive an SMS and an email with your repair number. If the repair is not covered by the warranty, you will also receive an email with a price quote. Payment must be made before the repair begins. In some cases, you may be asked for a copy of your receipt. Care1 also checks whether your device is covered by Screen Replacement etc. They will contact you if necessary.

## 90% of All Mobiles are Returned Within Two Weeks

As all damages are different and often require an experienced repairer, the staff in the store unfortunately cannot assess how long the repair of your mobile will take. But the vast majority of mobiles are repaired within two weeks.
