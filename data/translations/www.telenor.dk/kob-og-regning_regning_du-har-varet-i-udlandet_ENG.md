# You have been abroad

When you have used your mobile abroad, your bill may be higher than
normal. However, with Roam Away and Roam Away+ it does not cost extra to make calls,
send messages or use up to 20GB of data in included countries.

## Sms sent abroad

When you are abroad, it does not cost anything to receive SMS messages. But even if
you have free SMS, you pay a foreign rate for the SMS messages you send to both
Danish and foreign numbers - except when you have Roam Away and Roam Away+
countries. [Read more about Roam Away and Roam Away+
here](/shop/landingpage/mobil/roaming/).

  
Please note that emojis/mood icons in your SMS messages from abroad can fill
many characters. Sometimes a single emoji can fill more than one standard-
SMS of 160 characters.

[See foreign prices for SMS](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/udland/udlandspriser-i-udlandet/)

## Data usage abroad

The price for data used abroad depends on where in the world you are.
With Roam Away and Roam Away+ you can use up to 20GB in the included countries.
On [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/) you can see how much data you have used, and
have left in Roam Away and Roam Away+ countries.  
  
Note that SMART phones make automatic updates if data roaming is not
turned off. This may be email or other apps running in the background.
This happens when the mobile is turned on and gets network connection, and automatically
updates. These updates give a data consumption on the foreign network.

[See foreign prices for data per MB](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/udland/udlandspriser-i-udlandet/)

## Mms sent abroad

Mms sent from abroad is calculated as data usage, and the price may therefore vary
depending on the size of the content. With Roam Away and Roam Away+ you can use
up to 20GB in the included countries.

[See foreign prices for data per MB](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/udland/udlandspriser-i-udlandet/)

## Calls made and received abroad

Whether you make a call or receive a call yourself when you are abroad,
you pay a foreign rate. Remember that it is always free to receive calls in Roam
Away and Roam Away+ countries.

[See foreign prices](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/udland/udlandspriser-i-udlandet/)

Usage control abroad

You automatically have a data limit of DKK 450 per month. If you do not change your
data limit, the price for data used abroad will never be higher than DKK 450 per month. [Read more about Usage control in
abroad.](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/udland/surfkontrol-
udland/)
