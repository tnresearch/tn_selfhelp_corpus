# Transfer Contacts to Your iPhone

Follow the guide below to import contacts to your Apple iPhone. But if you have the slightest doubt about how to get all your contacts, pictures, and e.g. emails transferred to your new mobile, please visit your Telenor store.

[Find store](/find-butik/ "Find store")

  * ## Tap "Settings"

Make sure your Telenor SIM card is in the phone.

  * ## Select "Mail, Contacts, Calendars"

  * ## Select "Import SIM Contacts"

  * ## Select Account

Choose the account you want to transfer your contacts to.

**How do I transfer my contacts from Android to iPhone?**

    1. Make sure you have access to Google Play on your Android phone and the App Store on your iPhone
    2. On both phones, you now need to download the "Phonecopy" app
    3. Once the app is downloaded, you need to create a user account - follow the instructions in the app. We recommend that you do this on the phone where the contacts are located
    4. Synchronize the phone where the contacts are located - and then synchronize your iPhone
