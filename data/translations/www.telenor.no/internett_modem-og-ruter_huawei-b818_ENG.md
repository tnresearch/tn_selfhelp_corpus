# Help with Huawei B818

Here you will find help and guides for your Mobile Broadband or Wireless Broadband router.

## Simple steps to get online

  * #### Insert SIM card

Break out the SIM card to micro-SIM size, and follow the images below for help placing the SIM card in the router.

Make sure you insert the SIM card the correct way, and that it clicks into place.

  * #### Connect to power

Connect the power adapter to the back of the router and then to a power source. The router will turn on automatically.

  * #### Connect to WiFi

 **Windows**

    1. Left-click on the wireless network icon at the bottom next to the clock and date

    2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

    3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

 **Mac**

    1. Press the Airport icon for wireless network

    2. Find the network that matches the network name on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

    3. Enter the network password that is on the underside of the router and press **Connect**

  * #### Complete the wizard

    1. Make sure you are connected to the router's wireless network (step 3)

    2. Open the browser

    3. Follow the wizard that appears in the browser

Note: If the wizard does not appear automatically, you can start it by searching for 192.168.1.1 in the address field of the browser.

 **PIN code  
** In the wizard, you will be asked to enter a PIN code. This can be found in the letter you received with the SIM card for the router.

If you cannot find the PIN code, you can [retrieve the PUK code on My Pages](/mitt-telenor/)[r](https://www.telenor.no/privat/minesider/web/mobil/). Once you have entered the PUK code, you will be asked to create a new PIN code.

**How to retrieve the PUK code**

    1. Log in to My Pages

    2. Select the subscription in question

    3. Under **Devices and SIM**, select the SIM card in question

    4. Press **Show PUK code**
