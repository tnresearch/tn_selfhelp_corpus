# T-We App and Online TV

Here you will find help with logging in and features in T-We - in the app and on online TV.

T-We App can be used anywhere in Norway, EEA and EU

You need the following to use the T-We app or online TV on t-we.no

  * An active TV subscription and one T-We Box or card reader from Telenor

  * The mobile number of the subscription owner must be registered in our systems

  * Internet connection via mobile network or WiFi

Exceptions for T-We app:

Content may vary between TV box and app, as not all channels in optional
content are available in the app. If you want access to content that is
locked to the home, e.g. Premier League, then the app and TV Box must be connected
on the same network.

You can use the app everywhere without using the TV box, but there must be one registered
in your name.

  * Content that is locked to the home will not be available on t-we.no

## TV in Multiple Rooms

Download the T-We app on Apple TV, Chromecast with Google TV or newer smart-
TVs with Android TV. You can also use Airplay and Chromecast to display T-We
on the TV, or connect a PC to your TV with a cable to display Online TV on the TV-
screen.  
  
Click the buttons below for more info and guidance.
