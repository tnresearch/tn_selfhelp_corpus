# Content Services and Teletorg

Wondering who a 4- or 5-digit number belongs to, what Teletorg is, or how to block or allow the use of these services? You'll find answers to most questions here.

[Age Limit](/kundeservice/faktura/innholdstjenester/#aldersgrense)[Blocks](/kundeservice/faktura/innholdstjenester/#sperringer)[Disagree with the billed amount?](/kundeservice/faktura/innholdstjenester/#uenig)

## What are Content Services?

Content services are a collective term for services ordered and paid for via SMS. The service is charged by you receiving an SMS from a 4- or 5-digit number, or by clicking on a link in the message to complete a purchase.

The service itself can be anything from purchases in vending machines to parking and bus tickets. Some content services may result in starting a subscription, e.g. in connection with answering competitions. Then you will receive SMS/MMS that you will be charged for regularly. These messages should always contain information on how to stop the service.

## What is Teletorg?

Teletorg services are a collective term for a range of utility and entertainment services offered by telephone. These phone numbers start with 820 or 829. The cost of calling these numbers will vary and will be announced at the start of the call.

Recently, mandatory amount limits have been introduced for Teletorg numbers of a maximum of 50 euros _per call_ and 300 euros _per month_.

## Who has billed me?

You can find a complete overview of what you have been charged for, along with contact information for each individual provider, at [strex.no/minside](https://strex.no/min-side/). You can also find the name of the service provider on your invoice.

[Read more about Strex and mobile payment](/privat/mobil/mobiltjenester/strex/)

## Age Limit

Content services are available to everyone, but we require all content providers to perform an age check if the content is unsuitable for children.

Children under 18 should not be able to enter into agreements for subscription-based services, but it is important that we have correct information about the user's age to ensure that such cases do not occur.

If you want to be extra sure, you can add Blocking of SMS content services to the subscription in question.

Content services and childrenEnsure that children have the correct subscription and information linked to the mobile number. This way you avoid children gaining access to unwanted content services via SMS.

## Blocks

Blocking SMS content services or Teletorg is free of charge, and the change takes effect the day after you activate or deactivate the block.

* #### How do I block purchases via the mobile bill?

    1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/)

    2. Select **Mobile and subscription**

    3. Select the number in question

    4. Select **Settings**

    5. Select **Blocks**

    6. Find **Block purchases via the mobile bill** in the list and toggle the switch to activate the block

If you want to deactivate the block at a later time, simply toggle the switch off again.

###

* #### How do I order a Teletorg Block?

    1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/)

    2. Select **Mobile and subscription**

    3. Select the number in question

    4. Select **Settings**

    5. Select **Blocks**

    6. Find **Permanent block for Teletorg** in the list and toggle the switch to activate the block

If you want to deactivate the block at a later time, simply toggle the switch off again.

* #### What is the Amount Limit for content services and Teletorg?

Do you want control over costs without completely blocking the use of content services? You can choose to set an amount limit of 250, 500 or 1000 NOK/month.

To order an amount limit, you must contact customer service. [See contact information and opening hours.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

Disagree with the billed amount?

If you want to complain about an amount related to content services or Teletorg, you will find contact information for the provider on Strex's pages. Complaints and general inquiries about a content service or Teletorg number should preferably be directed directly to the service provider. You can find contact information at [strex.no/minside](https://strex.no/min-side/)

You can also complain to Strex (mobile operators' payment service) by using the contact form at <https://strex.no/klageskjema> or to Telenor by [contacting customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/).

Need more help or guidance from us, you can [contact customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/).
