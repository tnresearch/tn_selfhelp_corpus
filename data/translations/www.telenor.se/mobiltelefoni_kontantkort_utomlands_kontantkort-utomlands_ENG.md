# Prepaid Card Abroad

Of course, you can use your prepaid card abroad, but keep in mind that it
works differently depending on the country you are in. For your prepaid card
to work abroad, you must first have made calls with it in Sweden.

## Countries within the EU/EEA

You can use your prepaid card just like at home when you travel throughout the
EU/EEA - surf, call, text and mms as usual. You top up the card just as you
do when you are at home in Sweden.

Here you can see which countries are included in the EU/EEA.

## Countries outside the EU/EEA

If you are outside the EU/EEA, you need to [top up with
Extra Balance](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-kontantkort/) to be able to use
the prepaid card. Keep in mind that Swedish prepaid cards do not work in
certain countries.
