## Warranty Certificate

#### Devices purchased from DNA Store

**Device purchased with DNA Installment:** You can download the device's warranty certificate from the My DNA app under the "My Devices" tab.

**Device purchased with one-time payment:** The purchase receipt or order confirmation for devices purchased from DNA Store also serves as a warranty certificate. If necessary, a copy of the purchase receipt can be printed at DNA Store. A copy can be printed at any DNA Store.

Devices purchased with one-time payment from DNA Store are also visible in the My DNA app if the seller registered the device to your account during the purchase.

#### Devices purchased from DNA Online Store

If you need a warranty certificate, please contact customer service.

**Device purchased with DNA Installment:** You can download the device's warranty certificate from the My DNA app under the "My Devices" tab.
