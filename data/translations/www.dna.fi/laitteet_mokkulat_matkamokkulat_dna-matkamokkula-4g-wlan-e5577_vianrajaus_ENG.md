## Troubleshooting

What should I do if my modem's battery is swollen?

If your modem's battery is swollen even slightly, stop using it immediately.
Take the modem to a DNA Store. Swelling can be noticed directly by the
battery bulging or, for example, the back cover moving. With current battery
technology, a similar risk of swelling exists for every battery-powered
device. The risk increases if the use is really intensive, the modem is in a
place where its temperature rises critically, or if changes have been made to
it that are not mentioned in the manual.

A USB connection between the travel modem and the computer cannot be
established.

Remove old modem software from your computer. Then connect the USB cable
and driver again. Restart the computer.
Install the software. Run the installer by selecting **My computer ->
Mobile hotspot**.

Connecting to WiFi between the travel modem and the device fails.

Make sure that WiFi is enabled on the travel modem and that the 2.4 GHz
WiFi radio feature is in use.
Check that you have selected the correct WiFi network. See the WiFi network
name on the travel modem's display.

Restart the travel modem.

Bring the travel modem closer to the device.

The internet connection of a device connected to the travel modem does not
work.

Check that the SIM card is inside the travel modem and that the modem does
not display "PIN code locked" on the screen.

Bring the travel modem closer to the device or move the travel modem as
close as possible to the window.

Check that you have selected the correct WiFi network. See the WiFi network
name on the travel modem's display.

The connection is slow.

Bring the travel modem closer to the device and move the modem as close as
possible to the window.

If the travel modem switches between different network technologies (such as
4G and 3G), try locking the travel modem to one network.

Especially in densely populated urban areas, the WiFi network frequency can
be congested. If your device supports the 5 GHz WiFi feature, try enabling
this feature on the modem.

Additionally, you can change the WiFi network channel width and channels
from the settings pages. These may help with the speed.

The computer reports "Limited connectivity from the computer."

The travel modem is not connected to the internet. Check the connection and
restart the travel modem if necessary.

I cannot access the travel modem's management page.

Check that you have selected the correct WiFi network or that the USB cable
is connected. See the WiFi network name on the travel modem's display.

Enter the correct address in the web browser http://dna.mokkula or
alternatively 192.168.8.1

Turn off other internet connections on the computer and disconnect the
Ethernet cable.

You can also try downloading the manufacturer's own installation program by
downloading the Huawei Hi-Link app for Android or iOS operating systems
from their respective app stores.

The travel modem is not responding to anything.

Restart the travel modem by holding down the power button for five seconds
or remove the modem's battery.

Reset the travel modem to factory settings. Open the modem's back cover and
press the Reset button located under the back cover with a sharp pen tip.
Count slowly to five and release the button.
