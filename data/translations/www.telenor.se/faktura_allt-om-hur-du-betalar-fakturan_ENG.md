# All about how to pay your invoice

Paying bills isn't the most fun thing in life, but it still has to be done.
Here you'll find everything you need to know to make payments as smooth as
possible!

When and where?

  * The first invoice you receive when you become a customer of ours will land in your mailbox. In your physical mailbox if you've acquired a mobile subscription, and in your digital one, i.e. [Kivra](https://accounts.kivra.com/)*, if you've acquired broadband or TV.   
  

  * Invoices are created at the beginning of the month, and are available in [My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/) no later than the 8th of each month.  
  

  * The invoice must be paid by the end of the month, the exact date is stated on the invoice. Keep in mind that banks need some time to process the payment, so make it well in advance, especially if there are holidays (for example Christmas or Easter) before.  
  

* if you are not connected to Kivra, you will receive a paper invoice in your physical mailbox.

You decide how!

Once your first invoice has landed in your mailbox (digital or physical), it's
time to think about how you want to pay in the future.

  * **Do you want to avoid worrying about when the invoice is due?** Then you get **direct debit**. This means that the invoice amount is debited from your account on the correct date. The only thing you need to worry about is making sure there is money in the account. [Read more about direct debit](/support/faktura/autogiro/)
  * **Do you want a little more control? E-invoice** is your friend. It arrives in your online bank and you must approve it before it is paid. [Read more about e-invoice](/support/faktura/e-faktura/)
  * Do you love to Swish? Then you will of course choose to pay your invoice with **Swish in the [My Telenor app](/handla/tjanster/appar/mitt-telenor/).**   
[Read more about Swish](/support/faktura/swish/)

Unsure?

Are you unsure if the invoice has been paid or not? You can check that in your
online bank. If the amount has been debited from your account, the invoice has been paid.  
  
After a few days, the invoice will also be marked as paid in our systems, which you
can see in My Telenor.

Are you getting a lot of invoices from us?

If you receive more than one invoice per month from Telenor, it is because you have
multiple services from us. If you have both a mobile subscription and broadband or TV, you will
receive two invoices. If you also have a business subscription with us that you pay
yourself, there will be even more.

Strange amount?

The invoice amount can change from month to month, and this can be due to a lot of
different things, but the most common reason is that you have purchased something or changed something in
your subscription. Everything included in the amount is stated on the invoice, so look at it
if you wonder what is making the amount higher or lower.

## Frequently asked questions about invoices

  * I'm having trouble setting up direct debit. What should I do?

The most common reasons for problems setting up direct debit are:

    * **Incorrect customer number (also called payer number)**  
You can find your customer number on the invoice. You can find the invoices in [My
Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

    * **You have already applied for direct debit**  
There is a direct debit with the bank already.

    * **The bank giro number is incorrect**  
The correct number is 5572-4959

    * **Multiple customer numbers/payer numbers**  
If you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV with us, you have two
customer numbers and receive two invoices. This means you must apply for direct debit
for both, if that's what you want.

[Read more about direct debit](/link/cd73f7500b12469ea4605c59672dfa7a.aspx)

  * How do I see that my application for direct debit/e-invoice has been approved?

In the My Telenor app you can see how you pay your upcoming invoices. Click
on invoices, then on unpaid invoices and at the bottom you will see your payment method.  
  
Keep in mind that if you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV from us, you have
two customer numbers and receive two invoices. This means you must submit an
application for each customer number.

### Direct Debit

When it comes to seeing if a direct debit application has been approved, it varies from
bank to bank. Most banks send a confirmation by post when your application for direct debit has been approved. Contact your bank to find out their
procedures and how long it takes.

### E-invoice

For e-invoice, you can see directly in your online bank that everything is okay. Keep in mind
that you cannot see the actual e-invoice until we have sent it to your bank.
We do that once a month.

### If you receive a paper invoice anyway...

...it doesn't necessarily mean something went wrong. Depending on when you
set up direct debit or e-invoice, a paper invoice may be sent out before your consent to direct debit or e-invoice
is processed by the bank.

  * How do I view my invoice?

Log in to [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) to
view new and old invoices.  
  
In My Telenor you will also find

    * Payment status
    * When the next invoice will be sent
    * How to pay (e.g. with direct debit or e-invoice)
My Telenor is available both [here on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx), and as an [app
](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx)for those of you who have a mobile subscription
with us.
