# Security on your iPad

If you also use your tablet to update Facebook, send emails, and pay bills, we recommend that you consider your online security.

Although there are never any guarantees, you can make it more difficult for strangers to access your data with simple means.

**Good advice for your security**

  * Use a passcode
  * Make your tablet visible so you can find it again: Turn on Find My iPad
  * Do not give personal information to people you do not know or trust
  * Never download programs or apps from vendors you do not trust
  * Say yes to software updates. Updates are fixes for larger or smaller bugs in the software, and the fixes strengthen your security when you are online

## If your tablet is stolen

  * If you have Mobile Broadband, call and have the SIM card blocked. Customer service can also help with, for example, obtaining a purchase receipt for your insurance company
  * Change passwords for e.g. Facebook, your bank and other websites with personal information
