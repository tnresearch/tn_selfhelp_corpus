# Check the setting for Blocking calls, SMS and surfing when abroad

Make sure you haven't blocked traffic abroad. If you have, you won't be able to make calls, send SMS or surf the internet.

You can find the setting in My Telenor under Settings > Cost control. Log in here [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in [our app](https://uu429.app.goo.gl/gnXY) to change it directly.

Do you have Telenor Family? Please note that only the person listed as the [owner](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/for-familjen/medlemsprofiler/) of the subscription can change the setting.
