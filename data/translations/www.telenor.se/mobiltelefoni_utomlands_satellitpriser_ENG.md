# Calling, surfing and sending SMS when you are on a ship or plane

On ships and planes, you may end up in a so-called satellite network when using your mobile phone. In the price list below you can see what it costs to call, surf and send SMS or MMS via the satellite network when you have a mobile subscription or prepaid card with us.

On many ferries, for example between Sweden and Denmark, Poland and Germany, or on airplanes, you will end up in a so-called satellite network when using your mobile phone. This means that you are connected via satellite instead of the regular mobile network. This way you can get coverage in otherwise inaccessible places.

On some flights and cruise ships, other satellite operators may offer coverage. To see which operators we have agreements with and what prices apply, see below.

Good to know:

  * **Calls in a satellite network are not included** if you have Telenor Family.
  * **Prices in satellite networks differ from other prices in the country you are departing from**. We send an SMS with price information when you enter a satellite network. If you enter the same satellite network within seven days, no new SMS will be sent. If you remain in the same satellite network, you will receive a new SMS only the next time you travel in and out of the same satellite network again.
  * **Data via satellite network is turned off**. Do you want to be able to surf via satellite network? Call our customer service free of charge to activate it.
