## Features

  * Docsis 3.1 compatible cable modem (32/8 channels)
  * Wireless connection: Wi-Fi 802.11ac/n/g 2.4 GHz (4x4) and 5 GHz (4x4)
  * 4 x 1Gb LAN ports (e.g. for a set-top box or computer)
  * IPv6 support
  * Firewall and NAT
  * DNA's customized settings

Sales package contents: DNA Cable Modem Sagemcom F-3890v3, power cord,  
Ethernet cable, DNA quick guides, safety instructions, antenna splitter and antenna cable.

You can extend the WiFi network created by the DNA Cable Modem F-3890v3 by purchasing a compatible [Mesh WiFi extender
(F-266)](https://kauppa4.dna.fi/Nettiyhteydet-ja-laitteet/Modeemit-ja-
mokkulat/DNA-Ethernet--ja-Mesh-modeemi-WiFi-6/p/VMODE00007).

With a WiFi extender, you can create a seamless WiFi network that covers even a larger home's needs. A compatible WiFi extender works seamlessly with the F-3890v3 modem, which means your WiFi covers an even larger area, but the connection still works behind the same WiFi network (SSID) and password.
