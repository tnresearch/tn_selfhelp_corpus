# Help to Avoid Phishing (Fake Emails and SMS Messages)

Unfortunately, there are now many different types of fraud, and even though you are well protected as a Telenor customer, fraudsters are still finding new ways to try to scam you. Here you can get help recognizing the different types of fraud and good advice on how to react if something goes wrong.

## How do I recognize phishing?

#### 1. Watch out for spelling mistakes and strange wording

  * If the text is incoherent or contains spelling mistakes, it may be a scam.
  * Some of the most common phishing emails have these subject lines:
  * There is a problem with your account
  * You have won – redeem your prize now
  * You need to validate your login details
  * You have received a refund from the Danish Tax Agency (SKAT)
  * You have received a package waiting to be picked up

### 2. Pay attention to the sender's domain

When you receive an email from the bank, the Danish Tax Agency (Skattestyrelsen) or similar, always look at the email address it was sent from. Emails sent from hotmail, gmail or similar are very rarely from companies or organizations and never from the public sector – they have their own domain. Also remember to notice if an extra character has crept into the domain name - then the sender is not necessarily who you think they are. If in doubt, contact the sender by finding their phone number or email address on their website or on Google.

### 3. Be extra vigilant if the email contains links

Always be vigilant when an email contains links that you are encouraged to click on. The link may install malicious software on your device without you knowing. You should also be aware if the content of the email encourages you to follow a link to update your personal information, e.g. account details, CPR number or similar. Public authorities and trustworthy organizations never ask for personal information in an email or SMS.

### What do I do if I receive a phishing email or SMS?

If you have received an email or SMS from the bank, the Danish Tax Agency (Skattestyrelsen), PostNord or similar with a link, do not click on it. Instead, go to the browser and click on the sender's website, where the same information will be available if the email or SMS is trustworthy.

You can also find the phone number and call the company or organization directly - it is important that you do not call the phone number in the email you received, but find the number on their website, which you have searched for yourself in your browser.

Read more about phishing in our [guide to protecting yourself against phishing and vishing](/digital-tryghed/artikler/sikker-pa-nettet/vaer-paa-vagt-saadan-beskytter-du-dig-mod-phishing-og-vishing/) (phishing via phone calls).
