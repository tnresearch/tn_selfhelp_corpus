# Paying for Google Play Purchases with a Phone Bill

You can purchase apps from the Google Play Store on your phone or tablet, and
pay for your purchases with your DNA phone bill or the balance of your DNA
prepaid subscription. Please note that mobile broadband subscriptions are not
covered by this service.

#### The Google Play service works on all Android 2.2 and newer phones. Do the following:

  * Add operator billing as a payment method in the Google Play Store.
  * Purchases are protected by the password of your Google account. You can choose whether to be asked for a password for each purchase, every 30 minutes, or not at all. The more often you are asked for a password, the more secure shopping is.
  * A password is always required for apps designed for under 12-year-olds, unless you have specifically disabled the password prompt.
  * The default usage limit is €150/month, with a maximum single purchase of €50.
  * You will receive a transaction confirmation by email for all purchases. The transaction confirmation will be sent to the email address associated with your Google account.

#### If you wish, you can block Mobile Payment as follows:

  * You can block purchases with a P3 or stricter message restriction (time-spending and adult entertainment blocking services). Read more about restrictions and blocks [here](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/puhelinliittymat/lisapalvelut).

## Making Purchases in Practice

To purchase apps in Google Play, your subscription must have mobile data enabled. After activation, you can also pay when using a WLAN connection. The payment method is defined in the settings or during the purchase (see instructions below).

Adding a Payment Method

1\. Select the app you want from the app store and proceed to pay by pressing
the app's price. The app store will ask you to accept the permissions required by the app. Select 'Accept'.

2\. If you have not yet selected a payment method, select 'Continue'. You can select
a payment method by pressing the product price.

3\. Select 'Enable DNA Billing'.

4\. Your phone will request permission to send a message to 14789. The message is
free of charge.

5\. If you have not added billing information to your Google account, fill it in and save
your billing and contact information. The customer is responsible for the accuracy of the information provided to Google. DNA will not send you an invoice for your purchases, but purchases made from the app store will be added to your current phone bill or deducted from your prepaid
subscription balance.

6\. Once the payment is approved, you will be asked how often Google Play should ask
for your password during purchases.

After this, the app will be downloaded and installed automatically. The customer
will receive a transaction confirmation by email, showing the details of the transaction.

#### If DNA operator billing is not available in the payment methods, do the following:

  1. Make sure that WLAN is disabled.
  2. Clear the data stored by Google Play (does not delete installed programs).  
(Settings -> App Management -> Google Play Store -> Force Stop.
Wait a moment and select Clear Data)

  3. Restart your phone.

Making Purchases

1\. After selecting the app to purchase and accepting the app's terms of use, the previously selected payment method will appear below the app's name. If you have multiple payment methods available, you can select the desired payment method by pressing on the product price. Once you have selected the correct payment method, continue the order by pressing Buy.

2\. Confirm the order by entering your password (unless you have chosen in the settings not to be prompted for a password).

## Cancelling an Order (within 2 hours of purchase)

  * You can cancel the order within two hours of your purchase (only apps)
  * Upon cancellation, the app will be removed and charges will be removed from the bill.
  * A reservation made on the balance during the purchase will appear on the subscription with a slight delay.
  * Cancellation can only be done once per program.
  * You can cancel the purchase on your phone in the Google Play app store or on a computer at [play.google.com](https://play.google.com/).

How to cancel an order

1\. Find the product whose purchase you want to cancel. The easiest way to find the program is through the left upper corner menu in the Google Play Store. Select "My apps". In the product information, you will see a refund button, which you can use to cancel the order.

2\. Confirm the cancellation of the purchase by selecting Yes.

Requesting a Refund

#### You can file a complaint through the play.google.com page. Log in to the page
with the same Google account as the purchase you are complaining about.

Select the menu under the gear symbol in the upper right corner of the page
'My account'.

A view will open on the page, at the bottom of which you will see your order history. Select 'Report a problem' for the desired program (you can select the button at the end of the line).

Select the reason for the complaint and fill in the requested information.  
Google will process the complaint and respond to the customer by email.

You can find more information about refund policies on Google's
[support pages](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=fi&ref_topic=4443837&vid=0-635793818422298535-2815684721).
