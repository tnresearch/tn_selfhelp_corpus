# Rule out issues with router, wifi and patch panel

To rule out the most common equipment failures, we'll go directly to the source,
that is, where the internet enters your home.

You do this by connecting a computer directly to the broadband outlet.

If you have a [patch panel](/support/vanliga-fragor/bredband/vad-ar-en-
patchpanel/) unplug the network cable from the room outlet on the patch panel, and
connect it to the computer instead.
