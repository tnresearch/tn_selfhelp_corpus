# Activate Your Broadband

You have connected the router and now all that remains is to activate the broadband
connection. Here's how to do it.

## Broadband Activation, Step by Step

  1. Use a computer or mobile phone. Make sure you are **connected via wifi** (or network cable). If you are using your mobile phone, also **turn off mobile data.**
  2. Open a web browser and enter the address:  
  
aktivering.telenor.se  
  

  3. Select **Private Customer** and fill in the requested information.
  4. **Wait 15 minutes.**
  5. **Restart** the router.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Broadband

  * I'm having trouble activating my broadband – what should I do?

It depends on what happens when you try to activate the broadband on the page
aktivering.telenor.se  
  
 **If you immediately get a message saying you have activated your broadband**
– even though you haven't done so yet – it is probably because you have
opened the page on your computer, tablet or mobile phone and were connected
via a mobile network. Here's how to solve the problem:

    1. Turn off the mobile connection
    2. Connect using a network cable or the router's wireless network (wifi).

Does it still not work? Then you may need to clear the cache.

After that, you can activate your broadband via the address aktivering.telenor.se.  
  
**Can't reach the page aktivering.telenor.se at all?** Do one of the following:

    * Enter 10.255.1.33 instead of aktivering.telenor.se
    * Restart your router and computer/tablet/mobile phone
    * Test with another computer/tablet/mobile phone
    * Test with another web browser
    * Try disconnecting any router and instead connecting your computer directly to the network outlet on the wall. This only applies to those of you who have broadband via fiber.

  * How do I connect my router?

At telenor.se/surfa-nu you will find everything about how to connect your
router - whether you have just picked it up from the box or have already
started connecting it yourself.  
  
[Top, take me to telenor.se/surfa-

  * When can I start using the broadband service I have ordered?

It depends on the type of broadband you have ordered, but generally it is so
that if broadband is already installed, you can start using it as soon as you have
received and connected the hardware you received from us (router or modem).  
[More about delivery times](/link/afe7b4ca539b471bbfaa8ed0a3af2ceb.aspx)

  * When will my router arrive?

Have you ordered broadband from us and are wondering when your router will be delivered? It
depends on whether you have chosen to activate the broadband directly or not.

    * If you have chosen to activate the broadband **immediately**, the router will be shipped as soon as you have placed your order.
    * If you have chosen a **later date**, the router will be shipped about a week before the activation date.

support by phone. Choose broadband and TV when you call.
