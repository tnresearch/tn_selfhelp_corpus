## Frequently Asked Questions about Billing

Where can I find bill details?

You can find bill details by [logging in](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#)
to the DNA website in the "My Bills" section and in the home view of the [My
DNA](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus) app, under "Bills". You can
conveniently download the My DNA app to your phone or tablet from your own
app store. You can also ask for bill information as a logged-in user from the
bot on the dna.fi website and in the app.

What is a device payment plan?

DNA offers flexible payment plans of varying lengths for purchasing devices.
You can choose to pay for your device in installments monthly instead of a
one-time payment, for 12 months, 24 months, or 36 months with interest-free
and fee-free DNA Payment.

How do I pay off the installments of a payment plan at once?

You can order a final invoice from [self-service](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/laskut)
or through [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot) and pay off
the remaining installments of the device at once, even if the contract period
is still in progress.

Can I change the length of a payment plan?

It is not possible to change the length of a payment plan during the contract
period. However, you can always pay off the remaining amount in full at any
time by **ordering a final invoice** from our
[customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).

What are the invoice delivery methods? What does a paper invoice cost?

You can choose the most convenient way to receive your bills:  
DNA bills can be received by email, as an e-bill, by direct debit, or as a
paper invoice. You can also find bills for mobile subscriptions in your own
information by logging in to the [DNA.fi website](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/asiakkuus/laskutus)
and the [My DNA](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus) app.

E-bills, email bills, and invoices delivered to self-service are free of
charge. The price of a paper invoice and direct debit is €4.96/sent invoice.

You can activate e-bills and direct debit through your own bank. Changes to
other billing methods can be requested from customer service. A service fee
according to the price list will be charged for changes made through customer
service.

How do I activate an e-bill?

Switch to an e-bill in your online bank when you next pay a bill.

Select a new e-bill agreement in your online bank.

  * **Speech and mobile broadband subscriptions:** select "DNA Oyj" as the
    bill issuer and "Mobile Subscriptions and Payment Time" as the billing
    subject.
  * For invoices sent by **DNA Welho**, select "DNA Welho" as the bill issuer
    and "DNA Welho TV and Broadband Subscriptions" as the billing subject.
  * If your bill includes charges for **DNA Home Phone or DNA Home Line
    services**, select "DNA Oyj" as the bill issuer and "Home Phone
    Subscriptions" as the billing subject.
  * For invoices sent by **Digita**, create an e-bill agreement in your
    online bank and select "Digita Oy" as the bill issuer and "Digita TV" as
    the billing subject. You will also need a customer ID and reference
    number.

You will also need the customer ID and reference number shown on the bill.
Enter the reference number without spaces.

Finally, confirm the e-bill agreement. From now on, you will receive your
bills as an e-bill directly to your online bank. More detailed instructions
on how to activate an e-bill can be found on your bank's website and at
[dna.fi/elasku](https://www.dna.fi/elasku).

What should I do if a payment has not been registered to the bill, e.g., the
bill was paid with incorrect payment details?

First, find the following information from your online bank for the bill paid
with incorrect details:

  * Payment date
  * Payer's name
  * Amount
  * Account number to which the amount was paid
  * Reference number
  * Any information entered in the Description field
  * Archiving ID

Check that the payment recipient information stored in your online bank is not
incorrect. Delete outdated information if necessary.

After that, contact our
[customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).

What is DNA's minimum billing threshold?

DNA's minimum billing threshold for services is €5/month.

If a bill for one month falls below the threshold, the unpaid amount will be
combined with the next month's bill. The bill will be sent only when the
threshold is reached. However, you will receive a bill at least every three
months.

How can I move the payment date?

Please note that the payment date can only be moved before the bill's due
date.

You can move the payment date by contacting
[customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot). A fee according to the
price list will be charged for the change.

Where can I check the balance of my mobile subscription?

You can check the balance of your mobile subscription in [My
Services](https://www.dna.fi/minun-palveluni) under **Balance Information**.

What should I do if my mobile subscription has been suspended due to unpaid
bills?

If your service or subscription is temporarily suspended due to unpaid bills,
they will automatically reopen when the payments have been registered with
DNA. This takes approximately 1-5 business days from your payment. You can
see your bills in [My
Services](https://www.dna.fi/minun-palveluni) and the [My
DNA](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus) app.

If you need to speed up the reopening, you can request a quick reopening by
contacting customer service. A fee according to the price list will be
charged for the quick reopening. A quick reopening is not possible if the
bill has been forwarded to a collection agency.

A quick reopening is possible in the My DNA app for some DNA Welho products.

How do I make an address change?

You can most conveniently notify customer service of an address change for
mobile and home phone subscriptions using the [contact form](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)->
**Send message**.

You can change the billing address for DNA Welho services in [My
Services](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#) under **My
Information** -> **Contact Information Change**. Remember to save the new
address. You can also change the street address or email address in the [My
DNA](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus) app under: More > My Information >
Billing Settings.

When you move to a new address, please remember to update your address
information with DNA yourself.
