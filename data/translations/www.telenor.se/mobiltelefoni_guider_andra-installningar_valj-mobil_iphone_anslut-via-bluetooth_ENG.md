# Connect via Bluetooth

By using Bluetooth, you can connect your iPhone to other devices, such as
speakers and headphones.

This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest operating system (iOS)** from
Apple. So, please start by making sure it is installed on your mobile –
not least to ensure it works as well and securely as possible.  
  
[How to update the
operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-
mobil/iphone/uppdatera-operativsystemet/)  
  
Now you have the latest iOS installed, right? Then let's go!

  * ### Go to the home screen

  * ### Select "Settings"

  * ### Scroll down, select "Bluetooth"

  * ### Turn on Bluetooth

  * ### Select the device you want to connect to

The devices that are available and within range will now be displayed. Select the
device you want to connect to.

  
  
Is the device not displayed? Check that the Bluetooth function is enabled on the device
and that it is within range.
