# Online Email for Windows Mail

Here we show you how to set up your Online email or change
email settings in Windows Mail.

If you are experiencing problems sending or receiving email, you should check
the settings in the email program you are using.

Here we show you how to change the outgoing email settings, but you
can also follow the guide to check other settings in the
email program.

This guide also shows you how to set up an email account on your
mobile phone for the first time.

Choose what you want to do using the buttons below.

# Step 1

 **NB!** The guide may differ from your phone if you have a different version
of the operating system installed.

### Press Accounts

Next step

Account settings for Online email

 **Account name** : Optional

 **Username** : Your email address (what is before "@online.no", max 8 characters)

 **Password** : Your password. [Have you forgotten
your password](/kundeservice/e-post/glemt-passord/index.page)?

 **Incoming server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 993 w/SSL

 **Outgoing server**

  * Server: mail.online.no

  * Port: 465 w/SSL (You can also use 587 w/SSL or TLS)

Old setup with incoming server "imap.online.no" and outgoing server
"smtp.online.no" will also work.
