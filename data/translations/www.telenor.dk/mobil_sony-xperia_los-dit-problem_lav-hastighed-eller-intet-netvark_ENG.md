# Slow Speed or No Network

Are you suddenly experiencing your mobile phone being unable to connect to the internet, or is the speed significantly lower than usual? Try going through the checklist below.

  1. Turn off your mobile phone and wait 5 seconds before restarting it. A restart often solves the problem.
  2. Update your mobile phone to the latest software. Go to your phone's settings and see if a new update is available for your phone.
  3. [Check our service status](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/ "Driftstatus"). There may be temporary problems with the mobile network.
  4. [Check our coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/ "Dækningskort"). Perhaps you are in an area with poor coverage?
  5. Can you remove the battery from your mobile phone? Turn off the phone and remove the battery. Wait a moment and put it back in the phone. Now try turning on the phone.

## Is something interfering with your signal?

**Hills and Trees**  
If you are in a dip, or surrounded by hills or trees, the network signal may have difficulty reaching your mobile phone.

**Weather and Wind  
** The mobile signal is affected by the season. The best mobile signal day is actually a frosty winter day. While wet leaves, rain and snow will dampen the signal.

**Buildings  
** If there is a large concrete building between you and the nearest transmitter, the walls will lower the signal strength. And if you are inside a building with thick walls, you may also experience a poorer signal.

**Many Mobile Phones in One Place  
** If you are standing in the middle of a crowd, your mobile phone is competing with many other mobile phones to catch the signal. It takes a lot of your mobile antenna to keep the signal stable.
