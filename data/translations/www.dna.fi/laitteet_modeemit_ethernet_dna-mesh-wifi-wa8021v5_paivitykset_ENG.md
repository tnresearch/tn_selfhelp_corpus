## Updates

#### New version V5R020C10S280 released on April 3, 2024.

The update will cause a brief interruption, during which the device will restart.

  * Improvements to the topology view
  * Device serial number added to the settings page
  * Improvements to WiFi diagnostics (e.g., recommendation for encryption to use)
  * Other general improvements

#### New software version V5R020C10S226 will be updated during weeks 42-46 in October-November 2022

  * The device will update automatically overnight and does not require any action
  * The update will cause a brief interruption, during which the device will restart
  * If the device is not online during the update, the update will be run after the device is started
