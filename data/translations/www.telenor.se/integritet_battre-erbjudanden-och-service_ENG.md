Share your data and get a better experience

If you share your data, you can get that little extra. Both in terms of
offers and service. Change your settings in My Telenor on the web.

[To My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/)

Share to get personalized offers

From time to time, all customers receive offers. If we are allowed to use your
traffic data, we can give you better offers. Instead of a general
offer that applies to everyone, you can get a personalized one.

If we are allowed to use your traffic data, we can, for example, see what type of mobile
you are using and then come up with a good offer on the latest model. Or
we can suggest accessories, apps and other things that you might find
interesting.

Share for better service and support

Every time we get permission to use data to improve service,
services, products and network, we are really happy. We love to give
good service.

Perhaps we discover a fault before you do yourself. For example, if your
mobile coverage or broadband connection at home has suddenly become much worse
or if you always lose coverage on your way to work. If we are allowed to use data
we can contact you and solve the problem before you have even discovered it.

### Manage consent settings

#### Necessary cookies

Always active

Needed for the website to function securely and correctly. Therefore, they cannot
be turned off. Necessary cookies enable basic functions, so
that you can, for example, navigate between pages or access secure areas of
the website.

Information about cookies‎

#### Functional cookies

Functional cookies

These cookies allow the website to provide enhanced
functionality and personalization. They can be set by us or by
third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you
do not allow these cookies, some or all of these services may not
work as they should.

Information about cookies‎

#### Statistical and Analytical

Statistical and Analytical

These cookies give us information about how you use our site and give us
the opportunity to improve the user experience and improve our website. They
can be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, we may have limited
insight into how our website is used and therefore have a harder time improving your
experience.

Information about cookies‎

#### Marketing

Marketing

These cookies are set on our website by our advertising partners. They can
be used by these companies to create a profile of your interests and show
you relevant ads on other websites. They do not store personal
information directly, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and
internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less
targeted ads.
