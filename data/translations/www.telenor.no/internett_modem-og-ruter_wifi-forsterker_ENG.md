# Help with WiFi Extender

Here you will find information about your WiFi Extender, as well as guides and help
for connection.

## About WiFi Extender

A WiFi Extender acts as a link between your [WiFi
Router](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter/) and the area where you
are experiencing poor coverage. It does not need to be connected with a cable - it
communicates wirelessly with the WiFi router. The extender also has 2 ethernet
ports, so you can connect equipment that requires a cable.

An extender is therefore a quick and easy solution to provide better coverage in
places where the existing wireless network does not reach.

The WiFi Extender is only compatible with our [WiFi
Router](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-ruter/), and with one or
more extenders connected to the WiFi router, you get a MESH network at home.

## Placement of the Extender

An extender should be placed midway between the router and the location where the devices you
want to connect are usually located. If it is placed too close to the router, it has
no function, because it then does not significantly increase the range of the
wireless network.

So, place the extender at the edge of the coverage area of your wireless
router. But be careful not to place the extender too far from the WiFi
router – you should place it somewhere where you still have a good speed on the
wireless network. Feel free to test several locations to find out where you get
the best coverage.

**If you have a Telenor WiFi Extender, the extender will light up green if it is
placed correctly. A yellow light means the signal is okay. A red light means you should
move it closer to the WiFi router.**

### How to Connect Your WiFi Extender

Here you will find guides and videos for connecting the extender.

[Connect extender...](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/wifi-
forsterker/koble-til/)

  * #### Lamp Status

The extender has 2 lamps on the side of the box. One indicates power, and the
other indicates coverage.

 **Power Lamp:**

    * Dark: Not connected to power

    * White: Connected to power

 **Coverage Lamp** :

    * Dark: Not connected

    * Red: Weak coverage

    * Orange: Good coverage

    * Green: Very good coverage

  * #### Reset Extender

Press the reset button briefly using a pen or other thin object in the
reset hole on the back of the extender.

  * #### Extender Details

 **Dimensions:**  
Height: 176 mm. Width: 180 mm. Depth: 68 mm.

 **WiFi Standard:  
** 802.11b/g/n on 2.4Ghz and 802.11a/n/ac on 5Ghz  
Band Steering: the device connected will automatically switch between 2.4 and 5GHz
based on signal strength

 **Standard Encryption:  
** WPA2 PSK
