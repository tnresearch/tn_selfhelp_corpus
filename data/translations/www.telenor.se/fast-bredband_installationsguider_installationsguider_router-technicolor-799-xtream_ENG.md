# Router - Technicolor 799 Xtream

## Follow the steps to install your router.

  * ### Connect the cable or splitter

Connect the network cable with red connectors between the router and the fiber outlet in the wall.

  * ### Connect the power adapter

  * ### Connect your broadband

#### With network cable

Connect the cable with yellow connectors between the router and the computer.

#### Wireless

You will find all the information on the bottom of the router:  
SSID = the name of the wireless network  
Wireless key = the password for the network

  * ### Activate your broadband

Surf to [aktivering.telenor.se ](https://bit.ly/2kHu2ti) and follow the instructions.

Read more about [Technicolor 799 Xtream in the installation guide](http://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/GetPdf.aspx?ArticleNumber=TA921024).
Here you will also find instructions on how to install telephony.
