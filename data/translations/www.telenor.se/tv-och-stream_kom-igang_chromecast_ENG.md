# Watch Telenor Stream via Chromecast

Do you have a Chromecast? Do you want to watch TV? Great, you've come to the right place!
Here you will find everything about how to get started and get the most out of
Telenor Stream.

Getting Started

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (link opens in a new window).
  2. Plug the Chromecast device into an available HDMI port in your TV.
  3. Then connect the USB cable between the Chromecast device and the power adapter, and plug the power adapter's cable into a wall outlet.
  4. Pick up the remote control and select the source that says Chromecast. Now you can start streaming.
  5. Go into the app on your mobile or tablet, or the browser on your computer. Click on the streaming icon in the upper right corner. Now select your network. Click on any movie or series and it will now automatically play on your TV. 

Good to know

  * It costs nothing extra to create a streaming account with Telenor if you are already a TV customer with us. The only additional cost is if you rent a movie.
  * Having trouble casting? Make sure you have the latest software by going into the Google Home app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * Some channels are missing when I log in to Telenor Stream – why?

If you can see some channels on your TV but not in Telenor Stream, it is because
we have not been granted permission to distribute them there.

  * How do I change my login details for Telenor Stream?

You can change both your username and password for Telenor Stream in [My
Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * Can I stream to multiple devices at the same time?

Yes, you can stream on up to two devices simultaneously on your subscription.

  * What can I stream?

You can watch most of what is included in your TV package on Telenor Stream. In some
cases, a single channel in the TV package may be missing. This is because we do not
have permission to stream them from the rights holder.

You can also easily [rent movies via
the streaming service](/link/35b18662a05f46e58f582664db0643f4.aspx).

  * Where can I see which movies I have rented?

Log in to [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx). There
you will find your invoices. The invoice shows which movies you have rented.
