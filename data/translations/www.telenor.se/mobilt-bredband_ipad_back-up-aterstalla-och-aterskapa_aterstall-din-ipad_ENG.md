# Reset your iPad

If your iPad is running slowly or starting to malfunction, a reset can help.
This will erase all content and your iPad will be like new. We recommend that
you make a [backup](/support/mobilt-bredband/ipad/back-up-aterstalla-och-
aterskapa/sakerhetskopiera-din-ipad/) before doing so.

  * ### Tap "Settings"

  * ### Select "General"

  * ### Select "Reset"

  * ### Select "Erase All Content and Settings"

  * ### Select "Erase iPad"

Confirm again. You have now reset the tablet. Well done.

If you want to restore all content from a backup, we'll tell you how [to do
it here](/support/mobilt-bredband/ipad/back-up-aterstalla-och-
aterskapa/aterskapa-fran-en-sakerhetskopia/).
