## What do I need to fill in?

### Mobile Number

Enter your mobile number if you want to receive service information via SMS. Remember,
you may not be able to receive SMS messages if there are disruptions on the mobile network.

### Email

Enter your email if you want to receive service information via email.

### Username

If you are a broadband customer, you can enter your username to receive precise
service information. Your central, which connects you to the internet, may be
located outside your postcode. But your username tells us exactly where
your central is located.  
Your username can be found on your bill and starts with the letters "dsl"
followed by 4-6 digits.
