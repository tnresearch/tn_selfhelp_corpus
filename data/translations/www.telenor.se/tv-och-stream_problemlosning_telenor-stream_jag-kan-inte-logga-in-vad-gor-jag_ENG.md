# I can't log in – what should I do?

No worries, we'll sort it out. Do you need help with the password or
username?

###

I have forgotten my password

Go to [stream.telenor.se](https://stream.telenor.se/). There you will find a
link to reset your password.

I have forgotten my username

You can find your username in My Telenor. Here's how:

  1. [Log in to My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx)
  2. Select your TV subscription on the **My Overview** page
  3. Look for the heading **My Telenor Stream Account**. There you can change your username.
