# If you are moving

Moving soon? Here's what to do if you have TV, broadband, or home phone via
broadband from us.

Different addresses have different technical conditions. Therefore, we cannot guarantee
that you can take your subscriptions with you when you move. But you can transfer –
preferably to the person moving in after you.  
  
So start by seeing if you can move your subscriptions with you by entering
your new address in the search box [here for broadband](/handla/bredband/) and [here for
TV](/handla/tv/).  
  
How you proceed then depends on what results you get:

  * If we can offer the same services as you have today, you can **move** them with you.
  * If you cannot get the same services at your new address, you can **transfer** or **cancel** your subscription. 

### More information about

Moving

Call us at [020-222 222](tel:020222222) and we will help you move
the subscription to your new address.

During the move itself, you are responsible for the connection. On the [support
pages](/link/47e38a54dfb34f7b884766d3bf9af9f1.aspx) there is a lot of different
installation guides to help you.

If you want more hands-on help, for example if you have an outdoor antenna, you can
get help from Digihjälpen and our partner Smartify. Call [077-446 60
60](tel:0774466060) or email to [support@bbg.se](mailto:support@bbg.se).  
  
[Read more about Digihjälpen ](/link/76e6e1c84e1e45b0b9a123f0341ba70e.aspx)

Transferring

If you can **transfer** the subscription to the person moving in after you, you avoid
getting a high final invoice and hassle with returning equipment. And the
person taking over avoids start-up fees and can start using the service the same day
they move in. Great for everyone involved!  
  
[How a transfer works](/link/ee4b32ebcce541048e79bb56e1859019.aspx)

Cancelling

If there is no one who can take over the subscription, you have to **cancel** it.  
  
 **This applies when cancelling:**

  * The notice period is **one month** if the subscription was taken out **after** May 1, 2014 and **three months** if the subscription was taken out **before** May 2014
  * If your subscription is tied, you must pay the fee for your services for the entire contract period. 
  * If you have borrowed equipment, such as a router, from us, it must be returned. [All about returning hardware](/link/2fc401aacf524cdbab726e289f8ecb7f.aspx)
  * If there are remaining installments for equipment that you have purchased, you will receive an invoice for the entire amount.
  * If you need to cancel your **home phone via broadband** subscription and want to keep your phone number, contact your new operator and ask them to transfer your number from us.
