# Device Support

Make your home WiFi network your own. You can conveniently change the name and password of your WiFi network in the device settings.  
A personalized network name and password streamlines usage, as once you have defined them yourself, they are easier to remember and thus connecting new devices to your home network is easy and quick.

See the instructions for your device under the "Changing WiFi Settings" section.
