# View Historical Changes and Contact Telenor

In My Telenor, you will find a comprehensive overview of all relevant changes in your subscriptions and communication with Telenor – we call it "History". For example, you can see if you have been sent a new SIM card, have purchased a new phone, or have blocked a phone number.

History is also where you will find messages from Telenor, so you can always go back and find important details about your subscription and your orders.
