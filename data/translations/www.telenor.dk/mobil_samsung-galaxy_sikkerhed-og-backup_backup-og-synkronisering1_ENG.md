# Back up your mobile

If you regularly back up and automatically sync, you can minimize
the risk of losing photos and other content if you are unlucky, for example, if your
mobile is stolen or you need to restore it and start from scratch.

**Backup**  
Your backup of all content and all settings. With a backup, you can
restore your mobile and more easily set up new devices with the same content,
contacts, etc.

**Sync**  
Your guarantee that all your devices are always up to date and have the same content,
images, etc. You may have written a new contact in your phonebook or taken new pictures during the day. Be aware that just because you have synced, you have not backed up your settings.
