# Transfer of Mobile Number Ownership

Here you will find information on how to change roles or transfer ownership of your subscriptions.

[Does this apply to a business subscription? Click here](/bedrift/kundeservice/mobil-
abonnement/eierskifte/)

## Roles on a Mobile Subscription

A mobile subscription can have three different roles - legal owner, payer, and user.

## Owner

Legal owner and responsible for the subscription. The owner can make changes, order services, and cancel the subscription. As the legal owner, you have full control over the subscription, as long as it does not infringe on **privacy** (such as viewing the PUK code or specified usage if the user is a different person).

## Payer

The payer is the one who receives the invoice. This role has access to the invoice but cannot make changes or cancel the subscription. If you are listed as the payer and invoice recipient for a mobile number, you can always request to be removed from this role without permission from the legal owner.

## User

This role is the person who actually uses the subscription. The user role can make a few changes to services at no cost, but cannot change to another mobile subscription or cancel the number. A registered user can request to be removed as a user without permission from the legal owner.

If a user is registered for the subscription, only that person can retrieve the PUK code.

The user cannot order a transfer of ownership without approval from the legal owner.

## What happens when we perform a transfer of ownership?

The subscription is cancelled under the old owner and ordered under the new owner. All additional services are transferred, unless you request that some be removed or added when you speak with customer service.

**Order a transfer of ownership:**  
** To perform a transfer of ownership, we must speak with both the current owner of the mobile number and the person who wishes to take over.

We therefore recommend that both of you are present when you call. This way, we can speak with both parties and complete the change immediately. Alternatively, you must call customer service when you have the opportunity. The transfer of ownership is entered when both have confirmed over the phone that this is to be done.

**  
Invoice:**  
** The old owner will receive a final invoice for the subscription from the day it is transferred, and the new owner will receive the first invoice from the same date.

**  
Name Change:**  
** If you have changed your name, you do not need to order a change - this is automatically updated from the national registry (updated once a month).
