# Repairing Your Mobile

Before submitting your mobile for repair, we recommend that you go through the list of solutions below. Perhaps you can quickly solve the problem yourself. Please note that errors or damage related to the use of non-original spare parts are not subject to warranty claims. If a repair is not performed correctly, and in an antistatic environment, it may result in consequential damage or malfunctions.

You can try factory resetting the product yourself and test if the problem is solved.
REMEMBER to disable theft protection such as "Find My iPhone" or Google FRP, and reset the product and remove codes before the product is delivered/sent for repair.
