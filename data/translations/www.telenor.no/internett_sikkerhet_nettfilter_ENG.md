# Web Filter

The web filter prevents access to internet images depicting sexual abuse of children, without exercising any form of general monitoring.

## How does it work?

The web filter is centrally located in Telenor's network itself, and applies to all Telenor customers regardless of whether you are surfing the internet via a computer, a tablet or on your mobile phone.

The filter works such that if a Telenor customer attempts to open a page with child pornography content, a block page will appear with information about the filter, and a link to KRIPOS (Norwegian National Criminal Investigation Service). KRIPOS has so far registered several hundred websites with illegal child pornography.

## No Monitoring

Telenor shall not exercise any form of general monitoring. We shall not assess the legality of content transmitted by others, beyond what follows from the current legal duty of care requirements.

Telenor will, in special cases, nevertheless have a proactive attitude towards content that is transmitted - for example in the case of the distribution of child pornography.

We will not in any way store the data, or log who actually hits the filter.

Please note that there will be websites that are not caught by the filter, because it only stops pages that are registered with KRIPOS.

Distribution via file sharing, email and the like is not affected by the filter.
