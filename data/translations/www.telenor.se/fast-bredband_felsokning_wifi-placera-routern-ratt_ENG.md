# Place the router correctly

Okay, it's time to review your router placement to maximize your wifi.
Where the router is located actually affects the speed.

###  
Here are our best tips:

  * Place the router **centrally, freely and high up**. So rather on a shelf than in a cabinet. And remember that thick concrete walls and large metal objects degrade the signals.
  * **The closer you are to the router, the faster you surf**. The distance to the router affects the speed, so stay within a maximum of 20 meters of it. 
  * **Avoid other signals and kitchen equipment!** Do not have the router near things like a microwave, refrigerator and freezer, aquarium, TV, cordless phones or devices that use Bluetooth.
