# How do I find my SIM card number?

The SIM card number is a number of up to 22 digits associated with the SIM
card itself. It is used, for example, if you want to transfer your number
from another provider to us. It is also called an ICC or ICCID.

In My Telenor, you can find an overview of your subscriptions. Tap "See
details" next to any subscription to see the associated SIM card number and
PUK code.

You can also always take the SIM card out of your mobile phone and see it there.
