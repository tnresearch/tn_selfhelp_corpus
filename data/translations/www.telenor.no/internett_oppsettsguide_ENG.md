## 1. Equipment Placement ➜

### Place WiFi Router II centrally in your home so that the signals reach as many
rooms as possible. Feel free to hang the router and extender on the wall with the included
bracket.

Try to place the WiFi router as centrally as possible in your home. WiFi Router II should
be visible, not on the floor or in a closet/storage room. Place the WiFi router slightly
off the floor so that the signals don't have to pass through furniture and other
obstacles before reaching your mobile phone or PC. The WiFi router must not be covered
and should be free-standing.

Be aware that thick brick walls and tiled bathrooms can affect the signal. In that
case, we recommend having an extender or changing the router's location.

[Read more about router placement](https://www.telenor.no/privat/artikler/internett/tradlost-
nettverk/slik-plasserer-du-ruteren-din/)

### Tips and information about WiFi Router II

  * #### Lamp Status

This is what the different lamps mean

 **Bottom Lamp:**

    * Solid white color = Connected to power

    * Blinking white = Starting up

    * Blinking green = WPS in progress

* * *

 **Top Lamp:**

    * Solid green = Connected

    * Blinking yellow = Obtaining IP address

    * Blinking red = Connected, but waiting for configuration

    * Dark = Not connected

* * *

    * Both lamps blinking yellow = Software update in progress

  * #### Router Details

Dimensions, encryption and WiFi standards

 **Dimensions:**  
Height: 230 mm. Width: 165 mm. Depth: 69 mm.

 **WiFi 6E standard:**  
Tri-band with three radios, 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz with 2x2/4x4/3x3 internal
antenna configuration  
Backward compatible with older standards (802.11a/b/g/ac)  
7.8 Gbps WiFi bandwidth (AX7800)

 **Standard encryption:  
** WPA2 and WPA3 encryption

 **Ethernet ports:**  
One 2.5 Gbps WAN port, one 2.5 Gbps LAN port and 3 pcs. 1 Gbps LAN port

## 3. Connect Extender ➜

WiFi Router II is our most powerful and newest router, and will provide good coverage for
most medium or smaller homes. If you have a large house, it may be smart to expand with one or more **WiFi extenders** , so that you get good
coverage everywhere.

Here you get step-by-step guidance for connecting an extender so that you
get better wireless coverage in your home.

[Step-by-step extender installation](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-
ruter/wifi-forsterker-2/)

### Tips and information about WiFi Extender II

 **Wired network at home?  
** Use the included network cable between the wall socket and the LAN connector on the WiFi
Extender II, to achieve the best possible signals.

Make sure that WiFi Router II is also connected to the same network.

 **Placement of your extender  
** It is important that you place WiFi Extender II between WiFi Router II and
where you want better coverage. It should be visible and preferably high up. Feel free to use
the included bracket for wall mounting.

[Placement guide](/privat/artikler/internett/tradlost-nettverk/derfor-
trenger-du-forsterker/)

## 4. Network Name and Password ➜

 **Tip:** For you to easily get all your devices on the new wireless
network, you should change the network name and password to what you had before.

To change network name, password, parental control, set up guest network and
open more ports, you must log in to your router. You will find the admin password on
the label under your WiFi Router II and log in via the button below.  
  
You can find an extended guide on how to do this here: [My WiFi -
Administration of your router](/kundeservice/internett/wifi/administrere-ruter/)

[Log in to wifi.telenor.no](https://wifi.telenor.no)

### Activate Netvern+

 **For security reasons, we recommend that you activate Netvern+**  
  
When you surf on Telenor's network, Netvern+ is the filter that tells you if a page
may contain viruses or phishing. This way, you will be notified if you are
on your way to a potentially infected page, and be denied access. This applies
also if you click on links sent to you via email or SMS.

The security filter checks if the websites you are trying to access are safe.

[Read more and get help to activate](/kundeservice/internett/sikkerhet/)

## 5. Connect Devices ➜

 **Connect via cable:**

Some devices must be connected with a cable, or should be connected with a cable to get
optimal speed. Connect the network cable between your device and your WiFi Router
or extender.

 **Connect mobile or computer:**

  * #### Mobile

iPhone

    1. Open **Settings**

    2. Select **Wi-Fi** and tap the switch

    3. Tap the name of the network you want to connect to

    4. If the network is secured with a password: Enter the password and tap **Connect**

If you cannot tap _Connect_ , you have entered the wrong password.

  * #### Mobile

Android

    1. Open **Settings**

    2. Select **Connections**

    3. Turn on **Wi-Fi**

    4. Select the network you want to connect to from the list of available networks

    5. Enter the WIFI password if any and tap **Connect**

  * #### PC

Windows

    1. Click on the icon for wireless network at the bottom by the clock and date

    2. Click on your network, select **Connect**

    3. Enter your network password and press **Connect**

  * #### PC

Mac

    1. Click on Wi-Fi, the icon for wireless network

    2. Press your network

    3. Enter the network password and click **Connect**
