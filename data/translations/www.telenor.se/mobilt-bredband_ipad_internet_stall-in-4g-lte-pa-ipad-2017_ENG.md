# Set up 4G (LTE) on iPad 2017

iPad 2017 lacks certain settings which means you will only be able to
use 3G until you change according to the guide below. Once the settings are
made, however, it will not say 4G, but LTE. But that is perfectly normal.

  * ### Go to "Settings, Mobile Data, APN Settings"

  * ### Enter services.telenor.se on the APN line.

Under "Mobile Data", "LTE Setting" and "Personal Hotspot" enter
services.telenor.se.

  * ### Activate LTE to turn on 4G.

Back to "Mobile Data" and select "Activate LTE" at the top right by sliding
the control. Then select "Activate LTE" on the question to turn on 4G.
