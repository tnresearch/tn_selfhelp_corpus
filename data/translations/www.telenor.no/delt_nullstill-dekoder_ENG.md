# Reset Your T-We Box

Here we show you step by step how to reset the decoder via the TV menu
or physically on the T-We box.

If you have an older decoder, you must [go here for
guidance.](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/)

## What Happens to the Settings

With T-We, all profiles and profile settings are stored in the cloud. These are automatically
retrieved after a reset. Recordings are also retained unless you choose to
delete them yourself.

Note that settings that are linked to the decoder must be set up again,
e.g.:

  * Power Saving

  * Image Resolution

  * Internet Connection

  * Language and Subtitles

# How to Reset the Decoder

Choose the options that apply to you and follow the instructions to reset
the T-We box.

# Do You Have Access to the TV Menu?

If you see the menu on the TV screen and can use the remote control to navigate
around, the easiest way is to reset the decoder via the menu. If you cannot get
the menu to appear, you can reset it physically on the T-We box.
