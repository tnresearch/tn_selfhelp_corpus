#### Fast delivery even within a day

You can receive your order as early as the next business day. Delivery starts from €0. Estimated delivery time is 1-3 business days. Delivery options include Click & Collect delivery to a store, delivery to a mailbox or slot, home delivery, delivery to a Posti pick-up point or Posti parcel locker. Products may be shipped in multiple packages.

[Read more about delivery methods and
costs](/tuki/asiakkuus/nettiostaminen/toimitus)

#### Interest-free DNA Payment Time

Freely choose how to pay for your device: in one or more installments, without any extra fees or interest.

[Read more about payment
methods](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/asiakkuus/nettiostaminen/maksutavat)

#### Always free returns

All products have free returns. Please note that only unused devices can be returned.

[See return
instructions](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/asiakkuus/nettiostaminen/palautus-ja-
takuu)

#### Reserve online, pick up from a DNA Store

Reserve or order online, pick up from the store. You will receive your order without a delivery fee.

[Read more about reserving a
product](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/asiakkuus/nettiostaminen/varaaminen)

## Cookies in DNA services

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device when you visit DNA's digital services or mobile apps. It is a numerical, anonymous identifier that does not harm your device or files, but helps us develop our services.

#### Necessary cookies

Always active

These cookies are necessary for the site to function and cannot be disabled. They are usually set only when you use functions that create service requests, such as setting privacy preferences, logging in, or filling out forms. You can set your browser to block these cookies or warn you about them, but some parts of the site may not work properly then.

#### Measurement and analytics cookies

Measurement and analytics cookies

Analytics cookies help us count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve our site's performance. They help us identify popular and less popular pages and how visitors move around the site. All cookie data is collected anonymously. If you do not accept these cookies, we will not know when you have visited our site.

Learn more‎

#### Functional cookies

Functional cookies

Functional cookies allow us to provide better and more personalized features, such as videos and live chat. Cookies may be set by us or by a third party; such as a service provider whose services have been added to our pages. If you do not accept these cookies, some or all of these features may not work properly.

Learn more‎

#### Advertising and social media cookies

Advertising and social media cookies

These cookies are set by our website or services either by our advertising partners or by the social media service provider we use. Through cookies, these companies can build a profile of your interests and show you ads or social media content that you are interested in. This can also affect the content and messages you see on other websites. If you do not accept these cookies, you will not see targeted advertising on different websites and may not be able to use social media sharing tools. Cookies identify the browser and device.
