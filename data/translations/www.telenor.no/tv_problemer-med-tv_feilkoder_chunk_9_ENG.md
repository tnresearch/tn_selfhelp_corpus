#### F9003Missing rights in T-We app

The error is due to one of these missing playback rights:

  * Too many simultaneous streams for your subscription are being played - end some streams and try again.

  * The channel is limited to playback at home - try again when you are at home.

  * There are no rights to playback in the T-We app (regardless of location) - this channel can only be viewed on the T-We box.

#### F4105Content is blocked due to missing rights

This error message occurs because you are trying to view content in an area where the content is not available. It should not normally occur if you are in Norway, but if you have, for example, activated a VPN connection or are using networks that do not identify your location, it is interpreted as if you are abroad.

Try connecting your device to another network, and see if that solves the problem. If you still experience problems, you can contact Customer Service for further assistance. [See opening hours and contact information.](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

#### F2316

**Experiencing this error message can be due to several reasons.**

The error may be related to where you are located and that Telenor does not have the rights to show the content where you are geographically.

  1. **Are you outside the EU?** If so, the error code is because Telenor does not have the rights to show what you are trying to see outside the EU.

  2. **Router settings:** We have found that some 3rd party routers have built-in functionality (such as Content Filtering) that will create problems. The recommendation is to adjust / turn off this function in your router, and make sure you use Telenor's DNS settings.

If this does not help, the error can often be resolved by trying again later. If the error persists, try completely closing the T-We app and restarting it. If you still experience problems, you can contact Customer Service for further assistance. [See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
