# Delivery

Fast delivery, even within a day. Returns are always free.

## Delivery Area

Orders are delivered only to Mainland Finland (orders are not delivered to Åland). Depending on the product, you can choose delivery to a DNA Store of your choice, directly to your door, by letter, or as a small package directly to your mailbox, or delivery to a Posti parcel locker or pick-up point. You will always see the available delivery methods when placing your order. Delivery to a store is always free.

Delivery is fast - you will often receive your order as early as the next working day. Returns are always free.

## Delivery Methods

Free delivery to store: Click & Collect

Some orders have the option of Click & Collect, i.e. delivery to a store. If you choose this delivery method, you will receive a message when your order is ready for collection from the store you selected. Delivery to the store is free. Your order will be held for one week.

Delivery to your door

Your order will be conveniently delivered to your home, even as early as the next working day. Normal delivery time is 1-3 working days. In larger cities, delivery is between 9 am and 9 pm on weekdays. In other areas, deliveries are made by 2 pm on weekdays. You can track the shipment via the Posti OmaPosti app. Posti will contact you in advance by text message or email to arrange a suitable delivery time.

Depending on the order, the products may be shipped in multiple packages. Shipments are always transported as carbon-neutral Posti Green shipments. If you ordered a television, it will be delivered by a separate delivery, at no extra cost.

Delivery to Post Office or Posti pick-up point

The shipment can be collected from the Post Office or the selected Posti pick-up point, often as early as the next working day. Normal delivery time is 1-3 working days. You can track the shipment via the Posti OmaPosti app. You will receive a notification when the order is ready for collection by text message and in the OmaPosti app.

When collecting the package, it is advisable to bring the tracking number and identification. Packages are always transported as carbon-neutral Posti Green shipments.

Delivery to Posti parcel locker

The parcel locker is selected when placing the order. The shipment can be collected, often as early as the next working day. Our normal delivery time is 1-3 working days. You will receive a notification when the order is ready for collection by text message and in the OmaPosti app.

You should keep the message, as it contains a code required to collect the package. The package will be kept in the locker for 7 days from its arrival. Packages are always transported as carbon-neutral Posti Green shipments.

Posti small package to door service

Delivered 2–5 working days from shipment. Trackable.

Depending on the size, it is delivered either to the mailbox/letterbox or behind the door. You will receive a message when the package has been delivered. You can track the shipment online or via the OmaPosti app. Posti is a domestic and responsible choice. Always transported as carbon-neutral Posti Green shipments.

Delivery by Posti Express package

A quick package for companies, usually delivered by 4 pm on the next working day. Delivered without separate notification. Packages are always transported as carbon-neutral Posti Green shipments.

## Delivery Costs

Delivery costs vary depending on the chosen delivery method. Delivery to a DNA Store is always free.

## Delivery Time

The estimated delivery time for the products is 1-3 working days, even during the next working day. If the device's delivery time is longer than normal, this will be stated when placing the order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

When I order online, what is the delivery method and area?

Depending on the product, you can choose delivery directly to your door, by letter or as a small package directly to your mailbox, or delivery to a Posti parcel locker or pick-up point. You will always see the available delivery methods when placing your order.

The estimated delivery time for the products is 1-3 working days, but often delivery is even during the next working day. If the delivery time is longer than normal, this will be stated when placing the order.

The delivery area for orders from the DNA online store is Finland, excluding Åland.

What is the normal delivery time? Can the order be tracked?

The estimated delivery time for the products is 1-3 working days, but often the order is delivered as early as the next working day. If the delivery time is longer than normal, this will be stated when placing the order.

You can track packages via the Posti OmaPosti app.
