# Error 3: Software Download Failed

Are you getting the error message **Error 3: Software Download Failed** on
your TV screen?  

First, check that your TV box is connected directly to your service
distributor, your broadband outlet, or your router and **not** via any
additional equipment.  

If the TV box is connected to a service distributor, [check that it is
connected to the correct port](/support/portar/).
