# Incorrect local news/weather displayed

Currently, we have local news on SVT's live TV.

If you are watching back, the Stockholm news will be displayed.  
  
If you want to watch SVT's local broadcasts afterwards, they are
available in the SVT Play app or via the TV hub by pressing the Home
button and then going to "News and world events" and searching for the local
news you want to see.  
  
For TV4, we do not have any regional news or weather.
