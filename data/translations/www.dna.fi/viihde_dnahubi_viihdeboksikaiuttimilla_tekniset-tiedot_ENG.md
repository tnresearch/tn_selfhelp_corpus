# Technical Specifications

The DNA Entertainment Box with Speakers works in all homes, as it is Antenna
Ready HD- and Cable Ready HD-compatible.

The entertainment box's channel offering can be viewed in IPTV quality over the internet
everywhere in Finland. Nelonen, Jim, Liv and Hero channels are visible over the internet,
if the entertainment box is used with a DNA broadband connection.

The package includes the entertainment box, power cord, antenna cable, Bluetooth-
remote control, USB-C, HDMI and Ethernet cables, and a quick guide.

## Basic Features:

4K/HD antenna and cable tuner (1 pc)

DNA TV user interface (No separate Android TV home menu)

7-day program guide with search functions

Google Play app store

Parental lock

## Picture:

4K/UHD, HDR10

4K video resolution 3840 x 2160p @24/30/50/60 Hz​

HD video resolution 1920 x 1080p @24/30/50/60 Hz​

Dolby Vision

HDMI 2.1​

## Audio:

Built-in speakers, whose sound world has been optimized by Bang & Olufsen.

Number of speaker elements: three speakers and a bass speaker

Dolby Atmos

eARC support 11.3.2024 (beta)

## Memory:

RAM memory: 3 GB

Internal memory: 16 GB

## Operating System:

Android TV 12

## Dimensions:

Height: 83 mm​

Width: 165 mm​

Depth: 165 mm

## Connections and Connectivity:

Connectors:

  * 1 Gb Ethernet RJ45 port (for internet connection, entertainment services and updates)
  * HDMI 2.1
  * USB-A 2.0 (for example, for an external hard drive, mouse or keyboard)

Bluetooth: 5.0 BLE support

Wi-Fi: 1 X Wi-Fi 6 , 2 x 2 dual band (2.4 & 5 GHz)

## Other Features:

Google Assistant (in English)

eARC (Enhanced Audio Return Channel)

Spotify Connect

Recording channels to an external hard drive is not possible.

Sagemcom
