## Frequently asked questions about mobile subscriptions

  * How do I see when I signed up for my mobile subscription?

You can see this by logging in to My Telenor. There you will find your agreements. My
Telenor is available both [here on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) and as an
[app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

  * How is my number transfer going?

If you have chosen to transfer your mobile number to Telenor from another operator
you can see the status of the transfer in My Telenor – either here [on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) or in the [
app](/link/f80d97c00f0d437fbce32ae0510e86ed.aspx).

  
You will also see your temporary number that you can use before the transfer is
completed.  
  
[More about transferring your number to or from
Telenor](/link/6d4c5eb9c1b64f5fbbfbf2237b92d400.aspx)

  * How do I upgrade my phone with Telenor Change?

You do this in one of [our
stores](/link/28137742ca7249e2abe4b813fdb8c583.aspx). Bring your old phone
so that we can inspect it. Keep in mind that your old phone must be in [okay
condition](/link/7701e66610884224af0402a27bf13ea2.aspx).

[Read more about what is required to upgrade your
phone](/link/3a3fff6a51ce46b3973b1016a3a1eb35.aspx)

  * How do I know it's Telenor calling?

You can read about this on our page about [customer contact, under the heading
"Telephone sales"](/link/07ce73e097bd43b0a62b139ddb01ce79.aspx#telefonforsaljning)

  * How do I know if I can upgrade my phone to a new one?

You can see this in My Telenor. Either [here on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) or in the
[app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx "My Telenor").

See more

Fix it yourself in My Telenor

In the My Telenor app you can:

  * Check how much data you have used
  * Share data
  * See when you can change phone
  * See how many installments you have left
