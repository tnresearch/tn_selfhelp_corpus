# eSIM Support

## Activating an eSIM

#### Make sure your device is eSIM ready

eSIM readiness is available in the latest phone models, tablets and smartwatches.
Double-check this in the product information for your device.

#### Order an eSIM

You can get an eSIM for an existing or new subscription from the [DNA
Store](https://www.dna.fi/dnakaupat) or from DNA's sales service, tel: [0800
550 044](tel:0800550044) (Mon-Fri 9am-8pm, Sat 10am-4pm). The number is free of charge.

#### Start using it

After ordering an eSIM, you can activate it immediately using the activation code or QR code found in the Oma DNA app. If you prefer, you can also pick up the QR-
code from the nearest DNA Store. You can activate your eSIM immediately via the Oma DNA app:

  * **Open the Oma DNA app** (if you don't have the app yet, you can download it to your device from the app store).
  * **Select the mobile subscription for which you want to activate the eSIM** and select "SIM card and PUK codes" below it.
  * **Click: "Activate eSIM"** and follow the instructions in the app.

If you want mobile connectivity for a smartwatch with eSIM readiness, you can find instructions on the [smartwatch support pages](https://www.dna.fi/Tuki-alykellot/apple-watch).

## Frequently Asked Questions about eSIM

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM card that works like a traditional SIM card
in phones, tablets and, for example, smartwatches. Depending on the device,
you can choose in the future whether you want your subscription to work via a traditional SIM card
or digitally, with an eSIM. When ordering or
changing your subscription, you can choose an eSIM instead of a traditional SIM card if
your device is eSIM ready.

For which subscriptions is an eSIM available?

eSIM is currently available for all DNA mobile subscriptions, excluding
DNA Prepaid subscriptions.

What is the difference between a traditional SIM card and an eSIM?

You will only notice the difference during activation: you don't have to wait for an eSIM to be delivered by mail, choose the right card size or install it
yourself inside the device. Just order and activate it: activating the
ordered eSIM is possible in the Oma DNA app or at the DNA Store.

For which devices is an eSIM available?

For the time being, eSIM is available for the latest phones and tablets as well as, for example,
smartwatches; for devices where the device manufacturer has announced eSIM-
readiness. You can check this in the product information for the device.  
  
See more detailed instructions: [eSIM](https://www.dna.fi/tuki-
puhelinliittymat/esim) activation or ordering mobile connectivity for a
[Samsung Galaxy Watch](https://www.dna.fi/tuki-alykellot/samsung-galaxy-
watch) or [Apple Watch](https://www.dna.fi/tuki-alykellot/apple-watch)
smartwatch.

How do I get mobile connectivity for my smartwatch?

First, make sure your smartwatch is eSIM ready. Then you will also need the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service. It can be ordered either directly
from the smartwatch's own app (Apple Watch) or in advance from the DNA Store, DNA's
sales service or the Oma DNA app (Samsung Galaxy Watch). See
detailed instructions on the [smartwatch support pages](https://www.dna.fi/Tuki-
alykellot/apple-watch).

Please note that the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service can only be linked to a DNA
mobile subscription.
