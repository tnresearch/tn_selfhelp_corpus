# DNA TV App on Android TV

You can download the DNA TV app to your Android TV and use the app
conveniently this way. Activate the app, and if storage is included in your
subscription, you can record and watch recordings (except Nelonen, Liv, JIM,
Hero). In addition, you can easily watch movies you have rented through the
app.

## Getting Started

You can download the DNA TV app to Android TV when you have Android TV
version 5.0 or later.

#### Activate the service for the first time as follows:

Download the DNA TV app

Open the app

Log in with your DNA Credentials, using your email + password combination.

## Features and Usage

Homepage

Through the homepage you can find the most popular movies and series, explore
the Rental Store's offerings, and see the latest recordings (if your
subscription includes recording and you have recorded programs).

Favorites

Favorites shows the programs and movies you have marked as favorites.

Recordings

Recordings shows all the programs you have recorded.

Continue Watching

You can find all the movies and programs where you left off in the Continue
Watching section and easily continue watching from where you stopped.

Streaming Services

Through the Streaming Services section, you can easily navigate to MTV Katsomo,
Yle Areena and Ruutu.

Recorded Series, Movies and Sports

Through the categories, you can easily find your favorite recorded programs
easily organized into various subcategories such as comedy, drama or action.

Rental Store

In the Rental Store you can easily rent movies and series immediately after
the theatrical release. You need a personal four-digit DNA TV PIN code for
renting, which you received when you subscribed. You can also find it in My
Services and the Oma DNA app.

Program Guide

Through the program guide, you can view the program information for channels up
to 7 days in advance. Through the program details, you can go to the program
page, where you can see, for example, more detailed information about the
program as well as seasons and episodes of series.

If the recording feature is included in your subscription, you can:

  * Record upcoming programs as well as a program that is currently running by
    selecting record program.
  * If it is a series, you can record all episodes of the series by selecting
    Record Episodes.

Settings

Through the settings you can see more detailed information about the service,
change the app language, modify recording rules or log out of the app.
