## Frequently Asked Questions about Mobile Broadband

  * How do I buy extra data for my flexible mobile broadband?

You can buy more data for your mobile broadband in the [My Telenor app.
](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx)

  * How much does extra data cost for my flexible mobile broadband?

It costs SEK 169 for 15 GB. You can buy extra data in the [My Telenor
app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx). The extra data is
valid until the end of the next month.

If you have mobile broadband 15 GB or unlimited data, you can also use part
of the data abroad, in the EU/EEA and a number of other countries, [read more
here](/link/9653998f39c745e396d5367b7478cf92.aspx).

  * How do I top up flexible mobile broadband Pay As You Go?

If you want to top up Mobile Broadband Pay As You Go, you can easily do so via our [Telenor Top Up app](/link/8ad148929ae541328599af3f428082a6.aspx), or on our[
](/link/5775b1740ff94087bf80394b7a19e43e.aspx)[top-up page](/link/5775b1740ff94087bf80394b7a19e43e.aspx).
There you can also see how much it costs to top up. Good luck!

  * Can multiple people use my mobile broadband?

Absolutely! Get a [router](/link/79d78e28f44f465a80e4951c816f9195.aspx) so
that multiple people can use your mobile broadband.

  * How do I get a public IP address?

From January 22, 2025, Public IP is an add-on service you can get through
our customer service. [Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx)
and we will help you.
