## Frequently asked questions about TV

  * Which devices can I stream to?

**On the computer**  
Go to the address [stream.telenor.se](http://stream.telenor.se) and log in.  
  
**On mobile and tablet**  
To be able to use the service on your mobile and tablet, you need to download
the Telenor Stream app. The app can be found in the [App
Store](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/telenor-stream/id1165060878?l=en-se) and
[Google
Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.bredbandsbolaget.stream&hl=sv).  
  
**On the TV**  
To access the service via your TV, you can use Google Chromecast or
download the Telenor Stream app to Apple TV (gen 4 and newer) or Android
TV (version 9 or newer). Or stream directly on your smart TV from Samsung
or LG. [More information can be found
here](/link/32caafd011864baf8e56792a180757e6.aspx).

  * How do I log in to Telenor Stream for the first time?

Here's how:

    1. First, activate your account at [telenor.se/aktivera-stream. ](/link/7bf62d1cb0594c6a8e394ce82709c537.aspx)
    2. Then, either go to [stream.telenor.se](http://stream.telenor.se) or download Telenor Stream in the App Store or Google Play, and log in there.

  * Can I stream to multiple devices simultaneously?

Yes, you can stream on up to two devices simultaneously with your subscription.

  * Which TV packages can I choose from?

You can choose between four different TV packages – Basic, Medium, Large or Premium.
The difference between the packages is how many channels are included.

[Here you can search for your address and see which TV package you can
get.](/link/da04f05abecf4c5799b1a22b069a1017.aspx)

[The TV packages include the Telenor
Stream app](/link/7da15ed71c7d4874bbb0e4c3bd6ccd7b.aspx), so you can watch
your TV content on any screen you want, wherever you are. A TV box is also
included as a loan at no extra cost.

You can easily switch between the different TV packages, and your commitment period is not extended when you switch.

  * How can I watch the Champions League on Telenor?

You can find all Champions League broadcasts on Telenor Stream, in the Viaplay app
and on the V Sport channels. To watch the Champions League, you first need a
TV package [TV Premium](/link/59fef91711284d888c2678f184ede13e.aspx).
