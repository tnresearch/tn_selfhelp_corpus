# Prepaid Card for Alarm

Telenor's prepaid cards work not only for your mobile and tablet, but also for home alarms.

## Get a prepaid card for your alarm

Our prepaid cards also work with home alarms and [you can order a prepaid card free of charge here](/handla/kontantkort/bestall-startpaket-old/). The SIM card you receive will come in all three sizes. If you are looking for Telematics, you can read more about it further down on this page instead.

## Top up prepaid card for alarm

In the app [Telenor Top Up](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/), you can top up the card, see the balance and validity period, and also subscribe to a top-up. If you subscribe to a top-up, the card is automatically refilled and you never have to worry about the validity period expiring.

Please note that prepaid cards must be topped up once a year, otherwise the card will be terminated.

## The prepaid card in three sizes

###

Nano-SIM card

The smallest SIM card on the market. Originally, only the iPhone 5 and later iPhone models required nano-SIM, but now a number of other brands also use it.

Micro-SIM card

The middle size of SIM cards. Some tablets and newer smartphones require Micro-SIM.

Standard-SIM card

The oldest SIM card on the market. Most slightly older mobiles, modems and routers have this size.

## Telematics

Telematics is used for communication between machines. If you are a business customer, you can read more about subscriptions for connected devices on our page about [IoT for business](/foretag/iot/). Are you a private customer? Then you can instead get [our Smart Connectivity subscription](/handla/mobilabonnemang/internet-of-things/).

Telematics is used for, among other things:

  * Readings on vending machines, electricity meters, district heating meters or water meters
  * Alarms that can use the mobile network
  * Card terminals
