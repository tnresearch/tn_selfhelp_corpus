# How to Search for Content in the T-We App

The search function in the T-We App can be used to quickly find content when you know what you are looking for.

## Search Function

The search function is easily accessible at the bottom of the app and is marked with a magnifying glass.

Search displays content from all channels you subscribe to and all integrated streaming services. For streaming services, the search applies regardless of whether you subscribe to the service or not.

## Filter Search Results

To make it easier to find the content you are looking for, you can use filters to narrow the search. You can find this by entering your desired search and pressing the three lines in the top right corner. There are three different sorting options here: Program Type, Genre, and Relevance.

 **Under "Program Type" you will find:**

  * Movies and Series

  * TV Guide

  * Weekly Archive

In addition, you can choose from a long list of genres and change the order based on title or relevance.

## Delete Search History

If you want to delete the list of what you have searched for, you can do this directly in the app.

  1. Select **Search**

  2. Press **Delete Search History**

  3. Enter [PIN code](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-app/pin-kode/)

  4. The search history has now been deleted.
