**Making a Fault or Disruption Report**

You can make a fault or disruption report by calling customer service at 044
144 044. If the problem is with your modem or mobile broadband device,
please be near the device when you call. We provide 24/7 service for fault
and disruption situations.

Lost PUK Code?

You can check the PUK code for your mobile subscription or mobile broadband
connection free of charge by logging on to the DNA website. You can find the
PUK code in your subscription details.

**Here's how:** log in and select **Subscriptions and Services**. Select the
subscription in question and choose **SIM card and PUK codes**.

[Go to login](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen)

You can also find the PUK code in the My DNA app in your subscription
details.

You can also obtain the PUK code as a logged-in user via chat.

## Cookies in DNA services

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device when you visit
DNA's digital services or mobile apps. It is a numerical, anonymous
identifier that does not harm your device or files, but helps us develop our
services.

[Read more about DNA's privacy policy](https://corporate.dna.fi/tietosuoja)

Measurement and analytics cookies

functions may not work properly.
