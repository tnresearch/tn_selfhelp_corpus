# Insert SIM Card into Your iPad

For iPad Air, a nano-SIM card is used. Your tablet comes with a metal clip
that you will need to use to insert the SIM card. The metal clip is located on the first
page of the cover that is in the box itself, under the
protective plate on which your iPad rests.

Follow the guide below to insert it correctly.

  * ## You will find the SIM card holder on the bottom left side of your iPad

  * ## Open the SIM card holder

Insert the metal clip into the small hole on the cover where the SIM card holder is located.
Press gently until the holder "pops" out.

  * ## Pull out the SIM card holder

Use your fingers or the opposite end of the metal clip to pull
the SIM card holder all the way out.

  * ## Place the SIM card in the SIM card holder

Make sure the SIM card is facing the correct way.

  * ## Push the SIM card holder back into the phone

Now you are ready to use your mobile phone.
