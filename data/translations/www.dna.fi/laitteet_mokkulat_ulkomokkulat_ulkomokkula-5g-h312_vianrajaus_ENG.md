## Troubleshooting

The External Modem's WiFi connection cannot be found

The External Modem's WiFi will be disabled if no device is connected to it within two
hours. WiFi can be reactivated using the Huawei AI Life app, through the
management page opened in a web browser, or by restarting the External
Modem.

You may also be too far from the External Modem. Move closer and try
again.

The External Modem's WiFi is slow

The External Modem's WiFi connection is intended only for configuring the device's
settings. For internet use, connect a separate WiFi router (e.g., Google WiFi) to the External Modem.

Restoring Original Settings

Connect the External Modem to a power source and turn it on. Wait until the External Modem's
indicator light is on steadily. Press the Reset button on the power supply with a
narrow-tipped object for over 5 seconds, until the External Modem's indicator light
blinks.

After restoring the factory settings, the External Modem's settings must be defined
again.

Indicator light does not turn on when starting

If the External Modem is used at a temperature below -30 °C, it must
preheat for a moment before it starts working. At -40 °C,
preheating takes about 10 minutes. If the operating temperature of the External Modem is
really low, wait a moment and check if the External Modem
works correctly.
