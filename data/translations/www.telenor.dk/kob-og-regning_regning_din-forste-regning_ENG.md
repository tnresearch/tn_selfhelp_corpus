# Your first bill

Your first bill is higher than your normal subscription price, as it covers
more than one month. You are paying from the day your subscription starts and
until the 15th – plus one month after that. This is perfectly normal and simply
means that it takes a little longer to receive your first bill. The same applies
if you have a new subscription on the bill.

**Extra usage  
** On your bill you will find a total price for your subscription, selected
services and any extra usage. Extra usage may occur if you call
outside the EU, donate money or buy bus tickets with your mobile. You can find it
under _Extra usage_ on your bill.  
Here are some examples of what you can pay for with your mobile:

  * Content services – when you participate in competitions, download ringtones and play games
  * Purchase of bus and train tickets – when you use the SMS receipt as a ticket
  * Purchases in Google Play or App store – it can be apps, in-app purchases and licenses
  * Voting – it can be if you vote in Dancing with the Stars
  * Donations – it can be if you donate to the Cancer Society

[See more on My Telenor](/mit-telenor/), where you can continuously monitor your extra
usage each month.

**Usage max  
** We have set a usage max on your subscription to avoid overspending and
make it easier to keep track of your extra usage.  
[Read more about usage max](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/andre-
services/forbrugsmax/)

**Good to know about your bill**

  * The billing period runs from the 16th to the 15th of the month
  * You pay in advance for your subscription and selected services – that is, before you have used it
  * You pay in arrears for any extra usage – that is, after you have used it
  * You can find your bill on [MyTelenor.dk/bill](/mit-telenor/regning/) around the 20th of each month

**Is your bill under 59 DKK? Then we transfer it to next month  
** If your bill is under 59 DKK, we will transfer it to next month and
add it to your next bill. If you have 3 low bills in a row,
we will combine them and send you one consolidated bill.
