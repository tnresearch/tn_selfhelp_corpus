# Find the right port on the service distributor

Port 1 (LAN 1) is right for most, but not for everyone.

### How to find the right port:

  * Go to [telenor.se/port](/support/portar/) and enter your address
  * Connect to the port shown in the search results
  * Then activation usually only takes a quarter of an hour, but can take up to a day. But take it easy, you will receive an SMS when it is ready.

###  
Connected to the right port, but still can't surf?

  * If it says you should connect to port 1 and you still can't surf, you are probably **too early**
  * Check the date listed as "preliminary activation date" on the order confirmation you received by email

  
Seemed complicated? Take it easy, read again if you need to.
