# Subscriptions

In the My Telenor app, you get a simple and quick overview of your usage. If
you have a mobile subscription, you can, for example, see how much data you
have left. You can also see how many SMS messages you have sent and how many
minutes you have talked.

The My Telenor app gives you an overview of all subscriptions in the home, you
can, for example, follow whether the children are about to run out of data.
Press "Subscriptions" at the bottom of the menu and select the desired
subscription.

Other users in the family can see usage details for their own subscriptions.

If you have a bundle discount, this will also be included under your
subscriptions.

_Buy extra data_  
_ If you have run out of data, you can buy extra GB – or continue with reduced
speed (64 Kbit). You can buy extra data in the My Telenor app via the front
page or under the desired subscription. When you have used your data allowance,
you will also receive an SMS with a link where you can buy extra data via a
link._

Payments for purchasing extra data will only be deducted from your next bill.
It is a one-time purchase, and any unused data is not transferred to the next
billing period. The billing period for private customers runs from the 16th to
the 15th of the month. Prices are shown in the app.
