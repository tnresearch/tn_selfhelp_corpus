## Troubleshooting

How do I restore factory settings?

With the modem turned on, press the **Reset/Restore** button with a pointed
object for about 10 seconds, or until the power indicator light turns off.
After this, the lights will start flashing and the device's factory settings
will be restored. Restoring factory settings will reset the settings to
their defaults. You can
reconfigure them after the modem has been restored to factory settings.

What do I do if I forget the WiFi password or the web-based management page
password?

Restore the modem to factory settings and try again using the default WiFi
password and the login password for the settings page found on the info label.

What do I do if the 4G or 5G indicator light does not come on or I cannot
connect to the internet?

  1. Check that the Nano-SIM card is in place. See the SIM card size and correct installation method [here](https://www.dna.fi/dna-kotimokkula-5g-wifi-h122#kayttoonotto).
  2. If the problem does not go away, restore the modem's factory settings and try again.
  3. Check with DNA to see if the SIM card has been deactivated.

What do I do if the internet speed is low?

  1. Check the strength of the received 4G/5G signal either from the device's management page or using the Huawei AI Life (Android) or Huawei SmartHome (iOS) app. Adjust the router's position or move it closer to a window to improve signal reception.
  2. If the device is connected to the modem via WiFi, try moving the device to get a better signal.
  3. Switch to the 5 GHz band of WiFi if your device supports it.
  4. Restart the modem and device and try again.

How do I connect the modem using WiFi Protected Setup (WPS)?

  1. Make sure WiFi is turned on on your device.
  2. Hold down the Hi/WPS button on the router for at least one second and then release it; the WiFi indicator light will start flashing.

Why can't I use the modem's web-based management page?

  1. Make sure the computer is connected to the modem with an Ethernet cable or WiFi connection.
  2. Make sure the computer is set to obtain the IP address and DNS server address automatically.
  3. Restart the web browser or try using another browser or device.
  4. If the problem does not go away, restore the modem's factory settings.

Why is the content displayed on the web-based management page not up to date?

Depending on the browser's cache, the content on the management page may not
be up to date. Manually clear the browser's cache (e.g., open the browser and select Internet Options > General >
Browsing history > Delete) and reload the management page.

How do I connect to 5 GHz WiFi?

  1. Your modem supports 5 GHz WiFi, which is faster and less susceptible to interference. However, it does not penetrate walls as well as 2.4 GHz WiFi and can only be used with devices that support 5 GHz WiFi.
  2. If you have enabled the feature that prioritizes 5 GHz on your router, the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks share the same network name and the connection is automatically made to the faster network. If this feature is disabled, two WiFi network names will be displayed. Connect to the WiFi network whose name ends with "_5GHz". You can manage this feature on the modem's web-based management page.

If you are having problems with the modem:

  1. Restart the modem.
  2. Restore the modem's factory settings.
  3. See the help section of the web-based management page.
  4. Contact DNA.
