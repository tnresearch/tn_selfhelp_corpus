## Replacing the Fiber Box with XS-020X-A

Follow the instructions below carefully when replacing the fiber box (ONT). Be careful not to bend or damage the fiber during installation, and ensure that all connections are securely fastened before turning on the power.

There are significant differences from home to home in how the fiber box is mounted. We therefore recommend that you familiarize yourself with the availability, fastening method, and any need to make changes to the way the box is attached to fit the new box, _before_ you begin work. There may be variations in what type of screws you should use, the distance between mounting points, and the like.

## 1. Dismantling the Old Fiber Box

  1. Unscrew the router and fiber box. Pull out the power cord from the fiber box, which is usually black.

  2. Pull out the internet cable, which is usually a YELLOW LAN port.

  3. Pull out the fiber cable, most often a GREEN port and a laser symbol. Note that some fiber boxes have a safety mechanism that requires you to open a cover on the back to remove the fiber cable. If the cable seems difficult to remove, this may be the reason. Gently detach the box and open the cover before pulling out the cable.

 **NOTE!** The fiber cable must be bent as little as possible to avoid damage to the fiber.
The fiber cable transmits optical light. Therefore, do not look directly into the cable, and avoid exposing it to dust and dirt.

  4. Remove the defective fiber box and sort it as EE waste (Electronic waste).

## 2. Open New Fiber Box

Open the package with the new fiber box. Unless you have been informed that there is damage to your fiber cable itself, you should not use the new fiber cable that comes in the box, just connect to the old one.

  1. Turn the box over and find the entrance marked with a yellow triangle.

  2. Slide the cover away with two fingers.

  3. Leave the cover open to later insert the fiber cable.

## 3. Connect Fiber Cable

  1. Remove the plug under the cover.

  2. Under the plug is a green port.

  3. Carefully insert the green fiber cable into the green port.

  4. Put the cover back on, and gently pull on the fiber cable to make sure it is securely seated.

## 4. Connect Router and Power

  1. Adjust the wall mounts so that the dimensions fit the new fiber box.

  2. Hang the fiber box on the wall.

  3. Connect the internet cable to the port labeled LAN 10G.

  4. Plug the new power cable from the box into the outlet, and connect the black power cable (input labeled UPS). Press the black power button. Turn on your router.

  5. After a startup time of approximately 5-10 minutes, you will be back online. You can see this when the light on your router is green, and the fiber box lights up on POWER, LAN 10G and NTWK.
