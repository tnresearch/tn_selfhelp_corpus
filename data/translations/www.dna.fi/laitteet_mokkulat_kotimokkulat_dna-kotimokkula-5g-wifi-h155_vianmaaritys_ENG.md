## Troubleshooting

What should I do if no lights are on on the modem, but the power cord is connected?

**Possible cause:**

The modem is not receiving power. The adapter or modem may be faulty.

**Proposed solution:**

Connect the power cord as instructed in the user manual and check that the outlet has power. Bring the adapter and modem to a DNA Store for replacement.

What should I do if all the lights indicating a network connection are on, but I cannot connect to the internet?

**Possible cause:**

The modem may be frozen.

**Proposed solution:**

Disconnect the modem's power cord for about a minute and, after reconnecting the cord, wait for the network connection status lights to come on again. Restart your computer or other device you are using. Also try connecting with an Ethernet cable. You can also reset the modem.

What should I do if all the lights indicating a network connection are on, but the computer reports "limited connectivity"?

**Possible cause:**

The modem is not connecting to the internet.

**Proposed solution:**

Check the connection and connections. Also check the settings of your computer/device (e.g., internet settings and network card). If necessary, reset the modem, disconnect the modem's power cord for about a minute and wait for the lights to come on again.

What should I do if all the lights indicating a network connection are on and the settings have been checked?

**Possible cause:**

The firewall may be blocking traffic.

**Proposed solution:**

Check the firewall settings on your computer.

What should I do if all the lights indicating a network connection are on, the settings have been checked, and you are using your own connection sharing device, such as a router?

**Possible cause:**

Your own connection sharing device is frozen or faulty.

**Proposed solution:**

Connect the modem to the computer directly with an Ethernet cable or use the modem's wireless connection. You can also try setting the modem to bridge mode. Contact the party selling routers or other additional devices.

What should I do if the wireless network is not working?

**Possible cause:**

If the connection works with an Ethernet cable, the problem is with the WiFi network.

**Proposed solution:**

Restart the modem, check the settings and try the WiFi tips at: dna.fi/tuki-wifi. If these do not work, contact customer service.

What should I do if older devices cannot connect via WiFi?

  1. log in to the modem's settings page and select **Wi-Fi Settings**
  2. change **Security mode** from the drop-down menu from **WPA2-PSK/WPA3-SAE** to **WPA/WPA2-PSK**
  3. save the change by selecting **Save** at the end
