## Troubleshooting

What should I do if my modem's battery is swollen?

If your modem's battery is swollen even slightly, stop using it immediately.
Take the modem to a DNA Store. Swelling can be noticed directly by the
battery bulging or, for example, the back cover moving. With current battery
technology, a similar risk of swelling exists for every battery-powered
device. The risk increases if the use is truly intensive, the modem is in a
place where its temperature rises critically, or changes have been made to it
that are not mentioned in the manual.

Why doesn't the modem respond?

Try restarting the device. If that doesn't help, restore the device's factory
settings.

Why can't I always connect to the modem?

To save power, the modem's automatic WiFi connection shut-off function
disconnects the WiFi connection when there have been no connections for a
while. You can restart the WiFi connection by pressing the Power button.  
  
You can also disable this feature, but it may affect battery life. Log in to
the settings page or web interface and disable the automatic WiFi connection
cutoff feature.

Why won't the modem turn on?

The battery is likely empty. Charge the modem for a while and then turn it
back on.

Why did the modem suddenly turn off?

If the modem is used continuously for long periods, it may overheat and its
automatic protection system will activate. In this case, the WiFi connection
will be lost and the modem will turn off.  
  
Before turning the power back on, make sure the device has adequate
ventilation and has cooled down.

Why do I have to wait for the DFS function?

DFS is a new EU regulation that requires a battery-powered device to check
that its 5 GHz WiFi network does not interfere with radar stations. Scanning
for radar stations can take 1–15 minutes.

My modem's internet connection is slow

First, check from the top right corner of the travel modem's screen that the
4G connection is as good as possible. If necessary, place the modem where
the signal is best.  
  
Connect the computer directly to the modem via USB, if the speed is much
better, there is a problem with the WLAN.  
  
Read tips on improving your internet connection here [here](/tuki-
netti#liikkuva).
