# How to turn off voicemail

If you do not want to use voicemail, you can disable it in the [My Telenor app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/). Select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and forwarding". Slide the toggle to the right so that "Never" is selected.

  * Can I turn off voicemail temporarily?

Of course you can.

### For mobile telephony

Log in to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx) and select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and forwarding". Then slide the toggle to the right so that "Never" is selected.
