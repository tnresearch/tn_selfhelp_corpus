# Login Support

You can find your services by logging in to the dna.fi website or the Oma DNA app
with [Mobile Verification](https://www.dna.fi/liittymat-ja-
palvelut/turvapalvelut/mobiilivarmenne) or online banking credentials. You can download the Oma DNA
app directly to your device from the app store.

### When logged in, you can:

View and pay your bills, update your customer information, and modify
billing settings

Find your PUK code, update your subscription, and change channels in your Mix channel package

Easily identify yourself and take advantage of affordable DNA Customer Benefits prices

## Frequently Asked Questions

How do you log in to DNA services?

You log in to the online store, the DNA.fi website, and the Oma DNA app using
strong authentication, i.e., online banking credentials or
[Mobile Verification](https://www.dna.fi/mobiilivarmenne?queryID=9afa841a1e79052bceac97766a3369da&userToken=8d53d48a8e451322).
In the future, when using the Oma DNA app, you can log in with a PIN code you create yourself.

In some situations, you need a DNA ID to use the service. You create it
for your use when ordering the service (e.g., DNA TV and DNA Digiturva).

How do I log in as a business customer?

DNA's business customers also authenticate with online banking credentials or
Mobile Verification when using the Oma DNA app or other
consumer channels. In other services targeted at businesses, such as
YritysDNA self-service or DNA Exchange Service, business customers have
a separate ID.

Why do I see a Telia page when I log in with Mobile Verification?

We use the Signicat intermediary service for all authentication methods. Signicat
uses Telia authentication when using mobile verification. This is part of
the authentication process and after authentication you will return to the DNA pages.

How is my data processed?

Your personal data is stored by DNA, for example, when concluding a contract, registering a service, or logging in. See
[privacy](https://corporate.dna.fi/tietosuoja#dna-oyjn-asiakasrekisterin-
rekisteriseloste).

## What do I need a DNA ID for?

You use the DNA ID to log in to the DNA TV service and security services. The ID
is created for your use when ordering the services.

The username for the DNA ID is the email address you provided when ordering the service.

### Frequently Asked Questions about DNA ID

Is the DNA ID the same for all the services I use?

If you have both DNA Digiturva and DNA TV and provided the same email address when ordering both
services, you can manage the services with the same DNA ID. They will also have the same password.

If you provided different email addresses when ordering the DNA Digiturva and DNA TV services, you will then have two DNA IDs, whose
username is the email address you provided.

How can I change the DNA ID password?

You can renew the DNA ID password by entering the ID's email address
[here](https://sso.dna.fi/password/reset?locale=fi). The password will change
to all services ordered with the same email address.

Can the DNA ID password be changed in the Oma DNA app?

If the DNA ID was created before 4.10.2023, you can change the DNA ID password
under your information in the Oma DNA app or by
[logging in](https://www.dna.fi/kirjaudu) to the dna.fi website.
