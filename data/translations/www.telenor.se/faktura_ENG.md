  * Why does the invoice have a different amount than I expected?

The invoice amount can change from month to month, and it can be due to many
different things, but the most common reason is that you have **purchased**
something or **changed** something in your subscription. Or simply purchased a new subscription, as the **first invoice is often higher** than the following ones.  
  
[Read all about unexpected or high invoice amounts
here](/link/dd9a19a5155c4628a519809bd0ec86fa.aspx)

  * How do I view my invoice?

Log in to [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) to
view new and old invoices.  
  
In My Telenor you will also find

    * Payment status
    * When the next invoice will be sent
    * How to pay (e.g. with direct debit or e-invoice)
My Telenor is available both [here on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx), and as an [app
](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx) for those of you who have a mobile subscription
with us.

####  
Can't see your invoices in My Telenor?

This is probably because you have not verified yourself as the owner of your
subscription.

#### Do you have e-invoice?

Then you can also see your invoices in your online bank.

  * How do I see if my invoice has been paid?

To find out if we have received payment of your invoice, [log in to My
Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx). **The information
you see there is the same as we have access to.** We cannot therefore see if
the invoice has been paid before you can see it yourself.  
  
In My Telenor you will see your invoices divided according to whether they are **unpaid**
or **paid**. Those of you with a mobile subscription can also see your upcoming
costs.

My Telenor is also available as an [app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx)
for those of you who have a mobile subscription.

[If the invoice appears as unpaid or overdue despite you having paid, you can
read more here.](/link/b8dfe46d8f0644b1bcb95fdfe8dbe1da.aspx)

  * Why does the invoice appear as unpaid or overdue when I have paid?

If your invoice appears as unpaid or overdue despite you having paid, check this:

    * **Have you just paid?**  
It may take a business day for the payment to be registered in our systems.

    * **Has the money been deducted from the account?**  
If the invoice still appears as unpaid after a few days, log in to
your bank and check if the money has been deducted from the account or that the e-invoice is
approved.

    * **Correct payment details?  
** Double-check that you have entered the correct OCR number, bank or postal giro.
The payment details can be found in My Telenor.

    * **Do you have e-invoice?**  
Don't forget that you must approve the payment in your online bank for it to
be completed.

    * **What is the due date?  
** Perhaps the unpaid invoice is a new invoice that is not yet due?
