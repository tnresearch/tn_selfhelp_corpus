# Connect your Telenor router

Almost there! Follow the steps below to connect our great router.

  * ### Connect the network cable

Plug the network cable into the red port on the router.  
  
[More help connecting correctly](/support/portar/)

  * ### Connect the router to the service distributor

Plug the other end of the network cable into the port (Port 1 or LAN 1)
which is usually located furthest to the left on the service distributor.

  * ### Connect power

Plug the power adapter into the power outlet and into the power input on the router.

  * ### Turn on the router

The button is located on the back of the router.

  * ### Now you can connect to the network! 

Select your wireless network in your mobile phone or computer settings. The name of
the network and the password **are on the bottom of the router**.

More help to get started with your wifi:

[Wifi settings for Windows](/support/fast-bredband/wifi/kom-igang/anslut-
till-wifi-via-windows-10/)  
[Wifi settings for macOS](/support/fast-bredband/wifi/kom-igang/anslut-
till-wifi-via-macos/)  
[Wifi settings for Android](/support/fast-bredband/wifi/kom-igang/anslut-
till-wifi-via-android/)  
[Wifi settings for iOS (iPhone and iPad)](/support/fast-
bredband/wifi/kom-igang/anslut-till-wifi-via-iphone-och-ipad/)

If you don't want wifi, connect your computer to the router via a
network cable.
