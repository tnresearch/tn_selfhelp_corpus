# Complaints, right of withdrawal and open purchase

Have you changed your mind or did the product you bought not work as well as you thought? Here you will find help to become satisfied again!

## Right of withdrawal - for those who have shopped remotely

  
If you purchased your product remotely, for example online, in customer service or at event sales (in a location outside a store), you have a 14-day right of withdrawal. This also applies to door-to-door sales.

  * You exercise your right of withdrawal by contacting us by phone, call [020-222 222](tel:020222222) and we will help you.
  * We will pay any shipping costs.
  * Keep in mind that you may be liable for compensation if you have used the product. We therefore ask you to handle ordered products carefully to avoid the risk of being liable for compensation to us if you want to exercise your right of withdrawal. If the product has been used, you may have to pay for the depreciation that has occurred. You can easily avoid this by not using the product until you have decided that you want to keep it.
  * You are also liable for compensation if you have used the ordered service.
  * If you have ordered [a phone upgrade](/handla/tjanster/mobil/change/) via the website, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal on the booking, but once the purchase has been completed in the store, and you have received your new phone, the Telenor store's terms and conditions apply.

The right of withdrawal never applies if your service has been completed and you have expressly consented to the service being started. Exchanges through Telenor Change and Trade-in cannot be withdrawn.

Right of withdrawal and open purchase are not the same thing, read below what applies to open purchase.

## Open purchase - if you have shopped in a Telenor store

  
You always have a 30-day open purchase on accessories and phones (which are **not** purchased on installment) when you shop in our own stores. Please note that the product must be unused in its unopened packaging. Open purchase does not apply to phones purchased through Telenor Change and Trade-in.

When purchasing a mobile subscription and mobile phone/tablet, the purchase may be eligible for an open purchase within 14 days, even with broken packaging, see your agreement for more info.

Smart!

## Test our mobile subscriptions for 14 days with open purchase

  
As a new mobile customer with Telenor, you have the opportunity to **test subscriptions and coverage for 14 days,** before you decide to use us. Try it for 14 days, if you are satisfied just continue. If you are not satisfied, you can return the subscription together with the mobile or tablet you purchased. Of course, you can use the mobile or tablet during the 14 days to test us properly. You only pay for your usage, such as part of the monthly fee, international calls and paid services. Shipping is of course free.

### Open purchase applies:

  * Test the mobile subscription and mobile/tablet for 14 days from the date of sale or when the goods arrived at the collection point.
  * You can use any hardware you have purchased.
  * Hardware that is withdrawn must be in new condition and returned in the original packaging together with all included accessories.

### Open purchase does not apply:

  * If you have purchased a mobile subscription from an external retailer.
  * If you switch to a different subscription than the one you have today.
  * On prepaid cards.
  * If you only want to return the hardware and not the mobile subscription.
  * On accessories where the packaging is broken.
  * If insurance has been used or the hardware has been serviced
  * When exchanging mobiles through Telenor Change and Trade-in

## Complaints

You should feel safe with your purchase from us. So if you experience that we have not fulfilled a promise to you, or that we have delivered a product that does not meet your expectations, then we want to know about it. Call us on [020-222 222](tel:020222222) ([+46 (0)708 222 222 ](tel:46708222222) if you are calling from abroad).  
  
Do you want help from an impartial instance instead? Then there are two to choose from.

  * [Telekområdgivarna](https://telekomradgivarna.se/) provides free help for consumers regarding subscriptions within TV, telephony and broadband
  * [Online Dispute Resolution](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/odr/main/index.cfm?event=main.home.show&lng=SV) is a service from the EU that is intended to help dissatisfied customers

If we have made a decision that you are not satisfied with, you can contact the General Complaints Board (ARN) to have the matter tried. ARN is a government agency whose main task is to impartially - and without charge to the parties - try consumer disputes. Read more at [www.arn.se](http://www.arn.se)

  * How does the satisfaction guarantee for broadband via fiber and broadband via 5G work?

We guarantee that you are satisfied with your broadband service. This means that you can test us for 30 days, and if you are not satisfied, you can terminate your broadband subscription without notice - even if you have a contract period.

The guarantee applies to customers who have fixed broadband or broadband via 5G.  

If you want to terminate the subscription, it must be done within 30 days of activating the broadband. If you use the guarantee, we only charge for the time you have used the service and any activation fee.

Please note that the satisfaction guarantee does not apply if you have obtained your broadband subscription from any of our [retailers](/link/e264503c0c3f4d03be419f3a89441ac2.aspx).
