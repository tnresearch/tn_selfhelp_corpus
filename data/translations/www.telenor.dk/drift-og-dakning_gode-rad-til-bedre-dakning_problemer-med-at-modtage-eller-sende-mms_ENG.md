### If you can't send MMS messages

  * Try restarting your mobile – when you restart your mobile, it will automatically fix any errors and search for new software updates. If a software update is available, we recommend that you download it.
  * Do you have data coverage on your phone? An internet connection is required to send and receive MMS messages. See our coverage map and check the coverage in your area.
  * Can you send an MMS to yourself? If you receive your own MMS, the problem likely lies in the recipient's mobile setup.
  * Has your mobile just been reactivated after being blocked? It may take some time before you can send and receive MMS messages again.

### **If you don't receive MMS messages**

  * Check that your mobile is set to be able to receive MMS messages.
  * You may have been out of data coverage when the MMS was sent to you. Check if you have coverage and ask the sender to try sending the MMS again.
  * Your mobile doesn't have enough storage space to receive an MMS. Delete any images, apps, or other content that is taking up space on your phone.
  * You have been abroad, and roaming was turned off on your mobile when the MMS was sent.

## Questions and answers about MMS problems

How do I send MMS to email?

When you want to send an MMS to email, just enter the email address instead of a phone number.

How large can an MMS message be?

In practice, most mobile phones will automatically adjust images/videos so that they get a reasonable size for MMS. However, the recipient's operator and mobile phone are crucial for how large messages you can send.

Note that Telenor supports MMS messages up to 2 MB; but the other operators in Denmark do not. If you send an MMS that cannot be received by another operator due to its size, you will receive an SMS with a message about it. Sender is: "MMS INFO." MMS messages that are rejected due to size will not be charged.

Receiving MMS messages

When an MMS is sent, it can be retrieved for up to 72 hours. If it is not received, both the sender and the recipient will be notified via SMS. The sender of the MMS message will receive an SMS with the text: "The MMS message you sent to them could not be delivered to the recipient." The recipient of the MMS message will receive an SMS with the text: "has unsuccessfully tried to send you an MMS message. Please check that your phone supports MMS and is set up correctly." Telenor no longer maintains "MMS Inbox", where you previously had access to view and retrieve MMS messages online.

How do MMS messages appear on my bill?

Regular MMS messages appear as "Mms - multimedia messages" and MMS messages sent abroad appear as "Mms to abroad".

Can I send and receive MMS abroad?

You can send and receive MMS in all the countries where we cooperate with telecom companies that support MMS.
