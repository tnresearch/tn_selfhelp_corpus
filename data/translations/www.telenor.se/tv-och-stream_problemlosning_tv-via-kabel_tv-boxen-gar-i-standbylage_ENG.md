# The TV box is going into standby mode

Is your TV box going into standby mode? Here you will find four things you can start doing. These instructions apply to the Humax CDIG-2000C/R TV box.

###

Remove automatic shutdown

If you are not using the remote control, for example changing channels or adjusting the volume, within three hours the TV box will automatically go into standby mode.

  
Here's how:

  1. Get the remote control
  2. Press **Home** and then **Settings**
  3. Press **System Settings** and then **Power Management**
  4. Press **Automatic Shutdown** and select **Off**

Turn off power saving mode

Here's how:

  1. Get the remote control
  2. Press **Home** and then **Settings**
  3. Press **System Settings** and then **Power Management**
  4. Press **Power Saving Mode** and select **Off**

Change the timer

Here's how:

  1. Get the remote control
  2. Press **Home** and then **Settings**
  3. Press **System Settings** and then **Timer**
  4. Set the timer

Turn off power management for the TV

Here's how:

  1. Get the remote control
  2. Press **Home** and then **Settings**
  3. Press **System Settings** and then **HDMI-CEC**
  4. Press **TV power management** and select **Off**
