# Set up Outlook on your mobile

Follow the guide below to set up Outlook on your mobile.

  * ## Tap "Email"

  * ## Configure your email

Enter your **email address** and **password**  
  
**

Tap " **Next** ".

  * ## Synchronization settings

Choose your synchronization settings here.

  * ## Choose synchronization period

Here you can choose how far back you want your mail to be synchronized.

  * ## Choose synchronization schedule

Here you can choose the interval at which your emails should be synchronized.

Then tap " **Next** ".

  * ## Enter a account name (optional)

You have the option to give your account a **Personal account name**.

Then tap " **Done** ".

  * ## Find your mail under "Email"

Your email is now set up and ready to use.

You will be able to find it under ”Email” in the program menu in the future.
