# DNA TV Entertainment Library Support

The DNA TV Entertainment Library will be removed from the DNA TV service on January 31, 2025.

Until January 31, 2025, movies from the DNA TV Entertainment Library could be watched on various
devices with a DNA TV subscription that includes a DNA entertainment box.

We have informed customers affected by the change by email or SMS
message in December 2024.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of programs did the DNA TV Entertainment Library include?

The DNA TV Entertainment Library included approximately 200 movies. The selection of movies
included successful films from previous years, critics' favorites, family films, romance, comedy, and action.
