# E-invoice

With e-invoice, you avoid the fee for paper invoices and have full control
over the payment. You apply for e-invoice in your online bank.

## How to get an e-invoice

Start by logging in to your online bank. To make it easier for you,

Just remember that you cannot see the e-invoice in the bank until we have sent it,
which happens once a month. You can see the date we send it in [My

Good to know about e-invoice

  * **Use your online bank**
It is at your bank that you choose whether you want to pay with e-invoice, we at Telenor
cannot help you with that. Remember that you must be connected to the bank's
service for internet payments.

  * **Do you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV with us?**
In that case, you have two customer numbers and will receive two invoices, which means that you
must apply for e-invoice for both, if that is what you want.

  * **It may happen that you receive a paper invoice anyway**
Depending on when you get an e-invoice, a paper invoice may be sent out before your consent to e-invoice has been handled by the bank.

## Frequently asked questions about e-invoice

  * How do I see that my application for autogiro/e-invoice has gone through?

In the My Telenor app, you can see how you pay your upcoming invoices. Click
on invoices, then on unpaid invoices and at the bottom you will see your payment method.

Keep in mind that if you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV from us, you have two customer numbers and will receive two invoices. This means that you must make an application for each customer number.

### Autogiro (Direct Debit)

When it comes to seeing if an autogiro application has gone through, it varies from
bank to bank. Most banks send a confirmation by post when your autogiro application has been approved. Contact your bank to find out what their
procedures look like and how long it takes.

### E-invoice

For e-invoice, you can see directly in your online bank that everything is okay. Remember
that you cannot see the actual e-invoice until we have sent it to your bank.
We do this once a month.

### If you receive a paper invoice anyway...

...it does not necessarily mean that something has gone wrong. Depending on when you
get autogiro or e-invoice, a paper invoice may be sent out before your consent to autogiro or e-invoice
is handled by the bank.

  * How do I get autogiro/e-invoice if I do not have BankID?

You can easily apply for autogiro or e-invoice via your online bank by
using your bank reader. The information needed to apply can be found on
your paper invoice from Telenor.

The same information can be obtained from customer service, but then you need to identify yourself. If you do not have BankID, you can identify yourself with a personal code that you order by
[contacting](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) our customer service. The code
is sent to your registered address.

  * Why can't I see my invoices in My Telenor?

If you cannot see your invoices in My Telenor, it is probably because you
have not verified yourself as the owner of your subscription in My Telenor.

[So you verify yourself as the owner](/link/0044777ae98a4461b3616bdf3cb5f4c5.aspx)

  * Can I change the payment date on my invoice?

It depends on whether you have e-invoice or autogiro.

**E-invoice**
If you have **e-invoice** and want to change the date the invoice is paid **you
do it in your online bank**. You do this at the same time as you approve that
the invoice should be paid. But remember that the invoice must be paid by the
due date.

**Autogiro**
If you have **autogiro**, you **cannot change the payment date** on your invoice.
