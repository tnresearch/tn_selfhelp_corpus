# Watch TV on your mobile or tablet

A TV that fits in your pocket? Yep, you probably already have one. Here's how to watch Telenor Stream on your mobile or tablet.

Getting Started

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (link opens in a new window).
  2. Download the Telenor Stream app from the [App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/se/app/telenor-stream/id1165060878?mt=8) if you have an iPhone or iPad, or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.bredbandsbolaget.stream) if you have a mobile or tablet with Android as the operating system.
  3. Start watching!

Good to know

  * It doesn't cost anything extra to create a streaming account with Telenor if you are already a TV customer with us. The only additional cost is if you rent a movie.
  * Do you have a Chromebook? Then check [via browser](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/dator/) instead of using an app.
  * System requirements:
    * Android 8.1 or later (To ensure that our copy protection works, unlocked, so-called "rooted" systems are not supported.)
    * iOS/iPadOS 15 or later (iOS 12 for older devices)
  * Are you having problems with the streaming? Check if you have the latest version of the app, otherwise update it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * There are missing channels when I log in to Telenor Stream – why?

If you can see some channels on your TV but not in Telenor Stream, it is because we have not been granted permission to distribute them there.

  * Why are entire seasons and episodes of some programs missing?

In Telenor Stream you will find both programs from play services (for example Viaplay Film & Series) and from channels (for example SVT). The programs from the channels can be viewed when they are broadcast, just like on "regular TV", and in many cases up to seven days afterwards. Then they disappear. But the programs from the play services can be viewed at any time, and that is the explanation why you can see all episodes and all seasons of a series from Viaplay, but only the latest episode of På spåret (a Swedish TV show).

  * I'm waiting for my TV box, can I use Telenor Stream in the meantime?

It depends. If the start date for your TV service has passed, you can activate Telenor Stream even if you haven't received the TV box. But if the start date is further in the future, you must wait until it has passed.

  * How do I change my login details for Telenor Stream?

You can change both your username and password for Telenor Stream in [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * Can I stream to multiple devices simultaneously?

Yes, you can stream on up to two devices simultaneously on your subscription.
