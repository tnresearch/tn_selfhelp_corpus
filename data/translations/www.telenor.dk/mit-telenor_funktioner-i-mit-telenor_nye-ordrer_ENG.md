# Track Your New Orders

If you have ordered a new phone, a different router, or a new SIM card, you can
in My Telenor track your orders under the "Orders" tab. Here you will find everything from
status, order number, installation date, and expected delivery date. If you
are awaiting a shipment from us, you can also find the track & trace number here.
