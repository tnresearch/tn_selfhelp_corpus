## Troubleshooting

The modem's indicator lights are off, even though the power cord is connected

**Possible cause:**  
The modem is not receiving power. The adapter or the modem itself may be faulty.

**Suggested solution:**  
Make sure the power cord is connected as instructed and that the outlet has power. If the problem persists, bring the adapter and modem to a DNA Store for replacement.  

All the internet connection indicator lights are on, but you cannot access the internet

**Possible cause:**  
The modem may be frozen.

**Suggested solution:**  
Disconnect the modem's power cord for about a minute. Reconnect it and restart the modem. Wait for the indicator lights to come on and check if the connection works. Also, try restarting your computer, or try connecting it to the modem with an Ethernet cable.

All the internet connection indicator lights are on, but the computer displays a message "Limited connection"

**Possible cause:**  
The modem is not connecting to the internet.

**Suggested solution:**  
Check the connection and make sure all connections are securely connected. Also, make sure the network settings of the computer or other device connecting to the internet are correct. If necessary, restore the modem to its factory settings, disconnect the power cord for about a minute, and then try restarting it.

The internet connection keeps dropping

The internet connection dropping can be caused by several different reasons. Try the following to determine if the cause is in the modem:

  * Connect the device to the modem with an Ethernet cable.
  * Restore the modem to its factory settings.
  * Check the condition of all cables, the antenna socket, and the outlet.
  * Check the settings of your computer and other devices.
  * Call DNA customer service and check the line values.
  * Also, make sure there is good air circulation around the modem.  

All the internet connection indicator lights are on when using a separate router

**Possible cause:**  
The router or other device sharing the connection may be frozen or faulty.

**Suggested solution:**  
Connect the modem to the computer directly with an Ethernet cable, or use wireless WiFi. Contact the router manufacturer or seller to find out about a possible fault.

WiFi is not working

If the internet works with an Ethernet cable, but not wirelessly, the problem is with the WiFi network.  
Restart the modem, check the settings, and try [WiFi tips](http://dna.fi/tuki-wifi). If the problem persists, contact customer service.  

Older devices do not connect via WiFi

  * Log in to the device's settings page and press the gear icon on the right side of the WiFi menu.
  * Change the Security drop-down menu from WPA2/WPA3 Personal to WPA2/WPA Personal.
  * Save the change by selecting Apply at the bottom of the page.
