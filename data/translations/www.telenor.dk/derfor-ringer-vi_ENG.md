## Call from Telenor

Phone calls are a good way to connect with customers and provide understanding
of what we can offer, as many consumers find the various products
in the telecom market complicated.

We only contact consumers who have actively consented to be
contacted by Telenor either through contact forms, Telenor competitions, or
a third-party competition where the competition terms specifically state
that contact consent is given to Telenor along with other companies.

## Happy Customers

At Telenor, we collect happy customers who appreciate the service we can
offer, and most of those we speak with appreciate the advice we can
provide over the phone, whether they are or become customers with us.

If you wish to withdraw your consent, you can do so here. Fill out the form
and click send – we will ensure that you are no longer called by
Telenor. Please note that there may be a processing time of up to one business day.

## What is the Robinson List?

The Robinson List can be found on borger.dk and, among other things, aims to protect
consumers from unsolicited telephone inquiries from companies that
are allowed to make these without consent. This may include, for example, insurance
and subscriptions to rescue services. These companies must therefore not
contact you if you are registered on it.

Telenor is not among these companies, and we only contact consumers who
have given their consent to be contacted via contact forms,
Telenor competitions, or a third-party competition.
