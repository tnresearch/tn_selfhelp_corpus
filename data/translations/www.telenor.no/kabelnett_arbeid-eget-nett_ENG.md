# Work or measures on your own network/own intake cable

Telenor has a nationwide agreement with OneCo and Eltel Networks who are
authorized to perform work in Telenor's cable network. This applies to e.g.:  

  * Move or remove your own intake cable

  * Changes to your own installation/inside your home  

The contractor will invoice the client directly. Please contact the contractor
in your county to carry out the work.
