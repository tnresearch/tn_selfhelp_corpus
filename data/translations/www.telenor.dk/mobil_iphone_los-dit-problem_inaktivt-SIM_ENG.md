# Inactive SIM Card or No Service

If your phone displays "no service" or "inactive SIM card", it hasn't registered your SIM.

**The reason may be:**

  * You are a new Telenor customer, and our system hasn't yet registered your SIM. Call customer service on 72 100 100, and we will help you activate it.
  * Your mobile is blocked because you may have missed a bill or exceeded your limit on [Usage Control](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/opsatning/forbrugskontrol/ "Usage Control")
  * You have a new mobile, and your old SIM is outdated
  * You haven't used your SIM for 6 months or longer
  * You have cancelled your mobile subscription, and your SIM has been deactivated

**If none of the above applies to you, you can try to activate your SIM by doing the following:**

  * If you have a physical SIM card, you can try turning off your mobile and removing the SIM card. Gently clean the SIM card with a dry and clean cloth. Reinsert the SIM card and turn on the mobile
  * Insert the SIM card into another mobile. If the SIM card works in the other mobile, it suggests that your mobile needs a software update
