# How to Restore Your Sony Xperia

If you are experiencing issues with your mobile phone and want to restore it to factory settings, you can see how to do it in the guide below.

**Please note that the following will be deleted:**

  * User data, settings, images and music
  * Data and settings for system and programs
  * Downloaded programs

To retain these settings and data, it is necessary to back up your mobile phone before restoring it.

[Back up your Sony Xperia](/kundeservice/mobil/sony-xperia/sikkerhed-og-backup/backup-og-synkronisering/ "Back up Sony Xperia")

  * ## Press the menu button on the home screen

  * ## Select "Settings"

  * ## Select "Backup & Reset"

  * ## Select "Reset settings"

  * ## Select "Reset phone"

  * ## Select "Reset settings"

The phone will then be reset to factory settings.
