# Which price plans can I choose between?

### Telenor Fastpris prepaid card

A prepaid card for those who want to have the freedom, within Sweden or while
travelling in the EU/EEA, to call and text at a fixed price. With Fastpris
calls, SMS and MMS are all free!

Choose between nine different data allowances, from 0.5 GB - 150 GB.

Top up amount/price:

  * 0.5 GB surf for SEK 49
  * 2 GB surf for SEK 99
  * 5 GB surf for SEK 149
  * 10 GB surf for SEK 199
  * 20 GB surf for SEK 249
  * 40 GB surf for SEK 299 + 500 minutes abroad
  * 100 GB surf for SEK 349 + 1 000 minutes abroad
  * 150 GB surf for SEK 449 + 1 500 minutes abroad

The above top-ups are valid for 31 days, apart from 0.5 GB which is valid for
7 days.

Calls, SMS and MMS within Sweden or when travelling within EU/EEA: Included

**Calls, SMS and MMS to and from abroad**

[Find out about our international prices here](/roaming/). Top-ups 40 GB, 100
GB and 150 GB include minutes to EU/EEA, UK and USA. To use Telenor Fastpris
abroad (only outside EU/EEA), you need to top up with [Extrasaldo (Extra
Balance International)](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-kontantkort/#products).  
  
 **Telenor Hello – better prices for calls to the Middle East and Europe**  
If you make a lot of calls to the Middle East or Europe, you should choose
[Telenor Hello](/handla/kontantkort/hello-kontantkort/).

### Telenor Rörligt prepaid card

Rörligt is ideal if you make fewer calls and text messages and want to control
your costs.

With Rörligt, calls are SEK 0.69/minute and SMS/MMS are SEK 0.69.

These prices apply to regular Swedish phone numbers and when travelling within
EU/EEA.

Top up amount/price:

  * SEK 100
  * SEK 400

**Calls within Sweden or when travelling within EU/EEA**

SEK 0.69/min (SEK 0.99 connection charge)

SMS/MMS within Sweden or when travelling within EU/EEA.

SEK 0.69 each

Calls, SMS and MMS to EU/EEA countries and countries outside EU/EEA.

Find out about our international [prices here](/roaming/).

**Mobile surf**

SEK 1 for 1 MB
