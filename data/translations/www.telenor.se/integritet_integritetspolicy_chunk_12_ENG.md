**Unsubscribing from direct marketing**  
If you do not want to receive marketing or do not want us to use your
data for this purpose, you can easily let us know by
contacting customer service or [our
data protection officer](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt1). You can
also unsubscribe from receiving direct marketing via certain
marketing channels by following the unsubscribe instructions in
respective email, SMS or personalized marketing on
public websites.

Your rights

Data protection legislation aims, among other things, to strengthen your right to and
protection of your personal data. You can read more about the rights below and
if you want to exercise any of them, please [contact our
data protection officer](/support/integritet/integritetspolicy/#kontakt2). The following
rights you can also exercise yourself on My Telenor:

  * [Request a register extract](/mitt-telenor/nya-min-profil/personlig-information/)
  * [Revoke consent](/mitt-telenor/nya-min-profil/)

If you have chosen to exercise any of your rights, we will get back to you as
soon as possible and no later than one month from when we received your request. If we
cannot answer your request or need more time, we will explain why.
