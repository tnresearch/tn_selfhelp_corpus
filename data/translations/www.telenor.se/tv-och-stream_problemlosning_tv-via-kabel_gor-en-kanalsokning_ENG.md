# Perform a Channel Scan

Missing channels may reappear if you perform a channel scan.

  
Here's how to perform a channel scan if you have our most common box, Humax 1000c.

  1. Click on Menu on the remote control
  2. Navigate to Settings and click OK
  3. Scroll down to Installation and click OK
  4. Enter the PIN code for your box. The default PIN code is 0000
  5. Select Search channels and then Autoscan
