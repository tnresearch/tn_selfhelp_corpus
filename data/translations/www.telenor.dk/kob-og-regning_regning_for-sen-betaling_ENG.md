# Late Payment

If your bill is not paid within the payment deadline, we will send a
reminder with a reminder fee of DKK 100.

**Blocking and reopening of phone**

If your payment is not received after the first reminder, we will block your phone. It costs
DKK 200 to have your phone reopened, and this happens automatically when the bill
is paid.

Do you disagree with a reminder you have received? Then write to
rykkerindsigelse@telenor.dk

**Collection Agency**

If we do not receive payment for your bill after we have sent you a second
reminder with a reminder fee of DKK 100, we will forward your outstanding amount to a collection agency.
Once you have been forwarded to a collection agency, Telenor can no longer help you with
information about your subscription; you must contact the collection agency. If you
do not respond to inquiries from the collection agency, you risk being registered in RKI
Kreditinformation A/S.

**Automatic Card Payment**

It doesn't cost anything, and your bill will be paid on time.
Register your bill for automatic payment. Then the payment runs completely by
itself.
[Register for automatic payment](/mit-telenor/betalingsmetode/ "Register
for automatic payment").

We automatically deduct your payment from your card. You will receive your bill
sent by email before the money is deducted from your card. You can register the following cards for automatic payment: Dankort, VISA, MasterCard, JCB and
Maestro.
