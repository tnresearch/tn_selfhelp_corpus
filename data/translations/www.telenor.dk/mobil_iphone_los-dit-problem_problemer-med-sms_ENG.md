# Problems Sending and Receiving SMS

If you're unable to send or receive text messages, you can often solve the problem yourself. We've compiled a list of the most typical troubleshooting steps.

  * If there's a red exclamation mark to the right of a text message, tap it and try sending the message again. If you have iMessage enabled, check if you're on a wireless network.
  * If you're using iMessage, try turning it off and on again.
  * Turn your phone completely off and then on again. When you turn your iPhone back on, it will automatically search for any software updates. If there's an update, we recommend downloading it.
  * If you've used almost all the storage space on your phone, you may experience problems receiving SMS messages. Check how much space you have and free up space by deleting content that takes up a lot of space - for example, apps or videos.
  * Have you accidentally blocked the sender? Check Settings > Messages > Blocked
  * Take your SIM card out and carefully insert it into another iPhone. If you can now send and receive SMS messages, there are likely problems with your iPhone.

## Have you got a new phone?

Even if you've replaced your iPhone with another type of smartphone, iMessage may still be enabled on your phone number. This means that other users with iOS devices will still use iMessage when sending messages to you.

If you no longer have your iPhone, you can turn off iMessage on the Apple deregistration page [here](https://selfsolve.apple.com/deregister-imessage/dk/da).
