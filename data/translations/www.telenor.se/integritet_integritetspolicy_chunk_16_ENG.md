##

  * Read more about camera surveillance

Telenor Sverige AB operates camera surveillance in certain locations (in our stores, in our offices and in certain places where we have technical equipment, e.g. data centers). In places where we have camera surveillance, there is clear signage indicating that camera surveillance is in effect. In connection with camera surveillance, we may process your personal data.

The purpose of the processing is to prevent, detect and investigate crime, increase security for staff, customers and visitors, and to protect our assets. We perform the processing based on the legal basis of legitimate interest (i.e. our interest in preventing, preventing and investigating crime, increasing security and protecting our assets). Recordings are saved for up to two months after they have been recorded. In the event of a dispute or investigation, recordings may be saved for as long as the dispute or investigation is ongoing. In the event of suspected crime, real-time monitoring may occur.

Information for consultants, suppliers and others visiting Telenor's premises

When you visit Telenor's offices or technical sites, we register certain information about you in our systems.
