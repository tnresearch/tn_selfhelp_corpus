# Troubleshooting HDCP and HDMI OFF Issues

If you are seeing “HDCP” or “HDMI OFF” displayed on your T-We Box, you can follow these steps to resolve the issue.

If one step doesn't solve the problem, proceed to the next step.

## 1. Turn Equipment Off and On

  1. Turn off the set-top box by unplugging it from the power outlet.

  2. Turn off and unplug the TV and any other connected equipment

    * E.g. soundbar, home theater system etc.

  3. Plug the set-top box and the rest of the equipment back in after approximately 30 seconds.

  4. Turn on the TV and any other connected equipment.

  5. Turn on the set-top box.

## 2. Check Source/Input

Make sure your TV and other connected equipment are set to the correct source/input:

  * Check which HDMI port the HDMI cable is connected to on the TV. (e.g. HDMI 1)

  * Press the source button on the TV remote control.

  * Change the source to the one the T-We box is connected to. (e.g. HDMI 1)

Source selection on the remote control may vary from TV manufacturer to TV manufacturer.
The button usually looks like one of these.

## 3. Change Picture Resolution on the Set-Top Box

Select your T-We box to see how to change the resolution using the remote control.

  * #### T-We Box II (ADB 3770)

    1. Press the “Yellow” button on the remote control (do not hold the button down). The current resolution will be displayed on the set-top box.

    2. Press the keys “2” to “6” to select the resolution. This must be done immediately after pressing the “Yellow” button. Choose a resolution that your TV supports. If you are unsure what your TV supports, you can start with the lowest resolution (2) and work your way up.

      *  **2 = 576p (720 x 576)**

      *  **3 = 720p (1280 x 720)**

      *  **4 = 1080i (1920 x 1080)**

      *  **5 = 1080p (1920 x 1080)**

      *  **6 = 2160p / 4K (3840 x 2160)**

  * #### T-We Box (ADB 5743)

    1. Press the “Yellow” button on the remote control (do not hold the button down). The current resolution will be displayed on the set-top box.

    2. Press the keys “1” to “5” to select the resolution. This must be done immediately after pressing the “Yellow” button. Choose a resolution that your TV supports. If you are unsure what your TV supports, you can start with the lowest resolution (1) and work your way up.

      *  **1 = 576i (720 x 576)**

      *  **2 = 576p (720 x 576)**

      *  **3 = 720p (1280 x 720)**

      *  **4 = 1080i (1920 x 1080)**

      *  **5 = 1080p (1920 x 1080)**

    3. Alternatively, you can open the cover on the front of the set-top box and use the resolution button to change the picture resolution.

## 4. Connect Set-Top Box Directly to TV

  *  **Remove other connected equipment** and connect the set-top box directly to the TV with the HDMI cable.

  * Repeat the previous steps if this does not solve the problem immediately.

  * If you have already connected the set-top box directly to the TV, proceed to the next step.

## 7. Reset Set-Top Box to Factory Settings

  * #### T-We Box II (ADB 3770)

    1. Locate the RESET button on the back of the T-We Box II. It is marked with a red ring and is located between the HDMI and USB ports.

    2. Turn off the T-We Box II using the on/off button on the back of the set-top box.

    3. Hold down RESET (e.g. with a paperclip) while turning on the set-top box with the on/off button.

    4. Continue holding RESET until you see four dashes on the display (this takes about 15-20 seconds).

    5. The set-top box is now reset and you can release RESET.

  * #### T-We Box (ADB 5743)

    1. Open the cover on the front of the set-top box.

    2. Hold down the gear + resolution button + Down arrow simultaneously for 6-10 seconds (see images below).

    3. When the display is off, the reset is complete.

 **Note:** Do not hold for longer than 10 seconds - This will cause the set-top box to start a software upgrade (this can take a long time).

    * To cancel the software upgrade, repeat the method above.

**If none of this helps, your TV may be too old. The TV must support at least HDCP v1.2.  
** We recommend that you connect the set-top box to another newer TV to check if this solves the problem.

**Note that TVs, soundbars/home theater systems, and cables can be affected by thunderstorms and power outages and thus cause this problem.**

## Specifically for 4K Resolution on T-We Box II

T-We Box II supports 4K, which means that 4K is automatically selected if the set-top box is installed with a TV that has the same support.

If you have a TV with 4K support, you may experience the HDCP error message if you switch HDMI inputs on the TV, or switch to a TV that does not support 4K.

  * #### This is what you should check to use 4K resolution on T-We Box II

    * The TV supports 4K Ultra HD, HDMI 2.0 and HDCP 2.2

    * Connected devices between the TV and T-We Box II (e.g. soundbar or surround sound system/home theater) support HDMI 2.0 and HDCP 2.2

    * The HDMI input the cable is connected to on the TV supports HDMI 2.0 and HDCP 2.2

    * The HDMI cable you are using meets the requirements for 4K Ultra HD

Specifications can usually be found with the supplier of the equipment.
