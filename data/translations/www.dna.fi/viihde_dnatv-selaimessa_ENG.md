# DNA TV in the browser

You can use the DNA TV application in the Chrome browser on your computer at
[dnatv.dna.fi](https://dnatv.dna.fi/)

## Getting Started

**Enable the service the first time like this:**

Open DNA TV in the Chrome browser on your computer at
[dnatv.dna.fi](https://dnatv.dna.fi).

Open the application and select **Log in**.

Choose from two different login options:

  * Log in with DNA Credentials, using an email + password combination.  
  

  * With a quick link, i.e. enter the email address you provided when placing the order, and you will receive a message to your email. Follow the instructions in the message and continue to the application by clicking the link.   

After logging in, DNA TV is available on your smartphone, tablet and
computer at [dnatv.dna.fi](http://dnatv.dna.fi). The service is
available on five different devices simultaneously. You can also mirror
programs to a large screen via Chromecast or Apple TV.

## Usage and Features

### Programs View

In the Programs view, scroll down to see the entire range of the service in
one view and current recommendations. Basic channel live broadcasts,
watching programs afterwards and using the DNA TV Rental are included in
all DNA TV subscriptions.

Paid channels, streaming services and recording are separately subscribable
features and are displayed in the user interface according to your subscription. You can also access, for example, the Rental via the top navigation and to programs and movies you have marked as favorites via the Favorites view.

Channels Now

From the Channels Now view, you can watch ongoing live broadcasts. You can also find TV channels through the program guide.

Next Up View

The Next Up view is a condensed program guide. It is easy to see what
programs are coming up on the channel.

Continue Watching

Continue Watching appears if you have interrupted watching a program or movie.
You can easily continue watching from where you left off.

Latest Recordings

Latest Recordings is visible if your subscription includes recording and you
have recorded programs.

Watch Later

From the Watch Later view, you can access programs from specific channels
that are available for later viewing through the logos. For example, if you missed your favorite program from the previous
day, you can easily watch the program through this. Some channels' programs are available for 2-7 days after the broadcast and the remaining viewing time for each program is displayed. The view only shows channels that are included in your subscription and that offer this feature, the feature is not available on all channels.

Categories

Through the Categories (Movies, Series, Sports, Children, Entertainment, News &
Documents) you can easily find your favorite programs, which you can watch right away
or put on record, if your subscription includes recording. The view
presents programs only from channels that are included in your subscription. By pressing and holding on the program image, you can go directly to the program's program page. From the program page you can, for example, mark the program as a favorite, put it on record or remove the recording.

Rental Movies

DNA TV Rental presents new movies for you. By pressing "Show more", you can explore the entire selection of the Rental. Rented
movies are available for 48 hours and are billed on your DNA invoice. Renting is done with a DNA PIN code. You can find more information about the PIN code here

Streaming Services

At the bottom of the homepage there are recommendations for content from streaming services that are included in your subscription.

## Recordings

The Recordings section is only visible if the recording feature is included in your subscription. You can
view or delete recordings by managing recording rules. Recording programs is possible from the program details in the Programs or Categories view as well as from the TV & Program Guide view.

  * Storage space is unlimited. You can record simultaneously from all basic channels.
  * You can also start recording mid-program and watch whenever you want. With series recording, you can record all programs of an entire production season at once, with a single button press.
  * In the Recordings view, you can watch your recorded programs and search for them by category. Press on the desired program and select the pink play button in the next view. After the program starts, you will get the image on the full screen when you select the enlarge icon. You can also adjust the volume and make audio and subtitle selections via the icons.
  * In the Recordings view, you can delete recordings by pressing the three dots on the right side of the recording information. The Delete command removes the recording immediately.
  * You can view your own recording rules in "Recording Rules". You can find them in the upper right corner of the Recordings view. There you will find, for example, series that are being recorded. 

## TV and Program Guide

Through the program guide, you can watch programs currently being broadcast on channels included in your subscription. Channels available for viewing are marked with a pink play icon next to the channel logo. Select **Watch** next to the desired channel and program.

  * You can record an ongoing program by first selecting **Watch**. In the Watch view, there is a rec button in the upper right corner. Select **Rec** and the program will be recorded from the beginning.
  * By pressing the program name in the program guide, you can see additional information and a description of the program and put the program on record. If it is a series, you can record all episodes of the series by selecting **Record episodes**.
  * Through the program's additional information, you can go forward to the program page, where you can see, for example, more detailed information about the program as well as seasons and their episodes.

## Rental

You can easily rent movies and series immediately after their theatrical release from the DNA TV Rental. You need a personal four-digit DNA TV PIN code for renting, which you received when placing the order. You can also find it in My Services and the Oma DNA app.

See more detailed instructions for the DNA TV Rental.
