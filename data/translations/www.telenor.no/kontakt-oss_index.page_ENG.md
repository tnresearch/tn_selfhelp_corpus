  * #### ID check with Vipps

We have recently started using Vipps in our voice menu, so that it is safer and more
efficient to call customer service.

When you call us, you will be given a choice in the menu to receive an
identification request. When you accept this, you will receive a notification from Vipps
on your mobile phone. Tap on this notification, and you will be taken into Vipps to
identify yourself. Once this is done, you will be put through to customer service on
the phone. **You must have Vipps linked to the mobile number you are calling from.**

NOTE: If you have turned off notifications from Vipps, you must open Vipps manually to
complete the identification.
