# Surf now

Here you'll find help getting started with your broadband. Whether you've just unpacked the router from the box, or you're already partway there and wondering why it's not working.

## What do you need help with?

[ ](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/kom-igang-med-ditt-bredband/)

Installing the broadband

Here we show you, **step by step, how to install your broadband.** It takes a maximum of fifteen minutes, then you can surf.

[To the guide](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/kom-igang-med-ditt-bredband/)

[ ](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/dags-att-felsoka/)

Understanding why it's not working

You have **connected everything but can't surf**, even though it should work.

We'll help you figure out what's wrong and solve the problem.

[Troubleshooting](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/dags-att-felsoka/)

Digihjälpen. If you'd rather do something else.

Get help at home with the installation of your broadband, wifi bridge, TV box, Apple TV – or whatever you need to be able to live in a smart home.
