## Changes to e-Invoice Agreements

Almost 2 years ago, the banks introduced changes to how e-Invoices are ordered and sent - what we call Yes Thank You to All or Always e-Invoice. Until now, the old e-Invoice agreements that were created before the change have continued to function as normal, but from March 1, 2024, the banks will discontinue this variant.

[Private Customer](https://telenor.no/kundeservice/faktura/avtalegiro-og-
efaktura/endringer/#privat)[Business Customer](https://telenor.no/kundeservice/faktura/avtalegiro-
og-efaktura/endringer/#bedrift)

## Private Customer

## What does this mean for me?

 **If you do not make changes to your e-Invoice agreement, you will receive paper invoices from March.**

There may be several reasons why you have received a notification about this, but usually it is one of these reasons:

  * You have not activated Yes Thank You to All/Always e-Invoice in your online bank

  * The person receiving your invoice is someone else (e.g. spouse or child)

### What do I need to do to continue receiving e-Invoices?

Log in to your online bank and accept Yes Thank You to All/Always e-Invoice. If you need help with this, you can contact your bank.

If someone else is paying your invoice today, that person must be set as the invoice recipient with us. That person must also accept Yes Thank You to All/Always e-Invoice in their online bank. To change the invoice recipient, [call us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) - we will help you.

## Business Customer

## What does this mean for me?

 **If you do not make changes to your e-Invoice agreement, you will receive paper invoices from March.**

For businesses, this is usually because a private individual has created the e-Invoice agreement for the business. With the old variant of e-Invoice, this was possible, but Yes Thank You to All/Always e-Invoice can only be ordered if the invoice recipient is a private individual.

### What do I need to do to continue receiving e-Invoices?

If the private individual is still to receive and pay the invoice, that person must be set as the invoice recipient with us/on the account. If you want to do this, you must [contact us at Customer Service](/bedrift/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) - we will fix it!

 **If the invoice is to be paid by the company, you have two options:**

  * [Order ePostInvoice](/bedrift/kundeservice/epostfaktura/bestilling.page) - if you already have an ePostInvoice agreement with us, the affected accounts will be transferred to the same agreement when the e-Invoice is removed.

  * Register the company in [Elma ](https://samarbeid.digdir.no/elma/elma/24) and receive EHF invoices
