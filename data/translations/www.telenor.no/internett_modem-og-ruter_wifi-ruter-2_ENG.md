## About WiFi Router II

[Our new WiFi Router II](/privat/internett/wifi-utstyr/) is very powerful and
has the latest WiFi technology (WiFi 6E) in place to cover all your
network needs. It is easy to set up and provides super coverage and speed in
your home.

It works just as well with all our internet accesses, whether you have
broadband over fiber, cable or Wireless Broadband.

With the latest security encryption WPA3 and Smart WiFi, the router ensures that
you get a stable and secure internet connection. Smart WiFi is a feature that
automatically selects the best network for you and your many devices, whether
this is over 2.4GHz, 5GHz or 6GHz.

If you have a large home or difficult coverage conditions, you can expand with one
or more WiFi extenders to a mesh network, which provides good coverage throughout
the home. [Read more about WiFi Extender II...](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-
ruter/wifi-forsterker-2/)

## Details about WiFi Router II

  * #### What do the lights on WiFi Router II mean?

 **Bottom Light:**

    * Solid white color = Connected to power

    * Blinking white = Starting up

    * Blinking green = WPS in progress

* * *

 **Top Light:**

    * Solid green = Connected

    * Blinking yellow = Fetching IP address

    * Blinking red = Connected, but waiting for configuration

    * Dark = Not connected

* * *

    * Both lights blinking yellow = Software update in progress

  * #### Forgotten WiFi password?

Send SMS with keyword **WIFI XXXXXXXX to 2000** to receive
network name and password for the router.

You can find the serial number and instructions on how to send SMS on
the sticker on the underside of the router.

  * #### Router details

 **Dimensions:**  
Height: 230 mm. Width: 165 mm. Depth: 69 mm.

 **WiFi 6E standard:**  
Tri-band with three radios, 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz with 2x2/4x4/3x3 internal
antenna configuration  
Backward compatible with older standards (802.11a/b/g/ac)  
7.8 Gbps WiFi bandwidth (AX7800)

 **Standard encryption:  
** WPA2- and WPA3-encryption

 **Ethernet ports:**  
One 2.5 Gbps WAN port, one 2.5 Gbps LAN port and 3 pcs. 1 Gbps LAN port

## Change settings on your router?

Do you want to change the settings on your router, such as changing network name
or password? You can do this by logging in to My WiFi, at
<https://wifi.telenor.no/>.

To log in, you must be connected to your home network, and you log in
with the admin password that you find on the sticker under your WiFi Router II.
