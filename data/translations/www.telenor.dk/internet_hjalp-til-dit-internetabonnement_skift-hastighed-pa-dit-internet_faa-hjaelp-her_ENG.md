# How to Change Your Internet Speed

If you want to change the speed of your internet subscription, you can do so by
changing your subscription on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/).

When you change your subscription, your new speed will be activated in most cases
after 24 hours. In very few cases, a change in speed requires us to send a technician.
This means it may take up to 14 days before you can notice the difference and enjoy
your new subscription.

Generally, it is free to move your internet. In rare cases, there may be a cost if a
technician visit is necessary. The price of a technician visit depends on the provider,
but you will always be informed of the price in advance.

You will receive a confirmation of the speed change - and in this you will also be
informed if a technician visit is needed.
