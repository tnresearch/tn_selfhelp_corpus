## Writing and Sending Email

  1. Click the **Create a new message** icon (pencil button)

  2. In the **New Message** screen, you need to fill in the following:

    *  **To:** Recipient's email address

    *  **Subject:** What the email is about

  3. In the white field at the bottom, enter the actual content of the email

  4. If you want to add attachments, click the **Attachment** icon (binder in the upper left) and search for the file to add a file

  5. If you have created a signature, you can add it by clicking the **Signatures** dropdown menu and selecting the signature you want to add

  6. Click **Send** to send the email
