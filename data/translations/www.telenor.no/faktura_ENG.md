## Is the invoice not as expected?

The first thing you should check is the date/period the invoice applies to:

  * If this is the first invoice you receive from us, or an invoice after you have made changes to your subscription - Then the invoice will apply to a longer period than usual

It is also smart to check usage:

  * Have you e.g. called special numbers, been abroad or used the phone to pay for content services - This will affect the amount on your invoice

### Postpone Invoice

You can postpone the invoice on My Pages, in My Telenor or via customer service.

Postponement costs NOK 70 if you do it yourself, or NOK 140 if customer service arranges it for you.

### eInvoice and Direct Debit

With eInvoice, the invoice comes directly into your online bank instead of as a paper invoice in the mail, and with Direct Debit it is paid automatically on the due date.

Easy for you and smart for the environment!

## Collection and closed subscription

If you do not pay your invoice by the due date, you will receive a reminder.

If you do not pay the reminder within the deadline, the invoice may go to collection. When the invoice goes to collection, the collection agency takes over the claim from Telenor, and you must pay the bill to them.

If the invoice remains unpaid for too long, your subscription may be closed. How long it takes varies for mobile, internet and TV.

 **[Read more about how you can get your subscription opened](/kundeservice/faktura/for-sen-betaling/)**

## Questions and answers about the invoice

  * #### Disagree with the invoice?

If you disagree with the amount you are charged, we ask you to first check our invoice explanations.

If you still believe that you have been billed incorrectly, you can call customer service on 915 09 000. It may be helpful to have the invoice in front of you when you call.

A complaint about the invoice must always be directed to us before the invoice is due. When you contact us, the payment deadline will be extended until the case has been resolved.

NB: Note that the first invoice you receive will appear higher than expected. This happens because you pay the fixed monthly price in advance.

  * #### How does payment of the invoice work after termination?

When a subscription is terminated, all outstanding invoices must be paid as they are. If you have paid in advance, beyond the notice period, you will receive this back when the final settlement is produced approx. 3 weeks after the subscription is disconnected.

If you still have a subscription that is on the same invoice as the subscription you have cancelled, the credit amount will be deducted on the next regular invoice.

  * #### What is crediting and money to good?

If you have received a credit/credit note from Telenor, it may mean that you have money to good with us.

The money you have to good will be deducted on your next invoice or refunded to the account number it was last paid from. If you want to have the money paid out to another account number, call customer service on 915 09 000.

  * #### Can I change the fixed due date on my invoices?

No, unfortunately it is not possible to change the fixed due date on future invoices.

  * #### What is a Specified Invoice?

Mobile and telephone customers have the option to receive a specified invoice.

A specified invoice shows the telephone numbers that have been called or sent SMS/MMS to, with time, duration, cost and downloaded data volume (applies to mobile). A regular invoice only shows the distribution of consumption.

You can choose that the following calls should be specified:

    1. All calls

    2. All calls except internet dial-up calls

    3. All calls except internet dial-up calls and Norwegian calls

    4. Only calls to mobile phones and abroad

 **How do I get a specified invoice?**  
** All our private customers can get a specified invoice. You activate it yourself on [My Pages](/privat/minesider/logginn), under each subscription. Price for specified invoice is NOK 29,- per month.

If you use eInvoice, the details will be shown on the invoice in your online bank.

When you order a specified invoice, only future invoices will be specified.

  * #### Where can I find the customer number or subscription number?

This can be found at the top right on the front page of your invoice, along with the due date, invoice number and similar information.

  * #### Can I get a copy of the invoice on paper?

Yes, you can find and print your invoice in PDF format from [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/faktura/) at no extra cost.

If you cannot print a copy yourself, you can have a copy sent by post. Invoice copy then costs NOK 100 per invoice.  
[Contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/), and we will fix the rest.
