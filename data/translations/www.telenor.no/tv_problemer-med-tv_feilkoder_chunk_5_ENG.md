#### E0325, E0425 - No Channels Found

This error code appears if the set-top box cannot acquire channels during a channel search. A channel search is only necessary during initial installation.

Try again, and check that the antenna cable/network cable is securely connected at both ends.

If you are unsure about the correct cabling, you can select your equipment and [see the connection guide here.](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/indexer.page)

?

#### E0326 - No Signal Found

The TV box is not receiving a signal. Check that all cables are securely connected to both the set-top box and the wall outlet. If you have broadband over fiber, check if you can access the internet on your PC or mobile phone.

Restart the TV box. If the problem persists, [contact us on customer service.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

?

#### E0335 - Poor Signal

This error code may appear if you lack rights to one or more channels, or if you are missing audio and video on all channels.

  * If it concerns individual channels, you can follow the [guide for Missing Channel or Content](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/)

  * If it concerns all channels, you can follow the [guide for Missing TV Signal](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/)

?

#### E0350 - Missing TV Signal

The error code may be due to a weak or missing TV signal. To resolve the problem, you can follow the [guide for Missing TV Signal.](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/)

?

#### E0420 - E0424 - No Signal Found

The channel search failed due to a missing signal. The most common reasons are that the antenna cable and/or network cable is not connected, is loose, or is incorrect.

If you are unsure if the cables are connected correctly, you can select your equipment and [see the connection guide for the set-top box.](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/indexer.page)

If you are still experiencing problems, you can follow the [guide for Missing TV Signal.](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/)

?

#### E0425 - No Signal

The TV does not find a signal from the set-top box. Check that the HDMI cable between the set-top box and the TV is securely connected, and that you have selected the correct source.

If you are unsure how to select the correct source channel, you can [see the guide for Missing TV Signal.](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/)

?

#### E0327 - HDCP/HDMI OFF/Copy Protection

If you experience the message "HDCP" or "HDMI OFF" on the display of your T-We Box, you can go through these steps to resolve the problem.

###  
Turn the equipment off and on

  1. Turn off the set-top box by unplugging the power.

  2. Turn off the TV and other connected equipment

    * E.g. soundbar, home theater etc.

  3. Plug the power back into the set-top box after about 30 seconds.

  4. Turn on the TV and other connected equipment

  5. Turn on the set-top box  

 **If this does not help** , troubleshoot further from step 2 on this page: [Troubleshoot HDCP/HDMI OFF](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/hdcp-hdmi-off/)

#### E9040

 **Do the following:**

  1. Turn off the antenna power

  2. Wait 10 seconds, then reconnect the power

  3. After the antenna is reconnected, restart the T-We box by unplugging the power cord or using the on/off switch. Wait 10 seconds before plugging the power back in.

If the problem persists, [contact Customer Service.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
