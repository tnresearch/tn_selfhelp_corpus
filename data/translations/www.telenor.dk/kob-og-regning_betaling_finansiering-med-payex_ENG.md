# Buy hardware on installment with PayEx

At Telenor, you can split your purchase into small installments and pay over either 12 or
24 months.  
For example, if you buy a mobile phone on installment, you enter into an installment agreement with
PayEx, which as of June 19, 2024, is Telenor's financial partner.  
  
There are no interest or fees associated with taking out a loan through PayEx.

Does it cost anything to enter into an installment plan with PayEx?

No, it does not cost extra to pay via an installment plan. We have
entered into an agreement with PayEx, so it is interest-free and fee-free for you to pay
your purchase over 12 or 24 monthly installments. Therefore, you do not pay anything extra
for your new hardware.  
  
Please note that the total minimum amount is 1,200 DKK when you choose to buy on
installment.

How do I get an installment plan with PayEx?

When you choose to pay in installments over 12 or 24 months, you fill out a
loan application with PayEx.

**Here's how it works when you are approved for an installment plan with PayEx:**  
**  
The installment plan is created as a credit limit, which at PayEx is called an
installment account.

  * The credit limit is rounded up to the nearest 100 DKK and is simply the amount you are approved for. Ex: If you want to borrow for a phone costing 6,999 DKK, the credit limit will therefore be 7,000 DKK. You only owe the 6,999 DKK that the phone costs, and you pay it off over 12 or 24 months.
  * As you pay off your purchase, you get more available credit for future use. Ex: You buy a phone for 6,000 DKK, and you pay off 3,000 DKK. This gives you 3,000 DKK available, which you are already approved to use to buy new items.
  * If you want to buy an item that is more expensive than the credit you have available, you must be approved to increase your credit limit. Ex: You want to buy a new phone for 5,000 DKK, and you have 3,000 DKK available. This means you only need to be approved to increase the credit limit by 2,000 DKK.

Once your loan application with PayEx is approved, we confirm your order and send
your purchase by mail. You will also receive an order confirmation sent to your email.

Where can I follow my installment agreement?

If you want to follow your installment agreement, you can log in with MitID at
[payexone.com](https://payexone.com/portal/login).

Can I change the amount I pay in installments?

Unfortunately, your monthly installment amount cannot be changed. The amount depends on
the price of the item and whether you have chosen to pay over 12 or 24 months.

Why can't I choose the installment period I want?

The total minimum amount is 1,200 DKK when you choose to buy on installment.
Therefore, you may have limited options when choosing the first
piece of hardware you add to your order. Your options will be updated continuously as you add more products.

How do I contact PayEx?

If you have questions about your installment plan, you should contact PayEx directly at
**88 62 40 45.**  
**  
You can also find answers to most questions at PayEx, where you will also find
their [self-service portal](https://payexone.com/portal/login).  
  
You should also contact PayEx if you want to pay off your loan in full.

What do I do if MitID is not available on my phone?

MitID is a prerequisite for completing an application process at
Express Bank. Therefore, it may be a good idea to have a spare MitID, possibly
on a tablet or another phone. You can read more about this
[here](https://www.mitid.dk/en-gb/help/help-universe/have-a-spare-mitid/).

If you are in doubt about anything, you are always welcome to contact our
customer service - We are available Monday to Friday from 8 am to 8 pm.
