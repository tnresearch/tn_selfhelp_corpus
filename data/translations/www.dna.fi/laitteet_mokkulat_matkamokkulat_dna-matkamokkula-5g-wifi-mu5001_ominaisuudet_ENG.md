## Features

### DNA Travel Modem 5G WiFi MU5001

**Product Name: ZTE MU5001**

Take the DNA Travel Modem 5G WiFi MU5001 with you on your travels. This compact modem offers
fast and efficient 5G internet for multiple devices.

  * 5G NSA download speed max. 3.8 Gbit/s, upload speed max. 542 Mbit/s
  * 4G download speed max. 2.4 Gbit/s, upload speed max. 150 Mbit/s
  * 3G HSDPA download speed max. 42 Mbit/s, upload speed max. 5.76 Mbit/s
  * WLAN standard: 802.11ax (WiFi 6)
  * USB-C and Ethernet connections
  * 32 WiFi devices
  * 2.4 inch touchscreen
  * 4500 mAh battery, which supports fast charging
  * Micro-SIM card

**Sales package contents**

  * DNA Travel Modem 5G WiFi MU5001
  * power supply
  * USB cable
  * user guide
  * quick start guide
