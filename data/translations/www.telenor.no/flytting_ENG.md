# Moving your broadband? We can help!

There's a lot to keep track of when moving, and it's easy to forget things like forwarding mail, taking out new insurance, and, importantly, getting **broadband and TV** set up!

Whether you want to create a new subscription or change the address on an existing one, we're happy to help.

## Contact Us

If you want TV and/or broadband from Telenor, you can order in the chat between 09:00-16:30 on weekdays by clicking the chat bubble on the right side of the screen.

Alternatively, you can contact us on customer service to order a move. You can either call us or fill out a form and we will contact you with a good offer.

To ensure you have internet and TV in your new home, you should let us know **three weeks** before your desired connection date.

## Change address on mobile subscription?

When you move, you must also change the address on your mobile subscription - so that invoices and any information or new SIM cards we send to you arrive correctly.

To do this, you just need to contact us on customer service.

[Go to Contact Us](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

## Other questions about moving

  * #### Can I take my router and TV box from Telenor when I move?

Yes, if you have a router and other equipment from Telenor that you have purchased, you can take it with you to your new home. Use of the equipment requires that you have Telenor at your new address.

If you have rented a WiFi Router or TV box, these must be returned to Telenor. You will be sent new equipment to your new Telenor address.

  * #### Do I have to report a move if I only have a mobile subscription with Telenor?

Yes, you must register a change of address. Contact us on customer service, and we'll take care of it for you.

  * #### How do I cancel my subscription?

You can read more about [cancellation, notice period and other relevant topics here](/kundeservice/oppsigelse/).

If you want to cancel your subscription, you can use [cancellation form](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/e-post/) or [contact Customer Service](/privat/kundeservice/kontakt/).
