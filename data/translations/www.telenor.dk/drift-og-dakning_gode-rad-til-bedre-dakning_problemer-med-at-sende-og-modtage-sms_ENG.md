## Questions and answers about SMS issues

Problems sending SMS to a foreign mobile number

If you are having trouble sending SMS to foreign mobile numbers, you can start
by checking:

  * Can you send SMS to Danish mobile numbers? Send an SMS to yourself, for example
  * Have you written the country code in front of the mobile number? E.g. +44 or 0044 in front of the mobile number
  * Start a new SMS and manually enter the entire number in the to field

I haven't sent an SMS to 40 56 00 00

If your bill states that you have sent SMS to the number, +45 40 59
00 00, this is because foreign mobile operators are not allowed to tell us which
numbers you have sent SMS to. Therefore, this number may appear on your bill
instead of the foreign number. 40 59 00 00 is the number for Telenor's
message center in Denmark.

Please note that if you use emoticons in the SMS message, they take up more
than one character per emoticon. Therefore, you may be charged for more
SMS than you intended.
