  * I surf slower with wifi than with cable – why?

Generally, wireless connection is slower than connecting with a network cable.
Signals in the cable travel faster, simply put. If it is important for you to
get maximum speed, for example when downloading large files, you should
connect via cable. Need help with running a cable? [Read more about
Digihjälpen.](/link/76e6e1c84e1e45b0b9a123f0341ba70e.aspx)
