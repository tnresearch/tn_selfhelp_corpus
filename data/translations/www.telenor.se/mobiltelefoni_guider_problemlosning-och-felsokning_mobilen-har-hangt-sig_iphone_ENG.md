# How to restart your iPhone

All mobiles benefit from a restart now and then, and if the mobile has completely frozen, it is necessary to restart it. However, how you do it differs depending on how the mobile is doing.

## If your iPhone is having some trouble

This type of restart is good to do from time to time, or if you experience that the phone is slow and not working as it should.

### All models from iPhone X onwards:

  1. Press and hold one of the volume buttons and the side button simultaneously.
  2. When the slider appears, drag it to turn off.

### iPhone 6, 7, 8 or SE:

Hold the side button down until the shutdown slider appears.  
  

## If your iPhone has frozen

If the phone has completely locked up, you need to perform a so-called hard restart.

### All models from iPhone 8 onwards, incl. iPhone SE (2nd and 3rd generation):

  1. Press the volume button down and release quickly.
  2. Hold down the side button until the Apple logo (the white apple) appears.

### Do you have an older iPhone? Here's how to do a hard restart if you have a...

###

iPhone 6s or earlier, including iPhone SE (1st generation)

  1. Press and hold both the sleep/wake button on the side and the home button on the front.
  2. Hold the buttons down for at least 10 seconds, until the Apple logo (the white apple) appears.

##

To **start the phone** again, hold down the side button until you see the Apple logo.

Did this solve your problem?

If the phone still cannot be turned on or started, if it gets stuck during startup, or if there is another problem, you can get [more help on Apple's support pages](https://support.apple.com/sv-se/ht201412 "Apple's support pages").
