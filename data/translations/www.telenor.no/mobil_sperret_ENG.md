# Block or Unblock Subscription

Do you need help blocking or unblocking your mobile or wireless broadband subscription? You'll find the help you need here.

[Block or Unblock Subscription/SIM Card](/kundeservice/mobil/sperret/#sperre)[Blocked Wireless Broadband](/kundeservice/mobil/sperret/#geolock)[Collection/Closed](/kundeservice/mobil/sperret/#faktura)

## Block or Unblock Subscription/SIM Card

Here you will find information on what to do if your mobile is stolen, how to file an insurance claim, and how to reopen the subscription if the mobile is recovered.

  * #### Block/Unblock Subscription

 **Block Subscription**  
** We recommend that you block the subscription as soon as possible after discovering that the mobile is missing. This is to ensure that others cannot use it. You block the subscription by locking the SIM card, and you can do this in the [My Telenor app](/privat/mobil/app/mitt-telenor/) or on [My Pages](/mine-sider/).

    * Select **Mobile**

    * Select **SIM card**

    * Select **Block SIM card**

    * Enter a 4-digit code (you choose this yourself)

    * Press **Block SIM card**

[The Telmi chatbot](javascript:window.startChat\('elVWyO09DX'\)) can also help you with this.

Please note that the subscription fee will continue to run even if the SIM card is blocked. You can [order a new SIM card](/kundeservice/mobil/sim-kort/) if you want to use the subscription with another mobile.  
  
 **Unblock Subscription**  
If the mobile is recovered later, you can reopen the subscription by following the same procedure and entering the code you entered when you blocked the subscription.

  * #### I have lost my mobile

There are several things you should do if your mobile is lost or stolen:

    1.  **Report the theft**  
** If the phone is stolen, you must report the theft to the police before you can file an insurance claim. You do this by showing up at a police station or online. The police can also have the stolen mobile blocked in EIR, an international register that ensures that mobile phones cannot be used or resold.

    2. **Block the subscription**  
** We recommend that you block the subscription as soon as possible after discovering that the mobile is missing. This is to ensure that others cannot use it. You block the subscription by locking the SIM card, and you can do this in the [My Telenor app](/privat/mobil/app/mitt-telenor/), on [My Pages](/mine-sider/):

      * Select **Mobile**

      * Select **SIM card**

      * Select **Block SIM card**

      * Enter a 4-digit code (you choose this yourself)

      * Press **Block SIM card**

[The Telmi chatbot](javascript:window.startChat\('elVWyO09DX'\)) can also help you with this.  
  
Please note that the subscription fee will continue to run even if the SIM card is blocked. You can [order a new SIM card](/kundeservice/mobil/sim-kort/) if you want to use the subscription with another mobile.

    3.  **Open the subscription:** If the mobile is recovered later, you can reopen the subscription by following the same procedure and entering the code you entered when you blocked the subscription.

    4.  **Delete content on the mobile and register an insurance claim**  
[ **Read more about how you can easily file an insurance claim**](/forsikring/jeg-har-mistet-mobilen/)

  * #### Open mobile phone that has been recovered

When you report the theft to the Police, they will block the mobile phone in EIR.

If you have found/gotten the phone back, you must contact Customer Service to have it opened.

## Blocked Wireless Broadband

Have you received an SMS about usage outside the installation address?

If you take your Wireless Broadband with you and use it in places other than the installation address, it will be blocked. You can [**read more about this in our terms and conditions**](/privat/vilkar/).

There may be different reasons why you received the notification. Have you moved? Is the subscription at home, and are you experiencing that the notification is incorrect? Contact Customer Service so they can help you further.

## Invoice

If you do not pay your invoice by the due date, you will receive a reminder. If the invoice remains unpaid for too long, your subscription may be closed.

  * #### Collection and closed subscription

If you do not pay your invoice by the due date, you will receive a reminder.

If you do not pay the reminder within the deadline, the invoice may go to collection. When the invoice goes to collection, the collection agency takes over the claim from Telenor, and you must pay the bill to them.

If the invoice remains unpaid for too long, your subscription may be closed. How long it takes varies for mobile, internet and TV.
