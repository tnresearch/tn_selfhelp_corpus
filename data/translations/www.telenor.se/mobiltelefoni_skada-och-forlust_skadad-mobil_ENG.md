# Damaged mobile or tablet

Has your mobile screen cracked or has your tablet fallen to the ground? Here you will find everything you need to know about how to file a damage report!

## Where should I start?

How you file a damage report depends on whether you have any type of insurance (Trygg 48 or Screen Insurance) with Telenor or not. You can find out by logging in to My Telenor [here on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in the [app](https://uu429.app.goo.gl/gnXY).

The **Trygg 48** insurance covers all types of damage. You will receive a replacement mobile within 48 hours (weekdays), from the time you submit your report. Read more about [Trygg 48](/handla/tjanster/mobil/trygg-48/).

**Screen Insurance** is included in Unlimited Plus when purchasing a new mobile with installment payments. The insurance covers repair of a damaged screen, which is always repaired with original parts. You only pay the deductible. Read more about [Screen Insurance](/sakerhet/skarmforsakring/).

**If you do not have any insurance with us** [create a service request.](https://service.conmodo.com/neworder?key=7oyoU9RO9W712cZmx6JGDkEFkRhACjT2)

## For those of you with insurance with us

1\. If you have the **Trygg 48** or **Screen Insurance** insurance, [start a damage report](https://www.squaretrade.eu/se/telenor/). When you submit the report, we can see which insurance you have, just fill in the correct information and you will end up in the right place. The type of damage and whether it concerns a mobile or tablet determines which insurance is used. You will also find out how and if you should send in your device along the way.

2\. After you have submitted your damage report, you will receive a **confirmation via email or SMS** with a link that allows you to follow your case at all times. If you have questions about the case, call [+46 10 80 85 098](tel:+46108085098).

Information about the deductible can be found in the [terms and conditions for Trygg 48](/support/priser-villkor-och-blanketter/mobiltelefoni-och-mobilt-bredband/villkor-och-blanketter/) and in the [terms and conditions for screen insurance](/support/priser-villkor-och-blanketter/mobiltelefoni-och-mobilt-bredband/villkor-och-blanketter/).

## Have you forgotten this?

#### **Make a back-up**

Back up your device before sending it in for repair to avoid losing valuable information, such as contacts, pictures, and apps. Here's how to do it on [mobile phone](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/problemlosning-och-felsokning/valj-mobil/sakerhetskopiera/) and here's how to do it on [iPad](/support/mobilt-bredband/ipad/back-up-aterstalla-och-aterskapa/sakerhetskopiera-din-ipad/).

#### **Disable "Find My iPhone"**

With iPhone or iPad, the "Find My iPhone" function must be disabled when you send in your device to us. [Here's how to do it](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/andra-installningar/valj-mobil/iphone/aktivera-avaktivera-hitta-min-iphone/).

## Do you want to avoid the deductible?

If you want to use your product warranty instead of the insurance [create a service request](https://service.conmodo.com/neworder?key=7oyoU9RO9W712cZmx6JGDkEFkRhACjT2) instead of a damage report. If you are unsure whether the fault that has occurred is covered by the product warranty, you can call our service workshop on [08-400 045 04](tel:840004504), weekdays 10–17.

## Frequently asked questions about our insurance

  * What is the difference between Trygg 48 and Screen Insurance?

Trygg 48 is an optional insurance that you can add when purchasing a mobile or tablet from us. It covers all types of damage, and in the event of damage you will receive a replacement phone within two days.

Screen Insurance is included in [Unlimited Plus](/link/17844612ac5d4ab39e8169ffb973f772.aspx) when purchasing a new mobile with installment payments. It cannot be purchased separately and is valid as long as you have the subscription and are still paying off your mobile. Screen Insurance only covers screen damage.

Here you can read more about [Trygg 48](/link/27b3a4e6adb24318a94bbb4c31f3db5a.aspx) and [Screen Insurance](/link/bc32b0e98eb141d9a48267f8869d076c.aspx).

  * How do I get Screen Insurance?

Screen Insurance is automatically included for all mobiles you purchase with installment payments if you have the [Unlimited Plus](/link/17844612ac5d4ab39e8169ffb973f772.aspx) subscription.

Read more about our different [mobile subscriptions](/link/a96eaee9166b475194e657db89f197b2.aspx).

  * What happens if the damage is not covered by insurance?

If you have damage to your mobile or tablet that is not covered by the Trygg 48 or Screen Insurance insurance, you can [start a service request.](https://service.conmodo.com/neworder?key=7oyoU9RO9W712cZmx6JGDkEFkRhACjT2)

  * How much does it cost to repair my mobile/tablet if I don't have Trygg 48 or Screen Insurance?

How high the cost of a repair will be depends entirely on what the fault is. If the fault is not covered by the warranty, we will send a cost estimate that you can take a position on. [Here you can start a service request.](https://service.conmodo.com/neworder?key=7oyoU9RO9W712cZmx6JGDkEFkRhACjT2)

If you have [Trygg 48](/link/27b3a4e6adb24318a94bbb4c31f3db5a.aspx) or [Screen Insurance](/link/bc32b0e98eb141d9a48267f8869d076c.aspx) you should not use the link above.
