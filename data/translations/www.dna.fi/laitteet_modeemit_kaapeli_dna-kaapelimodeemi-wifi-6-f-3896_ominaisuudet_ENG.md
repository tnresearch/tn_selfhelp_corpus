## Features

Usable in DOCSIS 3.1 and EuroDOCSIS 3.0 cable networks. The modem acts as
the first mesh point and can be expanded with one or more F-266 Mesh
extenders.

#### Dimensions

  * Height 237 mm
  * Width 187 mm
  * Depth 65 mm

**Installation methods:** on a table (upright), wall mounting

**Power consumption:** 36W

#### Information

**Manufacturer** : Sagemcom

**Model:** F-3896

**Manufacturer's warranty** : 24 months

**Network connections:**

  * **cable** (coax), EuroDOCSIS 3.0 (32x8), DOCSIS 3.1 (2x2)

Local network connections:

  * ethernet 1 x 2.5 Gbit/s + 3 x 1 Gbit/s
  * **WiFi 6** (802.11ax), 5 GHz (4x4) and 2.4 GHz (4x4)

#### Sales package contents

DNA Cable Modem WiFi 6 F-3896, user manual, power cord and adapter,
antenna cable and data splitter, ethernet cable, safety instructions
