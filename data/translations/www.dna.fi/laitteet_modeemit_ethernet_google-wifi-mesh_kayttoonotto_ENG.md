## Getting started

  1. Place the Google WiFi in a central, visible location, on a shelf or TV stand. The app will help you with the device placement.
  2. Connect the Google WiFi to the modem or to the distribution cabinet with an Ethernet cable (Note! You may need a switch) 
  3. Download the Google Home app from the Google Play Store or App Store
  4. Open the app and follow the instructions in the app.

More detailed instructions for getting started can be found on [Google's
website](https://support.google.com/wifi/answer/7183148?hl=fi). Also see
the installation video.

### More detailed instructions for getting started

You will need these to get started:

  1. At least one Google Wifi point
  2. [Google account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/40560)
  3. One of the following mobile devices: 
    * Android phone with Android 6.0 or later
    * Android tablet with Android 6.0 or later
    * iPhone or iPad with iOS 12.0 or later
  4. The latest version of the Google Home app for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.chromecast.app) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-home/id680819774)
  5. Internet service
  6. Some internet service providers use VLAN tagging. Google Wifi operation may require additional devices. [See how to enable your network with an internet service provider that uses VLAN tagging.](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/9798157)
  7. Modem (not included)
  8. Ethernet cable for the primary Wifi point (included in the package)
  9. Power supply for each Wifi point (included in the package)

####  
Setting up the primary Wifi point of Google Wifi

Before setting up the first Google Wifi point (the primary Wifi point), make sure the modem is working and connected to the internet.

  1. Download the Google Home app for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.chromecast.app) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-home/id680819774).
  2. If you are using the Google Home app for the first time, [create a home](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/9155535).
  3. Select the location of the primary Wifi point. 
    * You can use any Google Wifi point as the primary Wifi point. 
    * The primary Wifi point must be connected to a working modem that is connected to the internet.
    * The signal is strongest when you place the primary Wifi point at eye level in an unobstructed location, such as on a shelf or TV stand.
    * Read more about [placing Google Wifi devices.](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/7183150)
  4. Connect the Ethernet cable to the WAN port of the Google Wifi point. Then connect the other end of the cable to the modem
  5. Plug the primary Wifi point into a power outlet.
  6. Wait 90 seconds until the light flashes slowly blue. This means you can continue setting up in the Google Home app.
  7. Open the Google Home app on your phone or tablet. 
  8. Select Add device or service -> Set up device. 
  9. Select New devices under Set up new devices in your home.
  10. Select your home.
  11. The app should find your Google Wifi device. Select Yes to continue. If you have multiple devices to set up, select your primary Wifi point.
  12. Scan the QR code on the bottom of the device. 
    * Hold your phone about 12 cm away when scanning. [See more tips for scanning QR codes](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/9235302).
    * If you cannot scan the QR code, select Continue without scanning and add the setup key on the bottom of the device.
  13. Select a room for the router or create a new one.
  14. Give the wireless network a name and a secure password.
  15. Finish setting up by following the instructions in the app.
  16. The router will now create your wireless network. This may take a few minutes.
  17. If you want to add another Wifi device, continue by selecting Yes in the app or add a point later by following the instructions below.

#### Adding a second point

You can add Google Wifi and Google Nest Wifi devices to the network you created with the primary Wifi point. All added wireless devices are considered points in the mesh network. Once you have decided on the [point
location](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/7183150) and plugged it into a power outlet, set it up through the Google Home app.

  1. Open the Google Home app 
  2. Select Add device or service -> Set up device. 
  3. Select New devices under Set up new devices in your home.
  4. Select your home if prompted.
  5. Your Wifi device should be found. Select Yes to continue. If you have multiple devices to set up, select the device you want to set up as a point -> Next
  6. Scan the QR code on the bottom of the device. 
    * Hold your phone about 12 cm away when scanning. [More tips for scanning QR codes](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/9235302)
    * If you cannot scan the QR code, select Continue without scanning and add the setup key from the bottom of the device.
  7. The point will now connect. Nest Wifi points: when you hear a sound from the speaker, select Yes. 
  8. Finish setting up by following the instructions in the app.
  9. If you want to add another point, select Yes in the app if necessary or add a point later by repeating these steps.
  10. Once you have added all your Wifi devices, the app will [run a mesh test](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/7620337) to confirm that everything is working.

####  
Troubleshooting setup

If the setup failed, try these:

  1. Unplug the modem, router, and point and plug them back in.
  2. Make sure the points are plugged into a power outlet and connected to the same wireless network.
  3. [Reset the point to factory settings](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/6246619)
  4. [Contact support](https://support.google.com/googlenest/contactflow)
