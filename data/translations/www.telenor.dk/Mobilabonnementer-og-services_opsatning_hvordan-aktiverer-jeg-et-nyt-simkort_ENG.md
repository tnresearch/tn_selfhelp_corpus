# How do I activate my new SIM card?

When you become a Telenor customer or switch to a new subscription, you
receive a plastic card containing three sizes of SIM card: standard,
micro, and nano. Before you press the SIM card out, you should check which
size fits your mobile phone. Most newer phones use nano, but double-check
to be sure, as the wrong size can damage the phone.

Once you have found the correct type of SIM card, you can gently press it out
of the plastic card, insert it into your mobile phone, and turn it on. See here how to
activate the SIM card:

If you are already a customer

If you are already a Telenor customer and have received a new SIM card, it will work
immediately. If it doesn't work, it may be because our system has not yet
registered your new SIM card. In that case, please call our
customer service on 72 100 100, and we will help you activate it.

If you are a new Telenor customer

If you are a new Telenor customer, follow these steps to activate your new
SIM card:

  1. Gently press the SIM card out, insert it into your mobile phone, and turn it on. If you are prompted for a PIN code, it is 0000. (If you want to activate a SIM card for mobile broadband, the PIN code is 1234).
  2. Enter the PUK code, which can be found on the front of the SIM card
  3. Choose your personal PIN code and confirm it
  4. Call 60 500 000 to activate your voicemail
