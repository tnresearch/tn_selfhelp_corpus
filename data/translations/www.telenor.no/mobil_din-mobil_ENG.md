## Order or activate SIM card

Have you experienced a lack of coverage after a change or after restarting your mobile? The chances are that this may have something to do with the SIM card.

Here we help you if you want to order a new SIM card for your mobile, want
to activate a SIM card against your subscription or if you have problems with
activation.

[Go to Help with SIM card](/kundeservice/mobil/sim-kort/)

## Splitt or Trade-in from Telenor

Splitt is a smarter way to buy a mobile.

With Splitt, you can both **split up** the payment over 24 or 36 months, and
**split up** what you pay for the mobile now: Pay 0,- today, or an
optional amount for a lower monthly price.

There are no interest, fees or commitment, and you can trade in your old
mobile for _even_ lower monthly price. [You can buy a mobile in our online store](/mobiltelefoner/).

Wondering about Splitt? Here you will find answers to [the most common
questions](/kundeservice/mobil/hjelp-med-splitt/).

  * #### Defective or damaged mobile? 

If the equipment you have purchased from us is not working as it should, it must be sent
in to us or delivered to a Telenor store. The way it is done
depends on whether the defect is covered by the warranty terms or not.

Defects covered by the warranty terms are weaknesses in the equipment, and not defects
caused by wear and tear, misuse or damage incurred after the purchase, for example physical damage, water damage or user error.  
You can read more about how to complain about an item on this page - [Help
to online store](/kundeservice/mobil/hjelp-med-nettbutikken/).
