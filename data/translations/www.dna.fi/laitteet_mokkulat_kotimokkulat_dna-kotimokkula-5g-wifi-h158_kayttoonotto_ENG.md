## Getting Started

  1. First, check if the SIM card is already in place at the bottom of the device, under the protective cover. If the SIM card is not pre-installed, remove the correct size Nano-SIM card from the SIM card holder and insert it.
  2. Connect the included power cord to the power connector of the Home Modem and the other end to a power outlet. Wait about a minute for the STATUS indicator light on the modem to first light up red, then change to blue. The 4G and 5G indicator lights will light up blue when the signal strength is good. Place the modem on a window facing the nearest base station. This will ensure the best possible connection speed. You can check the direction of the nearest base station on DNA's [coverage map](https://www.dna.fi/kuuluvuuskartta) by entering your address in the search field and clicking on the "more information about the network" section. You can also call customer service and ask for the best direction.
  3. You can connect devices to the modem in two different ways:  
**A.** Connect devices with an ethernet connection to the modem with an ethernet cable. This guarantees the highest quality internet connection. Plug the cable into either of the modem's ethernet ports.  
**B.** On your computer, phone, or other device, select the WiFi network created by the modem. You can find the network name and password on the sticker attached to the bottom of the device and on the modem's settings pages.

You can extend the WiFi network created by the DNA Home Modem 5G WiFi H158 by purchasing a compatible Mesh WiFi extender. The WiFi network created by the Home Modem covers approximately 70 square feet on one floor if the signal can travel through the rooms unobstructed. With a WiFi extender, you can create a seamless WiFi network that covers the needs of a larger home. A compatible WiFi extender works seamlessly with the home modem, which is why your WiFi will cover an even larger area, but the connection will still work over the same WiFi network (SSID) and password.

## Connections and Buttons

  1. **Ethernet Connections**  
The upper port functions as a LAN connection (1 Gbit/s), the lower port surrounded by a blue color functions as a LAN/WAN connection (2.5 Gbit/s).

  2. **Power Connection**  
The power cord included with the device is connected to this connection.

  3. **Landline Connection**  
The connection is not in use.

  4. **Pairing Button (WPS)**  
Press for 5 seconds to activate pairing.

  5. **Reset Button** (on the bottom of the device) Press the reset button on the bottom of the device gently with a sharp object for 5 seconds to restore the device to factory settings.

## Setting up the Wireless Network

The DNA Home Modem 5G WiFi H158 is encrypted with respect to the WiFi network. You can find the network name and password on the sticker on the back of the device and on the modem's settings pages.

  * SSID (network name): E.g. DNA-WIFI-XXXX E.g. DNA-WIFI-5GHz-XXXX
  * Password: Common password: XXXXXXXX

The DNA Home Modem 5G WiFi H158 supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz wireless WiFi networks. By default, the device has the 5 GHz network and the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz combined network turned on, so your device will choose which frequency to use. We recommend using the combined network (DNA-WIFI-XXXX).

## Tips for Building a High-Quality Home Network

  * It is recommended to place the modem near a window facing the direction of the nearest base station.
  * Never place the modem in a closed cabinet, electrical cabinet, or switch cabinet.
