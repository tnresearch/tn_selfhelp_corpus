# Unexpected or high invoice amount

Did you jump when you saw the amount on your invoice from us? Here you can read
about what affects the invoice amount.

New customer?

  * **First invoice**  
The first invoice is usually for a higher amount than the ones you receive later. Read more
about [first mobile invoice](/support/faktura/forsta-fakturan/) or [first
broadband/TV invoice](/support/faktura/din-forsta-faktura/).

  * **Trygg 48**  
The mobile insurance [Trygg 48](/support/vanliga-fragor/faktura/vad-ar-trygg-48/)
is free for the first two months. After that, it costs SEK 149/month (SEK 59
/month for tablet). If you have the Unlimited Plus mobile subscription, you pay
only SEK 49/month.

  * **Surf safely**  
The security package [Surf Safely](/sakerhet/surfasakert/) is free for the
first two months. After that, it costs SEK 59/month.

Existing customer?

  * **Have you made any changes?**  
If you have made changes to your subscription, it can lead to increased costs.
If you have a mobile subscription, you may have changed your data allowance, and if you have TV
you may have added C-more. If you have changed subscriptions, you may have lost
previous discounts.

  * **Have you purchased a service?**  
A service can be, for example, a movie from
[the Movie Store](/handla/tv/filmbutiken/), or a
[Data Package](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/innan-och-under-resan/innan-
resan/surfpaket-fast-pris-for-surfen/) for your trip abroad.

  * **Price changes and expiring discounts**  
Sometimes we change the price of our services (which we always announce in advance).
A higher amount on the invoice can also be due to a time-limited
discount having expired.

Mobile subscription?

  * **Have you changed your data allowance?**  

  * **Have you purchased extra data?**
  * **Have you sent SMS to abroad?**  
[See prices for different zones](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/priser-fran-
sverige-till-utlandet/priser/priser-fran-sverige-till-utlandet/).

  * **Have you sent pay-SMS?**  
Does the invoice say "Mediated Services"? This could be, for example, pay-SMS
during polls.

  * **Have you paid for parking with your mobile phone?**
  * **Do you have "Mobile phone" as a payment option in the App Store, Google Play or Microsoft Store?**   
In that case, any costs will appear on your mobile invoice. [Read
more](/handla/tjanster/betala-med-mobilfaktura/).

  * Why does the invoice have a different amount than I expected?

The invoice amount can change from month to month, and it can be due to many
different things, but the most common is that you have **purchased** something or **changed**
something in your subscription. Or simply purchased a new subscription, as the **first invoice is usually higher** than the following ones.  
  
[Read all about unexpected or high invoice amounts
here](/link/dd9a19a5155c4628a519809bd0ec86fa.aspx)

  * How do I view my invoice?

Log in to [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) to
view new and old invoices.  
  
In My Telenor you will also find

    * Payment status
    * When the next invoice will be sent
    * How to pay (e.g. with direct debit or e-invoice)
My Telenor is available both [here on the
web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx), and as an [app
](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx) for those of you who have a mobile subscription
with us.

####  
Can't see your invoices in My Telenor?

This is probably because you have not verified yourself as the owner of your
subscription.

#### Do you have e-invoice?

Then you can also see your invoices in your internet bank.

  * How do I see if my invoice is paid?

To find out if we have received payment of your invoice, [log in to My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx). **The information
you see there is the same as we have access to.** We cannot therefore see if the invoice is paid before you can see it yourself.  
  
In My Telenor you will see your invoices divided according to whether they are **unpaid**
or **paid**. If you have a mobile subscription, you can also see your upcoming
costs.

My Telenor is also available as an [app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx)
for those of you who have a mobile subscription.

[If the invoice appears as unpaid or overdue despite you having paid, you can
read more here](/link/b8dfe46d8f0644b1bcb95fdfe8dbe1da.aspx).

  * Why does the invoice appear as unpaid or overdue when I have paid?

If your invoice appears as unpaid or overdue despite you having paid? Check this:

    * **Have you just paid?**  
It may take a business day for the payment to be registered in our systems.

    * **Has the money been deducted from the account?**  
If the invoice still appears as unpaid after a few days, log in to
your bank and check if the money has been deducted from the account or that the e-invoice is
approved.

    * **Correct payment details?**  
Double-check that you have entered the correct OCR number, bank or postal giro.
The payment details can be found in My Telenor.

    * **Do you have e-invoice?**  
Don't forget that you must approve the payment in your internet bank for it to be
completed.

    * **What is the due date?**  
Perhaps the unpaid invoice is a new invoice that does not need to be paid
yet?
