# Transfer Mobile Internet Subscription

If you want to transfer or take over a mobile internet subscription, the
subscription must be out of contract.

The person who wants to have the subscription transferred must contact a
[store](/find-butik/ "Find store") or [contact
us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).

**When does the transfer take effect?**  
When subscriptions have been transferred, the new owner will be notified, confirming that
the subscription is now theirs. Until then, you pay for the subscription.
