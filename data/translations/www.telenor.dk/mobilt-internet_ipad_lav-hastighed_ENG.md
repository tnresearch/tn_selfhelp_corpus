# Slow Speed on Your iPad

If your wireless connection suddenly becomes slower than usual, you can usually restore the speed by restarting your tablet. If your iPad is connected to the internet via broadband from Telenor, check if your router has been accidentally turned off and that all cables are properly connected to the router.

If you are using Mobile Broadband instead, your internet speed will drop if you have used up your monthly data allowance. The speed will also decrease if, for example, trees or buildings block the signal, or if you are far from one of our masts.

## What Can Block Your Signal

**Hills and Trees:** If you are in a hollow or surrounded by hills or trees, the network signal may have difficulty reaching your mobile.

**Weather and Wind:** The mobile signal is affected by the season. The best mobile signal day is actually a frosty winter day. While wet leaves, rain and snow will dampen the signal.

**Buildings:** If there is a large concrete building between you and the nearest transmitter mast, the walls will lower the signal strength. And if you are inside a building with thick walls, you may also experience a poorer signal.

**Many in the Same Area:** If many people are using Telenor's mobile network at the same time, your speed may be lower.

## What You Can Do If You Experience Low Speed

  * Turn off your iPad. Turn it on again and let it start up
  * Update your tablet to the latest software
  * Check our [service status](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/ "Check service status")
  * Check our [coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/ "Check Telenor's coverage map")
  * Try resetting your network settings.   
**Select:** Settings > General > Reset > Reset Network Settings >
Restart your iPad.
