# Use Navigation

The following guide will help you get started using navigation on your mobile device.

First, make sure you have GPS turned on.

  * ## Check if Location is Enabled

Swipe down on the home screen for quick access to turn Location on/off. Location is enabled when the icon is green.

  * ## Tap "Settings"

  * ## Select "Privacy and Security"

  * ## Select "Location"

  * ## Turn on GPS

Swipe the button to the right to turn on Location.

Location is on when the button is green.

  * ## Tap "Accept"

Then go back to the home screen and tap "Maps".

  * ## Tap the navigation icon

Once you have opened "Maps", tap the blue icon in the bottom right corner to start navigation.

  * ## Choose your navigation

Enter your start and end destination and the mode of transport you want to use.

Then tap "Start navigation".

  * ## Choose your route

Tap the route suggestion that best suits you, the blue line is your selected route. Then tap the blue icon in the bottom right corner.

Navigation starts and you will now be guided to your final destination.
