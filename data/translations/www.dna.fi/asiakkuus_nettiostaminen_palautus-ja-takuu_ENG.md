# Returns and Warranty

Returns are always free. For some devices, in addition to the manufacturer's stated warranty period, you will receive an extended 3-year DNA Warranty.

## Returning a Device

The device must be returned no later than 14 days after submitting a possible cancellation notice.

Please note that only devices in like-new condition can be returned. A return and refund require that the device's sales packaging is returned in like-new and marketable condition. You can only superficially examine the device if you wish to return it.

A depreciation fee may be charged for the return of the device, up to the full price of the device, as well as return shipping costs.

What is considered use of the device?

Use of the device includes, for example:

  * connecting power to the device
  * removing the protective film from the screen or removing another similar protector
  * charging the device's battery
  * enabling the Find My iPhone (FMiP) program
  * inserting a SIM card or other similar smart card into the device and using the device
  * storage made in the device's memory
  * returning the sales packaging without the accessories, user manuals, remote controls, cables and other similar accessories included with the package.

## Extended 3-Year DNA Warranty

The warranty period for devices is always manufacturer-specific, but for phones, tablets and SIM-card enabled smartwatches, you will receive a longer **3-year DNA Warranty** for the device you purchase. The warranty extension offered by DNA extends the manufacturer's factory warranty to 36 months when the manufacturer's warranty is shorter than this.  
  
The warranty period is always stated separately in the product information.

Read more about DNA Warranty

#### Extended 3-Year DNA Warranty, Terms for Customers

**How is the warranty period determined?**

When you purchase a mobile phone, tablet, smartwatch or SIM-card enabled smartwatch from DNA, you will receive a longer warranty period (36 months) for the device you purchase. The warranty extension offered by DNA extends the manufacturer's factory warranty to 36 months when the manufacturer's warranty is shorter than this. The extended warranty is also valid for business customers.

**What does the warranty cover?**

The extended warranty covers the same things as the manufacturer's defined factory warranty and covers, for example, manufacturing defects when the device has been used appropriately and according to the instructions. The warranty does not cover, for example, defects or damage caused by dropping or immersing the device in water.

**How is the device repaired?**

During the extended warranty period, devices can be sent for repair directly to DNA's service partners or through DNA Store. Please check our service instructions to see how to proceed with your device. After the manufacturer's warranty expires, a faulty product will always be repaired when possible.

**Who are DNA's service partners?**

You can find our service partners and manufacturer-specific instructions on our support pages.

[Device Service Partners and Warranty](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/laitteet/huolto)

## Frequently Asked Questions

Do the products I order have a right of return?

You have the right* to cancel an order in distance selling by notifying DNA within 14 days. Distance selling refers to an order made either online or by telephone. The 14-day cancellation period begins after receiving the order confirmation. For goods, the cancellation period begins, however, upon receipt of the goods, if the product is received later than the order confirmation.

Please note that only devices in like-new condition can be returned. A return and refund require that the device's sales packaging is returned in like-new and marketable condition. You can only superficially examine the device if you wish to return it.

Returning the product is free of charge.

*According to Section 15 of Chapter 6 of the Consumer Protection Act.

How do I send a device purchased from DNA for warranty repair?

You can find comprehensive instructions for sending devices for repair and contact information for service partners on the [Device Service Partners and Warranty page](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/laitteet/huolto)
