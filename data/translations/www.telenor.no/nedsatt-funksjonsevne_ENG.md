# Products for the Hearing Impaired and Others with Disabilities

Here you will find products and services for people with disabilities.

[You can buy Doro phones in our
online store](https://nettbutikk.telenor.no/#attr-17%5B%5D=Doro)

  * #### 149 Text Telephone

Text telephone helps the deaf and hard of hearing to connect with others using
text-based communication. In addition to 149, you will also be able to
contact the emergency number 1412 Text Telephone.

For more information and to become a user of the service, see
[teksttelefon.no](https://teksttelefon.no/)

  * #### SMS and Text Forwarding 149

 **How it works**

    * You can communicate with the Text Telephone service via SMS by sending a text message directly to the number 149

    * The message is sent as an email to the Text Telephone service, which in turn contacts the sender or forwards the message

    * Write an SMS with the desired text, send to 149

 **Prices**

    * For Telenor subscribers (and subscribers of operators using Telenor's mobile network), the service costs the same as other SMS messages. [Check prices for your subscription](/mobilabonnement/)

    * Users who use mobile networks other than Telenor's network can use the service by sending SMS to 2080. Write an SMS starting with 149, then a space and the desired text. Then send the message to 2080.  
SMS to 2080 costs NOK 10 per SMS.

  * #### DirectCall Number

With DirectCall, you can pre-program your landline to call a
specific number.

People who have difficulty remembering phone numbers for family,
friends, or an on-call service may benefit from this service.  
Instructions for activating the service and more information can be found here:

[Information and guidance on DirectCall](/privat/telefoni/telefontjenester/)
