# Remote Control for T-We Box II

Do you need help with the remote control? Here we show you how to use the remote control; what the buttons are used for, how to connect the remote control and TV together, how it is charged and more.

The most important buttons on the remote control: At the bottom of the page you will find an overview of all the buttons.

[All Buttons ➜](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/fjernkontroll/#knapper)[Guide in PDF ➜](/binaries/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/fjernkontroll/brukerveiledning-fjernkontroll.pdf)

  * #### Battery Status

The light at the bottom of the remote control shows the battery status with the following colors:

    *  **Flashing red:** The battery is almost discharged.

    *  **Orange:** The remote control is charging.

    *  **Green:** The remote control is fully charged.

The remote control has a built-in rechargeable battery that must not be removed.

  * #### Charging the Remote Control

The remote control has a built-in, rechargeable battery that you charge with a USB cable.

When the red light at the bottom of the remote control flashes, it means it's time to charge it. The remote control may also feel **slow/unresponsive** if the battery status gets too low.

Connect the charging cable (which was in the remote control box) between the remote control and a USB port (for example in your PC, your mobile charger or **on the back of the T-We box**).  
If you are missing the charging cable, you can buy a new one, it must have a plug of the type Micro-USB (towards the remote control) and a USB-A (standard USB) at the other end.

Tip: Always leave the charging cable connected to the T-We box, then it is always easily accessible when the remote control needs to be charged.

When the battery is charging, the light at the bottom of the remote control will light up orange. When it lights up green, it is fully charged and you can unplug the cable from the remote control.

  * #### Connect Remote Control and TV Box

If the remote control loses connection to the T-We box, you can easily reconnect them:

    1. Turn on the TV and T-We box.

    2. Hold the remote control near the T-We box - about 50 cm

    3. Put the remote control into pairing mode by holding down the “MENU” and “OK” buttons simultaneously.

    4. Release the buttons when the **[On/Off]** button labeled “DECODER” (1) starts to flash.

If the pairing worked, the remote control will now be ready for use.

We have created a step-by-step guide and video showing how to do this. You can find it by clicking the button below.

[Remote Control Guide](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/fjernkontroll/bytte-fjernkontroll/)

  * #### Use the Same Remote Control on TV and TV Box

    1. Turn on the TV and T-We box

    2. Hold the number keys **[** 1 **]** and **[** 3 **]** simultaneously for 3 seconds until the light around the **[On/Off TV]** button starts to shine. The remote control is now in programming mode.

    3. Hold down the **[On/Off TV]** button on the remote control until the TV turns off. This may take several minutes, depending on what type of TV you have. As soon as the TV turns off, release the button. It is important to release the button immediately! If you release the button too late, the remote control will think it has not found the right TV and will continue to search inside the remote control until the TV turns off. This can take several minutes, depending on what type of TV you have. But once the TV turns off, release the button. 

    4. Turn off the TV box. When you turn it on again, the TV will also turn on automatically if it supports HDMI-CEC.

    5. Now try if the volume buttons work on the TV.

 **If the remote control does not work as expected, try programming it again.**

    1. Press the number keys **[1]** and **[6]** simultaneously until the light around [On/Off decoder] lights up. Then enter the numbers **[9 9 6]**.

    2. It will now flash twice around **[On/Off TV]**. Then you can program it again.

 **After the remote control is programmed, you will have access to the following TV functions:**

    * On/off

    * Input (select signal source)

    * Volume up

    * Volume down

If you want to adjust the volume for the box, hold down the **[Shift]** key while pressing one of the volume buttons.

Many other functions on the TV (such as menu, color keys and number keys) can now also be controlled with the box's remote control. To select a TV function, hold down the **[Shift]** key on the remote control while pressing the desired command.

[See video guide](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/fjernkontroll/bytte-fjernkontroll/#tv)

  * #### How to Adjust Volume on the TV Box

When your remote control is connected to both the TV box and your TV, you must hold down the Shift key while pressing the volume buttons to adjust the volume level on the TV box

## How to Make Your Remote Control Faster and More Stable

 **Are you experiencing poor performance with your T-We II Remote Control?**  
Then it may be time to charge and perform a pairing with the TV box, a so-called **_pairing_**.

A remote control with a low battery level will often lose pairing with the T-We box, but still work with IR signals (infrared light). This gives a poorer response and you have to point directly at the TV box. In addition, you will miss some features such as voice search.

When you connect the remote control and TV box, Bluetooth is used to communicate, which means that the TV box reacts faster and there is no need for a clear line of sight between them.

 **NB: We recommend that you first charge the remote control before performing the pairing**. Charge the remote control often, so you avoid the connection falling out. Need tips or help on how to charge, you will find it a little further up on this page.

### How to Connect the TV Box and Remote Control via Bluetooth:

  1. Turn on the TV and T-We box.

  2. Hold the remote control near the T-We box, about 50 cm

  3. Put the remote control into pairing mode by holding down the “MENU” and “OK” buttons simultaneously for at least 3 seconds

  4. Release the buttons when the **[On/Off]** button labeled “DECODER” (1) starts to flash  

If the pairing worked, the remote control will now be ready for use.  
If you still experience that the remote control responds poorly, you should try to **reset the remote control** and try pairing again. You can find guidance on how to do this further down on this page.

Are you wondering how you can check if you are using IR signals or Bluetooth today?

Press the voice search on the remote control (microphone button) - if you are not connected with Bluetooth you will get an error message.

## Tips!

If you experience that the T-We box does not respond as expected, you can use the yellow button as a first solution to update the box and ensure that normal functionality is resumed.  
  
  
  
By holding the yellow button on the remote control for at least 5 seconds, the T-We box will automatically be updated with the latest data from Telenor T-We.

This can also speed up the update after you have ordered new channels or streaming services in the content selector.

## Are you experiencing problems with the remote control?

  * #### If the remote control does not work

    * Check that the T-We box is turned on. Then the time is displayed in the display on the box and the red light around the On/Off button is on

    * Look at the battery lamp on the remote control, is it lit red? Then the remote control must be charged

    * Check that the T-We box is not exposed to direct sunlight, as it may overheat

    * If this does not help, you should try a power restart of the T-We box. You can either unplug the power cable, or use the power switch (which is located to the right on the back of the box). Let the box stand without power for about 10 seconds before turning it on again.

If the problem persists, [contact customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

  * #### Reset and Disconnect Remote Control

 **How to reset the remote control to factory settings:**

This resets the remote control to its original settings. After this, the remote control will not be connected to the T-We Box.

    1. Hold the number keys 1 and 6 until the **[On/Off]** button labeled **DECODER** on the remote control starts to light up.

    2. Then press the number keys 9 - 8 - 1. This deletes all data stored in the remote control including the pairing. The light around “DECODER” flashes to confirm and then turns off.

 **Connect remote control to decoder again:**

    1. Put the remote control into pairing mode. Hold the **[Menu]** and **[OK]** buttons simultaneously for three seconds. When the **[On/Off decoder]** button starts to flash slowly, release the buttons again.

    2. If the pairing is successful, the remote control is now ready for use.

* * *

  
If you experience that the remote control still works poorly, you should try a power restart of the T-We box. You can either unplug the power cable or use the power switch (to the right on the back of the box). Let the box stand without power for about 10 seconds before turning it on again.

  * #### Remote Control is Slow or Unresponsive

This is often due to the battery running low. When the battery status becomes too low, the Bluetooth connection will often drop, and the remote control will switch to IR mode instead.

To solve this, we recommend that you **first charge the remote control**, and then reconnect the remote control to the TV box.

 **How to connect remote control to TV box (Bluetooth):**

    1. Turn on the TV and T-We box.

    2. Hold the remote control near the T-We box - about 50 cm

    3. Put the remote control into pairing mode by holding down the “MENU” and “OK” buttons simultaneously.

    4. Release the buttons when the **[On/Off]** button labeled “DECODER” (1) starts to flash.

If this does not solve the problem, we recommend that you [contact us at Customer Service.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
