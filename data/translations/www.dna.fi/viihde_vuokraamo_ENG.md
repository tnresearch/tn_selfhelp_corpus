# DNA TV Rental Store Support

Instructions for renting and watching movies through the DNA Rental Store

From the Rental Store, you can rent movies and series from SF Anytime using the DNA TV app, set-top box, mobile devices on Android, or a computer's Chrome browser at [dnatv.dna.fi](https://dnatv.dna.fi/). You can also watch a rented movie on an iOS device.

## Renting Movies

The DNA TV Rental Store has thousands of movies, as well as a wide selection of series that you can rent one episode at a time. **A rented movie is available for viewing for 48 hours**.

Movie rentals usually cost 2-6 €, and series episodes 1-2 €. New release movies generally cost 6 € to rent. Rentals are charged to your DNA bill. You can view rental transactions and manage your subscriptions by logging in to the [Subscriptions and Services](https://kauppa.dna.fi/tili/liittymat-ja-palvelut) page.

Renting with a set-top box

  1. Select **Menu** and **Rental Store**. Movies and folders are navigated using the remote control's arrow buttons.
  2. You can browse movies by category using the **remote control's arrow buttons** up and down, and right and left. The category view shows the most watched movies in each category. By selecting the icon at the beginning of the row, you can view all the movies in the category, for example, all action movies. You can always return to the previous view by selecting the arrow button to the left below the Menu button on the remote control.
  3. Select the movie you want and confirm your selection with the **OK button on the remote control**. You can rent the movie or watch the trailer. If the movie description is not fully visible, select the down arrow button on the remote control and the description will be fully displayed.
  4. Confirm the rental with your personal DNA TV PIN code. If necessary, you can find the PIN code in the My DNA app or by [logging in](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#) to the dna.fi website.
  5. The movie is available for viewing 48 hours after rental. You can find active rentals in the **Continue Watching** folder.
  6. You can pause playback and, if desired, continue watching on another device: set-top box, Android mobile device, Chrome browser at [dnatv.dna.fi](http://dnatv.dna.fi), or iOS device.
  7. You can find the movies you have rented in your own folder, which you can continue watching on any device you want during the 48-hour rental period. You can also find action, comedy, drama, and children's movies in their own folders. When you select **Show All**, you can arrange the movies in order of release date, newest first, or most popular first. You can search for movies by name, director, or actor using the search function at the top of the view.

Renting with an Android mobile device or Chrome browser

  1. You can find the Rental Store through the Menu option that opens from the top left corner of the DNA TV app. On an iOS device, you can watch movies if you first rent the movie, for example, in a browser at [dnatv.dna.fi](http://dnatv.dna.fi)
  2. Select the movie you want and confirm the rental with your personal DNA TV PIN code. If necessary, you can find the code in the [My DNA](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus) app or by [logging in](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#) to the dna.fi website.
  3. The movie is available for viewing 48 hours after rental. You can find active rentals in the "Continue Watching" folder.
  4. You can pause playback and, if desired, continue watching on another device: set-top box, Android mobile device, Chrome browser at [dnatv.dna.fi](http://dnatv.dna.fi), or iOS device.
  5. You can find the movies you have rented in your own folder, which you can continue watching on any device you want during the 48-hour rental period. You can also find action, comedy, drama, and children's movies in their own folders. When you select **Show All**, you can arrange the movies in order of release date, newest first, or most popular first. You can search for movies by name, director, or actor using the search function at the top of the view.

Watching a rented movie on an iOS device

Once you have rented a movie with a set-top box, web application, or Android device, you can start or continue watching it on an iOS device. You can find the movies you have rented for 48 hours in the **Rental Store** menu under **My Rentals**.

For the rental, you need your personal **DNA TV PIN** code. You automatically received the personal four-digit code when you subscribed to DNA TV. You can find it:

  * In the order confirmation for the DNA TV product
  * By logging in to the dna.fi website
  * In the My DNA app

## Using a Movie Coupon or Promotional Code

If you have received coupons or a promotional code in connection with a campaign, you can use them to rent any full-price (1-6 €) movie by selecting the coupon or promotional code as the payment option. You can see the coupons available to you in connection with the payment option.

The movie coupon we offer as a new customer benefit is valid for 3 months after your DNA TV subscription. You can use the movie coupon to rent any full-price (1-6 €) movie.
