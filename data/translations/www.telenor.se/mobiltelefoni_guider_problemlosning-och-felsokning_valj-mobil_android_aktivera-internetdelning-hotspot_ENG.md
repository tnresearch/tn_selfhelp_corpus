# Enable internet sharing (hotspot)

## Samsung, Sony and Xiaomi

Is your friend out of data? Or do you want to use your computer in a place where
you don't have access to wifi? The solution: Create your own "hotspot" and
share the surfing from it!

This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest version of the Android
operating system.** So, please start by making sure it is installed on your
mobile – not least to ensure it works as well and securely as possible.  
  
[Here's how to update the
operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-
mobil/android/uppdatera-operativsystem/)  
  
Now you have the latest version installed, right? Then let's go!

  * ### Go to the home screen and swipe up to open the menu

  * ### Select "Settings​"

  * ### Search for and select "Mobile hotspot"

  * ### Set password and network name

This is an optional step, but if you want to name and/or password-protect
your network, click on "Mobile hotspot" and select "Configure" and make the
settings you want. Then click "Save".

  * ### Enable "Mobile hotspot"
