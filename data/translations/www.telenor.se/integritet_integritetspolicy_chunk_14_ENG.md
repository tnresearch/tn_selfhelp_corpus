##

  * Right to Object

You have the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. If you object to the processing, we will, based on your specific situation, assess whether our interests in using the data outweigh your interests in not having the data used for that purpose. If we cannot demonstrate compelling legitimate reasons that outweigh your interests, we will stop the processing you object to – provided that we are not required to use the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If you object to the processing, you also have the right to request restriction during the time we investigate the matter. Regarding direct marketing, you always have the right to object, and thereby unsubscribe from, such processing.

**Unsubscribe from Direct Marketing**

In cases where customer data is used for direct marketing purposes, you have the right to unsubscribe from such use at any time. You can unsubscribe from receiving direct marketing by following the unsubscribe instructions in each email, SMS or personalized marketing on public websites. You can also unsubscribe by contacting customer service or [our
Data Protection Officer](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt1).

**Right Not to Be Subject to Certain Decisions**

You also have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, if the decision may have legal consequences for you or significantly affect you in other ways. However, we have the right to use automated decision-making if it is necessary for entering into or fulfilling a contract between you and us or if you have given your consent to the processing. If you want to inform us that you object to such processing, you can contact customer service or [our
Data Protection Officer](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt1).

  * Right to Withdraw Consent

You have the right to withdraw any consent you have given for a specific use at any time. A withdrawal does not affect the legality of our processing before the consent was withdrawn.

If you are under 18 years of age, your guardian must give and/or withdraw consent.

  * Right to Restriction of Processing

Restriction of processing means that the data is marked so that it can only be used for certain limited purposes in the future.

The right to restriction applies:

    * When you believe that the data is incorrect and you have requested correction. In such cases, you can also request that the use of the data be restricted while we investigate whether the data is correct or not
    * If the processing is unlawful and you do not want the data to be deleted
    * When we no longer need to use the data for the purposes for which we collected it, but you need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims
    * If you have objected to the processing. In such cases, you can request that we restrict the processing while we investigate whether our interest in processing your data outweighs your interests

Even if you have requested that we restrict the use of your data, we have the right to use it for storage, if we have received your consent for use, to assert or defend legal claims or to protect someone's rights. We may also use the data for reasons relating to an important public interest.

When the restriction expires, we will inform you of this.

If we restrict the use of your data, we will also inform those to whom we have disclosed the data about the restriction, provided that it is not impossible or unduly burdensome. If you ask us to, we will also tell you to whom we have disclosed your data.

  * Right to Complain

If you have questions about our use of personal data, want to exercise any of your rights or have complaints about the use, please feel free to contact our
[Data Protection Officer](/link/2e30b5183c5c4c4b875191d32b2bdd4f.aspx#kontakt1).

The Swedish Data Protection Authority ([Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten](https://www.imy.se/om-
oss/kontakta-oss/), IMY, from January 1, 2021) is the Swedish supervisory authority for our use of your personal data. If you are dissatisfied with our use of your personal data, you can also contact them.
