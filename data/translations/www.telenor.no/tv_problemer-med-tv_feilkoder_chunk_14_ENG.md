#### K0180System Access Missing

This error message may indicate that your subscription does not have access to
this content. To get an overview of your subscription, log in to [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/).  
  
If you are still experiencing problems, you can [contact
customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) for more help.

#### K1006No Access

Error message K1006 is a general error message that can have many causes.

Try turning off the device you are streaming from (Chromecast, Shield or similar)
by unplugging the power cord and leaving it off for about 1 minute before
turning it on again.

If this does not help, you can [contact Customer Service](/kundeservice/kontakt-
oss/) on 915 09000 to investigate the problem further.

?

#### K2201No Access

Error code K2201 means that you do not have access to the content you are trying to select.
To access the content, log in to [My Pages](/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/) and select the content in the content selector there.

#### K2202Content Not Found

Error code K2202 means that content playback cannot be done at the moment.

Such error messages are usually temporary errors that resolve themselves, so we recommend that you try again a little later.

If the error persists for more than 2 hours, you can [contact
Customer Service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) on 915 09000 to investigate
the problem further.

?

#### K2340Insufficient Amount Available

Error message K2340 means that the maximum amount for film rental last month has
been exceeded.  
To avoid overspending, we have introduced a limit of NOK 3000 per month.
This corresponds to approximately 60 films per month.  
  
The amount limit is reset again at the turn of the month.

#### K4105Content is Blocked Due to Missing Rights

**If you are getting this error message, there may be several reasons.**

This error message occurs because you are trying to view content in an area where
the content is not available. It should not normally occur if you are in
Norway, but if, for example, you have activated a VPN connection or are using
networks that do not identify your location, it is interpreted as if you are abroad.

Try connecting your device to a different network and see if that solves
the problem. If you are still experiencing problems, you can contact Customer Service for
further help. [See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-
oss/)

#### K9003Too Many Concurrent Sessions

Error message K9003 means too many devices are connected to the same subscription.  
In the T-We app, go to "Settings -> Devices" and check that the list is not
full. Remove devices from the list if necessary.

#### K2309Playback Error

With this error code, the streaming device, router and cable modem/fiber box must be restarted.

Unplug the power to the equipment and **wait 10 seconds** before plugging it back in.
Also check that all cables are properly connected.

The error may also be due to problems with your cable or misconfigured equipment
(e.g. use of a router not supplied by Telenor).  
Go to **[Problems with Cables or 3.
Party Equipment](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/tv/kabler-og-
nettverksutstyr/)** for further troubleshooting.

If you are still experiencing problems, you can contact Customer Service for further help.
[See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
