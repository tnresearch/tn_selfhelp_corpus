# What is the problem?

We start troubleshooting by identifying the main problem.

  
But first: have you already **restarted the router** and **checked in**[ **My
Telenor?**](/mitt-telenor/) If there is a service disruption affecting you, it
will be displayed at the top of the "My Overview" page.  
  
Okay, let's go. You have restarted the router and are not experiencing a service
disruption. So what is the problem? Does the broadband not work at all, does
it work intermittently, or is it simply slow?  
  
Tip! Our chatbot Telenora can help you troubleshoot. Try it yourself by
clicking on Telenora in the lower right corner of the screen.
