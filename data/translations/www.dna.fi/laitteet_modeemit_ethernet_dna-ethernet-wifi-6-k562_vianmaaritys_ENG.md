## Troubleshooting

No lights are on on the modem (power cord is connected)

**Possible cause:**

The modem is not receiving power. The adapter or modem may be faulty.

**Proposed solution:**

Connect the power cord as instructed in the manual and check that the outlet
has power. Bring the adapter and modem to a DNA Store for replacement.

Wireless network does not work when the device is connected to Kotimokkula H158

**Possible cause:**

The device has received software update V500R020C10SPC312, which enables
mesh compatibility with DNA Kotimokkula 5G WiFi H158. If the Kotimokkula H158
you are using is in routing mode (default setting) and a K562 mesh device is
connected to it, the H158 will become the main device after the K562 software
update, and you will use the WiFi network (SSID and password) defined in the
H158.

**Proposed solution:**

  * If the K562s are connected to the network wirelessly, re-do the Mesh WiFi network setup for them. See instructions [Using as a WiFi Extender ](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/laitteet/modeemit/ethernet/dna-ethernet-wifi-6-k562/kayttoonotto#wifi-laajentimena) or <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2lJf5OI8jds>
  * If you want to use the same WiFi network name and password in the H158 as you previously had in the K562, you can change the WiFi settings of the H158. See instructions [Changing WiFi Settings](http://www.dna.fi/tuki/laitteet/mokkulat/kotimokkulat/dna-kotimokkula-5g-wifi-h158/asetukset).
  * You can also, if you wish, use the default WiFi network name and password of the H158 by connecting your WiFi-enabled devices to it.

All lights indicating an internet connection are on, but you cannot connect to the internet

**Possible cause:**

The modem may be frozen.

**Proposed solution:**

Unplug the modem's power cord for about a minute and wait for the internet
connection status lights to come back on. Restart your computer. Also try
with an ethernet cable. You can also reset the device.

All lights indicating an internet connection are on (Computer reports "limited
connection")

**Possible cause:**

The modem is not connecting to the internet.

**Proposed solution:**

Check the connection and connections. Also check the settings of your
computer/terminal (e.g. internet settings and network card). If necessary,
reset the modem, unplug the modem's power cord for about a minute and wait
for the lights to come back on.

All lights indicating an internet connection are on (settings checked)

**Possible cause:**

The firewall may be blocking traffic.

**Proposed solution:**

Check your computer's firewall settings.

All lights indicating an internet connection are on (settings checked, using
your own connection sharing device, e.g. router)

**Possible cause:**

Your own connection sharing device is frozen or faulty.

**Proposed solution:**

Connect the modem to the computer directly with an ethernet cable or use the
modem's wireless connection. You can also try setting the modem to bridge
mode. Contact the seller of routers or other additional devices.

Wireless network does not work

**Possible cause:**

If the connection works with an ethernet cable, the problem is in the WiFi network.

**Proposed solution:**

Restart the modem, check the settings and try WiFi tips at: [dna.fi/tuki-wifi](http://dna.fi/tuki-wifi). If these do not work, contact customer service.

Older devices do not connect via WiFi

  * Log in to the device settings page and select **My Wi-Fi** from the home page.
  * Change the selection in the **Encrypt** drop-down menu from **WPA2/WPA3-PSKandSAE** to **WPA/WPA2-PSK**.
  * Save the change by selecting **Apply** at the bottom of the page.
