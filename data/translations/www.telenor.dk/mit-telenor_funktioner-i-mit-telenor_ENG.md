# Get an overview of subscriptions and settings

In My Telenor you will find an overview of all your subscriptions. Under the menu
”Subscriptions” you can see all your agreements for mobile, mobile internet and
internet – and if you have Telenor Step subscriptions, that’s also where you can
get a good overview.

You can see what the subscriptions are called and how much you pay per month.
Press ”See details” next to each subscription to find information about
everything from account number and commitment period to SIM card number and PUK code.
