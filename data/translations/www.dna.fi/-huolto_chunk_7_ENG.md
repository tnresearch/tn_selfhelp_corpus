## Loan Phone for the Duration of Repair

A phone is nowadays a versatile device and a fixed part of our daily lives.
Therefore, we want to offer you the opportunity to get a loan device from
DNA Store while your phone is being repaired. You will get the loan device
free of charge.

We have many devices from different manufacturers in our selection at
different price points. We cannot guarantee the availability of a loan
device, as there is a limited quantity. You can find more detailed terms
and conditions and information about the availability of a loan device at
[DNA Stores](https://www.dna.fi/dnakaupat).

If the loan device breaks down, you may be charged the amount specified in
the loan agreement. The amount varies depending on the loan device. If the
loan device is damaged due to a reason beyond your control, please contact
the DNA Store as soon as possible.
