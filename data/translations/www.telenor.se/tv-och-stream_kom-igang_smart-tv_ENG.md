# Watch Telenor Stream on a Smart TV

If you have a smart TV from Samsung, LG, or Android, you can watch TV via the
Telenor Stream app.

The Telenor Stream app can be used on models from Samsung manufactured in 2020
or later and LG from 2020 or later. You can also download the app to
your Android TV, e.g., Sony and Philips with Android TV 9 or later. **  
  
**You can find the year of manufacture on a label on the back of the TV (the same label
where you find the model number).

Getting started with the app for Samsung

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (the link opens in a new window).
  2. Install the Telenor Stream app on your TV by searching for “Telenor” via the search function at the top of the “Apps” menu. You can also find it as a recommended app.
  3. Open the app, log in, and start the program you want to watch.

**Can't find the app?  
** It is probably because your TV was manufactured before 2020, and
the app is therefore not supported.

**Have you downloaded the app but can't get it to
work?** [Troubleshooting](/support/tv-och-stream/problemlosning/telenor-
stream/problem-med-telenor-stream-appen-for-samsung-smarttv/)

Getting started with the app for LG

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (the link opens in a new window).
  2. Find the Telenor Stream app on your TV by searching for “Telenor”. Can't find the search function? Press the Home button on the remote control and then select the magnifying glass.
  3. Open the app, log in, and start the program you want to watch.

**Can't find the app?  
** It is probably because your TV was manufactured before 2020, and
the app is therefore not supported.

Getting started with streaming via Airplay or Chromecast

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (the link opens in a new window).
  2. Download the Telenor Stream app to your phone or tablet ([more info about the app is here](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/mobil-eller-surfplatta/)).
  3. Open the app and start the program you want to watch.
  4. Click on the screen sharing symbol.
  5. Enjoy what you want to watch on your TV screen.

Getting started with streaming via Android TV

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/) (the link opens in a new window).
  2. Download the Telenor Stream app.
  3. Go to the Google Play Store on your TV. Find the search function and search for Telenor Stream. Alternatively: Press the Google Assistant button and say Telenor Stream.

**Can't find the app?**  
Your TV needs to have Android TV 9 or later. This corresponds to approximately TVs
from 2018.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * There are missing channels when I log in to Telenor Stream – why?

If you can see some channels on your TV but not in Telenor Stream, it is because
we have not been granted permission to distribute them there.

  * Why are entire seasons and episodes of some programs missing?

In Telenor Stream, you will find both programs from streaming services (e.g.,
Viaplay Film & Series) and from channels (e.g., SVT). Programs from
the channels can be viewed when they are broadcast, just like on “regular TV”, and in many cases
up to seven days afterwards. Then they disappear. But the programs from
the streaming services can be viewed at any time, and that is why you
can see all episodes and all seasons of a series from Viaplay, but only the
latest episode of På spåret.

  * I am waiting for my TV box, can I use Telenor Stream in the meantime?

It depends. If the start date for your TV service has passed, you can
activate Telenor Stream even if you have not received the TV box. But if
the start date is further in the future, you must wait until it has passed.

  * How do I change my login details for Telenor Stream?

You can change both your username and password for Telenor Stream in [My
Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * Can I stream to multiple devices simultaneously?

Yes, you can stream on up to two devices simultaneously on your subscription.
