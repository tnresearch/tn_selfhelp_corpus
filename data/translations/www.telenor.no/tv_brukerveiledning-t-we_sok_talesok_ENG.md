# Voice Search

With voice search, you can use your voice to search for what you are looking for in T-We.

Voice Search: This image will appear on the screen when you start a voice search. Hold down the
microphone button and speak into the microphone on top of the remote control.

## How to Use Voice Search

Please note that this service is only available on T-We Box II. You activate
voice search by holding down the button with a microphone while you speak. When you
release the button, the T-We box interprets what you have said and finds results that
match the words.

The screen will display "speak now" when the T-We box is ready to search. Starting
to say the words before this appears may mean that the voice search does not capture
everything you have said.
