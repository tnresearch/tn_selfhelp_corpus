# Change data allowance

Is your data often used up? Then it might be time to change your subscription, or as we call it: data allowance.

[ ](https://uu429.app.goo.gl/gnXY)

All about changing your data allowance

  * You can **increase or decrease your data allowance** a maximum of once a month. This means that if you have already increased your data allowance once, you cannot decrease or increase it again in the same month.
  * It is fine to switch **to both less** and **more** data. If you have a contract period on your subscription, you can only switch to more data.
  * You change it yourself, at any time, in the [My Telenor app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/). Select **Subscription** and then **Change data allowance**.
  * You can also change it in [My Telenor here on the web](/mitt-telenor/). 
  * Regardless of whether you switch to more or less data, you will have access to it **immediately**. 

  
Do you just want more data this month? Then you can [buy extra data instead.](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/surf/kop-extra-surf/)
