# Mobile Subscription Activation

Subscriptions picked up from a Post Office location, DNA Store, or a DNA reseller store **do not need to be activated separately**, and are immediately ready for use.

Contracts for products and services delivered directly to your home are automatically activated and ready for use within one hour of receiving the shipment containing the Post Office tracking code.

In other cases, the order must be activated at:
[aktivoi.dna.fi](http://aktivoi.dna.fi)

## How to Activate Your Subscription

  * You will need your personal online banking credentials or mobile certificate for activation. You can find the order number in the order confirmation email or postal mail. You will also receive the order number via text message immediately after the order is ready for activation.
  * As a business customer, only the person who made the order can activate the subscription using their personal online banking credentials. You will receive additional instructions during the activation process.

[Go to Activation](https://aktivoi.dna.fi/)

## Subscription Settings and Usage

Most devices have the correct settings automatically. If necessary, you can request the settings via message or configure them manually:

  * Order all settings via text message. Send a text message ASETUKSET (SETTINGS) to 14 000
  * Order multimedia (MMS) settings via text message. Send a text message ASETUKSET MMS to 14 000
  * Save the settings manually

## Activation in Different Services

Mobile Subscriptions

If you ordered a new phone number, it will be ready for use immediately after activation and you can replace the new SIM card in your device.

If you are transferring your mobile subscription from another operator, we will send you a text message after activation informing you when the subscription is ready for use. Replace the new SIM card in your device only after that.

Internet Subscriptions

If you ordered a new internet subscription, it will be ready for use immediately after activation and you can replace the new SIM card in your device.

Other Services

#### DNA Digital Security

If you ordered DNA Digital Security, you will receive an installation link via text message or email after activation. [Read the instructions for
setup](https://www.dna.fi/tuki-tietoturva).

#### Deezer Premium and Deezer Family music service

After activation, you will receive Deezer usernames and setup instructions to the email address you provided when ordering the subscription.
