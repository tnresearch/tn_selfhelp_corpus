# Getting Started with eSIM

Here we describe step by step what happens, and what you need to do, for your
eSIM to be ready on the day your number is ported over to us.

## Step 1

  * #### Check if your phone supports eSIM

 **How to check:**

    1. Open the dialer on your phone as if you are going to dial a number

    2. Dial ***#06#**

This will display IMEI codes and numbers. If you get a line called **eID**, it
means your phone supports eSIM.

## Step 2

You should prepare your eSIM already before it is transferred to Telenor. Log in to
[My Pages](/mitt-telenor/) either on your computer or your mobile phone. To
be able to log in, [you must have a TelenorID](/telenor-id/).

 **Click your way to "Mobile and Subscription" and look for a subscription with the status
"Processing".**

Here you will find the QR code that should be used to set up the eSIM profile on your
mobile. See below how to install eSIM using the QR code.

If you cannot find your subscription, you should contact us at Customer Service.

  * #### iPhone

    1. Find a **PC** and make sure the device you are going to activate eSIM on is connected to **WiFi**

    2. Go to **My Pages** with the PC and **find the QR code** under **Mobile and subscription** → **Devices and SIM** → **Main SIM** → **Show QR code**

    3. Go to **Settings** → **Mobile Network** → **Add eSIM** and **scan** the **QR code** with the device

    4. A **Telenor eSIM profile** is now downloaded and activated on Telenor's network  

If you do not have a PC or other device available to scan the QR code, you can take a screenshot of the QR code on your mobile phone and upload it manually. You
can find guidance for this at the bottom of this page.

  * #### Samsung/Android

    1. Find a **PC** and make sure the device you are going to activate eSIM on is connected to **WiFi**

    2. Go to **My Pages** with the PC and **find the QR code** under **Mobile and subscription** → **Devices and SIM** → **Main SIM** → **Show QR code**

    3. Scan the **QR code** and follow the instructions on the screen

    4. A **Telenor eSIM profile** is now downloaded and activated on Telenor's network

If you do not have a PC or other device available to scan the QR code, you can take a screenshot of the QR code on your mobile phone and upload it manually. You
can find guidance for this at the bottom of this page.

## Step 3

What do you need to do when the eSIM is installed on your mobile?

If you have a physical SIM card with your current provider, your new eSIM
will be added as an extra SIM card. Your old SIM card will work as
normal until you are transferred to us.

If, on the other hand, you have an eSIM with the other provider today, you must take a couple of
extra steps after the new eSIM is added. Below you will find guidance for
how to do this.

## Physical SIM Card to eSIM

  * #### iPhone

    * You can find your overview by going to Settings → Mobile Network

    * With a physical SIM from before, the eSIM is added as an extra/secondary SIM. It may be given a label.

    * Your physical SIM will continue to be on and work as before

    * It is important that you do not delete your new eSIM that you have downloaded in advance! If this is done, a new one must be ordered after your transfer is completed.

  * #### Samsung/Android

    * You can find your overview by going to Settings → Connections → SIM Card Manager

    * When an eSIM profile is scanned, the SIM card is activated and the correct settings are made

    * With a physical SIM from before, the eSIM is added as an extra/secondary eSIM

    * Your physical SIM will continue to be on and work as before

    * It is important that you do not delete your new eSIM that you have downloaded in advance! If this is done, a new one must be ordered after your transfer is completed.

## eSIM to eSIM

  * #### iPhone

    1. You can find your overview by going to Settings → Mobile Network

    2. When an eSIM profile is scanned, the SIM card is activated and the correct settings are made

    3. With eSIM, you will therefore lose connection on your existing eSIM

    4. You must manually go into the SIM card management settings and switch back to the previous eSIM profile to get the mobile network back on the existing eSIM

    5. The new profile is added as an extra profile

    6. It is important that you do not delete your new eSIM that you have downloaded in advance! If this is done, a new one must be ordered after your transfer is completed.

  * #### Samsung/Android

    1. You can find your overview by going to Settings → Connections → SIM Card Manager

    2. When an eSIM profile is scanned, the SIM card is activated and the correct settings are made

    3. With eSIM, you will therefore lose connection on your existing eSIM

    4. You must manually go into the settings and switch back to the previous eSIM profile to get the mobile network back on the existing eSIM

    5. The new profile is added as an extra profile

    6. It is important that you do not delete your new eSIM that you have downloaded in advance! If this is done, a new one must be ordered after your transfer is completed.

## Step 4

What happens on the day you are transferred to Telenor?

## Physical SIM to eSIM

  * #### iPhone

    * Your old physical SIM card will stop working at the transfer time

    * Activation of the eSIM profile should usually happen within a few minutes

    * If you do not get a connection, you can turn on airplane mode for 1-2 minutes, or restart your phone to get a connection to the mobile network after the transfer

    * Your old physical SIM card can be removed

  * #### Samsung/Android

    * Your old physical SIM card will stop working at the transfer time

    * Activation of the eSIM profile should usually happen within a few minutes

    * If you do not get a connection, you can turn on airplane mode for 1-2 minutes, or restart your phone to get a connection to the mobile network after the transfer

    * Your old physical SIM card can be removed

## eSIM to eSIM

  * #### iPhone

    * You can find your overview by going to Settings → Mobile Network

    * At the transfer time, the old eSIM profile will stop working and the new profile will be activated within a few minutes

    * You must turn on the new profile from Telenor to get on the mobile network

    * If you do not get a connection, you can turn on airplane mode for 1-2 minutes, or restart your phone to get a connection to the mobile network after the transfer

    * The old eSIM profile can then be deleted

  * #### Samsung/Android

    * You can find your overview by going to Settings → Connections → SIM Card Manager

    * At the transfer time, the old eSIM profile will stop working and the new profile will be activated within a few minutes

    * You must turn on the new profile from Telenor to get on the mobile network

    * If you do not get a connection, you can turn on airplane mode for 1-2 minutes, or restart your phone to get a connection to the mobile network after the transfer

    * The old eSIM profile can then be deleted
