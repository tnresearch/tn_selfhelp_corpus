## Spam Filter - Settings

Even if you use other email programs (such as Outlook), you can still
log in to Online.no and make changes to your spam preferences. These will
also work in the email program you normally use.

**_Note: This currently does not work in the mobile version of webmail, please use a
PC/Mac._** _ _
__

  1. Log in to webmail at [online.no](https://online.no)

  2. Go to **Preferences** (the gear icon at the bottom left)

  3. In the menu, select **Spam**

  4. Here you can choose between

    * **Filter Strength:** Light, Standard, or Strong

    * **Filter Effect:** Delete, Tag and deliver, or Quarantine

    * **Block:** Email addresses/domains

    * **Add:** Approved senders

  5. Choose the filtering you want and press **Save**

  * #### More about spam filter strength

**Filter Strength** – Each email received is evaluated and assigned a
score by an automated filter, based on the message's title and content.
If the score exceeds a specific limit, the email is categorized as
spam. The subject, message formatting, and message titles are examined for
"spam-like" characteristics.

    * **Light:** Light filtering sets the spam filter to a low sensitivity level. This setting allows more spam to pass through, but it is less likely to accidentally identify legitimate email as spam.

    * **Standard:** (recommended): Standard filtering sets the spam filter to a high sensitivity level. This setting has a 95 to 99% success rate in distinguishing between spam and legitimate email.

    * **Strong:** Strong filtering sets the spam filter to a high sensitivity level. This setting increases the likelihood that legitimate email will be classified as spam. We therefore recommend setting the filter effect to "Quarantine" or "Tag and deliver". You can read more about this below, in "More about spam filter effect".

  * #### More about spam filter effect

**Filter Effect** – determines what should happen when an email is
identified as spam.

    * **Delete:** This option will automatically delete all email flagged as spam. Emails classified as spam will not be delivered to your inbox and may be retrievable.

    * **Tag and deliver:** This option delivers all email flagged as spam to your inbox with **"***** _SPAM***_ **"** added to the beginning of the subject. You can sort these messages to another folder and delete them after review.

    * **Quarantine:** This option automatically places all email flagged as spam in a "Spam" folder.

If you have set the filter strength to "Strong", we recommend choosing
either the "Quarantine" or "Tag and deliver" option, so you can monitor
that legitimate emails are not being filtered as spam.

  * #### Block sender or domain

    1. Go to **Preferences** (the gear icon at the bottom left)

    2. In the menu, select **Spam**

    3. At the bottom right, under **Spam Senders**, enter the domain or email address you want to block

    4. Press **Save** at the top right

## Enable incoming email authentication

By doing this, you ensure that even more spam is removed. These controls
mark email as suspicious when the sender is not logged in to an outgoing
email server.

In **Preferences** and **Spam**, check the box where it says Enable
authentication of incoming email.

  1. Check that the following are checked:

    * Check SPF (Sender Policy Framework)

    * Check DKIM (DomainKeys Identify Mail)

  2. Press Save in the upper right corner

## Mark email as spam

The spam filter is not perfect, and some will always get through.
If the spam filter is too strict, you may actually risk that actual real
emails are stopped by mistake.
But there are still ways you can help train the spam filter, you do it like this:

  1. Mark the email in question

  2. Press the button with the stop sign - "Send to spam folder"

  3. The email is automatically moved to the spam folder

By marking email as spam, you help make the spam filter better!

## Message Filters

Message filters can be used for several actions. For example, you can create
a filter that says that all emails from a sender should be sent directly to a
specific folder, or that all emails that have a specific word in the subject line
should be sent directly to the trash.

**Here's how to create a message filter:**

  1. Under **Preferences**, click on **Message Filters**

  2. On the **Message Filters** screen, you must fill in the following:

    * Enter a name for the filter.

    * Choose **Any** or **All** from the dropdown menu **If... the following condition is met**

    * Choose **To**, **Subject**, **From**, **Copy or Priority**, and enter words, sender, or phrases that are relevant to the choice

    * To add an extra rule, select **Add condition**.

    * Choose action from the dropdown list **Perform the following action**, for example **Move and Spam**

  3. Click on **Add**.
