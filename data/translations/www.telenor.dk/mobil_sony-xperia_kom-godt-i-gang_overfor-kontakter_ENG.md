# Transfer Contacts and Data

Follow the guide below to import contacts to your Sony Xperia. But if you
have the slightest doubt about how to transfer all your contacts, pictures and
e.g. emails to your new mobile, then visit your Telenor store.

[Find store](/find-butik/ "Find store")

  * ## Start

Tap the application button.

  * ## Find Contacts

Tap Contacts.

  * ## Proceed

Click the three dots in the bottom right corner.

  * ## Import

Tap "Import contacts".

  * ## Select Import

Choose whether you want to import contacts from: "SIM card", "internal storage", "SD card"
or "USB storage" and follow the instructions on the screen.

  * ## Finish

Now the contacts have been transferred.
