# Help with IDTV

Do you have IDTV from Telenor? Here you will find information and guides for
connecting your card reader.

We are stopping the sale of card readers at the end of 2025

From 2026 we will no longer sell new card readers. We will still help
our customers, but new IDTVs will not be approved.

## About IDTV

IDTV means that you do not have a TV box, but a card reader directly in the TV. This gives you
the opportunity to watch digital/HD TV without an extra box and remote control.

 **To use IDTV, you need:**

  * Card reader approved by Telenor

  * A subscription and program card from Telenor

  * A TV model approved by Telenor for IDTV

Would you prefer our latest set-top box with support for 4K and HDR, among other things? [Read
more about the T-We box II](https://www.telenor.no/privat/tv/t-we/dekoder/)

## Installation

 **TVs that are prepared for IDTV have a port for a card reader (Common
Interface) on the back or side:**

  1. Unplug the power cable from the TV

  2. Carefully insert your program card from Telenor into the card reader with the gold chip facing up and towards the card reader.

  3. Carefully insert the card reader (with the program card) into the TV's "Common Interface" input. See the TV's user manual for how to insert the card reader.

  4. Reconnect the power to the TV by plugging the plug into the socket and follow the instructions in the TV's user manual regarding the card reader.

### Are you getting an error message on the screen?

  * If you get the message "No card inserted", the card reader does not have contact with the card, see Step 1.

  * If you get the message "No access", the card has not been updated yet. Select NRK1 and wait up to two hours for the update.

  * The card reader has no impact on the signal, or what the TV finds/does not find of channels. It only unlocks the channels you have in your subscription and that the TV has found during a channel search.
