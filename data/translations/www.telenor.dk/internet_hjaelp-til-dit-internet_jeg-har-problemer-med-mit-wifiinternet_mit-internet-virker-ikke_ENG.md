# My internet isn't working

Oh no, now I can't see who's winning "The Great British Bake Off!” Don't despair
– we are here to help.

If your internet connection isn't working at all, you can follow the guide below
to troubleshoot your internet problems. If you are experiencing only sporadic
outages, you can instead read this [guide](/kundeservice/internet/hjaelp-til-
dit-internet/jeg-har-problemer-med-mit-wifiinternet/mit-internet-er-
ustabilt/), and if the connection is slow, it's this
[guide](/kundeservice/internet/hjaelp-til-dit-internet/jeg-har-problemer-med-
mit-wifiinternet/mit-internet-er-langsomt/) you need.

If the internet isn't working on only some of your devices, it suggests that
the problem lies with the device and not the router/internet. Try to
restart the device that cannot connect to the internet and try to
connect again.

## Guide: How are the lights on the router lit?

Start by finding out how the lights on your router should be lit. You do this
by clicking on your router [here](/kundeservice/internet/kom_godt_i_gang/)
and reading how the lights should be lit - on most routers, there should be
green or white light in the bottom diode on the router. We only provide
support for routers purchased from Telenor.

The lights are not lit correctly

Try the following before contacting customer service:

  1. Router errors are often caused by a cable losing contact or operational disturbances. Therefore, start by making sure that all cables are firmly plugged into the router. Check that the cable has no visible damage
  2. If your router is connected to a power strip/socket, the problem may be that it is not getting enough power. Try connecting it directly to a wall outlet
  3. [Check our operational information](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/) to see if the problem is due to disturbances or outages at your address
  4. Try restarting your router by turning it off and on again. When you have turned off the router, wait a few seconds before turning it on again
  5. If you have missed a bill, we may have temporarily blocked your connection. Check quickly and easily if this is the case on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/). Here you can also check your order status if you have recently ordered a change to your subscription

Did that not solve your problem? Then contact customer service on 72 100 100.

The lights are lit correctly

Try the following before contacting customer service:

  1. If you are trying to go online via WiFi, check that your device is connected to the correct network. If you haven't changed the WiFi name, you can see the name of your connection under the bottom of the router. If your device is not connected to the network, simply reconnect
  2. If your devices are connected with a LAN cable, try pressing all the plugs firmly in and check that there is no visible damage to the cable
  3. To achieve the best possible connection between your router and the connected devices, you can advantageously place your router and your devices according to the following guidelines:

  * High
  * Free-standing
  * A central location in the home
  * Away from other electronic devices, cables and the like. Read the full guide with good advice for better WiFi coverage [here](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/).

If you have internet via the 5G network, you can try placing the router in a central location in the home where you know there is good mobile coverage.

Did that not solve your problem? Then contact customer service on 72 100 100.
