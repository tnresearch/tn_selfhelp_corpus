# Get abroad prices via SMS

Do you want to receive an SMS telling you what it costs to call and surf in the country you are visiting – even before you have landed?

You can order the SMS whenever you want. This is the same SMS you receive automatically when you arrive in a country.

Here's how to do it if you want the prices sent to you before you start your trip:

  1. Text the name of the country you are visiting to [10615](sms:10615). It doesn't matter if you write the country's name in uppercase or lowercase.
  2. After a few seconds, you will receive the welcome SMS. 

Keep in mind that if you have [purchased a Surf Package, those prices apply to you](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/innan-och-under-resan/innan-resan/surfpaket-fast-pris-for-surfen/), even if they are not included in the welcome SMS.

  * How to turn on surfing during your trip

To be able to surf during your trip, you must activate data roaming.

[Instructions on how to do it in your
mobile phone](/link/288523ae6c5a4fe083145390f68c3609.aspx)  
  

**Do you have a mobile broadband?**

    1. Make sure your subscription is open for roaming. If you have a modem: see the menu in the client. If you have a wifi puck, see the menu in the router.
    2. Make sure the router/modem/tablet is open for roaming.
    3. In some clients/routers, it may be necessary to manually connect when roaming and confirm that you accept it.
