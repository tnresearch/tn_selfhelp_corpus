## Frequently asked questions about prepaid card registration

  * Why do I have to register my prepaid card?

The Swedish Parliament has decided to introduce a new law requiring all
prepaid cards, regardless of operator, to be registered to a user. From August
1, 2022, all new prepaid cards must be registered to be used. In
February 2023, all existing prepaid cards must also be registered to their
user.

  * How do I register my prepaid card if I have a residence permit?

If you do not have a Swedish passport or driver's license but have a residence
permit in Sweden, select the option "Photo ID and camera" to verify your
identity.

  * Which photo IDs can I use to register my prepaid card?

    * Swedish IDs: Swedish passport, Swedish driver's license, National identity card, Swedish residence permit.
    * International IDs in over 190 countries: Passports, National driver’s license, State/Province driver’s license, National ID cards, Residence permits.

  * How do I register my prepaid card?

You can quickly and securely register here at
[telenor.se/registrera](/link/9d9b1dd3829a415580e98ad6af78f872.aspx) with
BankID or with a Photo ID and camera. You can also go to one of our
stores if you need help with registration.

If you purchased your prepaid card here at telenor.se, you do not need to do
anything, it was automatically registered at the time of purchase.

  * How does prepaid card registration work for me under 18 years of age?

You can register on your own prepaid card from the age of 16. If you are
younger than that, your guardian must register the prepaid card. The guardian
can continue to be registered on your prepaid card until you turn 18.
