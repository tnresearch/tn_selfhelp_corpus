# If you have a prepaid card

  1. If you are outside the EU/EEA, you should check that you have topped up [Dynamic/Balance](/support/frequently-asked-questions/prepaid-card/what-is-dynamic-balance/).
  2. If you have recently used up your data allowance and topped up again, it is a good idea to restart your mobile phone as old information may remain and block surfing.
  3. Check that you are not in a country where our prepaid cards do not work. If so, we recommend that you purchase a prepaid card from a local operator.
