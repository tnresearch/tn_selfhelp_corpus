# Buy extra data

If you run out of data, you can buy extra GB – or continue at a reduced
speed (64 Kbit).

When you have used your data allowance, you will receive an SMS with a link
where you can buy extra data directly via your mobile. You can also buy
extra data in [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/abonnement/detaljer/mobil/ekstra-data/).
Select ”Subscriptions”, click on the desired subscription and then click
”Buy extra data”.

Payment for purchasing extra data will only be deducted on your next bill.
