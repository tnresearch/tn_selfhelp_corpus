# WiFi Calling

With WiFi calling, you can make and receive regular calls anywhere with a
WiFi network. Essentially, this feature extends the coverage of DNA's mobile
network to everywhere there is WiFi.

To function, the service requires a mobile subscription and phone that
supports WiFi calling, as well as a wireless WiFi network. If you don't
already have a WiFi network at home, you can easily get one with DNA Net.
[Explore internet options](https://www.dna.fi/liittymat-ja-palvelut/netti).

## Better Coverage

The change brings a significant improvement in call coverage. For example,
basements and new low-energy houses are often challenging places in terms of
coverage. New energy-efficient windows also often degrade coverage at home.

However, WiFi calls also work where mobile network coverage is insufficient.
All that is needed is for the phone to have a connection to a wireless WiFi
network.

**Note.** Currently, emergency calls do not work over WiFi, but are always
automatically routed via the mobile network. The method of transmitting SMS
and MMS messages is device-specific. Some phones require mobile network
coverage to send SMS and MMS messages.

## No Extra Charges Abroad

In addition to coverage, the other clear home-field advantage of WiFi calls
is their price: they are always billed as calls made from Finland, even if
you are abroad. Calls and messages from Finland to abroad are always billed
according to a separate price list. Even in distant countries, you can now
carelessly share news over the phone at length without worrying about
roaming charges. DNA's WiFi calling home-field advantage follows you
everywhere!

**Note.** When traveling within the EU area and making calls to local
numbers or to another EU country, it is cheaper to make calls over the
mobile network. For example, a WiFi call from Germany to Germany/Sweden is
billed as a call from Finland to Germany/Sweden (= international call
charge), but a call made over the mobile network is billed as an EU roaming
call (= domestic price).

## Subscriptions Supporting WiFi Calling

The latest DNA subscriptions support WiFi calling. The service does not
require a separate subscription but is activated automatically for the
subscription. See supported subscriptions under "With which subscriptions
can you make and receive WiFi calls?". Currently, WiFi calls on Apple
products are supported on the DNA network only on Apple devices that support
iOS17.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is WiFi calling?

With this functionality, you can make and receive regular calls on your
mobile phone over a WiFi network. Essentially, this feature extends the
coverage of DNA's mobile network to everywhere there is WiFi.

WiFi calls work seamlessly with the mobile network. When you leave the WiFi
coverage area, the call continues normally over the mobile network.

What phone do I need to make WiFi calls?

The number of phone models that support WiFi calling is constantly growing.
The feature is supported on all new mobile phone models sold by DNA in its
own network, and generally on phones published in the 2020s by the
following manufacturers: Blackview, Caterpillar, Honor, Huawei, Motorola,
Nokia (HMD), OnePlus, Poco, Xiaomi, realme, Samsung, Sony, Ulefone, Apple
iPhone (starting with iOS17 version).

Currently, WiFi calls are supported on the DNA network only on Apple
devices that support iOS17.

What does the WiFi calling home-field advantage mean?

With DNA's WiFi calling, you get a real home-field advantage in the form of
better coverage and a lower price. The feature extends mobile network
coverage for calls. You can make and receive calls in places where there
would otherwise be no coverage (e.g., basements, low-energy houses). WiFi
calls are always handled as calls made on the DNA network, regardless of
where the WiFi network is located.

How do I enable the service?

WiFi calls require a subscription and phone that support the feature to
function. To use the service, you must activate it in your phone's settings.
The settings process varies depending on the model, and you can find more
detailed instructions in your phone's instructions.

How do I make a WiFi call?

Once you have enabled WiFi calls in your phone's settings and your phone is
connected to a wireless WiFi network, incoming and outgoing calls will
automatically be made as WiFi calls.

How do I know that a call is a WiFi call?

When making a WiFi call, an icon indicating a WiFi call will appear on the
phone (usually at the top). The appearance of the icon varies depending on
the phone model, but typically the icon contains symbols representing a call
and a wireless network.

How are WiFi calls billed?

WiFi calls are always billed as calls made from the home country, even if
you are abroad. The billing method depends on the subscription type and is
either usage-based, a monthly recurring package, or unlimited use.

On your phone bill, WiFi calls are not separated on their own line. WiFi
calls appear on the bill as calls made from the home country. It is worth
noting that foreign calls made over a WiFi network abroad are billed as
foreign calls made from the home country.

For example, a WiFi call from Thailand to Russia is billed as a call from
Finland to Russia. Similarly, a WiFi call from Germany to Austria is billed
as a call from Finland to Austria, and not as an EU network roaming call.

What are 4G calls, or VoLTE calls?

4G or VoLTE calls are routed via the mobile network just like 2G and 3G
calls.

The difference between 4G calls and 2G and 3G calls is slightly better
audio quality, faster call connection time, and sometimes better coverage.

You can make VoLTE calls on all new mobile phone models sold by DNA, as
well as generally on all 4G phones published in the 2020s, provided you
also have a 4G subscription.

How do emergency calls work?

Currently, emergency calls are not routed over WiFi. Emergency calls are
always automatically routed via the mobile network.

Can I send text messages or MMS messages?

Yes, you can. The method of message transmission is device-dependent. Some
phones cannot transmit messages solely over WiFi coverage, but still need
the traditional mobile network to deliver messages.

VoLTE roaming

DNA subscriptions are gradually starting to support VoLTE roaming, i.e. 4G
calls in foreign mobile networks. Support starts on July 1, 2022 in the
USA on AT&T's network, and will later be expanded to other countries and
networks. If VoLTE roaming is not yet supported in the country you are
traveling to, you can make regular calls over 2G and 3G networks as before.
In addition, you can make WiFi calls, for example, via a hotel's WiFi
network in the same way as normal calls. Please note that WiFi calls are
always billed as calls made from the home country to the destination
country.
