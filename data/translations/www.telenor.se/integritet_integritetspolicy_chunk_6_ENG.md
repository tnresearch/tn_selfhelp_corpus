##

  * Learn more about fulfilling agreements

We have the right to use your data if it is necessary to enter into or
fulfill an agreement with you. For example, we need to collect information about you to
identify you when we enter into agreements with you.

  * Learn more about legal obligation

We have the right to use your data if it is necessary for us to
fulfill a legal obligation. For example, we are obliged to process
certain of your personal data according to the Electronic Communications Act and
the Bookkeeping Act.
