# What is Flexible/Balance?

If you have chosen the prepaid top-up Fixed Price (for example, 10 GB or
Hello), you can use the Flexible/Balance top-up to make calls if your
call credit has run out or if you want to do something other than what is
included in the top-up. For example, send SMS to vote in a TV program or
pay for a Blocket ad.

You can also use your prepaid card in a country outside the EU/EEA using
Flexible/Balance. Then you top up the prepaid card with Flexible/Balance, and
then that is the amount you call and surf with on your trip. Flexible/Balance
is separate from the top-up you use for everyday calls. When you are at home,
you call and surf with your regular top-up again.

You top up Flexible/Balance in the same way as you usually top up, at
[ladda.telenor.se](/handla/kontantkort/ladda-kontantkort/) or in the app
[Telenor Top Up](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/)
