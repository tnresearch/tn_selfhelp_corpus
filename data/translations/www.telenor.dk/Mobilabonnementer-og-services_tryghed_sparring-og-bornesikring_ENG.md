# Blocking and Parental Control

On [My Telenor](/login/), you can easily block your or your child's subscription
for, for example, purchasing extra data. Our blocks are designed to help you
take care of your own and your children's mobile usage - both in Denmark, and when you
travel abroad.

On My Telenor, you can block your or your child's subscription for:

    * [Purchasing extra data](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/tryghed/sparring-og-bornesikring/ekstra-data-popup/ "Purchasing extra data blocking")
    * [Purchasing physical goods](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/tryghed/sparring-og-bornesikring/fysiske-varer/ "Purchasing physical goods blocking")
    * [Content-charged SMS messages](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/tryghed/sparring-og-bornesikring/indholdstakserede-smser/ "Content-charged SMS messages blocking")
    * [SMS messages](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/tryghed/sparring-og-bornesikring/smser-popup/ "SMS messages blocking")
    * [Data usage in Denmark](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/tryghed/sparring-og-bornesikring/dataforbrug-popup/ "Data usage in Denmark")
    * [Data usage abroad](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/tryghed/sparring-og-bornesikring/dataforbrug-i-udlandet-popup/ "Data usage abroad")

Additionally, you can subscribe to Parental Control, which blocks the purchase of games, ringtones
and images. NOTE! Please be aware that Parental Control _does not_ apply to purchases in, for example, the App
Store - but only applies to content-charged services.

## Block purchases of content in the App Store or Google Play

If you have children, it may be a good idea to set a PIN code on the devices
that your children use, which must be used to approve each purchase in the App Store
or Google Play. It is of course important that your children do not know
the code. Read more about how to set up your children's devices [here](/digital-
tryghed/artikler/sikker-pa-nettet/guide-til-bornesikker-surfing/)[.
](/digital-tryghed/artikler/sikker-pa-nettet/guide-til-bornesikker-surfing/)

## How do I subscribe to Parental Control?

Parental Control is free to subscribe to, and you do so in [My Telenor](/login/).

If you need help subscribing to or unsubscribing from a block, or if you want
other forms of blocking, you can [contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).
