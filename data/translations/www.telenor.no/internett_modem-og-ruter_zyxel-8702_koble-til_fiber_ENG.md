# Connection Guide for Zyxel 8702 (fiber)

## How to connect Zyxel 8702 (fiber)

  1. Connect the network cable with blue plugs to the port labeled WAN on the back of the Zyxel router.

  2. Connect the other blue plug to the ethernet port on the fiber box.

  3. Connect the power adapter to the router and the power outlet, and then press the power button on the right side.

  4. Check that the lights labeled POWER and WAN on the front of the broadband router are lit steadily before proceeding.

  5. Connect one end of the network cable with yellow connectors to the network port on your computer. 

  6. Connect the other end to one of the yellow network ports on the Zyxel 8702. 

  7. Check that the Ethernet light is lit on the router. [Read more about light status.](/kundeservice/internett/modem-og-ruter/zyxel-8702/)

  8. You can also connect to the computer wirelessly.
