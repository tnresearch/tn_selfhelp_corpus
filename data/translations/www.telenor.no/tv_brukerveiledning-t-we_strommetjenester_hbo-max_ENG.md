# Get Started with Max

The streaming service Max offers award-winning and popular world-class series.
Here we help you get started with Max!

To access the Max app, you must activate an account with Max. [The link to
activation](https://www.telenor.no/aktiver/max/) is provided when you select Max in
the content selector on My Pages.

Do you already have a Max account you want to reuse? [Scroll further down the
page](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-
we/strommetjenester/max/#eksisterende), and we will show you how to do it.

## How to Access Max

  1. Select Max on [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/)

  2. Follow the link to create a user account for Max and link it to T-We

  3. Log in to the Max app on T-We Box II or download the Max app to your mobile phone

## Questions and Answers

  * #### How many simultaneous users can have a Max app account?

You can create five user profiles, and 2 people can be logged in to the app
simultaneously.

  * #### Can I watch Max on the T-We app?

Yes, you can! If you have T-We Box II, you also have access to all content in the Max-
app there.

If you have one of our older TV boxes, such as [T-We Box](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-
og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks/), you will not be able to view the content via
the TV box. However, you can still view all the content elsewhere where the Max app
is available - via T-We App, in the Max mobile app (and share to TV via
Chromecast/Apple TV), in apps on TV etc.

  * #### How do I access all content in Max after selecting it in the content selector?

When you have selected Max in the content selector, you will receive an email and SMS with
instructions on how to register and download the Max app. You can also
view most of the content in T-We.

  * #### I already pay for a Max account, what do I do?

If you pay for a Max subscription directly to Max, you must choose whether you want to
cancel or keep this subscription. Information will be provided during
the ordering process when you choose Max for points in T-We.
