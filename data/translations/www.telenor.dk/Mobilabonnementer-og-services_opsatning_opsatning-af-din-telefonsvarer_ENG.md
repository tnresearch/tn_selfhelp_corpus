# Setting up Your Voicemail

Here you can find more information on how to set up your voicemail and personalize it.

# Accessing Your Voicemail

Call 60 500 000 to access your voicemail. Please note that
voicemail messages are automatically deleted after 7 days if you haven't saved
them.

## How do I access my voicemail from abroad?

To access your voicemail when you are abroad, call your
voicemail at 60 50 00 00. You may be asked to enter a PIN code.
The PIN code is the last 4 digits of your SIM card number - unless you have received a new
SIM card since your voicemail was set up. If you cannot find the PIN code, you can
contact customer service on 72 100 100, and they can help you get it
sent to you.

## How do I access older messages?

Call 60 500 000, press 1 to listen to older messages.

Once you have heard the message, you can call back to **(1)** , go to
the next message **(2)** , delete the message **(3)** , fast forward **(4)** , pause **(5)**
, replay the message **(7)** , hear the time **(8)** or be presented
with more options **(9)**.
