The service you are trying to access is not responding. There are no problems with
the internet connection. Please try again later.

The menu is unavailable while recording is in progress. You must stop the recording to resolve
the problem.

You do not have access to the selected channel. Check that you are subscribed to the channel
by [logging in to My Pages.](https://www.telenor.no/minesider/)

Feel free to follow the [guide for Missing channel or
content](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/) for more help.

#### E0426No access in this country

You are located in a country that does not have access to this channel.

If you are in Norway, the IP address may be incorrect. This can in
some cases be resolved by the router receiving a new IP address. Normally this will
happen automatically if the router has been turned off for more than 1 hour.

#### E2100T-We blocked for your software

If the T-We box has not downloaded new software, you can try to
restart all connected equipment. The restart can trigger a new update.

 **Restart the following equipment:**

  * T-We box - Unplug the power cord or use the on/off switch.

  * Router - Unplug the power cord or use the on/off switch.

  * Fiber box or Modem (depending on which connection you have) - Unplug the power cord.

Wait about 30 seconds before plugging the power back into all the boxes.

If you are still experiencing problems, [contact
Customer Service.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

#### E2351Blocked content

The content is blocked because we cannot access the service that checks that
your TV box is at your home address (line authentication).

To resolve the problem, you can try resetting your TV box:

 **Reset T-We Box**

  1. Press Menu/MENU on the remote control

  2. Select Settings and press OK

  3. Select Installation and enter PIN code (this is 1-2-3-4 by default)

  4. Select Factory Settings and press OK

  5. Select whether you want to delete or keep saved recordings

  6. Press Continue

If this does not help, you must try again later. If you are still experiencing
problems, you can contact Customer Service for further assistance.
[See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

#### E2360, E2360-22 Access Denied

Error code E2360 often appears when the TV box is located outside
Norway, or if you have used a VPN on your home network.

Our boxes do not work abroad, and VPNs can be perceived as being abroad. Turn off VPN and restart the box.

Within the EU/EEA, you can alternatively use the T-We app on your mobile phone, or go to
twe.no to watch TV.

* * *

If you get error code E2360-22, this is because sports content is blocked due to
missing rights (such as the European Football Championship or the Wimbledon tournament).
The sports content you want to watch will be available on the channel that has
the rights (e.g. the European Football Championship is available on NRK/TV 2).

* * *

If none of the above reasons are the reason, you must try to restart the TV box
by unplugging the power cable and letting it sit without power for about 10 seconds before plugging it back in.
If the problem persists, you should [contact us at
customer service.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
