# Subtitles for the Hearing Impaired

Here you will find help and guidance on how to turn on subtitles for the
hearing impaired on T-We boxes and the T-We App.

Telenor offers subtitles on most channels, both on live TV
broadcasts and for programs that have Start Over and Weekly Archive, in
addition to archive and Movie Rental. For some channels, we also have
subtitles for the hearing impaired. What kind of subtitles are available
varies from channel to channel and from program to program.

Subtitles for the hearing impaired are currently available on the following
channels: **NRK 1, NRK 2, NRK 3, TV 2, TVNorge, TV3, FEM, VOX and REX**.

On the NRK channels, you may occasionally experience the text being cut off
(missing words at the end of sentences) in the program on Start Over, in
the Weekly Archive and on some live TV broadcasts. We are working to fix
this.

  

## Permanent Subtitles

Via Settings in the TV menu, you can adjust language and subtitles. Here
you can turn on subtitles permanently, so that the channels that deliver
subtitles for the hearing impaired automatically have this activated.

 **Currently, this is only available for live TV**. If you are using Start
Over or Weekly Archive, you must continue to turn on subtitles for the
hearing impaired for each individual program.

  

### Turn on Permanent Subtitles

  1. Select **☰** from the TV menu

  2. Select **Settings**

  3. Under **Decoder Settings**, select **Language and Text**

  4. Set Yes on **Show Subtitles**

  5. Set Yes on **Hearing Impaired**

## Subtitles on Individual Channels

If you wish, you can turn subtitles on and off or change them on
individual channels.

For Start Over and Weekly Archive services, subtitles for the
hearing impaired are supported, but this must be turned on per program. You
can easily do this via OPTIONS or ?☰ button on the remote control.

## Subtitles on NRK TV App

In the NRK TV app, you will find NRK TV’s archive programs. Here, most
programs are subtitled. NRK does not support live TV on the app.

Once you have opened the NRK TV app and selected the program you want to
watch, you can turn subtitles on and off using the subtitle button at the
bottom right of the player.

See [https://info.nrk.no/tv/ ](https://info.nrk.no/tv/) for further
information on how to turn on subtitles.

  

## Subtitles via Teletext

Some channels, such as TV 2 and TV3, still have Teletext as an alternative
to subtitles. The Teletext service itself has been discontinued, but you
can still use it to turn on subtitles on live TV.

Subtitles on these channels are not available on Start Over or
Weekly Archive.  

  1. While watching TV 2 or TV3, press the **?☰** button on the remote
control to display the options window

  2. Navigate to the right with the arrow key and select **TELETEXT**

  3. Enter the page number used for subtitles - 222 (TV 2) or 333 (TV3)
