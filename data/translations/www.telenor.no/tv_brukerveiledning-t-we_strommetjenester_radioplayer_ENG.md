# Radioplayer on T-We Box

Radioplayer makes it easy to find and listen to your favorite radio and podcast
favorites. Here you will find more information about the service.

## Radio broadcasts via your TV and sound system

Many of us connect the T-We box to a good sound system – for
example to the stereo system in the living room or a soundbar. Therefore, it is also
practical to use the T-We box as a radio. To make it even easier to get good
radio experiences in optimal quality, Radioplayer has now been launched on T-We-
boxes. The service is completely free to use.

## Radioplayer is available on these T-We boxes:

T-We Box II

T-We Box

## How to find the service

You will find the app by pressing the T-We button on the remote control and selecting
“Streaming Services” in the top menu.  
Here, Radioplayer is clearly visible along with other external streaming services.

## Channels for everyone

Radioplayer lets you search for and listen to more than 110 radio channels, from very small
local radios to large national channels such as the NRK channels, the channels from
the P4 group and Bauer Media.  
In addition, several of these publish their programs as podcasts in
Radioplayer. At the time of writing, there are just over 300 podcast series from
Norwegian broadcasters available.  
  
When you start the service for the first time, you will be given the opportunity to search for
the channels and programs you know you like and add them to your
favorites.
