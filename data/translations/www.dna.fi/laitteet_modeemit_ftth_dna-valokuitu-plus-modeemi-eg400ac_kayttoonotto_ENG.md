## Getting Started

## Modem Installation

  1. Connect the fiber optic cable from the modem (SFP installed, see the modem's quick guide) to the wall socket in the room.
  2. Plug the power adapter into a power outlet and connect the other end to the EG400's power connector.
  3. Press the modem's power button. The modem lights will flash for about 5 minutes. The installation was successful when the power switch light turns on and the green lights turn on for the Internet and Broadband on the Ethernet modem. The EG400 installation is now complete.
  4. You can connect devices to the DNA Fiber Modem EG400AC in three different ways:

Option 1: By connecting an Ethernet cable from the modem's LAN ports to an external
router directly or via a switch cabinet.

Option 2: Wirelessly via WiFi (wireless WiFi network).

Option 3: By connecting directly to the modem and, for example, a computer or
set-top box with an Ethernet (RJ45) network cable.  

###

## Connecting Devices to the EG400

Various devices can be connected to the EG400AC fiber modem in many different ways.
The options are:  

#### Option 1: Utilizing the Apartment Switch Cabinet and External Router

Sometimes the fiber modem needs to be installed in a poor location in terms of WiFi coverage. In this case, it is recommended to use the modem only as a fiber converter and
enable WiFi with an external router. More information about the switch in the switch cabinet
and external routers:
[www.dna.fi/modeemit](http://www.dna.fi/modeemit).  

#### Option 2: To the EG400AC's Own WiFi Network

Wireless devices such as tablets, phones and laptops can be connected directly to the EG400AC modem's powerful WiFi 5
network.  

#### Option 3: Directly to the EG400AC's LAN Port

You can also connect a computer directly to the EG400's yellow LAN ports if needed.  

## Setting up the Wireless WiFi Network

DNA's broadband modems are encrypted in terms of wireless network. You can find
the network name and password on the label included in the package, on a sticker attached to the device, and if necessary, on the settings pages.

  * **SSID (network name):** E.g. DNA-WLAN-XXX, DNA-WLAN-2G-XXX and DNA-WLAN-5G-XXX 
  * **Wireless network password:** E.g. 12345678901  

Inteno EG400AC modem supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz wireless WiFi networks. Both are enabled by default. These are distinguished from each other by the 2 GHz and 5
GHz terms in the WiFi name (SSID). You can change the network name or even
turn off one on the modem's settings pages.  
The device also transmits a third WiFi network, which is a combination of 2.4 GHz and 5
GHz networks. Connect to this if you want the device to choose which
frequency to use.

**Forgot the password?** You can restore the factory settings by pressing
the RESET button on the back of the device for over 30 seconds.  
Restoring the factory settings will erase all changes made to the device's settings. After this, you can start modifying the modem's factory settings from scratch again via an Ethernet connection or by connecting to the wireless network with the default name and
password found on the modem's labels.

## Meaning of Front Panel Lights

  

**1. Power Indicator (Status)**

Green light | Power is on  
---|---  
Red light | Startup error (contact DNA customer service)  
Dark | Power is off  
  
  
**2. Broadband**

Green light | Connected to DSL/ETH-WAN network  
---|---  
Dark | Not connected to DSL/ETH-WAN network  
Flashing green | Data is moving over the WAN port  
  
  
**3. Internet Indicator**

Green light | IP address received and internet connection established  
---|---  
Red light | No IP address, no internet connection  
  
  
**4. WiFi Indicator**

Green light | WiFi enabled  
---|---  
Dark | WiFi disabled  
Flashing green | Traffic  
  
  
**5. Ethernet Indicator**

Green light | Ethernet cable connected to LAN port  
---|---  
Dark | No Ethernet cable connected to LAN port  
Green light flashing | WPS registration in progress  
  
**6. WPS**

Green light | End device registered via WPS  
---|---  
Green light flashing | WPS registration in progress  
  
  
**7. Update**

Flashing green light | Software update is available  
---|---  

## Device Connections

  1. **Reset button** The button on the back of the device restores the modem to its factory settings. If you have forgotten changes you have made to the modem's settings, e.g. the password, gently press a pointed object (e.g. a straightened paper clip) for 30 seconds into the hole of the reset button on the upper part of the back panel of the modem. Resetting restores all original factory settings.  

  2. **Power switch** (power on / power off) If you turn off the modem's power, always wait at least 15 seconds before turning it back on.  

  3. **Power connector** For the device's own power adapter.  

  4. **WNA connection** For example, to connect another modem to the EG400AC modem or to connect to the internet in the Ethernet destinations.  

  5. **Internal network** Ethernet ports (LAN 1-4, RJ-45). Connections e.g. to a computer, game console or set-top box.  

  6. **USB ports** You can connect, for example, a USB modem or mass storage to the USB port, which you can share with the local network using the modem.  

  7. **TEL ports** The user can connect their landline phone to the modem and use the fiber connection as a telephone network. Not commonly used in Finland.
