# Transfer Phone Number

You can transfer your mobile number to a new owner at any time – if you are
in a contract, the recipient of your number will take over the remaining
contract period.

**Here's how:**

The person who wants to take over the number must visit a [Telenor
store](/find-butik/) or [contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).  
We will then send you an email where you must confirm via MitID that you agree to the transfer.  
Once we have the confirmation, the number will be transferred on the selected date.
