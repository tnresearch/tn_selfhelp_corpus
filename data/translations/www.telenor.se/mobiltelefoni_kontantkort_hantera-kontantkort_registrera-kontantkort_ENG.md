# Register Prepaid Card

As of August 1, 2022, a new law applies which means that anyone purchasing a new prepaid card needs to register in order to use it. It is easy to register, and offers several benefits.

With the prepaid card registered to you, you can:

  * retain your number if you lose your mobile.
  * choose to make your number private. [Contact Customer Service](/archive/2020/kontakta-oss-old-old/) and they will help you.
  * block your card if you lose your mobile. This way you can retain the credit on the card.

At the Swedish Post and Telecom Authority (PTS), you can read more about the [registration requirement](https://www.pts.se/sv/privat/internet/integritet/brottsbekampning/forbetalda-kontantkort-ska-registreras/).

##
How to Register Your Prepaid Card

1\. Insert the SIM card into your phone.

2\. Go to [telenor.se/register.](/handla/kontantkort/registrera-kontantkort/ "telenor.se/register")

3\. Enter the number you want to register.

4\. Choose BankID or Photo ID.

5a. If BankID – follow the instructions on the screen and confirm in the BankID app as usual.

5b. If Photo ID – follow the instructions on the screen and first take a picture of your driver's license, passport or other photo ID, then a picture of yourself.

6\. Done!

You can of course also register a prepaid card from another phone, tablet or computer.

If you purchased your prepaid card at telenor.se, you do not need to do anything. The prepaid card was automatically registered at the time of purchase.

##
Do you want 200 SEK extra on your prepaid card?

If you transfer your number from another operator to your new prepaid card with Telenor, you will receive 200 SEK extra on the prepaid card. The offer applies once per personal identity number.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Prepaid Card Registration

  * Why do I have to register my prepaid card?

The Swedish Parliament has decided to introduce a new law requiring all prepaid cards, regardless of operator, to be registered to a user. As of August 1, 2022, all new prepaid cards must be registered to be used. In February 2023, all existing prepaid cards must also be registered to their user.

  * How do I register my prepaid card if I have a residence permit?

If you do not have a Swedish passport or driver's license but have a residence permit in Sweden, choose the option "Photo ID and camera" to verify your identity.

  * Which photo IDs can I use to register my prepaid card?

    * Swedish IDs: Swedish passport, Swedish driver's license, National identity card, Swedish residence permit.
    * International IDs in over 190 countries: Passports, National driver’s license, State/Province driver’s license, National ID cards, Residence permits.

  * How do I register my prepaid card?

You can quickly and securely register here at [telenor.se/register](/link/9d9b1dd3829a415580e98ad6af78f872.aspx) with BankID or with a Photo ID and camera. You can also go to one of our stores if you need help with registration.

If you purchased your prepaid card here at telenor.se, you do not need to do anything, it was automatically registered at the time of purchase.

  * How does prepaid card registration work for those under 18?

You can register on your own prepaid card from the age of 16. If you are younger than that, your guardian must register the prepaid card. The guardian can continue to be registered on your prepaid card until you turn 18.
