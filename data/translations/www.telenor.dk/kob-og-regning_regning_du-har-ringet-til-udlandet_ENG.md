# Calls to abroad from Denmark

When you call abroad from Denmark, this will always be listed separately on your
bill under 'Usage not included in subscription'. It will therefore state
which country you have called.

If you have a subscription with free calls, calls to abroad will not be
included in this.

If you send an SMS or MMS to a foreign number, this will be shown on
your bill as "SMS sent to abroad from DK" or "MMS sent to abroad
from DK". If you want to see which country or which number you sent
SMS or MMS to, this can be seen under usage on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/
"My Telenor").
