# DNA TV on Apple TV

You can download the DNA TV app to your Apple TV device (3rd generation) and
use the app conveniently through it. With older Apple TV devices, you can
mirror DNA TV to your TV via AirPlay.

## Getting Started

You can download the DNA TV app to Apple TV (3rd generation) and use
the app conveniently like this. Get the app up and running, and when storage is included
in your subscription, you can record and watch recordings, as well as watch
rented movies easily through the app.

With older Apple TV devices, you can mirror DNA TV to the big screen
via AirPlay, after first getting the DNA TV app up and running
on your phone, tablet, or computer.

### Getting the DNA TV app up and running on Apple TV (3rd generation)

Download the DNA TV app to Apple TV

Open the app

Choose from two different login options:

  * Log in with the DNA ID you created when you subscribed, using the email + password combination.
  * With a quick link, i.e. enter the email address you provided when you subscribed, and you will receive a message to your email. Follow the instructions in the message and continue to the app by clicking the link. 

After logging in, DNA TV is available on your phone, tablet,
computer at [dnatv.dna.fi](https://dnatv.dna.fi) and on Apple
TV. The service is available on five different devices simultaneously.

## Features and Usage

Home View

In the Home view, you can easily continue watching interrupted programs,
see the programs starting next in the program guide, and explore
the movie selection in the Rental Shop.

  * Continue watching appears if the program or movie has been interrupted. You can easily continue watching from where you left off.
  * Latest recordings are visible if your subscription includes recording and you have recorded programs.
  * Favorites shows the programs and movies you have marked as favorites.
  * Rental movies presents you with new movies from the DNA TV Rental Shop. By pressing "Show more", you can explore the entire selection of the Rental Shop. Rented movies can be watched for 48 hours and are billed on your DNA invoice. Rent a movie first on another device, such as an Android mobile device or a computer browser, and then continue watching on Apple TV. 
  * At the bottom of the home page are recommendations for content from streaming services included in your subscription.

Recordings

The Recordings section is only visible if the recording feature is included in your subscription. You can
watch or delete recordings by managing recording rules. Recording programs is possible from the Home and Program Guide views. You can see a more detailed list of recordable channels from the channel list.

  * Storage space is unlimited. You can record from all basic channels simultaneously, for example.
  * You can also start recording during a program and watch it whenever you want. With series recording, you can record all the programs in an entire production season at once, with the touch of a button.
  * A recording can be watched for two years and then automatically disappears from the service. Recordings can be watched anywhere in the EU. For programs recorded from the channels Nelonen, Liv, JIM and Hero, viewing is not supported with the DNA TV app on Apple TV, but you can watch all recordings on other devices, such as Android mobile devices or a computer browser, as well as on the DNA Hubi device. 
  * Deleting recordings is possible in the Recordings view when you go to the right edge of the recording. The Delete command deletes the recording immediately.
  * You can find recording rules in the upper right corner of the Recordings view. From there you can see, for example, series that are currently being recorded.

Program Guide

Through the program guide, you can view the program information of channels up to 7 days
forward.

  * You can record upcoming or ongoing programs by selecting "Rec" and the program will be recorded from the beginning.
  * If it is a series, you can record all episodes of the series by selecting Record episodes. If a function is blocked for a particular program, the selection will appear in gray and the function cannot be activated.
  * Through the program's additional information, you can go forward to the program page, where you can see, for example, more detailed information about the program, as well as seasons and their episodes of series.

Rental Shop

You can rent movies and series immediately after their theatrical release easily
from the DNA TV Rental Shop. This can be done on mobile Android devices, Chrome-
browser at dnatv.dna.fi and on the DNA Hubi device. You can also
watch the rented movie on an iOS device and Apple TV, or continue
watching on another device with the DNA TV app. If you wish, you can mirror
the movie from your mobile device to Apple TV and the big screen. You need a personal four-digit DNA TV PIN code, which you received when you subscribed, to rent. You can also find it in My Services and the Oma DNA
app.

Streaming Services

If you have, for example, a C More, V-channel package, or Viaplay subscription
from DNA, you can easily access the streaming services included in your subscription through this.

## Mirroring DNA TV using AirPlay

Through Apple TV, you can mirror live broadcasts or your recorded programs
to the big screen from your smartphone, tablet, or computer. In this case, the phone
or tablet acts as a remote control with which you can search and browse content,
play, rewind, and adjust the volume.

  * Before using, make sure that the Apple TV and your mobile phone are on the same local network.
  * Select the program you want to watch.
  * Select the AirPlay button at the bottom of the screen
  * Select the Apple TV device.
  * Continue watching on the big screen. Your device now acts as a remote control.
