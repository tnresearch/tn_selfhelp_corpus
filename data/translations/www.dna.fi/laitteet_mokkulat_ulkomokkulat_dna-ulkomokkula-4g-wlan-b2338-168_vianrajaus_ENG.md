## Troubleshooting

Why doesn't the Outdoor Modem's internet connection work?

Try restarting the Outdoor Modem. If that doesn't help, restore the device to its factory settings. Please note that the modem may not automatically set the SIM card's PIN code. Enter the PIN code on the settings pages or disable the PIN code request by first installing the SIM card in another device and removing the request.

Why won't the Outdoor Modem turn on?

Make sure the power cord is plugged into the outlet and the modem. Check that the power is switched on from the power switch.

Why did the Outdoor Modem suddenly turn off?

Ensure surge protection and wiring.

The Outdoor Modem's outdoor unit receives power from the indoor unit via an Ethernet cable. Therefore, make sure the indoor unit has power. Check the ventilation of the indoor and outdoor units for possible overheating and clean any moisture or debris from the outdoor unit.

The Outdoor Modem's speed is slow

First, check the signal strength on the indoor unit's panel of the Outdoor Modem to ensure the modem has the best possible 4G connection. If the signal is poor, adjust by rotating the outdoor unit if necessary.

Connect the computer directly to the modem using an Ethernet cable. If the speed is much better, the problem is with the Wi-Fi.
