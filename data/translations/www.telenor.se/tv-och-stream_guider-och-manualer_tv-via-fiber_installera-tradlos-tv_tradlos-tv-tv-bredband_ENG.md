# Install wireless TV if you have an Arris 2853 with a Wi-Fi extender

To get wireless TV to work if you have TV and broadband, you need
to use a Wi-Fi extender.  

Follow the instructions below.

  * ### Connect your router to the broadband

Plug it into the broadband outlet or fiber converter. Use a
network cable.

  * ### Place the Wi-Fi extender where you have your TV box

Then connect the Wi-Fi extender to the TV box. Use a network cable.

  * ### Start the Wi-Fi extender

Wait until the Wi-Fi extender's light shines with a white glow.

  * ### Press the synchronization button on the back of the Wi-Fi extender

The button is labeled **WPS**.  

During synchronization, the Wi-Fi extender's light will blink blue.
When the light turns to a solid green glow, everything is ready.  

Now you can watch wireless TV. Nice to get rid of the cable, isn't it?
