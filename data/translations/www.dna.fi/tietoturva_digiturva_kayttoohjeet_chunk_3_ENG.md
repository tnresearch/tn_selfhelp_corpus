## Device Protection

Enabling Automatic Scanning

#### Automatic Scanning on Phone or Tablet

You can configure the app to automatically check your device for viruses and other threats. When enabled, the app will automatically scan the device daily and whenever a new app is installed. Device protection is not available on iOS devices.

Here's how to ensure automatic scanning is enabled on your Android device:

1. Open the app and select **Device Protection**.

2. Select the settings menu in the upper right corner.

3. Ensure that **Antivirus** and **Scan using billed connection** are enabled.

#### Automatic Scanning on Computer

When you download Digiturva to your computer, automatic scanning is enabled during installation on both Windows and Mac computers. You can verify that automatic scanning is running by:

  * Opening DNA Digiturva. If the product's main view displays a notification that automatic scanning is disabled, select **Enable**.

Performing Manual Scans

#### Manual Scan on Phone or Tablet

You can check your device for malware whenever you want. To manually check the files on your Android device:

1. Open the app and select **Device Protection**.  
  
  

2. Select **Scan**.  
  
  

3. The scan will start and may take a few minutes.  
  
  

4. When the scan is complete, the app will notify you of the scan results.  
  
  

5. You can view a more detailed level of how many files and applications were scanned in **Security Reports**. Additionally, you will see all previous automatic and manual scans.

#### Manual Scan on Computer

1. Select Device Protection.

2. Select Virus Scan.

3. Select **Quick Scan** or **Full Scan**.

4. You can find more detailed information about scans in **Security History**.

Reviewing App Permissions

You can easily check which apps you have granted permissions to from within DNA Digiturva under Device Protection. By reviewing permissions, you can choose how extensive rights applications use and you can remove rights from applications if desired. Here's how to check app permissions:

1. Select **Device Protection**.

2. Below the threat scan, you will find the **App Privacy Overview** section. You can view the section **By Permissions** or **By App**.

3. You can view permissions at a more detailed level by selecting one of the permissions. For example, you can see that Calendar permission has been granted to six different apps, such as Gmail.

4. You can choose to view more detailed app permissions by selecting the desired app from the list. In the example, the Gmail app has been selected for review. You can see that Gmail has permissions to 4 different apps, such as Calendar, Camera, and Microphone. You can open your phone's own settings with the **Manage Permissions** button.

5. The app will directly open the view for managing the permissions of that app, from which you can remove permissions if desired. Note that some apps may not work correctly if you remove permissions from them. The example shows a view from Samsung phone settings.

Screen Lock Recommendation

DNA Digiturva will notify you if an automatic screen lock has not been set up on your device. An automatically locking screen helps keep your device protected from outsiders. If device locking is not enabled, the app will suggest enabling it and direct you to your phone's settings. Note, recommendation is only available on Android phones.

Does Device Protection Work on iPhones or iPads?

No, due to Apple's rules, Digiturva's device protection feature does not work on iOS operating systems. Apple users benefit from many other features of Digiturva.
