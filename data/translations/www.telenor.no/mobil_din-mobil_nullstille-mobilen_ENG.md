# How to Reset Your Mobile

Are you handing in your mobile or resetting it due to a problem?

Here you will find information about deleting content and settings on Android
or iOS.

## Take Care of Your Content

Your mobile contains a large amount of personal information, and before you
reset your mobile, it may be wise to back up the content you
want to keep.

We also offer services that make it easier for you to take care of both photos
and contacts. Read more about [My Cloud
](https://www.telenor.no/privat/mobil/app/min-sky/) and [See
Who](https://www.telenor.no/privat/mobil/app/se-hvem/).

## Reset Your Mobile

Resetting your mobile involves deleting all content and returning the mobile
to factory settings - as it was when it was brand new.

Once the mobile is reset, you can safely hand it in without worrying
that photos, messages or other personal information will be available
to others.

  * #### How to Reset Your Mobile - iPhone

    1. Go to **Settings**

    2. Select **General**

    3. Scroll down the page

    4. Select **Transfer or Reset iPhone**

    5. Then select **Reset**

If you have already handed in your mobile, it can also be reset by
logging in to [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/). This requires that
the mobile is linked to an Apple ID, and that 'Find My iPhone' is activated.

[How to Reset Your Mobile on
iCloud.com](/privat/kundeservice/mobilhjelp/iphone/deaktiver-find-my-iphone/)

  * #### How to Reset Your Mobile - Android

    1. Go to **Settings**

    2. Select **General management**

    3. Select **Reset**

    4. Select **Reset to factory data**

    5. Scroll down and select **Reset**

Please note that menu options may vary slightly depending on which Android
mobile you have.

If you have already handed in your mobile, it can also be reset by
logging in to [android.com/find](https://accounts.google.com). This
requires that the mobile is linked to a Google account.
