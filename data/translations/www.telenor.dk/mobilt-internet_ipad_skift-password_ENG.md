# Screen Lock and Passcode on Your iPad

The screen lock on your iPad is your security to ensure that no one has direct access to
your content, such as emails, SMS messages, or pictures.

  * ## Tap "Settings"

  * ## Tap "Passcode" in the left-hand menu

  * ## Enter your current passcode

  * ## Tap "Change Passcode"

  * ## Choose a new four-digit passcode

You must enter your new passcode twice to approve your change.

## Forgotten Your Screen Lock?

If you have forgotten your screen lock and passcode, it may be necessary to restore your
iPad.  
[See how to restore your iPad](/kundeservice/mobilt-internet/ipad/gendan-
din-ipad/ "Restore iPad")
