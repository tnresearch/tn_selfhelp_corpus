# Change Your Payment Method

During 2025, we will stop offering Betalingsservice, also known as PBS,
as a payment method for our private customers. Therefore, you need to switch
your payment method to automatic card payment or MobilePay. Since both
automatic card payment and MobilePay are fee-free, you will save 19 DKK per
month by switching. This corresponds to an annual saving of 228 DKK.

## Find Answers to Your Questions

What are the benefits of automatic card payment?

With automatic card payment, your bills are always paid on time
- just as you are used to with PBS. You also retain the easy overview, as you
receive an email each month with your payment and can easily get an
overview of all payments on Mit Telenor.

In addition, you save 19 DKK each month in payment fees. Automatic card
payment costs nothing.

What are the benefits of MobilePay?

With MobilePay, your bills are paid automatically on the 1st of each month,
and you choose whether the payment should be debited from a payment card or a
bank account – e.g. your budget account. With MobilePay, you get a complete
overview of your payment agreements, just as you know it from
Betalingsservice. If you get a new payment card and update your card details,
your payment agreements will be automatically updated.

How do I switch to automatic card payment or MobilePay?

It's easy to switch to automatic card payment.

](http://telenor.dk/betalingsmetode) or follow the guide
[here.](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/betaling/skift-til-automatisk-
kortbetaling/)

**Switch to MobilePay:** Go to [Mit Telenor
](http://telenor.dk/betalingsmetode) or follow the guide
[here.](https://www.telenor.dk/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/betaling/skift-til-
mobilepay/)

You can also get help in one of our Telenor stores or by contacting
Customer Service.

How do I see if I pay via Betalingsservice today?

On Mit Telenor, you can see which payment method you have.

  1. Log in to Mit Telenor
  2. Tap on Bills
  3. Tap on Account Settings
  4. Tap on Change payment method or card
  5. See under Current payment method

If you have Betalingsservice or manual payment as your payment method, you can
easily switch to automatic card payment by following the steps in the guide
[here](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/betaling/skift-til-automatisk-
kortbetaling/).

You will need your payment card and MitID.

Can I switch to automatic card payment if I don't have MitID?

No, you can only switch to automatic card payment if you have MitID.

I don't have MitID

It is possible to get MitID by visiting the Citizen Service, unless you are one
of the few citizens who have been assessed as exempt from using MitID.

I am exempt from MitID

Some of our other customers who are exempt from MitID have chosen to have their
subscription managed by a relative. This could be, for example, a daughter
or son, brother or sister, wife or husband. Could that be a viable solution
for you?

What if I have manual payment as my payment method?

During 2025, it will no longer be possible to pay your bill manually. This is
due, among other things, to the fact that with manual payment you can only see
your extra usage on the subsequent bill, which does not give you an accurate
overview of your consumption. With automatic card payment, you always have an
updated overview, and you are sure that your bills are paid continuously,
without you having to do anything.

We recommend that you switch to automatic card payment today on [Mit
Telenor](http://telenor.dk/betalingsmetode), or by following the steps in
the guide [here](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/betaling/skift-til-automatisk-
kortbetaling/).

When will I no longer be able to use Betalingsservice?

During 2025, it will no longer be possible to use Betalingsservice as a
payment method for our private customers. All our customers will be notified
of this well in advance, but we recommend that you switch to automatic card
payment now.

Switch to automatic card payment on [Mit
Telenor](http://telenor.dk/betalingsmetode), and get it sorted right away.

Can I continue to pay with Betalingsservice for my business?

Yes, Betalingsservice remains unchanged for our business customers.

What do I do if I want the amount to be deducted from my budget account?

If you want to ensure that your monthly payment is deducted from your budget
account, you need a payment card for the account you want to pay from.
Contact your bank, who can help you find the solution you need.

Avoid unnecessary fees by switching to automatic card payment now. On [Mit
Telenor ](http://telenor.dk/betalingsmetode) you can easily switch to a
different card afterwards. See how to switch
[here.](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/betaling/skift-til-automatisk-
kortbetaling/)

Is automatic card payment a secure payment method?

Telenor is the telecommunications company in Denmark with the greatest focus
on digital security, and therefore you will always have to approve with MitID
when you enter your payment details with us. With automatic card payment, you
receive notification before each subscription deduction, so you are never in
doubt that we are withdrawing money from your account.

Telenor does not process or store your information - payment data is
registered with NetAxept, which is approved according to international
security standards.

Why do I have to pay 1 DKK when I change my payment method?

When you sign up for automatic card payment or MobilePay, we reserve an amount
of 1 DKK to ensure that your card is active. The amount is reserved for up to
31 days, but is never deducted from your card.

When will my payment be deducted with MobilePay?

With MobilePay, your payment is automatically deducted on the 1st of each
month, and you will be notified as soon as the payment has gone through. You
can choose whether the payment should be deducted from a payment card or an
account already linked in MobilePay. You can also choose to create a new
account to pay the bills with.

I would like to pay all my subscriptions through MobilePay

If you want to pay all your Telenor subscriptions through MobilePay, simply
select "Change all" under point 3 when you follow the guide ["How to switch
to MobilePay"](https://www.telenor.dk/kundeservice/kob-og-
regning/betaling/skift-til-mobilepay/).

What do I do if I don't have money in my account on the 26th?

If we cannot deduct the money from your account on the 26th, we will try to
deduct it again on the last business day of the month. If you pay the money
via Mit Telenor in the meantime, you do not need to do anything. The system
will be updated automatically.

If we are unable to deduct the money, you will receive a reminder after 7
days.

Why does it say that my bill on Betalingsservice is unpaid?

From the 1st to the 8th of each month, the PBS bill appears as unpaid in our
system, even if it has been paid. This is because we have not yet received
the money from NETS. This is perfectly normal, and you do not need to do
anything.

What should I be aware of when I change my payment method?

With MobilePay, your payments will continue to be paid automatically on the
1st of each month, so you will not notice a big difference. If you switch to
automatic card payment, your bill will be paid on the 26th of each month
from now on - in addition, the only difference is that you save the PBS fee of
19 DKK each month.

If you change your payment method after the 15th, your payment will not be
deducted on the payment card or MobilePay until the following month. If you
switch before the 15th, everything runs as described in the
[guide](https://www.telenor.dk/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/betaling/skift-
til-mobilepay/) from the first payment.

There is an error when I try to switch to automatic card payment

If problems occur when you switch the payment method to automatic card
payment, it may be because you have entered a number incorrectly in your card
number. Always check that you have written the correct card number in the
field. The card number is 16 digits and is on your card.

If you continue to experience problems, you can call customer service on 72
100 100, who will help you get it set up.

I have received an email from Telenor, is it safe to click on the link?

As a general rule, you should always log in to Mit Telenor self-service to
change your personal information and payment method. We will never ask you to
provide information directly through a link.

If you have the slightest suspicion that the email is from a scammer, do not
click on any links.

How does a relative help me switch to automatic card payment?

If you have relatives who can help you switch payment method, you can change
it together on Mit Telenor by logging in with your MitID or code reader. If
you want to change the payment method through customer service, it is
important that you sit together, as for security reasons we will only change
your information together with you. Your relative will need a power of
attorney to make the change without you. The same applies if you would like
help in one of our stores and are not together. It is easy to fill out a
power of attorney on [Mit Telenor.](/mit-telenor/fuldmagter/)

What do I do if I don't want to transfer my subscription to relatives, even
though I am exempt from MitID?

Telenor is phasing out PBS, and that means that in the near future it will no
longer be possible to use PBS to pay for the subscription. Card payment and
MobilePay will replace PBS payments.

Unfortunately, it is not possible to maintain these PBS payments, because
Telenor is phasing out the relevant payment solution. We understand that this
causes inconvenience for customers who are exempt from MitID, but many of
these customers find solutions with the help of their relatives. If that is
not possible either, we unfortunately cannot offer a subscription at Telenor.
