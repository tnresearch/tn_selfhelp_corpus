#### E3000 - E3006, E3008, E3010 and E3100 - E3103 Problems with the T-We service

The error is due to problems with one or more T-We services. If you have recently received the error message, it often helps to try again later.

If the problem persists, you can reset your decoder to solve the problem:  
  
**Procedure:**

  1. Press MENU on the remote control

  2. Select Settings and press OK

  3. Select Installation and enter the PIN code

  4. Select Factory Settings and press OK

  5. You will be asked to reset all decoder settings and delete all recordings

    * Select Yes to delete all recordings

    * Select No to keep recordings

  6. Select Continue to confirm the reset

 _  
_ **If you do not have access to the TV menu, you can try resetting in this way:**

 ** _T-We Box II:_**

  1. Locate the RESET button on the back of the T-We Box II. This is marked with a red ring and is located between the HDMI and USB ports.

  2. Turn off the T-We Box II with the on/off button on the back of the decoder

  3. Hold down RESET while turning the box back on with the on/off button

  4. Continue holding down RESET until you see four lines appear in the display (15 - 20 seconds)  

 **T-We box:**

  1. Hold down gear + Resolution button + Down arrow, simultaneously for 6 - 10 seconds

  2. The display will then show different things, but when the display is off, the reset is complete

If the error persists, [contact Customer Service.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

#### E3007 - Audio/video

With this error code, the program card in the T-We box must be updated again. You can do this on My Pages or with SMS.

 **My Pages**

  1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/oppdater-programkort/)

  2. Select the **TV subscription in question**

  3. Select **Update Program Card**

If the rights are not updated within 5 minutes, unplug the decoder and plug it back in after 10 seconds.  
 **Note: It is not sufficient to turn off the decoder with the remote control.**

 **SMS**

  1. Send **SMS to 915 05000**

  2. Keyword **'ACTIVATE number on program card'**

  3. The number on the program card can be found in the decoder settings under Decoder Status.

If the rights are not updated within 5 minutes, unplug the decoder and plug it back in after 10 seconds.  
 **  
Note: It is not sufficient to turn off the decoder with the remote control.**

#### E9005, E9010 Failed to contact T-We services

This error message is caused by the decoder being unable to contact the T-We services. Reasons may be equipment that has been cancelled, that you have recently changed TV box or lack of payment.  
  
Restart the decoder by unplugging the power cord. Wait a few seconds before plugging the power back in. NB: It is not sufficient to turn off the decoder with the remote control. This puts the decoder into sleep mode instead of turning it off completely.  
  
If restarting the decoder does not solve the problem, write down the serial number of the TV box and contact Customer Service for further assistance.

[See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

#### E4104 There is a problem with the T-We service

Try to make the T-We box powerless for 30 seconds.  
If you still have problems, it may be that the T-We box cannot show the selected video resolution on the TV screen.  
You can easily change the video resolution by:

  1. Press MENU

  2. Select SETTINGS

  3. Under Decoder Settings, select Video Resolution

  4. Press the right arrow key to get up a list of available screen resolutions

  5. Use the arrow keys and select a lower resolution than the one that is marked and press OK

  6. Press right arrow and OK to accept the new setting.  
Press X to return to Live TV.

* * *

If the error persists, note down as much information as possible about what you were doing when the error occurred. Examples of information you can note are: title, time, date, episode, channel, etc.

Contact customer service for further assistance. [See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
