  * #### Technical Specifications

    * 1000 GB hard drive, approx. 200 hours of recording in HD quality (requires T-We Box II with recorder)

    * Up to 3 simultaneous recordings in addition to live TV (requires T-We Box II with recorder)

      * If you have the latest version of T-We Box II (ADB 3770SE), you can have 2 simultaneous recordings in addition to live TV

    * 4K resolution including HDR

    * Electronic Program Guide (EPG)

    * Dolby Digital Plus

    * HDMI v2.2

    * Optical digital audio output (TOSLINK S/PDIF)

    * Digital radio channels

    * Size: 220x190x62.5 mm (WxDxH)

  * #### Reset T-We Box II

Here you will find[ ](/privat/kundeservice/nullstill-dekoder/)[information on
how to reset the T-We Box](/kundeservice/delt/nullstill-dekoder/) via
the menu or physically on the decoder.

  * #### PIN Code T-We

The PIN code is by default set to 1-2-3-4.

Do you want to change the PIN code, or have you forgotten what you have changed it to?
[Read more about PIN codes here.](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/pin-kode/)
