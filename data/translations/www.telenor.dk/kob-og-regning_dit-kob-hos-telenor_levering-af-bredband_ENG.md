# Delivery of Internet and Phone Services Purchased on telenor.dk

You will receive a letter shortly after your order with all the details of your
internet subscription, delivery address, customer number, activation date, and the
router we are sending to you. The letter is your order confirmation.

You will receive your new router by mail at the latest the day before your activation date.
The new router is included in your internet subscription and costs 0 DKK.

**1. Check the Status of Your Order**  
You can always find the status of your order at [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/).

**2. When Will You Be Online?**  
Your broadband connection will be activated no later than 5 PM on your
activation date.

**3. Schedule a New Technician Visit**  
In a few cases, your order requires a technician visit. If this is the case,
we will let you know as soon as possible after we have processed your order.

At [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/) you can change the date of the technician visit.
