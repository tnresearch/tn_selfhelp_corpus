# Keep track of usage

Are you feeling a bit worried about mobile bills now that your children have their own phones?
Activate the right settings for the members of your family to avoid
unexpected costs and other surprises.

In My Telenor you will find settings that help you stay in control of
costs.

For example, you can **block calls and SMS to abroad** and **block
calls to premium rate numbers**, which prevents making expensive
international calls or other calls that cost more than regular calls.
**Blocking purchases in apps, games and SMS services** prevents buying, for example, SMS tickets or making donations via collections*. And if you
**block calls and SMS when abroad** it will not be possible to surf,
text or call abroad.

* For the blocking to work for purchases in apps and games, you must pay via mobile invoice in the App Store, Google Play Store and Microsoft Store. The function does not work if you use a prepaid card. [Read more about how to add a mobile invoice here.](/handla/tjanster/betala-med-mobilfaktura/ "https://www.telenor.se/handla/tjanster/betala-med-mobilfaktura/")

The settings are in My Telenor, which you can find both here [on
the web](/mitt-telenor/) and as an [app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/). Only the person listed as [owner](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/for-
familjen/medlemsprofiler/) of the subscription can change the settings, but they are also visible to other users.
