## Features:

Product Name: Huawei Echolife WA8021V5

  * Supports DNA Fiber Plus 1Gbit/s connections
  * Wireless connection: WiFi 5 (802.11ac 5Ghz 2x2, 802.11n 2.4 GHz 2x2)
  * Total of 3 LAN/WAN ports
  * 1 x 1 GB WAN port (Auto Detect)
  * 2 x 1 GB LAN ports (e.g., for a gaming console or computer)
  * IPv4 and IPv6 compatible
  * WPS, Parental Control, Firewall and QOS
  * DNA's customized settings

Sales package contents: DNA Mesh WA8021V5 modem, power supply,
RJ45 Ethernet cable, guide and quick start guide.
