# Insert SIM card

Here's how to insert a SIM card into the B535 router from Huawei.

  * ### Check that the SIM card is the correct size

It should be Nano-SIM **or** Micro-SIM.

  * ### Insert the SIM card

The router should be turned off when you insert the SIM card.  

Make sure you hold the card the same way as in the picture. Gently push
it in until you hear a click.

If you need to remove the SIM card, gently press it until you hear a
click. Then it will pop out by itself.
