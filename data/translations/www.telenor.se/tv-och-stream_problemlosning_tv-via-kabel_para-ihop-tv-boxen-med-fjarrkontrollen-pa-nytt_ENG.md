# Re-pair the TV box with the remote control

  
Here's how:

  1. Hold down the OK button and the 0 button simultaneously for at least 10 seconds
  2. Now you will see a box on the TV screen about the remote control's control mode. Press a number between 1-6 on the remote control.

If you have boxes in multiple rooms (multiroom), you must turn them off first.
