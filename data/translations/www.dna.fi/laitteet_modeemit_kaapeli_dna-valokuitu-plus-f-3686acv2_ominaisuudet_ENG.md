## Features

  * Docsis 3.0 compatible cable modem (24/8 channels)
  * Wireless connection: Wi-Fi 802.11ac/n/g 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz (3x3)
  * 4 x 1 Gb LAN ports (e.g., for a set-top box or computer)
  * IPv6 support
  * Firewall and NAT
  * DNA's customized settings
