# Help understanding the invoice

Here we explain everything that may appear on an invoice from us.

Is this your **first** Telenor invoice that you're wondering about? [Read more about your first invoice](/support/faktura/forsta-fakturan/)

### Glossary

Subscriber number

The phone number from which the calls on the invoice were made.

Installment payment

If you have an installment plan for a mobile or tablet, you will see it on the invoice. You will also see how many months you have paid off the total installment period in months.

Fixed fees

Fixed fees are the fees for your subscription and your services, for example [Trygg 48 insurance](/link/27b3a4e6adb24318a94bbb4c31f3db5a.aspx).  
  
Fixed fees are paid **in advance**.

Due date

The date your invoice must be paid and received by Telenor.

Mediated services

Mediated services can be SMS messages you sent to vote in a TV program, money you donated to charities via SMS, or a Blocket ad.

Customer number

A specific number you receive from Telenor as a customer.

OCR number

A reference number provided when paying via bank and postal giro. The OCR number refers to a specific invoice.

Plus services

Plus services can be, for example,
[Surf packages](/link/f672291cad3b43ffa33b92fbc87c3656.aspx), the [Trygg 48](/link/27b3a4e6adb24318a94bbb4c31f3db5a.aspx) insurance, or an installment plan for a mobile or tablet.

Variable costs

For example, movies from the Film Store and phone calls. Always charged in arrears.

Surf packages

Telenor Surf Packages is our service that gives you full control over your surfing costs in countries where surfing is not included.  
  
[More about Surf Packages](/link/f672291cad3b43ffa33b92fbc87c3656.aspx)

Surf pass

If you surf in a country where you _cannot_ use the data included in your subscription for free, the cost will appear on your invoice as a Surf Pass, under the heading Traffic. It is therefore a fixed price per day for your surfing.  
  
If you then purchase extra surfing, the cost of that will appear as Extra Surf Pass under the heading Plus Services.

Traffic

Traffic is a collective heading for variable charges, which are paid in **arrears**.  
  
This can be, for example, a paid call, a Blocket ad, money you donated to charity, or calling and voting in Melodifestivalen.  
  
On your **first invoice**, the period for variable charges may have started before the date you became a customer. But of course, you only pay for costs from and after the date you actually became a customer.

Trygg 48

Trygg 48 is an insurance. It has the addition Mobile or Tablet depending on which device the insurance applies to.

[More about Trygg 48](/link/27b3a4e6adb24318a94bbb4c31f3db5a.aspx)

Setup fee

The setup fee is a one-time fee charged when you purchase a new mobile on an installment plan.

The fee is charged on the first invoice after your purchase.

## Frequently asked questions about the invoice

  * Why does the invoice have a different amount than I expected?

The invoice amount can change from month to month, and it can be due to many different things, but the most common is that you have **purchased** something or **changed** something in your subscription. Or simply purchased a new subscription, as the **first invoice is usually higher** than the following ones.  
  
[Read everything about unexpected or high invoice amounts here](/link/dd9a19a5155c4628a519809bd0ec86fa.aspx)

  * Where can I find traffic specifications for mobile calls?

You can find traffic specifications for mobile subscriptions and home phones via the mobile network in the **invoice section in My Telenor**. My Telenor is available both here [on the web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx) and as an [app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

### Good to know:

    * Only the person using the number the call was made from can see the traffic specification. 
    * In accordance with the [General Data Protection Regulation GDPR](/link/4fcdd3292dde41b6b654606eaae91569.aspx), we save as little data as possible. This means that the traffic specification only shows calls that cost money, such as calls abroad and to premium rate numbers.

  * Why should I pay for calls made in an EU/EEA country?

In some countries within the EU/EEA, for example in Spain, they charge for calls to, among others, authorities and car rental companies (premium calls). So if you have been in a country where you should be able to call without extra cost but still received an invoice for calls made abroad, that may be the reason.  
  
On Berec's website ([VAS | BEREC (europa.eu)](https://eur02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.berec.europa.eu%2Fen%2Fvas-number-ranges%3Ffield_vas_country_target_id%3DAll%26field_name_description_value%3DAll%26field_vas_number_range_value%3D&data=05%7C01%7Cfelicia.wipp%40telenor.se%7Ca911513758cf4cc7347d08db5c388fc8%7C1676489c5c7246b7ba639ab90c4aad44%7C1%7C0%7C638205170217963164%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=qEeZlvsBzTOPTPdS60uxJN7hBgG5BPt9qik3%2FLXvgNE%3D&reserved=0)) you can search for which number series are premium calls in each EU/EEA country.
