# Setting up your Huawei B311

More

4G

## Written guide

**How to set up your router**

  1. Open the small door to the SIM card slot and insert your Telenor SIM card.
 **Note:** Do not use sharp objects for this, as it may damage the SIM card.

  2. Connect the power supply to the 4G router and the wall outlet. This is done as follows: turn off the wall outlet, mount the cable into the router, mount it into the wall outlet, and finally turn on the wall outlet.
  3. Note: Only use the supplied power supply for the router.

**How to connect**

  1. Turn on the router and wait until one or more of the signal indicator lights are lit (the signal indicator lights look like three "bars" that light up depending on the signal strength).
  2. Find your network name and password by looking at the bottom of the router.
  3. Search for wireless networks on your device and select your network name.
  4. Enter the network password.

Enjoy your new internet!

## What do the diodes (lights) on the router mean?

Network status indicator

Solid light:
**Cyan:** Connected to LTE network
 **Blue:** Connected to 3G network
 **Yellow:** Connected to 2G network
 **Red:** Not connected to network

WiFi indicator

**Solid light:** WiFi is enabled.
 **Blinking:** The router has detected a device that can be paired.

Network port indicator

**Solid light:** LAN is connected, but no data is being transferred.
 **Blinking:** LAN is connected and data is being transferred.

Signal strength indicator

More signal bars indicate a stronger signal.

WPS button

When WiFi is enabled, press to activate WPS or HiLink connection.

Power on/off button

Press and hold to turn the router on or off.

Reset button

Restore the router to factory settings.

LAN/WAN port

Connect a LAN device to the router to access the internet, e.g., a computer or switch. Connect the router to a wall-mounted Ethernet port for internet access.

## More settings

How to log in to the router in a web browser

You can configure the router yourself via a web browser, and this is also where you change the WiFi name and WiFi code.

  1. Connect a computer or mobile to the Telenor 4G router with WiFi or LAN cable.
  2. Open a web browser on the computer or mobile and type **192.168.1.1** in the browser's address bar.
  3. Use the password **admin**. Once logged in, you will be prompted to change the code.

Router specifications

**Router specs:**

  * 1x 1Gbit LAN/WAN port
  * 1x 1Gbit LAN port

**WiFi connection:**

  * IEEE 802.11b/g/n/ac/
