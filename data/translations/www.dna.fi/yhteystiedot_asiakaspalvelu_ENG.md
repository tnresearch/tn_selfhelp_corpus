## Call Us

#### Customer Service

Mon - Fri 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM Sat 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM

At the beginning of the call, you will be served by the digital DNA Assistant. Start by telling the Assistant what your matter is. After that, the call will be connected to a human customer service representative.

Outside of opening hours, you can inquire about a forgotten PUK code, as well as make a theft, loss, or fault report.

Having trouble with a device or service? If it concerns, for example, a modem or another device you purchased from DNA, please have the device at hand when calling. We are available 24/7 for fault and disturbance situations.

Calling and waiting in the customer service queue costs the same as a normal domestic call. When calling from abroad, the call will be charged according to the international price list. Calling from a DNA landline subscription is free.

[Call 044 144 044](tel:%20044144044)

#### DNA Non-Urgent Line

Mon - Fri 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

The DNA Non-Urgent Line is intended for all DNA customers who need non-urgent customer service and guidance, for example, due to advanced age or vision, hearing, or other limitations.

Calling and waiting in the customer service queue costs the same as a normal domestic call. When calling from abroad, the call will be charged according to the international price list. Calling from a DNA landline subscription is free.

[Call 044 144 244](tel:%20044144244)
