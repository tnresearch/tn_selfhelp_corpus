# What are e-Invoice and Direct Debit?

On this page you will find help on how to set up Direct Debit or e-Invoice. You can also combine both, and in this way avoid fees for paper invoices.

With e-Invoice, the invoice goes directly into your online bank instead of as a paper invoice in the mail, and with Direct Debit it is paid automatically on the due date. Easy for you and smart for the environment!

[Direct Debit](/kundeservice/faktura/avtalegiro-og-efaktura/#avtalegiro)[e-Invoice Reservation](/kundeservice/faktura/avtalegiro-og-efaktura/#reservasjon)

Setting up Direct Debit/e-Invoice

**If you want to set up Direct Debit or e-Invoice, you can easily do so yourself in your online bank**

Direct Debit and/or e-Invoice is a simple, inexpensive and environmentally friendly way to pay invoices.

  *  **Direct Debit** - An agreement where the invoice amount is automatically debited from the customer's bank account on the due date

    * The agreement is created and managed via online banking/the bank. To activate the agreement, you need the KID number from your latest invoice (14 digits that you find on your invoice)

  *  **e-Invoice** - An agreement where you receive the bill directly in your online bank and you only need to approve the e-Invoice in your online bank for the amount to be debited

    * You must turn on _Yes to All / Always e-Invoice / Automatic e-Invoice_ via online banking for the service to be activated

  *  **Postal Giro -** An agreement where the giro slip is signed and sent by post after agreement with your bank

    * If you need a giro slip from Telenor, please contact customer service

## Getting Started with e-Invoice

To get e-Invoice, you must give a general acceptance for this in your online bank or payment app.

This is called _"Yes to All", "Automatic e-Invoice", "Always e-Invoice" or "e-Invoice from all"_ , depending on which bank or payment app you use.  
Once this is activated, you will automatically receive e-Invoices on future invoices from everyone who offers this - in other words, you only need to **order it once!**

* * *

The way you order e-Invoice changed in _December 2021_, and after this, only Yes to All is valid for ordering e-Invoice agreements.  
**From 01.03.2024, banks will stop sending the old e-Invoice agreements.**
If you do not have Yes to All, you will receive the invoice on paper in the mail after this date.

You can read more about how to resolve this here: [Changes to e-Invoice agreements.](/kundeservice/faktura/avtalegiro-og-efaktura/endringer/)

## How does it work?

After you have accepted Always e-Invoice, the invoices will go directly into your online bank instead of in the mail. The e-Invoice will be in your online bank until it is approved by you.

After you have approved, the payment is completed. If you also have Direct Debit, you do not have to approve - then the payment happens completely automatically.

You also don't lose track: With e-Invoice, you can still go into your online bank and see all your invoices.

**Postpone payment with e-Invoice  
** If you need to postpone the payment, you can change the due date in your online bank after you have arranged for [payment deferral on My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/faktura/).

**Avoid paper invoice fee  
** If you set up e-Invoice, you avoid the fee for paper invoices (6.50 NOK).

### Can you opt out of e-Invoice from certain companies?

Yes, you can enter a reservation in your online bank.

**If you want to opt out of e-Invoice from us, here are the providers you can choose from:**

  *  **_Telenor Norge AS_** __ - Used on invoices for mobile, landline or broadband

  *  **_Telenor Norge AS TV/BROADBAND_** - Used on invoices for TV and broadband

We will then send the invoice by post, and inform you that you will be charged a fee for paper invoices.

If you need more help or guidance on how to enter a reservation, please contact your bank.

## Getting Started with Direct Debit

In most online banks, you will be offered to set up Direct Debit after you have paid an invoice. If you say yes to this, the agreement will be registered. If you need help setting up Direct Debit, your bank can help you with this.

## How does it work?

Direct Debit means that your invoice is paid automatically on the due date. This happens by us sending a copy of the invoice to your bank, and the bank debiting the amount from your account for you.

Remember that it is smart to have e-Invoice in addition. If you only have Direct Debit without e-Invoice, you will still receive a paper invoice in the mail. With e-Invoice, you still have access to your invoices in your online bank.

**What if I don't have sufficient funds in my account?  
** If you do not have sufficient funds in your account, the bank will attempt to debit the amount from your account for the next four business days. If you still do not have sufficient funds in your account after that, you must pay manually.

## Are you experiencing problems with activating Direct Debit or e-Invoice?

  1. Direct Debit - Delete the agreement  
e-Invoice - Turn "Always e-Invoice/Automatic e-Invoice" on and off in your online bank

  2. Try ordering again in a couple of days. If the problem persists, you must contact your bank.
