## Removal of Your Own Connection

If you want to remove the copper cable that goes into your home, this should
be done at your own expense. You are free to engage a professional actor
to perform the work for you, or use Telenor's partners
OneCo, Eltel or Netel. During execution, the cable must be cut down, and the end
secured to the pole. The cable must then be delivered to an approved receiver.

[Here you will find contact information for OneCo, Eltel and
Netel](/kundeservice/kabelnett/arbeid-eget-nett/) as well as an overview for your
county.

## Telenor's Remediation Program

Telenor is planning regional remediation of buildings, equipment, overhead cables and
poles that will no longer be in use or are planned for reuse in the modernization of Telenor's mobile and fiber network. Plans for removal of
old equipment will be made so that the remediation will take place in an environmentally friendly,
planned and economically sound manner. We therefore cannot remove specific
lines or poles on request.

The National Communications Authority, NKOM, has however decided that Telenor is not
permitted to remove existing infrastructure before the earliest autumn
2025 due to other actors who today use the copper network to deliver
broadband services. A process is underway to clarify Telenor's opportunity for
possible earlier removal of infrastructure.

Information about this [can be found on NKOM’s
website](https://www.nkom.no/aktuelt/nkoms-tilsyn-med-telenors-avvikling-
av-kobbernettet-gar-inn-i-en-ny-fase).

We can therefore not indicate a time for removal of poles and equipment right
now.
