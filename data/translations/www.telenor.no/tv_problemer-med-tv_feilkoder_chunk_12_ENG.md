#### E3408Remote Control is Not Paired with Decoder

To pair the remote control and decoder, hold down MENU and OK on the
remote control until the DECODER button flashes.  
If this doesn't work - Charge the remote control and try again.

For further help, see [more information about the remote control for T-We Box
II](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/fjernkontroll/)

?

#### E3410Reserved from Voice Search

The error code is due to you having opted out of using voice search. If you wish
to cancel the reservation, you can turn on personal recommendations on [My
Pages.](/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/)

[Read more about personalized content and reservation against
recommendations.](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/anbefalinger/)
