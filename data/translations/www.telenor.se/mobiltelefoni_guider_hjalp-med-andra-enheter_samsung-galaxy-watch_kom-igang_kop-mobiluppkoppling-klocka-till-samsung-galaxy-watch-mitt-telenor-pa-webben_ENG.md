# Purchase Mobile Connection for watch (My Telenor on the web)

Follow the steps below to purchase the Mobile Connection for watch service for Samsung
Galaxy Watch and Google Pixel Watch. Once you're done, you can leave your mobile
at home next time you go out.  

Keep this in mind first:

  * You must be a private customer
  * You must have verified yourself as the owner of the subscription and have BankID
  * The watch must support e-sim and be connected to the internet
  * The person who will use the service must have another device ready to scan a QR code with

  * ### Log in to My Telenor here on the web

If you haven't created a My Telenor account, you must do so first.  

[Go to My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/)

  * ### Select My overview and then the subscription it applies to

  * ### Scroll down to the heading Paid services

  * ### Select the Mobile Connection for watch service

  * ### Then you will receive instructions to help you purchase and activate the service. Follow them!
