# TV Guide for T-We App

In the TV guide, you will find an overview of all programs that are being broadcast or have been broadcast on live TV. Here you will find tips and information on how to use it.

## How the TV Guide Works

You open the TV guide by pressing **TV Guide** when you open the app.

The TV guide shows program information for all channels up to 14 days in advance. When you press the program title, icons will appear indicating whether it is possible to start the program from the beginning, or if it is [available in the weekly archive](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/ukesarkiv/). For example, if you want to start a program from the beginning, you can look for this icon: ↺ and Start from the beginning.

You can also [start recording programs](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/opptak/) that are currently airing and programs that are coming up in the future directly from the TV guide.

## Useful Options in the App

Here you can see which options control the individual functions in the TV guide.

At the top of the page, you can choose whether to view the TV guide for **All TV Channels** or **Favorite Channels**.

Select the day from the drop-down menu at the top.

You can switch between channels by swiping right or left, or go directly to the desired channel by pressing the squares at the top right.

See which programs are coming up, or have already been broadcast, by scrolling down or up under the desired channel.
