# You have paid with your mobile

If you have made purchases and paid with your mobile during your last billing period, you have made a payment to a third party. This will appear on your bill under "Payment with mobile" and may cover the following:

  * Mobile content services (competitions, ringtones, games, etc.)
  * Purchase of bus and train tickets (when you show the SMS receipt as a ticket)
  * Purchase of goods via mobile (theater tickets, DVDs, books, etc.)
  * Purchases in the App Store or Google Play (apps, in-app purchases, licenses, subscriptions, etc.)

On [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/ "My Telenor") you can see a detailed list of SMS numbers and your transactions. If you are unsure about the recipient, you can try googling the SMS number and find information about the recipient.

At Telenor, you also have the option to pay for your purchases in [App Store](/shop/landingpage/mobilbetaling-app-store/) and [Google Play](/shop/landingpage/mobilbetaling-google-play/) on your bill. These purchases will be charged to your monthly bill from Telenor.

## You are protected by Consumption Max

When you choose to pay with your mobile, you are protected by Consumption Max. This means that you can purchase content services for a maximum of 750 kroner per day or 1,500 kroner per week and a maximum of 2,220 kroner throughout your billing period.

Content services are everything you can pay for with your mobile. It can be anything from emergency aid donations, SMS competitions on TV, to bus tickets and parking, for example.

The Consumption Max service is automatically included in your subscription and costs 0 kr.
