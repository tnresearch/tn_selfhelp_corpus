# Why am I receiving an invoice even though I have cancelled my subscription?

You will receive at least one invoice after cancelling your subscription,
and in some cases two. For example: You call and cancel your subscription in
January and have a one-month notice period. Then you will receive your final
invoice in February. The final invoice includes any variable costs, which are
always invoiced in arrears. And if you have an installment plan for a mobile
phone, it will be paid as a lump sum on the final invoice.  
  
During your notice period, you can still use your subscription.
Any variable costs that do not have time to be included on the February
invoice may appear on a final invoice in March, i.e. one month after you
have ceased to be a customer with us.
