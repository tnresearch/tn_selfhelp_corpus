# Submit Your iPad for Repair

If you can't solve the problem yourself, just drop off your iPad at a Telenor store. The store staff will ensure it is sent securely and quickly for repair. We will contact you as soon as your device is back from repair and ready for pickup. You can easily borrow a phone while yours is being serviced. [Find the nearest store here.](/find-butik/ "Find store")

Once the order is created, you will receive an SMS and an email with your repair number. If the repair is not covered by the warranty, you will also receive an email with a price quote. Payment must be made before the repair begins. In some cases, you may be asked for a copy of your invoice.

## 90% of all repairs are returned within two weeks

Since all damages are different and often require an experienced repair technician, the store staff unfortunately cannot assess how long the repair of your specific device will take. But the vast majority are repaired within two weeks.
