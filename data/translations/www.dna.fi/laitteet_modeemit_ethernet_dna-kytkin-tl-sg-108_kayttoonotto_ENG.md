## Getting Started

The DNA Switch TL-SG108, to be placed in the switch cabinet, allows you to share
your internet connection and internal network to all ethernet outlets in the
apartment. In addition to the switch, you need a WiFi modem to create WiFi and
the internal network. This quick guide will help you install the switch in the
switch cabinet. You can also use the switch without a switch cabinet to increase
the number of ethernet ports on your WiFi modem. You can find the instructions
for your WiFi modem in the instructions included with the device.

### Installing the Switch in the Switch Cabinet

Place the switch in the switch cabinet or nearby. The switch cabinet is usually
located in the hallway or basement of the apartment.

Install the WiFi modem in the room with the best WiFi signal. Connect an
ethernet cable from the WAN port of the modem to the wall socket in the room
(image 1.2). Go to the switch cabinet and bring the internet connection to the
modem by connecting the apartment's internet input (image NOUSU 1) with an
ethernet cable to the room where you placed the WiFi modem (image HUONE 1.2).

Now WiFi and internet are working. Next, the internet will be brought to other
ethernet outlets in the apartment. First, connect any of the modem's LAN ports
with an ethernet cable to the ethernet outlet in the same room (image 1.1).

Connect the port from the WiFi modem (image HUONE 1.1) in the switch cabinet to
any LAN port on the switch.

Choose the rooms to which the internet will be connected via ethernet cable. In
the image, the internet is connected to both connections in room two (HUONE 2.1
and 2.2). The internet works wirelessly via the WiFi modem and via the switch
through the ethernet outlet in each room.
