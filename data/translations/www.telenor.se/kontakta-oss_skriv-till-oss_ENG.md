# Write to us

You'll always get the fastest response if you call us at **[020-222
222.](tel:020222222)** But if you prefer to write, here's how:

Use the messaging function in **[My
Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx)**. We can answer
all your questions there.

The messaging function in My Telenor is great when you have specific questions about your
subscriptions, but are unable to call. All you need to do is
log in and we'll help you there.

You can also use the contact form below to get in touch with us.
However, when using the form, we will only be able to answer a limited number of questions
and at a general level. This is due to the Swedish Post and Telecom Authority's rules
regarding identification.
