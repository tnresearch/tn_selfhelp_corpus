## Written guide

**How to set up your router**

Before you set up your router, a fiber box must be installed in your home. If you don't already have one, it will be installed during your technician visit or sent to you by mail. You can follow the process [here](/mit-telenor/ordrer/order-details/).

If you are already a customer with us and have ordered fiber, you must disconnect your old router before you can set up the new router.

Once you have ensured that you have a fiber box on the wall and that your old router is disconnected, you can begin the setup. Follow these steps:

  1. Plug the router into the power outlet - this is done as follows: turn off the power outlet, mount the cable into the router, mount it into the power outlet and finally turn on the power outlet
  2. Connect the network cable to the blue port (WAN) on the router and connect the other end to the fiber box. See which port you need to use here (link)
  3. Turn on the router and wait for the top light on the side to turn green, which means the router is online. Do not turn off the router while it's doing this

If the top light does not turn green, check that the cable and power are correctly connected. Contact customer service if the top light still does not turn green after you have followed the above steps.

**How to get online wirelessly with your devices**

  1. Find your network name (WiFi Name) and password (WiFi Code) by looking under the bottom of your router
  2. Search for wireless networks on your device and select your network name (WiFi Name)
  3. Enter the password (WiFi Code)

**How to get online with a cable**

  1. Plug a network cable into one of the yellow ports
  2. Plug the other end of the network cable into your device

Enjoy your new internet!

## **Written guide**

**How to set up your router**

  1. Connect the gray plug into the gray DSL port on the router
  2. Connect the other end of the plug into the telephone jack on the wall. If you have a three-prong telephone jack, you must use the three-prong adapter. Connect the adapter to your main jack and then the telephone cable to the adapter. If you live in a house and have multiple telephone jacks, the main jack is usually the one closest to the road. Sometimes there may be a TDC or TeleDanmark logo in the jack. If you have a double jack (three-prong telephone jack at the bottom and KAP jack above), it also indicates that it is a main jack. If you live in an apartment, the main jack is most often found near the back stairs or on the back of the building if there is no back stairs
  3. Connect the power plug and turn on the router

**How to get online wirelessly with your devices**

  1. Find your network name (WiFi Name) and password (WiFi Code) by looking at the bottom of your router
  2. Search for wireless networks on your device and select your network name (WiFi Name)
  3. Enter the password (WiFi Code)

**How to get online with a cable**

  1. Plug a network cable into one of the yellow ports
  2. Plug the other end of the network cable into your device

What the LEDs (lights) on the router mean

The router has two lights: the top one shows whether WiFi is connected, and the bottom one shows whether there is a connection. During startup, the bottom LED (WAN) briefly lights up **white**.

  * If the bottom light (WAN) lights up **red**, there is no physical connection to the internet.
 **What you can do:** check that the cables are correctly connected and pushed in all the way.

  * If the bottom light (WAN) is flashing **orange**, there is a physical connection, but no IP address.
 **What you can do:** Try resetting the router by pressing a pin or similar object into the reset hole at the bottom of the router. This must be done while the router is turned on, and the reset button is held down for 12 seconds.
 If the reset is performed correctly, the LEDs will start flashing about 10-20 seconds after the reset button is released.

  * If the bottom light lights up solid **green**, the router is receiving the IP address and is online.
 **What you can do:** Check that your devices are connected to the correct WiFi and that the cables are pushed in all the way.

  * When the top light lights up **green**, WiFi is turned on and turned off if the light is off. It flashes if WPS is enabled

## More settings

How to log in to the router (e.g. to change WiFi name and password)

To log in to the router, it is required that a connection to the router has been established from the device used for login.

In a browser, type **http://10.0.0.1:2033** and press **Enter** to open the login page.

**Username:** admin
**Password:** is on the underside of the router and is called "Router Code".

How to change WiFi name and password

  * Log in to your router by following the guide 'How to log in to the router'. Then select the menu item **WiFi**
  * Under **WiFi Settings** select **WiFi 2.4 GHz** or **WiFi 5 GHz** - depending on which network you want to change the name of
  * Note: if you do not want 2 different networks, it is recommended that you name them with the same name and password. This way, the router has the opportunity to move your device to the network that is best in terms of signal strength and speed

  * Once you have entered the new information, press **Save**
  * Note: If you have been connected via WiFi, your device will be logged off the network and you must log on with the new information

How to set up Port Forwarding

  * Log in to your router by following the guide 'How to log in to the router'. Then you must select the menu item **Network**
  * Then select the **Port Forwarding** tab
  * Enter the port information to be forwarded, as well as the MAC address to be forwarded to. Note: IP addresses must NOT be entered here
  * Select **Create**

How to read the signal strength of your WiFi

  * Log in to your router by following the guide 'How to log in to the router'. Then you must select the menu item **Connected Devices**
  * On the overview of connected devices, find the device you experience is slow or has dropouts, and click on the name
  * Here you read the signal strength (**Signal Strength**). If the value is in the range **-20 to -50**, it indicates that the device is receiving a good signal. From **-50 to -70** the signal will be acceptable, but you may experience that the received speed is lower. A signal strength from **-70 to -100** indicates that there is a low signal between the router and the device you are measuring on. This can be experienced in the form of WiFi dropouts or very low speed.

You can get good advice on how to improve your WiFi coverage in your home [here](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/).

How to connect devices to the router via WPS

If you have a device (printer, smart device, WiFi extender, etc.) that needs to be connected via WPS, this is done as follows:

  * Place the device you want to WPS-pair in immediate proximity to the turned on router
  * On the side of the router there is a button called **WPS**. Press once and briefly on this. If WPS pairing is enabled, the bottom LED on the router will flash. You now have 2 minutes to complete the following steps:

  1. Press the **WPS** button on your own device and wait up to two minutes
  2. WPS pairing is now complete

Router specifications

**Ports:**

  * 1x 1Gbit WAN port - Used when connecting to Fiber modem.
  * 4x 1Gbit LAN ports
  * 1x DSL port (only on DG2200/DG2400)
  * 1x USB 3.0 port

**WiFi connection:**

  * IEEE 802.11b/g/n 2.4 GHz
  * IEEE 802.11ac 5 GHz
