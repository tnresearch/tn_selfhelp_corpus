# Cannot rewind or pause previously broadcast programs

Here's how to watch a program that has already been broadcast.

Unfortunately, it is currently not possible to rewind/pause programs on 4an, 7an or 12.

  * ### 

Click the TV icon on the remote control and select any channel.

  * ### 

When watching a channel, click **OK** and then " **Guide** " located in the
bottom right corner of the TV screen.

  * ### 

You will then get a menu on the left side where you first select the date, then
select the program you want to watch and click **OK** on the program.
