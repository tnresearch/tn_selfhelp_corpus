## Troubleshooting

The modem's indicator lights are off, even though the power cord is connected.

The modem is not receiving power. The transformer or the modem itself may be faulty.

Make sure the power cord is connected as instructed and that the outlet has power. If the problem persists, bring the transformer and modem to a DNA Store for replacement.

All the internet connection indicator lights are on, but you cannot access the internet.

The modem may be frozen.

Disconnect the modem's power cord for about a minute. Reconnect it and restart the modem. Wait for the indicator lights to come on and check if the connection works. Also, try restarting your computer, or try connecting it to the modem with an Ethernet cable.

All the internet connection indicator lights are on, but the computer displays a message "Limited connection".

The modem is not connecting to the internet.

Check the connection and make sure all connections are properly connected. Also, make sure the network settings of the computer or other device connecting to the internet are correct. If necessary, restore the modem to factory settings, disconnect the power cord for about a minute, and then try restarting it.

All the internet connection indicator lights are on, even though the settings have been checked.

The firewall may be blocking communication. Check the firewall settings on your computer.

The broadband connection keeps disconnecting.

The modem may be connected to the phone jack incorrectly.

Make sure the modem is connected to the first phone jack and that a filter is in between.

All the internet connection indicator lights are on when using a separate router.

The router or other device sharing the connection may be frozen or faulty.

Connect the modem to the computer directly with an Ethernet cable, or use wireless WiFi. Contact the router manufacturer or seller to find out about a possible fault related to it.

WiFi is not working.

If the internet works with an Ethernet cable but not wirelessly, the problem is with the WiFi network.

Restart the modem, check the settings, and try [WiFi tips](https://www.dna.fi/tuki-wifi). If the problem persists, contact customer service.

The broadband indicator is off.

The phone line (RJ11) is not properly connected to the modem, filter, or phone jack.

Check the connections between the filter, phone line, and modem. If the Broadband indicator is still off, contact DNA.
