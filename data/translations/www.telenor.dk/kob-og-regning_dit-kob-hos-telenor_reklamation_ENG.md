# You have a 2-year warranty

When you shop with Telenor, you have a 2-year warranty. Your warranty
applies from the day you receive the item.

The warranty applies if the item is defective when you receive it, or
if it suddenly breaks due to a manufacturing defect. The warranty does not
cover if, for example, you drop your mobile in a glass of water or it is
physically damaged in another way.

If your item is defective, you must submit it to one of our stores. The staff
will send your item to our supplier Care1, who handles our repairs.
Here, specialists identify the defect and whether your warranty applies. It
is also the specialists who decide whether your mobile phone or tablet should
be repaired or replaced.

**2 year warranty**

A 2-year warranty is provided on completed repairs, and when replacing the
item, we reset your warranty. So save the receipt from the workshop.

Once the workshop has repaired the damage, your item is sent back to the store
you submitted it to.
