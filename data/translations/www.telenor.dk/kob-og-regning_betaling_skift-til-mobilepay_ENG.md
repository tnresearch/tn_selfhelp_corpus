# Switch to MobilePay

On My Telenor, you can change your payment method to MobilePay. Your payment
will be debited on the 1st of each month, and you can choose whether it should be debited from
a card or an account.

Follow the guide, and you'll be up and running quickly

  1. Log in to [My Telenor](http://telenor.dk/betalingsmetode)
  2. Press "Change payment method"
  3. Select "Create payment card or pay with MobilePay" under "Choose new payment method" and press "Continue"
  4. Press "Continue" to be redirected to MobilePay
  5. Enter your phone number
  6. Check that it says Telenor A/S and 1 DKK (the amount ensures that your card is active, it will never be debited)
  7. Swipe "Confirm and start" and you will be redirected to My Telenor
  8. Confirm your email and phone number on My Telenor and press "Confirm"

Voila! You are now registered for payment with MobilePay

If you want to pay with a different card or account, press "Change"
before confirming the payment.

Note that if you sign up for MobilePay after the 15th of the month, the invoice
for that month has already been created. Your payment will therefore only be
debited automatically from next month. If you have previously paid your bills
manually, you must therefore still pay your bill yourself until stated
otherwise on your bill.
