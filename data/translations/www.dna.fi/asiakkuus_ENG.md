## Cancelling Products and Services

Channel packages and program libraries

  * By logging in to the dna.fi website and then searching for the service in
the Entertainment section
  * By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

DNA TV subscription

  * By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

DNA TV service

By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

Speech and data subscriptions

Cancelling DNA phone and data subscriptions can be done in the following
ways:

  * By logging in to the dna.fi website and then searching for the service in
the [subscriptions and services](https://kauppa.dna.fi/tili/liittymat-ja-palvelut)
section
  * By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

Broadband

By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot) or
submitting a cancellation request in the chatbot

SquareTrade Device Protection

  * By logging in to the dna.fi website and then searching for the service in
the [subscriptions and services](https://kauppa.dna.fi/tili/liittymat-ja-palvelut)
section
  * By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

DNA Security Package, DNA Identity Security, Freedome VPN

By logging in to the dna.fi website and then searching for the service in
the [subscriptions and services](https://kauppa.dna.fi/tili/liittymat-ja-palvelut)
section

By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

DNA Browsing Security

You can cancel DNA Browsing Security in the [My DNA
app](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus)

If you have DNA Browsing Security with a prepaid subscription, it is managed
at <http://prepaid.dna.fi/>

Deezer

By contacting [customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot)

## Cookies in DNA services

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device when you visit
DNA's digital services or mobile apps. It is a numerical, anonymous
identifier that does not harm your device or files, but helps us develop our
services.  
  
  
[Read more about DNA's privacy](https://corporate.dna.fi/tietosuoja)
