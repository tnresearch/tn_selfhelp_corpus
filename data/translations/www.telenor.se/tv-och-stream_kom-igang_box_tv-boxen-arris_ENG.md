# Getting Started with the Arris TV Box

Here you can watch a video on how to install the TV box. Or you can
follow the text guides.

To watch the video, you need to [approve cookies for
marketing purposes.](javascript:OneTrust.ToggleInfoDisplay\(\))

## Connect the TV Box

  1. Connect the HDMI cable between the TV and the TV box.
  2. Connect the network cable between the TV box and either the broadband outlet, service distributor, or router.
  3. If you have a service distributor, connect the cable to the port labeled Tv. If you don't see any labels, you can see a table with which port suits you here: [Connect to the correct port](/support/portar/)
  4. Connect the power cable between the TV box and a power outlet.
  5. Turn on the TV box by pressing the On button on the back of the box.

## Connect the TV Box with the Remote Control

  1. Start by inserting batteries into your remote control.
  2. Make sure you have the correct input selected on your TV. You do this by selecting the button on your remote control labeled Source, Input, or AV. The button may also be marked with an arrow symbol. Then select the HDMI input to which the box is connected. To find the correct input, follow the HDMI cable from the TV box to the TV. For example, if the cable is connected to outlet 1, you should select HDMI 1 on your TV.
  3. Follow the instructions on the TV screen. Press Ok and the blue button simultaneously.
  4. Enter the four-digit code that appears on your TV screen with the remote control. Be careful and make sure each digit is registered.
  5. Done! Now your TV service should start automatically.

## Important things to consider

Cables

Use the cables that came with the box. Older cables may have a different current strength
than what your new TV box needs.  
  

Broadband

Make sure you are connected to the internet before starting the TV box. If you have also
purchased broadband from us, make sure to install it first: [Get started with your
broadband here.](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/)  
  

Placement

Your TV box does not need to be visible as it is powered by radio signals from
the remote control. However, the box should not be in a closed cabinet, or on top of
other electronic equipment, as it may overheat. Leave at least 20 centimeters
above for good ventilation.

## Not working?

  * Check that you have selected the **correct HDMI source** with the remote control.  
  

  * Also check that the **cables are in the correct outlets** and are securely plugged in.  
  

  * **Restart** the service distributor and router. Wait a few minutes, then restart the TV box.  
  

  * If you still can't get it to work, or if you get the error message "Set-top box is not registered" – [contact us.](/support/old-kontakta-oss/)
