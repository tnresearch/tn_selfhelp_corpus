# Connecting Your WiFi Router - Fiber

# Step-by-Step Installation

# 1\. Connect the network cable between the port labeled WAN on the router, and
the fiber box.

# 2\. Connect any other network cables you may have to the router (LAN).

# 3\. Connect the new power supply and turn on the router.

# 4\. When the top light is green, the router is connected to the internet.

# 5\. Turn the router upside down.

# 6\. Send an SMS with the keyword “wifi” to 2000 with the eight-digit code that
is on the label.

# 7\. Follow the instructions from the received SMS and press the WPS button on the router.

# 8\. You will receive a new SMS with the network name and password.
