## Top Up Prepaid Card

**You can top up your prepaid card in several ways:**

  *  **Online** : [Top up prepaid card online](/mobilabonnement/kontantkort/lade/)

  *  **In** **My Telenor** you can use Vipps to top up your prepaid card

  *  **Top-up Card** : Top-up cards or electronic top-up codes in the form of a receipt are sold in most grocery stores, gas stations and kiosks

Call the top-up phone on number **915 00 900** and enter the code you are given on the receipt.

[Top up prepaid card at telenor.no](/mobilabonnement/kontantkort/lade/)

## Check Prepaid Card Balance

**You can check your prepaid card balance in several ways:**

  *  **My Pages** : Log in to My Pages via the link below, select the number in question. The balance is on the right.

  *  **My Telenor:** Balance is displayed on the main page in the app

  *  **Telmi:** Ask the chatbot Telmi, and you will get an answer

  *  **Account Phone** : Call the account phone on number **915 00 500**

  *  **SMS:** Send BALANCE to 2525

The service is free of charge.

[Check balance on My Pages](/mitt-telenor/mobil/)

## Blocked prepaid card or lost call credit?

The prepaid card is valid for 15 months after the last top-up. If you do not top up the prepaid card within that time, any remaining top-up amount will be deleted and the number will be blocked.

The number can be reactivated for up to 2 months after it was blocked. If you want to reactivate the number, you just need to top up any amount, and it will be opened automatically.

Did you have call credit that has been deleted or has it been longer than 2 months? Contact us on customer service, and we will help you!
