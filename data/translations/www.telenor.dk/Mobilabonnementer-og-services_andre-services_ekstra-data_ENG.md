# Extra Data

If you run out of data on your mobile, you can choose to purchase extra data.

If you are in Denmark, you can alternatively continue at a significantly reduced
speed (64 Kbit). You can choose to wait until the next billing period, where you
will automatically be allocated data for use in Denmark, Roam Away and Roam Away+.

When you have used your data allowance, you will receive an SMS with a link, from which you can directly
via your mobile purchase extra data. You can use the extra data immediately.

## Extra Data and Prices

If you choose to purchase extra data, the money will be deducted from your next
bill. Please note that any unused data will not be transferred to the next
billing period.

## The Cost of Extra Data

  * 5 GB - 99 DKK
  * 10 GB - 149 DKK
  * 20 GB - 199 DKK 

When you purchase a data package, the purchased data can be used in Denmark, but parts of
it can also be used in the 55 Roam Away countries. See the table below for
an overview.

Extra Data | Roam Away  
---|---  
5 GB | up to 5 GB  
10 GB | up to 10 GB  
20 GB | up to 20 GB  
  
[Read more about extra data and data usage in

## Extra Data in the Rest of the World

You can continue to use data outside of the Roam Away countries, and at the same time avoid
high prices. You do this by purchasing data packages in advance, so you don't have to
worry about the price of every single MB. If you haven't purchased a package and have
roaming enabled, you will automatically receive Travel Data for 24 hours for
100 DKK daily.

[Check your next destination here](/shop/landingpage/mobil/roaming/) or [log
in to My Telenor and see your options](/mit-
telenor/abonnement/detaljer/mobil/ekstra-data/).
