# Support for Apple Watch smartwatches

We recommend familiarizing yourself with the manufacturer's more comprehensive user instructions
and device support.

[Go to Apple's support pages](https://support.apple.com/fi-fi/watch)

[Updating your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/fi-fi/HT204641)

## Enabling mobile connectivity on your Apple Watch

You can get mobile connectivity on your Apple Watch by subscribing to the DNA Multi-SIM
add-on service. Follow these instructions:

Start by opening the Apple Watch app on your smartphone.

Bring out the smartwatch and follow the instructions in the app on your phone.

This will start the process of subscribing to and
activating the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service, i.e. mobile connectivity.

Familiarize yourself with the terms and conditions and subscribe to the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service.

**Done!** Now you can make and receive calls on your smartwatch
and use the internet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I want calls and internet on my Apple Watch. What should I do?

Proceed according to the instructions on this page; the Apple Watch's own app will help
with activation and eSIM activation.

How fast is the internet on the Apple Watch?

When your phone subscription has the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service, you will surf the internet on your smartwatch
at a speed of 10 Mbit/s; you can listen to running music directly from the internet –
the limit will not be reached, as the secondary card's internet is also unlimited.

The speed of the internet on the main subscription, i.e. your phone, is determined by the type of phone subscription
accordingly, but there is no data cap for the internet on either device.

Please note that the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service does not work abroad.

What does it cost to use the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service?

The DNA Multi-SIM add-on service is charged according to the current price list
connection and monthly fee. Payments are charged on the main subscription's invoice. Charges based on the use of
linked secondary cards are invoiced on the main subscription's invoice and billing principles. For example,
if calls on the main subscription are billed by usage at €0.07 per starting minute,
calls made on the secondary card are billed using the same
billing principle. If the main subscription's monthly price includes a specified
size package of call minutes, calls made using the device with the secondary card
will consume the same package.

I have switched operators. How do I get my Apple Watch onto a new subscription?

Both the iPhone and the smartwatch must be on the DNA network. If your smartwatch has previously been on another operator's network, do the following:

  1. Open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone.

  2. Tap the My Apple Watch tab and select "Mobile Data". 

  3. If you want to add mobile connectivity to your Apple Watch, tap "Add a new subscription" and follow the on-screen instructions. If a previous subscription is still visible in the Apple Watch app, you can remove it yourself.

If you are setting up the smartwatch for the first time, follow the instructions on this page.

If I want to remove the Apple Watch subscription, how do I do it?

Do the following:

  1. Open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone. 
  2. Select the My Apple Watch tab and "Mobile Data".
  3. Tap the "Info" button next to the mobile subscription at the top of the screen.
  4. Select "Remove [operator] subscription". Confirm by tapping again.

If you no longer want to use mobile connectivity on the smartwatch, remember to also cancel
the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service with DNA customer service.

When you clear the Apple Watch and remove the device pair, you will also see an option
to remove the subscription from the Apple Watch. Remove the subscription when prompted
by selecting "Remove All".

What should I do if my phone or smartwatch is lost?

If you have a location service or a "Find My Device" app, use it and
temporarily close your subscription for safety. The add-on services of the subscription
(e.g. DNA Multi-SIM add-on service) will also close temporarily. Do the following:

**Lost phone**

The most important thing when a phone is lost is to lock the device with remote locking and close
the subscription of the lost phone
[in self-service](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#) or
[through customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).

If necessary, it is also advisable to restore the phone to factory settings, i.e. reset the device's data. Resetting the phone sounds final, but if
the data has been saved in the cloud, it is safe and easily recoverable to your phone.

If you are not sure if the phone has been used, remember to also change
the passwords of your most important services.

If you have the DNA Multi-SIM add-on service on your subscription, it will close
temporarily when the subscription is closed. Please note that this will also cut off mobile connectivity
from the secondary device – another phone or smartwatch.  
  
**Lost Apple Watch**

With the "Find My..." app, you can locate and protect not only your phone and tablet, but also your lost Apple Watch smartwatch. If you have already
enabled [Find My iPhone](https://support.apple.com/fi-fi/HT205362)
in the iPhone paired with the watch, it is automatically available to you
also on the Apple Watch. Read more instructions on Apple's
[support pages](https://support.apple.com/fi-fi/HT207024).

If your smartwatch has had mobile connectivity, remember to temporarily close the DNA
Multi-SIM add-on service as well. We will do it for you, contact DNA's
[customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).
