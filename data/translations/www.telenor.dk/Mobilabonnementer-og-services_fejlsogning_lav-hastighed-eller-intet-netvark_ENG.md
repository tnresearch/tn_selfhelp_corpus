# Slow Speed or No Network

Has your internet speed suddenly dropped, or have you completely lost signal on your mobile? Try fixing the problem yourself by going through the list below.

  * Restart your mobile and leave it turned off for 2-5 seconds. Turn the mobile back on and let it start up
  * Update your mobile to the latest software. Go to your mobile's settings and see if a new update is available for your mobile
  * Check our [service status](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/) for temporary problems with the mobile network
  * Check our [coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/). Maybe you are in an area where coverage is poor?
  * Check if there is coverage where you are. Maybe you are in a place where the signal has difficulty getting through. This may be the case if, for example, you are in a basement or a house built of concrete and glass
  * If you have the option to remove the battery from your mobile, try doing so - remember to turn off the mobile first. Wait a moment and put it back in the mobile. Now try turning on the mobile
  * If you have an iPhone, you can try resetting the mobile's network settings: Select **Settings** → **General** → **Reset** → **Reset Network Settings**

# Is Your Speed Still Slow?

Call our customer service on 72 120 000 if your connection has not improved. We can best help you when you have your equipment at hand when you call.

## Where Can I Report If I Experience Poor Mobile Network?

If you experience a poor network, it would be a great help to Telenor if you report it to us. You can do this through the [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/)-app.  

You can download My Telenor on [App Store here](https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/mit-telenor-dk/id1228187905) or on [Google Play here](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.telenor.self_service).
