## Warranty Terms

The warranty is the period during which the warrantor is responsible for the functionality of the device.
The warranty is limited by certain conditions, and generally, assembly, material, or quality defects are repaired under warranty.
Normal wear and tear, defects resulting from software updates, or failure to follow terms and instructions for use are not covered by the warranty.
If the serial number has been removed from your device, warranty service or replacement cannot be performed.

  * Please familiarize yourself carefully with the warranty terms.
  * During the warranty period, the validity of the warranty must be proven with a purchase receipt or certificate.
  * A defective device must always be delivered for service as quickly as possible after the defect is discovered during the warranty period.
    * If a defect causes the device to not function according to the user manual, its use must be stopped immediately.

**Generally excluded from warranty**

  * Consumable parts, such as batteries and protective coatings. (Batteries wear out and often have a limited warranty for manufacturing defects)
  * Mechanical or cosmetic damage, such as scratches or dents
  * Disadvantages caused by normal wear and tear or aging
  * Damage caused by accidents, misuse, or use contrary to the user manual
  * Damage caused by lightning strikes
  * Water damage with limitations (check the warranty coverage for water damage in the device's warranty booklet or on the manufacturer's website)
    * IP-rated devices are not waterproof, but water-resistant. Normal wear and tear, external damage, seawater, or chemicals can affect the device's water resistance
  * Devices that have used non-original manufacturer spare parts or have been repaired by yourself or by an unauthorized party
    * If you have had your device repaired through insurance or [device protection](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/palvelut/laiteturva), confirm the device's warranty status with the service provider or an authorized service center before repair.
  * Accessories included with the device (e.g., headphones, batteries, etc.)
  * Compatibility issues with third-party software or problems resulting from not installing software updates
  * Data contained within the device

If your device's warranty has expired or the defect cannot be repaired, it may be defective in accordance with the Consumer Protection Act's liability for defects. The assessment of liability for defects is affected by, among other things, the assumed lifespan, price, and the purpose for which the device was purchased. Please note that normal wear and tear is excluded from this.
Contact our customer service if you want to find out if your device is subject to liability for defects.
