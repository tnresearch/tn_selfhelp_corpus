# Lost or stolen mobile or tablet

How unfortunate that your mobile or tablet is missing! Here we describe how you
can find and/or lock your mobile, and how you replace what has been lost.

  * ### Find and/or lock the device

Start by using the manufacturers' smart ways to find and/or lock your
mobile or tablet.

**iPhone/iPad:**[Find My iPhone](https://support.apple.com/sv-se/HT201472)

**Samsung:**  
** If you have a Samsung account: [Find My
Mobile](https://www.samsung.com/se/apps/find-my-mobile/)  
You can also use Google's service: [Find My
Device](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6160491?hl=sv)

**Sony, Huawei and other Android devices:**[Find My
Device](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6160491?hl=sv)

  * ### Block and/or order a new SIM

Do you want to ensure that it is neither possible to call to or from the mobile? Then you
should block your SIM card/e-SIM. You can also take the opportunity to order a new one, if you
think you will not get your mobile back. You do all of this in [My
Telenor](/mitt-telenor/) here on the web. Click your way to your mobile subscription
and you will find the functions under the heading "Manage SIM".

You can also block your subscription by [contacting our
customer service](/support/old-kontakta-oss/). If you call outside of opening hours
you can block the subscription yourself by using the voice response system.

  * ### Logged in with TelenorID on the device?

If you are logged in with TelenorID on the missing device, we recommend that you
[log out of it.](/support/hjalp-med-inloggning/)

  * ### For those with Trygg 48 insurance

If you have Trygg 48, you will receive a new mobile or tablet within two days
from when your damage report is complete. If you are located
[abroad](/support/vanliga-fragor/skada-och-forlust/vad-ska-jag-gora-om-det-
hander-nagot-med-min-mobil-utomlands/) we cannot guarantee that you will receive a new
device within that timeframe, but we will send your new device as soon as your
damage report is registered and complete.  
  
By clicking the link below, you will be taken to our partner where you
make your damage report. They also handle any questions about the damage report and
can be reached at [+46 10 80 85 098](tel:+46108085098).

[Damage Report](https://www.squaretrade.eu/se/telenor/)

### For those who do not have Trygg 48

Contact your insurance company to see what your home insurance covers.
