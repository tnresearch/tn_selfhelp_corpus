# Late Payment and Collection

Here you will find information about late payment fees, suspended subscriptions, and collection.

 **Payment Reminder  
** If you do not pay your invoice by the due date, you will receive a
payment reminder. You will also be charged a fee for the reminder in addition to the
original amount. The fee will vary slightly depending on the service.

 **Collection  
** If you do not pay the payment reminder within the deadline, the invoice may be sent to
collection, and another fee will be added. When the invoice goes to collection,
the collection agency takes over the claim from Telenor, and you must pay the bill to them.

If the invoice remains unpaid for too long, your subscription may be suspended. How
long it takes varies for mobile, internet, and TV.

## How to Reactivate a Suspended Subscription

  * #### Reactivate Suspended Mobile Subscription

 **Pay by Card  
** For quick reactivation of the subscription, you can pay the **outstanding invoice
directly on the collection agency's website.** If you pay the invoice by card or
Vipps, the subscription will be reactivated within 60 minutes.

 **Pay Invoice via Online Banking  
** If you pay everything to the collection agency via online banking, the subscription will be
reactivated when the collection agency has received the payment. From the time you have paid
the bill in online banking, it usually takes up to 24 hours before the subscription is
reactivated.

Unfortunately, we cannot reactivate the subscription any faster even if you send us
proof of payment, as the collection agency must notify Telenor.

  * #### Reactivate Suspended Broadband or TV Subscription

If the signals on your TV or internet have been suspended, you must pay the
outstanding invoice to get them back. The subscription will be reactivated as soon as
payment is received by us. For internet, you must also pay a
reactivation fee, which will be added to your next invoice.

Invoices that have gone to collection must be paid directly to the collection company.
The subscription will be reactivated when the collection agency has received the payment. From
the time you have paid the bill in online banking, it usually takes up to 3 days before
the subscription is reactivated.

Unfortunately, we cannot reactivate the subscription any faster even if you send us
proof of payment, as the collection agency must notify Telenor.
