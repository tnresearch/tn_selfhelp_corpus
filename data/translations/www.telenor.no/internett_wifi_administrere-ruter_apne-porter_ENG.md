# Open Ports

Here you will find information on how to open ports on your wireless router.

### What is port forwarding or opening ports?

In short, it's a way to give others access to contact a computer or device on your network from another external network.

Your router will normally restrict this access to make you more secure, and therefore you must open or forward ports when you want to allow this type of traffic.

 **NB!** Remember that opening ports involves a security risk. Before you do this, you should be sure that the service exposed on the Internet is secured in a good way. You should avoid opening ports if you are unsure of the security risk involved.

### When do I need to do this?

There are few cases where you need to open or forward a port in the router. For everyday use on the internet, you can manage fine with the default settings, but there are some exceptions. The most common are in connection with computer games, IP cameras or similar devices.

  *  **Computer Games:** If you are going to "host a server", that is, facilitate the game to be run via your network. In this way, others can connect to your "server" and you can play together. NB: Most games do not require you to do this.

  *  **IP Camera:** These can be cameras you set up to monitor the entrance area, e.g. at the cabin. The camera itself does not require any port opening or forwarding, but if you want access to the camera over the Internet, you must set up the possibility for this.

# How to do it

Before you start creating the rules, you need to know which ports you are going to open. Often you can find these by searching for the game or device online.
