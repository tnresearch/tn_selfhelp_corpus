# Mobile Coverage in Sweden

Telenor's mobile network covers more than 99.9 percent of the Swedish population and over 90 percent of the country's area. This is a result of our investment in expanding the mobile network.

Telenor's mobile network covers more than 99.9 percent of the Swedish population and over 90 percent of the country's area. This is a result of our investment in expanding the mobile network.

For the majority of the Swedish population, we now offer high-speed 5G, or 5G+ as it is also called. 5G+ can provide speeds of up to 1000 Mbit/second and will reach more and more people.

  
  
Coverage Map

On the map below, you can see the coverage we have today on each mobile network. Please note that we will shut down the 2G and 3G networks on December 1, 2025, while simultaneously expanding our 5G network. [Read more here!](/om-telenor/digitalisering/varfor-stanger-vi-3g-natet/)  
  
Keep in mind that it is never possible to guarantee coverage at a single location as it can vary depending on many factors that our calculations do not take into account. [Read more about coverage](/support/driftinformation/mobiltelefoni-och-mobilt-bredband/vad-som-paverkar-mottagning-och-hastighet/) and surfing speed.

5G offers top speeds that are 10 times higher than 4G and networks that give you proper coverage.

2G and 3G Networks are Being Shut Down

We are shutting down 2G and the remaining 3G on December 1, 2025, and simultaneously expanding 5G. If you know that you are using technology that only works with 2G or 3G, it is high time to review your mobile phones, routers, alarms and repeaters. [Please contact us](/support/kontakta-oss/) and we will help you find the right solution.

[Read more](/support/vanliga-fragor/3g/varfor-slacks-3g-natet/)

Reception and Speed

There are many factors that can affect the coverage you have and how quickly you can surf. Did you know, for example, that if you use handsfree and place your mobile on a table, the reception can be improved?
