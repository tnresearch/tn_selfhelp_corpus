## Questions and answers about coverage abroad

What should I be particularly aware of when I am on a ferry?

When sailing on a ferry in international waters, the mobile network
doesn't always reach, and you will be connected to a satellite network, which charges the price of
consumption outside of our influence. You should therefore make sure to turn off mobile data
and roaming. Instead, use any available WiFi on board.

What happens when I turn mobile data on and off?

When you turn off mobile data, apps and services are prevented from using
the mobile network to connect to the internet.

When mobile data is turned on, apps and services use your mobile data connection,
when there is no available WiFi network, or if you have turned off the WiFi-
connection.
