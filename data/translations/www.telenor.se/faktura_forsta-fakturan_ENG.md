# Your first invoice may be higher than expected

Your first mobile invoice from us may be higher than the invoices you receive
afterwards. This is because you are paying for more than 30 days on the same
invoice.

How the first invoice works

### Month 1

You purchase and start using the subscription.

### Month 2

At the beginning of this month, you will receive your first invoice. It covers the costs for
month 1 and month 2. The amount depends, among other things, on when in month 1 you start
using the subscription.

### Month 3 and onwards

From now on, you pay month by month.

**Do you still think the invoice seems high?** It may be because you have changed
in the subscription, or purchased or used services that are not included. [Read more about
higher amounts than normal](/support/vanliga-fragor/faktura/varfor-har-fakturan-
ett-annat-belopp-an-jag-forvantade-mig/?epiprojects=1423)

Change payment method

If you want a payment method without an invoice fee, you need to switch to autogiro
or e-invoice.

[How to switch to autogiro](/support/faktura/autogiro/)

[I want to switch to e-invoice](/support/faktura/e-faktura/)

More questions?

Perhaps you are wondering how to easily see your invoice or know if it has been paid?
We have compiled that and much more.

[More questions and answers about the first invoice](/support/faktura/)
