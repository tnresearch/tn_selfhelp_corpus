#### G3000, G3007

Error messages G3000 and G3007 are usually due to temporary errors that resolve themselves.

#### G0131

**Error code G0131 is usually caused by problems with the network connection to your Apple TV.** Check if your internet connection at home is working.

If the internet is working normally, try turning off your Apple TV by unplugging it and leaving it without power for about 1 minute before plugging it back in.

If that still doesn't work, try restarting your modem/router in the same way.

If restarting both the Apple TV and the modem/router does not solve the problem, please [contact us at Customer Service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) at 915 09000 to investigate the problem further.

#### G3200

**If you are experiencing this error message, it may be due to several reasons.**

The error may, for example, be related to your location and that Telenor does not have the rights to show the content where you are geographically.

  1.  **Are you located outside the EU?** If so, the error code is due to the fact that Telenor does not have the rights to show what you are trying to see outside the EU.

  2.  **Router settings:** We have found that some 3rd party routers have built-in functionality (such as Content Filtering) that will cause problems. The recommendation is to adjust / turn off this function in your router, as well as ensure that you use Telenor's DNS settings.

If this does not help, the error can often be resolved by trying again a little later. If the error persists, try closing the T-We app completely and restarting it. If you still experience problems, you can contact Customer Service for further assistance. [See opening hours and contact information.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
