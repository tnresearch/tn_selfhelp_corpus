# Comprehensive 4G or Mobile Network

## What is 4G?

4G is so-called fourth-generation mobile technology that enables
versatile communication: calling a friend, WhatsApp messages with a group
of friends, following your favorite YouTuber, or even watching Netflix. 4G
can be used in a phone or tablet, or even as a fast internet connection
for your home via a modem.

In addition to the continuous development of the 4G network, DNA is
already building the fifth-generation mobile network, 5G.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is mobile broadband?

Mobile broadband is an internet connection that is always with you,
practically a SIM card with a data subscription. You can put the SIM card
directly into a tablet or modem, which can share the connection wirelessly
to multiple devices.

What is network speed and why does the speed vary?

Mobile network speed can be divided into two parts:

  * **Download speed** tells you how quickly data can be downloaded from the network. In a 4G network, the download speed varies depending on, for example, the user's subscription, device, and location. The normal range for a 4G network is 5-80Mbit/s.
  * **Upload speed** tells you how quickly data can be sent to the network. As with download speed, many factors also affect upload speed. 

There can be several reasons for speed variation:

**Signal quality.** Especially in sparsely populated areas, the base station
may be far away, and therefore the signal quality is poor. Poor quality
could be compared to a TV connection: If the TV signal is too weak,
interference will occur in the picture. In the case of mobile broadband,
interference causes reduced transfer speeds. You can improve the speed by
placing the modem near a window or using an additional antenna.

**Large number of users**. If a large number of users are using the network
at the same time within the area of one base station, the network speed
may drop. This is easier to notice in the evening, when network usage is
at its highest.

**Device in use.** 4G network only works if the device is 4G capable.
Make sure you are in an area where the 4G network works and that your
subscription and device support 4G. On your device's information, the 4G
network may also be indicated by the abbreviation LTE.

What is WLAN and how does it differ from mobile networks?

The range of a WLAN network is often very limited and may be limited to,
for example, only one apartment. 4G or 5G, on the other hand, offer
connections throughout Finland and you can move freely within the network
area.

WLAN stands for wireless data transfer technology. It is also often called
WiFi. WLAN support is included in almost all devices that use an internet
connection: computers, smartphones, game consoles and tablets. WLAN has
also become common in home electronics, such as smart TVs and alarm
systems. If you want a WLAN device to connect to the internet, it must be
connected to a WLAN access point, often called a router. The WLAN access
point must have an internet connection. In this way, the WLAN access point
shares the connection forward to the WLAN device.

What do the abbreviations mean: 4G LTE, DC-HSPA, 5G?

4G is a general term for fourth-generation mobile technologies. In DNA's
network, 4G is talked about in connection with LTE technology. The data
transfer rate enabled by LTE is clearly better than in a 3G network.

DC-HSPA, or Dual Carrier, doubles the speed of the 3G network. However,
Dual Carrier is not the same as 4G LTE network. If the phone is in the
area of a Dual Carrier network, it will automatically select the faster
network for use.

[5G](/5G-verkko) is a general term for fifth-generation
mobile technologies.

I heard that DNA will close its 3G network by the end of 2023. What does
that mean?

DNA intends to close its 3G network by the end of 2023. With the shutdown,
frequencies and resources will be released for use by 4G and 5G connections
and other new technologies, to the development of which DNA will transfer
the released resources. By the end of 2020, only just over 2% of the
mobile data used by DNA's customers was transferred over the 3G network.

The background to DNA's decision is, in particular, enabling a better
customer experience through technical renewal. 4G and 5G networks offer
significantly more capacity, reliability and speed than the 3G network to
meet the growing need for mobile networks. With the dismantling of the 3G
network, more frequencies will also be released for newer technologies.

The 3G network shutdown does not primarily require action from customers,
such as replacing the phone, as almost all devices operating in the DNA
network already utilize 4G or 5G networks. Old devices that cannot utilize
4G or 5G networks will automatically switch to the 2G network after 2023.
DNA is also aware that, for example, some trail cameras or payment terminals
used by corporate customers operate primarily on the 3G network. However,
these devices are already starting to be old, and their updating is likely
to become relevant anyway by the end of 2023. If such old devices are still
on the network at that time, they will switch to using 2G data.
