At Telenor, we process data about you and how you use our services.
We have access to a large amount of data and it is important to us that you
feel confident that we use your data in accordance with applicable
data protection legislation.

  
Telenor is covered by both the rules of the Data Protection Regulation regarding the processing of
personal data and the rules on privacy according to the Electronic Communications Act.

Telenor Sverige AB (org. no. 556421-0309) is the data controller for
the use of your personal data as described in this privacy policy
and takes place under the Telenor brand. Some of our services we offer
together with one or more partners. Telenor and our
partner may then be jointly data controllers. Read more about [joint
data controller responsibility](/support/integritet/integritetspolicy/#gempers) below.

As a data controller, we are responsible for the use of your data and
we have appointed a Data Protection Officer who helps us ensure that the use
takes place in accordance with data protection legislation. If you have questions about our
use of your data, you can contact us or our Data Protection Officer.
