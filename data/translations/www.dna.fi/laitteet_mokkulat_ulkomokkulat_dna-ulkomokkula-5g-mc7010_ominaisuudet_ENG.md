## Features

### DNA Outdoor Modem 5G MC7010

**Manufacturer:** ZTE

**Model:** ZTE MC7010 5G Outdoor CPE

**Processor:** Qualcomm SDX55 Arm Cortex-A7 (1.4 GHz)

**SIM Card:** Nano-SIM

**Connections and Connectivity**

  * Interface Type: Gigabit Ethernet 2.5GE
  * Ethernet (LAN) Ports: 1 pc
  * Network Technology: 5G and 4G LTE
  * Supported 5G Frequencies: 700/800/1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz
  * Supported 4G Frequencies: 700/800/1800/2100/2600 MHz
  * DL 5G 4x4
  * MIMO UL 2x2 MIMO

**Network Features**  
(Figures are theoretical maximum speeds, actual speeds depend on
the strength of the received signal, the capabilities of the mobile network,
and the type of subscription used)

  * 5G maximum download speed 1.65 Gbit/s
  * 5G maximum upload speed 250 Mbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum download speed 1.6 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum upload speed 150 Mbit/s

**Compatibility**

Using the device with a wireless local network requires a connection to a
separate WiFi router equipped with an Ethernet WAN port (e.g., Google WiFi).
Without a separate WiFi router, you can connect only one device to the
Outdoor Modem at a time by connecting the device to the Outdoor Modem's LAN
port with a separate Ethernet cable.

**Sales Package Contents**

  * DNA Outdoor Modem 5G MC7010
  * PoE power adapter
  * 10-meter Ethernet flat cable between the Outdoor Modem and the power source
  * Adapter kit for existing Ethernet cable run (incl. short ethernet connection cable)
  * Accessories for wall and pole mounting
  * Quick Guide

**Other Features**

  * Support for ZTE Link app (Android and iOS)
  * Indicator lights: 5G/4G connection, WAN signal strength, LAN port and power GPS positioning (reports location in latitude and longitude) Reset button
  * Operating conditions: Temperature -40 °C - 55 °C, humidity 5 % - 95 %

**Water Resistance**

Water and dust resistant construction (IP65 rating) Surge protection (6 kV, no
grounding)

  
**Dimensions and Weight**

  * Height 188.8 mm
  * Width 106.6 mm
  * Depth 29 mm
  * Weight 500 g

**Warranty**

Manufacturer's warranty: 24 months
