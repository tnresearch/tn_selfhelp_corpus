# Report Network Issues on Mobile

If you are experiencing outages or other problems, we encourage you to contact
customer service, who will attempt to resolve the issue. You are also always welcome
to share your experiences with us through the My Telenor app, where your input
is highly valuable in making general improvements to the network.

Please be aware that when you share your experience with us via the My Telenor app,
your case will not be fixed. If you need issue resolution, you must
contact [customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt/).

Before reporting an issue, you should always check our [service status](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/
"Service Status") and you can check the expected coverage on our
[coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/
"Coverage Map") in advance.

Download the My Telenor app for free from the [App
Store](https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/mit-telenor-dk/id1228187905 "My Telenor
app - App STore") or [Google
Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.telenor.self_service
"My Telenor app - Google Play").

We are glad this article was helpful.

Thank you for your feedback.
