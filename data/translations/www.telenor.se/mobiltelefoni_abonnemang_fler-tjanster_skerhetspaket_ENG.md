# Unique Security Package Included in Your Subscription

With us, you have a truly secure mobile subscription thanks to our unique
security package – here you can read more about everything it contains. For those of you who have the Unlimited Plus subscription, an extended security package is also included with, among other things, ID insurance.

## How to Report Online Abuse or Identity Theft

Reporting online abuse, identity theft, or payment card hijacking is done through our
partner **Help Försäkring (Help Insurance)**.

Opening hours: Monday - Thursday 9 AM - 5 PM, Friday 9 AM - 4 PM.  
Call [**0771-985 200**](tel:0771-985%20200) or email
[**info@helpforsakring.se**](mailto:info@helpforsakring.se)

Help also has the customer portal [**My Page**](https://helpforsakring.se/min-sida)
where you can follow your case, upload evidence, and communicate with your lawyer.

## What Happens After Reporting

When you report any type of online abuse, identity theft, or hijacking of payment and
credit cards via Help Försäkring, you will receive a response on the same day or by the morning
of the next business day. You will receive help and advice from a lawyer who will follow your
case all the way to court if necessary.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Security Package in Our Mobile Subscriptions

  * What is included in the insurance that provides help with online abuse?

The insurance includes:

    * 5 hours of free legal advice per year 
    * Reimbursement for psychological help up to 5,000 SEK 
    * Reimbursement for lost work income up to 5,000 SEK 
    * Reimbursement for legal costs in legal disputes up to 200,000 SEK

  * What terms and conditions apply to the security package?

Our security package is included in all new and existing mobile subscriptions regardless
of when they were taken out, with the exception of the insurance that provides help with online abuse.
The insurance is included in all mobile subscriptions taken out after 2022-05-18. If you
took out your mobile subscription before that, you can easily switch to one of
our updated subscriptions to get help with online abuse included. The insurance applies to the person who has the subscription and if several of you share
Telenor Family, it applies to all of you.

[Here you can read the full terms and conditions for the insurance against
online abuse.](/link/750c93522f4e4c5cb65e1efb9d7c9518.aspx)

  * What is included in Security Package Plus, which is included in the Unlimited Plus subscription?

In addition to the security services included in all subscriptions, those with the
Unlimited Plus subscription also receive screen insurance, ID insurance, and extended
legal protection.

  * How do I activate blocking of malicious sites?

**Activate on your mobile**

The block is activated in all our mobile subscriptions from the start. If you have turned it off, turn it on again in My Telenor here on the web. Click through to
Subscription and then to Settings and Payment Services. Or go into the app
and select Subscription and then Settings.

Blocking of malicious sites is also included in our broadband subscriptions. [See how to
activate it here.](/handla/tjanster/natskydd/)

  * How do I disable blocking of malicious sites?

We recommend that you always have the block turned on, for your own safety.
If you still choose to turn it off, you can do so in My Telenor here on the web
under Subscription and Settings and Payment Services or in the app under
Subscription and Settings.

Blocking of malicious sites is also included in our broadband subscriptions. [See how to
turn it off here.](/handla/tjanster/natskydd/)[
