# Restore Your Mobile

If your mobile is frozen and completely unresponsive, restoring it and forcing it to start from scratch can help. Restoring your mobile will not damage it.

**Important Before Restoring Your Mobile**  
When you restore your mobile, you also delete all content and all the settings you have made. We therefore recommend that you take a backup before restoring.

[Back up your Samsung Galaxy](/kundeservice/mobil/samsung-galaxy/sikkerhed-og-backup/backup-og-synkronisering1/ "Backup of your Samsung Galaxy")

Once you have backed up your data and settings, follow the guide here step by step.

  * ## Go to "Settings"

Here you select "**Backup and reset**".

  * ## Select "Reset to factory settings"

  * ## Tap "Reset device"

Remember to back up your content before resetting your mobile.

  * ## Tap "Delete all"
