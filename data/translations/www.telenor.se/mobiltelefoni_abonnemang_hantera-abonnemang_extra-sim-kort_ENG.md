# Extra SIM card

Do you need an extra SIM card for your tablet, or for your child who has just gotten their first mobile phone? Whatever the reason, you should start a Telenor Family plan!

With our mobile subscription, it is perfectly possible to start a Telenor Family plan with the ability to add up to eight users. This could be, for example, your child or a tablet. You do the same regardless of what the extra SIM card is to be used for.

You can find your subscription by logging in to My Telenor – either here [on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in [our app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/).

## Frequently asked questions about extra SIM cards

  * How do I change the PIN code on the SIM card?

**If you have an Android phone:**  
1. Go to Settings  
2. Select Lock screen  
3. Select Screen lock type and then follow the instructions  
  
 **If you have an iPhone:**  
1. Go to Settings  
2. Select Cellular and then SIM PIN  
3. Select the slider to the right of SIM PIN and then follow the instructions.
