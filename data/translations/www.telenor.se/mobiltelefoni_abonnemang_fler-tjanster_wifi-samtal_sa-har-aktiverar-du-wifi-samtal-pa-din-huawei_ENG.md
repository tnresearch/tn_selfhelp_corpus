# How to activate wifi calling on your Huawei

Once you have activated wifi calling, you will be able to make mobile calls anywhere
when you are connected to wifi. It also works when you have your phone in
airplane mode. Keep in mind that you need to be connected to a Swedish wifi network
that supports calls. Read more about what [wifi calling entails
here](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/fler-tjanster/wifi-samtal/).

  * ### Open Settings

  * ### Select Wireless & networks

  * ### Click on Mobile network

  * ### Tap on wifi calling 

You will find it under sim 1 or sim 2, depending on which SIM card you are using.

  * ### Slide the wifi calling toggle to open it and turn it blue

  * ### Tap Activate in the box that opens

The information that emergency calls do not work via wifi is incorrect. Huawei
will change this message as **emergency calls work via wifi**.
