# Getting started with e-SIM

An e-SIM means the SIM card is built into the mobile phone. Instead of inserting
a physical plastic card into the phone to make calls, you need to activate
your e-SIM. Here you will find all the help you need to get started.

## Good to know:

  * e-SIMs are not available for prepaid cards.
  * Your mobile phone needs to support e-SIM. [Here you can see what applies to your mobile](/support/vanliga-fragor/e-sim/vilka-enheter-har-stod-for-e-sim/).

## Do you want to order and activate your e-SIM?

I want to order an e-SIM

You can easily order it in the My Telenor app. Depending on whether you are the owner or
a user of your subscription, the process is slightly different. Choose below and
we will guide you correctly.

The owner is the person who signed up for the subscription and is responsible for payment on
the invoice.

A user is you if you have been added to a subscription with Telenor Family.

You can easily activate it in the My Telenor app or directly via your mobile phone. In My

activate the e-SIM. The procedure is slightly different depending on whether it is an e-SIM for the owner or if it is
an e-SIM for a user who has been added to the subscription with Telenor

If you want to activate the e-SIM directly in the mobile phone, you need to have both your new and
your old mobile phone at hand, both must be iPhones, and both must have iOS 18 or
later installed.

## Frequently asked questions about e-SIM

  * How do I get an e-SIM?

As the subscription owner, you can easily order an e-SIM yourself in My Telenor. If you
do not own the subscription, you must ask the owner to order it for you.  
  
Tip! First find out if your mobile phone can have an e-SIM. [Read about which devices
support e-SIM](/link/70cd7ea07bfc4223bdd2dbe4cac526cb.aspx)

  * I already have an e-SIM, why do I need a new one?

Because an e-SIM is associated with the mobile phone in a different way than a SIM card,
a new one is needed when you change mobile phones.

  * I scanned the QR code with the wrong mobile phone, what do I do now?

If you have scanned the QR code with a different mobile phone than the one the subscription that is
linked to your e-SIM should be used with, you must order a new e-SIM. You do this in My Telenor here [on the web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx)
or in [our app](/link/f80d97c00f0d437fbce32ae0510e86ed.aspx).

  * Can I get the QR code by email instead of mail?

No, the QR code that you activate the e-SIM with is a negotiable instrument and therefore
we do not send it by email. You can always access your QR code via My Telenor.

  * My mobile phone has dual SIM, what does that mean?

Dual SIM means you can have two subscriptions linked to the mobile phone. Perfect
for those of you who want to have your company number and private number on the same mobile phone.

How it works in practice varies depending on the mobile phone. Some have
space for two SIM cards, but you can of course also use
[e-SIM](/link/2f7e620c6914457b9712bac4372b2d0d.aspx).
