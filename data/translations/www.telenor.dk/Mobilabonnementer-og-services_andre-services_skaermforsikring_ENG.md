# Screen Insurance

With Screen Insurance, you can easily and affordably get your screen replaced if an accident happens. Screen Insurance is included in your subscription when you purchase a new mobile phone with a mobile subscription from Telenor – whether you buy it on telenor.dk, in your local Telenor store, or at Elgiganten. This applies to purchases made from April 27, 2020.

Screen Insurance is valid for three years from the date you purchased your mobile phone with a Telenor subscription, and remains valid as long as you have at least one active mobile subscription with Telenor. Screen Insurance covers one screen replacement per rolling 12-month period. For example, if you had your screen replaced in May, you can have it replaced again in May the following year.

## How to Use Screen Insurance

1. To use Screen Insurance, you must create a case with our insurance partner [Squaretrade](https://www.squaretrade.eu/dk/telenor/). If you have questions about the product or need help, call our Customer Service at 72 100 100. If the Squaretrade form itself is causing problems - contact Squaretrade directly [here](https://www.squaretrade.eu/dk/telenor/).

2. Go to your nearest [Telenor store](/find-butik/) once you have created your case. From there, we will send your mobile phone to our repairer.
3. After approximately 5 business days, your screen will be replaced. In the meantime, we offer you a loan phone if the store has one in stock. When your mobile phone is ready, you can pick it up from the store where you dropped it off. If the repairers find damage other than to your screen, they will give you a price estimate to have it repaired, and it is then up to you whether you want to use this.

## What Does Screen Insurance Cost?

The only time Screen Insurance costs anything is when you use it. When you need to have your screen replaced, you pay an excess, which depends on what your mobile phone cost when you bought it – excluding any discounts.

**The new price of your mobile phone:** 0-2,999 DKK
**Your excess:** 149 DKK.

**The new price of your mobile phone:** 3,000 – 6,999 DKK.
**Your excess:** 299 DKK.

**The new price of your mobile phone:** 7,000+ DKK.
**Your excess:** 499 DKK.

You pay when you drop off your mobile phone at the Telenor store. We reserve the amount on your payment card, but only deduct the money when the workshop has assessed the damage and started replacing the screen.

## What if the Mobile Phone Has Damage That Is Not to the Screen?

When the repairers receive your mobile phone, they first check if there is any other damage to it. If they find damage that is not to the screen, they will contact you and give you an estimate of the cost of fixing it. You decide for yourself whether you want to use that offer.

If it is necessary to repair the damage in order for it to be technically possible to replace your screen, you will also be informed of this. However, it can also happen that the repairers assess that your mobile phone is too damaged to make it worthwhile to replace the screen. This may be the case if the mobile phone is bent, or if the entire phone has been damaged. If this happens, you will get your mobile phone back without charge.

## Questions and Answers

How can I see if I have Screen Insurance on my mobile phone?

[Log in to the My Telenor app](/mit-telenor/). Find your mobile phone on the "Devices" tab – you can then see if you have Screen Insurance on it.

How do I create a case in SquareTrade?

**Submit a Claim**

Note, if you want to use Screen Insurance, you must first create a claim on Squaretrade's website.
Click [here](https://www.squaretrade.eu/dk/kundeservice/) to submit a claim:

*   Select **Telenor**
*   Select **File a Claim**
*   Select **I have my device**
*   Select Yes in **"Is your front screen cracked?"**
*   Select No in **"Does it have liquid damage?"**
*   Select the time of the accident and describe the problem. You must select **"I have my device"** and "yes" in **Is your front screen cracked?** and select **cracked back/screen**
*   Enter name and address details

You will now be forwarded to a window where you need to pay the excess. Once the excess has been paid, you will receive a confirmation email with a claim number. This must be used when the phone is dropped off at one of our stores.

Does Screen Insurance only cover the screen itself?

Yes. With Screen Insurance, you are only covered for damage to the screen. Our repairers will let you know if they find any other damage - so you can assess whether you want them repaired, but they are not covered by Screen Insurance.

What is the difference between Screen Insurance and Mobile Insurance?

In short, Screen Insurance only covers damage to your screen, while Mobile Insurance covers your entire mobile phone. Mobile Insurance also covers a wide range of different damages, e.g. if your mobile phone suddenly stops charging, if the sound does not work, or if you drop it in the toilet. And it covers no matter where in the world the damage occurs.

Screen Insurance is included in your subscription, while [Mobile Insurance](/shop/landingpage/mobiler-voice/mobilforsikring/) costs from 49,-/month to 89,-/month depending on the price of your mobile phone.

A final difference is that you can also keep your Mobile Insurance if you cancel all your Telenor subscriptions, while Screen Insurance only applies as long as you have at least one active mobile subscription with Telenor.

How long is Screen Insurance valid?

Screen Insurance is valid for three years from the date you purchased your new mobile phone with a subscription from Telenor. However, Screen Insurance stops if you cancel your subscriptions, transfer them to another person, or move your subscriptions to another provider.

What happens if I buy a new mobile phone on my current subscription?

When you buy a mobile phone from Telenor together with one of our subscriptions, you get Screen Insurance included in the purchase. If you buy a new phone for your existing subscription, you get Screen Insurance on the new phone, but lose Screen Insurance on the old mobile phone.

Do I still have Screen Insurance if I am no longer a Telenor customer?

No, Screen Insurance only applies as long as you have at least one active mobile subscription with Telenor.
