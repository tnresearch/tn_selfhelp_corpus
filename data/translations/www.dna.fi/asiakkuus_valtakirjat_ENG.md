# Powers of Attorney

A power of attorney ensures that only trusted individuals with definite permission manage your services. Therefore, a power of attorney is important – both for small and large changes.

The person you authorize can handle matters related to your services both at DNA customer service and in DNA Store stores: e.g., change subscription type, modify your orders, cancel your services, or order new ones. You can grant authority for all transactions at once or only for handling a single matter.

**Please note that processing a power of attorney may take a few business days. An electronic power of attorney is valid immediately.**

## Choose from the general power of attorney options:

### Electronic Power of Attorney

Using an electronic power of attorney depends on the services you have ordered – currently, its use is not possible in all situations. By logging into the DNA website, you can see if creating an electronic power of attorney is currently possible.

Here's how to create an electronic power of attorney

  1. [Log in](https://www.dna.fi/kirjaudu) to the DNA website by authenticating with online banking credentials or a Mobile Certificate.
  2. Go to your own information
  3. If an electronic power of attorney appears in your information, fill out and save the form. The authorization will take effect immediately and will be available to both DNA Store salespersons and customer service. You can define the validity period and scope of the authorization in the power of attorney.
  4. Can't find an electronic power of attorney under your information? Use a PDF power of attorney. Read the instructions below, under "PDF Power of Attorney".   

### PDF Power of Attorney

If you cannot find an electronic power of attorney in your customer information, print a PDF power of attorney or create one by hand by entering the same information on paper.  

Here's how to fill out a PDF power of attorney

  1. Download the PDF power of attorney to your computer.
  2. Print, fill out, and sign the power of attorney. If you do not have a printer, write the exact same information on paper as is in the power of attorney template.
  3. Scan the power of attorney or take a clear photo of it with your phone.
  4. [Log in](https://www.dna.fi/kirjaudu) to the DNA website and send the scanned power of attorney [as a message](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/yhteystiedot/asiakaspalvelu) to DNA customer service.

## Number Transfer and Number Assignment Power of Attorney

You can transfer your DNA phone subscription number to another person using a number transfer and number assignment power of attorney. Number assignment can be done either at a DNA Store or with the Oma DNA app.

Make the authorization at a DNA Store

When the transferor (the current owner of the number) and the recipient (the new owner of the number) visit a DNA Store together, the number assignment can also be completed immediately. A fee of €12 will be charged to the number transferor for the number assignment, according to the price list.

If the current owner cannot go to a DNA Store, the number assignment can also be done with DNA's official number transfer power of attorney:

[Download the number transfer power of attorney
(PDF)](http://www.dna.fi/documents/753910/3591291/DNA_numeronsiirtovaltakirja.pdf/45469213-54ec-a72c-6fa0-fd5625191e3a?t=1648470878790)

Make the authorization with the Oma DNA app

  1. Open the Oma DNA app. If you do not yet have the Oma DNA app, you can download it to your phone from the app store.
  2. Go to the "My Services" tab.
  3. Select the subscription whose number you want to transfer.
  4. Select "Subscription Management".
  5. Select "Number Assignment".
  6. Enter the required information and submit the form.

### Good to know: for the user of the power of attorney

Be prepared to prove your identity – whether you are dealing with DNA customer service or a DNA Store salesperson. Bring your ID card or passport with you – without identification, using the power of attorney will not succeed.

In the event of death, please contact DNA customer service or visit a DNA Store.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Power of Attorney

Where can I find a ready-made power of attorney?

It is recommended to use an electronic power of attorney when it is available - the electronic power of attorney is currently in use only in selected services. If you cannot find an electronic power of attorney under your information, you can download and print a PDF power of attorney. Instructions for both options can be found above on this page.

Start by logging in to dna.fi to find out if an electronic power of attorney is available to you. If it is, you will find it in your own information.

What can be authorized with a power of attorney?

You can decide how broad the authorization you want to give is – authorization for all transactions or only for a selected topic, e.g., picking up an order placed in your name via Click & Collect from a DNA Store or changing the subscription type. You can also determine the duration of the power of attorney yourself.

A separate number assignment power of attorney allows you to transfer your current phone subscription to another person's name. See instructions for using the power of attorney above on this page.

What transactions does a power of attorney not authorize?

Device installment agreements cannot be made with a power of attorney. If you want to pay for a new device in installments, the agreement for this will always be made directly with you.  
Transactions subject to guardianship can only be carried out through official channels. The guardian is appointed by the Digital and Population Data Services Agency or the court, and this cannot be reported with a power of attorney.  

Can the power of attorney be in free form?

No, but you can write it by hand if necessary. Make sure that all the same information as in the PDF power of attorney is on the paper.  

A relative of mine has passed away, can I handle their affairs with a power of attorney?

Our condolences!

In the event of death, it is recommended to contact customer service. This way you can handle the affairs of the estate.

What matters can I handle without a power of attorney?

In practice, you can only report product or service failures without a power of attorney. Even in these cases, customer information or information about the customer relationship cannot be provided without a power of attorney, so fault repair may not always be fully successful.  

Can matters subject to guardianship be handled with a power of attorney?

Matters subject to guardianship are always handled through official channels; the guardian is appointed by the Digital and Population Data Services Agency or the court, and this cannot be reported with a power of attorney.

However, it is worth informing DNA customer service of the final decision so that handling matters will be easier in the future. Let us know about the decision as soon as it is made.

How can a power of attorney already made be revoked?

Notify customer service of your request.
