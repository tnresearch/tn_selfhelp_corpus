# Payment Methods

Depending on your order, you can pay it either by online banking, invoice,
credit, or with a interest-free and installment-free DNA Payment Time. Get
to know the options!

## Payment and Payment Methods

You can pay for the devices you order from the DNA Online Store in your online bank.

## Payment by Invoice

Paying for the order by invoice requires identification either with a DNA
User ID, online banking credentials, or Mobile Certificate. You will not be
charged anything during the identification process. By identifying yourself,
we ensure secure and reliable transactions.

You can also pay for device orders from the online store in installments with
a DNA Payment Time agreement, if you wish.

Payment with DNA Payment Time

You can pay for most device orders with a interest-free and installment-free
DNA Payment Time agreement, allowing you to get up to 3 years of interest-free
DNA Payment Time if you wish. The device is then paid in equal monthly
installments, and the total price is formed by the sum of the monthly
installments.

The DNA Payment Time option is always mentioned on the product card online or
in the store for each product when it is possible for that particular order.

## Payment by Credit Card or Online Banking

You can pay for some products either by online banking or credit card at the
time of order. The payment methods applicable to each product or service are
always stated on a product-specific basis.

Card payments are secure via a protected SSL connection. Supported credit
cards are Visa and MasterCard. After filling in the credit card details, the
order will be verified in online banking, where you can confirm your purchase
with your online banking credentials. By identifying yourself, we guarantee
secure transactions.

Online banking payments are made with personal online banking credentials.

## Payment with MobilePay

You can conveniently pay for your purchases at the DNA Online Store with
MobilePay, easily with a single swipe. Here's how:

When you are finalizing your order at the "Confirm and Order" stage, select
MobilePay as the payment method.

Log in to MobilePay using your PIN, fingerprint, or facial recognition.

Approve the payment by swiping.

If you don't already have MobilePay on your phone, download it from your
phone's app store.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I would like to pay for the device in installments. What additional costs will
this incur?

You can pay for the devices in 12, 24 or 36 monthly installments if you wish.
The payment time is interest-free and there are no additional costs. The
monthly installments are billed on a monthly invoice. The total price of the
device is formed by the sum of the monthly installments.  
  

When does billing for services start?

Billing starts when the services have been activated and are available to you.
