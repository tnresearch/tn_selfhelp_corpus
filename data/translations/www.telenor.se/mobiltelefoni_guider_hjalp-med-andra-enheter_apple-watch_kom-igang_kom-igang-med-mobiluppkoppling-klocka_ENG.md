# Get started with Cellular Connection on your watch

Follow the steps below to get started with the Cellular Connection service on your
Apple Watch. Once you're done, you can leave your iPhone at home next time you
go out. Aren't you excited? Okay, let's get started.

  * ### Pair, update and set up the watch and your iPhone 

**Important!** You must have these versions **or later** before purchasing
the service: Watch operating system (watchOS): 5.1.2  
Your iPhone's operating system (iOS): 12.1.1  
Operator settings on your mobile: Telenor 35.1  
  
You may need to restart both the watch and the mobile after updating
the operating systems and operator settings. But it's quick.  
  
Feel free to follow the guide to set up the watch on Apple's site.  
[Set up Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/sv-se/HT204505)

  * ### Open the Watch app

You'll find it easily.

  * ### Click on Cellular

  * ### Click on Set up Cellular

  * ### Enter your personal code and click Log in

Here's where to find the code:

[My Telenor (web)](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/hjalp-med-andra-
enheter/apple-watch/kom-igang/hitta-din-personliga-kod-mitt-telenor-webb/)  
[My Telenor app](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/hjalp-med-andra-
enheter/apple-watch/kom-igang/hitta-din-personliga-kod-appen/)  
  

  * ### Check that you agree to the terms and press Buy and activate 

You're almost there, just a little more to go.

  * ### Click on Close

Activation takes about five minutes. You will receive an SMS when it is
complete.  
  

  * ### When it looks like this, the activation is complete.

You did great! Now you can, among other things, call and text with the watch.
Hope it works well for you.
