# Weekly Archive

Did you miss a program on TV? Find the program or browse through all the content available in the weekly archive. We're here to help you find what you're looking for.

Weekly Archive in the TV Guide: Programs that have weekly archive rights will be marked with a blue frame. You can also scroll back in the TV guide using the arrow keys on your remote control to see programs that have already been broadcast on TV. The color of the frame will then switch between blue and gray depending on whether or not there are weekly archive rights.

## Is this program in the weekly archive?

The Weekly Archive is available for some programs and channels for up to seven days back in time. These programs are highlighted with a blue frame in the TV guide. If the program is not available in the weekly archive due to rights issues, this will be marked with a gray frame.

You can find the weekly archive for channels by scrolling back in the [TV Guide or from the channel banner](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/tv-guide/) while watching TV.
