# Private and hidden number

Do you want to keep your number private, hide it temporarily, or prevent it from being found via search services? That's perfectly possible.

## Private number and hidden number for search services

  
You cannot have both of these options activated at the same time. You can either choose to have a Private number, or you can choose to hide your number on search services such as Eniro and hitta.se.

Both of these settings are available in My Telenor, [here on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or [in the app](/handla/tjanster/appar/mitt-telenor/).

  * With **Private phone number**, your number will not be displayed when you call from your mobile. Your personal details will also not be released by any directory assistance service.
  * With the **Searchable phone number** setting, you can hide your number from search services. It will then not be visible in directory assistance services such as Eniro and hitta.se. Your number may still be displayed in a potential caller ID.

## Temporarily hidden number

  
A temporarily hidden number only means that your number is not displayed when you make an outgoing call, and does not affect searchability in directory assistance. Here's how to hide your number:

  1. Open the keypad on your mobile in the same way as when you are going to enter a phone number.
  2. Press #31# and then the number you want to call. Example: #31#0701740645
  3. Call and talk.

**Please note** that the next time you make a call, your number will be displayed as usual again.
