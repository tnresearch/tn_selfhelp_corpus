# How to Search for Content in T-We

The search function can be used to quickly find content when you know what you are looking for. Here we show you how to use it.

Search Function: Start the search by holding down the microphone button on the remote control - no matter where you are in T-We.

## Search Function

The search function is always in the top menu and is marked with a magnifying glass.

Search shows content from all channels you subscribe to and all streaming services except Netflix and YouTube. For example, you can search for "Nytt på Nytt" in T-We, and get hits on the program and then open it directly in the NRK app.

For streaming services, the search applies regardless of whether you subscribe to the service or not, but you will receive information on how to add the services you do not have.

If you have T-We Box II, you can also use [voice search to find what you are looking for.](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/sok/talesok/)

### Search in Streaming Services

You have access to several streaming services from the TV menu, but search may work differently depending on which streaming service it is.

If you have chosen HBO Max as part of your TV package, it will be part of the search for all content in T-We. It is therefore not possible to search only in movies and series that are in HBO Max via T-We.

## Delete Search History

If you want to delete the list of what you have searched for, you can do this via [your profile](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/profiler/).

  1. Select profiles from the menu

  2. Select **Edit Profile**

  3. Press **Delete Search History**

## Filter for the Search Function

To make it easier to find the content you are looking for, you can use filters to narrow the search. Here you have three different sorting options: All content, genre and relevance.

Under All content you will find:

  * Movies and series

  * TV guide

  * Recordings

  * Weekly archive

Under genre, you can choose from a long list of genres, both in Norwegian and English. In addition to this, the order can be sorted alphabetically, by title, year of production, relevance or IMDb score.
