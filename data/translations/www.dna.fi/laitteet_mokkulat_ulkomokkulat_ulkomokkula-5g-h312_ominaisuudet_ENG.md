## Features

**Product Name: Huawei 5G CPE Win H312-371**

The powerful Outdoor Modem 5G H312 picks up high-speed 5G internet to your home WiFi-
router seamlessly directly from outdoors. Easy to attach to a wall or
pole.

Using the device with a wireless local network requires a connection to a separate
WiFi router equipped with an Ethernet WAN port (e.g., Google WiFi).
Without a separate WiFi router, you can connect only one
device to the Outdoor Modem at a time, by connecting the device with a separate Ethernet-
cable to the LAN port of the Outdoor Modem's power supply.

  * 5G maximum speed from the network 1.65 Gbit/s
  * 5G maximum speed to the network 250 Mbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 1.6 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed to the network 150 Mbit/s
  * Water- and dust-resistant construction according to IP65 rating
  * Functionality at -40 – 55 °C temperature
  * Protection from 6 kV voltage surge

### Sales package contents

  * Outdoor Modem 5G H312
  * Power supply and cord
  * 10 meter Ethernet flat cable between the Outdoor Modem and the power supply
  * Base for wall mounting
  * 4 screws and wedge anchors
  * Adjustable strap for pole mounting
  * Nameplate
  * Quick guide and warranty card
