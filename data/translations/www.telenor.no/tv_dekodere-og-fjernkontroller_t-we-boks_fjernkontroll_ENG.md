## How to Make Your Remote Control Faster and More Stable

Are you experiencing problems with your T-We Remote Control? Then it may be time to **_change the batteries_** and pair it with the TV box, a so-called **_pairing_**.

A remote control with a low battery level will often lose pairing with the T-We box, but will still work with IR signals (infrared light). This gives a poorer response and you have to point directly at the TV box.

When you pair the remote control and the TV box, radio waves are used to communicate, which means that the TV box reacts faster and there is no need for a clear line of sight between them.

**Here's how to do it:**

1. Turn on the TV and T-We box

2. Put the box into pairing mode. You do this by opening the front panel of the T-We box and pressing the up and down arrow keys simultaneously. You can now see the channel list on the screen and the cursor will jump back and forth between the channels. This is normal.

3. The message "Decoder is now ready to be paired with the remote control" should now appear on the TV screen

4. Put the remote control into pairing mode. You do this by holding down the Menu and OK buttons simultaneously for at least three seconds. When the **[On/Off]** button labeled **DECODER** lights up constantly, release the buttons again. The remote control is now in pairing mode for 30 seconds.

5. Now follow the instructions on the TV screen to pair the remote control and the T-We box

If the pairing worked, the remote control will now be ready to use.

* #### Use the Same Remote Control on TV and TV Box?

  1. Make sure the TV and T-We box are turned on. Turn on the sound.

  2. Hold down the number keys 1 and 3 simultaneously for three seconds, until the light around the **[TV On/Off]** button begins to shine. The remote control is now in programming mode.

  3. Hold down the Mute button on the remote control until the sound disappears and the Mute symbol appears on the TV screen. This may take several minutes, depending on the type of TV you have. As soon as the sound on the TV disappears, release the button. It is important to release the button immediately. If you release it too late, the remote control will continue to search for another TV.

  4. Test if the volume buttons work.

  5. If the volume buttons work, end the programming. Press the **[On/Off]** button on the T-We box. The **[TV On/Off]** button will flash twice.

**The remote control for the T-We box will now also work with your TV.**

* #### Program Remote Control - IR

You can program functions from other IR-based remote controls. This can be used if the functions you have already programmed do not work as expected, or if you, for example, want to add volume control from an external sound system. Almost all remote controls are IR-based. Only in special cases will the remote control not be IR-based.

1. Hold down the number keys [4] and [6] simultaneously for three seconds, until the light around the **[TV On/Off]** button begins to shine.

2. Hold/Place the two remote controls facing each other, IR eye to IR eye. The distance between them should be between 3 and 6 cm.

3. On the Telenor remote control: Press the button you want to program.

4. On the remote control you want to copy the function from: Press the button you want to copy to the Telenor remote control.

5. While holding the buttons down: Make sure the two remote controls are still facing each other, IR eye to IR eye. The distance between them should be between 3 and 6 cm.

6. When this is done, one of the following things will happen:

* If the function is received by the Telenor remote control, it will be saved in memory. The **[TV On/Off]** button will flash twice to confirm programming.

* In case of error, the **[TV On/Off]** button will flash **three times** to indicate that the programming failed.

* If the Telenor remote control does not receive any IR code from the other remote control within 30 seconds, you will receive an error indication that the **[TV On/Off]** button flashes **three times.** The remote control will automatically return to normal use mode.

**Note!** If the programmed button belongs to a command for the box, you must also press the **[Shift]** button on the remote control to use the self-programmed function.

* #### Disconnect Remote Control from TV

**Here's how to disconnect the remote control from TV and T-We box:**

1. Hold down the keys 1 and 6 simultaneously for three seconds until the light around the **[On/Off]** button labeled **DECODER** on the remote control begins to shine.

2. Enter 9 - 9 - 6.

3. The **[On/Off]** button flashes twice as confirmation that the remote control is now reset.

* #### How to Adjust the Volume on the TV Box

When your remote control is connected to both the TV box and your TV, you must hold down the Shift button while pressing the volume buttons to adjust the volume level on the TV box.

## Tips!

If you experience that the T-We box does not respond as expected, you can use the yellow button as a first solution to update the box and ensure that normal functionality is restored.

By holding down the yellow button on the remote control for at least 5 seconds, the T-We box will automatically update with the latest data from Telenor T-We.

This can also speed up the update after you have ordered new channels or streaming services in the content selector.

## Are You Experiencing Problems with the Remote Control?

* #### If the Remote Control Doesn't Work

  * Check that the T-We box is turned on. The time is displayed in the display on the box and a red light shines around the ON/off button on the box.

  * **Change the battery in the remote control** if the battery indicator lights up red when you press the remote control. The indicator is located at the bottom of the remote control, labeled "Battery low"

  * If this does not help, you should try to power restart the T-We box. You can either unplug the power cable, or use the power switch (located on the right on the back of the box). Let the box stand without power for about 10 seconds before turning it on again.

If the problem persists, [contact customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

* #### Reset Remote Control and Reconnect

**Here's how to reset the remote control to factory settings:**

This resets the remote control to its original settings. After this, the remote control will not be connected to the T-We Box.

1. Hold down the keys 1 and 6 for 5 seconds

2. Then press 9 - 8 - 1 on the remote control. The **DECODER** key should now flash a couple of times before turning off

3. Press OK

**Connect Remote Control to Decoder Again:**

1. Open the front panel of the T-We box and press the up and down arrow keys on the T-We Box simultaneously until a message about pairing appears on the TV screen.

2. Hold down the Menu and OK buttons on the remote control simultaneously for three seconds.

3. Now follow the instructions on the TV screen to pair the remote control and the T-We box.

* #### Remote Control is Slow or Unresponsive

This is often due to the battery starting to run low. When the battery status becomes too low, the radio wave connection will often drop, and the remote control will switch to IR mode instead.

To solve this, we recommend that you **first change the batteries in the remote control**, and then reconnect the remote control to the TV box.

**Here's how to do it:**

1. Turn on the TV and T-We box

2. Put the box into pairing mode. You do this by opening the front panel of the T-We box and pressing the up and down arrow keys simultaneously. You can now see the channel list on the screen and the cursor will jump back and forth between the channels. This is normal.

3. The message "Decoder is now ready to be paired with the remote control" should now appear on the TV screen

4. Put the remote control into pairing mode. You do this by holding down the Menu and OK buttons simultaneously for at least three seconds. When the **[On/Off]** button labeled **DECODER** lights up constantly, release the buttons again. The remote control is now in pairing mode for 30 seconds.

5. Now follow the instructions on the TV screen to pair the remote control and the T-We box
