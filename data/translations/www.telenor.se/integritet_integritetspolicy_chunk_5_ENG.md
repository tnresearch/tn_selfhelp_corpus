Traffic data

|

Traffic data is created when you use any of our electronic
communication services. The data is needed for us to be able to provide
the service to you (e.g. transfer calls, SMS and email) and for us to
be able to invoice you.

Examples of traffic data are information about which phone number you have
communicated with, at what time, how long the communication has lasted,
which networks have been used for the communication and where you were when you
called or were connected, e.g. to which mast you were connected.
Traffic data can also be data on how you have used/consumed
our services as well as technical data about connected devices (e.g. TV box,
router or mobile phone) such as MAC address, IP address, WiFi (SSID) and ID on
equipment.

|

The data is collected from you and/or created by you using any of
our services.

|

The data is necessary for us to be able to fulfill agreements with you.
