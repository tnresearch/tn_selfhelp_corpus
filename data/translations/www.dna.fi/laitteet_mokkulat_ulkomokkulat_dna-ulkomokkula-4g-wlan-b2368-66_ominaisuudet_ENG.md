## Features

**Product Name: Huawei B2368-66**

The DNA Outdoor Modem 4G WLAN is a modem designed for fixed outdoor
installation, providing you with powerful 4G internet access via an indoor
unit and external antenna. The internet connection is reliable even if
coverage is weak, and you can get an even better signal in areas with
already good coverage.

The outdoor modem has three 1 GB Ethernet ports, allowing you to connect a
computer, television or set-top box, and a gaming console to the internet.
AC-WLAN (2x2) promises a top speed of over 867 Mbit/s for the modem's WiFi
internal network. With this modem, you can create a WLAN connection for up
to 32 devices.

**Connections and Connectivity**

  * SIM card: Standard SIM
  * Connection type: WLAN & Ethernet
  * Maximum Ethernet connection speed: 1000 Mbit/s
  * Ethernet (LAN) connections: 3 x 1 Gbit/s
  * Network technology: 4G LTE CAT13/12 2xCA 4x4MIMO
  * WLAN: 802.11ac Dual-Band
  * WLAN frequency bands: 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
  * Theoretical maximum WLAN speed: 867 Mbit/s
  * WLAN range: excellent
  * Theoretical maximum network speed: 600 Mbit/s
  * Connection connection type: Modem

**Network Features**

  * 4G LTE CAT13 maximum speed from the network 600 Mbit/s
  * 4G LTE CAT12 maximum speed towards the network 150 Mbit/s
  * Support for 3.5Ghz frequency band
  * Support for Ipv6

**Other Features**

Comprehensive router features: bridging mode, LTE MHz lock, signal
information as lights on the outdoor and indoor units, omnidirectional
outdoor unit, POE Ethernet, updates from the internet, comprehensive
software features.

**Sales Package Contents**

  * DNA Outdoor Modem 4G WLAN B2368-66 
  * Indoor and outdoor unit
  * Power supply
  * 20 meter Cat 5e POE Ethernet cable
  * 1.5 meter Cat 5e Ethernet cable
  * Quick guide
  * Wall mount
  * Mounting brackets for pole mounting

See more detailed pictures of the package contents in the [PDF
