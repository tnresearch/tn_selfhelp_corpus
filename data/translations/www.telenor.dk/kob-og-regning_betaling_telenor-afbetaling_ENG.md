# Telenor Installment Plan

With Telenor, you have the option to split your payment into 3 smaller installments with
Telenor Installment Plan. This means you pay the 1st installment when you purchase
your new product (e.g., mobile phone or accessories) and then the remaining 2
installments in the coming months.

On this page, you can find answers to most questions about Telenor Installment Plan.

How does Telenor Installment Plan work?

With Telenor Installment Plan, your payment is divided into three smaller installments. You pay
the first payment when you complete the purchase, and then you will be invoiced for the
remaining two installments over the next two months.

Do I need credit approval for Telenor Installment Plan?

Yes, you do. But the approval is lightning fast and takes no time –
credit approval takes place in the purchase process itself, so it is simple and
easy.

Does Telenor Installment Plan cost extra?

Telenor Installment Plan does not cost you extra – you pay neither interest nor
fees.

How is the payment made with Telenor Installment Plan?

You pay the first installment in cash when you complete the purchase in
the purchase process. Thereafter, you will be invoiced for the remaining two installments
in the coming two months – the amounts will be automatically debited from the
credit card you used when you completed the purchase.

_**Note:**_  
For purchases made from the 1st to the 15th of a month: the 2nd payment will be debited in the same month.  
For purchases made after the 15th of a month: the 2nd payment will be debited in the next
month.
