# Recordings and Recording Lists in T-We

Here we show you how to use the recording function and find your recordings when you want to watch them. We also show how you can delete recordings.

Recordings: When you select recordings from the menu, you get an overview of all your recordings sorted into lists. You can also search for a specific program by pressing the Recordings List.

## Start a Recording with T-We

You can start a recording by pressing the record button on the remote control while watching a program, or you can start the recording from the information page.

You get information about the program by pressing Info or OK when you are watching the program, or you can do the same from the [TV Guide](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/tv-guide/).

If you are going to record a program that has multiple episodes, you will also be given the option to "Record Series". Then all future episodes on this channel will be recorded in addition to the one you just selected.

 **Did you know:**  
Many of the programs are also available up to a week back in time in the [Week Archive](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/ukesarkiv/). The Week Archive for channels can be found by scrolling backwards in the TV Guide or from the channel banner when you are watching TV.

## How to Find Your Recordings

T-We recordings can be found as a separate option in the top menu. When you press **Recordings** you get two lists, one with _All Recordings_ and one with your _Recent Recordings_.

You can scroll right or left in the lists, or you can open a filter to sort your recordings by pressing "See more...". This can be found when you scroll all the way to the left in the lists.

You will also find more lists sorted as buttons at the bottom, under **More Recording Lists.**

If you press See more... in the overview of your T-We recordings, a separate view will open where it is possible to sort your recordings with more filters.

## Navigate All Content with Filters

If you often record series, movies and other content, it can be difficult to find your way around the amount of recordings over time. With the filter for T-We recordings, you can sort these by time and genres such as documentary, action, news etc.

You can also search in the recording list if you know that there is a specific program you are looking for.

## Delete Recordings

Next to the filters you have a button labeled "Delete". This is what you use to mark a recording you want to delete.

Filter Selection: In the picture you see the different filter selections that are available for Recent Recordings on T-We. Here, "Delete" is selected.

Deleting: In the picture you see how to check the recordings that are to be deleted. When you have made your selections, press Confirm to complete the deletion.
