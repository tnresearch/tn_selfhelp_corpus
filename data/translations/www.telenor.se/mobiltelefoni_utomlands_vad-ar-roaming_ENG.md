# What is roaming?

Roaming comes from the English word for wandering. And that's exactly what it's
about – wandering between different networks.

## Feel at home in 39 countries!

In the summer of 2017, the EU regulation "Roam like at home" was introduced. It
means that we can call, surf, send SMS and MMS just like at home – i.e. without
extra cost – within **the entire EU/EEA**. The only thing you need to think about
is to [activate data roaming](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/andra-installningar/valj-
mobil/aktivera-inaktivera-roaming/).

If you have a mobile subscription from Telenor, you can also surf like at home in
**USA, Turkey, Andorra, Switzerland, Serbia, United Kingdom (including
Gibraltar), Montenegro, Thailand, Malaysia** and **Myanmar.**

## Roaming in other countries

Surfing abroad has long been associated with high and unpredictable costs. And
yes, it is expensive to use roaming in some countries.

So to protect you from unpleasant surprises, we have:

  * **blocked surfing** in the countries and satellite networks where roaming is
most expensive. _  
_

  * added an **automatic surfing block** to your subscription. This means that if
you surf abroad for SEK 625 during the same month, we will block you from
surfing further. Read more about [how the surfing balance lock works](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/innan-och-under-resan/innan-resan/automatisk-saldosparr-for-surf/), and how to remove the lock if you want to.
  * created **[Surf Packages](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/innan-och-under-resan/innan-resan/surfpaket-fast-pris-for-surfen/)**. If you purchase a surf package, you pay a fixed price for a certain amount of data that you can use for two weeks.

If you choose not to order a surf package but still use data roaming in the
country, you pay for a Surf Pass, which is a certain amount of data per day.
You can find the prices for Surf Passes by [looking up the country here](/roaming/),
or by [getting foreign prices as an
SMS](/support/mobiltelefoni/utomlands/innan-och-under-resan/innan-resan/fa-
utlandspriser-som-sms/).
