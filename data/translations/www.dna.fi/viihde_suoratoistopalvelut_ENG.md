## Frequently Asked Questions About Streaming Services

Does DNA offer free streaming services for the DNA entertainment box?

**These free streaming services can be downloaded to the DNA entertainment box:**

  * Yle Areena
  * YouTube
  * MTV Katsomo

How do I subscribe to a streaming service?

If you are using the DNA TV service, you can manage the subscription and administration of the streaming service directly on the DNA entertainment box. Alternatively, you can subscribe directly from DNA's sales service – either with or without the DNA TV service.

On what devices can I watch the streaming service?

On the DNA entertainment box, with the DNA TV app, or directly in a browser; on your phone, computer, or tablet.

Can I subscribe to a streaming service without the DNA TV service?

Yes, you can, contact [our sales service](/myyntipalvelu)!

What streaming services can be watched on the DNA entertainment box?

**You can subscribe to and manage these streaming services directly from the DNA entertainment box:**

  * MTV Katsomo (included in MTV channel package subscriptions)
  * Viaplay 
  * Netflix 

**These streaming services can be watched on the DNA entertainment box*:**

  * Disney+ 
  * HBO 
  * SkyShowtime 
  * Prime Video 
  * Apple TV+ 
  * Crunchyroll

*Subscription is done directly through the service provider.
