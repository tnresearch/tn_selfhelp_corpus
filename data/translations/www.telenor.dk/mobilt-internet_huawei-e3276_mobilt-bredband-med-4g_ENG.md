# Mobile Broadband and 4G Network

Our 4G network covers a very large part of the country, and therefore we only sell
mobile broadband subscriptions and equipment that are geared towards 4G.

Even if your equipment is ready for 4G, you will experience lower speeds when you
are in an area with only 3G coverage. And if you take your mobile
broadband with you to a summer house, for example, depressions or tall trees can worsen your signal.

[Check our coverage](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/
"Check our coverage")

## Do you have an old subscription?

If your subscription was purchased before March 20, 2013, you may only have equipment
to use the 3G network. A lot has happened since then, and you most likely have 4G where you live.

If you are unsure whether your equipment works with 4G, you can [contact
us](/kundeservice/kontakt/) or [visit one of our stores](/find-
butik/) with your equipment. We can quickly help you find a subscription
that suits you.
