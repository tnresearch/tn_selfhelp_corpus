# Devices and pause devices

In the app, you can temporarily turn off the connection for devices connected
to your wifi.  

Here's how:

  * ### Click on devices in the menu

Here you can see all the devices that have been connected to your network.
”Connected” means they are connected right now, while ” Offline” means the
device has been connected but is not currently.

If you want to see all devices that are paused, go to Home in the menu and
scroll down and click on "Internet Access".

  * ### Click on the device you want to pause

  * ### Click on pause
