# Follow your usage

If you want to see the usage for a specific subscription, select
”Subscriptions” from the menu, click ”View details” next to the desired
subscription, and then select ”Usage”.

If you have a mobile subscription, you can see, for example, how much data you have left.
You can also see how many SMS messages you have sent and how many minutes you have
talked. At the bottom of the page, you will also find a detailed overview of who you
have called and sent messages to.

On the right side of the screen, you can see when your usage period expires.
The usage period for private customers always runs from the 16th to the 15th of
the month.
