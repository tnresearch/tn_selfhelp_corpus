# Streaming Services in T-We

Here you will find information about the various streaming services available through the TV menu.

Streaming Services: Here you will find an overview of all the streaming services available for T-We.

## Streaming Services and Apps

Select Streaming Services from the menu at the top to get an overview of all apps and streaming services. These are divided into external streaming services and streaming services you can choose as part of your [TV package](https://www.telenor.no/privat/tv/t-we/innholdsoversikt/).

A streaming service that you do not subscribe to or have chosen for points will be marked with a "stop icon" in the upper right corner.

## Streaming Services as Part of T-We

Most streaming services can be added to and removed from your TV subscription for points. Sometimes movies and series will be loaded into T-We, and other times you will be linked to the provider's app. You can return to T-We by pressing "X" once or the "T-We button" several times on the remote control.

## Streaming Services in the T-We App

Under the main menu in the T-We App you will find an overview of all available streaming services. These are streaming services you can choose as part of your TV package.

For a streaming service you do not subscribe to, the content will be marked with "Subscribe" in the upper left corner.

### discovery+ Entertainment

If you already have discovery+, you can use the same login on T-We or create a separate account.

[Read more about activating discovery+](/kundeservice/tv/velge-kanaler/aktiver-discoveryplus/)

### Viaplay

If you have chosen V series & film or V premium in your TV package, you also have access to the Viaplay streaming service and the Viaplay Film & Series subscription.

[Follow our guides to activate Viaplay](/kundeservice/tv/brukerveiledning-t-we/strommetjenester/viaplay/)

## External Streaming Services

These are all apps you can open from the T-We box, but are not an integral part of T-We. These streaming services behave the same regardless of whether you use them via the T-We box, or use the provider's app on a smart TV, PC, mobile or tablet. Here you also use login details from the provider instead of Telenor.

External streaming services have their own search function and content that is not mixed with other content from T-We in the overviews for Movies and Series, for example.
