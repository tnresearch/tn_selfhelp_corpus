# Credit Note

If you receive a credit note or a credit balance, it means you have
money to your credit. We will deduct the money from your next bill, where the amount will
appear as "Credit from previous bill".

If you have a credit balance on your last bill after cancelling your
subscription, the amount will be automatically paid to your NemKonto (Danish bank account) 6 days after
your last bill has been calculated. If you owe anything from the past, the outstanding amount will be reduced.

You do not need to do anything yourself if you receive a credit note. You can
find old credit notes along with your bills on My Telenor.

We will not pay out the money if you owe anything on other accounts. If we do not
have your CPR number (Danish personal identification number), we cannot pay the money to your NemKonto – in
that case, we will contact you.
