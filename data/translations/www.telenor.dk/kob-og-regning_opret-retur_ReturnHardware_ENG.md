## How to Return

1

### Create a Return on Telenor.dk

Fill out our return form with the reference number found on your delivery note.

2

### You Will Receive a QR Code

You will receive an email or SMS with a QR code, which you must show at a PostNord parcel shop.

3

### Drop Off the Package at PostNord

PostNord will scan your QR code and print a return label for you. You will also receive a receipt for the drop-off.

4

### We Receive Your Package

You can track the entire package's journey and see when it arrives at our location.
