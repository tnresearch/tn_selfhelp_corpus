# T-We Box II: Information about Cables and Network Equipment

It's easy to think you can continue to use the same setup with cables and equipment as before when you change your set-top box. That isn't always the best solution.

## Have you remembered to change the cables?

Did you know that old cables can give you problems with TV signal, Internet, or picture quality? When you switch to newer equipment, such as the T-We Box II, you also switch to equipment with better quality and perhaps different technology than you had before. This also means that different requirements are placed on the cables you use to the T-We box and from the T-We Box into the TV.

## 1. Use the correct network cable

Are you constantly getting a message that the T-We box is upgrading? If this happens every time you turn on the T-We box, it's a good indication that something is wrong with the network cable.

The network cable included with the T-We Box II is labeled _"CAT.5e STP Patch cable to TIA/EIA-568.2"_

You can use any network cable that supports Gigabit Ethernet (CAT.5e or newer), but we recommend using the cable that came with it.

Do you often see this screenshot when you turn on the T-We Box II? It may be a sign of problems with the network connection. Check that you are using the correct network cable.

### Still having problems?

If you still experience the symptoms of updating on every startup, and the network cable has been replaced, it may also be due to problems with the wall socket or other network cables you are using in connection with the TV or network.

Three different network cables supplied by Telenor. Make sure you use the gray cable with yellow ends as this is the newest type with the best quality.

## 2. Does the HDMI cable support 4K?

If you have a T-We Box II, it is especially important to replace the HDMI cable with the one that came with the box. The T-We Box II supports the 4K resolution, and if you use an older cable, problems with the picture may occur when you connect it to a TV that supports 4K. Here you may experience errors in picture quality, missing picture, or [HDCP problems.](/kundeservice/tv/problemer-med-tv/hdcp-hdmi-off/)

## 3. Check other network equipment

T-We is delivered over different technologies: Cable TV or TV over Internet (IPTV). Regardless of which technology you have, we recommend using equipment and cables you have received from us when [connecting the T-We box](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/koble-til/), but it is especially important with IPTV.

If you still want to use equipment from a third party, you should be aware that the equipment must support certain functions. You also use such a connection at your own risk as we have no guidance for this beyond what you find here.

**Example -** For a switch to work with T-We, it must be of a newer date and support the following:

  * Gigabit Ethernet (speed of 1000 Mbps)

  * Multicast

  * IGMP
