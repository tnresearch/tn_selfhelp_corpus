# Delivery of Mobile and Mobile Internet Purchased on telenor.dk

Your new SIM card and any equipment for your mobile internet will be delivered to
your address within 1-3 business days by PostNord. Our express delivery costs
0 kr.

## You will receive an order confirmation by email

Your purchase is completed when you receive an email with your order confirmation. It
may take up to 20 minutes from when you confirmed your purchase until we have
registered it and send you the confirmation.

If you have not received the order confirmation or have accidentally deleted it, you can
[contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).

## Track the package with PostNord

When your package has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number by email as soon as
the package is registered. Use it if you want to track the delivery on the [PostNord
website](https://www.postnord.dk/).
