# Settings in T-We

Reset the T-We box or set up the network from settings. In the menu
you will find access to useful services and functions in T-We.

Settings: When you select settings from the menu, several options appear -
installation for setting up the Internet and decoder, and decoder settings.

## The Menu

When you select ☰ from the TV menu, you get an overview of:

  * Services

  * My T-We

  * Settings

Services gives you an overview of the TV guide, recording list and radio.

My T-We is an overview of your own choices and preferences such as profiles,
favorite lists, content you have rented from movie rental, tips and tricks for T-We
through User Tips, and the ability to change reservations for recommended content
on the Home menu.

Services, My T-We and Settings: These options appear when you select ☰
from the menu.

## Overview of All Settings

When you select Settings, you will again get several options on the TV screen.
Below we list the different options so you know what information you will find
under each one, and what you can change.

### Installation

Under this setting, you can reset the T-We box so that it is set
back to factory settings, update the software or search for
channels manually. You will also find network settings in this overview.

### System Information

Under system information you will find technical details. You will normally not
need this information, but may in some cases be asked to provide
information about software or card and serial number when contacting
[customer service by phone.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

  *  **Decoder Status  
** Here you will find, among other things, the card number on the smart card, serial number and name of
the T-We box

  *  **Build-info  
** Information about software, decoder and network

  *  **Disk-info  
** Information about hard disk and available storage space

  *  **HDMI-info  
** Information about which resolutions are supported by the TV the T-We box is
connected to

  *  **Signal Meter  
** Measures signal strength and quality of the TV signals

  *  **Smart Card Info  
** Status of the smart card, such as subscription status and card number
