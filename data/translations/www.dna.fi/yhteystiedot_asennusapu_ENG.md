# DNA Assistant Installation Help

Don't be left alone with questions when you can be two! Facing something new – whether it's DNA TV, internet, or a mobile subscription, a phone or another device – let us help you get started: by phone, chat, or on-site. User guides and practical help – you can get both!​

## Assistance via Chat

Make setup easier or let us help you choose a new subscription or device. Start a chat, we're here. ​Chat is open on weekdays from 8 am to 6 pm and on Saturdays from 9 am to 4:30 pm.

## Meet at a DNA Store

​Stop by a store! You can also meet our expert, efficient service personnel face-to-face at the nearest DNA Store.

[ Find the Nearest DNA Store ](https://www.dna.fi/dnakaupat)

#### Services at a DNA Store:

You can get help with setting up a new device and subscription in the store. Some services are free, others have an additional fee. Learn more:

DNA TV Service and DNA Internet Training Area

All DNA Store locations have a training area where, after subscribing to DNA TV service and a set-top box or DNA Internet, you can try setting up the service together with an expert. Using the training area is free for customers.

First Phone Setup for a Child

For example: ​

  * Creating and logging into user accounts​
  * Installing & configuring DNA Digital Security and setting restriction settings​
  * Backing up device data​
  * Installing and providing instructions for using cloud services​

####

Transferring Data from an Old Phone to a New One

#### **30 min or 60 min**

  * Transferring contacts, photos, and files to a new, similar device ​
  * Transferring apps to a new device, when the operating system is the same as the old one (iOS or Android)​
  * Data transfer does not include logging into apps or backing up the WhatsApp app.

Secure Recycling of an Old Device

Easily and securely erase data from an old device or recycle the device. The service is free for new device purchasers.

Usage Instructions for a New Phone or Tablet

#### **30 min or 60 min​**

30 or 60 minutes of personalized instruction on how to use a phone or tablet.

## Invite a DNA Assistant for a Home Visit

This is possible when you live within the Assistant's territory. The Assistant will bring the devices you ordered and install them for you. You will also receive guidance and tips on how to use the devices and services. You can get a household deduction for all services ordered for home delivery.

#### Services during a Home Visit:

Depending on your location, a DNA Assistant will also come to your home if necessary. Examples of services:

DNA Internet Setup

If you wish, you can order the Assistant to your home to do the installation for you. The Assistant will bring the devices with them, install the service and guide you on how to use it.

DNA TV Service Setup

If you wish, you can order the Assistant to your home to do the installation for you. The Assistant will bring the devices with them, install the service and guide you on how to use it.

Outdoor Modem Installation and Setup

An outdoor modem is usually installed in a gutter or other similar location to ensure the best possible 5G signal. The DNA Assistant will bring the devices with them, install them in place and connect the devices to the home WiFi network.

Service installation takes about 1 hour.

Get Internet Working - Visit

Having problems with the internet? ​DNA Assistant will fix the internet even after setup: solves home internet problems, connects devices to the home WiFi network, or helps with the correct placement of the modem. Call and invite the Assistant!​
