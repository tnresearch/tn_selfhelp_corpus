# How do I get a private number?

On [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/) you have the option to omit your
phone number or make your phone number private. Please note that it
may take up to 24 hours for the change to take effect.

**Private number means:**

  * No one can find your number on directory assistance services, such as krak.dk or dgs.dk - however, your name and address will still appear on these services if you have a private number

**Omitted number means:**

  * Directory assistance services, such as krak.dk or dgs.dk, do not show anything – neither your number, your name, or your address

[Sign up for omitted number on My Telenor.](/mit-telenor/selvbetjening/)

## **Hide your number temporarily**

You have these options to hide your number when calling others:

  * **Mobile:** You have the option to hide your number for the individual call by entering **#31#** followed by the phone number you want to call
  * You can also hide your number for outgoing calls via your settings on your mobile phone  
 **Permanently hidden number:**

Permanently hidden number means that the recipient of your call cannot see your
number - it will therefore appear as a private or omitted number. This service
is called "Hidden Number" and can be selected by contacting customer service on 72 100
100.
