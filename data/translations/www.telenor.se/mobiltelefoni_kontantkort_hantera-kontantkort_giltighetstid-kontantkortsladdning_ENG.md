# Validity Period for Prepaid Card Top-up

If you haven't topped up your prepaid card for a year, it will automatically expire. Then
you need to purchase a new card.

In the [Telenor Top-up app](/handla/kontantkort/telenor-ladda/) you can see the
date by which you must top up your prepaid card to keep it active.

## Validity Period for Top-ups

  * Telenor Fixed Price: 0.5 GB is valid for 7 days. 2-150 GB is valid for 31 days.
  * Hello 1 GB and 10 GB: 31 days
  * Telenor Flexible: 1 year from the date of top-up.
  * Extra Balance: 365 days
  * Telenor Surf: Choose between 1 month, 1 year or 2 years validity. 

If you have previously made a top-up with a fixed price and international minutes at
the same time, you received a validity period for that top-up. If at
the next occasion you have international minutes remaining and only make a top-up for fixed
price, the validity period for your international minutes will not be extended.
