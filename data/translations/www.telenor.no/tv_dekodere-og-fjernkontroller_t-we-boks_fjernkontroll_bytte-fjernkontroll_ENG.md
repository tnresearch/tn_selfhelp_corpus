# Replacing Your T-We Remote Control

Here we help you replace your old remote control and connect the new one to your TV.

[Use the same remote control for your TV ➔](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks/fjernkontroll/bytte-fjernkontroll/#tv)

## Video Guide

If the guide on this page doesn't work, you should try resetting the remote control before trying again.

  * #### How to Reset the Remote Control

 **How to reset the remote control to factory settings:**

This resets the remote control to its original settings.

    1. Hold down the 1 and 6 buttons for 5 seconds

    2. Then press 9 - 8 - 1 on the remote control. The **DECODER** button should now blink a couple of times before turning off

    3. Press OK

## Step by Step

Guide with text and images

## 1. Turn on Your TV and T-We Box

Turn on the power switch on the back of the T-We box and turn on the TV.

## 2. Put the Box into Pairing Mode

Open the front panel of the T-We box and press the up and down arrow buttons simultaneously for 3 seconds.

## 5. Follow the Instructions on the TV Screen

Press OK on the remote control when the information on the screen asks you to.

When the message " _The decoder is now connected to the remote control_ " appears on the screen, you can use the new remote control.

 **If the screen doesn't appear**

If you don't get the screen as described, you can repeat steps 2 and 3 before holding down MENU and OK simultaneously for 3 seconds. Release when the blinking stops in the DECODER button.

## 6. Old Remote Control

Your old remote control should not be returned, but delivered to EE recycling locally or at a recycling station.

## Using the Same Remote Control for TV and T-We Box

You can program the remote control for the T-We Box to also control the TV, so you don't have to deal with multiple remote controls. Press the play button on the video below, or read the step-by-step guide.

  * #### Using the Same Remote Control on T-We Box and TV

Step by step

    1. Make sure the TV and T-We box are turned on. Turn on the sound.

    2. Hold down the number keys 1 and 3 simultaneously for three seconds, until the light around the **[TV On/Off]** button begins to shine. The remote control is now in programming mode.

    3. Hold down the Mute button on the remote control until the sound disappears and the Mute symbol appears on the TV screen. This can take several minutes, depending on the type of TV you have. As soon as the sound on the TV disappears, release the button. It is important to release the button immediately. If you release it too late, the remote control will continue to search for another TV. 

    4. Test if the volume buttons work.

    5. If the volume buttons work, end the programming. Press the **[On/Off]** button on the T-We box. The **[TV On/Off]** button will blink twice.

 **The remote control for the T-We box will now also work with your TV.**
