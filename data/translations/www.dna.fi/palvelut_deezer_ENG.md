# Deezer

The Deezer music service works on almost all devices and web browsers,
including the DNA TV Hub. See supported devices on the [Deezer
website](https://www.deezer.com/fi/devices).

###

Frequently Asked Questions about Deezer:

How do I transfer playlists and favorites from another music service to Deezer?

First, subscribe to Deezer and log in. Then:

  1. **On your phone:** Open the "Settings" tab and then "Transfer your favorites".  
**On your computer:** Open “Settings” and select "Transfer favorites" and then
"Choose service". Select the service you want from the list.

  2. Wait for your content to transfer – this may take a moment, depending on the number of playlists and favorite music you have. 
  3. Done!

On which operating systems does Deezer work?

The Deezer app is available for Android and iOS operating systems. You can also
use the service through a web browser at
[deezer.com](http://www.deezer.com)

I already have DNA Deezer Premium. Can I switch to Deezer Family?

You can switch your account to Deezer Family. If you subscribed to your current Deezer Premium
account from DNA, please contact
[customer service](https://www.dna.fi/yhteystiedot).

How many profiles can be in one Deezer Family account?

A total of 6. One main user and up to 5 family members can access a
Premium profile. The condition is that the members live in the same household.

The main profile is the primary account used for logging in and managing the Family
account. Secondary profiles are created or invited under the main profile
after logging in to the main profile. Invited profiles have their own
username and password.

How can I create a new Deezer Family sub-profile or link an existing
Deezer account to Family?

### Sending a Family Invitation

1. Go to "My Music" and select "Settings"

2. Select "Family Members"

3. Add a new member by touching the plus icon

4. Select "Send Invitation"

5. Choose the application where the invitation will be sent and send the invitation. You can also
copy the link to the clipboard for sending.

####

####

### Receiving a Family Invitation

1. Click the link in the message and accept the invitation.

2. Log in with your credentials or create a new Deezer account.

How many different end devices can be connected to one Deezer Family account?

You can connect up to 3 devices to the main account and 2 devices to each of its profiles. Each family member can only listen to music on one device
at a time.

What happens to the profiles if I cancel the main account subscription?

When you cancel your Deezer Family subscription, your account will change to a free Deezer
account. All linked profiles will also become free accounts and their
users can change them to independent Deezer accounts through the website
[www.deezer.com](http://www.deezer.com/ "Follow link"). No one's
playlists or saved albums will be lost during the change.
