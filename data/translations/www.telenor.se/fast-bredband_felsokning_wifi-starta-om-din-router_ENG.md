# Restart your router again

It's time to restart the router again, to get it to choose the channel
that has the least traffic right now.

  
When you restart your router, it automatically performs a channel search to find
the least loaded channel, which will give you faster browsing.  
  

### Here's how:

  1. Unplug the power cable from the router and wait at least one minute.
  2. Plug the cable back in and restart your computer or other equipment.
