# Troubleshooting

How to restore factory settings?

With the modem on, press the Reset/Restore button with a pointed object for
about 15 seconds until the indicator lights turn off. After this, the lights
will start flashing and the device's factory settings will be restored.
Restoring factory settings will reset the settings to their defaults. You can
reconfigure them after the modem has been restored to factory settings.  
  

What should I do if I forget the WiFi password or the web-based management
page password?

Restore the modem to factory settings and try again using the default WiFi
password and the login password for the settings page found on the info label.

What should I do if the NETWORK indicator light is red or I cannot connect to
the internet?

  1. Check that the Nano-SIM card is in place.
  2. If the problem does not go away, restore the modem's factory settings and
     try again.
  3. Check with DNA to see if the SIM card has been deactivated.

What should I do if the internet speed is low?

  1. Adjust the router's position or move it closer to the window to improve
     signal reception.
  2. If the device is connected to the modem via WiFi, try moving the device to
     get a better signal.
  3. Switch to the 5 GHz band of WiFi if your device supports it.
  4. Restart the modem and device and try again

How to connect the modem using WiFi Protected Setup (WPS)?

  1. Make sure WiFi is turned on on your device.
  2. Hold down the WPS button on the router for at least 5 seconds and then
     release it; the WiFi indicator light will start flashing.

Why can't I access the modem's web-based management page?

  1. Make sure the computer is connected to the modem with an Ethernet cable or
     WiFi connection.
  2. Make sure the computer is set to obtain the IP address and DNS server
     address automatically.
  3. Restart the web browser or try using a different browser or device.
  4. If the problem does not go away, restore the modem's factory settings.

Why is the content displayed on the web-based management page not up to date?

Depending on the browser's cache, the content on the management page may not
be up to date. Manually clear the browser's cache (e.g., open the browser and
select **Internet Options > General > Browsing history > Delete**) and
restart the management page.

Does the modem support 5 GHz WiFi? How do I connect to its 5 GHz WiFi?

  1. Your modem supports 5 GHz WiFi, which is faster and less susceptible to
     interference. However, it does not penetrate walls as well as 2.4 GHz
     WiFi and can only be used with devices that support 5 GHz WiFi.
  2. The modem has band steering enabled by default, which prioritizes 5 GHz on
     your router, and the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks share the same network
     name and the connection is automatically made to the faster network. If
     you want to make sure you are using the faster 5GHz network, connect to
     the WiFi network with 5GHz in the name. You can manage the band steering
     feature on the modem's web-based management page.
