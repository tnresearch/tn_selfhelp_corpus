# Restart

If your TV box is connected via a service distributor or router, do the following:  
  
**First** : Restart the service distributor and router. You do this by unplugging
the power cables and waiting 10 seconds. Then plug the cables back in.

**Then** : When you can use the internet again – restart your TV box by
unplugging the power cable and waiting 10 seconds. Then plug the cable back in.
