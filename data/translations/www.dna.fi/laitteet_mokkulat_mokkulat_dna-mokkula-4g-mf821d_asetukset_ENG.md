## Forcing 4G or 3G Network Connection

If you are using the modem on the border between 4G and 3G, or 3G and 2G networks, the modem may switch between these networks. You can check the coverage areas at [dna.fi/kuuluvuus-ja-peittoalueet](/kuuluvuus-ja-peittoalueet).  

Switching between networks can cause the internet connection to drop and slow down the modem. In this case, the modem may be left relying only on the 2G network, resulting in a very slow internet connection. A solution to the problem may be to force the modem to either the 4G or 3G network. Note! Forcing must be done when the modem is not connected to the network.  

Select **Settings > Modem Settings > Preferred Network Selection**.

  * Select **3G only** if you want to lock the modem to the 3G network.
  * Select **4G LTE only** if you want to lock the modem to the 4G LTE network.

## Tips for Improving Modem Connection

Modems work most efficiently on 4G and 3G networks. If the internet connection is slow or drops, try the following tips:  

  * Indoors, the connection is most effective near windows
  * Use an external antenna with the modem. Learn more about modem antennas [here](https://kauppa4.dna.fi/Lis%C3%A4laitteet-ja-tarvikkeet/c/muut-laitteet?q=%3Arelevance%3AaccessoryType%3ALis%C3%A4antennit).
  * Connect the modem to the computer with a USB extension cable and place the modem as far away from the computer as possible
