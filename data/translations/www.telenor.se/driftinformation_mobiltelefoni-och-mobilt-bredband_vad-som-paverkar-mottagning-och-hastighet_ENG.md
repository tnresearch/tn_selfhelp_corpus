# What affects reception and speed

There are many factors that can affect your coverage and how quickly you can surf.

The biggest factors are temporary [service disruptions](/support/driftinformation/driftstorningar-pa-mobilnatet/) and what [type of network is available where you are](/support/driftinformation/mobiltelefoni-och-mobilt-bredband/mobiltackning-i-sverige/). But there are other, smaller factors that also have an impact.

### Which mobile – and which gadgets – you have

New mobile phones can receive more frequencies from a mobile signal than an older one, which in turn provides better coverage. And some mobile cases dampen the signals, resulting in poorer coverage. If you use a handsfree set (i.e., a cable with headphones and a microphone) and place your mobile on, for example, a table instead of holding it in your hand or having it in your pocket, the reception may improve.

### Which mobile operator you have

Which operator you have can affect your coverage, as all operators have their own areas in the mobile network (even if they share networks in some places).

### Where you are located

It's quite obvious that coverage is affected by whether you are indoors or outdoors. And some house walls can really affect coverage for the worse.

[Tips from PTS on better indoor coverage](https://pts.se/internet-och-telefoni/mobiltackning/)

Outdoors, a large concrete building between you and the nearest transmitter can weaken the signal. The same applies if you are surrounded by mountains or trees; then the radio signal may have difficulty reaching your mobile. And the further you are from the radio mast, the weaker the signal becomes.

When you are among a lot of other people, for example in rush hour traffic on your way home from work or in a shopping center, many mobiles are simultaneously struggling to capture a limited number of signals.

## What surfing speed can you expect?

The worse the coverage, the slower it is to surf. But what surfing speeds can you actually expect?

Generally, the following normal speeds apply to the different networks.

5G+: up to 1000 Mbit/s

4G and 5G: up to 200 Mbit/s

2G and 3G will be shut down at the end of 2025

**4G, 5G and 5G+, what is the difference?**

Apart from the speeds, the response time also differs. 5G and 5G+ give you a better experience if, for example, you play games or watch live broadcasts that depend on short response times to avoid lag. 5G+ also creates better conditions if several people in the household are surfing at the same time, whether it's streaming, working from home, endlessly scrolling on social media or playing online games with friends.

However, this applies to ideal circumstances, and as you have been able to read above, many factors can affect the speed. Also, remember that if you have a subscription with limited data and it suddenly becomes very slow to surf, it is probably because your data is used up (which you can check in [our app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/)).

You can measure your speed on the website [Bredbandskollen](http://www.bredbandskollen.se/), or by downloading their app. Search for "Bredbandskollen" in the App Store or on Google Play.
