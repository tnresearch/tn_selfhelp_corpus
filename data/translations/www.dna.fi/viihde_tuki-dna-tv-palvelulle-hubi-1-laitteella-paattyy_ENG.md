# Support for DNA TV service on DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 1.0) device ends
26.5.2025

From 26.5.2025 onwards, the DNA TV service cannot be used with the DNA
Viihdeboksi (Hubi 1.0) device. The DNA TV service includes, for example, the ability to watch TV
channels, recordings, or rent movies. We will inform customers in a phased manner
who are using a DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 1.0) device. The first
customer notifications about the change will be sent in weeks 48 and 49.

We offer current DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 1.0) customers the opportunity to
obtain a DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 2.0) device free of charge.

The new set-top box can be obtained through the following channels:

  * Visit the nearest DNA Store to pick up a new device (no delivery fee)
  * Order a free device via the chat on the dna.fi website (delivery fee €4.99)
  * Call our sales service at 0800 550 044, weekdays from 9 am to 8 pm, Sat from 10 am to 4 pm, toll-free number (delivery fee €4.99)

DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 2.0) offers a better viewing experience, as
the device has a newer Android TV version, more memory and more powerful
performance. This makes using applications such as Yle Areena and
YouTube even smoother and faster.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does this require any action from me?

If you are a current DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 1.0) customer and want to continue using the DNA TV
service after 17.3.2025, you must upgrade your device to a DNA
Viihdeboksi (Hubi 2.0) device.

The new set-top box can be obtained through the following channels:

  * Visit the nearest DNA Store to pick up a new device (no delivery fee)
  * Order a free device via the chat on the dna.fi website (delivery fee €4.99)
  * Call our sales service at 0800 550 044, weekdays from 9 am to 8 pm, Sat from 10 am to 4 pm, toll-free number (delivery fee €4.99)

Is the device exchange completely free of charge?

There is a delivery fee of €4.99 for remote sales. You can also pick up the device from
the store free of charge.

What is new in the DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 2.0) device?

The DNA Viihdeboksi (Hubi 2.0) device has more internal memory and
a more powerful processor, which improves the use of recordings and applications.
The package includes a new Bluetooth remote control, but the remote control of the DNA Viihdeboksi
(Hubi 1.0) device also works with the new device.

Will my network recordings be preserved when I change the device?

Network recordings and other content services will remain the same, and
there will be no changes to them. An external hard drive will not work with the new device.
