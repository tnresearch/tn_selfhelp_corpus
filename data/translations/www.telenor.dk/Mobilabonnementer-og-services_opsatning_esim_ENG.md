# eSIM

eSIM is a smart and digital alternative to the physical SIM card you know
today. The 'e' stands for embedded (built-in), because the function is built into
the phone. eSIM is supported by many newer mobile phones.

eSIM is quick to activate by scanning a QR code, and you don't have to
wait for a physical SIM card to be delivered. eSIM can be combined with a physical
SIM card, so you can use two phone numbers on the same phone, e.g.
work number and private number, which you can quickly switch between while you are
on the go.

eSIM can also be used to connect your phone to your smartwatch, so you can
receive calls and messages without having your phone with you. It just requires a
Smart Number from Telenor and a smartwatch that supports eSIM. You can
read more about it [here](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/opsatning/smartnummer/).

How do I order eSIM?

  1. Order your eSIM on [My Telenor](/login/) under the subscription you want an eSIM for.
  2. First you will receive an order confirmation and then an email that you will need to activate your eSIM. Follow the instructions in the email to activate your eSIM.

How do I activate eSIM?

You have two options when you want to activate your eSIM:

  1. Find and scan the QR code on [My Telenor](/login/)
  2. Find the email you received when you ordered your eSIM. The subject line says **Activate eSIM**. Follow the instructions to activate your eSIM with the QR code or link in the email. 

Note! The QR code can only be used once. If the link/QR code does not work,
try ordering a new one on My Telenor or call customer service on 72 100
100, and we will help you.

What does eSIM cost?

eSIM is free with Telenor. You order it on [My Telenor.](/login/)

What do I do if I lose my mobile with eSIM?

If you lose your mobile with eSIM, you can order a new one via self-service on
My Telenor. The new eSIM is ready within a few minutes and costs 0 kr. The
new QR code is displayed on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/).
