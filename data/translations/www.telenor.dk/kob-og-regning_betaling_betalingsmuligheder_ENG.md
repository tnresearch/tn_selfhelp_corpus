# Payment with Telenor

At Telenor, we are phasing out PBS and manual payment as payment options. We believe that the future is 100% digital and without bills as we know them. We are working to ensure that you will not have to deal with bills and payment fees in the future, but can simply follow your consumption in My Telenor – just as you know it from streaming services like Netflix and Spotify.

This means that we will automatically deduct your payment from your payment card - completely without fees. You sign up with your payment card, and then the payment is arranged every month, without you having to think about it.

Sign up for automatic card payment [here](/mit-
telenor/login/?blockedMode=TelenorLogin&returnUrl=%252fmit-
telenor%252fbetalingsmetode%252f).  
In My Telenor you can also see your bills and consumption.

## Manual payment and payment service

If you receive your bill by post and have to pay it manually, it costs DKK 49 per bill. With PBS payment, there is a fee of DKK 19. Towards 2026, we are phasing out manual payment and PBS in order to be able to offer more transparency about consumption and payment in the future.

When is my payment deducted?

The payment is deducted on the 26th of each month, and if the 26th falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, the payment will be deducted on the next business day.

Can I register my payment with PBS?

No, with us you can pay by card. It doesn't cost extra, and the payment happens completely automatically.

How do I change my payment card?

If you have a new payment card, you can easily update your card details on [My Telenor](/login/).

Which payment cards can I pay with?

You can register the following cards for automatic payment: Dankort, VISA, MasterCard, JCB and Maestro.

Remember to update your card details if your registered payment card expires or is blocked.

How do I sign up for automatic card payment?

You do this on My Telenor - it's easy and quick. Click [here](/mit-
telenor/login/?blockedMode=TelenorLogin&returnUrl=%252fmit-
telenor%252fbetalingsmetode%252f) and get started right away.

Good to know about payment with us

  * The payment period runs from the 16th to the 15th of the month.
  * You pay in advance for your subscription and selected services – i.e. before you have used it.
  * You pay in arrears for any extra consumption – i.e. after you have used it.
  * Smart Agreement: The first installment typically falls in the month after the agreement is entered into, and therefore the last installment also falls in the month after the Smart Agreement stops.
