# Get Started with Viaplay

If you have chosen V series & film or V premium in your TV package, you also have access to the streaming service Viaplay with the Viaplay Film & Series subscription.

### Already chosen a package that includes Viaplay, but haven't activated it yet?

If you have chosen V series & film or V premium, you must create a Viaplay account to start streaming.

Create account and download app

## Haven't purchased a TV package that includes Viaplay before?

**Here's how to access Viaplay:**

  1. Go to either [V film](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/innholds-velger/?channelId=CH0241) or [V premium](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/tv-internett/twe/premium-tv/produkt-info/?productId=CH0022) in the content selector on My Pages (the links will take you directly to the packages)

  2. Order the desired package

  3. When the package is activated, press **Activate Viaplay Account**

  4. Email address is auto-filled, but you can change it to another address if you wish

    * If you already have a Viaplay account, you can press "Log in here" on the Viaplay page and log in with your existing username and password instead.

  5. Choose a password and press Activate

  6. Start watching your favorite movies and series!

  * #### How do I log in to the Viaplay app on T-We box II

    1. Go to streaming services

    2. Select Viaplay

    3. Select Start Viaplay

    4. Press Log in

    5. Select "log in with code instead"

    6. Retrieve the code

    7. Enter the code at viaplay.no/activate

    8. You are now logged in to Viaplay

  * #### Where can I watch Viaplay?

Viaplay is a separate app and you can watch the content wherever you want. You can install it on smart TVs, Apple TV, mobile phones, tablets and game consoles in addition to [viaplay.no](https://viaplay.no/).

Viaplay app is now also available on T-We Box II!

  * #### Problems using Viaplay?

If you experience problems with playback or using Viaplay, you can read more about the service at [kundeservice.viaplay.no/](https://kundeservice.viaplay.no/).

You will also find contact information there if you need help.

  * #### Can I watch the content in places other than the Viaplay app?

You can also find some of the movies in the streaming services V Film and Viaplay Originals in the T-We App and on your T-We Box.

Viaplay app is now also available on T-We Box II!

  * #### I have purchased both V series & film and V premium, can I then get two Viaplay accounts/subscriptions?

No, you only get access to one subscription.
