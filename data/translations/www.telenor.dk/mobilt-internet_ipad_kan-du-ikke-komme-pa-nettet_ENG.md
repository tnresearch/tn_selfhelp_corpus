# Can't Get Online with Your iPad?

If you're having trouble getting online on your iPad via mobile data, it may be due to poor or no coverage in the area.

[Check our coverage map](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/ "Coverage Map")

**If there is coverage in the area, try to:**

  * Restart your iPad
  * Check if there are any temporary [network issues](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/ "Network Status") with the mobile network.
  * If "Telenor DK" is not displayed in the top left corner of the screen, the SIM card is likely defective. [Contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/) and have a new SIM card sent to you by mail – or visit [one of our stores](/find-butik/) and get a new one right away.

If you still can't get online, try resetting the network settings:  
 **Select:** Settings > General > Reset > Reset Network Settings

If you are still having trouble getting online, you can always [contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).

## What Can Block Your Signal

**Hills and Trees:** If you are in a dip, or surrounded by hills or trees, the network signal may have difficulty reaching your mobile.

**Weather and Wind:** The mobile signal is affected by the season. The best mobile signal day is actually a frosty winter day. While wet leaves, rain and snow will dampen the signal.

**Buildings:** If there is a large concrete building between you and the nearest transmitter, the walls will lower the signal strength. And if you are inside a building with thick walls, you may also experience a poorer signal.

**Many in the Same Area:** If many people are using the Telenor network at the same time, your speed may be lower.
