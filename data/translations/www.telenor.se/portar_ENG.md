Tips!

  * Use the **cables that come with** the router or TV box. Older cables may have a different current strength that prevents it from working.   
  

  * If you have purchased both broadband and TV from us, you need to **install the broadband first**.  
  

  * **Correct placement of the router** is important for it to function really well. Here you can read all about [where your router should be for maximum wifi](/handla/bredband/allt-om-wifi/placering-av-router/).  
  

  * Do you have a cabinet in the hallway with a lot of outlets? Congratulations, then you don't have to have cables running all over your home! The cabinet contains a **patch panel**, and creates a local wired network (home network). Connect the network cable between the outlet labeled IN and the room where you want the router. [Read more](/support/fast-bredband/surfa-nu/kom-igang-med-ditt-bredband/fiberuttag-hur-vill-du-koppla-upp-dig/fiberuttag--koppla-in-routern-fran-telenor/)  
  

  * The **TV box** does not need to be visible as it is powered via radio signals from the remote control. But it **should not be in a closed cabinet**, or on top of other electronic equipment, as it may overheat. Leave at least 20 centimeters above for good ventilation.

Does it not work?

  * Try **restarting** the service distributor, router and TV box. If the wrong port has been connected, it may take up to 10 minutes before it starts working.  
  

  * Double-check that the **correct port** is connected.   
  

  * Check that you have selected the **correct HDMI source** with the remote control.  
  

  * If it still doesn't work, [contact customer service](/support/old-kontakta-oss/)[ ](/support/) and we will help you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * Can I get help with broadband installation?

Absolutely! At telenor.se/surfa-nu you will get help regardless of whether you have just unpacked
the router from the box, or have already come a long way and wonder why it is not
working.  
  
[To telenor.se/surfa-nu](/link/a481b520caa74fa78c97823e1292d558.aspx)

  * Can I use a different router or TV box?

Yes, you can use a different router than the one we send out. If you need
to contact customer service, however, we do not have the same opportunities to troubleshoot when you
use your own router.

However, you cannot use a different TV box than the one that comes with it
because the program card is linked to the TV box.

  * What is a service distributor?

A service distributor is sometimes called a switch, media converter or
fiber converter. It is the device used to connect
broadband or TV via fiber if you live in a home with an open network.

It is the property owner or city network that provides your
service distributor, often it is already installed in your home.

  * What is a patch panel?

In some houses and apartments there is a patch panel. With its help, a
local wired network (home network) is created that allows you to choose exactly where you
want to connect your router and TV box - without having to have cables all over
your home. Very practical!  
  
The patch panel is often in a connection cabinet in the hallway, together with a
list of which outlets are in which rooms.

  * What do I need to be able to connect my broadband or TV?

To connect broadband or TV when you live in an open network, you need
network cable, router or TV box and
[service distributor](/link/844b799355d547ef88cb7536aa04f518.aspx). When you
order from us, we send out a router or TV box and the cables that
are needed. The service distributor is usually already in the home.

  * What is an open network?

Open network means that you as a customer have the opportunity to choose freely among all
fiber providers in the network. The opposite is a closed fiber network where
often one provider has exclusive rights to deliver services.
