# How to install your Tv-hub

  * ### Insert batteries into the remote control

Before plugging in the tv-hub to your tv, insert batteries into the remote
control. This way you avoid having to pair the remote later.

  * ### Connect the tv-hub to the tv

Connect by plugging the tv-hub into the HDMI input of your TV. Then plug in
the power cord. Turn on your TV and find the correct HDMI input using the
source button on the remote control.

  * ### If you need to pair your remote control

If the pairing does not happen automatically for some reason, a picture will
appear on your TV showing you how to pair them manually, hold down the buttons
with the "house" and "back arrow". When the pairing is complete, a message
will appear on the screen indicating that it is done.

  * ### Set desired language

Keep in mind that it is the tv-hub's interface you are setting the language
for, not third-party apps such as YouTube.

  * ### Connect to the internet

If you have an Android phone, you can set up the tv-hub's internet via your
phone by following the instructions on the TV. Otherwise, choose "Skip" when
prompted. Select your WiFi network and enter the password.

  * ### The Tv-hub is updating

To ensure you can fully utilize your service, the tv-hub will perform an
update as soon as you have connected to the internet. This takes varying
amounts of time, depending on how fast your internet connection is. When the
update is complete, the box will restart.

  * ### Customize settings

Follow the instructions on the screen, including whether you want to keep or
change the default settings. You can also change these later.

  * ### Log in or create an account in Google Play

To be able to download your own apps such as Disney+, you need to log in to
your existing Google Play account or create a new one. [Here we tell you more
about how to do it.](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/box/telenor-tv-hubb/)Several
apps are already pre-installed on the box and do not require a Google Play
account, such as SVT Play, YouTube, Netflix, Viaplay, MAX and TV4 Play.
However, you may need a subscription or to create a username and password for
some of these apps.

  * ### Configure the remote control

Follow the instructions on the TV and choose the settings you want. You can
also do this later.

[Here you can find out what all the buttons on the remote control
mean.](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/Original/TA23031.jpg?638461170899603802)
