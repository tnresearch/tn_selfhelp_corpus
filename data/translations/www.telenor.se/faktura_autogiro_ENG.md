# Autogiro (Direct Debit)

With Autogiro, the amount is automatically debited from your bank account each month, and you avoid invoice fees. You apply for Autogiro in your online bank.

Keep in mind that your Autogiro application may not be approved before the next invoice is sent. So don't be surprised if you receive an invoice the old way before the application is approved.

Good to know about Autogiro

  * **Debited on the same date every month**
Autogiro debiting takes place on the 28th of each month. If the date falls on a public holiday, the money will be debited on the previous working day.

  * **We no longer use forms**
Previously, you had to have a form to apply for Autogiro, but now you apply in your online bank.

  * **Do you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV with us?**
In that case, you have two customer numbers and will receive two invoices, which means you must apply for Autogiro for both, if that is what you want.

  * **You may still receive a paper invoice**
Depending on when you get Autogiro, a paper invoice may be sent out before your Autogiro consent has been processed by the bank. In that case, the paper invoice should be paid. If you are a new customer with us, you cannot get Autogiro until you have received your first invoice.

## Questions and answers about Autogiro

  * How do I see that my application for Autogiro/e-invoice has been approved?

In the My Telenor app, you can see how you pay your upcoming invoices. Click on invoices, then on unpaid invoices and at the bottom you will see your payment method.

Keep in mind that if you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV from us, you have two customer numbers and will receive two invoices. This means you must make an application for each customer number.

### Autogiro

When it comes to seeing if an Autogiro application has been approved, it varies from bank to bank. Most banks send a confirmation by post when your Autogiro application has been approved. Contact your bank to find out what their procedures look like and how long it takes.

### E-invoice

For e-invoice, you can see directly in your online bank that everything is okay. Keep in mind that you cannot see the actual e-invoice until we have sent it to your bank. We do this once a month.

### If you receive a paper invoice anyway...

...it does not necessarily mean that something has gone wrong. Depending on when you get Autogiro or e-invoice, a paper invoice may be sent out before your consent for Autogiro or e-invoice has been processed by the bank.

  * I have problems getting Autogiro. What should I do?

The most common reasons for problems getting Autogiro are:

    * **Incorrect customer number (also called payer number)**
You can find your customer number on the invoice. You can find the invoices in [My
Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

    * **You have already applied for Autogiro**
There is an Autogiro with the bank already.

    * **The bank giro number is incorrect**
The correct number is 5572-4959

    * **Multiple customer numbers/payer numbers**
If you have both mobile telephony and broadband or TV with us, you have two customer numbers and will receive two invoices. This means you must apply for Autogiro for both, if that is what you want.

[Read more about Autogiro](/link/cd73f7500b12469ea4605c59672dfa7a.aspx)

  * How do I get Autogiro/e-invoice if I do not have BankID?

You can easily apply for Autogiro or e-invoice via your online bank by using your bank card reader. The information needed to apply can be found on your paper invoice from Telenor.

The same information can be obtained from customer service, but then you need to identify yourself. If you do not have BankID, you can identify yourself using a personal code that you order by
[contacting](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) our customer service. The code is sent to your registered address.

  * How do I get Autogiro if I am a legal guardian?

[Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx)[
](tel:020222222) and we will help you.

  * Can I pay with Swish if I have Autogiro?

No. Since you are connected to Autogiro, your invoice is paid automatically each month. Therefore, there is no reason to pay it with Swish.

**Received a reminder?**
If there is not enough money in the account when the Autogiro payment is debited and you receive a payment reminder, you **can use Swish to pay the reminder**.
