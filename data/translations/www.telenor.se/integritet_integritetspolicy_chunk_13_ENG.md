##

  * Right to Access (Register Extract)

You have the right to know if we are using your personal data. If we are, you
also have the right to receive information about what data we are using and how we
are using it. You also have the right to receive a copy of all personal data we have
about you.

If you are interested in certain specific data, please indicate this in
your request. For example, you can specify if you are interested in a certain type of
data (e.g. what Contact and identification information we have about you)
or if you want access to data from a specific time period.

  * Right to Rectification

If any of the data we use about you is incorrect, you have the right to have
it corrected. You also have the right to supplement incomplete data with
additional data that may be needed to ensure the data is accurate.

Once we have corrected your data, or it has been supplemented, we will
inform those to whom we have disclosed your data about the updated data, provided that it is not impossible or unduly burdensome. If you
ask us, we will also tell you to whom we have disclosed your data.

If you request rectification, you also have the right to request restriction during the time we
investigate the matter.

  * Right to Erasure

In certain cases, you have the right to have the data we have about you erased.

You have the right to have your data erased if:

    * The data is no longer needed for the purposes for which we collected it
    * If we are using your data based on your consent and you withdraw your consent
    * If the data is used for direct marketing and you unsubscribe from it
    * If you object to use based on our legitimate interest and we cannot demonstrate that our reasons for use outweigh your interests
    * If the personal data has been used unlawfully
    * If erasure is required to comply with a legal obligation

If we erase your data after you have requested it, we will also
inform those to whom we have disclosed the data about the erasure, provided
that it is not impossible or unduly burdensome. If you ask us, we will
also tell you to whom we have disclosed your data.

  * Right to Data Portability

Data portability means that you have the right to obtain the data we have
collected about you, from us, in a structured, generally available and
machine-readable format, and that you have the right to transfer this to another
controller.

The right to data portability only applies:

    * To data that has been collected from you
    * If the use is based on your consent or to fulfill an agreement with you, and
    * The data is not in paper format
