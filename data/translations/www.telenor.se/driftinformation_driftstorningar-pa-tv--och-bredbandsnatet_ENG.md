Service Information in My Telenor

Do you suspect that a service disruption is affecting your broadband or TV
subscription? Check in [My Telenor! ](/mitt-telenor/)An ongoing disruption
on Telenor's own network will be displayed at the top of the "My Overview" page. If nothing is displayed there
there is no disruption on our network. Disruptions on other networks are not displayed, however.  
  
You can also see information about major disruptions below, or
search for your address on our
[disruption map](/support/driftinformation/driftstorningar-pa-tv--och-bredbandsnatet/#driftkarta).

[To My Telenor](/mitt-telenor/)

Report a Fault?

If you can't find any information about a disruption, either in [My Telenor](/mitt-
telenor/) or on the [disruption map](/support/driftinformation/driftstorningar-pa-tv--och-bredbandsnatet/#driftkarta), but are sure that there is a disruption on
Telenor's own network causing your problems?  
  
[Contact us ](/support/kontakta-oss/#productcategoryid=1&categoryid=5\))to
report the fault. Need to report a fault outside of our opening hours, email us via
"My Messages" in the My Telenor app, or via [My Telenor](/mitt-
telenor/) here on the web.

Tip: use Telenor Stream

[Telenor Stream](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/) is your salvation if you
temporarily can't see your channels via the TV box. Then you can watch TV anytime and
anywhere.
