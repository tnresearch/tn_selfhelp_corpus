## Fraud Protection

Enabling Chrome Protection

DNA Digiturva has both a browser extension for Chrome on Android devices,
and its own browser for secure browsing.

#### How to enable Chrome protection

1. Open DNA Digiturva.

2. Select the **Secure Browsing** section.

3. If you don't yet have Chrome protection enabled, you will see a prompt
encouraging you to enable it. Press **Start**.

4. From the Chrome protection summary, press **Start** again.

5. Allow the accessibility permission and select **Continue**.

  1. DNA Digiturva will open the accessibility settings on your phone.
  2. From the list of installed applications, find DNA Digiturva.
  3. Grant the application access. Depending on your phone or tablet model, this may be a selection button that needs to be activated or a similar method.
  4. If the device asks for confirmation of administrative rights, press **Allow**.

6. After allowing accessibility settings, you will return to the DNA Digiturva
application. Next, DNA Digiturva needs permission to display floating icons. Press **Continue**.

  1. From your settings, find the "Display over other apps" section and find DNA Digiturva there. Some devices will direct you straight to the "Display over other apps" section and you can directly search for DNA Digiturva from the list.
  2. Press the **selection button** next to DNA Digiturva to activate it.

7. Chrome protection is now enabled.

WiFi Protection on Android Devices

1. Open DNA Digiturva.

2. Open **Fraud Protection** by pressing the section.

3. If you don't yet have WiFi protection enabled, you will see a prompt
encouraging you to enable it. Press **Start**.

4. Enable notification of connection to a malicious network by pressing
**Start** again.

5. Check that the WiFi protection and notification selection buttons are
**active**. WiFi protection is now enabled.

6. You can choose whether the application should notify you only of unsecured networks or all
WiFi networks. Unsecured networks are selected automatically. You can change
this by pressing the selected option and choosing **All networks**.

Message Protection for Phone

**Android Phone**

1. Open **DNA Digiturva**.

2. Open **Fraud Protection** by pressing the section.

3. If you don't yet have message protection enabled, you will see a prompt
encouraging you to enable it. Press **Start**.

4. Automatically block malicious messages sent by criminals by pressing
**Start** again.

5. Grant the application permission to access text messages. Press **Allow**.

6. The phone will confirm whether you want to allow reading and
sending text messages. Press **Allow** again.

7. Grant permission to contacts by pressing **Allow**.

8. The phone will confirm whether you want to allow the use of contacts. Press
**Allow** again.

9. Message protection is now enabled.

10. You can view fraudulent messages in the **Fraud Reports** section of the Message Protection section.

**iPhone Phone**

Enable filtering of unknown senders in device settings:

1. Open **DNA Digiturva**.

2. Select **Fraud Protection** and then **Text Message Protection**.

3. Open the device's **Settings**.

4. Select **Unknown Senders & Spam**.

5. Enable **Filter Unknown Senders**.

6. When you switch this setting on, a list of selectable filters will appear.

7. Select **DNA Digiturva**.

8. The phone will confirm that you want to allow the product to filter your messages. Press
**Enable**.

9. Message protection is now enabled.

10. You can view fraudulent messages in the **Fraud Reports** section of the Message Protection section.

Secure Browsing on iPhone and iPad

DNA Digiturva has both a browser extension for Safari and its own browser
for secure browsing.

#### How to enable the Safari browser extension on iPad or iPhone

1. Open DNA Digiturva.

2. Open **Fraud Protection** by pressing the section.

3. Grant Safari protection the necessary permissions by pressing the section.

4. Continue granting permissions by pressing **Configure**.

  1. DNA Digiturva will open the Safari browser. Press the **settings icon** or **aA character** from the address bar.
  2. Press **Manage Extensions**.
  3. Enable the Secure Browsing extension with the **selection button** and press **Done**.
  4. Allow protection usage permissions by selecting the **settings icon** and pressing **Secure Browsing extension**.
  5. Press **Allow Always** and **Allow on All Websites**.
  6. Once all necessary permissions have been granted, the browser will display the text "Secure Browsing extension is enabled!" You can now press **Close** and exit the browser.
  7. The Safari extension is now enabled and will work when you open Safari.

Secure Browsing on Mac Computer

Browser protection requires browser extensions to protect your web browsing,
online banking, and shopping, and to show you security information while
browsing the Internet.

1. After installing DNA Digiturva, you need to configure
browser extensions to be enabled.

2. Select the definition for the browsers you use on your computer and follow
the instructions from DNA Digiturva.

3. The browser extension can be enabled in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and
Edge.

Trust Ratings and Ad Ratings

#### Trust Ratings on Phones and Tablets

On phones and tablets, DNA Digiturva displays a trust rating when you are on a page and you have Chrome protection or the Safari extension enabled. The rating is only visible when using the Chrome browser on an Android device and the Safari browser on an iOS device.

**Android Devices**

If you have enabled Chrome protection and allowed Floating Icons,
you will have trust rating display in Chrome browser.

**iPhone and iPad**

If you have enabled the Safari browser extension, you will have trust rating display in Safari browser.

#### Ad Rating Icons in Search Results on Computers

DNA Digiturva displays the website's ad rating on the search results page when
you use Google, Bing, Yahoo, and DuckDuckGo search and you have the browser
extension for that browser installed.

1. Open the DNA Digiturva program.

2. Open the Secure Browsing/Protected Browsing section.

3. Make sure the browser extensions are enabled and installed.

  * Windows: Click **Settings**, check that the **Shopping Security selection button** is active and show **notifications for secure shopping sites** and **suspicious shopping sites** are selected.
  * Mac: Select **Browser Extensions** on the left and make sure they are installed. Then open **Settings** and make sure **Show website ad rating in search results** is selected.

**Ad Rating Icons**

  * Green icon: The site is, to our knowledge, safe. We have not found anything suspicious on the site.
  * Yellow icon: The site is suspicious. We recommend acting with caution when visiting this site. Avoid downloading files and providing personal information.
  * Red icon: This site is malicious. We recommend avoiding visiting this site. The administrator may also have blocked the site, and you cannot visit it.
  * Dark gray icon: The site has not yet been analyzed and no information is available about it.
  * Light blue icon: This web site's use is never blocked.

Blocking Ads on Websites

Ad blocking works automatically on Windows and Mac computers if
the Protected Browsing browser extension is installed. The feature prevents content
from loading from the most common ad servers, which makes it harder to track you
online and makes using the internet more comfortable. In the upper right corner of the Protected Browsing view, it shows how many ads the product has blocked.

If a site does not work due to the ad blocker, you can temporarily disable the ad blocker:

1. Open the DNA Digiturva application.

2. Select the **Fraud Protection** section.

3. Open **Settings**.

  * Windows: Select Settings from the Protected Browsing view, disable Ad Blocker.
  * Mac: Uncheck **Block Ads**. Making changes requires administrator rights.

4. When you load the page in your browser, it will display ads.
