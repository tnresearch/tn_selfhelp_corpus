# Where are you located?

In some countries, we have blocked data roaming because it is so expensive.

To prevent you from incurring an unexpectedly high cost, we have blocked your
subscription from data roaming in the following countries:

**Angola, Benin, Burundi, Djibouti, Equatorial Guinea, Guam, Guinea, Yemen,
Cuba, Laos, Libya, Namibia, New Caledonia, Syria, Togo**  
  
If you need to use data roaming, you can call us free of charge at [+46 (0)708 222
222](tel:46708222222). We will then help you remove the block and increase the
Data Roaming Limit for data abroad. The cost of data roaming is SEK 199/MB.

Statistical and Analytical
