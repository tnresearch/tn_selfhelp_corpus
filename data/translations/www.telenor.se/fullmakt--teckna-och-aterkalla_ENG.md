# Power of Attorney – Grant and Revoke

Do you want someone else to be able to handle your affairs? Then you can grant a power of attorney giving an agent the right to do so. Form and envelope? Not needed. You grant or revoke a power of attorney entirely digitally with BankID.

**This is what you – the grantor and the agent – need before you begin:**

  1. Access to a computer, mobile phone or tablet
  2. BankID
  3. Email addresses that you check regularly

Choose below whether you want to grant or revoke a power of attorney. Follow the instructions in the new window that opens. **Tip!** If you are going to revoke a power of attorney, you need to be able to state the date when the power of attorney was granted.

Good to know

About the Power of Attorney

  * Only the person who owns the subscription can grant a power of attorney
  * The power of attorney can cover different types of matters. When you grant the power of attorney, you choose which ones. A couple of examples are the right to receive information about the subscription and the right to terminate it when the contract period expires.
  * It takes about an hour from when the power of attorney is submitted until it appears in our systems.
  * The power of attorney is valid for three months after it was signed
  * The power of attorney can be revoked at any time by the grantor
  * The owner of the subscription remains payment responsible and receives the invoices even after the power of attorney has been granted
  * If the power of attorney relates to a mobile subscription with multiple users (Telenor Family), it applies to all mobile numbers that are part of the subscription
