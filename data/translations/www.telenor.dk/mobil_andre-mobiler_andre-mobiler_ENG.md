# General help for your mobile

If you are experiencing problems with your mobile, you can start by:

  * Restarting your mobile
  * Checking our [service status](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/ "Check our service status")
  * Checking your [coverage](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/dakning/dakningskort/ "Check your coverage")
  * Updating your smartphone to the latest software
  * If your mobile has completely run out of power, it may take up to an hour for it to start up again

If you need more specific help, you can find our mobile guides here for
[iPhone](/kundeservice/mobil/iphone/ "iPhone") and [Samsung
Galaxy](/kundeservice/mobil/samsung-galaxy/ "Samsung Galaxy").

## Is your mobile running out of power quickly?

  * Check if your mobile is set to frequently sync mail
  * GPS, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and games use a lot of power
  * If possible, turn down the brightness of the display
  * Be aware of apps that automatically download content from the internet

## Is your mobile freezing in the menu?

  * Restart your mobile
  * Update your mobile to the latest software
  * If your smartphone is almost full, delete e.g. videos and pictures
  * Close the apps you are not using

## You have coverage, but your mobile cannot connect online

  * Reset your APN/network settings and restart your mobile
  * Take out the SIM card and clean it with a dry cloth
  * Test the SIM card in another smartphone. If the other smartphone cannot connect to the internet, your SIM card is likely broken. You can order a new one from Customer Service or at your nearest Telenor store.

## You cannot make calls

  * Reset your APN/network settings and restart your mobile. Here's how on iPhone, Android, Windows Phone
  * Take out the SIM card and clean it with a dry cloth

If you are being forwarded to Telenor Customer Service, regardless of the number you are calling, you have either reached your limit on Usage Control or missed an invoice. Call again and let us help with a solution.
