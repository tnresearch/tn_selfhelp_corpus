# Using Your Own Modem

**Does the modem matter?** To get the most out of your internet connection, we recommend
using modems tested by DNA. You can find out more about the modems offered by DNA
[here](https://www.dna.fi/laitteet/modeemit-ja-mokkulat/modeemit).

However, if you want to use your own modem, you need to consider a few
things.

## Things to consider when using your own modem

1. Ensure the modem's software is up to date

If you are using a modem not supplied by DNA, DNA does not have
access to the modem's software. In this case, your modem cannot be updated remotely.

2. Remember security updates!

Taking care of security is important – remember to take care of it. If
a vulnerability or security flaw is detected in your modem, DNA has the right
to block the modem's traffic to the network.

3. Your modem may not support the highest additional speeds

When you have a modem purchased from DNA and DNA Net, we promise
at least the minimum speed specific to your subscription for your internet connection. When you have a modem purchased from elsewhere, the speed cannot be guaranteed. The modem may have a theoretical download and upload speed specified, but it may not
support the highest additional speeds.

4. You will only receive usage support or troubleshooting for DNA's own modems

Our customer service can only provide usage support (e.g. for connections or WiFi network
settings) for modems sold by DNA. In the event of a connection fault, we can verify the network's functionality remotely, but
unfortunately, troubleshooting cannot be performed on modems purchased from elsewhere.

## DNA's recommended modems

Modems are optimized for the DNA network, their functionality has been tested, and you can
be sure of the performance and compatibility of the modems with DNA Net.
Depending on your subscription, you can achieve speeds of up to 1 Gigabit (1000 Mbit/s).

Setting up DNA Net has been made very easy and effortless. All
necessary settings are pre-configured, so you just need to turn on the
modem and connect it to the network.

[Learn more about modems](https://www.dna.fi/laitteet/modeemit-ja-
mokkulat/modeemit)

## Frequently asked questions

Why is it important to update the modem's software?

By updating your software regularly, you also take care of
security. DNA continuously monitors the vulnerability status of end devices connected to the network and updates software as needed. However, this only applies to modems sold by DNA. Regarding vulnerabilities, we cooperate with, among others,
[The National Cyber Security Centre](https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/fi/).

In extreme situations, DNA may even have to block modems containing vulnerabilities or errors from the network, either proactively or
at the request of the authorities.

What benefit would a DNA modem be to me?

By purchasing a DNA Valokuitu Plus modem, you can be sure that you have a working, excellent performance and easy-to-use
modem in the future.

The settings in DNA modems are pre-installed for the DNA network and you can
freely modify them. The features and performance of the modems are tested
thoroughly both in the laboratory and in real use. Additional features that improve usability and functionality have also been added to the modems in addition to the manufacturer's features. By using a modem purchased from DNA, you can be sure that your internet use is secure.

Can I order a DNA installer to my home in the event of a fault if I am using a modem purchased from elsewhere?

DNA serves the customer in the event of faults, and in certain situations this
requires a service visit. But if the fault is found to be due to a self-purchased
modem or peripheral devices, DNA has the right to charge you for the costs incurred by the service visit.

Does the modem affect the speed of my internet connection?

Often the culprit for slow internet is precisely the modem, not necessarily the subscription you ordered or its speed. The modem should be replaced with a new one every few years to ensure that the device's security features, performance and settings are up to date. When you purchase your modem from DNA, you get a compatible internet
and modem and a worry-free home network for years to come.

You can test your current internet speed on the
[speed test page](https://www.dna.fi/liittymat-ja-
palvelut/netti/nopeustesti). Please note that you do not have the right to terminate the subscription agreement if the internet speed falls below the promised speed when the slowness is due to a self-purchased modem.

What are the minimum requirements for a modem in cable technology?

Your own modem, cable splitter and antenna cable must meet the following conditions (the necessary cables and splitters are included with modems sold by DNA):

**Minimum requirements for the modem**

  * At least EuroDocsis 3.0 approved cable modem with Excentis certificate
  * The modem has IPv6 and Dual Stack features
  * The device must be equipped with a CE mark
  * The modem must be installed according to the manufacturer's instructions so that the modem does not cause a fire hazard
  * The radio device must comply with the Communications Regulatory Authority's radio regulations

**Minimum requirements for the cable splitter**

  * Frequency range 5-1218 MHz or Class A screening effectiveness
  * Nominal impendance 75 Ohm
  * At least ED 3.0 or preferably ED 3.1 compliant attenuation

**Minimum requirements for the antenna cable**

  * 1st & 3rd Shield Aluminium/Polymer/Aluminium Foil
  * Frequency range 5-1300 MHz or Class A screening effectiveness
  * Nominal impendance 75 Ohm
  * At least ED 3.0 or preferably ED 3.1 compliant attenuation
  * As the cable lengthens, the requirements for attenuation and material increase
