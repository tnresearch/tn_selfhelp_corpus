# Change so the correct number is displayed on iPhone

Have you moved your number or done a number port and therefore used a
temporary number?  
  
The temporary number is registered in your iPhone and continues to be the one
that appears on the screen of those you call, even after the number port. But
don't worry, it's easy to fix!  
  
This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest operating system (iOS)** from
Apple. So feel free to start by making sure it is installed on your mobile –
not least to ensure it works as well and securely as possible.  
  
[Here's how to update the
operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-
mobil/iphone/uppdatera-operativsystemet/)

  * ### Go to the home screen

  * ### Select "Settings"

  * ### Select Messages

  * ### Disable iMessage

  * ### Back one step and select FaceTime

  * ### Disable FaceTime

  * ### Back one step and select Phone

  * ### Click on My Number

  * ### Delete your number and tap Save

  * ### Restart the mobile

You can do this most easily like this:  
  
\- Back to Settings  
\- Select General  
\- Scroll to the bottom and select Turn Off  
\- Slide the slider to turn off the mobile

  * ### Check that it was correct

Check that the correct number is displayed under Phone and that iMessage and FaceTime are
activated.
