# T-We App - Android TV and Chromecast with Google TV

Here you will find help with logging in and using the Android TV app, Chromecast with
Google TV and older Chromecast

[Chromecast (without Google TV)](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-app/android-tv-
app/#chromecast)

## Telenor T-We on Android TV

The Android TV app can be installed on all devices with Android TV (9 or
higher) as the operating system, including Chromecast with GoogleTV, some SmartTVs
and TV boxes.  
 ** _NOTE:_** _Samsung and LG SmartTVs are excluded here as they use a different
operating system._

 **This is a Beta version** , so you may experience minor bugs or similar, but
we are working hard to launch a full version. If you want to give us
feedback on bugs or anything else, you can do so in the Google Play Store.

T-We app on your TVNow you can watch T-We without having a decoder connected to the
TV itself!

## How to use Telenor T-We on Android TV

  1. Install the app

    * The procedure for how to do this will vary somewhat depending on where you install the app, but generally you go to **Google Play Store** / **Play Store** on the device you want to add it to. Then search for "Telenor T-We" and install the app

  2. Log in with [Telenor ID](/telenor-id/)

    * Scan the QR code that appears on the screen with your mobile phone, verify that it is the same number you see on the screen and log in with your Telenor ID

  3. You are ready to watch TV!

## Google TV and Chromecast

If you have Chromecast with Google TV, download the T-We App from the Play Store, and
use the associated remote control to navigate.

NOTE!

If you have an **older Chromecast (without Google TV)** , you can use the T-We App
on your mobile phone or tablet to navigate/find content, and play content
on Chromecast. This only works when Chromecast and mobile phone or tablet
are on the same network.

Before you start

If you have not [configured your Chromecast
](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/2998456)before, this must
be done before you can use the device

## Connect to Chromecast

  1. Check that your phone or tablet is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your Chromecast device

  2. Start the T-We app

  3. When the T-We app "sees" the device, the casting button will appear in the bottom right corner of your screen

  4. Press the casting button and select which Chromecast you want to play the content through

  5. Navigate to the desired content in the app and press start. The content will now start playing on the Chromecast you are connected to.

More information about Chromecast can be found [on their
website](https://store.google.com/product/chromecast), and if you need more
help you will find many tips on [Chromecast's
help pages](https://support.google.com/chromecast/chromecast/?hl=no#topic=3058948).
