# Surf Control Abroad

If you are a Telenor customer, you automatically have Surf Control abroad and a
data limit of 450 DKK per month. Unless you change your data limit, the cost of
data used abroad will never exceed 450 DKK per month.

With Roam Away, you can freely call, send SMS, and use up to 35 GB of data in 47 countries, and with Roam Away+ in 55 countries (See the amount of GB under
your subscription). See My Telenor for more information about the data allowance in your
subscription.

[Read more about roaming with Roam Away here](/shop/landingpage/mobil/roaming/).

## How Surf Control Works Abroad

You can surf and send MMS messages until you reach your data limit. Before you reach
your data limit, you will receive an SMS. When you reach your data limit, your
connection will be blocked until the next billing period. You will receive an SMS when
this happens.

If you want to temporarily increase your maximum to double, you can send an
SMS with the text ‘ **surfcontrol increase** ’ to 1221. You can also [ contact
us](/kundeservice/kontakt/) – remember that calling costs foreign rates. Please note that Surf Control no longer applies when you have purchased additional
data packages abroad. Instead, you can set a maximum amount in Surf Control,
which you can find in [My Telenor ](/mit-telenor/) or the My Telenor App.

Mobile subscriptions for children and young people are automatically blocked from using data abroad.

## **How do I prevent my child from raising the data limit with SMS?**

If your child has one of our mobile subscriptions for children, it is automatically blocked
for using data abroad, so in that case, you don't need to do anything.
If your child has another mobile subscription, you can [contact us
](/kundeservice/kontakt/) and have the temporary raising of the data limit blocked.
That way, your child cannot temporarily raise the data limit by sending an SMS
to 1221.
