## Frequently asked questions

Where can I find my PUK code?

You can find your PUK code on [My Telenor. ](/mit-telenor/login/?blockedMode=TelenorLogin&returnUrl=%252fmit-telenor%252f "My
Telenor") You can also call Customer Service on 72 100 100, or visit your
nearest Telenor store.

After entering your PUK code, your PIN code will be reset, and you will be
asked to create a new 4-digit PIN code.

Are there network disruptions in my area right now?

You can easily check if there are current or planned disruptions on the
network in your area by clicking [here](/kundeservice/drift-og-
dakning/driftstatus/tjek-driftstatus/), and you will get a status report for your
location.

Can I get a power of attorney to a store so I can help my family?

Yes, you can! [You can find the power of attorney right here.
](/shop/andet/fuldmagt/ "Power of Attorney")

How do I use NetSikker and NetSikker Plus?

If you have been subjected to digital abuse, have been scammed by a
webshop, or have been a victim of identity theft (Note: only Extended Security Package
covers identity theft), you should contact HELP Insurance. You can do this via
helpforsikring.dk, by phone at 39 90 09 00 or by sending an email to
[kontakt@helpforsikring.dk](mailto:kontakt@helpforsikring "HELP Insurance
email").

If you want to use [ID Guard](/shop/udvidet-tryghedspakke/advarsler-ved-
datalaek/ "ID Guard") (Note: Only for Extended Security Package), you must log in to
[My Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/?blockedMode=TelenorLogin&returnUrl=%252fmit-
telenor%252f "My Telenor") – you will find ID Guard under the security tab.

Here you will find a [guide to getting started with ID Guard](/kundeservice/saadan-
aktiverer-du-advarsler-ved-datalaek/ "Get started with ID Guard").

[Here you can read more about Telenor Security Package](/shop/telenor-tryghedspakke/
"NetSikker"), and [here you can read more about Extended
Security Package.](/shop/udvidet-tryghedspakke/ "NetSikker Plus")

What do I do if I regret my purchase?

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal if you regret your purchase. You can read
more about this on our [help page about the right of withdrawal. ](/kundeservice/kob-og-
regning/dit-kob-hos-telenor/returnering-og-fortrydelse/ "Help page
right of withdrawal")

If you purchased your item at Elgiganten, you must contact them to cancel
your purchase.

What is included in my subscription abroad?

It varies what is included in the different subscriptions, and not least what
conditions apply in which countries.

On our [roaming page](/shop/landingpage/mobil/roaming/ "Roaming") you can get
answers to your questions – and get precise information about the conditions
that apply to the country you are traveling to.

What do I do if I have questions about my bill?

You can find your bill on My Telenor. We have also compiled [a page that
gives you answers to most questions about billing and
payment](/kundeservice/kob-og-regning/ "billing and payment") – here you can
read more about everything from why your first bill is higher than expected, what
you should do if you have not paid a bill, what the various
fees are, and much more.

How do I get started using My Telenor?

You log in to My Telenor with MitID. If you need help, you can follow
[this guide to logging in to My Telenor](/kundeservice/mit-
telenor/saadan-logger-du-ind/).

You can log in to My Telenor [through telenor.dk](/mit-
telenor/selvbetjening/), or you can download My Telenor as an app for your mobile.
You log in the same way, regardless of which option you choose.

How do I use Screen Insurance?

To use Screen Insurance, you must first create a claim on
[SquareTrade](https://www.squaretrade.eu/dk/telenor/) and then take your
mobile to your nearest [Telenor store](/find-butik/).

On [My Telenor](/login/) you can see if you have Screen Insurance on your mobile.
Find your mobile under the 'Devices' tab and click on it. Then it will state whether you have Screen Insurance on that particular mobile.

You can read more about Screen Insurance
[here](/shop/landingpage/skaermforsikring/).

How do I create a claim in SquareTrade?

**Create Damage Claim**

Note, if you want to use Screen Insurance, you must create a
damage claim on Squaretrade's website first

Click [here](https://www.squaretrade.eu/dk/kundeservice/) to create
damage claim:

* Choose **Telenor**
* Choose **Report damage**
* Choose **I have my device**
* Choose Yes in **"is your screen cracked?"**
* Choose No in **"does it have water damage?"**
* Choose the time of the accident and describe the problem. You must choose **"I have my device"** and "yes" in **Is your screen cracked?** and select **cracked back/screen**
* Enter name and address information

You will now be forwarded to a window where you need to pay the deductible. Once
the deductible is paid, you will receive a confirmation email with a claim number. It
must be used when the phone is delivered to one of our stores

How do I use Mobile Insurance?
