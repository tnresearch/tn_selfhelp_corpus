## Menu

You can easily access the DNA TV Menu via the **Menu** button on your remote control. Use the up, down, left, and right arrow buttons on your remote control. Confirm your selection with the **OK** button on the remote control. You can always return to the previous view by selecting the **Back** arrow button on the remote control.

Shop

You can easily order popular channel packages and streaming services, as well as manage your subscriptions, through the DNA TV user interface. You can cancel your current subscription and change the channels in your DNA Mix channel package via the My Subscriptions section.

Available content includes:

  * C More Total, C More Sport and C More (C More channel packages also include the C More streaming service)
  * Viaplay Total and Viaplay
  * DNA Mix 4, 8 and 12 channel packages
  * DNA Sport
  * DNA S channel package

You need your personal DNA TV PIN code to confirm purchases, which is also used to confirm rentals in the DNA TV Rental Store. You can find your DNA TV PIN code in your order confirmation or easily in My Services and the My DNA app.

  1. Select Shop from the Menu.
  2. Learn more about channel packages and streaming services through their descriptions. You can see the price and promotional terms next to the description.  

  3. Press **Buy** next to the channel package or streaming service you have chosen.  

  4. Confirm the purchase with your personal DNA TV PIN code.
  5. A notification will appear on the screen when the purchase is successful.  
  

  6. After this, you can return to watching programs. The channels or streaming service you ordered will start appearing within a few minutes of the order.
  7. You will receive a confirmation email after the order and the subscription will be charged to your DNA bill.

Search

The **Search** function is located at the top of the **Menu**. You can use the search to find content available on the DNA TV service.

You can use voice search or type a search term. When you are on the search bar, press the right arrow button on your remote control to bring up the keyboard. You can search, for example, by program name or category.

#### Tabs:

  * The **Previous** tab lists TV programs that have been broadcast on TV channels and are available to watch immediately.
  * The **Upcoming** tab lists programs that are coming up and you can, for example, set to record.
  * The **Rental Store** tab lists movies and series from the DNA TV Rental Store that match your search.

Recordings

In the **Recordings** view, you can watch your recorded programs. Select the program you want with the **OK** button on the remote control and select Watch in the next view. If it is a series, you can see other episodes of the series on the Episodes tab. You can delete the recording from the **Recorded** tab.

[Read more detailed instructions on recording](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/viihde/tallennus)

My Apps

You can find downloaded apps in the **Menu** under **My Apps**. You can also easily download new apps and manage existing apps by pressing the **Add Apps** button.

TV and Channel Strip

When you select **TV** from the Menu, you can browse TV channels. By default, the list shows the channels to which you have a subscription. If you want to modify the channel list, go to **Settings** and the **Channel Management** section through the Menu.

You can also press the OK or arrow button on your remote control to display the channel strip. By browsing the channel strip, you can quickly see what programs are currently running on other channels and easily switch channels. Use the left and right arrow buttons on your remote control and confirm your selection with the **OK** button.

#### Details of the current program

Press the OK or arrow button on your remote control to display more details about the current program. You can navigate the view with the arrow buttons on your remote control. Selection is confirmed with the **OK** button.

You can change the program's subtitle and audio options via the corresponding icon (to the right of the REC icon).

The last icon on the line shows the image quality of the channel. You can change the image quality through the same icon. We recommend **HD quality** because the image quality is high definition, i.e. better than SD. You can also set the image quality in the Settings of the Menu. You do not need to make changes to the setting for a single program.

You can access the program page of the program via the **i-icon** below the description text. If it is a series, you can see other seasons and episodes of the series on the Episodes tab.

Categories: Find what to watch and watch programs afterwards

Through the categories (Movies, Series and Sports) you can easily find your favorite programs, which you can watch immediately or set to record if recording is included in your subscription. Some of the channels' programs are available to watch 2-7 days after broadcast, and each program shows how long the program is still available to watch. Some programs are available in a third-party application (Yle Areena, MTV Katsomo).

The view shows programs only from channels included in your subscription or available through Yle Areena or Katsomo.

Rental Store

You can find the Rental Store in the DNA TV user interface Menu. You can rent a movie immediately by selecting the movie you want and confirming the selection with the OK button on the remote control. You can use the up and down arrow buttons on the remote control to browse the different categories of the Rental Store.

You need a personal four-digit **DNA TV PIN code** for rentals, which you received with your order. You can also find it in [My Services](https://minunpalveluni.dna.fi/kirjautuminen#) and the [My DNA](https://www.dna.fi/oma-dna-sovellus) app.

If you choose to pay with the DNA PIN code, the rental will be charged to the next bill. You can also use a gift code or coupon if you have received one in connection with a campaign.

[Read more about the Rental Store](https://www.dna.fi/tuki/viihde/vuokraamo)

Program Guide

You can find the program guide in the Menu under **Program Guide**. You can access the program guide with the EPG button on your remote control. You can navigate the view with the arrow buttons on your remote control and confirm your selection with the OK button.

You can browse the program information for the desired day by selecting the days at the top of the view (today + 7 days forward). Program information is displayed as far as it is available.

At the bottom of the view, you can see more details about the program when browsing programs in the program guide. When you press the **OK** button on the remote control next to the program name, more details about the program will appear. From the more detailed program information, you can, for example, add the program to your favorites or record the program.

Settings

Through the **Settings** menu, you can access channel management, DNA TV settings, and Android system settings.

**From Channel Management you can:**

  * Tune channels and modify the channel list, i.e. choose which channels are displayed.
  * Change the channel quality. For example, you can choose to display the channel primarily in HD quality or IPTV quality over the internet. We recommend HD quality because the image quality is high definition, i.e. better than SD.

**From DNA TV settings you can:**

  * Change the menu language of the DNA TV application to Finnish, Swedish or English.
  * Enable or change the PIN code for parental control.
  * Restart the DNA TV application in case of errors.
  * Check the latest update to the DNA TV service in the Service Information section.

**From System Settings you can:**

  * Make changes to network and internet settings.
  * Add a Google account or change its login details.
  * Make changes to installed applications.
  * Change other device settings.
  * Connect other devices via Bluetooth.
