# Inactive SIM or No Service

If your phone displays **no service** or **inactive SIM card**, it hasn't registered your SIM card. Here are several possible reasons why your SIM card isn't registered:

  * **You are a new Telenor customer**
** Our system hasn't yet registered your SIM card. Call [customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt/) at 72 100 100, and we'll help you get it activated.

  * **Your mobile is blocked**
** Your mobile is blocked, for example, if you've missed a bill or exceeded your spending limit on usage control. Log in to [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/), and pay your bill, or increase your spending limit.

  * **You've gotten a new mobile, and your old SIM card is outdated**
** You need to order a new SIM card - see your options further down the page.

  * **You haven't used your SIM card for six months or longer**
** If you haven't used your SIM card for a long period, you need to order a new one.

  * **You've cancelled your mobile subscription**
** If you've cancelled your subscription, your SIM card will be deactivated. See our subscriptions [here](/shop/abonnementer/abonnementer-til-dig/), if you want a new subscription, or contact us at 72 100 100.

## None of the above apply to you?

If none of the above apply to you, you can try to activate your SIM card by doing the following:

  * If you have a physical SIM card, you can try turning off your mobile and removing the SIM card. Gently clean the SIM card with a dry, clean cloth. Reinsert the SIM card and turn on the mobile.
  * Insert the SIM card into another mobile. If the SIM card works in the other mobile, it suggests that your mobile needs a software update.

## Get a New SIM

If you're still having problems with your SIM card, you have the option to order a new one by mail or get a new one immediately by visiting a Telenor store. A new physical SIM card costs 49 DKK.

  * **New physical SIM card**
If you want a new SIM card right away, you should visit a [Telenor store](/find-butik/), where we can help you with a new one. If you can wait, you have the option to call our customer service at 72 100 100 – we will send your new SIM card by mail the same day. You can also order a new SIM card on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/), after which it will be sent to you by mail.

  * **New eSIM**
eSIM is a smart and digital alternative to the physical SIM card. With eSIM, a new QR code is generated in a few minutes, and it doesn't cost you anything. eSIM is free with Telenor. Read more about [eSIM](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/opsatning/esim/).
