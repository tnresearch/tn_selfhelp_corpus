# Find your PUK code

There may be two reasons why you need to enter your PUK code:

  * you have just inserted your new SIM card into your tablet
  * you have entered the wrong PIN code three times

The PUK code is an 8-digit code that ensures that only you can use your SIM card.

The easiest way to find your PUK code is to log in to our self-service portal
My Telenor. The code is also in the welcome letter you received with your new
SIM card.

Once you have entered your PUK code on your iPad, your previous PIN code will be
reset, and you will be prompted to enter a new PIN code.

You can also have your PUK code provided to you if you visit one of our stores (remember
photo identification).
