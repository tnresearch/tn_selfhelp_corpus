# Get started with voicemail

## Activating voicemail

If you have a mobile subscription, your voicemail has been activated automatically
from day one.

If you have a prepaid card, your voicemail is activated when you register.
Remember that you can call voicemail even if you don't have money on your mobile.

## Personal greeting

You can record a personal greeting if you don't want to use the
default phrase that is already preset. To set up your personal
greeting, call 888 and follow the instructions. Your greeting can be
up to five minutes long.

## Settings

Click on the link below for information about all the settings you can
make in your voicemail. For some settings, you need to log in to the [My
Telenor app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/).

Here you will find information about [all the settings you can make in
voicemail](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/rostbrevladan/sa-anvander-du-
