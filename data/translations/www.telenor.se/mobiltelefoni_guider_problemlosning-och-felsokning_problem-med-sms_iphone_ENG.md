# Problems with SMS?

If you can't send or receive SMS messages with your iPhone, you can often solve
the problem yourself. Here we have collected a list of the most common
troubleshooting steps.

  * If you have a red exclamation mark next to the message, tap it and try sending again.
  * If you are using iMessage, check that you are connected to the internet via a mobile data network or Wi-Fi.
  * Try turning iMessage off and on.
  * Restart your mobile and check for any updates. Check that your phone's memory is not full.
  * Go to _Settings > Messages > Blocked_ and check that the contact is not on the block list.

## Do you have a new mobile?

If you have switched your iPhone to another smartphone, it can cause problems
if iMessage is still activated on your old iPhone. This means that SMS
messages sent to your number will not be delivered. The solution is to deactivate
iMessage, which you can do on [Apple's
deactivation page](https://selfsolve.apple.com/deregister-imessage/se/sv), if
you no longer have your old iPhone.
