Report Scam Attempts to Us

Have you received a scam message via SMS or email that you would like to report
to us? Then you can [report it here.](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/svindel/#rapporter)

By reporting messages you receive as a customer, we can more quickly detect
scam attempts and assess whether these should be blocked in our
systems. We assess all reports we receive - but unfortunately cannot respond
to all the messages we receive. If you need more help with the matter, please
contact us on customer service.

## Technical Inquiries

We are unable to process inquiries about faults with broadband, Online
email, TV, telephony or mobile **via email.**

You can try **our help pages** to see if you can solve the problem yourself,
or [contact us by phone.](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)
