# Weekly Archive T-We App

Did you miss a program on TV and want to watch it on the go? Find the
program or browse through all the content available in the
weekly archive. We're here to help you find your way.

## Which programs are in the weekly archive?

The weekly archive is available for some programs and channels for up to seven days
back in time. If a program is not available in the weekly archives due to
rights issues, this will be marked with "Not Available".

The weekly archive for channels can be found by scrolling back in the [TV
guide](/kundeservice/tv/t-we-app/tv-guide/) or by going directly from the main page in the app.

For some channels, you will be redirected to the channel's own app when you want to
play the program, e.g., the NRK TV app for NRK channels. If you do not have
these apps installed, you must do so via the Appstore or Google Play to
be able to watch the program.
