# Lost Your Mobile

If you have lost your mobile, please [contact us](/kundeservice/kontakt/)
or [visit your nearest Telenor store](/find-butik/). We will immediately
block your SIM card so no one can misuse your mobile subscription. It is possible
to have your mobile blocked 24 hours a day.

At a Telenor store, you can get a new SIM card immediately. If you order a new
SIM card through customer service, we will send it by post the same day.

**Good to know**

  * When you block your SIM card, you do not block your mobile subscription. You pay for the subscription while you wait for your new SIM card.
  * If you block your SIM card on a weekday, we will call you within 24 hours to see if we can help you further. For example, we can send you a copy of your purchase receipt.
  * If you find your mobile again, you can have your SIM card reactivated at one of our stores or by [contacting us](/kundeservice/kontakt/).
  * You can also block your SIM card via the [My Telenor App](/mit-telenor/)

## Has Your Mobile Been Stolen?

**Report the theft to the police  
**[Here](https://politi.dk/da/tyveri-og-haervaerk/anmeld-tyveri-af-genstande
"Here") you can report the theft directly to the police.

**Report the theft to your insurance company  
** When you report the theft to your insurance company, they will usually ask for:

  * Your policy number
  * Receipt for the police report
  * Receipt for the mobile
  * Description of the make and model

If you purchased your mobile from Telenor, customer service can help you obtain
a copy of your purchase receipt.
