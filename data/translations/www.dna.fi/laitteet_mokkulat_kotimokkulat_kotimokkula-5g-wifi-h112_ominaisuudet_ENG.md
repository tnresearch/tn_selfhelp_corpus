## Features

**Product Name: Huawei 5G CPE Pro H112-370**  

The Home Modem 5G WiFi H112, capable of gigabit speeds, harnesses the power
of the latest 5G subscription for you to its full potential. The modem is
ready to use right out of the box, Plug and Play style, so you can enjoy
fast internet simply by inserting your new 5G subscription's SIM card and
plugging the power cord into an outlet.  

  * 5G maximum speed from the network 2.33 Gbit/s
  * 5G maximum speed towards the network 1.25 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 1.6 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed towards the network 150 Mbit/s
  * WLAN 802.11ax/ac/a/n 4 x 4 and 802.11b/g/n 2 x 2, MIMO
  * WLAN theoretical maximum speed: 5100 Mbit/s
  * Dual-frequency WiFi antenna with six signal amplifiers
  * Two TS-9 ports for 5G external antennas
  * 2 Gigabit Ethernet (LAN) ports (one with WAN)
  * 5G and 4G LTE network technology
  * 5 GHz Wi-Fi preferred support
  * Nano-SIM card
