##

  * Read more about access and visitor registration

When you visit Telenor offices or technical sites, we register information about
you because we have a legitimate interest in controlling who is allowed into our
premises and for what reason they are visiting. In addition, it is important
for us to know how many people are in a building and approximately where they
are located, in case something happens that requires the building to be
evacuated. Visitor data is normally not disclosed to any external party, but
in the event of an incident, it may be disclosed to the police or other
relevant authority.  
  

**Consultants and suppliers at technical sites  
** As a consultant or supplier to Telenor, you may in some cases be granted
access to relevant technical sites within the scope of the agreed assignment,
by activating your own optional card. All permissions and accesses (entry and
exit) are personal.

We register the following information about you in our access card system:

    * Your first and last name
    * The organization you work for
    * Your mobile number

Your personal identity number or ID06 ID may in some cases, e.g. when visiting
protected sites, be registered in a separate authorization system. The time of
presence on the site is also registered in a separate system (sitelog).

In addition to verifying permissions, the information may be used:

    * in incidents such as burglary, theft, vandalism and the like
    * to ensure access to technical sites including access systems and key cabinets
    * attendance registration is used if, for example, an alarm goes off on the site
    * forwarding of information about permissions to, for example, property owners and security companies to enable access to external buildings where Telenor rents space.

Your personal data is deleted when the supplier themselves removes you as a
user in the system or when the contract expires. Logs that provide information
about individual accesses (entry and exit) are stored for a maximum of 3 months
after the date of visit.  
  

**Consultants and suppliers at offices  
** As a consultant or supplier to Telenor, you may in some cases be granted
access to relevant office premises within the scope of the agreed assignment,
through a personal access card. All permissions and accesses (entry and
exit) are personal.

We register the following information about you in our access card system and on
the receipt that you sign:

    * Your first and last name
    * The organization you work for
    * Your mobile number
    * Photo

Your ID document is checked when the access card is issued. No information
about the ID document is saved, however, we note that a check has been
performed.

In addition to verifying permissions, the information may be used:

    * in incidents such as burglary, theft, vandalism and the like
    * for de-identified statistics to measure visitor frequency, general movement patterns in the building and to check technical functions in door environments such as how often a door opens.

Your personal data is deleted when the access card is returned. Logs that provide
information about individual accesses (entry and exit) are stored for a maximum
of 12 months after the date of visit.  
  

**Temporary visitors to the offices  
** If you are a temporary visitor to Telenor's offices, we register the following
information about you in our visitor system:

    * Your first and last name
    * The organization you work for
    * Your mobile number
    * The name of the person from Telenor who receives you and is responsible for your visit
    * Which office you are visiting
    * Which date and time we register your visit.

Your personal data is stored for a maximum of 12 months after the date of
visit.

Information about background checks

When Telenor recruits new employees or consultants, it is important for us
to verify that the person in question has a suitable background for working
with us. Therefore, we or the recruitment firms and consulting companies we
hire regularly order so-called background checks on current candidates.
