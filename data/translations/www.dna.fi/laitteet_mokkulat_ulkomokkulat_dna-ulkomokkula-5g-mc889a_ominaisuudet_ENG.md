## Features

• With an outdoor modem, you get the best 4G and 5G connections.  
• Ready for high-speed 5G connections.  
• Easy to install, withstands frost and rain.

The DNA Outdoor Modem 5G MC889A is a modem placed outdoors that can withstand the frost of winter, the scorching sun, and rain. The outdoor modem supports the latest network technology and enables a high-speed 5G connection, so you can watch movies in 4K resolution and enjoy a top-notch gaming experience with a lag-free internet connection.

### Place the DNA Outdoor Modem 5G MC889A outdoors or indoors

The outdoor DNA Outdoor Modem 5G offers a fast internet connection. Enjoy the speed and low latency of the 5G connection. Easily attach the outdoor modem to an outside wall or pole. By connecting the Outdoor Modem and the DNA Mesh WiFi router, you get a high-speed local network in your home where even large files download quickly.

### The outdoor modem improves your 5G connection

The MC889A outdoor modem has built-in antennas. Together with intelligent antenna switching technology, the outdoor modem increases coverage and improves your internet connection.
