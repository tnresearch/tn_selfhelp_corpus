# How to Delete an E-mail Account

Here you will find information on how to cancel your Online e-mail account on
My Pages.

Keep this in mind when deleting your e-mail account

Your e-mails will be deleted if the account is deleted. If you want to save them, you can
[follow this guide](/binaries/kundeservice/e-post/oppsigelse-online-
epostkonto/veiledning%20eksportering%20av%20filer%20Online%20e-post_f50cf955-b1d7-4be7-98bf-b371daefdedb.pdf)
which shows you how to export your e-mails and contacts.

If you use your Online e-mail account as a username in other logins, you must
also remember to update these with a new e-mail address.

**Is there anyone else who uses the e-mail account you own?**
If someone other than yourself is a user of the e-mail account, you should
contact them before it is deleted. You can also transfer ownership to
another person if desired. This is done by [contacting
customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/).

## How to do it:

### 1.

[Log in to My Pages](/mitt-telenor/e-post/)

### 2.

On your e-mail overview, press **Settings** for the account you
want to delete.

### 3.

Within settings, press **Delete account** and confirm the deletion
by pressing **Yes, I am sure**.
