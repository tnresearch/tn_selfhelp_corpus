## Getting Started

**Note.** When replacing an old cable modem with a new one, the new cable
modem must be registered for use by providing the device number (CM MAC)
to customer service.

Getting Started and Installation

**Note.** Registering the modem on the DNA cable network usually takes approx.
10 minutes, maximum 2 hours.

  * Connect the white antenna cable to the modem's RF connector **3** and the other end to the data socket of the antenna socket (if the antenna socket does not have its own data socket, use the included data splitter).
  * Connect the power cord to the power outlet and connect the other end to the F-3896 modem. **6**
  * The modem will start up automatically.
  * Installation is correct and the internet connection is established when the device's internet indicator light is solid. The internet connection is now ready!
  * You can connect devices to the modem in two different ways:

  1. Connect devices with an ethernet port to the modem with an ethernet cable. This guarantees the best connection and a smooth video image. You can install the cable into a free LAN port. **1**
  2. Select the WiFi network created by the modem on your computer, tablet or mobile phone. You can find the network name and password on the label attached to the device and on the settings pages.

Setting up Wireless Network

DNA Mesh WiFi modems are encrypted in terms of WiFi network. You can find the network name and password on the label at the bottom of the device as well as on the settings pages.

You can also connect the device to the default network automatically by scanning the QR code found on the label at the bottom of the device.

The DNA Mesh WiFi modem supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz wireless WiFi networks. By default, the device has the 5 GHz network and the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz combined network turned on, so your device will choose which frequency to use.

E.g. DNA-WIFI-XXXX

Modem Placement

#### Tips for effective modem placement

  * It is advisable to place the modem in a central location well above floor level
  * Never place the modem in a closed cabinet, electrical cabinet or apartment distribution box

Modem LED Indicators

The modem's indicator lights tell you the status of the modem and the quality of the connection.

You can select dark mode for the modem, in which the device's LED indicators dim after 5 minutes of connecting to the internet. You can turn on dark mode on the device's settings page. If you have activated dark mode, the device's indicators will automatically light up in the event of an error. You can also check the status of the indicators by briefly pressing the WPS button.

## Connections and Buttons

  1. **LAN ports**  
Ethernet connectors for devices connected with a cable. Port 4 (bottom) is a 2.5 Gbps port

  2. **Reset button**  
Press for 5 seconds to restore the device to factory settings

  3. **Antenna cable RF connector**  
Connector for the antenna cable

  4. **WPS pairing button**  
Press for at least 2 seconds to activate pairing mode

  5. **WiFi button**  
WiFi On/Off

  6. **Power cord connector**
