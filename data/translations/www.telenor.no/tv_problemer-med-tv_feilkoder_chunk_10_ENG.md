#### H4101Missing rights

This error message means that the content you are trying to view cannot be played in the location you are in. One of the most common reasons for this is that you are abroad, and the content you are trying to view cannot be played abroad.

Another possible reason is that you are connected to a VPN, so the device you are using "thinks" it is abroad.
