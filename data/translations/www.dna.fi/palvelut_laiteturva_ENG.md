# SquareTrade Device Protection

SquareTrade device protection secures your mobile devices against damage from day one. SquareTrade full protection also covers theft or loss of the device.

## Making a Damage Claim

If something happens to your device, you must submit a damage claim to SquareTrade as soon as possible. If you have purchased SquareTrade full protection for your device and your device is stolen, you must provide a police report of the stolen device to SquareTrade along with the compensation claim. Please note that all damage claims have an excess, which varies depending on the purchase price of the insured product*.

*The purchase price is the normal price of the device without any possible promotional discount.

[ Submit a Damage Claim ](https://www.squaretrade.eu/fi/dna/) [ Download Insurance Terms and Conditions (pdf) ](/753910/853465/DNA+ADH%2BTheftLoss+T%26Cs+FI+Final.pdf/)

## Warranty for Repaired and Replaced Devices

Devices repaired or replaced through SquareTrade may not be covered by the manufacturer's warranty. If you have previously replaced your phone through SquareTrade, you must contact SquareTrade in the future for warranty matters, [takuu@squaretrade.fi](mailto:takuu@squaretrade.fi).

SquareTrade is committed to a two-year warranty on replaced devices from the date of commissioning, if the manufacturer's warranty has expired.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What types of damage does SquareTrade device protection cover?

The device protection covers the repair or replacement of your insured device in the event of accidental damage, as well as mechanical and electrical faults caused by normal use after the manufacturer's and retailer's warranties have expired. Full protection also covers your device if it is stolen or lost.

The device protection **does not cover** mechanical or electrical faults or damaging damage caused by

  * misuse of the insured device or its careless or negligent handling, either intentionally or otherwise
  * severe weather conditions such as thunderstorms, floods or strong winds
  * accessories or peripherals that were not an integral part of the insured device's original configuration;
  * software or programming
  * an electronically delivered virus

Is the Device Protection insurance also valid abroad?

Yes, your insurance is valid everywhere regardless of where you travel. Replacement products can be delivered to any address within the European Union. However, we can only offer repair services in Finland. We may not be able to offer device repair in remote areas. In such cases, we will inform you of how to proceed when submitting your compensation claim.

Will I incur costs for sending the device for repair?

No, there are no costs for the delivery of the replacement and broken device. We pay for shipping in both directions.

What should I do if my device breaks down?

If the insured device is damaged, has a mechanical or electrical fault, or the device is lost or stolen, you must submit a damage claim on [SqureTrade's website](https://www.squaretrade.eu/fi/dna/) as soon as possible.

In the event of theft, we need a copy of the police report as soon as possible. The police report must include at least the IMEI number (if necessary), a description of the event, as well as the date, time and place of the event.

The damage claim cannot be approved until the excess indicated in the insurance certificate has been paid. The excess must be paid by credit card when submitting the compensation claim. If the value of the replacement product is lower than the excess, we will reduce the excess amount to the level of the replacement product's value. Depending on the excess and the case-by-case circumstances of the compensation claim, you may be able to choose between repair and a replacement product.
