## Antenna Placement

*   Should only be mounted **outdoors**

*   Can be attached to the outside of a house wall next to a window, or on a window pane.

*   Should be mounted at least 2 meters (approximately 6.5 feet) above the ground. The higher the placement, the better the signal.

*   Must not be placed behind things that can interfere with the signal strength (e.g., close to trees/bushes, near other buildings, etc.)

*   The antenna must not be placed on a window where it could cause damage to people or animals if it were to fall down.

## Installation

*   The power adapter should be plugged into an **indoor** power outlet.

*   Use only the original parts you received from Telenor.

*   Cable should be run from the antenna and through the window opening. Choosing a window that is not frequently opened/closed will result in less wear and tear on the cable.

## Simple Steps for Installation

*   #### Wall or Window Mounting

Choose whether you want to mount the antenna on a wall next to a window (A), or directly on the window pane (B)

**A - Wall**

    * This is our recommended mounting method

    * Screw on the smallest bracket with four screws. The notched side of the protrusion should face down.

**B - Window**

    * Clean the window pane with the included wet wipe. The window pane must be clean and dry

    * Double-sided tape must be pressed against the window for a few seconds

    * Glue the bracket with the notched side of the protrusion facing up

    * **The tape must cure for 24 hours before proceeding**

**Common:**

    * Find a small star screwdriver

    * Insert the cable into the yellow network port and screw on the two screws on the cover

    * Attach the antenna to the bracket

*   #### Find Direction

    * Adjust the antenna according to the map in the app for the best signal strength and correct speed.

Follow the recommendation from the digital setup guide

*   #### Cable Through Window

    * Open the window, feed the cable through the window, and lay it flat against the frame

    * Connect the cable to the network port on the power adapter, labeled "POE"

    * Plug the adapter into an indoor power outlet. The light labeled "Status" should light up steadily green on the antenna within a couple of minutes.

*   #### Test Signal Strength

To ensure you get the correct speed, we need to measure the antenna's signal strength. While this is in progress, it is important that you do not touch the antenna, turn it off, or disconnect anything.

    * Press START in the app

    * This takes about 2-3 minutes

    * Follow the conclusion that appears in the app

*   #### Connect to Router

    * Connect one end of the network cable to the network port on the power adapter, labeled "LAN", and the other end should be connected to the WAN port on the back of your router

    * If you have a WiFi Router from Telenor:

      * Connect the power supply to the router and turn it on

      * Wait until the top light is green, it may take about a minute. When it lights up steadily green, the router is connected to the internet.
