## Google Play Store and Applications

### Google Play Store and Applications on the Entertainment Box

The Entertainment Box's Google Play Store contains thousands of applications, including popular games and entertainment apps. To download and use the applications you want, you need to log in to the Google Play Store with your Google account. It works the same way as the app store on your phone.

You can access the Entertainment Box's home screen, or Android home menu, by pressing the Android TV button on the remote control.

Google Accounts

By entering your Google account and allowing apps to use your location, you can take advantage of all the features of the Entertainment Box, such as downloading various apps, e.g., YLE Areena, Ruutu, mtv, Disney+, Spotify, or various games. YouTube and Netflix are pre-installed on the device. Google credentials are any username and password for a Google service, e.g., a Gmail email address and password. You can use existing Google accounts or create new ones if needed [here](https://accounts.google.com/signin/v2/identifier?passive=1209600&continue=https%3A%2F%2Faccounts.google.com%2FManageAccount&followup=https%3A%2F%2Faccounts.google.com%2FManageAccount&flowName=GlifWebSignIn&flowEntry=ServiceLogin).

You can log in to the Entertainment Box with multiple different Google accounts, but only one Google account can be used at a time.

Logging in to the Google Play Store

  1. When you are on the Entertainment Box's home screen, i.e., the Android home menu, use the buttons on the circular center button of the remote control and select the gear icon **Settings** from the upper right corner of the view.
  2. Use the buttons on the circular center button of the remote control and navigate to **Accounts and sign-in**.
  3. Select **Add account**.
  4. Follow the instructions on the screen.

Through the Settings menu of the Google Play Store, you can set, for example, parental controls and define purchase authorization if needed.

Downloading Applications

You need Google credentials to download applications. Instructions for logging in can be found in the previous section. Once you are logged in to Google, you can download applications.

  * When you are on the Entertainment Box's home screen, i.e., the Android home menu, use the buttons on the circular center button of the remote control and select **Apps** from the first row or channel.
  * Select the plus icon " **Add app to favorites** " and you will be taken to the app selection. From the list, you will find a selection of popular apps that you can easily add to your favorites by simply clicking the center button of the remote control on the app.
  * To select apps from the Google Play Store, select **Play Store** and you will be able to explore the thousands of different apps that the Google Play Store offers for Android TV. The app store's home screen has a diverse selection of the most popular apps. By scrolling down the view, you can also explore different apps through the App Categories view and browse apps related to, for example, sports, education, or health and fitness, in addition to entertainment apps. Some apps are completely free, while others are paid. Before installing an app, you can see a more detailed description of the app and its price in the app's additional information.
  * You can use the Google Play Store search function in the upper right corner of the menu. You can search by speaking or typing a search term. The search defaults to voice search, but you can switch to text search by pressing the circular center button of the remote control to the right (voice search works with the DNA Entertainment Box (Hubi 2.0) as well as with the Android TV Remote remote control app on a smartphone).
  * There are thousands of apps in the app store, i.e., all the apps published for Android TV. Even if a service does not have its own Android TV app specifically for television, many services can still be used by mirroring from a tablet or phone via Google Cast. The Entertainment Box includes Google Cast.

Once you have found the app you want, confirm the selection with the circular center button on the remote control and select Install. After installation, the app will be available to you and can be easily found on the Entertainment Box's home screen in the Android home menu under **Apps**.

Google Play Subscriptions Billing

The Google Play Store works on the Entertainment Box in the same way as on your phone. You can add credit card information to your Google Play account, so all billable subscriptions will be charged to the credit card linked to the account. These invoices will not appear on the DNA invoice.

You can define purchase authorization through the **Settings** menu of the Google Play Store if needed.

Managing the Home Screen or Android Home Menu

The Android home menu view includes, in addition to the DNA TV app, Netflix, and YouTube, other channels or views from the DNA TV app. Through the home screen, you can conveniently browse live TV broadcasts currently running on channels or easily access your recordings, if recording is included in your subscription.

Your favorite apps, which you have selected yourself, and highlights or channels from these apps also appear in the view. You can edit the view by scrolling down and from " **Edit channels** ". Channels are recommendation rows published by apps, and the channels or recommendation rows in the view can be moved up or down by selecting the desired channel and pressing left on the remote control, which will display the additional option **move**.

Game Controller Installation

You can connect an Android TV-compatible game controller to the Entertainment Box and play games as if it were a game console. Download the games you want from the Google Play app store.

  1. Turn on the Entertainment Box.
  2. Turn on the game controller.
  3. Go to Android TV settings (gear icon in the upper right corner of the Android home menu) and select Remote & Accessories and then Add Accessory.
  4. Press the pairing button on the game controller.
  5. The Entertainment Box will find the device and pair them.

After this, you can use the Entertainment Box with the game controller and play games.

Android TV Remote Control App

You can control the Entertainment Box with a separate app downloaded to your phone. You can also use Android TV's voice search with it. Voice search makes it easy to search for, for example, movies, music, or games. You can use the remote control app on an iOS or Android device.

**Set up the Android TV Remote app as follows:**

  1. Download the Android TV Remote app from your device's app store: if you are using an iOS phone (iPhone), download the app from the App Store. If you are using an Android phone (e.g., Samsung, Huawei), download the app from the Google Play Store.
  2. Open the app and accept the terms of use to continue.

  3. Turn on the Entertainment Box by pressing the power button in the upper right corner of the remote control.
  4. Make sure that the Entertainment Box and the device with the remote control app are on the same network. If the Entertainment Box is connected to the local network with a cable, both the Entertainment Box and the phone on the WiFi network must receive an IP address from the same router. With Android phones, you can also use a Bluetooth connection.

  5. 6. Select DNA Hubi from the device list. When the devices are on the same local network, DNA Hubi will be found in the device list of the Android TV Remote app.

  7. Enter the code displayed on the screen into the Android TV Remote app to connect the devices.

  8. Click **Pair device** to complete the device connection.

  9. The installation is now complete. You can use the app as a remote control and make searches using the phone's keyboard and voice search. WiFi must be enabled on the phone and the devices must be on the same network. A Bluetooth connection can also be used with Android devices.
