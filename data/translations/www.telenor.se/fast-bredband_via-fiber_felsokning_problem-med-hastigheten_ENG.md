# Speed issues

If you have **restarted the router** and checked for any
[service disruptions](/support/driftinformation/driftstorningar-pa-tv--och-
bredbandsnatet/) but are still experiencing slow browsing, you can
try the following.

  * ### Check the speed

Start by checking your browsing speed on
[Bredbandskollen.se](http://www.bredbandskollen.se). Keep in mind that the speed
shown is 80-90% of your actual speed. This is because
control traffic is sent to ensure your connection works properly.

**Tip – before using Bredbandskollen**

1\. Make sure the computer you are testing with does not have any programs or apps running
that use the internet. This could be, for example, streaming services or
file downloads.

2\. Temporarily disable antivirus programs and firewalls on the computer you
are testing with

3\. Make sure no other devices (computers, tablets, mobiles) are connected
to your broadband connection when you test

  * ### How is your computer doing?

An older computer can affect your browsing speed. If browsing is slow, it may be because the computer's operating system is old, the drivers are not
updated, or the computer has low performance.  Low free memory also affects performance.

You can double-check the browsing speed with another computer to rule out
computer problems.

  * ### Have you made any changes to your computer?

Was your internet connection faster before? Sometimes speed problems arise when installing new programs on your computer.

  * ### Operating system

Make sure your computer is always updated with the latest operating system.
This can be set to automatic.

  * ### Antivirus program

Do you have an updated antivirus program on your PC or anti-malware on your
Mac? Viruses and malware can significantly degrade performance.

  * ### Other equipment

Other equipment can contribute to a slow connection. Try connecting the computer directly to your router or fiber outlet.
