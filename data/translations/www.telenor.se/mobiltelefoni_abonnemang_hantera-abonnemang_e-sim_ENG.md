# E-sim

When you get a mobile subscription with us, you can choose between an e-sim or a "regular" plastic SIM card.

E-sim stands for "embedded sim", meaning the SIM card is built into the phone, and has several advantages.  

It's secure, as it cannot be removed from the phone. Fast, because if you shop online or by phone, you don't have to wait for a SIM card to be sent by mail. It's future-proof, as some new phones don't even have a slot for a SIM card. And, last but not least – we avoid using plastic unnecessarily! **Smart, fast and resource-efficient** then!

E-sim also works in your tablet, of course.

## Frequently asked questions about e-sim

  * How do I activate my e-sim?

It depends on what phone you have and whether you own the subscription or not.  

[All about activating e-sim](/link/1877940c8454421facd6f534e677a762.aspx)

  * How do I get an e-sim?

If you own the subscription, you can easily order an e-sim yourself in My Telenor. If you do not own the subscription, you must ask the owner to order it for you.  

Tip! First find out if your phone can have an e-sim. [Read about which devices support e-sim](/link/70cd7ea07bfc4223bdd2dbe4cac526cb.aspx)

  * How much does it cost to order an e-sim?

It costs nothing to order an e-sim. It also costs nothing to switch from a SIM card to an e-sim – or vice versa, for that matter.

  * What is required to be able to use e-sim?

Here's what you need to be able to use e-sim:

    * [A phone that supports e-sim](/link/70cd7ea07bfc4223bdd2dbe4cac526cb.aspx)
    * Wifi or mobile data
    * A QR code, which leads you to your e-sim profile, is also needed in most cases (except if you own your subscription yourself and have an iPhone, then you can activate it in the My Telenor app without a QR code)  
  

The QR code can be found by the subscription owner in My Telenor, immediately after purchasing a subscription and choosing e-sim.  

If it is an e-sim that is to be used by someone else, you can share the code with the users in the subscription. You can also have the QR code sent to your registered address. **  
  
**

  * Which devices support e-sim?

There are many devices on the market that support e-sim. For example, all [iPhones](/link/bfb891c403954bd98d4fecf08b9819a5.aspx) from XS/XR onwards, and [Samsung Galaxy](/link/688c62ef61fb4c548e76ee3d7b4de1d8.aspx) S20 and S21 series and onwards.  

[Google Pixel](/link/c3508c8161814bd69b0c4bde296d5527.aspx) and [Fairphone](/link/873eb03dac25473c90e3c3a1a5306aa1.aspx) also support e-sim.
