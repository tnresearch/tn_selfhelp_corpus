## What others are asking about right now:

  * How do I set up or change to autogiro (direct debit)? 

You can read about it on [our autogiro page](https://www.telenor.se/support/faktura/autogiro/).

  * How do I log in to My Telenor? 

If you are the owner of the subscription, log in to [My
Telenor](https://www.telenor.se/handla/tjanster/mitt-telenor/) with BankID, if
you are not the owner of a subscription, log in with Telenor ID.

  * What is the contract period and notice period for me? 

You can see any contract period in My Telenor [here on the
web](https://www.telenor.se/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).
If you have a mobile subscription, you can also see it in our
[app](https://www.telenor.se/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

  * Why is the invoice a different amount than I expected? 

The invoice amount can change from month to month, and it can be due to many
different things, but the most common is that you have **bought** something or
**changed** something in your subscription. Or simply bought a new subscription,
because the **first invoice is usually higher** than the following ones. [Read
all about unexpected or high invoice amounts
here](https://www.telenor.se/support/faktura/ovantat-eller-hogt-fakturabelopp/).

  * How do I see that my application for autogiro/e-invoice has been approved? 

In the My Telenor app, you can see how you pay your upcoming invoices. Click
on invoices, then on unpaid invoices and at the bottom you will see your
payment method. Keep in mind that if you have both mobile telephony and
broadband or TV from us, you have two customer numbers and receive two
invoices. This means you need to submit an application for each customer
number.

### Autogiro

Regarding seeing if an autogiro application has been approved, it varies from
bank to bank. Most banks send a confirmation by post when your autogiro
application has been approved. Contact your bank to find out their procedures
and how long it takes.

### E-invoice

For e-invoice, you can see directly in your online banking that everything is
okay. Keep in mind that you cannot see the actual e-invoice until we have sent
it to your bank. We do this once a month.

### If you still receive a paper invoice...

...it doesn't necessarily mean something went wrong. Depending on when you
set up autogiro or e-invoice, a paper invoice may be sent before your consent
for autogiro or e-invoice is processed by the bank.
