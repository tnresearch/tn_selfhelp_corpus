# Connect the T-We Box Mini (ADB 2840)

Always start by connecting the cables from the box to the TV. Power to the box should be connected last.

  1. Connect the antenna cable.

  2. Connect to the TV with an HDMI cable, or SCART if it is an older TV.

  3. Connect to the internet with a network cable (Ethernet cable). If you do this, the T-We app will know you are at home and you will have access to all T-We content in the app.

  4. Connect the power.

  5. Insert the program card with the gold chip facing down and in.

  6. Turn on the decoder and TV.

Now follow the installation guide that appears on the screen.
(You can also see our [installation guide](/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-
fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-mini/installasjon/).)
