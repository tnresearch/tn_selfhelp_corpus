# Home Number on Mobile

With a home number on your mobile, your friends can call you at the same price as calling other landline numbers, the difference being that the calls go directly to your mobile.

With a home number on your mobile, you get two numbers on your mobile, a landline number and a mobile number. You can answer all your calls on your mobile and you choose which number to display when you call.

The service is linked to and uses the subscription's data, SMS, MMS and calls, and the mobile subscription's price list and terms apply.

If you have a landline number with another operator, you can of course keep your phone number. To transfer a number to us, the owner of the number with the other operator must order the transfer. If you need a new number, you will receive a landline number from Telenor.

[Contact](/support/old-kontakta-oss/) customer service to transfer an existing number to your mobile.

## Order a Home Number on Mobile

You order a home number on your mobile by [contacting](/support/old-kontakta-oss/) customer service.

When you order the home number on mobile service, you get two phone numbers on the same SIM card.

The home number on mobile service does not work if you have a prepaid card.

## Set Which Number to Display

You set which number to display when you make outgoing calls by calling the voice response system on 825 from your mobile. The mobile number is the default from the start.

If you have chosen a private number on the subscription, the landline number is also private.

If you have chosen a searchable number on the subscription, the landline number is also searchable.

## Cost

Home number on mobile costs only 50 SEK per month, and is invoiced on your regular mobile bill. You cancel the service by calling us, and there is no notice period for the home number on mobile service.
