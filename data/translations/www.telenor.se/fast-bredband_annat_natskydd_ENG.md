# Network Protection

As a Telenor customer, you should feel safe. That's why Network Protection is included in all our mobile and broadband subscriptions. It's a filter that blocks harmful websites.

Network Protection is a filter that blocks websites that may contain fraud attempts, viruses, or other malicious software. This can include so-called malware (files or code intended to sabotage), scam sites, fake competitions, SMS links, and invoices. The intention is to access your card and ID details. Instead, you are redirected to a warning page.

Network Protection is included in all our mobile subscriptions and broadband*. Network Protection protects you from harmful sites when you browse via the mobile network and wifi. We recommend that you keep Network Protection turned on at all times. If you still want to temporarily turn it off, you can do so in My Telenor [here on the web](/mitt-telenor/) or in the [app](https://uu429.app.goo.gl/gnXY).  

 _*Applies to all broadband in Telenor's own networks (broadband via fiber & 5G) and in certain open networks._

## Frequently Asked Questions about Network Protection

  * What is included in the insurance that provides assistance in the event of online harassment?

The insurance includes:

    * 5 hours of free legal advice per year
    * Reimbursement for psychological help up to SEK 5,000
    * Reimbursement for lost work income up to SEK 5,000
    * Reimbursement for legal costs in legal disputes up to SEK 200,000

  * What terms and conditions apply to the security package?

Our security package is included in all new and existing mobile subscriptions, regardless of when they were taken out, with the exception of the insurance that provides assistance in the event of online harassment. The insurance is included in all mobile subscriptions taken out after 2022-05-18. If you took out your mobile subscription before that, you can easily switch to one of our updated subscriptions to get help with online harassment included. The insurance applies to the person who has the subscription and if several of you share Telenor Family, it applies to all of you.

[Here you can read the full terms and conditions for the insurance in the event of online harassment.](/link/750c93522f4e4c5cb65e1efb9d7c9518.aspx)

  * Does Network Protection work when I am connected to wifi?

Network Protection works if you are connected to wifi via Telenor's own networks, and in some open networks. If you are unsure about the type of network you have in your broadband service, you can contact our customer service on 0200 - 114 115.

  * How do I activate blocking of harmful sites?

**Activate on mobile**

The block is activated in all our mobile subscriptions from the start. If you have turned it off, turn it on again in My Telenor here on the web. Click your way to Subscription and then to Settings and payment services. Or go into the app and select Subscription and then Settings.

The blocking of harmful sites is also included in our broadband subscriptions. [See how to activate it here.](/handla/tjanster/natskydd/)

  * Does Network Protection protect my computer and other devices?

No, Network Protection only works when you browse via the mobile network. If you want good protection on your other devices, we recommend that you get Surf Safely, [read more about it here](/link/e8f2c21cdf3b4dd88eec421103d79fd5.aspx).
