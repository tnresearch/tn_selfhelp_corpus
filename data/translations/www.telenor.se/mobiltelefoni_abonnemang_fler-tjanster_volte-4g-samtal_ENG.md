# VoLTE – mobile calls with 4G

Surf quickly and talk at the same time when you call via the 4G network with the VoLTE service. If you experience poor coverage indoors, try activating VoLTE on your phone.

## What is VoLTE?

VoLTE (Voice over LTE) or voice calls via 4G is exactly as it sounds – a way to call via the 4G network. This means you get improved call coverage and even higher call quality. VoLTE/4G calls are available and automatically included in our mobile subscriptions and prepaid cards.

## Phones that have VoLTE

To be able to make 4G calls, you need a mobile phone that supports the service in Telenor's network. Each operator's solution for 4G calls is unique, so the mobile phone must support the solution that Telenor uses.

**All iPhone models** from iPhone 6 and with software update iOS 12.1 or later support VoLTE (as well as Wi-Fi calling).

Most mobile phones (from, for example, Samsung, Sony or Huawei) with **Android as the operating system** launched after 2018 and intended for use in the Nordic countries support both VoLTE and Wi-Fi calling. However, there may be exceptions. Tip! Make sure your phone is updated with the latest version of the operating system.

## Frequently asked questions and answers about VoLTE/4G

  * What is VoLTE?

VoLTE stands for "Voice over LTE" and means calls over the LTE network. LTE is another word for 4G.

  * How do I get VoLTE?

VoLTE is included in our mobile subscriptions. This means that when you sign up for a new subscription, the VoLTE service is automatically included.

For VoLTE to work, you need a phone that supports the service. On our VoLTE page you can see [which phones support the
service](/link/0e1334dd546642ca8e045f55bf902c78.aspx).

  * How do I make a VoLTE/4G call?

You call just as usual, with the only difference being that the call goes over the 4G network.

If you want to forward your calls, you can do so in the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx).

  * Will 4G calls affect my data usage?

No, a 4G call, VoLTE, does not affect your data usage.

  * Can I talk on the phone and surf at 4G speed at the same time?

Yes, it will be possible to surf at 4G speed while you talk on the phone. Previously, it was only possible to surf and talk at the same time in the 3G network.

  * Is the connection time for a call improved with 4G?

Yes, with 4G calls connect faster.

  * What will the VoLTE service cost?

The service costs like a regular call.

  * I have a prepaid card, does VoLTE work for me?

Yes, it is possible to call over 4G (VoLTE) with a prepaid card. VoLTE also works with [our subscriptions](/link/a96eaee9166b475194e657db89f197b2.aspx) as long as you have a phone that supports the service.
