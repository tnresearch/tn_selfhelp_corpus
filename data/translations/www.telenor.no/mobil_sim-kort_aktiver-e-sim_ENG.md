## What is eSIM?

[eSIM (Embedded SIM)](/mobilabonnement/sim/esim/) is a built-in, electronic
SIM card. With eSIM, you don't need to insert a physical SIM card into your
mobile, tablet or smartwatch. It is activated digitally and works just like
a traditional SIM card. You can use eSIM as Main, Twin or
Data Card.

**How do you activate eSIM?**  
**This depends on which device you are going to use eSIM on.** Is it on
the main card on your mobile or to Samsung Galaxy Watch? Then you first order
eSIM on My Pages or in My Telenor.  

This requires that you have created a [Telenor ID](/telenor-id/). If it's Apple
Watch, you start the activation on your mobile, and eSIM is ordered during
that process.

**NB! Activating eSIM also requires that you have access to BankID.**

* #### How to check if your mobile supports eSIM

    1. Open the dialer on your mobile as if you are going to dial a number

    2. Dial ***#06#**

You will get up serial numbers and numbers. If you get a line called **eID**, it
means that your mobile supports eSIM.

## How to find the QR activation code

If you are going to activate eSIM on e.g. Samsung Galaxy Watch or Google Pixel
Watch (4G LTE version), you must scan a QR code in the activation process. It
is also possible to upload an image of the QR code.

Below you will find guidance on where you can find the QR code on My Pages and
in the My Telenor app. The QR code is valid for 14 days.

**NB: If you have just ordered eSIM, it may take a few minutes before the QR-
code becomes available.**

### My Pages

1. Log in to [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/) and select _See details_ on your mobile subscription

2. Scroll down to SIM card and select the SIM card in question

3. Click on _Show QR activation code_

4. Verify with Bank ID

### My Telenor

1. Log in to the [My Telenor app](/privat/mobil/app/mitt-telenor/)

2. Select the subscription you have ordered eSIM on

3. Scroll down to SIM card and select the SIM card in question

4. Press _Activate eSIM_ at the top of the page

    * Activate on this device (Downloads eSIM profile)

    * Activate on another device (Show QR code)

5. Confirm with BankID

6. Press the desired activation

## iPhone - Have switched SIM/phone and will activate eSIM

* #### Replace current physical SIM card with eSIM

You can replace your current physical SIM card with eSIM by following the steps
below

1. **Open Settings** on your iPhone

2. Select **Mobile Data**

3. Select **Convert to eSIM**

4. Press **Convert mobile subscription**

5. Press **Convert to eSIM** again

6. Wait while eSIM is activated and your physical SIM is deactivated

7. Remove the physical SIM card from the iPhone and restart the phone

If you need to switch back to a physical SIM card, you must activate the SIM
card manually in My Telenor or on My Pages.

* #### Switching mobile and want to activate eSIM on your new iPhone (quick transfer)

If you switch to a new iPhone, you can activate eSIM by following the steps
below. Both devices must have iOS 16 or later.

If you are asked to “Transfer SIM card” while setting up a new iPhone, follow
the instructions on the screen. If you need to transfer more than one SIM,
follow the instructions below.

1. Open **Settings** on your new iPhone

2. Select **Mobile Data**

3. Select **Add eSIM**

4. Select **Transfer from iPhone nearby**

5. Follow the instructions on the previous iPhone and confirm by pressing **Continue**

6. Enter the one-time code for verification that appears on the other iPhone

7. Select the subscription to be transferred from the other iPhone

8. Confirm by pressing **Transfer number**

9. Wait a bit. You will now be automatically taken to Telenor's pages to complete the activation

10. Enter the last five digits of your social security number and press **Log in**

11. Press **Transfer subscription**

12. Press **Close**. The mobile subscription is now activated on the new iPhone, and your previous SIM is deactivated

## Activate eSIM - iPad or Tablet

We recommend that you use Data Card on iPad or tablet, Twin SIM is
intended for voice and SMS.

* #### On iPad

My Pages

We recommend that you use Data Card on iPad, Twin SIM is intended for voice
and SMS.

1. Find a **PC** and make sure the device you are going to activate the electronic SIM card on is connected to **WiFi**

2. Go to **My Pages** with the PC and **find the QR code** under **Mobile and subscription** - **Devices and SIM** - **Data Card** - **Show QR code**

3. On your iPad: Go to **Settings** - **Mobile Data** - **Add eSIM** and **scan** **QR code** with the device

4. A **Telenor eSIM profile** will now be downloaded and activated on Telenor's network

5. If you replace an existing SIM card, it will be deactivated when the eSIM card is activated

* #### On iPad

My Telenor

We recommend that you use Data Card on iPad, Twin SIM is intended for voice
and SMS.

1. Log in to the My Telenor app

2. Select the subscription you have ordered eSIM on

3. Scroll down to SIM card and select the SIM card in question

4. Press _Activate eSIM_ at the top of the page

    * Activate on this device (Downloads eSIM profile to the device you are logged in to)

    * Activate on another device (Show QR code)

5. Confirm with BankID

6. Press the desired activation again and complete

* #### On Android/Samsung Tablet

My Pages

We recommend that you use Data Card on tablet, Twin SIM is intended for
voice and SMS.

1. Find a **PC** and make sure the device you are going to activate eSIM on is connected to **WiFi**

2. Go to **My Pages** with the PC and **find the QR code** under **Mobile and subscription** - **Devices and SIM** - **Data Card** - **Show QR code**

3. Scan **QR code** with the camera on your mobile and follow the instructions on the screen

4. A **Telenor eSIM profile** will now be downloaded and activated on Telenor's network

5. If you replace an existing SIM card, it will be deactivated when the eSIM card is activated

* #### On Android/Samsung Tablet

My Telenor

We recommend that you use Data Card on tablet, Twin SIM is intended for
voice and SMS

1. Log in to the My Telenor app

2. Select the subscription you have ordered eSIM on

3. Scroll down to SIM card and select the SIM card in question

4. Press _Activate eSIM_ at the top of the page

    * Activate on this device (Downloads eSIM profile to the device you are logged in to)

    * Activate on another device (Show QR code)

5. Confirm with BankID

6. Press the desired activation again and complete

* #### Switching tablet and want to activate eSIM on your new iPad (quick transfer)

If you switch to a new iPad, you can activate eSIM by following the steps
below. Both devices must have iOS 16 or later.

If you are asked to “Transfer SIM card” while setting up a new iPad, follow
the instructions on the screen. You can also transfer eSIM from another iPad
after configuration.

**If the new iPad is logged on to the same iCloud account as the old iPad:**

1. Open **Settings** on your new iPad

2. Select **Mobile Data**

3. Select **Transfer iPad subscription**

4. If you get a list of available subscriptions for transfer, select the subscription you want to use, and press **Continue**.

5. Confirm by pressing **Transfer**

6. Follow the instructions on the previous iPad and confirm by pressing **Transfer SIM**

Wait a bit. You will now be automatically taken to Telenor's pages to complete
the activation

7. Enter the last five digits of your social security number and press **Log in**

8. Press **Transfer subscription**

9. Press **Close**. The subscription is now activated on the new iPad, while the subscription is deactivated on the old iPad

**If the new iPad is NOT logged on to the same iCloud account as the old
iPad:**

1. Open **Settings** on your new iPad

2. Select **Mobile Data**

3. Press **Transfer from iPad nearby**, if this appears

4. Follow the key selections, and on the old iPad you must enter the **verification code** that appears on the screen on the new iPad

5. On your new iPad, select the Telenor subscription to be transferred, and confirm with **Transfer**

Wait a bit. You will now be automatically taken to Telenor's pages to complete
the activation

6. Enter the last five digits of your social security number and press **Log in**

7. Press **Transfer subscription**

8. Press **Close**. Your new eSIM is now activated on the new iPad, and your previous SIM is deactivated

## Activate eSIM - Apple Watch

Before you start, make sure you have the latest version of iOS and watchOS.
The activation of eSIM may fail if the devices are not updated.

1. Open the Watch app on your iPhone

2. Press the My Watch tab

3. Inside the Watch app, select **Mobile Data**

4. Press further on **Configure mobile data**

5. Enter the last 5 digits of your social security number

6. Press **Add Apple Watch**

If you do not get the opportunity to press Configure mobile data, we recommend
closing the Watch app and going through the steps again.

## Activate eSIM - Samsung Galaxy Watch

[Order eSIM on My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/) or in
My Telenor app. You select eSIM as SIM card when you order
[Twin](https://www.telenor.no/mobilabonnement/sim/tvilling-sim/) on your
subscription.

When eSIM is ordered, you will find the QR activation code on My Pages or in
My Telenor. This code is necessary to activate eSIM.

### Galaxy Wearable

Download the Galaxy Wearable app on your phone via Google Play or Samsung
Galaxy Watch app via App Store, and connect the watch to your mobile via
bluetooth.

Follow the setup in the app, approve permissions, and scan the QR code. You
are now ready to take the watch with eSIM into use!

## Activate eSIM - Google Pixel Watch (4G LTE)

To order electronic SIM for Google Pixel Watch, you must log in to
[My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/) and order Twin (see
procedure at the top of the page). During the ordering process, select eSIM.
Remember that you must be a customer, or become a customer, to activate the
eSIM card on your Google Pixel Watch.

If you have already ordered Twin eSIM and want to connect it to your smartwatch?
You can easily do this by downloading the [Google Pixel Watch
app](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=google+pixel+watch&c=apps&hl=en)
from Google Play and follow the steps in the app.
