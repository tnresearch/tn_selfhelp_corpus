# Parental Controls

Here you will find information on how to set up parental control rules
in your Huawei B818 router.

## Log in to the Router

  1. Check that you are connected to your home network.

  2. Open a browser and enter http://192.168.1.1 in the address field, and press enter.

  3. Enter your password.

  4. The password is the same as your original wireless password. You can find it on the sticker on the back of the router after **WIFI**.   
**Note:** If you have changed the password to the menu yourself, enter the one you
have chosen.

## Setting up Rules

  1. Press **Tools**

  2. Select **Parental Control** in the menu on the left.

  3. Press the **plus sign** to set up rules.

  4. Fill in the desired time interval (e.g. 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM), Mon-Fri.

  5. Check the devices the rule should apply to, and press **OK**.
