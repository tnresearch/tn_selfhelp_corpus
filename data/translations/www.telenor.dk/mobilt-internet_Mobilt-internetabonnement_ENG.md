# Change Your Mobile Internet Subscription

If it's been a while since you purchased your subscription, it may no longer meet your requirements and needs. If you are out of contract, it is free to change your subscription, regardless of whether you want to switch to one with more or fewer GB.

Your contract period is 6 months, from the date you received confirmation of your subscription.

**Your Bill After Changing Subscription**
If you have changed your subscription, your bill may look different than usual. This is because you pay for your new subscription – and receive money back from your old one. This is perfectly normal, and you will not pay too much.
