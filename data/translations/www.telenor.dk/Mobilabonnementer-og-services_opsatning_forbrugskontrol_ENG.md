# Usage Control

Usage Control keeps track of everything that goes above your monthly
subscription. That is, the amounts that are added to your subscription price when you, for example,
call abroad or shop online and pay with an SMS.

You sign up for Usage Control on [My Telenor](/login/) or in the My Telenor
App.

## Choose your maximum yourself

It's entirely up to you how much you want to be able to spend extra per
month. Note that Usage Control must be at least 50 DKK in addition to the
included usage. Therefore, it is not possible to create a Usage Control that
blocks as soon as the included usage is used up.

When you have used 80% of your maximum amount, we will send you an SMS. And if you exceed
your maximum, your mobile will be automatically blocked within 24 hours.

If you want to change your maximum amount, you can do so via [My Telenor
](/login/) or in the My Telenor App.

## Even if your mobile is blocked, you can:

  * Receive calls
  * Call 112

What do I do if my mobile is blocked?

If your mobile has been blocked due to Usage Control, you can call us
and have it unlocked again. When the new billing period starts, your
maximum amount will return to the original.

If you want to increase your maximum amount, you can do so via [My Telenor ](/mit-
telenor/login/) or in the My Telenor App

Usage Control abroad

When you travel outside Denmark's borders, you automatically have Usage Control
abroad and a data cap. We call that Surf Control. You can read much more
about Surf Control [here](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/udland/surfkontrol-udland/).
