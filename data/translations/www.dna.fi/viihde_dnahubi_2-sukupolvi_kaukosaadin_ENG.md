# DNA Bluetooth Remote Control Support

## User Guide and Tips

The DNA Entertainment Box (Hubi 2.0) and the DNA Entertainment Box with speakers come with a
Bluetooth remote control that makes it easy to find program recommendations and your
favorites, even with voice search. Thanks to the Bluetooth connection, you can keep
the DNA Entertainment Box (Hubi 2.0) even hidden under your TV stand, as the remote control
also works without a direct connection to the device.

## Charging the Remote Control

Charge the remote control with the USB-C cable included in the package.

  * It is a good idea to charge the DNA Bluetooth remote control immediately before taking the entertainment box into use. You can use the remote control normally while it is charging. 
    * We recommend using the USB-C cable included in the package to charge the remote control. Using a different, more powerful cable, especially during initial setup, can cause problems with the remote control. You can find tips for troubleshooting further down this page. 
  * After charging, the remote control battery lasts approx. a month with normal use.

You can check the battery level by selecting:

## Getting Started

When you see the following image on the TV screen, wake up the remote control by pressing
the center button and the power button at the same time for 3 seconds.  
Then press the center button and the DNA button at the same time for 3 seconds.

After this, the remote control is ready to use.  

## Voice Search

Press the voice search button on the DNA Bluetooth remote control. Say the name of the
program you are looking for, or for example, the name of an actor. You will see the search term in the
lower left corner of the screen and the search results on the screen.

**To search within the DNA TV service:**

Select **DNA TV Menu**

Scroll to the top of the Menu

Select the magnifying glass 🔍

Search by speaking, i.e. say the name of the program you are looking for or for example
the name of an actor. You will see the search results on the screen.
