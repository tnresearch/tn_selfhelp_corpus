# Transfer TV or Broadband

Here you will find information on how to transfer TV or broadband
services.

###

Subscription Transfer

Subscription transfer is done digitally with BankID authentication. Click
[here](https://go-
printer.scrive.com/public/index?hash=5572d1f6-259e-4279-8e70-c422f681cc4f) and
follow the steps to sign your transfer with BankID. Please note that
transferor and transferee cannot be the same person.

Once the transfer has been signed by both the transferor and the transferee, it will take a few
hours before it is registered in our systems. Once the request has been registered, we can
start processing your transfer. In normal cases and with an approved
credit check, the actual transfer process will take approximately 7 weekdays.

Transfer of subscription from an estate

If you want to transfer a subscription that currently belongs to an estate, fill in
the form [Transfer of subscription for
estate](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/GetPdf.aspx?ArticleNumber=TG31188).  
  
**Tip!** Download the form and save it to your computer or mobile before you
fill it in or print it.  
  
You will also need to attach a death certificate, which you order via the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket).
Ensure that the transferee is included on the death certificate.  
  
Email the certificate and completed form to us via our
[email form,](/link/315e5eb9c6e647d7bab050c041bfc2fe.aspx) or by post
to Telenor, FE 100, 106 56 Stockholm. Mark the envelope with "transfer".  
  
Once the transfer is completed, you will receive confirmation via SMS.  
  
Please note that any installments on hardware such as mobile phones, tablets,
Apple TV, and similar, will be finally invoiced upon transfer.

Transfer of installation of Telenor-owned village fiber

Do you have an agreement with us for **future fiber installation**, but want to transfer
it to someone else? Then you should fill in [this transfer form](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/Pdf/TA32160.pdf)
and email it to us using our
[email form](/link/315e5eb9c6e647d7bab050c041bfc2fe.aspx). You can also
send it by post to Telenor, FE 110, 106 56 Stockholm. Mark the envelope with
"transfer".

Don't forget that the form must be signed by both parties.  
  
**Tip!** Download the form and save it to your computer or mobile before
you fill it in or print it.

Good to know about transfer

  * Transferring subscriptions is free of charge.
  * Transferor and transferee cannot be the same person.
  * The person taking over the subscription also takes over its terms and payment responsibility. Therefore, we perform a credit check before the transfer is approved.
  * If you want to transfer a subscription but do not have BankID, you can print a form [here.](https://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/Pdf/TF921080.pdf)

After the form is filled in, you can [email](/support/old-kontakta-oss/skriv-
till-oss/) it to us or send it by post to:

Telenor  
FE 110  
106 56 Stockholm.
