Credit assessment

|

Before entering into an agreement with us, we may assess your ability to pay for the product or service you order from us.

We collect information from credit reference agencies. The credit decision made is normally an automated decision based on information about, for example, income, debt balance and any remarks. We are informed of whether you are approved or rejected.

If you believe that the automated credit decision is incorrect, you can request that the decision be reconsidered by us through a renewed assessment. You have the right not to be subject to automated decision-making, provided that we cannot demonstrate to you that an automated decision is necessary to enter into or fulfill an agreement between you and us or is otherwise supported by law in Sweden or within the EU.

If you are already a customer with us and want to order something more (which requires a credit assessment), we collect a credit report as above. We may also make a check based on information we have with us, e.g. which products and services you already have with us and how you have previously made payments.
