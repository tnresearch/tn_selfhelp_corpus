# Activate VoLTE on your iPhone

VoLTE (Voice over LTE) or voice calls via 4G is, as the name suggests, a way to make calls over the 4G network.

To be able to make VoLTE calls, you simply need to [update the software](~/link/5388a50b85cc443786398e78f471d17a.aspx) on your iPhone to the latest version. You may now be asked if you want to accept new operator settings. Once you have done so, VoLTE calls should be activated on your iPhone.

Want to double-check that it really is? Go to **Settings** > **Cellular** > **Cellular Data Options** > **Voice & Data**. There, **4G** or **5G** should be selected.

If you do not get the question about whether you want to accept new operator settings, or if you have already chosen not to accept them and now regret it, you can go to **Settings** > **General** > **About**. Then the question will appear.
