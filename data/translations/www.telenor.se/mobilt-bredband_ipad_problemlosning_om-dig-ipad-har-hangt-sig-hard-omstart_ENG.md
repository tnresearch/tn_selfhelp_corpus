# Has your iPad frozen?

Is it not responding to swipes or button presses? A hard restart may be the solution to the problem.

Force your iPad to restart by holding down both the sleep/wake button on the side and the home button on the front for at least 10 seconds.

Keep the buttons pressed until the white apple (Apple logo) appears.

If you need more help, [Apple shows you how here](https://support.apple.com/sv-se/ht201412 "Apple's support pages").
