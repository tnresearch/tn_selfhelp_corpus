# Replacing Your T-We II Remote Control

Here we help you replace your old remote control and connect the new one to your TV.

[Use the same remote control for your TV ➔](https://www.telenor.no/kundeservice/tv/dekodere-og-fjernkontroller/t-we-boks-ii/fjernkontroll/bytte-fjernkontroll/#tv)

## Video Guide

If the guide on this page doesn't work, you should try resetting the remote control before trying again.

  * #### Reset the Remote Control

 **Here's how to reset the remote control to factory settings:**

This will restore the remote control to its original settings.

    1. Hold down the 1 and 6 buttons until the **[On/Off]** button labeled **DECODER** on the remote control starts to light up.

    2. Then press 9 - 8 - 1. This will delete all data stored in the remote control, including the pairing. The light around “DECODER” will flash to confirm and then turn off.

## Step by Step

Guide with text and images

## 1. Turn on Your TV and T-We Box II

Turn on the power switch on the back of the T-We box and turn on the TV.

## 2. Connect the Remote Control

  1. Hold the new remote control near the box (approx. 50 cm).

  2. Press **MENU** and **OK** until the **On/Off Decoder** key (top left on the remote control) starts to flash.

  3. Release the buttons when the flashing stops.

## Using the Same Remote Control for TV and T-We Box

You can program the T-We Box remote control to also control the TV, so you don't have to deal with multiple remote controls. Press the play button on the video below, or read the step-by-step guide.

  * #### Using the Same Remote Control on T-We Box II and TV

    1. Turn on the TV and T-We box

    2. Hold down the number keys **[** 1 **]** and **[** 3 **]** simultaneously for 3 seconds until the light around the **[On/Off TV]** button starts to light up. The remote control is now in programming mode.

    3. Hold down the **[On/Off TV]** button on the remote control until the TV turns off. This may take several minutes, depending on the type of TV you have. As soon as the TV turns off, release the button. It is important to release the button immediately! If you release the button too late, the remote control will think it hasn't found the right TV and will continue to search inside the remote control until the TV turns off. This can take several minutes, depending on the type of TV you have. But once the TV turns off, release the button. 

    4. Turn off the decoder. When you turn the decoder back on, the TV will also turn on automatically if it supports HDMI-CEC.

    5. Now try if the volume buttons work on the TV.  

 **If the remote control does not work as expected, try programming it again.**

    1. Press the numbers **[1]** and **[6]** simultaneously until the light around [On/Off decoder] lights up. Enter the numbers **[9 9 6]**.

    2. The light around **[On/Off TV]** will now flash twice. Then you can program it again.

 **After the remote control is programmed, you will have access to the following TV functions:**

    * On/Off

    * Input (select signal source)

    * Volume up

    * Volume down  

If you want to adjust the volume for the box, hold down the **[Shift]** key while pressing one of the volume buttons.

Many other functions on the TV (such as menu, color buttons, and number buttons) can now also be controlled with the box's remote control. To select a TV function, hold down the **[Shift]** key on the remote control while pressing the desired command.
