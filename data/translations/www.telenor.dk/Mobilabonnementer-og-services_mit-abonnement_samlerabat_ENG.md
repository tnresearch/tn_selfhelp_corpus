# Bundle Discount

Bundle your family's mobile subscriptions with Telenor and get the Best price.
The Bundle Discount also applies to housemates, neighbors, or friends – as long as
all mobile subscriptions are linked to one owner, there are no restrictions.
The Bundle Discount only applies to mobile subscriptions and cannot be combined with other
discounts or offers.

For each additional mobile subscription linked to your bill, you save 50 DKK.
You get a discount as soon as you sign up for a second mobile subscription.

How much can we save?

You save 50 DKK for each mobile subscription you purchase in addition to the first.

When do I get the Bundle Discount?

You get the Bundle Discount when you sign up for a second mobile subscription. For each additional
mobile subscription linked to your bill, you save 50 DKK.

**Note!** If you have one or more mobile subscriptions that you purchased in
connection with a campaign, you must change to a mobile subscription without a discount
on [My Telenor](/mit-telenor/login/) or contact customer service on 72 100
100, and we will help you renew your subscriptions so you can get the
Bundle Discount.

How do I get the Bundle Discount?

When you sign up for your second mobile subscription with Telenor, you automatically get
the Bundle Discount – it requires that the same person orders the
subscription. Note that the Bundle Discount only applies to subscriptions that are not
linked to other discounts and offers.

Does it have to be the same type of mobile subscription?

No, it doesn't have to be. You can freely mix all mobile subscriptions that
are not linked to other discounts or offers.

Can a friend and I team up and get a Bundle Discount?

You can team up with your friend. However, it is important that the subscriptions
are ordered by one of you to get the discount.

How do I switch my old Telenor subscriptions to new ones with
Bundle Discount?

If you have one or more mobile subscriptions that you purchased in connection with a
campaign, you can change them in self-service at [My Telenor](/mit-
telenor/login/). You can also contact customer service on 72 100 100, and we will help
you renew your mobile subscriptions so you can get the Bundle Discount.
