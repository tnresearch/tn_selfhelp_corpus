##

  * Read more about information for the next of kin

A Telenor employee has designated you as a person we can contact if an incident
occurs that you should be aware of. This could be an accident or a health
problem that our employee has experienced.

We have noted your name, the contact details for you provided by our employee,
and your relationship in cases where our employee has provided such
information. The information will be deleted or updated when we receive
information about changes from you or our employee regarding which contact
person and contact method they want us to use.

Primarily, our employee's immediate manager has access to your personal data;
it is not available to all our employees.

The legal basis for our processing of your personal data is a legitimate
interest for us to facilitate contact with relevant relatives, especially in
the event of workplace incidents. Telenor assesses the risk of privacy
infringement in connection with our processing of your personal data as
limited, but you can of course object to the registration. Contact us in such
cases via **personuppgifter@telenor.se**

Updating this privacy policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will publish an updated version
here. In the event of major changes, we will also notify you of the change in
another way (for example, through special information on the invoice, via
email or another suitable channel). The privacy policy was last updated on
April 29, 2025.
