# Net Protection protects you and your data

The service reduces the risk of, among other things, identity theft, fraud from fake websites, phishing, and hacker attacks.

## How the Net Protection and Net Protection+ security filters work

The security filters always check if the websites you are trying to access are unsafe. If you click on an unsafe website, the security filter will block the attempt and stop you before you are tricked.

Even though Net Protection stops many fraud attempts, it is wise to take precautions when you are online. **You can read more about this and Net Protection+ by clicking the button below**.

NB: Net Protection+ must be activated for you to be extra protected online - both at home and on the go.

[Read more about Net Protection +](/nettvern/)

### Wondering if Net Protection is activated?

Make sure you are logged into your home network or mobile network before testing.

# Activate or deactivate Net Protection+

### Mobile

**For mobile subscriptions, Net Protection can be activated and deactivated in the My Telenor app or on My Pages.**

* Mobile

#### Activate/Deactivate Net Protection+

\- My Telenor

    1. Open the My Telenor app

    2. Select **Security** in the menu at the bottom

    3. Select **Net Protection+**

    4. Select the subscription you want to activate/deactivate

    5. Select the desired **security filter**

    6. When activating Net Protection+, you must put your phone in airplane mode for a few seconds before turning airplane mode off again to complete the activation. You can also restart your phone

* Mobile

#### Activate/Deactivate Net Protection+

\- My Pages

    1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/mobil/)

    2. Select the subscription in question

    3. Press **Manage** under Services - Net Protection+

    4. Press **Order this service / Cancel this service**

    5. When activating Net Protection+, you must put your phone in airplane mode for a few seconds before turning airplane mode off again to complete the activation. You can also restart your phone

## Broadband

**For broadband subscriptions, you can turn Net Protection+ on/off at** [**wifi.telenor.no**](https://wifi.telenor.no) **.  
** NB: You must be connected to WiFi at home to be able to activate or deactivate Net Protection+

* Broadband

#### Activate/Deactivate Net Protection+

\- wifi.telenor.no

    1. Log in to [wifi.telenor.no](https://wifi.telenor.no)

    2. Select **Net Protection+**

    3. Select the degree of security filter by pressing **Net Protection+**

    4. Press **Activate Net Protection+**

    5. Net Protection+ is now activated!

**If you want to change the degree of security filter, just press the desired level and save the selection.**

# Activate and deactivate Net Protection

We do not recommend that you deactivate the service, but if you still want to do so, the guides below will explain how.

### Mobile Subscription and Mobile Broadband

**For mobile subscriptions, Net Protection can be activated and deactivated in the My Telenor app or on My Pages.**

### Broadband

**To activate or deactivate [Net Protection](https://www.telenor.no/nettvern/) on your broadband, you must be connected to your router at home.**

* Broadband

#### Have a router from Telenor

\- WiFi Router II, WiFi Router, Zyxel 8702

**Here's how to deactivate Net Protection on your router:**

    1. Log in to [wifi.telenor.no](http://wifi.telenor.no/)

    2. Select **Net Protection+**

    3. Under _Select degree of security filter_, press **Deactivate**

    4. Press **Deactivate security filter**

    5. Confirm by pressing **Deactivate** in the popup/dialog box

**Manual DNS settings:**

    1. Log in to [wifi.telenor.no](https://wifi.telenor.no)

    2. Select **Advanced settings** from the menu at the top

    3. Select **DNS settings**

    4. Enter **Primary** DNS address under **Custom DNS servers** (this is: 130.67.15.198)

    5. Enter **Secondary** DNS address under **Custom DNS servers** (this is: 193.213.112.4)

    6. Select **Save**

**Are you using IPv6?**
To deactivate Net Protection on IPv6, the following DNS addresses must be entered in addition to those described above:

    * Primary IPv6 DNS: 2001:4600:4:fff::53

    * Secondary IPv6 DNS: 2001:4600:4:1fff::53

* Broadband

#### Have a router from another provider

To activate or deactivate Net Protection, you must log in to the web interface of your router. Check out the support pages from the provider of your router for information on the procedure for updating DNS settings.

**To deactivate Net Protection, you must update these two:**

    * Primary DNS address: 130.67.15.198)

    * Secondary DNS address: (this is: 193.213.112.4)

On your router, these fields may be called "Primary DNS", "Static DNS", "DNS Server 1 and 2" or similar.

**Are you using IPv6?**
To deactivate Net Protection on IPv6, the following DNS addresses must be entered:

    * Primary IPv6 DNS: 2001:4600:4:fff::53

    * Secondary IPv6 DNS: 2001:4600:4:1fff::53

**Activate Net Protection:**

If Net Protection is not activated, first try turning your router off and on. If the problem persists, this may be because your router has settings other than those automatically delivered from Telenor.

To activate Net Protection on a router, you can follow the same procedure as when deactivating.

    1. Find guidance on where to enter the DNS address on your router

    2. When you get to this point, you should choose that **the router retrieves settings automatically from the ISP**.

    3. Save the settings, and then turn the router off and on.

DNS addresses that permanently have Net Protection activated are:

    * Primary DNS: 148.122.16.253

    * Secondary DNS: 148.122.164.253

These can also be used instead of retrieving settings automatically from the ISP.
