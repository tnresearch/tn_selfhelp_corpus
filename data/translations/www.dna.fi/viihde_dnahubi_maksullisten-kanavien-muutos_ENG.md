## Changes to the way of watching paid channels with the DNA Entertainment Box (Hubi
1.0)

From 7 November 2024, it will not be possible to watch
paid channels via the cable network with the DNA Entertainment Box (Hubi 1.0). Watching paid channels will be possible
after the change via an internet connection (IPTV). The change in the way of watching also applies to the V Sport+ Suomi channel included in the DNA TV subscription and
the Monthly Channel, which changes monthly.

Devices that are currently used to watch paid channels via the cable network will
automatically switch to the correct way of watching at the time of the change. The change will not
cause interruptions in channel viewing.

The removal of cable network viewing applies only to paid channels, i.e.
basic channels can still be watched both via the cable network and
via the internet connection (IPTV).

If you want to watch paid channels on the entertainment box via cable after the change,
you can upgrade your device to a newer model by contacting
our free sales service at 0800 550 044 (service hours weekdays from 8:00 a.m. to
6:00 p.m.).
