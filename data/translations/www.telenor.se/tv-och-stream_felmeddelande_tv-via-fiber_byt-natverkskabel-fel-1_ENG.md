# Replace Network Cable

There may be a fault with your network cable – try replacing it with another one.

**Would you like us to send you a new network cable?**
[Email us](/support/old-kontakta-oss/mejla/) and tell us that you
need a new network cable. Remember to state how long a cable you need.
Ordering a new network cable is free of charge.

Of course, you can also purchase a network cable yourself if you prefer.
