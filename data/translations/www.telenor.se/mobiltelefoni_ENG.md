# Everything about mobile telephony and your mobile subscription

Hundreds of customers find what they need here every day. Stay a while and look
around.

## Fix it yourself in My Telenor

In My Telenor you can among other things:

  * Check how much data you have used
  * Allocate and buy more data
  * See when you can upgrade your mobile
  * See how many installments you have left
  * Read messages regarding your mobile subscription

My Telenor is available both [here on the web](/mitt-telenor/) and as an
[app.](/handla/tjanster/appar/mitt-telenor/)
