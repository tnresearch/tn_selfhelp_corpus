# Back up

Backing up your mobile is an important step that many often forget. With
a constant growth of personal information, pictures and contacts on our
mobile devices, the risk of data loss is high.

By regularly backing up your mobile, you ensure that you
can get your valuable data back if an accident happens. A
backup stored in the cloud can then be restored wirelessly to your
new mobile.

This guide shows you how to do it in the **latest operating system (iOS)** from
Apple. So feel free to start by making sure it is installed on your mobile –
not least so that it works as well and safely as possible.  
  
[How to update
the operating system](/support/mobiltelefoni/guider/sakerhet/valj-
mobil/iphone/uppdatera-operativsystemet/)  
  
Now you have the latest iOS installed, right? Then let's go!

  * ### Go to the home screen

  * ### Select "Settings"

  * ### 3. Select the top option (where your name is)

  * ### Select "iCloud"

  * ### Select what type of data you want to back up

  * ### Select "iCloud Backup" at the bottom

  * ### Tap "Back Up Now"
