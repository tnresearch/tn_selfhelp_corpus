# Data Usage Abroad

How much of your data can you use abroad when you have Mobile Broadband?
And what does it cost in countries outside the EU/EEA? Here you will find information about what
applies.

You can use parts of your data allowance in the EU/EEA as well as in Thailand, Malaysia,
Myanmar (Burma), USA, Switzerland, Turkey, Andorra, Serbia and Montenegro.  
  

Mobile Broadband | Data in EU/EEA  
---|---  
Mobile Broadband 15 GB | 15 GB  
Mobile Broadband Unlimited | 50 GB  
  
  
We have based it on what the EU says regarding data allowance per subscription
(calculated on monthly cost versus GB price in the EU). Then we have added extra
data allowance to better adapt to our customers' surfing habits.

If you have Mobile Broadband Home with Unlimited data, you cannot use it
abroad.  
  
If you want to use your mobile broadband in other countries, it costs according to
the price list below.
