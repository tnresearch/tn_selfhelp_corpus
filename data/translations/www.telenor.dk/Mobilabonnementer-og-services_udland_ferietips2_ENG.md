# Travel Tips

As a Telenor customer, you have a number of services included in your subscription
that make your trip abroad safer. With Roam Away, Roam Away+ and Surf Control abroad, we help keep your international roaming bill down.

## Roam Away in 47 countries and Roam Away+ in 55 countries

With Roam Away, you can freely make calls and
send SMS and use up to a specific amount of data in the included countries in 47 countries, and with Roam Away+ in 55 countries. See
[My Telenor](/mit-telenor/) for more information about the data allowance in your
subscription.

All subscriptions except Lillenor and pay-as-you-go have Roam
Away or Roam Away+. Read more about [roaming with Roam Away and Roam Away+
here](/shop/landingpage/mobil/roaming/).

## Extra data outside of Roam Away countries

Are you travelling to a country that is not included in Roam Away or Roam Away+? It
is still possible to use data outside of Roam Away countries and avoid
high prices. You can do this by purchasing extra data packages, so you don't have to worry about the price of every single MB. If you have not purchased a
data package and have roaming enabled when you are abroad, you will
automatically receive the data package, Travel Data 24 hours, for DKK 100 per
data package daily. Check your next destination here or log in to My Telenor and see
your options.

## On ferries

When you sail on ferries in international waters, it costs extra to use
your phone – so put it on airplane mode or turn off data and roaming if you
don't want to pay. On a large number of routes, you will automatically receive 500 MB travel data in
24 hours for DKK 29, if you choose not to turn off data and roaming. Check
the routes here under Ships (MCP).

## Surf Control abroad

To give you extra security on your trip, your subscription is automatically
registered for Surf Control abroad. You can pre-determine how much you
want to spend on data abroad per month. If you do not set a limit yourself,
Surf Control will ensure that your bill for data used abroad never exceeds DKK 450 per month.

[More about Surf Control abroad.](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-
services/udland/surfkontrol-udland/)

## Tips and tricks for using data

Remember this before your trip

  * Update your mobile phone so you have the latest version of the operating system
  * Download the most important maps and apps for your vacation
  * Download entertainment for the flight/drive home from home
  * Turn off "push" functions and emails before enabling data roaming
  * Turn off automatic synchronization and updates
  * Be aware of the use of emoticons/emojis. Each individual emoji can fill a lot of characters - one emoji can sometimes fill more than a standard SMS of 160 characters
  * When you are abroad, always use the country code on all calls. Save your contacts with the country code (e.g. +45 or +47) in front of the phone number

What you can do with 1 GB of data

  * Stream on Netflix or HBO in Standard Definition for 1 hour
  * Find your way for 16 days with Google Maps
  * Send or receive 1,000 images on Snapchat
  * Listen to your favorite song on repeat for 20 hours
  * Check the weather on dmi.dk 10,000 times
  * Retrieve 90,900 emails (or 10,000 if there is a Word document attached)
