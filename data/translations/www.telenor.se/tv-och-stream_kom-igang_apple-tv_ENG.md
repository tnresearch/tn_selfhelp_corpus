# Watch Telenor Stream via Apple TV

If you want to use your Apple TV to watch streamed TV from us, you've come to the right place! Here you'll find everything about how to get started and get the most out of Telenor Stream.

Getting Started

  1. [Activate Telenor Stream here](/aktivera-stream/)
  2. Download the Telenor Stream app on your Apple TV.
  3. Start watching!

Good to know

  * It doesn't cost extra to create a streaming account with Telenor if you are already a TV customer with us. The only additional cost is if you rent a movie.
  * When using Apple TV, we recommend at least 10 Mbit/s in surfing speed for a good streaming experience. You can easily test your speed at [Bredbandskollen.se](http://www.bredbandskollen.se/ "Bredbandskollen").
  * The Telenor Stream app works on fourth generation Apple TV - and later generations of course!
  * Connect your Apple TV with an Ethernet cable. That's the best way.
  * Having problems with streaming? Check if you have the latest version of the app, and update if necessary.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  * Some channels are missing when I log in to Telenor Stream – why?

If you can see some channels on your TV but not in Telenor Stream, it's because we haven't been granted permission to distribute them there.

  * How do I change my login details for Telenor Stream?

You can change both your username and password for Telenor Stream in [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).

  * Can I stream to multiple devices simultaneously?

Yes, you can stream on up to two devices simultaneously with your subscription.

  * What can I stream?

You can watch most of what's included in your TV package on Telenor Stream. In some cases, a single channel in the TV package may be missing. This is because we have not been granted permission to stream them from the rights holder.

You can also easily [rent movies via the streaming service](/link/35b18662a05f46e58f582664db0643f4.aspx).

  * Where can I see which movies I've rented?

Log in to [My Telenor](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx). There you will find your invoices. The invoice will show which movies you have rented.
