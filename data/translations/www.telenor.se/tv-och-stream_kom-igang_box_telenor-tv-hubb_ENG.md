## Frequently asked questions about the Tv-hub

  * What do I need to be able to use the Tv-hub?

You need a broadband connection of at least 50 Mbit/s and the Tv-hub connects
wirelessly via wifi.

  * How do I connect the Tv-hub?

Follow our step-by-step guide on how to install the tv-hub by [clicking
here.](/link/aa399c11ffa743a9a55e7e0e2be8418e.aspx)

  * Can't change the volume?

_If_ you cannot adjust the volume with the TV-hub's remote control and possibly
get a notification about "Configure remote control buttons" each time you press
the volume buttons, you need to do the following:

1. Go to Settings (the cogwheel) -> "Configure remote control buttons"
-> select "Add device"

2. Select device and manufacturer

(If you have, for example, a TV from Andersson or if your TV model is not
included, you can try the Vestel brand).

3. Choose to configure volume

Make sure you have a reasonable volume set on the TV (with the TV's remote
control)

Now test all available codes until you hear that you can adjust the volume
with the TV-hub's volume buttons. Press Yes to confirm that it works.
Otherwise, press No and another code will be tested and you try again to
adjust the volume in the same way. Repeat these steps until it starts
working.

Hopefully you will find a code that works to adjust the volume on your TV.
When you have confirmed that it works, you will be taken to the guide's
homepage where you press "Done". Make sure you do NOT configure the On/Off
button.

But if you have tried all available codes for your TV manufacturer but still
haven't gotten it to work, you will unfortunately have to continue using your
TV's remote control to adjust the volume.

  * How does the remote control for the Tv-hub work?

There are number buttons to change channels, a button to turn subtitles on and
off, buttons to fast forward in programs when you are not watching live, and
more. There are also shortcut buttons to some popular apps, YouTube, Netflix
and SVT Play. There is also a button that takes you directly to an overview
of all your apps so you can easily find what you are looking for.

  * How do I get local channels/local news?

Currently, we have local news on SVT's Live TV. If you watch back, the
Stockholm news will be shown.

If you want to watch SVT's local broadcasts afterwards, they are available in
the SVT Play app or via the Tv-hub by pressing the Home button and then going
to "News and current affairs" and searching for the local news you want to
see.

For TV4, we have no regional news or weather.

  * How do I get text-tv?

Text-tv is not a pre-installed function in the Tv-hub, but you can easily
download it yourself. Search for "Text-tv" in the Google Play Store and
choose the app you want to download. Currently, there are several different
apps for text-tv to choose from.

  * Can I fast forward and pause on 4an, 7an and 12an?

On 4an, 7an and 12an you cannot fast forward or pause when watching a
program.
TV4 does not allow you to skip commercials and due to technical limitations
right now, we have had to stop all these functions.
We are working to change the technology to allow you to fast forward and pause
in the program but still prevent it in commercials.

  * How do I download apps?

To be able to download apps, you need a Google account. [Read more about Google
accounts and how to create one
here.](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441?hl=sv)

**Here's how:**

**1. Add your Google account to the Tv-hub**. To do this, you can go
two ways, either click the button at the bottom right of the remote control
(the button for all apps), or click the settings button.

**2. Select the Google Play Store**

**3. Enter your email address and password** using the keyboard that appears
on the screen, or use your mobile phone or computer to log in.

**4. Now you are logged in to the Google Play Store** and can search for the
app you want.

**Adding/installing apps:**

**1. Click the button at the bottom right of the remote control** (all
apps). Then select the Google Play Store.

**2. Find apps** by either browsing the existing apps (use the navigation
buttons on the remote control), or by clicking the magnifying glass icon to
search for new apps using voice or the keyboard that appears on the screen.

**3. Select the app you want to download** and click OK on the remote
control. Then select install and follow the instructions.

**4. When the app is installed** you will find it under "All apps" (the
button at the bottom right of the remote control) or in the left menu on the
home screen.

You can visit their website by clicking [here.](https://play.google.com/store/apps)
to get an overview of the apps available on Google Play.

  * How do I use Chromecast?

With the built-in Chromecast functionality in the tv-hub, you can stream
content that you do not have access to via your tv package. To stream content
via Chromecast, the device used to stream must be connected to the same
wifi network as the tv-hub.

**Here's how:**

1. First select the app you want to use. The app you are using must also
support streaming to Chromecast. If you do not see the Chromecast icon in the
app you are using, make sure you have the latest version installed.

2. Then click the icon at the top right and select your Chromecast device.

3. Start a clip/program or function in the app you have chosen and start
streaming.

The Chromecast built into the tv-hub is a Chromecast Ultra.
