# How voicemail works abroad

Receiving messages abroad is always free. However, you pay the same price as when you call home to Sweden (from the country you are in) if you want to listen to your voicemail messages.

Therefore, you do not need to turn off or disable voicemail even if you travel abroad. See [full prices abroad](/roaming/).

## Frequently asked questions about voicemail abroad

  * Can I temporarily disable voicemail?

Of course you can.

### For mobile telephony

Log in to the [My Telenor app](/link/45841c7ae4b544a1af75d6856f4a54a8.aspx)
and select "Settings" and then "Voicemail and call forwarding". Then slide
the slider to the right so that "Never" is selected.

### For home telephony via broadband

[Go to guide for
home telephony](http://mediearkivet.telenor.se/Data/mw/GetPdf.aspx?ArticleNumber=TA921041)

  * What is the cost of forwarding/call forwarding to voicemail?

The cost is the same as your normal per-minute rate.

  * How do I remove forwarding/call forwarding to voicemail?

### Remove forwarding to voicemail (mobile)

[Log in to the My Telenor app](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx
"My Telenor") and click on settings in the menu and select "Voicemail and
call forwarding" to remove the forwarding to voicemail. It is not possible to
change settings via our customer service on the phone.

### Remove forwarding to another phone number (mobile)

[Log in to the My Telenor app](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx
"My Telenor") and click on settings in the menu and select "Voicemail and
call forwarding" to remove a forwarding to another phone
number. It is not possible to change settings via our customer service on
the phone.

To remove your forwarding, slide the slider all the way to
the right.

### For home telephony via broadband

To enable call forwarding:

    * Press 1. The call forwarding function is enabled

To disable call forwarding:

    * Press 2. The call forwarding function is disabled
