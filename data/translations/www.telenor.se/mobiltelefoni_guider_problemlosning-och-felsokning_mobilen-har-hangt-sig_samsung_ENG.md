# How to restart your Samsung

If your phone has frozen or feels slow, you may need to restart it. The restart will not affect the phone's content.

You can restart your phone in three ways:

## Turn off and on again

  1. Hold down the power button and select "Turn off".
  2. To turn the phone on again, hold down the power button until the Android symbol appears.

## Hard restart

  1. On some models, hold down the power button, on other models you should hold down both the power button and the volume up button.
  2. Wait until the phone turns off.

## Remove the battery

If you have a mobile where the battery can be removed, it may be a solution if nothing else works.
