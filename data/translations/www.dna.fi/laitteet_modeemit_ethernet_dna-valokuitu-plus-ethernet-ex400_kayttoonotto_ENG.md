## Getting Started

### Installing the Modem in an Ethernet Location

  1. In your apartment, there is a connection cabinet (often in the apartment's electrical cabinet) where you can determine which of the apartment's network sockets your home internet connection will come from. You can also get a connection to multiple network sockets by purchasing a [DNA Switch](https://kauppa4.dna.fi/Nettiyhteydet-ja-laitteet/Modeemit-ja-mokkulat/DNA-Kytkin-ethernet-talouksiin-TL-SG108/p/VKYT00001).
  2. Connect an ethernet cable (RJ45) from the ethernet socket in the room to the WAN port of the modem.
  3. Connect the power cord to a power outlet and connect the other end to the power connection of the EX400 modem.
  4. Press the power button on the modem. The lights on the modem will flash for about 30 seconds. The installation is successful when the status indicator light on the modem turns solid green. The installation of the modem and the internet connection is now complete! 

When the update light (status indicator) is flashing, an update is available for the modem. You can start the update by touching the indicator light.

## Meaning of the Lights on the Front Panel

  1. During startup, "Status" is yellow. This indicates a waiting state, where the device has not yet started and has not received an IP address. Immediately at the beginning of startup, the LED light is red for a few seconds.  
  
When the device has fully started and received an internet connection, the "Status" light
turns solid **green**.  
  
If a fault occurs in the device or the internet connection is lost, the "Status" light
turns solid red.

  

  2. When the device detects a software update, the modem's "Update" light will flash slowly green. The user should then press the "Update" button to start the software update.  

# Device Connections

  1. **WAN connection** for example to connect another modem to the EX400 or in Ethernet locations an ethernet (RJ45) cable to connect to the wall socket.
  2. **Internal network** ethernet port. Connection to a computer, set-top box or even a game console.
  3. **Reset button** on the back of the device resets the modem to factory settings. If you have forgotten the changes you made to the modem settings, e.g. the password, press a sharp-pointed object (e.g. a straightened paper clip) lightly for 15 seconds into the hole of the reset button on the back panel of the modem. Resetting the modem restores all original factory settings.
  4. **Power connector** for the device's own power adapter. 
  5. **Power switch** (power on / off). If you turn off the power, always wait at least 15 seconds before turning the power back on. 
  6. **USB ports.** You can connect a USB modem or, for example, a mass storage device to the USB port, which can be shared with the local network.

## WiFi Network Name and Password

DNA modems are encrypted in terms of WiFi networks. You will find the network name and
password on the sticker included with the packaging and on the settings pages.

  * **SSID (network name)** : E.g. DNA-WIFI-XXXX, DNA-WIFI-2Ghz-XXX and DNA-WIFI-5Ghz-XXX 
  * **Wireless network password** : E.g. 25694177898

The EX400 modem supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz wireless WiFi networks.
Both are enabled by default. You can distinguish these from each other by the 2 GHz and 5 GHz
terms in the network name (SSID). You can change the network name or turn off
the other on the modem settings pages. The device also transmits a third WiFi
network, which is a combination of 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks. Connect to this and
the device will choose which frequency to use.

**Forgot the password?** When you reset the modem to factory settings, the original settings (including the password) will be restored. Reset the modem by pressing
the reset button on the back of the modem for 30 seconds. All lights will flash simultaneously on the modem.
