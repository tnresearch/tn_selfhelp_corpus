# I can't make calls abroad. What should I do?

Try this!

1\. **Restart your mobile phone**

2\. Check that you are **connected to a network**. Look at the top of the
screen: you should see the symbol that shows signal strength (usually four
bars in a row where the first is weak signal and the last is strong signal).

3\. Check that you have not **blocked calls and SMS to abroad**. This
setting is in My Telenor, so log in here [on the web](/mitt-
telenor/) or in [our app](/archive/2019/mitt-telenor-old/). If you have
Telenor Family, remember that only the person who is listed as the
[owner](/support/mobiltelefoni/abonnemang/for-familjen/medlemsprofiler/) of
the subscription can change the setting.

  
4\. **Do you have a prepaid card?** Check that your prepaid card works in the
country you are in [here](/roaming/). Are you outside the EU/EEA? Then you
should also check that you have [topped up with
Flexible/Balance](/support/mobiltelefoni/kontantkort/ladda-och-saldo/ladda-
kontantkort/olika-laddningar/). If you are within the EU/EEA, this is not
necessary. ****
