# Number Porting

Trouble with new number combinations? Of course, you can keep your phone
number when you switch operators to Telenor. It is free of charge and takes
about six days.

## Porting a number to Telenor

It's easy to switch to Telenor. If you want to keep your old number, simply
choose that option when you get the new
[mobile subscription](/handla/mobilabonnemang/) with us. The same applies,
of course, if you buy a [new mobile phone](https://www.telenor.se/handla/mobiltelefon/)
with a corresponding subscription.

### Good to know

Contract period

Check that you do not have any remaining contract period with your current
operator. If you do, we can port the mobile number only when it has expired.
But you can, for example, choose a date further ahead when the contract has
expired and still receive a new mobile delivered as long as possible.

Porting a number for someone else

When you buy a new mobile phone from us, you will be asked if you are
responsible for payment for the number to be ported. If you are not, we will
send an SMS to the person who owns the subscription so that they can approve
the porting.

Please remind the person who needs to approve, as your order will not be
placed until we have received the approval.

Prepaid cards must be registered

If it is a prepaid card number you want to port, the prepaid card must be
registered. You can do this at
[telenor.se/registrera](/link/9d9b1dd3829a415580e98ad6af78f872.aspx).  
  
If the prepaid card is from another operator, you will probably find
information on how to register on their website.

Temporary number

You will receive a SIM card from us with a temporary phone number. The
temporary number is valid until your actual number is ported to us on the
date you have chosen. You can use it during the interim period or wait until
the number porting has taken place. The SIM card you received will not be
replaced but will continue to be valid even after your actual number has been
ported. The card will arrive by post within approximately 1–2 working days.  
  
 **Tip!** You can also find your temporary number in [My Telenor on the
web.](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx)  
  
 **Things to consider if you have an iPhone**  
When you use the temporary number, it is registered in your iPhone and
continues to be the one that appears on the screen of those you call, even
after the number porting. But don't worry, it's easy to fix!

  1. Go to "Settings"
  2. Select "Phone"
  3. Select "My Number"
  4. Enter the correct number

You cannot change to any number you want, it must be your correct number.

When is it done?

The porting itself takes about six days (16 days if it is a landline number)
and you will receive an SMS when it is completed.

If you have already obtained a subscription or prepaid card from Telenor and
want to port with your old number

[Contact us here](/link/12530dbd72b84becbbc79096199f64db.aspx) or visit
one of [our stores](/link/28137742ca7249e2abe4b813fdb8c583.aspx) and we
will help you with the number porting by sending a request to your previous
operator.

## Did the number porting fail?

Sometimes number porting fails, and this can be due, among other things, to
the fact that there is a remaining contract period on the number to be
ported, or that the person who initiated the porting is not the owner of the
number. If you have tried to port a number without success, please call us on
[020-222 222](tel:020222222).

### Porting a number from Telenor

You want to port your mobile number from Telenor to another operator

If you want to transfer your number from us to another operator? To avoid
unexpected costs, we always recommend that you contact us before porting your
number.

**Here's how it works:**

  1. Contact us on [020-222 222](tel:020222222) and we will check for any
    remaining contract period and ensure that you are aware of any costs, for
    example if you still have installment payments for your phone. Here you
    will also find out if a final invoice will be sent to you and its amount.
  2. If everything is in order, you can then contact the new operator and ask
    them to carry out the number porting.
  3. When the number porting is complete, we will terminate your subscription
    with Telenor.

You want to port your landline number from Telenor to another operator

Of course, you can also port landline numbers from Telenor to another
operator. [Contact us](/link/0c492f5608894771aeabe6efb2d6d096.aspx) and we
will help you find out what applies to your subscription.

## Frequently asked questions about number porting

  * Why isn't my number listed on Hitta and other number information services?

Since 2018, we no longer automatically send new phone numbers to number
information services. So if you want your new number to appear on, for
example, hitta.se and Eniro, you must actively choose it.

### How to do it if you have a mobile subscription

Log in to My Telenor and activate the setting "Searchable phone number".

### How to do it if you have home telephony via broadband

Log in to [My Telenor Web](/link/a4531ddbfeef48d3a256e369df95234d.aspx).
Disable the "Secret number" service.

  * I can't make calls or SMS now that my number has been ported to Telenor, what do I do?

Are you having problems with the wrong number appearing when you call/SMS after
your number porting is complete and have an iPhone? Then you may need to
update ‘’My Number’’ in your phone's settings, read more [here](/support/vanliga-
fragor/felsokning/min-iphone-visar-fel-nummer.-vad-ska-jag-gora/).
