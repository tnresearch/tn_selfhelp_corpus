## Extended 3-Year DNA Warranty

The warranty period for devices always depends on the manufacturer, but for phones,
tablets, and smartwatches with a SIM card, you get a longer **3-Year DNA Warranty**
for the device you purchase. The warranty extension offered by DNA

#### Extended 3-Year DNA Warranty, terms for customers

**How is the warranty period determined?**

When you purchase a mobile phone, tablet, smartwatch, or smartwatch with a SIM card
from DNA, you get a longer warranty period (36
months) for the device you purchase. The warranty extension offered by DNA extends the manufacturer's factory warranty
to 36 months when the manufacturer's warranty is shorter than this. The extended warranty is also valid for business customers.

**What does the warranty cover?**

The extended warranty is the same in content as the factory warranty defined by the device manufacturer and covers, for example, manufacturing defects when the device has been
used appropriately and according to instructions. The warranty does not cover, for example,
defects or damage caused by dropping or wetting the device.

**How is the device repaired?**

During the extended warranty period, devices can be sent for repair directly to DNA's
service partners or through DNA Store. Please check our service instructions to see how
you should proceed with your device. After the manufacturer's warranty expires, a faulty
product will always be repaired when possible.

**Who are DNA's service partners?**

You can find our service partners and manufacturer-specific instructions on our support pages.
