# Troubleshooting Mobile Issues

Sudden loss of coverage, or problems sending SMS? Has internet stopped working on your mobile? Here you will find help and guidance for the most common problems we experience on mobile phones.

[Coverage](/kundeservice/mobil/problemer/#dekning)[Calls](/kundeservice/mobil/problemer/#samtaler)[Mobile Data/MMS](/kundeservice/mobil/din-

Have you tried turning the equipment off and on again?

In many cases, a simple restart of the equipment is all that is needed to fix problems with the mobile network.

Before continuing troubleshooting, we therefore recommend that you **restart** **mobile/tablet/watch** by turning it completely off and then on again. It can also help to update the operating system on your phone to the latest version.

If that doesn't solve the problem, we have some more tips and tricks you should try listed on this page.

## Coverage

Did the coverage disappear at home suddenly? Or have you never had coverage at home?

  * #### Sudden loss of coverage?

If you experience a sudden loss of coverage, it may be due to a fault at our base stations. First of all, you should check if a fault has already been reported in the area.

**Here's how to check if there are registered faults in the coverage area:**

    1. Go to the coverage map [https://www.telenor.no/dekning/](https://www.telenor.no/dekning/#dekningskart)

    2. Accept location or search for the street address

    3. Press on Faults and press in the map where it is red to see what fault has been reported

**If no fault is registered in the area, you should do the following:**

    1. Restart your mobile phone

    2. Check with others you live with, neighbors, etc. if they are experiencing the same problem.

      * If it's just you experiencing the problem, the chances are high that it's due to your equipment

    3. Try your SIM card in another mobile if possible

If you still experience problems, we recommend that you [contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) so we can troubleshoot further.

  * #### Have had poor coverage for more than 6 months

If the coverage has never been there, or there has been poor coverage for a long time - it is probably not a fault.

Then you must contact us to report a coverage request: [Contact customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/)

## Problems with SMS

Are you unable to receive SMS, or are you having trouble sending? Here are a couple of things you should check:

  * #### Unable to send SMS

**When you cannot send SMS, there are a couple of things you should specifically do or check:**

    * Restart your mobile

    * Test by sending an SMS to yourself - does it arrive? If so, the problem is not with you, but perhaps with the person you are trying to send SMS to.

    * If you have an Android mobile, check that you have the correct message center number **  
How to find the message center:**

      * Open Messages and press the three dots in the upper right corner

      * Select settings

      * More settings

      * Text messages

      * Message center  
The message center number should be **+4790002100**

    * If you have an iPhone, it may be necessary to _reset network settings_ on your device to fix the problem. Be aware that this deletes all WiFi and Bluetooth connections, and these must be set up again.

If this has no effect, check that you have coverage on your mobile phone. If you are missing coverage, you should check the coverage map to see if a fault has been reported. [You can find the coverage map here](https://www.telenor.no/dekning/#dekningskart)

If none of these tricks help, we recommend that you [contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) so we can troubleshoot further.

  * #### Unable to receive SMS

**If you do not receive SMS, you can try the following:**

    * Restart your mobile phone

    * Check that you have not blocked the number trying to send you SMS on your phone

    * If you have an iPhone - check if it is iMessage or SMS that is not working

    * **Send an SMS to yourself** - if it arrives to you normally, it is likely that the problem lies with the sender

If none of this helps, check that you have coverage on your mobile phone. If you are missing coverage, you should check the coverage map to see if a fault has been reported. [You can find the coverage map here](https://www.telenor.no/dekning/#dekningskart)

If none of these tricks help, we recommend that you [contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) so we can troubleshoot further.

  * #### Specifically for prepaid

    * Double-check that you have enough money on your balance

    * Has it been a long time since you last topped up? If it has been more than 15 months since you last topped up, the chances are high that your number has been blocked. Contact us at customer service, and we will help you reopen the number!

Are you experiencing other problems with prepaid, such as problems with charging or similar? See our help page for Prepaid for more help: [Help for Prepaid](/kundeservice/mobil/abonnement-og-tjenester/kontantkort/)

## Problems with calls

Are you unable to make calls or are people unable to call you? Here are a couple of things you should check:

  * #### Unable to make calls

**Is it only one person you cannot call, or does it apply to everyone?**  
If it only applies to one person, the fault is most likely on their equipment. Ask them to check through their settings to double-check that they have not blocked your number by mistake.

**When you cannot call anyone, there are a couple of things you should specifically do or check:**

    * Restart your mobile

    * Check the coverage map to see if a fault has been reported. [You can find the coverage map here](https://www.telenor.no/dekning/#dekningskart)

    * If you have recently received a new SIM card, check if it is activated by:

      * Log in to [My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/)

      * Press on "Mobile and subscription"

      * Select mobile number

      * Scroll down to "Devices and SIM" and select your device  
Check that the SIM number here matches what is on your device (It may be necessary to remove the SIM card to check this)

    * It may be necessary to _reset network settings_ on your device to fix the problem. Be aware that this deletes all WiFi and Bluetooth connections, and these must be set up again.

    * Specifically for iPhone:

      * Check if the **"Do Not Disturb"** function is activated, turn it off if so

If none of these tricks help, we recommend that you [contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) so we can troubleshoot further.

  * #### Unable to receive calls

**Is it only one person you cannot receive calls from, or does it apply to everyone?**  
If it only applies to one person, the fault is most likely on your equipment. Therefore, check through your settings to double-check that you have not blocked the number.

**When you cannot receive calls from anyone, there are a couple of things you should specifically do or check:**

    * Restart your mobile

    * Check the coverage map to see if a fault has been reported. [You can find the coverage map here](https://www.telenor.no/dekning/#dekningskart)

    * It may be necessary to _reset network settings_ on your device to fix the problem. Be aware that this deletes all WiFi and Bluetooth connections, and these must be set up again.

If none of these tricks help, we recommend that you [contact us at customer service](/kundeservice/kontakt-oss/) so we can troubleshoot further.

  * #### Specifically for prepaid

    * Double-check that you have enough money on your balance

    * Has it been a long time since you last topped up? If it has been more than 15 months since you last topped up, the chances are high that your number has been blocked. Contact us at customer service, and we will help you reopen the number!

Are you experiencing other problems with prepaid, such as problems with charging or similar? See our help page for Prepaid for more help: [Help for Prepaid](/kundeservice/mobil/abonnement-og-tjenester/kontantkort/)

## Problems abroad

Are you having problems making calls, sending or receiving SMS, etc. when you are abroad? Here are a couple of things you should check:

  * Data roaming must be turned on in the settings on your phone

  * Check if we have an agreement with the country you are in. If you cannot find the desired country, it means that we do not have an agreement with that country.

  * Turn the phone off and on

  * Try to connect to another provider/operator manually

  * If you still experience problems when using abroad, please contact customer service so we can help you further with troubleshooting

  * #### Turn data roaming on/off

**iPhone/iOS:**  
Go to: Settings ➜ Select: Mobile Network ➜ Select: Mobile Data Options ➜ Turn on: Data Roaming

**Android:**  
Go to: Settings ➜ Select: Mobile Networks ➜ Select: Data Roaming ➜ Turn on: Data Roaming

  * #### Does Telenor have an agreement with the country I am in?

To check if Telenor has an agreement with a country, you can go to: [International Prices](/privat/mobil/utlandet/)

    1. Scroll down to the tabs In abroad/To abroad

    2. Search for the country you are in

    3. Scroll down to the blue button: See operator list for <country>

  * #### Switch operator manually

If you still experience coverage problems, this is often due to operator selection on your phone. The phone should automatically connect to the preferred operator/network, but sometimes you have to do this manually.

If you are near a border _can_ your phone connect to the wrong operator, then you should search for another operator manually.

**iPhone/iOS:**  
Go to: Settings ➜ Mobile Network ➜ Network Selection ➜ Turn off: Automatic

**Android:**  
Go to: Settings ➜ Connections ➜ Mobile Networks ➜ Network Operators ➜ Select manually

When automatic operator selection is turned off, the phone will perform a manual search. Then a list of all available networks will appear.

  * #### Specifically for USA

In the USA, a mobile phone with support for 4G (VoLTE) is required.

Most mobile phones launched after 2014 have support for 4G, but if you have an older mobile phone and are planning to travel to the USA, it is wise to be aware of this. The reason is that US operators began phasing out their 2G and 3G networks in 2022.

Mobile data and SMS will work as normal. But, without the ability for 4G Voice, neither outgoing nor incoming calls will work. This also applies to emergency numbers.

**Alternative:**  
**Use apps like FaceTime, Messenger and WhatsApp or similar to make calls.**

  * #### Specifically for prepaid

    * Double-check that you have enough money on your balance

    * Has it been a long time since you last topped up? If it has been more than 15 months since you last topped up, the chances are high that your number has been blocked. Contact us at customer service, and we will help you reopen the number!

    * We do not have an agreement with all countries in the world for prepaid. Check on the international page we have linked to below, search for the country you are in and see if we have an agreement.

      * You can see this by clicking on the button "See operator list for....". There will be a small box with "Prepaid" in the list if we have an agreement.

Are you experiencing other problems with prepaid, such as problems with charging or similar? See our help page for Prepaid for more help: [Help for Prepaid](/kundeservice/mobil/abonnement-og-tjenester/kontantkort/)
