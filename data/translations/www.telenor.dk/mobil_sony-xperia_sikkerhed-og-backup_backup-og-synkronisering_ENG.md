# Backup and Sync Your Mobile

Regularly backing up and automatically syncing can minimize the risk of losing things like photos if you accidentally lose your mobile or need to restore it and start from scratch.

**Backup:** Your security copy of all content and settings. With a backup, you can recreate your mobile and more easily set up new devices with the same content, contacts, etc.

**Sync:** Your guarantee that all your devices are always updated and have the same content, photos, etc. You may have written a new contact in your phonebook or taken new photos during the day. When you sync, you haven't backed up your settings.

  * ## Tap the menu button on the home screen

  * ## Select "Settings"

  * ## Select the account you want to sync

  * ## Tap the menu button in the upper right corner

Remember to choose which services you want to sync before tapping the menu button.

  * ## Select "Sync Now"

Your account will now be synced.
