# Troubleshooting Internet Issues

Having problems with slow, unstable, or no internet connection? Try our guides to troubleshoot the connection, or check if there are any reported issues in your area.

## Smart Troubleshooting

Solve your internet problem or report faults directly to our technicians with our new Smart Troubleshooting service.

Only applies to internet over fiber or coax.

[Go to My Pages to troubleshoot](/mitt-telenor/feilsok/)

## Have you tried turning the equipment off and on again?

In many cases, simply restarting the equipment is all that's needed to fix problems with your home network!

Before continuing troubleshooting, we recommend restarting both the **router, extender, and fiber box/modem/antenna adapter** by unplugging the power, leaving it unplugged for a while, and then plugging it back in. Also restart your **mobile phone or PC** by turning it completely off and then on again.

If this doesn't solve the problem, select the issue you are experiencing below to continue troubleshooting.

### How to restart your router and extender:

  1. Unplug the power cord or use the on/off switch.

  2. Leave the equipment unplugged for 10 seconds.

  3. Plug the power back in. Note! It may take a few minutes for the router to connect to the internet again.

### How to restart your fiber box or modem

  1. Unplug the power cord.

  2. Wait 1 minute.

  3. Plug the power cord back in.

Note: It may take a few minutes for the connection to be restored.
