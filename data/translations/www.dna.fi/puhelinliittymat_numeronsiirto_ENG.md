# Number Porting

## Number Porting from Another Owner

Changing the ownership of a subscription is possible through number transfer. You can transfer the number by visiting a DNA Store and through the My DNA app, but receiving the number is only possible at DNA Stores. After this, the new owner of the number can make a new subscription agreement for the transferred number.

The transferor (the current owner of the number) and the transferee (the new owner of the number) can visit a DNA Store together, allowing the transfer to take place immediately. A fee of €12 will be charged to the transferor according to the price list.

If the current owner cannot visit a DNA Store, the number transfer can be done using DNA's official number transfer power of attorney or by transferring the number through the My DNA app.

[Download number transfer power of attorney (pdf)](https://www.dna.fi/documents/753910/3591291/DNA_numeronsiirtovaltakirja.pdf/45469213-54ec-a72c-6fa0-fd5625191e3a)

### **Number Transfer in the My DNA App**

  * Download and open the My DNA app
  * Instructions for getting started
  * Go to the My Services tab
  * Select the subscription whose number you want to transfer
  * Select Subscription Management
  * Select Number Transfer
  * Enter the required information and submit the form

**Note.** The number transfer is valid for 30 days, during which time the new customer must visit a DNA Store to make a new subscription agreement for the number.

## Transferring a Subscription from Another Operator to DNA

Transferring your current subscription to DNA takes a minimum of 5 business days. Keep your new SIM card safe, and use your old SIM card in your phone until your subscription is activated.

  * We will handle the termination of your subscription with your current operator; you don't need to do anything.
  * You will receive a text message with an estimated transfer date, and later another message confirming the transfer date.
  * When the transfer date arrives, put the new SIM card into your phone. Remember to restart your phone so it can find the network.
  * If the SIM card is already in your phone, it is recommended to restart the device if the subscription does not start working immediately.

If you have ordered a subscription online and the new SIM card has been delivered to you by mail, you must activate the subscription in order to start the number porting process. Activation requires secure authentication using online banking credentials or a mobile certificate. Activation initiates the number transfer, which takes 5 business days unless otherwise agreed. [Go to subscription activation](https://aktivoi.dna.fi/#/front)

## Transferring a Subscription Registered in a Company's Name

Transfer a consumer subscription owned by your company either in the My DNA app or by contacting [Business Customer Service](https://www.dna.fi/yrityksille/yhteystiedot).

Read instructions on how to get started with the My DNA app [here](https://www.dna.fi/yrityksille/oma-dna).
