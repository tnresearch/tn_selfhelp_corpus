# Getting Started with TV – How and Where You Want

Congratulations, you've come to the right place to get started with TV
from Telenor! Start by choosing how you want to watch.

## Equipment from Telenor

  
This is your choice if you have TV via fiber or cable and want to watch on your TV.

[In the next step, choose which equipment you have](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-
igang/box/)

## Apple TV

  
This is your choice if you want to watch Telenor Stream on your TV via Apple TV.

[To the Apple TV guide](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/apple-tv/)

## Chromecast

  
This is your choice if you want to watch Telenor Stream on TV via your Chromecast

[To the Chromecast guide](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/chromecast/)

## Smart TV

  
Watch directly on your smart TV if you have a Samsung, LG or a TV based
on Android TV, such as Sony or Philips.

[To the smart TV guide](/support/tv-och-stream/kom-igang/smart-tv/)
