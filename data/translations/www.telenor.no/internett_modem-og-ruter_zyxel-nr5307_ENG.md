# Help with Zyxel NR5307

Here you will find help and guides for your 5G router for Mobile Broadband.

## Simple steps to get online

  * #### Insert SIM card

If you have a large-sized SIM card, you need to break out the Nano-SIM.
The power adapter must not be connected when you insert the SIM card. The opening
for the SIM card is located on the back of the router.

 **Here's how to insert the SIM card:**

    1. Open the SIM cover

    2. Remove the SIM tray

    3. Place the SIM card in the tray

    4. Insert the tray into the router and close the cover

  * #### Connect to power

    1. Connect the included power adapter to the back of the router and to a power source

    2. Press the ON button

    3. You will see that the power light will illuminate on the front

  * #### Connect to WiFi

 **Different ways to connect to the Internet  
** The network name (WiFi name/SSID) and password can be found on the underside of
the router.  

    1.  **Connect with SSID**

      * Mobile - Scan QR code on the underside of the router and follow the wizard

      * PC: Search for the network and log in using the password

    2.  **Connect with WPS**  
Hold down the WPS button for 3 seconds - Within 2 minutes you must search for
your network and connect.  
If this does not work, you must connect with username and password.

    3.  **Connect with cable**  
Connect ethernet cable to the yellow LAN port

  * #### Complete the wizard

    1. Make sure you are connected to the router's wireless network

    2. Open the browser

    3. Enter 192.168.1.1 in the address field of the browser and follow the wizard

 **PIN code  
** In the wizard, you will be prompted to enter a PIN code. This can be found in
the letter you received with the SIM card for the router.  
If you cannot find the PIN code, you can [retrieve the PUK code on My
Pages](https://www.telenor.no/privat/minesider/web/mobil/). Once you have entered
the PUK code, you will be prompted to create a new PIN code.

**How to retrieve the PUK code**

    1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/privat/minesider/web/mobil/)

    2. Select the subscription in question

    3. Under **Devices and SIM** , select the SIM card in question

    4. Press **Show PUK code**

## Questions and answers about Zyxel NR5307

  * #### How to update software?

When new software is available, the router is automatically upgraded to
meet Telenor's strict requirements for security and stability. You can also download
new software from Telenor's support pages and perform the update from
a PC/Mac.

Device and SIM card information is required to perform
the software update.

  * #### How do I reset the router?

If you are experiencing problems connecting to the internet, it can often help to
reset the router. This resets the router to the settings it had when you received it.

    1. Find something thin, such as a paperclip or a blunt needle

    2. Insert the object into the reset hole on the underside of the router and hold it in for 5 seconds

    3. The router turns off and on automatically, and you can connect to the network again

When the router is reset, the network name and password will be what is on
the sticker on the back of the router. Follow the wizard to set up the router
again.

  * #### How to change advanced settings in the router menu?

In the router's menu, you can see status, signal strength, change PIN, change
network and IP addresses, among other things. You access the advanced settings by
opening the browser and entering 192.168.1.1 in the address line.

The password for the administration page is the same as the network password that
is found on the label on the underside of the router. If you have changed
the password to the administration pages, use this one.
