## Features

#### Dimensions and Weight

  * Height 205 mm
  * Width 100 mm
  * Depth 100 mm
  * Weight 754 g

#### Information

**Manufacturer:** ZTE

**Model:** MC8020

**SIM card:** Nano-SIM

**Manufacturer's warranty** : 24 months

#### Connections and Connectivity

**Interface type:** WLAN & Ethernet

**Gigabit Ethernet (LAN) ports:** 2 pcs

**Network technology:** 5G, 4G LTE, UMTS

**Supported 5G frequencies:** 700/800/1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz

**Supported 4G frequencies:** 700/800/1800/2100/2600 MHz

**DL 5G 4x4 MIMO:** 1800/2100/2600/3500 MHz, 5G 2x2 MIMO: 700/800 MHz

**DL LTE 4x4 MIMO:** 1800/2100/2600 MHz; LTE 2x2 MIMO: 700/800 MHz

  * 5CC DL carrier aggregation support in 4G network, 1CC/2CC in 5G network (2CC in 5G only at 3500 MHz frequency)
  * 256 QAM modulation support
  * WLAN: 802.11 b/g/n/a/ac/ax 
  * WLAN frequency bands: 2.4 GHz (2x2 MIMO) and 5 GHz (4x4 MIMO)
  * 5 GHz Wi-Fi Preferred support
  * WLAN theoretical maximum speed: 5400 Mbit/s

#### Network Features

(Figures are theoretical maximum speeds, actual speeds depend on
the strength of the received signal, the capabilities of the mobile network,
and the type of subscription used)

  * 5G maximum speed from the network 3.45 Gbit/s (NSA 5G), 1.4 Gbit/s (SA 5G)
  * 5G maximum speed to the network 600 Mbit/s (NSA 5G), 900 Mbit/s (SA 5G)
  * 4G LTE maximum speed from the network 2.0 Gbit/s
  * 4G LTE maximum speed to the network 150 Mbit/s

#### Sales package contents

  * DNA Home Modem 5G WiFi MC8020
  * Power supply (12 V/3.3 A)
  * Ethernet cable
  * Quick guide
