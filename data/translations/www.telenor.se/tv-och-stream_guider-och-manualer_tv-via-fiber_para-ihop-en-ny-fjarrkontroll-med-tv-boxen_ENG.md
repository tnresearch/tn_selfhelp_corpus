# Pair a new remote control with the TV box

Follow this guide **if you have received a new remote control** that you want to connect
to your TV box.

  * ### Put the TV box in standby mode

The standby button on top of the TV box should be lit red.

  * ### Press and hold the standby button

Do this for at least ten seconds until the TV box restarts.

  * ### Get the new remote control

Then look at the TV screen. You will now see a picture of a remote control. Then do the following:  
  
Press **OK** and the **blue button** on the remote control simultaneously.

  * ### Enter the four digits

Now four digits will appear on the screen. **Enter these in the same order using
the remote control**.  
  
That's it! The TV box starts and you can channel surf.
