# Privacy

You've probably heard it before. With great power comes great responsibility. That's pretty accurate when we talk about privacy. We have access to a lot of data, but we use it with respect. And the purpose is always clear: either to deliver our services, give you something better, or improve ourselves.

[ ](/support/integritet/vilken-typ-av-data-behandlar-vi/)

What type of data do we process?

We collect and process two types of data: customer data and traffic data. When you browse our website, cookies are also used to give you a better user experience.

[This is the data we save > ](/support/integritet/vilken-typ-av-data-behandlar-vi/ "https://www.telenor.se/kundservice/integritet/din-trygghet/vilken-typ-av-data-behandlar-vi/")

[ ](/support/integritet/hur-samlar-vi-data/)

How we collect and use data

We receive. We create. We buy. To collect data, we use several different methods. For example, when you become a customer and provide information, or when you use our services, some data is created.

[Read more about our data usage >](/support/integritet/hur-samlar-vi-data/)

GDPR

Think about the impact of these four letters. The General Data Protection Regulation is the European data protection regulation that came into effect on May 25, 2018.

GDPR is comprehensive and places increased demands on how we collect, process, and manage data. Part of this means that we need to ask for consent to use your data.

[More about GDPR >](/om-telenor/gdpr/)

You decide

To give or change your consent, log in to My Telenor web. Under the My Profile tab, you can read more under Privacy Issues. Give or change your consent by clicking View or change how my personal data is used.

Remember that you can always change your choices afterwards. You are in control of your data.

[Give consent on My Telenor >](/mitt-telenor/)

Cake or cookie?

A cherished child has many names, at least two. Cookies are a text file that is stored in your device or disappears when you leave the page. There are different cookies on our website. They are used to improve the customer experience, for statistics/analysis and marketing.

[Read our cookie policy >](/support/integritet/information-om-cookies/)

Digital Security

Today, security is more than just locked front doors. How do you actually surf safely on your mobile? Why shouldn't you turn off roaming abroad, and is it bad to use autofill for usernames and passwords?

[Read more about digital security > ](/inspirera-mig/sakerhet/)

Share your data and get a better experience

Customers receive offers regularly. If we are allowed to use your traffic data, we can give you better offers. Instead of a general offer that applies to everyone, you can get a personalized one.

You can also choose to share your data to improve service and support. We love to provide good service, but we need your permission to be able to give that little extra.

[How to share your data > ](/support/integritet/battre-erbjudanden-och-service/)

#### Necessary Cookies

Always active

Needed for the website to function securely and correctly. Therefore, they cannot be turned off. Necessary cookies enable basic functions, so that you can, for example, navigate between pages or access secure areas of the website.

Information about cookies‎

#### Functional Cookies

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They can be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of these services may not function as intended.

Information about cookies‎

#### Statistical and Analytical

Statistical and Analytical

These cookies give us information about how you use our site and allow us to improve the user experience and improve our website. They can be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, we may have limited insight into how our website is used and therefore have a harder time improving your experience.

Information about cookies‎

#### Marketing

Marketing

These cookies are set on our website by our advertising partners. They can be used by these companies to create a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other websites. They do not directly store personal information, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less targeted ads.
