# How do I cancel my mobile subscription?

We are sorry to hear that you are considering cancelling your mobile subscription with us. Have you
remembered everything that makes your Telenor subscription so special? Your subscription also includes [Telenor Security Package](/shop/telenor-
tryghedspakke/), which makes your online life much safer.

If you still wish to cancel your mobile subscription, the easiest way is to
call us on 72 100 100, and we will help you in a few minutes. Alternatively, you can
visit your nearest [Telenor store](/find-butik/) or write to
us [here](/kundeservice/kontakt/skriv-til-os/).

## **Your commitment and cancellation period**

If you are in a contract, you can cancel your subscription when you are out of your
contract period. You have 30 days' notice on your mobile subscription. This means that your subscription will be active for 30 days after you have cancelled
the subscription.

## **Your final bill**

As mobile subscriptions are prepaid, you will initially be charged for
the full billing period, regardless of when the subscription ends with Telenor.
You will then receive your final bill from Telenor, where you will be
refunded the amount you have overpaid. If you have had extra
usage in the last few days before the subscription closed with Telenor, you will also
be charged for that here. It all happens automatically, so we are sure that
you will not pay too much.

  * **Cancel Direct Debit after the last bill is paid**
** If you have registered your bill with Direct Debit, we recommend that you first
cancel the Direct Debit agreement when you have paid your last bill.
If you cancel the agreement before, you will receive the bill by post, and it will cost
49 DKK in a billing fee.

## Stay with Telenor, but change subscription

Are you considering cancelling your subscription because it does not meet your needs? On
[My Telenor](/login/) you can customize or change your subscription. Easy,
fast and simple. Read more about [changing your
subscription](/kundeservice/Mobilabonnementer-og-services/mit-abonnement/skift-
abonnement/).

Remember that as a Telenor customer you get a lot extra with your
mobile subscription:

  * Use your mobile safely when you are [abroad](/shop/landingpage/mobil/roaming/)
  * [Telenor Security Package](/shop/telenor-tryghedspakke/) included - help with digital violations and online fraud
  * [Network](/shop/landingpage/netvark-i-verdensklasse/) world class - 5G included

## Get a discount on family subscriptions

There is also good money to be saved if several people in the household need
a mobile subscription. If you combine two or more mobile subscriptions with
us, you save 50 DKK/month for each extra mobile subscription you own in addition to your
primary mobile subscription. Read more about
[Bundle Discount](/shop/landingpage/samlerabat/).
