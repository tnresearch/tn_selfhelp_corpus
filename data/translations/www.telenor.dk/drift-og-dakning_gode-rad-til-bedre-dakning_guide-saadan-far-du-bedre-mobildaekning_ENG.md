## How to Enable VoLTE

Turn on VoLTE on your iPhone

  1. Tap **Settings**
  2. Tap **Cellular**
  3. Tap **Cellular Data Options**
  4. Tap **Enable LTE**

Turn on VoLTE on your Android or Samsung phone

  1. Tap **Settings**
  2. Tap **Connections**
  3. Tap **Mobile Networks**
  4. Enable **VoLTE calls** to turn on the feature

## Questions and Answers about Mobile Coverage

What is good to have ready if I need help from customer service?

When experiencing call problems, it is good to be able to mention three examples of time and
place where you have experienced the problems.

When experiencing data problems, we would like to know where the problems occur (e.g., whether it is on
a specific location, indoors or outdoors), and whether it is slow
or no connection. If the data connection is slow, please take a [speed test](/kundeservice/drift-og-dakning/hastighed/hastighedstest/)
before contacting customer service.

Where can I report coverage problems?

You can tell us about
coverage problems in the [My Telenor app](/mit-telenor/selvbetjening/). This is valuable knowledge for us in the development of
the network.
