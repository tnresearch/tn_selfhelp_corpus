# Outdoor Modems

Outdoor modems are similar to Indoor Modems, but consist of a Wi-Fi creating indoor unit and a directional 5G/4G external antenna, between which data is transferred without loss. You get a reliable 5G/4G connection, even if the coverage is poor, or you find an even better signal in an area with already good coverage. Place it outdoors, like a [5g antenna](https://www.dna.fi/laitteet/modeemit-ja-mokkulat/ulkomokkula).
