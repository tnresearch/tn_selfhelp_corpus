# Switch Internet Provider to Telenor

If you need to switch your internet connection to Telenor, there are two ways to do it. The easiest way is to give us power of attorney to terminate your internet subscription with your current provider. This method minimizes the risk of you either being left without an internet connection, or having to pay for multiple internet subscriptions for a period of time. We will, of course, also coordinate the termination with the establishment of your new agreement, so the switch happens on the same date. Alternatively, you can terminate your current subscription yourself. You can do this using the termination form at the bottom of the page. Note that you should only use the termination form if you choose to terminate your current internet subscription yourself.

You can read more about the methods and see what you need to do below:

We Terminate Your Subscription For You

We are happy to terminate your current internet subscription for you - it just requires that:

  * the switch must happen on the same date
  * the switch is made on fixed internet connections. In other words, you cannot use the process to switch from or to a mobile internet connection
  * the switch happens at the same address. You cannot use the process in connection with a move.
  * it must be the same customer who is registered on both the existing and the new connection. That is, you cannot transfer the internet subscription to another person in connection with the switch

[You can read more about the process
here.](https://www.teleindu.dk/branchesamarbejde/sbbu/)

**Here's how to do it:**

  1. When you order your new internet connection from us, you have the option to fill out a termination power of attorney, which gives Telenor permission to terminate your current internet subscription on your behalf.
  2. In the termination power of attorney, you select the date you want your internet connection to be transferred. In some cases, we cannot guarantee that you will get the delivery date you requested in the power of attorney. If this is the case, it will say "Desired delivery date" in the power of attorney, and you will receive a confirmed delivery date sent by email as soon as possible. It is the confirmed delivery date - that is, the one you receive by email - that applies.
  3. Once we have received the power of attorney, we will send it to your current provider, and you will simultaneously receive an order confirmation for your new order. Shortly after, you will receive a confirmation of your termination from your current provider.

Terminate Your Current Internet Subscription Yourself

You can also choose to make the switch to Telenor yourself. You do this by sending your termination to your current provider in an email. To make it easier for you, we have created a form that you can copy and paste your own information into.

It is important that you send your termination by email, rather than calling. This way you have documentation of your termination, so you can easily and quickly get started with your new subscription with us.

You can use our termination form below - that way you are sure that you include all the information.

If you need help, you are more than welcome to contact Telenor's customer service on 72 100 100.
