# WiFi Network Support

## Problems with your WiFi network?

Try using an ethernet cable. If the problem disappears, the fault is likely
in the wireless home network. In this case, please refer to the instructions below!

If the problem persists with both WiFi and ethernet, it may be a problem with
the network side, such as a local interference. In this situation, it is
advisable to contact customer service, which has the latest information on
network disruptions and, for example, the location of base stations or available
frequencies.

#### First, do these:

  * Restart the modem by pressing the power button or unplugging the power cord for about 30 seconds.
  * Make sure you have selected the correct WiFi network created by your modem.
  * Check that there are no electronic devices causing interference near the modem or that the modem's ventilation is blocked.
  * Update your modem if possible (from the modem's settings page, update button, or mobile app).
  * Restart your devices. It is recommended to restart a Windows 10 computer while holding down the shift key. For example, tablets and other mobile devices should be restarted by first turning off the power for over 30 seconds.

#### Also try these:

  * Reset the modem. The reset button is usually located on one side of the device. Press it for 30 seconds. This will restore the modem to its factory settings and fix some modem freezes.
  * Update your devices: tablets, phones, TVs and all other devices connected to the modem.
  * Clear the cache of the browser you are using.
  * Tip: If you have multiple rooms or solid wall structures in your home, the WiFi network produced by a single modem or dongle may not reach every corner of the apartment. The solution to this is a [Mesh network](https://www.dna.fi/laitteet/modeemit-ja-mokkulat/mesh).

## Maximum Speeds of Wireless Network

Currently, it is not possible to achieve peak speeds with a WiFi network. If you want
to use your internet at maximum speed and with the lowest possible latency,
we recommend connecting your computer directly to the modem with an ethernet
network cable (RJ45).

Read more

The table below shows the theoretical maximum speed of the most commonly used WiFi
standards, as well as the typical speed in normal use. The speed of a WiFi connection depends on the technology of the modem and the device connected to the internet, as well as the environment. The speeds stated are theoretical and maximum (provided that the terminal device you are using is also fully technically compatible). You can find the theoretical maximum speeds of dongles and modem-specific wireless home networks, as well as broadband connections, on the product pages and in the information included with the subscription or device.

Please note that to achieve the maximum speed of, for example, the 802.11ac 4x4 standard, the devices connected to the internet (e.g. computer, tablet, phone) must also be up to date and meet the same conditions. Check settings, updates and consider possible interference factors in the environment.

## WiFi Connection Encryption

You should always protect your wireless local network. A wireless network also works
through walls, so your WiFi connection may extend to your neighbor's home.

By encrypting your connection, you prevent outsiders from using your internet without authorization and prevent them from taking control of your connection. Encryption applies only to the wireless internet connection between the modem and the computer. In a wireless local network, we recommend using so-called **WPA2** (Wi-Fi Protected Access) encryption. It is currently the most secure of the available options.

DNA's customized modems are encrypted by default, meaning they require a password. More information and instructions on encrypting your own device can be found on the
[modems](https://www.dna.fi/tuki-modeemit) and
[dongles](http://www.dna.fi/tuki-mokkulat) support pages.

Please remember that a secure connection between the modem and the computer does not protect against threats from the internet, such as network connections, viruses or data breaches. [Learn more about DNA's security solutions](https://www.dna.fi/tietoturva)
