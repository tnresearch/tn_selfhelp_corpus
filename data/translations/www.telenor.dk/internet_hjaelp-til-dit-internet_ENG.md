## How to Improve Your WiFi Coverage

Fortunately, you can do a lot yourself to improve your WiFi coverage, and we'll give you 10 good tips here so you can get the most out of your internet connection:

  1. Place the router high and centrally in the home, e.g. on a shelf or on top of a cabinet in your living room
  2. Avoid placing the router in the kitchen, where a lot of other electronic equipment can interfere
  3. Place the router with good distance to glass and concrete (glass and metal reflect the WiFi signal)
  4. Place the router as far away from other wireless equipment as possible (e.g. baby monitor).
  5. Extend the signal with a WiFi extender. Read more and buy WiFi extender from Telenor
  6. Turn the router off and on again – this will allow the router to switch channels if the used channel is congested  

If you want to know more about why these measures improve your WiFi coverage, you can read the full article [here](/shop/landingpage/bedre-wifi/).

What speed can I expect?

**WiFi**  
Since WiFi is a wireless sharing technology, there are many factors that influence the experienced speed. The factors can be divided into two categories: **Equipment** (both router and your own devices) and **physical circumstances** (distance between router and devices, walls, furniture, electronic interference and the like).  
To get the optimal network experience on WiFi, it therefore requires both that you use a good router (your router is good if you have Telenor), and that your own equipment is of high quality (and has good WiFi specs).

Next, you need to make sure to give the router and equipment good opportunities to communicate (the distance is as low as possible, and there is a clear view from the router to the device). The expected speeds on WiFi can range from 1-900 Mbps, depending on these factors. If you experience that your WiFi is bad, you always have the opportunity to contact customer service on 72 100 100 and get help with optimization.

  
**Wired Connection**  
If you do not want to use WiFi, you have the option to connect your device to the router with a cable. Here, the speed is more guaranteed, as all LAN cables of newer date (Cat5e or newer) support up to 1000 Mbit throughput over 100 meters distance. If you have chosen a subscription with more than 1000Mbit, the speed is only guaranteed if you use a Cat6 cable. We always recommend that you use the cable that comes with the router.

What is WiFi?

It may seem almost magical, the way WiFi gets the internet to fly through the air and to a device. In reality, it is different radio waves that the router emits. Telenor's routers use two different frequencies to transmit the radio waves, namely 2.4 GHz and 5GHz. The two have different advantages and disadvantages: 2.4GHz has a good range and penetrates walls better than 5GHz. In return, 5GHz has a shorter range, but supports much higher speeds.

On Telenor's connections, the router controls whether you are on 2.4 or 5 GHz, depending on what gives you the best connection. In practice, this means that when you move away from the router, you are moved from a fast speed with a short range to a slower speed, but with better range.

In addition to the two different frequencies, several of Telenor's routers also support a relatively newly developed standard called WiFi6. WiFi6 has a better range than previous standards as well as a number of features that optimize the signal's journey from router to your device, so you receive a faster and more stable signal. Even if your device does not support WiFi6.
