# Pay Invoice

Here you will find help and guidance for paying your invoice on My Pages or
in My Telenor.

## Pay Invoice in My Telenor

On the homepage in the app, scroll down until you find **Invoice** and tap on it.

Here you will find options to **pay, postpone or open** the invoice itself.

### 1. Find Invoice

Tap on the unpaid invoice.

### 2. Invoice Details

Tap on "Pay Invoice". Here you can also postpone the invoice if needed.

### 3. Choose Payment Method

Tap on the payment method you want to use.

### 4. Payment with Card

If you choose to pay with a bank or credit card, fill in the information and
tap on "Pay".

## Pay Invoice on My Pages

  1. Tap on the box below, it will take you directly to the Invoice pages.

  2. Unpaid invoices are at the top, tap on "Pay Now".

  3. Then choose how you want to pay the invoice, and follow the instructions further.

    * Payment Card

    * Vipps

    * Credit Card
