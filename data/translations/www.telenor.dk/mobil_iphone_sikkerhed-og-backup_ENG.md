# Your Mobile Security

If you update your social media profiles, send emails, and access your bank via your smartphone, you can easily make it more difficult for strangers to gain access to your data.

**Good advice for your security:**

  * Use a passcode on your mobile.
  * Make your mobile visible so you can find it again: If you have an iPhone, turn on Find My iPhone. If you have an Android or a Windows Phone, you can link it to your Google account or your Microsoft account, respectively.
  * If you receive calls from unknown, foreign numbers, block them afterwards on your mobile. The call does not cost you anything. But do not call back unknown foreign numbers - it can be expensive for you.
  * Do not give personal information to people you do not know or do not trust.
  * Never download programs or apps from vendors you do not trust.
  * Say yes to software updates. Updates are fixes for larger or smaller bugs in the software, and the fixes strengthen your mobile security.

## If Your Mobile is Stolen

  * Call and have the SIM card blocked immediately.
  * Change passwords for Facebook, your bank, and other websites with personal information.
