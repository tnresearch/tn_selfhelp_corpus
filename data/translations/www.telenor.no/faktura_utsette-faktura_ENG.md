# Postpone Invoice

Some months can be more difficult financially than others. You can postpone
payment yourself when you need to, and here we show you how to do it.

If you postpone the invoice on My Pages or in My Telenor, a fee of NOK 70 will be
added to your next invoice. The postponement is valid for 21 days from the
date you postpone it.

 **Exceptions:**

  * It is not possible to postpone the same invoice multiple times

  * It is not possible to postpone more than two different invoices at the same time

  * You cannot postpone the first invoice you receive from us

  * Invoices that have been sent to a collection agency cannot be postponed

## How to Postpone

## My Pages

 **How to postpone an invoice on My Pages:**

  1. [Log in to My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/faktura/)

  2. Find the invoice you want to postpone, and press Postpone

[Postpone Invoice on My Pages](https://www.telenor.no/mitt-telenor/faktura/)

## My Telenor

 **How to postpone an invoice in My Telenor:**

  1. Open the app

  2. Scroll down to Invoice and tap the banner

  3. Select the invoice you want to postpone

  4. If the invoice can be postponed, you will see a button with 'Postpone Payment'

  5. Press the button

If the invoice cannot be postponed, you will receive an explanation why.

[Open the My Telenor app](https://tlnr.no/mt)
